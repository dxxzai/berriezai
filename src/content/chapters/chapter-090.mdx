<h1>Chapter 90</h1>

<p>Crazy...</p>

<p>Seeing my favorite's mask up close made my heart start pounding.</p>

<p>'The Perception Suppression Mask!'</p>

<p>It definitely felt different from seeing it in the game.</p>

<p>I stared at the smooth mask as hard as I could, but as expected, I couldn't make anything out.</p>

<p>The perception-suppressing effect was working perfectly.</p>

<p>I was so captivated by the mask that I only belatedly realized my own situation.</p>

<p>Before I knew it, I was being carried by CEO Cha.</p>

<p>He released my waist and set me down on the ground.</p>

<p>Since the paralysis in my lower body still hadn't worn off, I naturally collapsed into a sitting position.</p>

<p>...Gasp.</p>

<p>My body jolted.</p>

<p>The white sword was embedded in the ground between my legs.</p>

<p>Behind Daeil Yeorae, which had sliced through the asphalt as if it were tofu, CEO Cha crouched down.</p>

<p>Squatting there with a rough, delinquent-like posture, he looked at me.</p>

<p>Unable to run because my legs were paralyzed, I could only stare back at him in fear.</p>

<p>He shoved the contract toward me.</p>

<p>"I-I'm not mentally prepared yet..."</p>

<p>"If you don't want to sign, want to use your fingerprint instead?"</p>

<p>Ignoring what I had just said, he even kindly suggested a different method of signing.</p>

<p>That was so very like CEO Cha.</p>

<p>I decided to try refusing politely first.</p>

<p>"CEO Cha-nim, thank you for personally coming to recruit me."</p>

<p>I started with a respectful greeting, hoping to make a good first impression, then cautiously got to the point.</p>

<p>"But I'm far too lacking to join Paseong—"</p>

<p>Before I could even finish speaking, the two directors aimed their gun and sword at Won Taebeom.</p>

<p>Won Taebeom looked at me with tense eyes.</p>

<p>CEO Cha spoke indifferently.</p>

<p>"Ten seconds."</p>

<p>The sheer professionalism with which he took a hostage and issued a threat...</p>

<p>That, too, was perfect CEO Cha.</p>

<p>I found myself admiring my favorite's personality all over again.</p>

<p>"Trainee Han. No matter what happens, you must never sign."</p>

<p>Won Taebeom spoke firmly.</p>

<p>But CEO Cha really was the sort of person who would cut off Won Taebeom's head without hesitation.</p>

<p>"Five seconds."</p>

<p>Standing before CEO Cha as he calmly counted down, I frantically wracked my brain.</p>

<p>"Three."</p>

<p>"W-W-Wait a second, CEO Cha-nim!"</p>

<p>"One."</p>

<p>"I'm your fan!!"</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>Even with the mask covering his face, I could feel the look of utter disbelief coming from him.</p>

<p>Still, I had successfully bought myself a little time, so I hurriedly continued.</p>

<p>"The chance to work with you would be amazing. Of course I'll sign! But I have conditions. If you agree to them, I'll sign immediately."</p>

<p>"Speak."</p>

<p>At the short command, I carefully began.</p>

<p>"First... I don't want the Trial Response Agency or anyone affiliated with it to suffer any more harm."</p>

<p>I had almost said "the people affiliated with it," but Samra came to mind, so I changed my wording.</p>

<p>The fact that CEO Cha was here meant Samra had already been defeated.</p>

<p>Considering how many of his functions were restricted, the fact that Samra had held out against CEO Cha wielding Daeil Yeorae for this long was incredible.</p>

<p>To truly oppose CEO Cha, he would have needed to operate not as a defensive android, but as one equipped for lethal combat.</p>

<p>Judging from the flash of light I'd seen earlier, he'd taken a serious hit.</p>

<p>I wondered if he'd been damaged beyond repair.</p>

<p>'I wonder if he can be repaired.'</p>

<p>The electronic billboards and streetlights still hadn't come back on.</p>

<p>Samra's inverse-phase field seemed to still be active.</p>

<p>His power source probably hadn't been destroyed, so even if he'd suffered major damage, he could likely tell me how to repair himself.</p>

<p>Putting my worries about Samra aside, I waited for CEO Cha's response.</p>

<p>I expected it to take at least a little time.</p>

<p>It wasn't an easy condition.</p>

<p>"And?"</p>

<p>Instead, he brushed it off as though it were nothing.</p>

<p>As long as I signed the contract, he clearly didn't care about the Trial Response Agency at all.</p>

<p>I struggled to keep my expression under control.</p>

<p>That was completely different from what I'd expected.</p>

<p>I'd assumed Paseong had some deeper motive for pulling a stunt like this.</p>

<p>Some grudge against the Agency that I didn't know about.</p>

<p>Or maybe there was something important there.</p>

<p>'But... they really attacked the Agency just because of me?'</p>

<p>I couldn't understand what Paseong stood to gain by going this far to recruit me.</p>

<p>At this point, they'd done more than just fall out with the Agency—they'd become mortal enemies.</p>

<p>They'd reached the point where it would practically be more profitable to accept an expensive contract and sell me overseas.</p>

<p>Every suspicious possibility ran through my head.</p>

<p>I really didn't want to be thinking like this.</p>

<p>But my opponent was CEO Cha.</p>

<p>I couldn't help it.</p>

<p>'At least Lieutenant Won and Samra are safe now.'</p>

<p>I'd thought the first condition would buy me at least a little time.</p>

<p>Instead, it had completely failed.</p>

<p>That made my second condition all the more important.</p>

<p>I thought carefully.</p>

<p>Something CEO Cha absolutely couldn't agree to easily...</p>

<p>Something natural, not excessive...</p>

<p>I could feel his gaze on me.</p>

<p>Knowing his personality, there was no way he'd patiently listen to a third condition.</p>

<p>This had to settle everything.</p>

<p>I swallowed.</p>

<p>My Adam's apple bobbed.</p>

<p>There wasn't any more time to think.</p>

<p>"I want to see your face."</p>

<p>The moment I finished speaking, Seo Moonchan and Mok Saehwa, who had been watching, reacted.</p>

<p>Seo Moonchan, whose glasses were cracked, let out a grin.</p>

<p>Mok Saehwa couldn't contain herself.</p>

<p>"An Adaptee like Goyo-nim absolutely has to come to Paseong!"</p>

<p>Apparently no Adaptee had ever dared tell CEO Cha to take off his mask right to his face.</p>

<p>'Then again... they all probably died.'</p>

<p>I waited for him to refuse.</p>

<p>Naturally, I expected him to reject me outright and demand another condition.</p>

<p>But for some reason...</p>

<p>He stayed silent.</p>

<p>The only part of his face visible through the mask—his eyes—remained fixed on me.</p>

<p>I couldn't tell what color they were.</p>

<p>But I could feel his gaze.</p>

<p>"You're curious about my face?"</p>

<p>"...Yes. Please show me."</p>

<p>He leaned in a little closer.</p>

<p>For a moment, it felt as though he might actually remove the mask.</p>

<p>Unable even to blink, I stared at him.</p>

<p>He spoke slowly.</p>

<p>"Sign the contract first."</p>

<p>There was mechanical distortion in his voice.</p>

<p>Somehow, it even sounded like he was smiling.</p>

<p>For about 0.1 seconds...</p>

<p>I had a very bad thought.</p>

<p>'Should I just sign it?'</p>

<p>Actually...</p>

<p>Maybe it had been closer to a full second.</p>

<p>After barely shaking the thought off, I asked,</p>

<p>"...Are you really okay with that? Showing your face...?"</p>

<p>I'd played enough of the game to know how fiercely he avoided revealing it.</p>

<p>'Even after playing together a thousand times, I'd never seen his face.'</p>

<p>As memories of our time in the game surfaced, he interrupted sharply.</p>

<p>"Those are all your conditions, right?"</p>

<p>"Ah, not yet."</p>

<p>"Han Goyo."</p>

<p>CEO Cha tilted his head.</p>

<p>"I can hear those gears turning."</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>He grabbed my wrist.</p>

<p>Then placed my hand on top of the contract.</p>

<p>The paper glowed.</p>

<p>A quill appeared in my hand.</p>

<p>Holding it, I broke out in a cold sweat.</p>

<p>There was nowhere left to run.</p>

<p>At this rate, all that remained was becoming CEO Cha's pet hamster that cleared Trials for him.</p>

<p>"Roasted Chestnut."</p>

<p>The immediate problem wasn't just CEO Cha.</p>

<p>Dominic spoke to me.</p>

<p>"Are you going to sign the contract?"</p>

<p>The pudding slime sitting on my shoulder watched with bright blue eyes.</p>

<p>Unlike the Fire Dragon's Mark, this contract would leave traces upon both my body and my soul.</p>

<p>There was no way Dominic would like that.</p>

<p>If I couldn't resolve this myself...</p>

<p>He might intervene however he pleased.</p>

<p>Back inside Slow to Full, at least he still had the convenient disguise of being a "mage."</p>

<p>Here...</p>

<p>There were no such restraints.</p>

<p>I didn't even want to imagine what Dominic—who no longer even pretended to be a mage—might do.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh, my only hope, still wouldn't arrive for over three minutes.</p>

<p>Long enough to finish cooking instant noodles.</p>

<p>More than enough time to sign a contract.</p>

<p>As slowly as possible, I lowered the quill toward the paper.</p>

<p>Then suddenly, Samra's words resurfaced.</p>
<br/>
<p><span class="flashback">"From this moment, I will generate an inverse-phase electromagnetic field for fifteen minutes and thirty-seven seconds."</span></p>
<br/>
<p>He'd already finished giving all the relevant explanations underground.</p>

<p>Yet he'd deliberately repeated the same words in front of CEO Cha.</p>

<p>Even though there was no advantage to letting him know how long it would take Je Hyun-oh to arrive.</p>

<p>'Wait.'</p>

<p>Samra had never actually said that the duration of the field matched Je Hyun-oh's arrival time.</p>

<p>That was only my own conclusion based on the situation.</p>

<p>CEO Cha had probably reached the same conclusion.</p>

<p>The instant that realization struck me...</p>

<p>The darkness around us deepened.</p>

<p>The moon—the only remaining source of light after the city had gone dark—was suddenly blocked.</p>

<p>I looked up.</p>

<p>Six black metallic wings spread across the sky, silhouetted against the moon.</p>

<p>It was the most welcome crow I'd ever seen in my life.</p>

<p>With all my might, I sent a telepathic message.</p>

<p>But right now...</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh was all I had.</p>

<p>I expected him to immediately engage Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>Just like Je Hyun-oh had once carried a sack of supplies.</p>

<p>Dangling helplessly, I stared at him in confusion.</p>

<p>Then looked back at the two directors.</p>

<p>"Run."</p>

<p>The instant he gave the order, all three took off at terrifying speed.</p>

<p>For a brief moment I was too stunned to react.</p>

<p>Then I screamed,</p>

<p>"Lieutenant Colonel Je—"</p>

<p>"Ghk!"</p>

<p>CEO Cha tightened his arm around me, squeezing the air from my lungs.</p>

<p>Pinned beneath his arm, I couldn't even finish calling for help.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh was already growing farther away.</p>

<p>"Director Mok."</p>

<p>"Yes."</p>

<p>At his call, Mok Saehwa stopped running and immediately aimed her gun at Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>He still hovered in the air, blocking the moon.</p>

<p>Mok Saehwa narrowed her eyes and sighted him through her scope.</p>

<p>The two remained locked on each other.</p>

<p>The instant it passed over Mok Saehwa—</p>

<p>Ratatatatat!</p>

<p>She unleashed a barrage of bullets.</p>

<p>The black streak twisted through impossible trajectories, avoiding every shot.</p>

<p>But she'd never intended to hit him.</p>

<p>She'd only wanted to slow him down.</p>

<p>The radiance was bright enough to turn night into day.</p>

<p>White brilliance scattered everywhere, briefly blinding the surroundings.</p>
