<h1>Chapter 11</h1>

<p>Red dots speckled the white restraint suit like a constellation. The moment the order to fire came down, I'd be Swiss cheese.</p>

<p>This was exactly when I needed Samra's cooperation.</p>

<p>I rolled my eyes toward him.</p>

<p>Samra wore a white uniform — different from the standard TRA gear.</p>

<p>The outfit that marked him as something special stood out even more against the sea of dark uniforms surrounding us.</p>

<p>Samra placed a hand on my shoulder. He patted it a couple of times, as if to show that even this touch was safe.</p>

<p>"Han Goyo is human."</p>

<p>A distinct ripple of murmurs spread at Samra's declaration.</p>

<p>Just then, the wall of uniforms split in half, and a man with graying hair walked through the gap.</p>

<p>The moment I saw his medal-heavy uniform, I knew exactly who he was.</p>

<p>The Director of the Trial Response Agency — Jang Seokyun.</p>

<p>Despite being past sixty, he had a solid, well-built frame. Power radiated from the eyes beneath his thick brows.</p>

<p>I shot a quick bow to the highest authority in this place.</p>

<p>"A pleasure to meet you, Director. I'm Han Goyo."</p>

<p>What I really wanted was a full ninety-degree bow, but one wrong move and my body would probably fall apart, so I settled for a nod.</p>

<p>Jang Seokyun received my greeting with a meaningful smile.</p>

<p>"First time meeting me, yet you know my face. That's supposed to be confidential, you know."</p>

<p>He strode over to me.</p>

<p>"Director!"</p>

<p>Urgent protests came from all around, but Jang Seokyun ignored them.</p>

<p>"He's human, isn't he? And Samra's here."</p>

<p>He stopped right in front of me and offered his greeting.</p>

<p>"Good to meet you. I'm Jang Seokyun."</p>

<p>Then he studied me carefully.</p>

<p>"A handshake... hmm, that might be difficult."</p>

<p>The restraint suit had both arms bound, making a handshake impossible.</p>

<p>I tried to put on an innocent smile to emphasize that I was a harmless human.</p>

<p>"Sorry I can't greet you properly. If it's not too much trouble, could you call off the sniper rifles?"</p>

<p>At Jang Seokyun's gesture, the red dots retracted.</p>

<p>But the pointers aimed at my head, heart, and other vital spots stayed put.</p>

<p>Samra had vouched for my humanity, but I was still a suspicious bastard — that hadn't changed.</p>

<p>Still, this was good enough.</p>

<p>"Thank you."</p>

<p>"Haha, I'd like to clear them all away, but I suppose I've gotten cautious in my old age. Hope you understand, Goyo."</p>

<p>"Of course."</p>

<p>I smiled lightly, but inside I was on high alert.</p>

<p>Jang Seokyun looked like a good-natured man, but he was actually terrifying.</p>

<p>A man who devoted himself to the nation and the TRA, who believed sacrifice was inevitable for justice.</p>

<p>One wrong step, and I could become that inevitable sacrifice.</p>

<p>"You were a pro gamer, weren't you, Goyo. Is that what they call a sport these days?"</p>

<p>"...Yes."</p>

<p>"Quite impressive. But how did you end up here?"</p>

<p>If there was ever a time to go all in, it was now.</p>

<p>With practically the entire TRA gathered here, I had to make them recognize that I was useful.</p>

<p>No — not just useful.</p>

<p>Essential.</p>

<p>"Director."</p>

<p>"Hm?"</p>

<p>"Please spare my life."</p>

<p>Caught off guard by the completely unexpected words, Jang Seokyun barked out a laugh.</p>

<p>"Hahaha!"</p>

<p>He asked, his voice still brimming with amusement.</p>

<p>"And if I spare you?"</p>

<p>I declared to the TRA:</p>

<p>"I'll clear and bring back three True Endings within a month."</p>

<p>If I could move my hands, I would have held up three fingers.</p>

<p>Just spare my life, Director.</p>

<p>I locked eyes with Jang Seokyun, my gaze blazing with determination.</p>

<p>It was a calculated bet.</p>

<p>Either way, I had to enter Trials to keep my limbs intact. If Jang Seokyun accepted me here, I could enter Trials with the TRA's backing.</p>

<p>And the Jang Seokyun I knew...</p>

<p>He would never be able to refuse my offer.</p>

<p>Jang Seokyun had stopped laughing. His tiger-like eyes narrowed.</p>

<p>"Are you saying this whole incident wasn't just luck?"</p>

<p>"That's right."</p>

<p>"Samra."</p>

<p>"Han Goyo's statement is factual. He has sufficient capability to achieve what he's proposed."</p>

<p>Samra met Jang Seokyun's gaze squarely.</p>

<p>"Furthermore, Han Goyo's identity has been verified."</p>

<p>His unblinking eyes were unsettling.</p>

<p>Outwardly no different from a human, but the eyes made no effort to hide the machine within.</p>

<p>"This is the judgment of Samra Mansang of the Trial Response Agency."</p>

<p>After Samra's statement, a heavy silence settled over the room.</p>

<p>No one had expected Samra to step up and defend me so actively.</p>

<p>"Well, now."</p>

<p>Jang Seokyun finally spoke after a long pause.</p>

<p>"At my age, there aren't many things that surprise me anymore. But you, Goyo, you've got this old man's heart going for a ride."</p>

<p>He raised one hand. The red laser pointers aimed at me vanished completely.</p>

<p>I let out a long, silent breath of relief.</p>

<p>I was alive...</p>

<p>"Let's have a talk somewhere quiet. Follow me. You too, Samra."</p>

<p>Jang Seokyun turned and led the way.</p>

<p>But I couldn't follow in my restraint suit.</p>

<p>My legs were bound too — I'd need to be wheeled around on something like a stretcher.</p>

<p>Samra quietly called out to Jang Seokyun.</p>

<p>"Director."</p>

<p>"Ah. I wasn't thinking. My apologies."</p>

<p>Realizing my predicament belatedly, Jang Seokyun allowed me to change clothes.</p>

<p>I was finally free from that damn restraint suit!</p>

<p>When they handed me a comfortable tracksuit — the kind TRA soldiers wore during training — I was almost moved to tears.</p>

<p>'I'm going to live a good life from now on.'</p>

<p>I never wanted to wear a restraint suit again.</p>

<p>After changing, I sat in the Director's office side by side with Samra.</p>

<p>The office I'd visited in dot graphics had the exact same layout as the game. Even the decorative orchid pot was in the same spot — like someone had copy-pasted it.</p>

<p>Just how much did the Creator know?</p>

<p>There was no way a human could do this.</p>

<p>"Han Goyo."</p>

<p>Jang Seokyun, who had been looking out the window with his hands behind his back, turned to face me.</p>

<p>"Do you know the founding purpose of the Trial Response Agency?"</p>

<p>I knew, but I pretended not to.</p>

<p>"No."</p>

<p>"It's a national agency that responds to calamities and threats caused by superordinate beings."</p>

<p>His voice was calm.</p>

<p>"We judged you to be a superordinate being — a National Calamity. Now that you've been confirmed as human, that judgment is suspended."</p>

<p>Suspended, not fully withdrawn.</p>

<p>Well, I understood. No matter how firmly Samra vouched for me, I was still too suspicious.</p>

<p>"In any case, you seem to be special. This is the first time I've seen Samra take someone's side like this."</p>

<p>He flashed a brief, playful smile, then turned serious.</p>

<p>"Can you really clear True Endings?"</p>

<p>"If you trust me, I'll repay that trust."</p>

<p>Jang Seokyun looked at me for a long moment.</p>

<p>"You remind me of myself when I was young."</p>

<p>A compliment... I think?</p>

<p>After a moment's hesitation, I replied with a "Thank you."</p>

<p>My answer seemed to amuse him — he let out a short laugh. Then he picked up a folder from his desk.</p>

<p>"The number of Trials hatching keeps increasing, but we're desperately short on Adaptees to respond. And permanent closure is out of the question."</p>

<p>A note of helplessness crept into his voice.</p>

<p>It was the helplessness of being stuck in a swamp — no matter how you struggled, nothing got solved, and things only got worse.</p>

<p>"It's an age that needs heroes."</p>

<p>Jang Seokyun said it like a light joke. But it was earnest truth.</p>

<p>"Whatever you are, Goyo — if you can reduce even one Trial on this soil of South Korea."</p>

<p>He held out the folder.</p>

<p>"I'll back you."</p>

<p>"I'll give it my all."</p>

<p>I answered politely and took the folder.</p>

<p>Deal sealed.</p>

<p>Jang Seokyun issued his order as Director of the TRA.</p>

<p>"Effective immediately, I am forming a Director-level Special Mission Team centered on Han Goyo. Duration: one month. Team members: Han Goyo and all field team leaders, with Samra assigned as support."</p>

<p>Samra gave a crisp salute at the assignment order.</p>

<p>I felt dazed. I'd figured I wouldn't be sent into the Trials alone, but I never expected a whole special team to be formed.</p>

<p>And field team leaders plus Samra as support — the best operatives the TRA had to offer. An elite squad.</p>

<p>"As for Han Goyo's status..."</p>

<p>I belatedly opened the folder I'd received.</p>

<p>"Trainee."</p>

<p>Inside was a TRA trainee contract stamped with the Director's seal.</p>

<p>"During your time as a trainee, you'll stay at the TRA and be protected."</p>

<p>Jang Seokyun said with a benevolent smile.</p>

<p>"Take care of yourself, Trainee Han."</p>

<p>And just like that, I became a one-month trainee of the TRA.</p>

<hr class="calibre3"/>

<p>I stood in front of the mirror, fidgeting awkwardly with my sleeve.</p>

<p>The uniform was a sleek dark blue with silver trim. The full set included a cap and gloves, but since most people only wore the uniform outside of official occasions, I followed suit.</p>

<p>Never thought I'd be wearing a TRA uniform...</p>

<p>The fact that Trials existed, the fact that I'd become a TRA trainee — none of it felt real.</p>

<p>Last night, I'd fallen asleep in what was supposed to be my assigned quarters — a quarantine room masquerading as a dorm. A studio with a bathroom, plenty of space for one person.</p>

<p>I'd passed out from exhaustion, and when I woke up, it was morning. I ate the meal served on a tray, then changed into the uniform from the closet.</p>

<p>And now I was waiting for whoever was coming to pick me up.</p>

<p>I felt like I was dreaming, staring at the mirror for a long time until a knock pulled my gaze away.</p>

<p>The steel door opened, and the moment I saw who walked in, I hurriedly bowed.</p>

<p>"Hello."</p>

<p>"Hey, Goyo. Kwak Hanmuk."</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk, who'd flung the door open, bent at a perfect ninety-degree angle to match my bow. Caught off guard by the reciprocal bow, I floundered.</p>

<p>"You can relax. I'm younger than you anyway. Drop the formalities."</p>

<p>"Really? Then call me hyung."</p>

<p>Then he shifted to a different kind of pressure.</p>

<p id="chestnut">"Want me to give you a nickname so we sound closer? Was it Chestnut?"</p>

<p>It's Roasted Chestnut...</p>

<p>"Just call me Goyo, please."</p>

<p>I quietly asked, not wanting to be called Chestnut or Roasted anything in real life.</p>

<p>"Must be because you're tall. The uniform looks good on you."</p>

<p>"Thank you."</p>

<p>"Huh, he looks more normal than I expected. Don't you think, Captain Mo?"</p>

<p>At that, Mo Haein stepped out from behind Kwak Hanmuk.</p>

<p>She'd arrived just now, but his large frame had hidden her.</p>

<p>"Captain, hello."</p>

<p>I bowed just like I had to Kwak Hanmuk. Mo Haein was silent for a moment.</p>

<p>I was a little tense — our last encounter hadn't ended well.</p>

<p>'Well, I did show her my head exploding.'</p>

<p>She looked at me with an unreadable expression, then gave a short nod.</p>

<p>"I'll be counting on you for the next month, Trainee."</p>

<p>My shoulders, tense without me realizing, finally relaxed.</p>

<p>I smiled faintly and said I was counting on her too.</p>

<p>The two of them led me to the Special Mission Team office.</p>

<p>It wasn't far from my quarters.</p>

<p>During the short walk, stares poured in from all directions, uncomfortably sharp.</p>

<p>I'd wanted to explore the TRA building if I got the chance, but it looked like I'd have to stay cooped up in my room for a while.</p>

<p>The Special Mission Team office, set up in just one day, was still a bit messy.</p>

<p>Unpacked boxes were stacked up, and a large round table sat on the other side.</p>

<p>The break room, however, was fully stocked.</p>

<p>Massage chairs, a cot, a sofa, even gaming consoles.</p>

<p>Not to mention expensive snacks, a refrigerator, and an espresso machine.</p>

<p>But the things that should have been there were missing.</p>

<p>No conference room, no meeting projector, no office computers or desks.</p>

<p>As I wondered why, I saw an android emerge from the break room carrying a tray of snacks and coffee — and I understood.</p>

<p>Ah. Samra.</p>

<p>A supercomputer could handle all the office work, so field personnel only needed to focus on field operations. That was the philosophy behind this setup.</p>

<p>'Besides, each team leader probably handles their individual work in their own office.'</p>

<p>This was only possible because we were a ridiculously spoiled team using Samra as support.</p>

<p>I looked at Samra with greedy eyes as he set the tray down on the round table and greeted me.</p>

<p>"Welcome, Han Goyo."</p>

<p>There were four cups of coffee — including one for the android. The machine built with future technology worked fine even with liquid inside.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein both looked baffled as Samra served coffee and snacks. Kwak Hanmuk asked seriously.</p>

<p>"Samra... are you sick? No, wait, what's the right word. Did you get hacked?"</p>

<p>"I am perfectly fine. And with current Earth technology, hacking me is impossible."</p>

<p>Samra drank his iced coffee like a human and projected a screen into the air instead of using a beam projector.</p>

<p>"I will begin the Special Mission Team briefing."</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk rustled around adding sugar to his coffee, but Samra ignored him and started the briefing.</p>

<p>"The first Trial we will enter is &lt;Dead Zone Delivery&gt;."</p>

<p>The Special Mission Team's first mission was a zombie game.</p>
