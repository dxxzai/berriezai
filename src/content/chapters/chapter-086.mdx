<h1>Chapter 86</h1>

<p>It really was still a dream, wasn’t it?</p>

<p>Listening to the Code Red announcement, I thought that for a moment.</p>

<p>However, I soon felt the soft sensation of the dangling pudding on top of my head and realized this was reality.</p>

<p>Dominic remained firmly attached to my head without falling off despite the android running.</p>

<p>“Roasted Chestnut. It is a little noisy. I am worried it may have a negative effect since you are still recovering.”</p>

<p>This wasn’t… exactly just a little noisy.</p>

<p>I admired the pudding slime’s choice of words and tried moving my body, but I had absolutely no strength.</p>

<p>When I briefly woke up earlier, I thought I was fine, but that was completely wrong.</p>

<p>Samra explained the situation to me in a calm voice that did not match the chaos around us.</p>

<p>“Han Goyo-ssi has regained consciousness after approximately 14 days. Use of your body is not recommended.”</p>

<p>…while saying that, he was sprinting through the hallway at incredible speed, yet he never collided with another person or object.</p>

<p>“Samra! Stop for a moment!”</p>

<p>Soldiers occasionally tried to grab him, but Samra avoided all of them in an instant.</p>

<p>I was being carried on Samra’s back with the feeling that I had not fully woken up yet.</p>

<p>While that was happening, I heard a voice.</p>

<p>“Goyo-ssi! Goyo-ssi, where are you? I’ll protect you!”</p>

<p>It was Research Division member Ham Jiwol calling for me.</p>

<p>Normally, she stuttered and had a quiet voice, but right now her pronunciation was extremely clear and her voice was as booming as a general’s.</p>

<p>Ham Jiwol in this state was several times more dangerous than usual.</p>

<p>I quickly grabbed around Samra’s neck and whispered.</p>

<p>“Let’s go faster.”</p>

<p>Perhaps Samra agreed with me, because he increased his speed.</p>

<p>When Samra reached the stairs, he ran down to the lower floors.</p>

<p>I had vaguely assumed we would go to the rooftop and take a helicopter, so I was confused when we headed downward.</p>

<p>At this point, I felt like I had to ask.</p>

<p>“Um, Samra? Where are we going?”</p>

<p>“There is an escape route connected to an adjacent structure near the underground area. We will move outside.”</p>

<p>“Then… what if we hide inside the virtual space?”</p>

<p>“Not recommended. The longer one remains in virtual space, the higher the possibility of cognitive impairment. Han Goyo-ssi’s physical functions are currently reduced, so you are not suited to withstand the mental burden.”</p>

<p>It sounded like he had experimented with putting multiple people into virtual space before.</p>

<p>“Additionally, because physical movement is impossible within virtual space, there is a possibility of becoming trapped inside the Trial Response Agency. Among the available options, I have judged this to be the best choice.”</p>

<p>During this urgent situation, I couldn’t hold back and asked the thing I was most curious about.</p>

<p>“Is there a specific reason why you’re carrying me like this while running…?”</p>

<p>When I asked about this analog method of transportation, Samra’s running speed briefly slowed.</p>

<p>Samra, who normally answered instantly no matter what I asked, replied after a slight delay.</p>

<p>“I specialize most in information retrieval. Other than that, I only possess combat functions intended for defensive purposes.”</p>

<p>Since he was a cutting-edge android, I had vaguely imagined he could transform and fly like a jet, but apparently he did not have such a function.</p>

<p>Because of the sudden terrorist attack, Samra had chosen carrying me on his back as the safest way to move me.</p>

<p>It seemed he judged that the reliable Special Mission Team members were all away on missions, and receiving assistance from other soldiers carried too much uncertainty.</p>

<p>‘Is it because there was a possibility that Ham Jiwol or Director Jang Seokyun could interfere?’</p>

<p>Especially the former was someone who could easily use the chaos as an opportunity to secretly move me somewhere else.</p>

<p>“I predicted the terrorist attack beforehand, but CEO Cha of Paseong used a Trial item to create an error in my calculations.”</p>

<p>From his following explanation, it seemed that Samra had originally intended to let me recover in my hospital room for as long as possible rather than evacuating me immediately.</p>

<p>However, CEO Cha, who understood Samra’s methods, used an item to catch him off guard.</p>

<p>He used an incredibly rare consumable item, one impossible to place a value on, to conceal his movements.</p>

<p>CEO Cha, who had temporarily escaped Samra’s information network, immediately carried out a terrorist attack on TRA.</p>

<p>‘Even androids make excuses.’</p>

<p>Would it be an exaggeration to think that he seemed slightly embarrassed?</p>

<p>I kept the thought to myself for Samra’s sake and asked another question.</p>

<p>“By the way, why did CEO Cha launch a terrorist attack on TRA?”</p>

<p>CEO Cha did not act emotionally.</p>

<p>He was not the type to start a fight where he would lose without a clear reason.</p>

<p>Just as I thought there could never be enough reason to commit a terrorist attack on TRA—</p>
<br/>
<em><p>“Code Red. Code Red. This is a real emergency. Code Re—”</p></em>
<br/>
<p>Crackle!</p>

<p>The regularly repeating broadcast became filled with static and abruptly cut off.</p>

<p>The loud sirens that sounded like they would burst my eardrums also stopped, and sudden silence fell over the TRA building.</p>

<p>Samra stopped completely and looked up at the ceiling.</p>

<p>His holographic eyes flashed.</p>
<br/>
<em><p>“Samra.”</p></em>
<br/>
<p>A voice mixed with mechanical tones came through the speakers.</p>
<br/>
<em><p>“Enough playing around. Hand him over before I blow up all of TRA.”</p></em>
<br/>
<p>There was no emotional excitement in the calm tone. Because of that, it felt even colder.</p>

<p>CEO Cha asked:</p>
<br/>
<em><p>“Where is Han Goyo?”</p></em>
<br/>
<p>I doubted my ears.</p>

<p>When I had gone to Chairman Cheon-gang’s house before, I had heard that CEO Cha was looking for me.</p>

<p>But he had launched a terrorist attack on TRA just to find me.</p>

<p>Why was he going this far to find me?</p>

<p>And….</p>

<p>‘What are you planning to do now?!’</p>

<p>He had openly committed terrorism, but I had no idea what he intended to do after this.</p>

<p>My head hurt from my favorite person’s insane behavior.</p>

<p>Suddenly, I felt sorry toward Mo Haein.</p>

<p>Was this how she felt when she looked at me like I was crazy?</p>

<p>‘Captain Mo, I’ll do better from now on.’</p>

<p>While apologizing to Mo Haein internally, Samra calmly spoke again.</p>

<p>“After escaping outside the Trial Response Agency, we will prepare for urban combat.”</p>

<p>I was startled.</p>

<p>This android was saying something dangerous.</p>

<p>He had even said his combat functions were focused on defense, so what did he mean by urban combat?</p>

<p>“I’ll just talk with CEO Cha. Because of me, TRA is like this right now, and regardless of that, urban combat in the middle of Seoul is…”</p>

<p>“Han Goyo-ssi.”</p>

<p>Samra cut me off.</p>

<p>“I promised unconditional cooperation.”</p>

<p>The contents of the deal were unconditional cooperation toward ‘Han Goyo,’ under the premise that it would not harm humanity.</p>

<p>I had not expected that promise to remain valid even in a situation where Seoul, including TRA, might be destroyed.</p>

<p>‘I don’t think I’m that important.’</p>

<p>Am I? Am I really that important?</p>

<p>If I considered myself the only being capable of providing Samra with new learning data, it did seem somewhat plausible.</p>

<p>The fact that Samra was acting as if he was evacuating while carrying only the most expensive item from a burning house made it feel even more that way.</p>

<p>Regardless, it was something I was grateful for.</p>

<p>I liked CEO Cha, but because I knew his personality well, I did not want to be dragged along in a situation like this.</p>

<p>Seeing CEO Cha willing to openly oppose TRA and search for me meant there was a benefit worth that much.</p>

<p>I could easily imagine a future where I became CEO Cha’s Trial-closing slave and was exploited.</p>

<p>Or perhaps something even worse.</p>

<p>While I briefly imagined the things CEO Cha could do with me, Samra entered the underground area.</p>

<p>When he opened the hidden iron door on Underground Floor 5, dry and stale air filtered through a ventilation system poured out.</p>

<p>The passage leading to another building existed solely for evacuation purposes.</p>

<p>The thick protective walls had exposed concrete surfaces, and the only lighting came from emergency lights illuminating the passage at regular intervals.</p>

<p>In that place, where the only sound was the low hum of the air circulation system, an unexpected person was waiting.</p>

<p>“Samra.”</p>

<p>It was Lieutenant Won Taebeom from Field Team 2.</p>

<p>I never expected to encounter him like this after briefly seeing him right before entering Sla2Plate.</p>

<p>I only felt sorry toward him, since he had made time to come but had been pushed out of entering because of Dominic.</p>

<p>Won Taebeom looked at me and Samra and was speechless.</p>

<p>He was clearly bewildered by the cutting-edge android’s analog method of transportation.</p>

<p>Even while being carried by Samra, I greeted him.</p>

<p>“Hello, Lieutenant.”</p>

<p>Then, after receiving Won Taebeom’s incredulous gaze, I quietly closed my mouth.</p>

<p>Apparently, it was not the right time for greetings.</p>

<p>“Lieutenant Won Taebeom. Why are you here?”</p>

<p>Although Samra said it like that, he did not seem surprised.</p>

<p>He already knew Won Taebeom was at the escape route.</p>

<p>It felt like he had avoided all the other soldiers but allowed himself to meet Won Taebeom.</p>

<p>Won Taebeom answered Samra’s question politely.</p>

<p>“I will provide support.”</p>

<p>“Is this Director Jang Seokyun’s order?”</p>

<p>“No. It is my personal judgment.”</p>

<p>Samra’s pupils briefly enlarged and shrank repeatedly.</p>

<p>After determining the truthfulness of his statement, Samra allowed Won Taebeom to accompany us.</p>

<p>“Support approved.”</p>

<p>Kuuung!</p>

<p>The moment Samra finished speaking, a massive sound and vibration occurred.</p>

<p>If even the deeply underground escape route received a slight tremor, then the situation above must have been a complete disaster.</p>

<p>Samra stared into empty space for a moment.</p>

<p>After finishing gathering information about the current situation, Samra spoke.</p>

<p>“After reaching the surface, I will block the electromagnetic field within a 1 km radius, including TRA, for 15 minutes and 37 seconds.”</p>

<p>“Confirmed.”</p>

<p>Won Taebeom immediately answered, but Samra did not pay him any attention.</p>

<p>He had not been speaking to him in the first place.</p>

<p>“Han Goyo-ssi. When the electromagnetic field is blocked, you will experience slight dizziness along with electrical stimulation to your body.”</p>

<p>At his kind warning, I quickly answered.</p>

<p>“Yes, confirmed.”</p>

<p>“It should not be beyond your limits even in your current physical condition, but if you feel even the slightest abnormality, inform me immediately.”</p>

<p>I wanted to tell him that I wasn’t that weak.</p>

<p>But considering I could barely even stand properly and was currently being carried on Samra’s back, my statement would not have much credibility.</p>

<p>So I obediently only said I understood.</p>

<p>From that moment, Samra ran without a word.</p>

<p>It was a speed that even Won Taebeom, a Trial-infected person, could barely keep up with.</p>

<p>If I had not been on his back, Samra likely would have moved even faster.</p>

<p>I periodically checked whether Dominic was still attached to the top of my head.</p>

<p>Whatever material the slime was made of, he absolutely refused to fall off and remained stuck there.</p>

<p>He only squished slightly up and down following the motion of running.</p>

<p>Reassured, I focused only on holding onto Samra properly.</p>

<p>Before long, we exited the underground passage.</p>

<p>We entered an unfamiliar building connected to the passage, but it seemed everyone had already evacuated, as it was completely empty.</p>

<p>We climbed the building stairs toward the first floor and finally went outside.</p>

<p>The sun had set, and it was pitch-black night.</p>

<p>As I inhaled the fresh air, I suddenly sucked in my breath.</p>

<p>A black jeep parked in front of the building.</p>

<p>A man sat on the large vehicle body, illuminated by a streetlight like a spotlight, sitting in a careless posture.</p>

<p>Both hands were shoved into the pockets of his windbreaker jacket, and the combination of headphones around his neck and his tracksuit gave him a carefree appearance.</p>

<p>A sword in a white sheath hung loosely at his waist.</p>

<p>A matte white mask stared at me.</p>

<p>The mask, which only revealed the eyes, caused cognitive interference in those who looked at it, making even the color of his eyes impossible to distinguish.</p>

<p>But I immediately realized who the person was.</p>

<p>How could I not?</p>

<p>It was CEO Cha.</p>

<p>“Han Goyo.”</p>

<p>The mechanical voice flew toward me.</p>

<p>“It’s pretty difficult to see your face even once?”</p>
