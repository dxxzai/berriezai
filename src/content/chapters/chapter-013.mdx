<h1>Chapter 13</h1>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk's jaw dropped.</p>

<p>"...Whoa."</p>

<p>He sprinted over and slung an arm around my shoulder with a solid thump.</p>

<p>"I've heard people called crazy bastards before, but I've never actually done it myself."</p>

<p>Grinning, he showed off his sharp canines and praised me.</p>

<p>"Goyo, you really seem like a crazy bastard."</p>

<p>"Thank you."</p>

<p>When I thanked him, he burst out laughing — HAHAHA!</p>

<p>I also picked up a baseball bat and gave it to Kwak Hanmuk as a gift.</p>

<p>Encouraged by his enthusiastic agreement, I laid out the plan.</p>

<p>"There's a group that's taken over a convenience store."</p>

<p>The bastards occupying the big mart had committed every crime you could in a zombie world — murder, arson, looting.</p>

<p>A group of about five or six. If left alone, they'd keep tripping up players all the way through DeZonDeal.</p>

<p>Bad enough to make zombies seem like the better option.</p>

<p>In an apocalypse where public order had collapsed, vigilante justice was necessary.</p>

<p>The fastest strategy was to clean them out early and then start for real.</p>

<p>"Let's go."</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk listened to the gist of it, asked no further questions, and tucked the baseball bat under his arm.</p>

<p>I led the way as he followed at a leisurely pace. We reached the destination. The big mart's name was painfully straightforward.</p>

<div class="fieldset"><p>Dead Mart</p></div>

<p>The clean front glass showed the inside clearly.</p>

<p>DeZonDeal's zombies had degraded sight and smell — they only reacted to sound, so there was no need to cover the windows.</p>

<p>As I registered the store, a System window appeared.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<div class="systembox_wrap">
<p>
◆Main Quest: Drive out the group occupying Dead Mart.<br/>

You have chosen a reckless approach for a quick clear.<br/>

The occupants of the mart, always brimming with rage, are formidable opponents who want to turn you into zombie chow.<br/>

But you believe that with a strong companion by your side, this adventure might just succeed.
</p>
</div>
</div>

<p>The System window wasn't wrong. If I'd been playing alone, I never would have attempted this.</p>

<p>But now I had a reliable ace up my sleeve.</p>

<p>"There."</p>

<p>The moment I pointed at the mart, Kwak Hanmuk shot off like an arrow.</p>

<p>CRASH!</p>

<p>The baseball bat swung clean and smashed the front glass window. Kwak Hanmuk leaped inside with the shards.</p>

<p>The bastards who'd been lounging against the shelves, laughing among themselves, scrambled to grab their weapons. One of them even had a gun — god knows where he got it.</p>

<p>But it meant nothing in front of Kwak Hanmuk.</p>

<p>BANG!</p>

<p>The gunshot echoed everywhere.</p>

<p>The bullet hit nothing but a poor bag of chips.</p>

<p>The innocent potato crisps burst into unrecognizable pieces. The baseball bat came down on his head right after.</p>

<p>THWACK! — the sound of impact, and the man with the gun crumpled to the floor.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk caught the gun as it slipped from his hand, effortless.</p>

<p>He tucked the gun into his waistband and swung the bat at the staggering bastards.</p>

<p>The man who took a full hit flew like a baseball and knocked over a shelf. Spicy chicken cup noodles rained down on top of him.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk stepped onto the fallen shelf and launched himself into the air. A clean kick split the air and sent the bastard clutching a knife flying.</p>

<p>There was a huge difference between a professionally trained soldier and guys in their early twenties who hadn't even done military service yet.</p>

<p>A gap so obvious it was embarrassing to even mention.</p>

<p>I just watched from the back, and whenever the bastards squirming on the floor tried to get up, I swung my baseball bat and put them back down.</p>

<p>The man who'd been hit first by Kwak Hanmuk groaned in pain and wheezed out,</p>

<p>"F—... who the hell are you people..."</p>

<p>Oh!</p>

<p>I hurried over to him.</p>

<p>And rescued the spicy chicken cup noodles that were about to be soaked in his blood.</p>

<p>That was close. Valuable supplies almost got damaged.</p>

<p>I turned the cup noodles over in my hands, confirmed they were safe, and asked back,</p>

<p>"Why would you want to know that?"</p>

<p>The man was staring up at me with wide eyes. I tilted my head.</p>

<p>"It's not like knowing who I am would extend your life."</p>

<p>"What? ...Ghk!"</p>

<p>The man tried to shout something with a bewildered face, so I hit him once with the baseball bat.</p>

<p>He stopped making useless noise and quietly closed his eyes, unconscious.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk was still rampaging.</p>

<p>I organized the scattered supplies while I waited. Before long, only the groans of unconscious men filled the mart.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>You have won the battle against the occupiers of Dead Mart!</p></div>

<p>A cleaner victory was impossible.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk checked the victory notification on the System window and clicked his tongue.</p>

<p>"Done?"</p>

<p>He swung the baseball bat at the air, like he was disappointed.</p>

<p>"Captain Kwak is the best."</p>

<p>I shot him two thumbs up.</p>

<p>I'd like to use you often in the future, Captain.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk grinned at my heartfelt flattery — all for the sake of the future. He rested the bat on his shoulder.</p>

<p>"This is F — ing fun. Goyo, let's do this again, yeah?"</p>

<p>As it happened, I had plenty of things planned for him in DeZonDeal.</p>

<p>Hitting zombies hard. Smashing zombies into pieces. Sending zombies flying far away.</p>

<p>I was feeling pleased that I'd given him work suited to his nature when a System window appeared.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>You keep hesitating to register 'Mo Haein.'<br/>
But registration is an essential process for growing as a System.</p></div>

<div class="systembox"><p>You think it might not be a bad idea to take a small risk and register 'Kwak Hanmuk' first.</p></div>

<div class="systembox"><p>Would you like to register 'Kwak Hanmuk'?</p></div>

<p>Since I wouldn't register Mo Haein, the System was now compromising on its own.</p>

<p>Used to ignoring System windows by now, I picked up a backpack that had fallen on the floor.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>You have acquired a backpack. Your portable item capacity has expanded.</p></div>

<p>DeZonDeal had no basic inventory, so you had to farm backpacks.</p>

<p>It was a setting meant to add realism, but having to pack everything manually was a huge pain.</p>

<p>I stuffed the supplies into the backpack one by one. I paused while picking up the spicy chicken cup noodles and glanced at the packaging.</p>

<div class="fieldset"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Spicy chicken flavor, born from the blazing tomb that burns ■■■!</p></div></div>

<p>It looked similar to Korean cup noodles, but the text on the packaging was bizarre.</p>

<p>When I'd checked it as an item description in-game, it only told me roughly what flavor it was.</p>

<p>This was my first time seeing the actual packaging, so I looked at the other food around me more closely.</p>

<div class="fieldset"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Tuna mayo, hauled from the boiling river of ■</p></div></div>
<br/>
<div class="fieldset"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Salmon mayo, hauled from the bubbling river of ■</p></div></div>

<p>The triangle kimbap was just as grim. If this weren't a zombie world, I wouldn't want to touch any of this with a ten-foot pole.</p>

<p>"Goyo, what's up?"</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk had grabbed a bag of chocolate candies. He tore it open and poured the whole thing into his mouth.</p>

<p>"The descriptions are all weird."</p>

<p>"That's just how the Trial is."</p>

<p>Fair enough. Item descriptions were often bizarre.</p>

<p>While I packed supplies, Kwak Hanmuk tossed a few snacks into my backpack and neatly tied up the mart's occupiers, stacking them in a corner.</p>

<p>He cracked his knuckles.</p>

<p>"So are we killing these bastards?"</p>

<p>"No need for you to go to the trouble, Captain. They're useful."</p>

<p>The supplies seemed enough for the three of us to share. I slung the backpack over my shoulder and looked out the window.</p>

<p>"Ughhh..."</p>

<p>Zombies who'd heard the gunshots were dragging their feet toward the mart. At a glance, there were at least a few dozen surrounding us.</p>

<p>"Let's feed the zombies before we go."</p>

<p>I gave the mart's occupiers exactly what they'd done to survivors they'd kidnapped in the Green Zone.</p>

<p>I used them as bait.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk and I slipped safely past the swarm of zombies that gathered. He let out a low whistle at the sight of the zombies feasting.</p>

<p>"Now we should head to the Green Zone and join up with Captain Mo."</p>

<p>"Yeah."</p>

<p>He followed me obediently, then suddenly asked,</p>

<p>"You're twenty-four?"</p>

<p>"Yes."</p>

<p>"Seven years younger than me."</p>

<p>Click.</p>

<p>The sound of a gun being loaded.</p>

<p>I'd been walking ahead. I stopped. Hoping I was wrong, I slowly turned around.</p>

<p>"I know this Trial is basically an infinite repeating illusion. But when it's right in front of you..."</p>

<p>His gun was aimed at me.</p>

<p>"Even an Adaptee who trained like hell at TRA would have a hard time acting without hesitation."</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk made a shooting sound with his mouth. Pew pew.</p>

<p>"But Goyo just feeds people to zombies like it's nothing?"</p>

<p>Now that I thought about it, it was a reasonable suspicion. But it was because this was a Trial. If it weren't...</p>

<p>Well.</p>

<p>I recalled Park Seonggyeon, who'd turned into a golden firework, and corrected my thought.</p>

<p>"I suppose I am a bit cold-hearted."</p>

<p>"A bit?"</p>

<p>"Well, I have to survive too."</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk let out a genuine chuckle.</p>

<p>My answer didn't seem bad, but with Captain Kwak as my opponent, I couldn't be sure if it would pass.</p>

<p>He was the kind of man who could laugh like that and still pull the trigger.</p>

<p>"I just... have clear priorities."</p>

<p>I spoke slowly as I walked toward Kwak Hanmuk.</p>

<p>I stopped right in front of his gun.</p>

<p>"And Captain Kwak is included in my priorities."</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk stared at me. His eyes asked why. I answered.</p>

<p>"We're a team."</p>

<p>We're on the same side now, so stop doubting me. I don't stab my teammates in the back.</p>

<p>...I couldn't say it outright, but there was no way Kwak Hanmuk missed my meaning.</p>

<p>He laughed out loud and tucked the gun back into his baseball jacket.</p>

<p>"Can't mess with Hyung-nim Goyo."</p>

<p>I didn't know why he called me Hyung-nim, but I answered quickly.</p>

<p>"Thank you for trusting me."</p>

<p>If he'd kept the gun aimed at me, things would have gotten complicated in a lot of ways. Lucky. I didn't want to blow Kwak Hanmuk's head off.</p>

<p>I took Kwak Hanmuk to the gun shop.</p>

<p>The gun shop was a place you could only find the hidden location and entry method after completing all sorts of miscellaneous quests in-game.</p>

<p>But I led us there like it was my own home, and Kwak Hanmuk looted the items like it was his.</p>

<p>Seeing him follow without a word gave me a strange feeling.</p>

<p>I hadn't given TRA a proper explanation.</p>

<p>Samra knew that I'd experienced the Trial and the people tied to it through the game.</p>

<p>But TRA hadn't pressed me for anything since forming the Special Mission Team.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk was the same. He had nothing to say about me pulling out strategies and rare item locations.</p>

<p>'As long as I can close the Trial, they don't care what I am.'</p>

<p>The attitude was clear — they intended to use me as a tool.</p>

<p>I knew TRA prioritized practicality, but it really didn't feel like a government agency.</p>

<p>By the time we finished looting the gun shop, the sun was already setting.</p>

<p>I'd added a duffel bag to my load. With the blazing red sunset at my back, I headed toward the Green Zone.</p>

<p>Zombies moved slowly during the day, but at night, their speed increased.</p>

<p>If you didn't want a chase with sprinting zombies, it was best to reach the Green Zone before dark.</p>

<p>DeZonDeal was divided into four zones.</p>

<p>The Green Zone — a safe area where survivors stayed. Only a very small number of zombies appeared here, manageable for survivors.</p>

<p>The Yellow Zone was where players started the game, and where zombies began appearing in earnest. Few zombies during the day, but double at night.</p>

<p>The Red Zone was crawling with zombies day and night.</p>

<p>And finally, the Dead Zone.</p>

<p>The entire Dead Zone was always treated as night, even when the sun was up. And it spawned special zombies called "Dead Zombies."</p>

<p>These zombies were far superior to regular ones in strength and speed.</p>

<p>The delivery destination — the lab — was located in the Dead Zone.</p>

<p>If you played by the book, you couldn't start the delivery until the player had built up enough strength.</p>

<p>Of course, that method had nothing to do with me.</p>

<p>Before entering the Green Zone.</p>

<p>I asked Kwak Hanmuk to do one thing — something he'd really be good at.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk found it highly entertaining and readily agreed.</p>

<p>Once inside the Green Zone, the number of zombies shuffling around dropped noticeably.</p>

<p>The survivors had gathered at an elementary school.</p>

<p>The people standing guard at the barbed-wire gate shot us wary looks.</p>

<p>A couple of people pointing guns at me didn't faze me anymore. I opened my backpack to show them.</p>

<p>Their eyes went wide at the sight of the supplies packed inside.</p>

<p>"I'll give you information on supplies. Please call a woman named Morae. She should have arrived here today."</p>

<p>When food was involved, suspicion and everything else took a backseat. One of them ran inside, out of breath.</p>

<p>Not long after, a man who looked about fifty appeared with several subordinates.</p>

<p>"Where'd you get the supplies?"</p>

<p>Mo Haein wasn't with him.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk and I looked at each other.</p>

<p>There was no way a few scrawny men could stop Mo Haein from coming out.</p>

<p>She must have heard the commotion outside. The fact that she was staying quiet meant only one thing.</p>

<p>She hadn't made it to the Green Zone.</p>

<p>Why?</p>

<p>There was no reason for that at all, which made it even more suspicious.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk narrowed his brow. "Hm." Then he lowered his voice and whispered to me.</p>

<p>"Captain Mo... I think she might have been bitten by a zombie."</p>
