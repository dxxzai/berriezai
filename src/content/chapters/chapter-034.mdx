<h1>Chapter 34</h1>

<p>It took me a while to dredge it up from the memories I'd been forgetting.</p>

<p>It had been too long since I'd last revisited them.</p>

<p>'Maybe I have seen them before...'</p>

<p>To be precise, it wasn't that we'd met — just that there was a chance they might know who I was.</p>

<p>'But that doesn't make sense.'</p>

<p>Then again, it was a long time ago, and I wasn't exactly the kind of person who left a lasting impression.</p>

<p>Someone like Chairman Cheon-gang probably had talented people crawling all over them.</p>

<p>They was so far above my station that they'd have neither the reason nor the leisure to remember someone like me.</p>

<p>"If you wish, Han Goyo-ssi, I can arrange a meeting with Chairman Cheon-gang."</p>

<p>"The Chairman...?"</p>

<p>"Yes."</p>

<p>Samra looked like he didn't see what the problem was.</p>

<p>Apparently, if the chairman of a major conglomerate wanted to meet you, you just... met them.</p>

<p>I wasn't sure if this was the power of the TRA or Samra Mansang himself, but it was surreal.</p>

<p>Either way, I needed Good-Looking. It would be an enormous help if I used it in Clean3.</p>

<p>I'd lost count of how many times I'd wished I'd had Good-Looking back when I played Clean3.</p>

<p>I made up my mind and spoke.</p>

<p>"I'd like to meet them."</p>

<p>"Understood."</p>

<p>Samra stared into space for a moment. He was probably contacting someone.</p>

<p>Light swirled in his holographic pupils.</p>

<p>Watching him, a random question popped into my head.</p>

<p>'How does he go outside?'</p>

<p>He'd probably cover himself up like Je Hyun-oh, but even if he wore goggles, his hair would be a problem. To completely block the holographic light from leaking out, he'd have to wrap his whole head in a cloth or something.</p>

<p>While I was indulging in that useless speculation, Samra finished his contact.</p>

<p>"Lunch in two days, at noon."</p>

<p>"...Can you just schedule something with the Chairman that easily?"</p>

<p>"Why wouldn't I be able to?"</p>

<p>Samra acted as if my reaction didn't make sense.</p>

<p>"Right now, there is no one more important in the Republic of Korea than Han Goyo-ssi."</p>

<p>His casual downgrading of Chairman Cheon-gang made me let out an awkward laugh.</p>

<p>He was probably the only one who thought that way.</p>

<p>After finalizing the appointment with the Chairman, Samra quietly looked at me.</p>

<p>"How is your body?"</p>

<p>"I'm fine."</p>

<p>Samra, who had all my physical data at his fingertips, knew my condition better than I did.</p>

<p>Sure enough, the real topic came next.</p>

<p>"I would like to know why you pushed yourself so hard to clear it."</p>

<p>Since there was no reason to lie to him, I answered honestly.</p>

<p>"To become the Top Contributor. I wanted to prove my worth to the TRA."</p>

<p>"Was my testimony not enough?"</p>

<p>"No, that's not it. Thanks to you, I became a Trainee... I just wanted to be more certain. That was my greed."</p>

<p>Samra stared at me as I hurriedly made excuses, without so much as blinking.</p>

<p>There was silent reproach in his inhuman gaze.</p>

<p>Ever since I entered DeZonDeal, Samra had told me to prioritize myself above all else.</p>
<br/>
<p><span class="flashback">"Han Goyo-ssi, you are a unique existence at this point in time."</span></p>
<br/>
<p>Recalling his words, I put on my most apologetic expression.</p>

<p>"I'll be more careful from now on."</p>

<p>Samra still didn't take his eyes off me, but fortunately, he moved on to another topic.</p>

<p>"There is something I need to confirm regarding the items you acquired."</p>

<p>I waited for his words with an attitude full of willingness to cooperate.</p>

<p>"Items cannot be extracted from you. Is this an inherent ability?"</p>

<p>"...What?"</p>

<p>But the question was completely incomprehensible.</p>

<p>Item extraction?</p>

<p>I just stared at him with wide eyes at the unfamiliar term, and Samra immediately inferred that I didn't know and explained.</p>

<p>"The Trial Response Agency extracts items acquired by affiliated Adaptees when they clear a Trial."</p>

<p>This was news to me.</p>

<p>'Extraction...'</p>

<p>How did that even work?</p>

<p>The word felt distasteful, but I could understand it — if you were a soldier, ownership of items would naturally transfer to the state. I could accept that.</p>

<p>'Still, I wonder if you get rewarded for good items.'</p>

<p>There had to be some kind of compensation, given that people like Je Hyun-oh and the Captains risked their lives entering Trials.</p>

<p>Shaking off some of my discomfort about extraction, a natural question arose.</p>

<p>Because it was strange that I hadn't known this.</p>

<p>I'd learned all kinds of miscellaneous information while playing the Archive's games. I knew several things that could be considered classified. It made no sense that I hadn't known something so basic — something even the regular office workers at the TRA would know.</p>

<p>Even if I assumed the Creator had deliberately restricted the information, the reason was unclear.</p>

<p>'Is he hiding negative aspects from the Player? But why?'</p>

<p>It felt a bit lacking as a motive to keep me playing the game.</p>

<p>"I had no idea about extraction at all. Likewise, I don't know why the items I've acquired haven't been extracted."</p>

<p>Samra's pupils dilated, then contracted. He seemed to have judged my statement as true based on my physical reactions.</p>

<p>"Extraction failed when you cleared &lt;Happy Smile Factory&gt;. Director Ham Jiwol wanted to halt extraction for physical data collection when we attempted re-extraction, and I agreed."</p>

<p>And extraction had also failed after clearing &lt;Dead Zone Delivery&gt; this time.</p>

<p>I hadn't known that.</p>

<p>It must have happened while I was still unconscious.</p>

<p>After finishing his explanation, Samra asked me.</p>

<p>"Will you show me the items you acquired?"</p>

<p>Perfect timing.</p>

<p>This would be a natural opportunity to ask Samra to listen to the music.</p>

<p>I fished the small candy and the cassette tape wrapped in wired headphones out of my inventory and handed them over.</p>

<p>"Happy Smile Candy and a Portable Music Player."</p>

<p>But when Samra received the items, he made a strange expression.</p>

<p>"These are..."</p>

<p>He looked at me as if he couldn't believe it.</p>

<p>"Bound items."</p>

<p>Items obtained as Trial clear rewards were sometimes bound to the Adaptee. Once bound, only that Adaptee could use the item. But it was extremely rare — there were almost no cases worldwide.</p>

<p>Yet both items I'd acquired were bound type, usable only by me.</p>

<p>Two in a row, at that.</p>

<p>Unlike me, who was flustered, Samra seemed filled with interest.</p>

<p>"True Ending clears and binding shouldn't be related... What kind of items are they?"</p>

<p>"Ah, well."</p>

<p>Feeling a bit embarrassed, I read him the descriptions written on the items.</p>

<p>"A candy that makes you feel happy and laugh when you eat it, and a music player containing a cassette tape with 666 exciting songs. Listening to the music is said to make you feel joyful."</p>

<p>I paused mid-sentence, about to argue the usefulness of the candy and music player before he could criticize me for bringing useless things.</p>

<p>Samra's eyes were flashing.</p>

<p>They were glowing so brightly that the small hospital room seemed to brighten.</p>

<p>"Samra, the light in your eyes..."</p>

<p>I said, nearly squinting from the brightness.</p>

<p>Then Samra blinked for the first time since entering the room, and the light dimmed back to normal.</p>

<p>"This is the first time an emotion-affecting item has appeared. There must be a meaning to it."</p>

<p>Samra examined the items carefully for a while, then returned them to me.</p>

<p>Because they were bound anyway, and because I might end up using them inside a Trial.</p>

<p>In exchange for getting them back, I promised to provide Samra with information if I ever used the items.</p>

<p>"And regarding the Creator — I have identified a user who watched his broadcasts and played his games."</p>

<p>"...!"</p>

<p>This time, I was the one surprised by the unexpected news.</p>

<p>If my eyes could glow too, they would definitely have been flashing.</p>

<p>"However, obtaining a statement from this individual is proving difficult, so I am currently exploring various methods."</p>

<p>You could schedule a lunch with Chairman Cheon-gang, but this was hard?</p>

<p>I had no idea who it could be that Samra judged as difficult.</p>

<p>But even if I asked who it was, he probably wouldn't tell me.</p>

<p>'If he could tell me, he would have already.'</p>

<p>I didn't ask.</p>

<p>And when Samra asked if I needed anything else, I said no for now.</p>

<p>I only added one request — that once I got my phone back, he make sure no AI-generated videos showed up in my M-Tube algorithm.</p>

<p>I liked watching animal videos when I was bored, but lately there were too many fakes.</p>

<p>After he left, I was alone in the hospital room, staring at the closed door.</p>

<p>'He didn't ask about my eyes?'</p>

<p>Samra hadn't asked about my pupils, which had turned gold during the Trial.</p>

<p>I couldn't tell if he simply wasn't mentioning it, or if the people who entered with me hadn't reported it.</p>

<p>Either way, I had no reason to poke a hornet's nest, so I stayed quiet.</p>

<p>'Let's just rest.'</p>

<p>Recovery came first.</p>
<hr class="calibre3"/>

<p>Until the day of the lunch appointment with Chairman Cheon-gang, Ham Jiwol visited the hospital room several times.</p>

<p>Fortunately, each time, Samra chased her away using all sorts of creative methods.</p>

<p>The hospital room door would automatically lock. A sudden emergency broadcast calling for the Director would go out. Things like that.</p>

<p>Thanks to him, I was able to focus on recovering without losing so much as a single gram of my fingernails.</p>

<p>On the day of the appointment, Samra came to the hospital room to pick me up.</p>

<p>After changing into the casual clothes I'd been issued, I stared wide-eyed at Samra.</p>

<p>Dressed in a light-colored suit, Samra now had beige hair and eyes.</p>

<p>It was still eye-catching, but compared to his original holographic color, it was practically the look of a model student.</p>

<p>"How did you change it?"</p>

<p>Samra smiled a little at my startled reaction.</p>

<p>"I used an item."</p>

<p>"Wow..."</p>

<p>I desperately wanted to ask what kind of item he'd gotten from which Trial, but I held back.</p>

<p>"Lieutenant Colonel Je Hyun-oh has a similar item."</p>

<p>I grew even more curious, but I couldn't ask for details.</p>

<p>Because there was a TRA person accompanying Samra. It might be confidential, so I kept my mouth shut.</p>

<p>The place we arrived at in a black sedan was incredibly intimidating.</p>

<p>"Um... Are we eating at... Chairman Cheon-gang's residence?"</p>

<p>"Yes."</p>

<p>With Samra beside me, who saw no problem at all, I looked up at the Hannam-dong mansion before us.</p>

<p>In my vague imagination, I'd thought we'd eat in a private room at a Korean restaurant, like in a drama.</p>

<p>I never expected something even more pressure-inducing than that.</p>

<p>I stood blankly in front of the gate for a moment.</p>

<p>Then a large van — the kind celebrities rode in — pulled up smoothly.</p>

<p>As the door opened, an irritable voice was the first thing that hit my ears.</p>

<p>"Grandma, what charity project are you starting now?"</p>

<p>A woman in a suit got out, followed by a strikingly beautiful woman who scrunched her face in annoyance as she stepped out of the car.</p>

<p>It was Kang Byeokrin, the fourth-generation chaebol and Chairman Cheon-gang's granddaughter.</p>

<p>Well-known even among the general public, she was incredibly popular on social media thanks to her outstanding looks.</p>

<p>Though I'd heard her personality wasn't exactly gentle.</p>

<p>"I told you I don't do that 'hanging out with commoners' thing!"</p>

<p>...It seemed that was true.</p>

<p>"If you bring in shabby people and make them work, the results will be shabby too. I'm already in a bad mood lately, and Grandma is going to drive me cra—"</p>

<p>Suddenly, Kang Byeokrin's words cut off. She'd spotted me and Samra standing in front of the gate.</p>

<p>I tried to greet her first, but I couldn't.</p>

<p>For some reason, Kang Byeokrin was staring at me with a deeply shocked expression.</p>

<p>She looked more stunned than when I'd run into Je Hyun-oh in the middle of the night.</p>

<p>Kang Byeokrin stared at me as if in a daze, then suddenly jumped and shouted.</p>

<p>"W-wait, hold on! Clothes! I need to change clothes! I'll be right back. No, come inside. Director Woo, escort the guests!"</p>

<p>Then she dashed into the house.</p>

<p>Left behind, I stood there dumbly, then looked at Samra.</p>

<p>I shot him a look that said, 'This is a disaster, isn't it?' but Samra only gave a meaningful smile and added an unhelpful comment.</p>

<p>"It's going well."</p>
<hr class="calibre3"/>
<p>Kang Byeokrin raced up the stairs.</p>

<p>She usually took the elevator to her third-floor space, but she was too flustered for that and sprinted all the way up.</p>

<p>Panting, she caught her breath in front of the door to the innermost room — a room even Director Woo wasn't allowed to enter freely, let alone the cleaning staff.</p>

<p>Kang Byeokrin grabbed the doorknob with trembling hands.</p>

<p>The door slowly opened, revealing the room inside.</p>

<p>The room was filled with posters, photos, photocard binders, dolls, and all kinds of official and unofficial merchandise.</p>

<p>They were all about one person. The quantity was enough to fill the spacious room, but the variety was surprisingly limited.</p>

<p>Staggering toward the display cases where dozens of identical items were lined up in rows, Kang Byeokrin groaned.</p>

<p>"I'm losing my mind..."</p>

<p>Then, with her face flushed bright red, she muttered.</p>

<p>"Why is Goyo at my house...?"</p>

<p>She was a fan of the pro gamer 'Goyo.'</p>
