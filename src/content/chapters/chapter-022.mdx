<h1>Chapter 22</h1>

<p>Yi Gaon's eyes went wide.</p>

<p>"A cure?"</p>

<p>She'd expected the vaccine request, sure. But asking her to develop a treatment? That caught her completely off guard.</p>

<p>And she wasn't the only one whose eyes went wide.</p>

<div class="hyunoh"><p>O O</p></div>

<p>Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>Yi Gaon flinched and stumbled back at the new emoticon floating above his goggles.</p>

<p>I was a little embarrassed, but I pretended not to notice and kept going.</p>

<p>"Actually, my colleague's zombie transformation is progressing too."</p>

<p>"Ah..."</p>

<p>Yi Gaon's eyes welled with sympathy in an instant.</p>

<p>"So that's why. I thought you seemed a bit strange, but I guess it really is because of the infection."</p>

<p>She said this while turning to look at Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>"But your colleague isn't very aggressive, is he? Is it because of the equipment he's wearing? Or—"</p>

<p>"It's not him. It's a different colleague."</p>

<p>"R-Right."</p>

<p>Yi Gaon fidgeted, sneaking glances at Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<div class="hyunoh"><p>ㅠ ㅜ</p></div>

<p>She trembled when Je Hyun-oh put up a crying emoticon on his goggles, but her reaction was a little better than before.</p>

<p>Anyway, it seemed like I'd managed to build some common ground.</p>

<p>Yi Gaon edged closer to me, putting a bit more distance between herself and Je Hyun-oh, then spoke.</p>

<p>"I want to make a cure, but... I'm not confident. Vaccines and treatments are completely different, and if my oppa were here, maybe, but alone..."</p>

<p>Yi Gaon bit her lip. She was the one who needed a cure more than anyone. If she was saying this, she really didn't have confidence.</p>

<p>It was true — it wasn't something one person could pull off in a short time.</p>

<p>But does she really have to struggle through the research alone?</p>

<p>There were perfectly useful tools lying around. I made a suggestion to Yi Gaon, who'd fallen back into gloom.</p>

<p>"What if we recycled the trash?"</p>

<p>"Trash?"</p>

<p>"Yes."</p>

<p>I clarified for her sake.</p>

<p>"The human trash."</p>
<hr class="calibre3"/>
<p>It was just a matter of going down the emergency stairs from the rooftop to the fifth floor.</p>

<p>But that wasn't easy.</p>

<p>Mo Haein breathed in deep, then let it out. Her breath came out hot from the fever that had started up again.</p>

<p>She'd been on edge ever since, afraid that if she lost focus for even a second, she'd lunge at Kwak Hanmuk and bite him.</p>

<p>She could feel deep in her bones why DeZonDeal's principle was execution upon infection.</p>

<p>Her teeth itched. Mo Haein gasped roughly, her face scrunching and relaxing in cycles.</p>

<p>"Captain Mo. Hanging in there?"</p>

<p>"Yeah."</p>

<p>"Want me to put you down?"</p>

<p>"...I'm taking you with me before I get put down."</p>

<p>At her vow of mutual zombification, Kwak Hanmuk cackled. He was so infuriating that for a moment, even the pain of transformation was forgotten.</p>

<p>Mo Haein glared at him with eyes that said she wanted to take just one bite, then muttered.</p>

<p>"Still, I'm glad you're my teammate. For this operation."</p>

<p>"Goyo's on the team too."</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>Mo Haein said nothing. But in her head, she was already picturing Han Goyo.</p>

<p>Director Jang Seokyun had made Han Goyo's purpose clear.</p>

<p>Right now, Han Goyo was neither a TRA soldier nor a colleague. He was just a tool for closing the Trial.</p>

<p>He was dangerous, so they'd confirm his usefulness over a month, and if he wasn't helpful, he'd be disposed of — discarded for research.</p>

<p>Mo Haein had tried to approach Han Goyo with that same mindset.</p>

<p>But it was harder than she'd expected.</p>

<p>The moment she'd seen Han Goyo locked in the quarantine room came to mind.</p>

<p>It was one of the better rooms in TRA's isolation wing, but it was still a prison. The structure wouldn't even let him open the door from inside.</p>

<p>He'd been waiting for someone to open it from outside, and when Kwak Hanmuk swung the iron door open, he'd bowed politely. The sight had left her speechless.</p>

<p>Dressed neatly in his uniform, slightly tense, Han Goyo's eyes as he looked at Mo Haein had been clear.</p>

<p>There wasn't a trace of resentment for being treated this way.</p>

<p>When she returned his greeting, he'd even smiled faintly.</p>

<p>There was no way she could treat him like a tool or a monster after seeing that.</p>

<p>But in the Trial, he's the craziest bastard she's ever seen.</p>

<p>The polite, respectful demeanor that didn't fit someone his age was gone without a trace.</p>

<p>He couldn't live up to his name and ran wild like a mad dog — a lunatic so brazen he'd shocked even Mo Haein, who'd seen all kinds of people since joining TRA.</p>

<p>She never thought she'd meet another bastard like Kwak Hanmuk in her lifetime.</p>

<p>Thinking about Han Goyo brought all kinds of complicated feelings, but one thing was clear.</p>

<p>He's not a bad kid. Suspicious as hell and weird, but still.</p>

<p>Even now.</p>

<p>When she'd become a zombie, Han Goyo could have easily argued for her execution. But he didn't.</p>

<p>Could he pull off the True Ending this time too?</p>

<p>She was trying not to think about the uncertain future, but she couldn't help hoping.</p>

<p>She didn't want to die yet.</p>

<p>No — she couldn't die yet.</p>

<p>"Hanmuk."</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk stopped in front of the fifth-floor door and looked back.</p>

<p>The steel pipe Han Goyo had picked up from the rooftop was slung over his shoulder. He looked like a total thug.</p>

<p>Mo Haein asked, one step left on the last stair.</p>

<p>"You okay?"</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk looked down at her, his expression asking why she'd even bother.</p>

<p>"Fucking fine."</p>

<p>"No... haah, forget it."</p>

<p>She'd wanted to hear his thoughts on Han Goyo, but it was pointless.</p>

<p>Clearing DeZonDeal with a True Ending was the priority.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk whistled as he pushed open the emergency door.</p>

<p>Mo Haein frowned involuntarily.</p>

<p>Maybe it was the zombie transformation progressing, but her hearing was sharper than usual.</p>

<p>On the other hand, her vision was narrowing. It felt like she was seeing less than half her normal sight.</p>

<p>The sensation of her body changing in real time wasn't pleasant.</p>

<p>If anything, the discomfort seemed to be driving her aggression even higher.</p>

<p>She'd have to bite down on a wad of cloth soon, she thought, following Kwak Hanmuk into the fifth floor.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk cracked his neck.</p>

<p>"Feels like the researchers are here."</p>

<p>Before she could ask how he knew —</p>

<p>"ANY SURVIVORS IN HERE!! COME ON OUT!!!"</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk bellowed so loud the whole corridor rang.</p>

<p>A beat late, Mo Haein clamped her hands over her ears and swallowed a curse.</p>

<p>Of course, no survivors came out. Only the zombies that had been sleeping stirred, growling as they lunged.</p>

<p>Mo Haein suppressed the urge to join the zombies and attack Kwak Hanmuk together, and drew the Black Moon Blade.</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>A sharp pain shot through her palm. The Black Moon Blade was starting to reject its owner.</p>

<p>She could still use it. But by tomorrow, she probably wouldn't be able to hold it.</p>

<p>Feeling the Black Moon Blade's displeasure at her growing zombification, Mo Haein smiled bitterly.</p>

<p>Luckily, the Dead Zombies on the fifth floor weren't that numerous, so they were dealt with quickly.</p>

<p>Just as she finished off the last one.</p>

<p>The quiet corridor was suddenly filled with the sound of a sealed door opening, and two men appeared.</p>

<p>A ding sounded as a system window popped up.</p>

Search for survivors and find the vaccine developer. (3/4)

<p>They were surviving researchers.</p>

<p>Counting the female researcher they'd met earlier, that made three. One more to go.</p>

<p>The researchers who'd just appeared looked to be in their early fifties and mid-forties, respectively.</p>

<p>Doesn't match the age of the vaccine researcher.</p>

<p>She'd have to go down to the third floor, Mo Haein thought, sheathing the Black Moon Blade.</p>

<p>The researchers trembled as they surveyed the zombie-filled corridor, then their faces lit up when they saw Kwak Hanmuk.</p>

<p>"F-Finally!"</p>

<p>They must have thought he was a soldier who'd come to rescue them, because they darted out like startled mice.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk held up a hand to stop them.</p>

<p>"Whoa, whoa, guys. Hold up."</p>

<p>Then he tilted his head back.</p>

<p>"I'll check your ID cards first. Hand 'em over."</p>

<p>The men fumbled for their ID cards.</p>

<div class="fieldset"><p>Tak Jucheol</p></div>

<div class="fieldset"><p>Paeng Sangdo</p></div>

<p>The older one was Tak, the younger one Paeng.</p>

<p>Beaming as if they were saved, the two researchers stood before Kwak Hanmuk, who narrowed his eyes with a "Hmm."</p>

<p>He studied the ID cards carefully, then turned to Mo Haein.</p>

<p>"No vaccine researcher here."</p>

<p>"Did you need to check the names for that? She was supposed to be in her mid-twenties."</p>

<p>"Hey, could be a baby face."</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk's "impeccable logic" made Mo Haein take a deep breath. She felt like her zombie transformation was speeding up from sheer irritation.</p>

<p>"A vaccine! Are you talking about the vaccine?!"</p>

<p>Paeng Sangdo, who'd been gauging the atmosphere, quickly cut in. He seemed to have realized the soldiers weren't here to rescue survivors.</p>

<p>"The team leader here and I were researching the vaccine together. We can develop it, but we're short on supplies..."</p>

<p>He rapidly established his usefulness. Kwak Hanmuk nodded along, then asked.</p>

<p>"What about the other researchers?"</p>

<p>"We're the only survivors."</p>

<p>"There's two more, guys."</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk slammed the steel pipe against the wall in warning.</p>

<p>"Don't make this difficult."</p>

<p>The pipe was so stained with blood you couldn't even see its original color.</p>

<p>Paeng Sangdo and Tak Jucheol looked at each other. Tak Jucheol cleared his throat and stepped forward.</p>

<p>"There were two more, like you said. But those people..."</p>

<p id="manners">"Losing your manners<sup><a href="#mannersfoot">1</a></sup>, are we?"</p>

<p>"...There were two more."</p>

<p>Tak Jucheol corrected himself, face red.</p>

<p>Mo Haein felt a flicker of satisfaction watching Kwak Hanmuk's rotten personality turn on someone else, and waited for what came next.</p>

<p>"But they disagreed with us, so they left the lab."</p>

<p>"We can develop the vaccine with just the two of us! We can even show you our research data."</p>

<p>Paeng Sangdo was struggling to persuade Kwak Hanmuk when —</p>

<p>Tak Jucheol finally noticed Mo Haein standing behind him and screamed.</p>

<p>"AAAGH! A zombie, a zombie!"</p>

<p>At Tak Jucheol's scream, Paeng Sangdo also freaked out. They fell on their asses, causing a commotion, so Mo Haein calmly spoke up.</p>

<p>"I'm a soldier too."</p>

<p>But it was useless. Tak Jucheol pointed and spat.</p>

<p>"You're turning into a zombie! You'll be one in a day or two! We're researching a vaccine, not a cure!!"</p>

<p>After shouting that, the two scrambled back into the lab.</p>

<p>The lab door was a sealed type that required an ID card to open, and since it was designed to block viruses, they couldn't just break it down.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk clicked his tongue and reached a clear conclusion.</p>

<p>"Let's go to the third floor."</p>

<p>But things didn't go as well as hoped.</p>

<p>They entered the third floor, cleared the zombies, and searched — but they couldn't find the last researcher.</p>

<p>"Don't tell me he's dead..."</p>

<p>As Mo Haein spoke, unable to hide her anxiety, Kwak Hanmuk — who'd been opening the last door — clicked his tongue too.</p>

<p>"Wanna head back to the fifth floor? Might be with Goyo and the Lieutenant Colonel."</p>

<p>A fair amount of time had passed, so it seemed better to regroup first.</p>

<p>When they returned to the fifth floor with Kwak Hanmuk —</p>

<p>Mo Haein witnessed an unbelievable sight.</p>

<p>"WAAAAAH!"</p>

<p>"EUUUUGH!"</p>

<p>It was Tak Jucheol and Paeng Sangdo, struggling in Je Hyun-oh's grip, drool streaming from their mouths.</p>
