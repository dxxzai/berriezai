<h1>Chapter 36</h1>

<p>Samra didn't answer.</p>

<p>Not that it would've mattered if he had denied it.</p>

<p>If Kang Cheonseo was saying it out loud in front of Samra, it meant she was sure.</p>

<p>For once, I couldn't keep my expression under control.</p>

<p>'The CEO of Paseong? After me?'</p>

<p>Paseong was the most famous of the private military companies founded by Adaptees.</p>

<p>They had a global reputation — a mission success rate of ninety percent.</p>

<p>All thanks to their CEO.</p>

<p>When a Paseong mercenary failed a clear, the company asked the client whether they wanted to attempt re-entry.</p>

<p>In that case, the client had to pay an additional fee, but if they accepted the re-entry, the CEO of Paseong personally entered the Trial.</p>

<p>He had never once failed a clear.</p>

<p>But unlike Je Hyun-oh, the Paseong CEO was a pure human — not infected with the BT Virus.</p>

<p>Yet his success rate was still astonishing, and no one could question that he possessed extraordinary ability.</p>

<p>The Paseong CEO kept every detail of his personal life a secret.</p>

<p>The only thing known to the public was his surname and title — "CEO Cha" — and even that might not have been real.</p>

<p>He also always wore a white mask that covered his face, an item that, along with voice modulation, caused cognitive degradation in those who perceived him.</p>

<p>So even Paseong's own mercenaries didn't know his face unless they were top-level executives.</p>

<p>'Chairman Cheon-gang is one of the few people who's seen it.'</p>

<p>Meaning you had to be at least on Chairman Cheon-gang's level for the Paseong CEO to show his face.</p>

<p>In the Archive, the Paseong CEO was a divisive character.</p>

<p>Because of his cold personality — cold enough to make people wonder if he was a psychopath or a sociopath.</p>

<p>He used everyone as tools, except for a very small circle of trusted people.</p>

<p>His actions were judged not by good and evil but by usefulness, and his arrogant speech often caused unnecessary conflict with others.</p>

<p>Even so, he was indispensable for dealing with the Trials occurring worldwide.</p>

<p>Those who found him charming and those who found him unpleasant couldn't understand each other and traded insults.</p>

<p>I understood both sides, but in the end, I was on the Paseong CEO's side.</p>

<p>You could call it biased judgment toward my favorite character.</p>

<p>And now he was tracking me.</p>

<p>The fact that my favorite character was paying attention to me felt both a little good and a little creepy.</p>

<p>Because I knew exactly how terrifying he was.</p>

<p>'In that sense, he's kind of like Je Hyun-oh...'</p>

<p>On the other hand, I was also puzzled.</p>

<p>Considering the relationship between the TRA and Paseong, there was no reason for him to track me.</p>

<p>In South Korea, Trials and items were fundamentally all state property.</p>

<p>But the TRA alone couldn't handle all the Trials hatching in Korea.</p>

<p>So the TRA had established cooperative relationships with private companies, and its main partner was Paseong.</p>

<p>In exchange for handling the TRA's requests, Paseong got ownership of some Trials and items.</p>

<p>They also received BT Virus vaccines and treatments.</p>

<p>The TRA and Paseong prioritized exchanging or trading necessary information and items with each other.</p>

<p>They needed each other, so Paseong had absolutely no reason to antagonize the TRA.</p>

<p>Yet now they were tracking me — the person the TRA was currently watching most closely.</p>

<p>They had to know this was a move that could shake a well-maintained relationship.</p>

<p>"Goyo. Strange, isn't it? Why hide something like that? I just don't get it."</p>

<p>Kang Cheonseo looked at me and asked slyly.</p>

<p>"The TRA seems to be treating you badly, Goyo. Want me to take care of it?"</p>

<p>"Chairman Kang. Any further remarks would be an act against the Trial Response Agency."</p>

<p>"Oh my, how scary."</p>

<p>A faint sneer spread across her lips.</p>

<p>Her expression seemed to say: A fly-by-night organization putting on airs.</p>

<p>But she didn't voice any more complaints.</p>

<p>No matter how young the agency was — only four years old — in the current situation, the TRA was one of the few means of stopping the Trials.</p>

<p>She surrendered cleanly to Samra.</p>

<p>"Fine. I'll stop here."</p>

<p>Kang Cheonseo declared in a cheerful voice and turned to me.</p>

<p>"Goyo, you eating okay?"</p>

<p>She handed me a card. A black card — something I'd only ever heard about.</p>

<p>"Buy yourself something good with this. Think of it as allowance from an adult, okay?"</p>

<p>"Chairman Kang."</p>

<p>"The TRA doesn't even appreciate it, so just let it slide. Goyo, because of Samra here, I can't even give the kid pocket money."</p>

<p>Kang Cheonseo, whining about losing face, looked thoroughly playful.</p>

<p>But my blood ran cold.</p>

<p>It was easy to guess that her black card was a trap.</p>

<p>If I took it and actually used it, it would be used to track my location and movements completely.</p>

<p>'Does she want to push me toward Paseong?'</p>

<p>I was curious about her goal.</p>

<p>If this was bait to shake things up between Paseong and the TRA, the situation made some sense — but I couldn't see how that would benefit Cheon-gang.</p>

<p>Especially knowing how much Kang Cheonseo valued Cheon-gang.</p>

<p>In the past, she had gone by the name Kang Seoyoung.</p>

<p>She had trampled countless people on her way to the chairman's seat, and on the day she finally reached the top of Cheon-gang —</p>

<p>Kang Seoyoung changed her name to Kang Cheonseo.</p>

<p>A name change declaring that she was Cheon-gang.</p>

<p>If someone like that was starting to walk a tightrope that might affect Cheon-gang, there had to be a reason.</p>

<p>"I'll bring you the Good-Looking item instead of pocket money. I have to go get it myself, so — hold on a moment?"</p>

<p>"Thank you, Chairman."</p>

<p>I stopped thinking and answered with a quick smile.</p>

<p>Getting the Good-Looking item from Cheon-gang was the most important thing.</p>

<p>Since I wasn't sure why Paseong was looking for me, I didn't want to lose the TRA's trust by showing too much interest in them.</p>

<p>And while I liked the Paseong CEO, honestly — even with my favorite-character filter on — his personality was pretty terrible.</p>

<p>Compared to him, Samra was a gentleman.</p>

<p>'Besides, I need Samra more right now.'</p>

<p>For now, staying with the TRA was my best option.</p>
<hr class="calibre3"/>
<p>Kang Cheonseo saw Samra and Han Goyo out to the front door.</p>

<p>She watched Han Goyo bow his head politely, her expression warm — until the door closed. Then it went cold.</p>

<p>Staring at the closed door, she turned and walked down the long hallway.</p>

<p>Her phone rang. She answered immediately.</p>

<p>"Oh my, look who it is."</p>

<p>As if the coldness from moments ago had been a lie, she launched into banter the moment she picked up.</p>

<p>"CEO Cha! To what do I owe the pleasure of an old woman getting a call from you?"</p>

<p>But the response on the other end was frigid.</p>

<p>—Chairman Kang. I'm hurt.</p>

<p>He threw out the words without any explanation. Kang Cheonseo grinned.</p>

<p>"You have someone watching me? That's terrifying. How are you getting information so fast?"</p>

<p>—I can't help but be hurt that you handed over the item I procured for you to the TRA instead of returning it to Paseong.</p>

<p>"Hmm, couldn't be helped. And I didn't give it to them. I lent it."</p>

<p>—Tell me why.</p>

<p>"A bit hard to do over the phone. How about we see each other's faces for the first time in a while?"</p>

<p>— ......</p>

<p>Irritation radiated from the silence. Kang Cheonseo let out a silent scoff.</p>

<p>'Kids these days, no manners at all.'</p>

<p>But she wasn't angry. Because the situation was turning out quite interesting.</p>

<p>"You'll be satisfied when you hear what I have to say. I guarantee quality. Our CEO Cha is a busy man, so I'll match your schedule. Anytime."</p>

<p>—It's difficult to visit in person. If you could just tell me now—</p>

<p>"I met Han Goyo."</p>

<p>Kang Cheonseo smiled broadly — a smile he couldn't see — and said,</p>

<p>"So how about we talk in person?"</p>

<p>Then she hung up. Because her granddaughter came running over like a foal.</p>

<p>Kang Byeokrin, who had been locked up on the third floor and only just gotten permission to come downstairs, looked like the sky was falling.</p>

<p>"He's... gone...?"</p>

<p>Kang Cheonseo shrugged.</p>

<p>"Left a while ago."</p>

<p>"Grandma!"</p>

<p>Kang Byeokrin cried out, full of grievance — how could she do this when she knew Byeokrin was Goyo's fan?</p>

<p>Of course she knew. The only reason Cheon-gang sponsored the gaming team was because of Kang Byeokrin.</p>

<p>"That's why I didn't let you meet him, Rin."</p>

<p>"What?"</p>

<p>"Do you think it's appropriate for someone who's going to be the heir of Cheon-gang to act like such a fool? I went through the trouble of inviting Han Goyo just so you could see him, and the moment you met him, you just fell apart."</p>

<p>"...I'm sorry."</p>

<p>"Good, as long as you know you messed up. Always remember — you are Cheon-gang. Don't do anything that makes you look ridiculous, Rin."</p>

<p>"Yes, I'll keep that in mind."</p>

<p>Finishing the brief scolding, Kang Cheonseo shook her head.</p>

<p>"Honestly, you... Han Goyo's only twenty-four, but he's surprisingly mature."</p>

<p>"Right? Goyo's got depth. He's really adult for his age."</p>

<p>"Oh dear."</p>

<p>Kang Cheonseo shot her granddaughter a tired look as Byeokrin beamed at the comparison.</p>

<p>Still, she couldn't help but smile.</p>

<p>Her granddaughter had never disappointed her.</p>

<p>And she allowed her granddaughter small indulgences — one of which was Han Goyo.</p>

<p>Back when Kang Byeokrin had been going crazy over him, she'd only received reports through her secretaries.</p>

<p>Today was the first time she'd actually met him, and she could see why he'd captured her granddaughter's heart.</p>

<p>His personality was bold and audacious without being rude, and he seemed to have a decent head on his shoulders.</p>

<p>Good-looking too. If it had been possible, she would have kept him close as one of her own.</p>

<p>'But Paseong makes that difficult.'</p>

<p>Even the all-knowing Samra probably hadn't expected the relationships to get tangled like this.</p>

<p>She smiled at her chattering granddaughter while simultaneously planning how to get the maximum benefit from the situation between the TRA and Paseong.</p>

<p>CRASH!</p>

<p>A sound that could never come from the Cheon-gang chairman's mansion.</p>

<p>Kang Cheonseo's eyes flew open at the tremendous noise of destruction.</p>

<p>The person who had sent the front door flying off its hinges stepped inside.</p>

<p>This time, Kang Cheonseo couldn't smile.</p>

<p>"CEO Cha..."</p>

<p>A voice modulated into mechanical tones came from the man in the matte white mask.</p>

<p>"I came to hear the story, Chairman Kang."</p>
<hr class="calibre3"/>
<p>After returning to the TRA with the Good-Looking item, I headed to the Special Mission Team office in the afternoon.</p>

<p>For the next mission briefing.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh wouldn't be attending the meeting — he was set to join directly at the mission site.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein, whom I hadn't seen in a few days, were sitting in the office. They stood up so violently their chairs nearly tipped over.</p>

<p>"Trainee!"</p>

<p>Mo Haein ran over first and checked me over. After confirming I was fine, she bit her lower lip, then released it.</p>

<p>"They wouldn't even let me visit your hospital room."</p>

<p>"I'm okay."</p>

<p>I meant it as a reassurance — that she didn't need to worry — but Mo Haein snapped back immediately.</p>

<p>"Yeah, you're okay. You're so okay you crawled out again pretending to go on a mission instead of lying in a hospital bed."</p>

<p>As I bowed my head apologizing — "Sorry, sorry" — Kwak Hanmuk chimed in from the side.</p>

<p>"Thought you'd gone from roasted chestnut to shelled chestnut there."</p>

<p>He crossed his arms and looked down at me, having created a new chestnut series.</p>

<p>"Next time, let's agree before you pull some crazy shit, yeah? Got it, Goyo?"</p>

<p>"Yes, sir."</p>

<p>I answered quickly to his warning tone, and Mo Haein let out a heavy sigh beside me.</p>

<p>Just as I was getting roasted to a crisp by the Captains, Samra rescued me.</p>

<p>"We will begin the Special Mission Team meeting."</p>

<p>Thanks to Samra, I finally managed to sit down and start the meeting.</p>

<p>Samra began the briefing.</p>

<p>"The second Trial the Special Mission Team will enter is &lt;Clean! Clean! Clean!&gt;. The Trial grade is four stars. Lieutenant Colonel Je Hyun-oh will be entering for the first time. Captain Mo Haein and Captain Kwak Hanmuk have cleared this Trial multiple times."</p>

<p>Clean3 was a game where you were hired as a short-term cleaner for a massive mansion and had to clean it for a week.</p>

<p>You had to clean the mansion to a level that satisfied the butler NPCs — but despite the straightforward clear condition, the gameplay was trickier than it sounded.</p>

<p>Because it wasn't just about cleaning the mansion. You also had to eliminate the monsters called "Creatures" hiding inside.</p>

<p>And there was one decisive factor that made things even harder for players.</p>

<p>The roulette.</p>

<p>The player's outfit, the tools used for cleaning, even the weapons for killing monsters — everything was decided by spinning a roulette.</p>

<p>If you got unlucky on that roulette spin, you could end up starting with nothing but a single lollipop while practically naked. That was Clean3.</p>

<p>And here was the part that made you want to tear your hair out even more: the two butler NPCs in the mansion scrutinized whether the cleaners suited the mansion.</p>

<p>If you were dirty and unsanitary, or wearing clothes that didn't match the mansion's atmosphere — immediate eviction.</p>

<p>If you spun the roulette and got a shabby cleaner's uniform?</p>

<p>You couldn't even set foot inside the mansion.</p>

<p>"Clean3? Clean3? Clean3 of all things?"</p>

<p>The moment they heard the second mission was Clean3, Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein's faces turned grim.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk, muttering "Clean3, seriously" over and over, looked at me with hollow eyes.</p>

<p>"I had to clean wearing a maid outfit before..."</p>

<p>He recounted his experience of spectacularly failing the roulette, but I couldn't exactly sympathize.</p>

<p>Because I'd never failed to pick what I wanted.</p>
