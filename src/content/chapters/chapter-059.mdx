<h1>Chapter 59</h1>

<p>I had made the proposal, but I had no idea whether Dominic would accept it.</p>

<p>I might have to repeat death and resurrection again.</p>

<p>This time… I probably wouldn’t be able to endure it.</p>

<p>Just remembering the pain from that moment made my body tremble like a Pavlovian response.</p>

<p>Thinking that I would stay together with the very being who had given me such an experience…</p>

<p>Captain Mo would definitely curse me out if he found out.</p>

<p>This time, Captain Kwak might join him.</p>

<p>I wanted to laugh, but I didn’t have the strength.</p>

<p>I just weakly chuckled to myself internally as I waited for Dominic’s choice.</p>

<p>“Roasted Chestnut.”</p>

<p>His gaze was strange.</p>

<p>It was difficult to interpret it as either acceptance or rejection.</p>

<p>If I had to describe it, curiosity was probably the closest word.</p>

<p>“You continue to surprise <em>me</em>.”</p>

<p>That was the vague evaluation he gave.</p>

<p>His words were impossible to understand, making me anxious.</p>

<p>‘Is he accepting or not?’</p>

<p>If he was going to pass a death sentence on me, I wished he would just say it quickly.</p>

<p>At least then I could prepare myself mentally.</p>

<p>‘Is he saying he’ll make the contract?’</p>

<p>But Dominic didn’t come to a conclusion.</p>

<p>He only looked down at me silently.</p>

<p>‘Or not?’</p>

<p>After carefully reading his expression for a long time, Dominic finally gave me his answer.</p>

<p>“Very well. I will make the contract.”</p>

<p>I looked up at him with disbelief.</p>

<p>I had suggested it because I judged there was at least some possibility he would accept, but now that it actually happened, I was dazed.</p>

<p>‘It actually worked.’</p>

<p>If he refused the contract, I had planned to try solving it another way.</p>

<p>But this was, essentially, the best possible result.</p>

<p>After returning alive from the edge of a cliff called death, I felt like I had gained strength I didn’t have before.</p>

<p>Just moments ago, I truly couldn’t even move a finger.</p>

<p>Now, moving my tongue was something I could do without much trouble.</p>

<p>“Thank you. Then, um…”</p>

<p>I realized a major problem.</p>

<p>I didn’t know how to make a contract.</p>

<p>In the game, I simply performed the ritual, and once the Outer God was summoned, the other side handled the contract.</p>

<p>Since I was trying to do it myself, I had no idea what I was supposed to do.</p>

<p>‘Do I need to draw something like a magic circle?’</p>

<p>There were those kinds of images commonly seen in media.</p>

<p>Maybe I had to chant some kind of spell.</p>

<p>Or spill some blood.</p>

<p>If nothing else worked, I even wondered if I should make a written contract, in a very Earth-like way.</p>

<p>That was when Dominic suddenly asked:</p>

<p>“What name did you use for the ritual when you summoned me?”</p>

<p>Why was he asking that?</p>

<p>Still, I answered quickly.</p>

<p>“Roasted Chestnut… I think.”</p>

<p>Since it was the ritual I performed in the game, it should have been Roasted Chestnut.</p>

<p>“So that was why.”</p>

<p>After hearing my answer, Dominic praised the name Roasted Chestnut.</p>

<p>“It does not perfectly represent your essence, but I believe it is not a bad name.”</p>

<p>When I first met him in CallOVo, Dominic had said that the name 'Little Lamb' did not suit me and called me by my real name.</p>

<p>That was why I hurriedly came up with the name Roasted Chestnut.</p>

<p>Dominic didn’t particularly like it, but from then on, he had continued calling me Roasted Chestnut.</p>

<p>It seemed he had accepted the name because it was somewhat familiar.</p>

<p>After giving me unexpected praise, Dominic politely informed me:</p>

<p>“Please excuse me for a moment, Roasted Chestnut.”</p>

<p>I widened my eyes.</p>

<p>Then slowly lowered my head.</p>

<p>Uh…</p>

<p>Even while watching it happen, I couldn’t make myself believe it.</p>

<p>Dominic’s hand was inside my body.</p>

<p>Yet I felt no pain.</p>

<p>No blood spilled out.</p>

<p>There was simply no sensation.</p>

<p>The hand that had entered through my chest grasped something.</p>

<p>Thump. Thump. Thump.</p>

<p>My heart beat violently.</p>

<p>In a calm voice, like someone signing a business contract, he spoke.</p>

<p>“I remember a little now. It seems this is my first time making a contract with ■■ ■. Since Roasted Chestnut’s rank is still low, I suppose I have no choice but to use this traditional method…”</p>

<p>That was all I heard.</p>

<p>I immediately lost consciousness.</p>
<hr class="calibre3"/>

<p>“…!”</p>

<p>The moment my consciousness returned, I jolted awake and sat up.</p>

<p>I pressed my hand against my chest.</p>

<p>…Fortunately, I could feel my heartbeat.</p>

<p>It didn’t seem like my organs had been taken.</p>

<p>Aside from that, I felt no pain, bleeding, injuries, or anything else.</p>

<p>In fact, my condition was better than before I lost consciousness.</p>

<p>Vitality flowed through my body.</p>

<p>‘He could have at least told me beforehand.’</p>

<p>Cutting open someone’s body without warning was not something a sane person did.</p>

<p>The unsettling sensation still seemed to remain, and I rubbed my arm, which was covered in goosebumps.</p>

<p>After calming myself down a little, I belatedly looked around.</p>

<p>Dominic was nowhere to be seen.</p>

<p>The underground chamber was empty.</p>

<p>The space where all the followers had disappeared was silent.</p>

<p>However, there was still one person left.</p>

<p>“Cult Leader…”</p>

<p>It was Edel.</p>

<p>Only then did I realize that I had been on top of the altar this entire time.</p>

<p>It seemed Edel had stayed by my side the whole time.</p>

<p>She appeared flustered that everyone except her had disappeared, but she was trying her best to remain calm.</p>

<p>Perhaps she had come to understand the current situation through her own logic, because she cautiously asked me:</p>

<p>“Did… God save us?”</p>

<p>“That’s right.”</p>

<p>I didn’t have the energy to come up with an excuse, and it wasn’t entirely wrong, so I simply answered yes.</p>

<p>After hearing my response, Edel bit her lip with a complicated expression.</p>

<p>Even though this should have been the moment she had always wished for, there was no sign of happiness on her face.</p>

<p>I felt sorry.</p>

<p>I had wanted to show Edel the scene she had always dreamed of, but I couldn’t.</p>

<p>“Edel will… be free now.”</p>

<p>After hearing my words, Edel’s expression darkened further.</p>

<p>I wondered if I had said something wrong, but then Edel suddenly spoke.</p>

<p>“It was strange from the very first time we met.”</p>

<p>“What was strange?”</p>

<p>“Everything. Even now, I have a strange thought.”</p>

<p>Edel approached me, who was sitting on the altar.</p>

<p>“Could it be… that you are my ■?”</p>

<p>The most important word was mixed with static noise and could not be heard.</p>

<p>The exact same thing happened when Dominic spoke about the contract earlier.</p>

<p>It seemed to be a word I was currently unable to hear.</p>

<p>Because I hadn’t heard the question properly, it was difficult to answer.</p>

<p>Edel did not ask again despite my silence.</p>

<p>It seemed she never expected to receive an answer in the first place.</p>

<p>Instead, she asked me something else.</p>

<p>“Can we meet again?”</p>

<p>I hesitated for a moment, but soon smiled faintly.</p>

<p>“Of course.”</p>

<p>Edel was happy with my lie.</p>

<p>‘It was good that I went for the true ending.’</p>

<p>If I had left only her behind in this place, she would have continued coming to mind.</p>

<p>Fortunately, I had kept my promise to Edel and given her the freedom to escape being bound to this trial.</p>

<p>“Edel. Earlier, for saving me…”</p>

<p>I was about to thank her, but my words trailed off.</p>

<p>At some point, Edel had already disappeared.</p>
