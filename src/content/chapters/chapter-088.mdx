<h1>Chapter 88</h1>

<p>Seo Moonchan and Mok Saehwa were fanatics devoted to CEO Cha.</p>

<p>To earn the trust of a man who trusted no one, they had voluntarily written contracts staking their own lives and presented them to him.</p>

<p>Contracts created with Trial items could bind every aspect of an Adaptee, depending on how their clauses were written.</p>

<p>The fact that they had drafted such contracts with their own hands and offered them to him was exactly why CEO Cha trusted only those two directors without reservation.</p>

<p>Since they were the people supporting my favorite character in every possible way, I liked Seo Moonchan and Mok Saehwa too.</p>

<p>...Just not like this.</p>

<p>It didn't look like Lieutenant Won Taebeom could handle both directors from Paseong while carrying me in one arm.</p>

<p>The directors seemed to know it too. They already looked like they'd won before the fight had even started.</p>

<p>Just then, a system window appeared.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>'Seo Moonchan' and 'Mok Saehwa' are characters that can be registered. Would you like to register them?</p></div>

<p>The sudden prompt made me blink.</p>

<p>...Wait. You stayed quiet when CEO Cha was standing right in front of me.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>'Cha■■' cannot be registered yet.</p></div>

<p>I had secretly hoped the system would reveal CEO Cha's real name, but it was still censored.</p>

<p>Why couldn't he be registered? Why was even his name hidden?</p>

<p>Perhaps sensing my curiosity, the system offered an unusually helpful explanation.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>You think that if you completely digest ■■■■, you may be able to register 'Cha■■'.</p></div>

<p>The problem was that I couldn't read the censored part.</p>

<p>Digest what?</p>

<p>It almost felt like the system knew I wouldn't understand anyway, so it didn't bother explaining properly.</p>

<p>Considering its track record, I couldn't help becoming suspicious.</p>

<p>What about Lieutenant Won Taebeom?</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>You judge that it is still a little too early to fill a slot with 'Won Taebeom.'</p></div>

<p>He is one who follows his convictions.</p>

<p>What was that supposed to mean?</p>

<p>That he wouldn't listen to me?</p>

<p>The system gave no further explanation.</p>

<p>Instead—</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Current available registration slots: 6</p></div>

<p>I already had Je Hyun-oh, Mo Haein, and Kwak Hanmuk occupying three slots, meaning I now had a total of nine.</p>

<p>It had originally been only three.</p>

<p>I'd gained quite a lot.</p>

<p>Still, I had absolutely no intention of registering anyone.</p>

<p>The whole "real-name registration" incident had happened only recently.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>You believe that, as a Young God seeking to improve your abilities, a little embarrassment is acceptable.</p></div>

<p>I absolutely do not.</p>

<p>And it wasn't "a little" embarrassment, either.</p>

<p>The system really was like a divine being.</p>

<p>It had no understanding whatsoever of ordinary human circumstances.</p>

<p>While I was silently arguing with the system, Mok Saehwa smiled sweetly and said something that completely clashed with her chubby cheeks.</p>

<p>"Lieutenant Won. This is our first time fighting each other, isn't it? Don't worry. We'll keep it to about four weeks in the hospital."</p>

<p>Won Taebeom didn't answer.</p>

<p>Meanwhile, I desperately searched for some solution.</p>

<p>If a member of Field Team 2 ended up hospitalized for a month because of me, I'd never be able to face Captain Mo again.</p>

<p>Isn't there some way around this...?</p>

<p>Talking things out would obviously be the best option.</p>

<p>Maybe I should at least ask to see this contract they kept talking about.</p>

<p>Not because I intended to join Paseong.</p>

<p>I was just... curious.</p>

<p>Seo Moonchan turned to look at me.</p>

<p>His eyes sparkled as if he'd discovered some legendary creature.</p>

<p>Despite that, his greeting was impeccably polite.</p>

<p>"Goyo-nim. I'm Seo Moonchan of Paseong."</p>

<p>I nodded lightly.</p>

<p>"I'm Han Goyo."</p>

<p>"We're not trying to bully you, Goyo-nim. We'd simply appreciate it if you'd sign this contract. Think you could do that?"</p>

<p>Perfect.</p>

<p>I immediately took him up on the offer.</p>

<p>"Please show me the contract."</p>

<p>Seo Moonchan snapped his fingers with a smooth smile.</p>

<p>A document appeared in front of me and fluttered downward.</p>

<p>I instinctively caught it.</p>

<p>Please don't tell me this is the life-pledge contract they gave CEO Cha...</p>

<p>The instant I read it, my eyes widened.</p>

<p>Thankfully, it wasn't.</p>

<p>Though honestly...</p>

<p>...the contents were somehow even worse.</p>

<p>Every single clause was complete hell.</p>

<div class="fieldset">
<div class="systembox_wrap">
<p>
Article 1. Purpose <br/>

The purpose of this contract is to permanently transfer all rights regarding Party B's existence, soul, body, and posthumous state to Party A, thereby establishing Party A's absolute dominion.
</p>

<p>
Article 2. Ownership of the Body<br/>

Upon execution of this contract, every cell, organ, drop of blood, and bone composing Party B's body shall become the private property of Party A.<br/>

Without Party A's permission, Party B may not cut, damage, or otherwise alter even a single strand of hair or clipping of fingernail.
</p>

<p>
Article 3. Ownership of the Mind<br/>

Party B shall think only of Party A twenty-four hours a day.<br/>

Party B may harbor no reason or negative emotion that could threaten the well-being of either Party A or Party B.
</p>

<p>
Article 4. Contract Period and Termination<br/>

This contract shall remain in effect permanently.<br/>

The death of Party B shall not constitute grounds for termination.<br/>

After death, all rights concerning Party B's corpse and soul shall also belong exclusively to Party A.<br/>

This contract may be terminated solely through Party A's unilateral declaration.<br/>

Party B possesses absolutely no right to request termination.
</p>

<p>
Article 5. Special Provisions<br/>

If Party B considers any clause of this contract unreasonable, that very mental state shall itself constitute a breach of contract and be subject to immediate punishment.
</p>
</div>
</div>

<p>Naturally, Party A was CEO Cha.</p>

<p>Party B was me.</p>

<p>Reading beside me, Dominic sounded genuinely impressed.</p>

<p>"...This is rather remarkable. Are contracts like this common on your planet, Roasted Chestnut?"</p>

<p>Absolutely not, Dominic-ssi.</p>

<p>CEO Cha is just completely insane.</p>

<p>The contract had finally pushed me into mentally cursing my favorite character.</p>

<p>I honestly couldn't imagine what kind of mindset it took to write something like this.</p>

<p>The reason he'd seemed pleased when I kept staring at him earlier...</p>

<p>...must have been because he'd been looking forward to making me sign this slave contract.</p>

<p>From behind me, Won Taebeom—who had also glanced at it—offered a brief comment.</p>

<p>"Trial Response Agency is better."</p>

<p>For once, I wholeheartedly agreed.</p>

<p>At least Director Jang Seokyun's trainee contract had looked like a normal legal document.</p>

<p>Holding the slave contract, I looked back at Paseong's two directors.</p>

<p>Mok Saehwa gazed at me with sparkling anticipation.</p>

<p>"CEO Cha wrote it himself. Isn't it a wonderful contract?"</p>

<p>"...You're serious?"</p>

<p>Seo Moonchan burst into laughter at my question.</p>

<p>"I completely understand why you'd be shocked. But once you join Paseong, you'll realize how joyful it is to belong to CEO Cha."</p>

<p>"We'll treat you incomparably better than the Trial Response Agency."</p>

<p>"We're planning to make you a director right away..."</p>

<p>He went on listing dazzling benefits.</p>

<p>High salary.</p>

<p>Excellent welfare.</p>

<p>All kinds of privileges.</p>

<p>After listening to everything, I reached only one conclusion.</p>

<p>...So I'd basically become CEO Cha's pet hamster.</p>

<p>I'd spend my days running on a wheel inside the cage he'd built for me, munching sunflower seeds...</p>

<p>...and occasionally be let out to clear a Trial.</p>

<p>Unfortunately, I needed the freedom to close seven Trials within two months.</p>

<p>There was absolutely no way I was signing CEO Cha's contract.</p>

<p>The problem was...</p>

<p>The directors clearly intended to make me sign by force.</p>

<p>I lowered my voice.</p>

<p>"Lieutenant Won."</p>

<p>His calm eyes looked down at me beneath thick brows.</p>

<p>"Go ahead."</p>

<p>"I think... if we can hold out for about eleven more minutes, we'll be fine."</p>

<p>There had to be a reason Samra had specified exactly 15 minutes and 37 seconds for the inverse-phase field.</p>

<p>Most likely, that was how long it would take Colonel Je Hyun-oh to arrive.</p>

<p>More than four minutes had already passed.</p>

<p>That meant about eleven minutes remained.</p>

<p>Won Taebeom understood immediately.</p>

<p>"If we're lucky... it's possible."</p>

<p>Which was basically another way of saying it wasn't.</p>

<p>He only had one gauntlet equipped because he was carrying me.</p>

<p>His offense and defense were both restricted.</p>

<p>Surviving eleven minutes like this would be extremely difficult.</p>

<p>Still...</p>

<p>I thought it was worth gambling.</p>

<p>After a brief hesitation, I spoke.</p>

<p>"Lieutenant Won... if you don't mind... could you lend me the other gauntlet? I'll support you."</p>

<p>Won Taebeom stared at me.</p>

<p>That look somehow reminded me of Mo Haein.</p>

<p>I hurriedly explained.</p>

<p>"Based on what's written in the contract, my body appears to be extremely valuable to CEO Cha."</p>

<p>"The directors can't kill me... and they probably can't seriously injure me, either."</p>

<p>In other words...</p>

<p>I could act as Won Taebeom's shield.</p>

<p>And maybe, if possible, help attack too.</p>

<p>"And I'll brief you on the directors' weapons."</p>

<p>That clearly caught everyone off guard.</p>

<p>Won Taebeom's eyebrows twitched.</p>

<p>Even the two directors watching us looked intrigued.</p>

<p>Paseong's directors rarely took missions themselves.</p>

<p>Everyone knew they were powerful.</p>

<p>But almost no one knew what weapons they used.</p>

<p>Because anyone who encountered them...</p>

<p>...died.</p>

<p>Only team leaders and above possessed that information.</p>

<p>Naturally, Won Taebeom had no idea how they fought.</p>

<p>Yet I was offering to explain it.</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>He studied me for a long moment.</p>

<p>Having nearly joined the Special Mission Team before, he already knew I wasn't ordinary.</p>

<p>Finally, he handed me the remaining gauntlet.</p>

<p>I slipped it onto my right hand.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Paladin's Oath (Right Hand):
Where the silver hand reaches, despair shall not exist.
Where the silver hand remains, evil shall find no foothold.</p></div></div>

<p>A perfect description for Lieutenant Won.</p>

<p>Flexing my fingers, I began explaining.</p>

<p>"Director Seo's weapon, Mischievous Predator, is a living sword."</p>

<p>The weapon itself was practically a monster.</p>

<p>Its attacks were irregular because the blade could split open like a mouth and bite its target with rows of teeth.</p>

<p>It was easiest to imagine it as a monster whose mouth happened to be shaped like a sword.</p>

<p>"And only Director Seo can neutralize the poison stored in those teeth."</p>

<p>Then I continued.</p>

<p>"Director Mok's weapon, Blooming Funeral, causes flowers to bloom wherever its bullets strike, draining the target's life force."</p>

<p>The more life it absorbed, the stronger Mok Saehwa became.</p>

<p>When all the bullets were spent, the flower serving as its magazine would wither.</p>

<p>Plucking the dead flower caused a fresh bud to bloom—</p>

<p>reloading the weapon.</p>

<p>"The worst-case scenario is being poisoned by Director Seo while Director Mok drains your life force."</p>

<p>"The life-drain accelerates the poison by increasing blood circulation."</p>

<p>"We absolutely have to stop at least one of those."</p>

<p>Looking up at Won Taebeom, I said,</p>

<p>"Use me as your shield, Lieutenant."</p>

<p>His expression remained unreadable.</p>

<p>He was a frontline tank by nature.</p>

<p>Someone who always stood at the very front.</p>

<p>Hearing me volunteer to become his shield...</p>

<p>...must have felt strange.</p>

<p>Then applause echoed through the street.</p>

<p>"Wow..."</p>

<p>"Goyo-nim."</p>

<p>"I'd heard rumors, but it's really true."</p>

<p>"You seriously know everything."</p>

<p>Mok Saehwa clapped enthusiastically, her eyes shining.</p>

<p>"I really want to bring you to Paseong."</p>

<p>Then she smiled brightly, little eye-smiles forming beneath her eyes.</p>

<p>"But, Goyo-nim."</p>

<p>"Our medical treatment is amazing."</p>

<p>"We're really good at reattaching severed limbs."</p>

<p>That sounded disturbingly similar to Ham Jiwol.</p>

<p>She raised her rifle.</p>

<p>And fired directly at me.</p>

<p>Bang!</p>

<p>Instead of smoke, flower petals scattered from the muzzle.</p>

<p>Won Taebeom dodged while carrying me—</p>

<p>but Seo Moonchan was already there.</p>

<p>His toothed sword slashed downward.</p>

<p>Clang! Clang!</p>

<p>Steel rang as gauntlet and blade collided again and again.</p>

<p>Won Taebeom tried to push the sword back with his gauntlet.</p>

<p>Seo Moonchan only grinned, gripping the sword tighter.</p>

<p>Fighting one-handed while carrying me put Won Taebeom at a clear disadvantage.</p>

<p>In the instant he evaded Mok Saehwa's bullet—</p>

<p>the toothed sword slipped past his guard.</p>

<p>It lunged straight toward me.</p>

<p>The blade split wide like a gaping mouth, exposing rows of razor-sharp teeth.</p>

<p>Its target...</p>

<p>...was my forearm.</p>

<p>I clenched my teeth and raised my gauntlet to block.</p>

<p>Then—</p>

<p>"...!"</p>

<p>Seo Moonchan.</p>

<p>Won Taebeom.</p>

<p>And I.</p>

<p>All three of us froze in shock.</p>

<p>Just before its teeth could bite into my flesh...</p>

<p>...the sword quietly closed its mouth instead.</p>
