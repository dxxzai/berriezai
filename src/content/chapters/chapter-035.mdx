<h1>Chapter 35</h1>

<p>Han Goyo. Pro gamer handle: Goyo.</p>

<p>It was pure coincidence that Kang Byeokrin found out about Goyo.</p>

<p>She'd never had any interest in gaming or pro gamers to begin with.</p>

<p>But one day, Goyo's match videos started showing up on her M-Tube algorithm.</p>

<p>She ignored them, of course. She hit "not interested" over and over — but the videos kept appearing in her feed like some kind of glitch.</p>

<p>She was in the middle of marking one as uninterested yet again when, without meaning to, she found herself staring at the thumbnail.</p>

<p>After seeing it so many times, his face had become familiar.</p>
<br/>
<p><span class="flashback">'He is good-looking, though.'</span></p>
<br/>
<p>Why was he playing games instead of becoming an idol or an actor? She couldn't understand it. If he'd been a celebrity or a model, she'd have considered him for a subsidiary ambassador.</p>
<br/>
<p><span class="flashback">'Isn't he short?'</span></p>
<br/>
<p>If he was this good-looking and hadn't become a celebrity, that had to be the only reason.</p>

<p>Kang Byeokrin played the video for the first time.</p>
<br/>
<p><span class="flashback">'What the — why won't it show me his face?'</span></p>
<br/>
<p>Multiple people were moving around these tiny character things playing a game, but the video only showed the game screen — no people.</p>

<p>Her competitive streak kicked in. She started skipping through the video in ten-second intervals, searching for the part where the person she wanted to see would actually appear.</p>

<p>As she skipped, a voice that sounded like a commentator kept calling out the same name.</p>
<br/>
<p><em><span class="flashback">"Goyo! Goyo! GOYOOOO! Unbelievable! Player Goyo has no intention of losing! He simply cannot be defeated!"</span></em></p>

<p><em><span class="flashback">"Ah, it's always the underage players that are the scariest, isn't it? Their reflexes are the sharpest. And — right as I say that — Player Goyo, wow, WHOOOOA! Goyo takes down his opponent once again! Absolutely dominant!"</span></em></p>
<br/>
<p>Other names came up too, but "Goyo" was the overwhelming majority.</p>

<p>So this Goyo guy wasn't just good-looking — he was actually skilled.</p>

<p>She patiently skipped through the thirty-minute-long video in ten-second bursts, but the Goyo she wanted to see never properly appeared.</p>

<p>His face showed up occasionally during the match, but only in a tiny, low-quality box in the corner of the screen.</p>

<p>And even that was frustratingly brief.</p>

<p>Kang Byeokrin kept hammering the skip button. She finally got a proper look at Goyo after the match ended.</p>

<p>His face was expressionless — no, cold. The corners of his eyes tilted up, giving him a rather sharp look.</p>

<p>But the moment victory was confirmed —</p>

<p>The corners of Goyo's mouth curled up as he took off his headset. His face, which had been utterly indifferent the entire time, broke into a wide smile.</p>

<p>Kang Byeokrin stared blankly at the screen.</p>

<p>For a moment, she thought — he kind of sparkles.</p>

<p>And by the time she came to her senses, she was searching up every single video of his.</p>
<br/>
<p><span class="flashback">'He's 181cm? That's pretty tall. He hasn't been a pro for long, and he turns adult next year... He's younger than me.'</span></p>

<p><span class="flashback">'Mid? Mid-laner...? I have no idea what that means, but Goyo seems to be the best at it. He wins every match, doesn't he? His name gets called the most too.'</span></p>
<br/>
<p>She stayed up the entire night, binge-watching every Goyo video she could scrape together.</p>

<p>The next morning, Kang Byeokrin admitted it, her eyes bloodshot like a rabbit's.</p>

<p>She had developed a tremendous interest in this young pro gamer.</p>

<p>It was the first and last time in her life that she became someone's fan.</p>

<p>While Goyo was active, Kang Byeokrin was happy.</p>

<p>At first, she got into him for his looks. But later, she fell for his matches.</p>

<p>She couldn't show her favor publicly — her secretaries coached her that it didn't fit the image of a chaebol heiress — but she genuinely loved Goyo as a player.</p>

<p>But it was only one year.</p>

<p>The time Kang Byeokrin got to see Goyo.</p>

<p>"Haah..."</p>

<p>Coming out of her memories, Kang Byeokrin let out a quiet sigh.</p>

<p>His last match was still vivid in her mind, like it had been yesterday.</p>

<p>She'd been worried sick every day since hearing he'd suddenly disappeared recently — she never expected to run into him like this.</p>

<p>'Is his body okay?'</p>

<p>She'd been so startled earlier that she'd run away, so she hadn't gotten a good look at him.</p>

<p>Her heart was still pounding. She took a deep breath in and out through her mouth.</p>

<p>If she approached a player who'd retired due to injury as a fan, it would only burden him.</p>

<p>Besides, the fact that she liked a pro gamer was supposed to be a secret from the public.</p>

<p>'Just act natural. Like I don't know him. Natural!'</p>

<p>Kang Byeokrin hugged Goyo's twenty-five-centimeter character plushie and reminded herself several times.</p>

<p>Then she ran to her dressing room, tore it apart, and changed into clothes that looked effortless but weren't.</p>

<p>With a solemn resolve, she went down to the reception room where Goyo would be waiting.</p>

<p>And her 'acting natural' failed spectacularly.</p>
<hr class="calibre3"/>
<p>Guided by Director Woo, I stepped into the mansion and looked around with fascination.</p>

<p>'It's really huge.'</p>

<p>The garden was massive too. So this was what rich people's houses looked like.</p>

<p>The interior was elegant and refined, as if announcing the owner's taste.</p>

<p>But I couldn't exactly relax and enjoy the tour.</p>

<p>Because Director Woo was standing nearby, ramrod straight.</p>

<p>'They keep staring at me.'</p>

<p>As the executive director of Cheon-gang's household, they'd definitely know about TRA. Even if they didn't know Samra was an android, they'd know he held an important position. But they showed no interest in Samra at all — they only watched me.</p>

<p>Every time our eyes met, Director Woo gave me a polite business smile, but there was something chilling about it.</p>

<p>I glanced at my watch. Five minutes before the appointed time.</p>

<p>"The Chairman will arrive on the dot."</p>

<p>Director Woo spoke with a subtle smile.</p>

<p>"Thank you."</p>

<p>I gave a quick thanks, and Director Woo's smile deepened.</p>

<p>Which somehow made them look even more displeased...</p>

<p>I didn't know why, but I felt like I needed to finish my business here and leave as soon as possible.</p>

<p>CRASH!</p>

<p>A loud noise came from upstairs.</p>

<p>Director Woo rushed toward the stairs, and I shot up from my seat.</p>

<p>Kang Byeokrin limped down the stairs, supported by Director Woo.</p>

<p>"Are you alright?"</p>

<p>I hurried over to ask, and Kang Byeokrin's face turned bright red.</p>

<p>I realized my mistake and stepped back slightly. She must be embarrassed about falling — maybe I'd acted too familiar.</p>

<p>But Kang Byeokrin immediately blurted out,</p>

<p>"I'm fine!"</p>

<p>Her voice cracked. Kang Byeokrin's face flushed all the way down to her nape, and Director Woo was now openly glaring at me.</p>

<p>'I don't know what I did, but I'm sorry.'</p>

<p>I was apologizing internally when Kang Byeokrin asked a strange question.</p>

<p>"Are you... okay?"</p>

<p>I hesitated for a moment, not understanding the intent behind her question, but then the host of today's appointment entered the reception room.</p>

<p>"Samra!"</p>

<p>A booming voice filled the high-ceilinged house.</p>

<p>A woman with voluminous medium-length hair strode in. She looked like a middle-aged woman despite being well past sixty.</p>

<p>Chairman Cheon-gang of Cheon-gang Group — Kang Cheonseo — approached Samra with wide strides and extended her hand.</p>

<p>"Been well?"</p>

<p>"I have been well."</p>

<p>Samra shook her hand and replied politely.</p>

<p>"Haha, still with the military?"</p>

<p>"I plan to remain so."</p>

<p>"What a shame. If you ever quit, give me a call, alright? I'm first in line."</p>

<p>"You are not first in line, Chairman Kang Cheonseo."</p>

<p>"Come on, we're close. Let me cut in."</p>

<p>"That is a subjective judgment, Chairman Kang Cheonseo."</p>

<p>Despite the seemingly rude response, Kang Cheonseo laughed heartily.</p>

<p>Then she turned her gaze to me.</p>

<p>"I was wondering why our precious Samra would venture outside."</p>

<p>Kang Cheonseo's eyes were sharp, the contrast between iris and white vivid.</p>

<p>She extended her hand to me.</p>

<p>"Turns out it was because of Goyo. I was quite surprised. Nice to meet you. I should have greeted you when you were still a player, but I'm only seeing you now after your retirement."</p>

<p>I froze at the unexpected words. Kang Cheonseo gave a gentle smile.</p>

<p>"We were a sponsor, remember? I got into that because of Goyo."</p>

<p>"...Thank you."</p>

<p>I barely managed to move my lips and bowed belatedly.</p>

<p>"I never imagined you would remember me. Please forgive my late greeting. It is an honor to meet you, Chairman."</p>

<p>"Honor, my foot. I'm the one honored to meet Goyo."</p>

<p>She waved her hand lightly a couple of times, then let go and shot a glance at Kang Byeokrin.</p>

<p>"Rin. Go upstairs and get treated first."</p>

<p>"Grandmother...!"</p>

<p>Kang Byeokrin called out to Kang Cheonseo in the most aggrieved voice in the world.</p>

<p>But Kang Cheonseo cut her off coldly.</p>

<p>"Kang Byeokrin."</p>

<p>Kang Byeokrin's face crumpled, but in the end, she headed for the elevator with Director Woo's support.</p>

<p>After confirming Kang Byeokrin was gone, Kang Cheonseo elegantly shook her head.</p>

<p>"I failed to educate my granddaughter properly. The girl's a wild colt, I tell you."</p>

<p>The Chairman looked at me steadily and asked,</p>

<p>"Goyo, you can understand, can't you?"</p>

<p>I gave a gentle smile and replied.</p>

<p>"I saw nothing."</p>

<p>Kang Cheonseo laughed out loud and said cheerfully,</p>

<p>"Shall we go eat? Goyo must be hungry."</p>

<p>I felt like I was being treated a bit like a child.</p>

<p>But considering Kang Cheonseo's age, it wasn't strange to receive that kind of treatment.</p>

<p>Honestly, if I could just get the Good-Looking item, I could handle being treated like a child — or even like an animal.</p>

<p>I debated which would damage TRA's honor more — crawling on all fours or barking — as I followed Kang Cheonseo to the dining room.</p>

<p>Silent-moving staff were already setting warm food on the marble table.</p>

<p>Kang Cheonseo seated me and Samra, recommended various dishes, and led the conversation.</p>

<p>Unexpectedly, the Chairman had a way with words that put people at ease — which only made me more nervous.</p>

<p>I kept my mind razor-sharp so I wouldn't get swept up in her skilled business rhetoric and let something slip.</p>

<p>It wasn't until after the meal, when dessert arrived, that we got to the main topic.</p>

<p>Kang Cheonseo dismissed everyone around us and opened her mouth meaningfully.</p>

<p>"So, Goyo. What favor did you want to ask that made you seek me out?"</p>

<p>I was eating the persimmon sorbet in its ceramic bowl, but I stopped and immediately put down my spoon.</p>

<p>"Hm? Why'd you put it down? Eat while we talk."</p>

<p>"Yes, thank you."</p>

<p>Still, I didn't pick it up again. I waited for the Chairman to continue.</p>

<p>Kang Cheonseo's eyes crinkled deeply.</p>

<p>"It must be a huge favor, right? Samra coming outside, helping someone — that's never happened before."</p>

<p>Given her personality, she'd hate it if I beat around the bush. I stated my request directly.</p>

<p>"I would like to request the support of the Good-Looking item in your possession, Chairman, in order to clear the Trial."</p>

<p>"Hmm..."</p>

<p>Kang Cheonseo let out a troubled hum, but even I could tell she wasn't genuinely troubled.</p>

<p>"It's hard to just hand it over empty-handed."</p>

<p>"State your reason."</p>

<p>"Samra, don't be so harsh. Paseong went through a lot of trouble to get this for me. If I just hand it over to TRA, how would that look?"</p>

<p>Paseong...!</p>

<p>I instinctively pressed my rising backside back into the chair.</p>

<p>Then, with the calmest face I could manage, I asked,</p>

<p>"Did CEO Paseong procure the Good-Looking item?"</p>

<p>"That's right. CEO Paseong is quite capable, you know. Goyo knows CEO Cha too, right?"</p>

<p>I do. I know him very, very well.</p>

<p>I barely swallowed the words threatening to burst out of me.</p>

<p>Because CEO Cha of Paseong was —</p>

<p>My absolute favorite character!</p>

<p>'So he really does exist in real life too.'</p>

<p>I was trying to suppress my overwhelming emotions when Kang Cheonseo fanned the flames.</p>

<p>"What to do... Would Goyo like to ask CEO Cha for permission yourself? I'll patch you through."</p>

<p>She pulled out her phone and waved it.</p>

<p>A call with my favorite character. I couldn't miss this opportunity.</p>

<p>Struggling to hide my excitement, I said,</p>

<p>"If it wouldn't be an imposition, I would like to request —"</p>

<p>"No."</p>

<p>Samra cut me off.</p>

<p>His face went blank. The features that had been mimicking human reactions disappeared, leaving a face that was shockingly cold.</p>

<p>"Chairman Kang Cheonseo. What are you doing?"</p>

<p>"What's wrong, Samra? It's just one call. Unless there's something you're hiding."</p>

<p>"That is not your concern, Chairman Kang Cheonseo."</p>

<p>"Shall I guess?"</p>

<p>Kang Cheonseo said with a sly expression.</p>

<p>"CEO Cha of Paseong has been tracking Goyo, hasn't he?"</p>
