<h1>Chapter 17</h1>

<p>I almost didn't recognize her. It seemed Kwak Hanmuk was in the same boat.</p>

<p>"Whoa, shit, I almost ran you over…."</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk, who had barely stopped himself from plowing straight into her thinking she was a zombie, muttered a curse.</p>

<p>Mo Haein slowly lowered the thumb she had raised.</p>

<p>Since she hadn't been bitten long ago, she still retained much of her human appearance.</p>

<p>But drenched in blood as she was, in the split second the headlights hit her in the darkness, she really did look like a zombie.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk and I hurriedly unbuckled our seatbelts and got out of the car. Kwak Hanmuk stared at Mo Haein with a twisted expression.</p>

<p>"Captain Mo...."</p>

<p>Mo Haein's eyes widened slightly at his trembling voice. Kwak Hanmuk whispered seriously.</p>

<p>"This is the first time someone I know has turned into a zombie."</p>

<p>Me too…!</p>

<p>I secretly voiced my agreement internally.</p>

<p>"You think I have?"</p>

<p>Mo Haein asked, sounding dumbfounded, and Kwak Hanmuk hid behind me.</p>

<p>Then, giggling, he made a machine-gun firing gesture at Mo Haein with his hands.</p>

<p>I meekly raised both hands to signal my surrender.</p>

<p>At the sight, a deflating laugh escaped Mo Haein. She murmured bitterly.</p>

<p>"Still, it's a relief we met before I turned completely."</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk stopped his machine-gun barrage and asked.</p>

<p>"Your condition?"</p>

<p>"Bite location is the left forearm. High fever started immediately upon infection. No pain. Aggression is at a tolerable level."</p>

<p>Mo Haein's eyes glinted blood-red even in the darkness.</p>

<p>Bloodshot eyes, skin so pale the blue veins were clearly visible. All were classic signs of zombification.</p>

<p>In three days, she would become a complete zombie.</p>

<p>By protocol, she should be shot. But neither I nor Kwak Hanmuk aimed a gun at her.</p>

<p>If we just clear the True Ending.</p>

<p>Everyone can get out safely.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk briefly filled her in on what we had been through. Hearing of Je Hyun-oh's atrocities, Mo Haein gritted her teeth and cursed.</p>

<p>"That fucking psychopath…."</p>

<p>"Did you get bitten by a zombie while doing something, Captain? Was it the Lieutenant Colonel?"</p>

<p>"Half of it is because of the Lieutenant Colonel. The other half isn't."</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk, who had been firmly convinced it was all Je Hyun-oh's fault, raised an eyebrow.</p>

<p>Mo Haein hesitated for a moment. It was an uncharacteristically unconfident attitude.</p>

<p>"The researchers… there are four of them."</p>

<p>It was an impossible situation.</p>

<p>Originally, in DeZonDeal, there was only a single, final researcher developing the vaccine.</p>

<p>Mo Haein explained the details to us.</p>

<p>"I opened my eyes at the border of the Yellow Zone, close to the Red Zone."</p>

<p>While moving to find me and Kwak Hanmuk, she had discovered a survivor being chased by zombies in the Yellow Zone.</p>

<p>Mo Haein couldn't believe her eyes.</p>

<p>"They were wearing a lab coat."</p>

<p>With a researcher's ID card dangling from their neck, no less.</p>

<p>Mo Haein rescued the researcher from the zombies. And then she heard a shocking story.</p>

<p>There were more researchers who had survived in the laboratory.</p>

<p>Since Mo Haein had also memorized the entire strategy guide, she knew the fact that there was only one researcher.</p>

<p>She set aside the incomprehensible situation and first tried to bring the researcher to join us.</p>

<p>"But the researcher begged me, saying they had to return to the lab immediately."</p>

<p>There was another injured researcher at the lab, and their condition was so critical that they had risked coming all the way to the Yellow Zone to find medical supplies.</p>

<p>Mo Haein had no choice but to agonize over it.</p>

<p>What if the injured person at the lab was the researcher who would develop the vaccine?</p>

<p>The researcher she had rescued didn't match the personal details of the vaccine researcher recorded in the DeZonDeal strategy guide.</p>

<p>She didn't know how long it would take to regroup, and if the vaccine researcher died in the meantime, it would be a disaster.</p>

<p>If the vaccine researcher died, forget the True Ending — clearing the Trial itself would be impossible.</p>

<p>Left with no choice, Mo Haein headed for the lab with only the Black Moon Blade in hand, without even having farmed items properly.</p>

<p>She passed through the Red Zone safely, but in the Dead Zone, the lab's boss zombie tripped her up.</p>

<p>She succeeded in diverting the boss zombie's attention and getting the researcher safely inside, but in the process, her strength bottomed out, and she ended up getting bitten by a zombie on her way back.</p>

<p>"And that's how I ended up in this sorry state."</p>

<p>Mo Haein assessed her own condition cynically.</p>

<p>But she had absolutely no reason to put herself down. Quite the opposite.</p>

<p>Insane.</p>

<p>Knowing the game's difficulty, all I could think was how incredibly amazing it was.</p>

<p>She'd gone into the lab alone, protecting a survivor without any decent items, and even faced off against the boss zombie.</p>

<p>Even if she had pulled out the Black Moon Blade, it was absurd. This was something only Mo Haein could have pulled off.</p>

<p>"Captain Mo. You're truly incredible."</p>

<p>My overflowing admiration slipped out before I knew it.</p>

<p>At my heartfelt words, Mo Haein leaned back slightly. Then she glanced at Kwak Hanmuk.</p>

<p>"Goyo wasn't bitten."</p>

<p>Even after hearing Kwak Hanmuk's reply that his brain was perfectly fine, she didn't withdraw her skeptical gaze.</p>

<p>Her staring at me with those bloodshot eyes was a little scary.</p>

<p>"How did the researchers survive?"</p>

<p>Fortunately, thanks to Kwak Hanmuk's question, I was able to escape zombie Mo Haein's stare.</p>

<p>"The Lieutenant Colonel was the cause."</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh had entered DeZonDeal ten days earlier than us.</p>

<p>Normally, he would have progressed through the clear route in a standard way, but for some reason, this time he had slaughtered zombies the moment he entered.</p>

<p>Not just the Red Zone and Dead Zone, but he even went inside the lab and killed every zombie there.</p>

<p>Thanks to that, four researchers survived, when originally all but the last one should have been wiped out.</p>

<p>And one of them had escaped the lab before the zombies respawned, made it all the way to the Yellow Zone, and met Mo Haein.</p>

<p>I had a feeling Je Hyun-oh was up to something weird….</p>

<p>I just didn't know he was causing this much large-scale havoc.</p>

<p>If he was going to rampage, I wish he'd just keep rampaging and make a zombie-free world. I had no idea why he'd stop halfway and create such an ambiguous situation.</p>

<p>The most troublesome part was that he might have blown away the True Ending conditions.</p>

<p>Mo Haein looked at me, a faint anxiety showing. Kwak Hanmuk also stared at me silently.</p>

<p>Under their combined gazes, I slowly opened my mouth.</p>

<p>"I think we need to check the lab. As quickly as possible."</p>

<p>We had to enter the lab immediately and confirm whether the researcher developing the vaccine was intact, and whether the other True Ending conditions were in place.</p>

<p>The problem was how to enter the lab.</p>

<p>With our team's current stamina and held items, it wasn't even certain we could pass through the Yellow Zone.</p>

<p>Even if we were lucky enough to get through the Yellow and Red Zones safely, we'd be blocked in the Dead Zone.</p>

<p>Since everyone was thinking the same thing, the mood became slightly gloomy.</p>

<p>But there was a way to solve all of this.</p>

<p>In theory, it was a perfect method.</p>

<p>"I do have a good… well, a bad method."</p>

<p>I explained the method I had come up with. At that, Mo Haein threw out a heartfelt remark.</p>

<p>"Trainee, are you insane?"</p>

<p>It seemed to be a pure, unfiltered emotion she couldn't hold back, just like I had earlier.</p>

<p>With that, it was already the second time she had called me a madman.</p>

<p>In the calmest, most composed voice I could muster, trying not to look insane, I persuaded her and Kwak Hanmuk.</p>

<p>"Because there's no other way. I want to try it."</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk nodded as if deeply empathizing, then said something completely unrelated.</p>

<p>"Captain Mo. From now on, let's call him Crazy Chestnut."</p>

<p>"Haaaaa...."</p>

<p>Mo Haein let out a long sigh. But she knew, too.</p>

<p>That right now, there was no other sharp solution, and if we delayed any longer here, a situation could arise where we all ended up as zombie infectees.</p>

<p>"Fine. Let's give it a shot."</p>

<p>In the end, my plan was accepted.</p>
<hr class="calibre3"/>

<p>I had my own reasons for going through with this.</p>

<p>Of course, since I couldn't tell Mo Haein and Kwak Hanmuk everything, it was a method that made me look like an absolute madman….</p>

<p>Anyway, as long as the result is good, that's all that matters.</p>

<p>I stood in the middle of an eight-lane intersection, scraps of newspaper and trash swirling around me.</p>

<p>The wind blew between the collapsed buildings.</p>

<p>The night breeze was quite chilly, making my body shiver involuntarily. It was because my stamina was low.</p>

<p>If I lost a bit more stamina here, my heart would probably start pounding, and my vision would darken.</p>

<p>Still, seeing as no warning notification about a near-death state had popped up, it seemed I could hold on for now.</p>

<p>I need to manage my hunger and thirst, too.</p>

<p>In DeZonDeal, you died if you didn't eat or drink for a certain period, so I had to pay attention to managing that.</p>

<p>It looked like I'd have to put something in my mouth as soon as I dealt with this.</p>

<p>I took a deep breath.</p>

<p>And then I shouted with all my might.</p>

<p>"LIEUTENANT COLONEL JE HYUN-OH!!!"</p>

<p>The quiet street rang with my voice.</p>

<p>A couple of zombies that were still around came rushing over, but before they could even get close to me, their heads and bodies were separated by a flashing spear blade.</p>

<p>Not long after, a chilling sensation washed over me. It was a killing intent so sharp it stung my skin, not just making it tingle.</p>

<p>I clutched the bead rosary wrapped around my waist tightly, like a lifeline.</p>

<p>At some point in the quiet night street, a metallic sound could be heard.</p>

<p>The sound of metals lightly clashing against each other was cold.</p>

<p>I focused my hearing.</p>

<p>The slow, continuous sound drew closer.</p>

<p>A little more, and a little closer still.</p>

<p>The instant the sound abruptly cut off, a gust of wind slammed into my face.</p>

<p>The bead rosary tied around my waist twitched.</p>

<p>I reflexively closed my eyes, and when I opened them, I flinched in surprise.</p>

<p>Black goggles were shoved right up to the tip of my nose.</p>

<div class="hyunoh"><p>o O</p></div>

<p>An emoticon of one eye opening wide filled my vision.</p>

<p>I reflexively felt around my neck.</p>

<p>Thankfully, it was still attached....</p>

<p>Seeing as Kwak Hanmuk's bead rosary hadn't pulled me away, it seemed there was no intent to kill.</p>

<p>I shifted my pupils slightly to look beside the goggles.</p>

<p>The tips of the sharp metal feathers were all pointed at me.</p>

<p>If he swung even one of the six wings, my limbs would probably go flying off like nothing.</p>

<p>"I called you because I wanted to talk."</p>

<p>'Message.'</p>

<p>"First, let me introduce myself. I am Han Goyo, enlisted as a trainee in the TRA."</p>

<p>While I was saying whatever with my mouth, I was busy inputting a Message internally.</p>

<p>A System window appeared in front of Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Event Quest: Protect 'Han Goyo'.</p></div>

<p>It was a Message disguised as a quest window.</p>

<p>To secure my safety, I had fired off the quest name first, then hurriedly inputted the contents.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<p>
You have demonstrated astonishing martial prowess. <br/>

Perhaps you could be the one to end this horrific zombie world.<br/>

'Han Goyo' is the sole hope who will create the True Ending with you.<br/>

You must protect him and create the True Ending.
</p>
</div>

<p>"Lieutenant Colonel, you seem to have some misunderstanding about me. If there's anything wrong, please let me know. I'll correct it immediately."</p>

<div class="systembox">
<p>
Reward: &lt;Dead Zone Delivery&gt; True Ending Clear.<br/>

However, if 'Han Goyo' dies, Bad Ending.
</p>
</div>

<p>I sent the Message, making it roughly similar to how a System window would prattle on.</p>

<p>I wondered if I shouldn't be able to give out real quests if I was supposed to be the System….</p>

<p>Well, I'm probably just lacking in ability still.</p>

<p>So, I made a fake quest window.</p>

<p>Honestly, who's to know if it's fake or real?</p>

<p>A System window pops up right in front of your eyes, going ding-ding. No Adaptee would ever suspect it was fake.</p>

<p>I subtly observed Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>The light on his goggles was off. As if he were deep in thought about something.</p>

<p>After a long silence, a question mark floated up on the goggles.</p>

<div class="hyunoh"><p>? ?</p></div>

<p>I was hesitating, not knowing what it meant, when—</p>

<p>A terribly low voice reached me.</p>

<p>"It's you, isn't it?"</p>

<p>A rough, deep bass that resonated so low it was hard to believe it came from a human.</p>

<p>It took a few seconds to realize that the sound, close to a monster's cry, was a voice.</p>

<p>To me, frozen by the eeriness hammering at my eardrums, Je Hyun-oh spoke.</p>

<p>"You're the one who told me to stay still."</p>
