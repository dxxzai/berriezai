<h1>Chapter 124</h1>

<p>If Won Taebeom had to pick the most perplexing moment of the last few years, it would be right now.</p>

<p>For a moment, he even wondered if he was hallucinating.</p>

<p>Won Taebeom immediately went into a state of alert.</p>

<p>This was because he expected the Daeil Yeorae to be aimed at either Kwak Hanmuk or himself.</p>

<p>However, CEO Cha silently sheathed the Daeil Yeorae into its scabbard.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk raised his eyebrows slightly.</p>

<p>“CEO Cha… Are you sick? Did you perhaps get hit by a gauntlet before I arrived?”</p>

<p>Even though he was openly provoking him, CEO Cha only spoke coldly.</p>

<p>“Where is Han Goyo?”</p>

<p>“Our Trainee Han is busy.”</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk and CEO Cha stared intently at each other.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk grinned, revealing his fangs. However, his eyes remained unsmiling.</p>

<p>“CEO Cha, you need to play the prison warden well so things won’t get too busy. If the Chief of Security stages a coup, just pretend to go along with it. Done. Easy, right?”</p>

<p>He shrugged his shoulders as he spoke, then deliberately scratched at him one more time.</p>

<p>“I heard you’ve agreed to support Trainee Han, CEO Cha. If you can’t handle it, tell me right now. I’ll pass the message on to him.”</p>

<p>CEO Cha let out a sarcastic laugh.</p>

<p>“Me?”</p>

<p>He tilted his head slightly, keeping his gaze fixed on Kwak Hanmuk.</p>

<p>“Captain Kwak. I’m already playing along on my own. Don’t get on my nerves.”</p>

<p>A mechanical voice spat out a sarcastic warning.</p>

<p>“You should just keep acting like the TRA's dog.”</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk let out a mocking bark as if he had been waiting for this moment.</p>

<p>However, CEO Cha didn’t even show a hint of disbelief.</p>

<p>“Yeah. He barks well.”</p>

<p>He tossed that remark out and walked straight out as if his business was finished.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk let out a censored curse behind CEO Cha’s back.</p>

<p>Then, turning to Won Taebeom, he said:</p>

<p>“Lieutenant Won. Go to the medical officer’s quarters.”</p>

<p>He continued with idle chatter, saying he had to do what Trainee Han had ordered, and that Lieutenant Won should ask Trainee Han if he had eaten breakfast.</p>

<p>When Won Taebeom tried to brush it off and walk away, Kwak Hanmuk waved his hand and urged him again.</p>

<p>“I’m not just saying this; make sure you check on Trainee Han.”</p>

<p>“What kind of check are you talking about?”</p>

<p>“He looks quiet, but he’s quite the lunatic. It feels like he’s causing trouble right now, so keep a close eye on him.”</p>

<p>Did he hear this from Han Goyo?</p>

<p>His tone was strange, as if he had been relayed the situation without meeting him in person.</p>

<p>However, Won Taebeom did not bother to ask.</p>

<p>Gray eyes on an expressionless face turned toward Won Taebeom.</p>

<p>“Lieutenant Won, you don’t want to die a meaningless death here, do you? We need Trainee Han to get out, so don’t get any foolish thoughts right now. Put Trainee Han first and move. Understood?”</p>

<p>“Understood.”</p>

<p>Won Taebeom headed toward the medical officer’s quarters in obedience to his superior’s orders.</p>

<p>The hem of his black cassock fluttered as he took long strides.</p>

<p>As he walked alone down the dimly lit hallway, complex thoughts swirled in his mind.</p>

<p>The first question that came to mind was CEO Cha.</p>

<p>He had thought something was off since the terrorist attack on the TRA, but what just happened made it even clearer.</p>

<p>CEO Cha had shown a particularly lenient reaction toward Han Goyo.</p>

<p>No matter how useful Han Goyo was, it was strange for CEO Cha to act this way given his temperament.</p>

<p>It was also difficult to gauge Han Goyo’s intention in calling him now.</p>

<p>Up until now, Han Goyo had only asked him to gather information.</p>

<p>It was an instruction that conveyed he would not reveal too much to him.</p>

<p>It would not have been an exaggeration to say that Won Taebeom had been isolated during this mission, so he was simply puzzled that he suddenly sought him out first.</p>

<p>Just then, a whistling sound was heard.</p>

<p>Starting with a whistle, it was a mix of the hissing of a snake and giggling laughter.</p>

<p>Won Taebeom ignored it out of habit.</p>

<p>His own strangely twisted voice lingered in his ears.</p>
<br />
<p><em>“Whose turn is it this time? Who do you intend to sacrifice again to become the sole holy butcher?”</em></p>
<br />
<p>Since being infected with the BT-DFH virus, he had occasionally heard auditory hallucinations.</p>

<p>He had never mentioned this to anyone.</p>

<p>Because even if he did, he would only be isolated at the TRA as an infected person.</p>

<p>Won Taebeom reached the medical quarters, acting as if he could hear nothing.</p>

<p>“…….”</p>

<p>He was speechless for a moment.</p>

<p>Han Goyo was covered in blood.</p>

<p>Perhaps thinking the visitor was an intruder, Han Goyo was glaring intensely.</p>

<p>He was even holding an iron pipe in his hand.</p>

<p>He looked this way with a pale face, but as soon as he recognized Won Taebeom, he smoothed out his furrowed brow.</p>

<p>“Lieutenant Won?”</p>

<p>His sharp gaze, which had been wary, softened slightly, revealing a hint of familiarity toward someone he knew.</p>

<p>Not a trace of negative emotion regarding the unreasonable and terrifying situation he was currently in was visible.</p>

<p>Even in his disheveled state.</p>

<p>At that moment, Won Taebeom felt a suffocating tightness in his chest.</p>

<p>Emotions are not important to the greater good.</p>

<p>The sacrifice of the few for the many is inevitable.</p>

<p>If paying the price of a single person's misfortune could save countless others, there is even less reason to hesitate.</p>

<p>However, no matter how clear his rational judgment was, he could not suppress the emotions that arose in the moment.</p>

<p>The illusory pain of his collar constricting his neck blocked his airway.</p>

<p>A cruel whisper was heard as if it had been waiting for this moment.</p>
<br />
<p><em>“That child is your new victim!”</em></p>
<hr />

<p>I had drunk Neo in the new prison guard training room, but my memory of that time was unclear.</p>

<p>I concluded that my return to normal was merely a stroke of luck caused by a system error.</p>

<p>Therefore, I thought that if I consumed Neo again, the probability of becoming a prison guard was very high.</p>

<p>However, it wasn't a certainty.</p>

<p>I decided it was worth taking a bet.</p>

<p>In fact, that was practically the only option available.</p>

<p>Although Kwak Hanmuk possessed the neutralizing agent, it was difficult to meet him before the transformation into a prison guard was complete due to circumstances.</p>

<p>And Dominic’s remark recommending Neo gave me my final certainty.</p>
<br />
<p><span class="flashback">“Haven’t you already done this once? The second time will be even easier. Maybe it won’t even bother you.”</span></p>
<br />
<p>As soon as I heard Dominic's words, I thought of DeZonDeal's zombie virus.</p>

<p>The zombie virus was neutralized on its own inside me.</p>

<p>What if I had been the one to neutralize Neo earlier as well?</p>

<p>I decided it was a gamble worth taking, so I drank Neo.</p>

<p>Three bottles, no less.</p>

<p>The Chief of Security wasn't satisfied with just one bottle, so he made me drink three.</p>

<p>When I drank the last bottle, it was so disgusting I couldn't even swallow it properly, but the Chief of Security was incredibly kind and poured it directly into my mouth himself.</p>

<p>There was only one benefit to drinking Neo.</p>

<p>That the torn wound on my head healed instantly.</p>

<p>Aside from that, everything else was the worst.</p>

<p>I barely managed to pretend to be fine in front of the Chief of Security, but from the moment I left the interrogation room, I could no longer bear the pain.</p>

<p>A burning heat surged from my chest and spread throughout my entire body.</p>

<p>Sweating profusely, I barely made it to the medic's quarters.</p>

<p>As soon as I opened the door, I collapsed onto the floor.</p>

<p>Crouching up tightly, I trembled violently and gasped for breath.</p>

<p>“Hah, hah….”</p>

<p>It was hot and agonizing, as if someone had set my heart on fire.</p>

<p>How did I endure it when I drank Neo at the new guard training center before?</p>

<p>But even when I tried to recall the memory, it was only hazy, like a thick fog.</p>

<p>My mind wouldn't function properly because of the pain.</p>

<p>It wasn't just my heart that was suffering.</p>

<p>The heat radiating from my heart traveled through my veins and burned my entire body, and my eyes throbbed with a burning sensation.</p>

<p>I gasped for breath, tormented by the fever that wouldn't subside.</p>

<p>
<em>I couldn't bear this unpleasant sensation.</em><br />

<em>I wanted to get rid of it immediately.</em>
</p>

<p>“Roasted Chestnut.”</p>

<p>Gagging from the fever, I looked at Dominic.</p>

<p>A blue light filled my blurry vision. A shimmer like a cosmic nebula stole my gaze.</p>

<p>“I wish to quickly drive away this unpleasant sensation.”</p>

<p><em>It feels awful.</em></p>

<p>“It is natural. After all, it is not yours, Roasted Chestnut.”</p>

<p>A pleasant voice slurred softly.</p>

<p>“You can do it. Go ahead and try. It will be easier than before.”</p>

<p>I thought, intoxicated by the fever.</p>

<p>I had desperately searched for a way to get rid of Neo, but suddenly, a certain instinct led me in a different direction.</p>

<p>A realization struck me like a light suddenly turning on.</p>

<p>There was no reason for me to act.</p>

<p>For a moment, my eyes burned.</p>

<p>I gave <em>an order</em>.</p>

<p><em>“Get out.”</em></p>

<p>The pounding in my heart subsided.</p>

<p>The heat that felt like it was burning my veins also slowly vanished.</p>

<p>I could sense it instinctively. Neo, who had been stirring up my insides at will, had evaporated.</p>

<p>Without realizing it, the corners of my mouth curled up slightly.</p>

<p>At that moment, a soft touch slapped against my cheek.</p>

<p>Having been suffering from a fever just moments before, the cool sensation sent a shiver down my spine, causing me to squint one eye.</p>

<p>“You did a great job. You are becoming more and more skilled. As expected, you learn quickly.”</p>

<p>At his endlessly affectionate praise, I opened my mouth.</p>

<p><em>“Dominic-ssi.”</em></p>

<p>And then I was terrified.</p>

<p><em>“Um, what is going on? My voice…!”</em></p>

<p>I was so shocked that it felt as if even my hair was standing on end.</p>

<p>It was clearly my voice, yet it sounded bizarre.</p>

<p>No, perhaps saying I 'heard' it was inaccurate.</p>

<p>It wasn't a voice.</p>

<p>It felt as if something with form was pouring its will directly into my brain.</p>

<p>Moreover, for some reason, the voice sounded endlessly childlike.</p>

<p>Watching me in my astonishment, Dominic let out a low laugh.</p>

<p>“You will return to normal soon, so it won’t interfere with your daily life.”</p>

<p><em>“Really…?”</em></p>

<p>“Of course. It will be easier to converse with me from now on.”</p>

<p>He praised my rapid progress once again.</p>

<p>Listening to his praise, I waited for my voice to return.</p>

<p>“…Ah.”</p>

<p>Fortunately, I regained my normal voice not long after.</p>

<p>I let out a huge sigh of relief.</p>

<p>I was so shocked that I could still feel my heart pounding.</p>

<p>After calming down a bit, I sent a message to Kwak Hanmuk and Je Hyun-oh first.</p>

<p>After handling the urgent matter, I thought with a calm mind.</p>

<p>I drank Neo for the second time, following the new prison guard training center.</p>

<p>However, I was not infected by Neo this time either.</p>

<p>Just as I had recovered from DeZonDeal’s zombie virus.</p>

<p>The infection mechanism, and the residue and zombies.</p>

<p>There was a feeling that DeZonDeal and Cell-X were somewhat similar. I pondered it deeply, but nothing immediately struck me.</p>

<p>‘It’s kind of funny that the virus disappeared just because I spoke in a strange voice, though.’</p>

<p>It did seem like I had driven Neo out with the power I possessed.</p>

<p>However, the more I ruminated, the stranger the feeling became.</p>

<p>My mind was completely hazy from the fever, but my consciousness was clear for the brief moment I said, <em>'Get out.'</em></p>

<p>At that moment... it was as if something was <em>connected</em>.</p>

<p>But I couldn't tell what was connected.</p>

<p>I pondered it for a moment, but I couldn't find an answer. In fact, it was a problem with no solution.</p>

<p>Thinking that I hoped things wouldn't get any stranger here, I shifted my thoughts in a different direction.</p>

<p>The bait I offered the Chief of Security was simple.</p>

<p>It was to act as a double agent.</p>

<p>The main point was that I would drive out the Warden and make the Chief of Security the top dog here.</p>

<p>However, there was one thing I couldn't understand.</p>

<p>&quot;Dominic-ssi, why did they keep a subordinate like the Chief of Security in a position like that?&quot;</p>

<p>I couldn't understand why he had been left in that spot, even though the Chief of Security wanted the Warden's position.</p>

<p>If I were the Octopus Tentacle, I would have seated him in the highest position.</p>

<p>Because of that, I didn't realize sooner that he was a subordinate of the Octopus Tentacle.</p>

<p>Come to think of it, it didn't seem like he heard the 'Voice of God' very often.</p>

<p>I had no idea while playing the game, and even after entering Cell-X in reality, I didn't sense any such indication at all.</p>

<p>As someone who uses messages several times a day, almost like chatting, it was something I couldn't understand.</p>

<p>Dominic explained it to me simply.</p>

<p>&quot;Because a subordinate is merely a being to be commanded.&quot;</p>

<p>It meant there was no need or reason to meticulously examine every single detail like that and treat them well.</p>

<p>I felt a strong sense of defiance.</p>

<p>'They entrusted their life and death to me, so shouldn't I naturally treat them well?'</p>

<p>I thought it was right to do everything I could for them.</p>

<p>However, since it would be useless to tell Dominic about this, I kept it to myself and let it go.</p>

<p>I should have slowly gotten up and started to sort out this disastrous situation one by one, but...</p>

<p>I really just wanted to close my eyes for a little while, just ten minutes.</p>

<p>Having been tormented since morning without even eating, I had absolutely no strength left in my body.</p>

<p>I was in a state where I couldn't even knead pudding slime.</p>

<p>I glanced at the bed, then just flopped down onto the floor.</p>

<p>I was in such a filthy state that I didn't even feel like lying on the bed.</p>

<p>“Dominic-ssi… please wake me up if I sleep too long.”</p>

<p>As soon as I mumbled those words, I fell asleep almost as if I had fainted.</p>

<p>Having fallen asleep as if sucked in, I suddenly opened my eyes wide.</p>

<p>At first, I thought I had woken up.</p>

<p>But I soon realized I was still in a dream.</p>

<p>I was on a vast ocean.</p>

<p>Floating in mid-air, I surveyed my surroundings.</p>

<p>Under the dark sky, everything around me was filled with dark blue water. It was a boundless ocean, its width and depth unknown.</p>

<p>Something that makes you feel strange just by looking at it.</p>

<p>Suddenly, the surface of the water rippled. I looked down at my feet.</p>

<p>From the deepest part of the turbulent sea, something surged rapidly upward.</p>

<p>A massive water shadow drew closer, then tore through the surface to reveal its true form.</p>

<p>It was like a mountain.</p>

<p>Hundreds of tentacles tangled and wriggled amidst the shattered waves, but they were only a fraction of it.</p>

<p>I could easily imagine the form that stretched endlessly beneath the surface.</p>

<p>And its eyes.</p>

<p>The eyes of the deep sea, revealed between the tentacles, <em>stared</em> at me.</p>

<p>The pitch-black pupils dilated to take in my existence.</p>

<p>Primordial terror, a living abyss, darkness that sucks in all the light of the world.</p>

<p>It <em>perceived</em> me.</p>

<p>Having fully realized my existence, it extended its tentacles.</p>

<p>The hand reaching out toward me was greedy, yet I felt no threat.</p>

<p>On the contrary, I could not hold back and burst into laughter.</p>

<p>A golden light flashed, piercing through the darkness.</p>

<p>I, too, <em>perceived</em> it.</p>

<p>That was it.</p>

<p>The one that had touched my subordinate.</p>
