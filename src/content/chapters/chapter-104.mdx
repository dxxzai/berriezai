<h1>Chapter 104</h1>


<p>There was a simple reason why I specifically mentioned Paseong.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh was hostile toward both the Trial and the Adaptees.</p>

<p>He had no reason to show leniency to a Adaptee who wasn't even affiliated with the Trial Response Agency.</p>

<p>He had gone easy on me so far only because I was part of the Agency’s Special Mission Team; otherwise, he would have torn me to shreds long ago.</p>

<p>We had been thrust into this situation suddenly, like a bolt from the blue—or a crashing meteorite.</p>

<p>I doubted Je Hyun-oh had heard from Samra about whether I was renewing my contract, so I sent him a telepathic message.</p>

<p>Upon receiving the message, Je Hyun-oh lowered his head toward me.</p>

<p>The glossy surface of his goggles drew a little closer.</p>

<p>Perhaps because the surroundings were dim, his dark silhouette seemed to loom over me like a cascading shadow.</p>

<p>“Goyo.”</p>

<p>His low, heavy voice whispered languidly.</p>

<p>“Did you sign a contract with the Agency?”</p>

<p>It was a rough, guttural bass—no different from the growl of a beast.</p>

<p>I glanced at the nearby guards. They showed no sign of surprise, even though the creature had spoken.</p>

<p>Since Je Hyun-oh was classified as a creature, the guards likely heard his voice merely as the roar of a monster.</p>

<p>I replied in detail via telepathic message.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>I plan to sign the contract once we leave this Trial. If you cooperate just a little, Lieutenant Commander Je, I promise a swift closure of the Trial and an immediate signing of the contract.</p></div>

<p>I fired off the message rapidly, almost like rapping. Then, I carefully gauged his reaction.</p>

<p>Sure enough... the atmosphere seemed to soften just a bit.</p>

<p>Breathing a sigh of relief inwardly, I cautiously walked past Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>Then, I threw open the door to Solitary Cell No. 13—which was already unlocked—and called out to him.</p>

<p>“Creature Inmate No. 13. Please come this way.” A snort of suppressed laughter was heard.</p>

<p>It was Kwak Hanmuk, who had been watching the scene and couldn't hold back his amusement.</p>

<p>Dominic also chimed in regarding my imitation of a doctor.</p>

<p>“Since Roasted Chestnut’s subordinate is in a somewhat unstable state, it would be wise to combine counseling with treatment while we have the chance.”</p>

<p>I deeply agreed with Dominic’s advice, given his background as a former psychiatrist.</p>

<p>However, if Je Hyun-oh’s mental instability were the sort of condition that could be cured, the Trial Response Agency would have already fixed it using every means at their disposal.</p>

<p>I looked at Je Hyun-oh with a pounding heart.</p>

<p>He watched me standing there gripping the cell door, and then, a moment later, a glowing emoticon flashed across his goggles.</p>

<div class="hyunoh"><p>^ ^</p></div>

<p>I had never been so happy to see an emoticon.</p>

<p>While I was moved by the sight of an emoticon after such a long time, Je Hyun-oh walked toward me.</p>

<p>Then, he docilely stepped inside the cell.</p>

<p>“…!”</p>

<p>A stir ran through the guards at the sight of such submissive behavior from a creature that had previously been violent enough to kill a guard.</p>

<p>While they were flustered, Kwak Hanmuk held out his palm to them; he had evidently joined a betting pool in the meantime.</p>

<p>Since he likely wagered on my survival, it looked like he was about to make a tidy profit.</p>

<p>Thinking I should ask him to treat me to a delicious meal later, I settled into the solitary cell alongside Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>In truth, the real challenge was about to begin.</p>

<p>The schedule displayed upon the completion of my mission had indicated that "Creature Inmate No. 13" would be present today; consequently, I had to treat the relevant section of the operational manual as the absolute truth and carry it out.</p>

<p>I was required to treat Creature Inmate No. 13 "kindly," but I had no idea how Je Hyun-oh would perceive my actions.</p>

<p> ‘Well, at least I’ve got that emoticon displayed on my goggles.’</p>

<p>I decided to trust the emoticon and just go for it.</p>

<p>Since things had come to this, the best course of action was to get the rounds over with right now.</p>

<p>“Creature Inmate No. 13. Could you please sit here?”</p>

<p>When I pointed to the chair, Je Hyun-oh sat down.</p>

<p>Bolstered by his cooperative attitude, I bravely took out my stethoscope.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Please bear with me for a moment. It’ll be over really quickly.</p></div>

<p>Of course, I didn’t forget to send a message ahead of time.</p>

<p>I gently placed the stethoscope against Je Hyun-oh’s chest.</p>

<p>Tap.</p>

<p>My hand trembled at the slight sensation of the stethoscope making contact.</p>

<p>I was ready to bolt instantly if any metallic clanking sound occurred, but Je Hyun-oh remained perfectly still.</p>

<p>After going through the motions of an examination, I hurriedly pulled the stethoscope back and announced:</p>

<p>“Everything is normal! You’re in excellent health.”</p>

<p>Then, I quickly retrieved the chart from my inventory.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Patient Record Chart (For Creatures): A chart for recording the patient's condition. Creatures require special attention regarding these records.</p></div></div>

<p>Since I wasn’t a real doctor, I only needed to make a simple note indicating a normal status.</p>

<p>After finishing the entry, I swallowed hard. Only one final hurdle remained.</p>

<p>That was to treat him ‘kindly’...</p>

<p>In the original game, this was an action performed toward empty air.</p>

<p>But now that Creature Inmate No. 13—specifically Je Hyun-oh—had actually arrived as an inmate, the difficulty level had skyrocketed.</p>

<p>With grim determination, I took an item out of my inventory.</p>

<p>Then, I placed it gently—very, very gently—onto Je Hyun-oh’s knees.</p>

<p>“…….”</p>
<p> Je Hyun-oh stared intently at the object resting on his knee.</p>

<p>It was a yellow candy.</p>

<p>In reality, it wasn't a candy, but a nutritional supplement.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Children's nutritional supplement: A candy-shaped supplement with a refreshing lemon flavor. Suitable for both humans and creatures.</p></div>

<p>After looking at the supplement, Je Hyun-oh raised his gaze to look at me.</p>

<p>I squeezed my eyes shut and blurted out:</p>

<p>“I… I only give these to well-behaved creature patients…!”</p>

<p>My voice rang out loudly across Zone X.</p>

<p>I hadn't actually spoken that loudly, but the silence of the surroundings made the sound carry much further.</p>

<p>Feeling embarrassed, I cautiously opened my eyes again.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh was still staring intently at me.</p>

<p>His goggles lit up.</p>

<div class="hyunoh"><p>O O</p></div>

<p>…Does he like candy?</p>

<p>Two large circles appeared on his goggles, though I couldn't quite guess what they signified.</p>

<p>Since he reportedly ate whole cakes with Kwak Hanmuk, it seemed likely he enjoyed candy, too.</p>

<p>In any case, I was off the hook. It seemed a bit of camaraderie had developed between us after all.</p>

<p>I quickly wrapped up the examination and stepped out of solitary cell No. 13.</p>

<p>Clang—the door shut.</p>

<p>I glanced back at Je Hyun-oh through the bars.</p>

<p>He sat motionless, faithfully playing the role of a prisoner.</p>

<p>Seeing him confined in that small, stifling space, I felt an urge to let him out, even though I was the one who had locked him up.</p>

<p>As I stared at him, lost in thought, a different question suddenly crossed my mind.</p>

<p>Why was Je Hyun-oh at the TRA? It seemed unlikely that Jang Seokyun had managed to trap Je Hyun-oh in an inescapable contract; given Je Hyun-oh’s personality, he wouldn’t have accepted just anything.</p>

<p>‘I’d like to ask, but he probably wouldn’t answer...’</p>

<p>It seemed best to just focus on shutting down Cell-X as quickly as possible.</p>

<p>After all, playing the role of a prisoner wouldn’t be easy for Je Hyun-oh, and I wanted to get him out of there fast.</p>

<p>I let out a deep sigh inwardly and looked at the prison guards.</p>

<p>I saw Kwak Hanmuk mouthing a word that looked like 'chestnut.'</p>

<p>Just as I was wondering if he meant Chestnut...</p>

<p>Ding! Ding! Ding! Ding!</p>

<p>Sounds signaling changes in stats erupted all at once.</p>

<p>The notifications rang out so rapidly and chaotically that it was impossible to tell where the sounds were coming from.</p>

<p>System windows popped up and jumbled together haphazardly, making it difficult to distinguish whose stats were rising.</p>

<p>It wasn't just the guards; the creatures were experiencing stat changes as well.</p>

<p>Having seen how I treated Creature Inmate No. 13, it wasn't strange for the creatures' stats to fluctuate.</p>

<p>But the magnitude of the increases was bizarre.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Affinity +9</p></div>

<div class="systembox"><p>Affinity +30</p></div>

<div class="systembox"><p>Affinity +62</p></div>

<div class="systembox"><p>Suspicion +18</p></div>

<div class="systembox"><p>Suspicion +67</p></div>

<div class="systembox"><p>Affinity +55</p></div>

<div class="systembox"><p>Suspicion...</p></div>

<p>As I frantically checked the system windows, I flinched.</p>

<p>‘Affinity +62?’</p>

<p>It was an absurd increase.</p>

<p>The Affinity +55 that followed was also unusual; there were simply too many double-digit jumps.</p>

<p>It made no sense. Increases of that magnitude almost never happened.</p>

<p>Moreover, since Suspicion rose easily while Affinity was notoriously difficult to raise, the whole situation felt like a system error.</p>

<p>‘Wait a minute...Who is the one with +67 Suspicion?’</p>

<p>When the stat exceeds 50, a major shift occurs in the relationship with that individual.</p>

<p>It often triggers a quest; if the person is significant, that quest could drastically alter the ending.</p>

<p>I reflexively checked the Chief of Security’s stats.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Chief of Security<br/>
&#45; Suspicion: 80<br/>
&#45; Favorability: 58</p></div></div>

<p>…Crazy.</p>

<p>Back at the infirmary, the stats had been 13 Suspicion and 3 Favorability—but now, they had skyrocketed.</p>

<p>‘The Chief of Security was the one with +67 Suspicion and +55 Favorability?’</p>

<p>I was so dumbfounded that my mouth fell slightly open.</p>

<p>Even granting the rise in Suspicion, I couldn't fathom why Favorability had increased as well, especially after I’d just treated a creature.</p>

<p>But there was no time to analyze the numbers.</p>

<p>“Little Lamb.”</p>

<p>I felt a chill run down my spine as I looked at the Chief of Security.</p>

<p>In Cell-X, an increase in a guard's Favorability—especially when accompanied by a rise in Suspicion—was never a good thing.</p>

<p>“Shall we go somewhere quiet to talk?”</p>

<p>“…Ah, well. Since I’m already here, I should finish my rounds with the remaining creature patients…”</p>

<p>The Chief of Security spoke with a smile.</p>

<p>“This isn't a request.”</p>

<p>I was screwed.</p>

<p>I realized I was in deep trouble.</p>

<p>The Chief of Security stepped closer and placed his hand on my shoulder. His large hand pressed down firmly.</p>

<p>“Let’s go.”</p>

<p>“…Yes.”</p>

<p>As I was practically dragged away, I hurriedly sent a message to Kwak Hanmuk.</p>

<p>And the moment I stepped into the Chief of Security’s office...</p>

<p>Bam!</p>

<p>I was grabbed by the scruff of my neck and slammed face-first onto the desk.</p>

<p>“Urgh…”</p>

<p>A groan of pain involuntarily escaped my lips. Since I had anticipated the impact, I managed to turn my head slightly and narrowly protect my face.</p>

<p>Had I not, the force of the blow would have certainly caused a nosebleed.</p>

<p>Dominic spoke in a startled voice as I lay pinned flat.</p>

<p>“I’ve never seen such a brute. Roasted Chestnut, shouldn’t we punish him?”</p>

<p>Dominic clearly believed I was capable of firing a laser beam.</p>

<p>But I was just a powerless human, quietly pinned against the desk.</p>

<p>“Little Lamb.”</p>

<p>The Chief of Security asked the question while pressing down on me from behind.</p>

<p>“Are you able to communicate with Creature Inmate No. 13?”</p>

<p>I had deliberately ignored Je Hyun-oh’s words and replied via telepathic message instead, but the Chief of Security was sharp.</p>

<p>For now, I feigned ignorance.</p>

<p>“I don’t know what you mean… Gack!”</p>

<p>The pressure intensified. My chest was being crushed, making it difficult to breathe.</p>

<p>As oxygen ran low, my vision began to blur.</p>

<p>“Roasted Chestnut?”</p>

<p>Dominic’s voice seemed to drift further away.</p>

<p>Just as I thought I was about to lose consciousness, the Chief of Security eased the pressure.</p>

<p>I took a deep breath.</p>

<p>Looking down at me as I panted, the Chief of Security spoke.</p>

<p>“Did you know? We are always short on prison guards here.”</p>

<p>“…….”</p>

<p>“With just a little training, you might make a fine colleague, Little Lamb.”</p>

<p>So, it was that quest after all.</p>

<p>There was a specific quest that triggered once your levels of suspicion and favorability with the Chief of Security crossed a certain threshold.</p>

<p>It was the kind of quest I’d rather avoid in real life, but it seemed I couldn't escape it now.</p>

<p>The Chief of Security grabbed me by the scruff of the neck and dragged me along.</p>

<p>He headed toward a corner of the office, where a large iron door lay on the floor.</p>

<p>As he lifted the door with his free hand, a passage leading underground was revealed.</p>

<p>It looked like a ventilation shaft, but it was wide enough for a grown man to tumble right through.</p>

<p>An eerie darkness hung within the bottomless hole.</p>

<p>“Little Lamb. I’ll be waiting for you.”</p>

<p>Whispering this into my ear, the Chief of Security shoved me inside.</p>

<p>“Ugh…!”</p>

<p>Roughly thrust in, I plummeted into the unfathomable depths of the passage.</p>

<p>In an instant, the light vanished, and darkness enveloped my entire body.</p>

<p>Amidst the fall, I frantically groped around my head, worried the Pudding Slime might have fallen off.</p>

<p>I felt a soft, squishy texture and let out a sigh of relief.</p>

<p>Then, the moment a flash of green light crossed my vision, I took a sharp breath in.</p>

<p>Splash!</p>

<p>I plunged into a pool of neon-green liquid.</p>

<p>The viscous fluid coated my entire body.</p>

<p>I had been mentally prepared for it, but the actual sensation of it touching my skin was incredibly unpleasant.</p>

<p>Swallowing a curse, I thrashed about in a panic. The doctor’s coat wrapped around my body, hindering my movements, but I managed to swim nonetheless.</p>

<p>Struggling desperately while trying to avoid swallowing the liquid as much as possible, I finally reached solid ground.</p>

<p>Soaking wet, I hauled myself onto the land and took a moment to catch my breath.</p>

<p>“Haa... haa...”</p>

<p>A quest window appeared before my eyes.</p>
