<h1>Chapter 30</h1>

<p>His tone sounded like he was handing out party invitations.</p>

<p>'Finish it tonight?'</p>

<p>Theoretically, it was possible. But that was all it was — theoretical.</p>

<p>Even I had never cleared it on the first night.</p>

<p>Because if you played the game by the rules, it was impossible.</p>

<p>Unless you used cheats.</p>

<p>But right now, standing before me, was a being no different from a cheat.</p>

<p>My mouth went dry. I gathered saliva and swallowed.</p>

<p>I couldn't tell if it was tension or anticipation.</p>

<p>The sun, barely showing its edge, cast one last red glow.</p>

<p>From a distance, carried on the wind, came a faint wailing.</p>

<p>A terrible cacophony spread over the ruined city. A thin line appeared on the horizon.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh, bathed in the sunset, spread his wings.</p>

<p>The black metal wings let out a sharp sound as they unfurled into the light — and the sun sank completely.</p>

<p>A remnant of light still lingered in the sky like embers, but soon darkness would swallow everything.</p>

<p>The line on the horizon began to churn.</p>

<p>It writhed unnaturally, closing in at a grotesque speed, its undulating shape almost like violent waves.</p>

<p>Waves that wouldn't end until the next morning.</p>

<p>As the zombies surged in, Je Hyun-oh stood still with his metal wings spread, watching me.</p>

<p>He took a step back.</p>

<p>His black body dropped.</p>

<p>I knew he'd be fine, but I still ran to the railing and looked down.</p>

<p>I found nothing.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh was already soaring upward.</p>

<p>With his black wings fully spread, he looked down at the zombies from the highest point.</p>

<p>Then he dove straight into the center of the wave.</p>

<p>Like a bullet fired from a gun.</p>

<p>BOOM!</p>

<p>An impact that didn't sound like a landing.</p>

<p>The ground shook in every direction, and for a moment, the zombie wave stopped.</p>

<p>A brief pause.</p>

<p>Then the zombies all rushed toward the source of the sound at once.</p>

<p>Hundreds of zombies, endlessly converging on a single being — it wouldn't be an exaggeration to call it a scene from hell.</p>

<p>Any normal person would have been terrified.</p>

<p>No — no matter how strong they were, they wouldn't have picked such a reckless fight in the first place.</p>

<p>But Je Hyun-oh offered himself as bait, drawing every zombie toward him.</p>

<p>Not with fear — with elation.</p>

<p>"KYAAAAAAK!"</p>

<p>The zombies screamed. Even though they shouldn't feel pain, the screams were soaked in agony.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh rampaged like a beast thrown into a flock of sheep.</p>

<p>His metal hands tore through zombie bones, his wings following close behind, slicing through flesh.</p>

<p>Wherever the black figure passed, flesh and bone fragments rained down.</p>

<p>It was a one-sided slaughter.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh was best at large-scale area attacks.</p>

<p>He could have taken out dozens at once, but he deliberately killed only four or five at a time.</p>

<p>It was easy to imagine how he'd been rampaging for the ten days he'd entered DeZonDeal ahead of me.</p>

<p>'But at that rate, he won't finish by tonight.'</p>

<p>Maybe he was just messing around before getting serious.</p>

<p>Come to think of it, Je Hyun-oh had been behaving himself for a few days.</p>

<p>I watched him for a moment with the mindset of a beast tamer, then thought of Yi Gaon.</p>

<p>The noise and vibration from Je Hyun-oh's landing must have reached her in the lab.</p>

<p>But she seemed fine.</p>

<p>Not a vague hope — she genuinely seemed that way.</p>

<p>Before preparing the lab's defense, I'd explained what would happen over the next three nights.</p>

<p>Even when I told her that hordes of zombies would attack every night, Yi Gaon showed no sign of fear.</p>
<br/>
<p><span class="flashback">"I see... You're done explaining, Roasted Chestnut-nim? I'm in a hurry."</span></p>
<br/>
<p>She'd half-listened, then gone straight back to her research.</p>

<p>Her mind was already completely consumed by the work — nothing else could get through.</p>

<p>Given the situation, it made sense.</p>

<p>'She has to develop both the vaccine and the cure simultaneously in three days.'</p>

<p>With all her assistants gone, it was a mission beyond human limits for one person.</p>

<p>But the current Yi Gaon seemed like she could do it.</p>

<p>Just like bosses have a berserk state, characters have an awakened state.</p>

<p>Though this was the first time I'd seen a researcher in an awakened state in DeZonDeal.</p>

<p>'Though Yi Gaon seems closer to berserk than awakened...'</p>

<p>I recalled the gleam in her eyes.</p>

<p>No matter what happened outside, Yi Gaon would only focus on her research.</p>

<p>All I had to do was defend the lab from the rear.</p>

<p>I slid the sniper rifle's barrel through the rusty railing and locked the bipod in place.</p>

<p>Holding an actual M24 SWS felt strange — unfamiliar yet subtly familiar.</p>

<p>Probably from shooting it a million times in-game.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk had given me a crash course, so I could at least hold it properly.</p>

<p>I brought my eye to the scope and looked for Mo Haein first.</p>

<p>Since she couldn't use the Black Moon Blade due to the zombification, she'd taken a submachine gun instead.</p>

<p>Mo Haein, framed in the scope's crosshairs, was the picture of a perfect soldier.</p>

<p>She held an MP5 submachine gun in her hands, a duffel bag full of 9mm magazines strapped crosswise over both shoulders, and an ammunition belt around her waist.</p>

<p>Mo Haein watched the approaching zombies from behind a large stone block, then slapped the charging handle down.</p>

<p>Click. The loading sound was followed immediately by the muzzle flashing.</p>

<p>BRRRRRRRRT!</p>

<p>Shell casings cascaded like a waterfall. Zombie heads burst like watermelons.</p>

<p>Despite the heavy recoil of the submachine gun, Mo Haein remained calm, expressionless, blowing zombies apart.</p>

<p>Only her short bob, shaking violently, betrayed the weapon's kickback.</p>

<p>'As expected of Captain Mo.'</p>

<p>Even without the Black Moon Blade, her skills hadn't dulled.</p>

<p>I caught zombies approaching from her blind spot in my scope.</p>

<p>I breathed in, let half out, and held it. Then I squeezed the trigger with my index finger.</p>

<p>BANG!</p>

<p>The barrel kicked back briefly.</p>

<p>The zombie's head in my scope exploded.</p>

<p>'Not bad.'</p>

<p>Whether thanks to the Trial's physical compensation or not, I could snipe well enough to be useful — if not as good as in-game.</p>

<p>I pulled the bolt back. The hot casing rolled across the floor as a fresh round slid into the chamber.</p>

<p>Then a deafening megaphone blared out.</p>

<p>"Ah, ah, zombies. This is an announcement."</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk, leaning out of the driver's window of a jeep, was shouting into a megaphone.</p>

<p>"Zombies, please proceed immediately to the central area toward Lieutenant Colonel Je Hyun-oh. I repeat. If you zombies would kindly head to the center, I'd be fucking grateful."</p>

<p>Of course, the jeep's blaring megaphone drew the zombies straight to him.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk didn't flinch. He kept broadcasting stubbornly while steering with one hand.</p>

<p>Then he drove the jeep toward where Je Hyun-oh was.</p>

<p>The zombies almost caught him a few times, but each time he narrowly fended them off with his Bead Rosary.</p>

<p>I quickly picked off a few zombies with the sniper rifle.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk, driving like he was performing acrobatics, finally herded the horde straight toward Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>"LIEUTENANT COLONEL JE!"</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk bellowed through the megaphone.</p>

<p>"STOP PLAYING AROUND AND GET TO WORK!"</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh, who'd been having a grand old time slaughtering zombies indiscriminately, swung his metal wings.</p>

<p>Metal feathers shot from the wings, thwacking into the zombies.</p>

<p>Dozens of zombies nearby dropped as their foreheads were pierced.</p>

<p>The metal feathers that had punched through their skulls slid smoothly out of the bone and returned to his wings.</p>

<div class="hyunoh"><p>—    —</p></div>

<p>Je Hyun-oh displayed an emoticon on his goggles that I guessed was a sullen face. But he still moved his wings, lifting himself into the air.</p>

<p>Flying up to a moderate height, he raised his metal hand.</p>

<p>I quickly sent him a Message.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Captain Mo is near the church.</p></div>

<p>I'd sent it because it looked like he was about to unleash an attack without considering her position.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh checked the system window floating before him, then turned to look at me.</p>

<p>And, as if showing off, he swept his hand down toward the church.</p>

<p>A line over ten meters long split the ruined city.</p>

<p>The black line stretched all the way to the church where Mo Haein was positioned.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk, knowing what disaster would follow, floored the jeep's accelerator and shouted through the megaphone.</p>

<p>"CAPTAIN MO!!"</p>

<p>No explanation — he just yelled.</p>

<p>But the moment she heard his shout, Mo Haein ran without looking back.</p>

<p>Not long after she sprinted at full speed, the black line began to split open.</p>

<p>The zombies caught in the line were bisected on the spot, their entrails spilling out as they died instantly.</p>

<p>The ones that dodged the line met a similar fate.</p>

<p>"KAAAAAK!"</p>

<p>"KREEEEEK!"</p>

<p>Buildings and ground split apart, zombies plunging into deep darkness.</p>

<p>However far he'd dug into the earth, the zombies that fell never crawled back out.</p>

<p>Several hundred were deleted in an instant — enough to stall the tide of zombies surging in like a tsunami.</p>

<p>A system window appeared.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<p>
First defense successful!<br/>

(Current defense successes: 1/3)
</p>
</div>

<p>He'd already filled the quota for the first night.</p>

<p>But Je Hyun-oh didn't stop. He clapped his metal hands together.</p>

<p>CLANG!</p>

<p>The impact rang clear. Then came a scraping sound — screech, screeeech — like metal grinding.</p>

<p>It wasn't just noise from his hands meeting; it was something stranger.</p>

<p>The source of the discordant sound revealed itself as he spread his hands apart.</p>

<p>Between his palms, small black crescents swarmed like a hive of bees.</p>

<p>They clashed against each other, letting out constant scraping sounds, moving erratically.</p>

<p>The black crescents, darting around maddeningly, turned toward the sky.</p>

<p>As they rose in a line, new crescents kept forming and joining them.</p>

<p>They multiplied in an instant, too fast for the eye to follow, blanketing the sky.</p>

<p>The sky, still faintly lit, darkened like midnight.</p>

<p>Screech, screech, screeeech...</p>

<p>The hair-raising sound tortured my eardrums mercilessly. On top of that, the zombies, excited by the metallic noise, roared along.</p>

<p>I forced myself not to cover my ears and kept watching Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>When the sky was completely covered in black crescents.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh finally stopped creating them.</p>

<p>He slowly clenched his metal fist.</p>

<p>As his hand closed, the black crescents covering the sky scraped against each other, making a terrible noise.</p>

<p>Just as I felt it had reached its limit —</p>

<p>He opened his hand. Like letting go of the reins.</p>

<p>Black rain poured down.</p>

<p>It felt almost like a meteor shower.</p>

<p>After a very short downpour.</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>I was speechless.</p>

<p>Every zombie in sight was dead.</p>

<p>Ding.</p>

<p>A system window appeared.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<p>
Second defense successful!<br/>

(Current defense successes: 2/3)
</p>
</div>

<p>...Crazy.</p>

<p>I threw down the sniper rifle and shot to my feet.</p>

<p>The Death's Three Days quest looked like you had to defend for three full days, but you could actually finish early.</p>

<p>If you killed a certain number of the infinitely spawning zombies, it counted as a successful defense.</p>

<p>The number for the first successful defense was ten thousand.</p>

<p>And the number for the second successful defense was...</p>

<p>One million.</p>

<p>I only knew this number because of hidden information I'd gotten from a sub-quest. I'd never actually achieved it.</p>

<p>Because without cheats, it was an absolutely impossible number to fill.</p>

<p>And Je Hyun-oh had filled the impossible number.</p>

<p>As I stared blankly at the defense success window, somber music began to play.</p>

<p>It signaled the start of the third battle — the music announcing DeZonDeal's final boss, 'the Nothing.'</p>

<p>The corpses of the dead zombies writhed, flesh and bone tangling together like clay.</p>

<p>They absorbed not just human remains but scattered steel frames, stone, plastic — all the garbage around — and began to form into a new existence.</p>

<p>With a hideous, nightmare-like appearance, it grew rapidly, expanding to a height even larger than the lab.</p>

<p>I looked up at the Nothing with bewildered eyes.</p>

<p>'Wasn't it smaller than this before?'</p>

<p>Hundreds of pupils were embedded in its slimy outer shell.</p>

<p>Zombie eyeballs.</p>

<p>They spun around in different directions, then suddenly stopped.</p>

<p>All of them were looking at me.</p>

<p>Before I could feel the horror —</p>

<p>"...!"</p>

<p>My body jerked violently and lifted into the air.</p>

<p>Startled, I looked up to see Je Hyun-oh flying, his metal hand cunningly hooked around my belt.</p>

<p>"Goyo."</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh sounded like he was having the time of his life.</p>

<p>"Don't die."</p>

<p>Then he flew over the boss monster —</p>

<p>And dropped me.</p>

<p>"AAARGH! LIEUTENANT COLONEL JE!"</p>
