<h1>Chapter 56</h1>

<p>The moment I heard those confirming words, countless thoughts flashed through my mind.</p>

<p>I swallowed a curse.</p>

<p>Then, using every ounce of calm I could muster, I spoke.</p>

<p>“Even if I clear it, there’s a chance I might not be able to leave.”</p>

<p>—That is correct. We cannot even be certain that the space you are in is a trial.</p>

<p>“The trial… I think it is. There are quests, and it is a game created by the developer whose games I have played.”</p>

<p>—Could you explain what kind of trial CallOVo is?</p>

<p>I briefly explained CallOVo to Samra, and after hearing my explanation, he immediately began searching for information in real time.</p>

<p>—As you know, Han Goyo-nim, the location where a trial hatches is related to the content of the trial. If it had hatched normally, it most likely would have been a trial under the jurisdiction of the United Kingdom. However, at this point, I cannot find any information regarding this trial anywhere.</p>

<p>After saying that, neither of us spoke for a while.</p>

<p>Even Samra was at a loss.</p>

<p>However, Samra immediately began asking questions to resolve the situation.</p>

<p>—How did you use the badge?</p>

<p>“I got a little blood on it.”</p>

<p>—I have tested similar items numerous times, but this is the first time one has successfully activated.</p>

<p>There was a slight hint of surprise in Samra’s voice.</p>

<p>Then his next words surprised me.</p>

<p>—I will operate.</p>

<p>He was offering to act as my operator.</p>

<p>It seemed that my value to Samra had not yet run out.</p>

<p>The sense of security from being connected to the outside world helped keep my mind from collapsing.</p>

<p>It felt like I had been drowning and had luckily managed to grab onto a tube.</p>

<p>I attached the badge back inside my clothes and asked,</p>

<p>“Thank you. Can you hear the sounds around me too, or only my voice?”</p>

<p>—I can only hear Han Goyo-nim’s voice.</p>

<p>Even without holding it near my ear, Samra’s voice came through clearly.</p>

<p>Perhaps because it was an item, its performance was far superior to ordinary communication equipment.</p>

<p>I quickly searched through the mayor’s office while continuing the conversation I had not finished.</p>

<p>“There’s something… strange happening in CallOVo right now.”</p>

<p>—What kind of abnormality?</p>

<p>“I promised an NPC butler from Clean3 that I would find their master, but that master is here in CallOVo.”</p>

<p>I could not be completely certain since we were only communicating through the device, but I felt that Samra was greatly shaken by my words.</p>

<p>His response came much later than usual.</p>

<p>—…I have received the report regarding the forced transfer caused by the butler NPC.</p>

<p>I took out the wire and opened a locked drawer while asking,</p>

<p>“Are my lieutenant colonel and Captain Mo safe?”</p>

<p>—They are safe. For now, focus on Han Goyo-nim’s safety. Please explain the NPC called the master.</p>

<p>“Yes…”</p>

<p>When I opened the locked drawer, it was filled only with miscellaneous items.</p>

<p>However, the depth of the drawer was shallower than it appeared from the outside.</p>

<p>While pressing various places to remove the bottom panel, I explained Dominic.</p>

<p>His height was 3 meters, he had lost his memories and did not know what kind of existence he was, he could interfere with the system, influence NPCs, transform objects, and so on.</p>

<p>By the time I finished explaining my plan to use his favorability to clear the trial and escape, I finally separated the bottom of the drawer.</p>

Documents detailing the cult’s crimes.</p></div></div>

<p>“…So right now, we are moving separately.”</p>

<p>Come to think of it, I had been talking by myself for quite a while.</p>

<p>Worried that the communication might have been cut off, I quickly called his name.</p>

<p>“Samra?”</p>

<p>Only then did Samra’s voice come through.</p>

<p>—Han Goyo-ssi.</p>

<p>“Yes?”</p>

<p>—I am well aware that Han Goyo-ssi creates new results because you do not follow general common sense. However, this situation is far too dangerous.</p>

<p>His calm, emotionless voice was more frightening because of how composed it was.</p>

<p>I immediately apologized.</p>

<p>“I’m sorry. But there was no other way to get out…”</p>

<p>—Is it impossible to cancel the companion relationship?</p>

<p>“It is possible, but if I cancel it unilaterally, we will immediately become enemies.”</p>

<p>—I will search for similar cases. Please wait a moment.</p>

<p>I used the brief pause to quickly photograph the documents.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Perfectly photographed!
Recorded as decisive evidence.</p></div></div>

<p>Despite saying “a moment,” Samra returned quickly.</p>

<p>—It would be better not to cancel the companion relationship.</p>

<p>I expected that.</p>

<p>I nodded inwardly and placed my hand on the windowsill to leave the mayor’s office when Samra spoke again.</p>

<p>—The hologram badge has one additional function besides communication.</p>

<p>“What function?”</p>

<p>—Escape.</p>

<p>“…Escape?”</p>

<p>—It is a function created by utilizing the fact that the trial is based on a game.</p>

<p>The moment I heard Samra’s explanation, I understood.</p>

<p>In games, there was an escape function that could be used when a character was placed in an abnormal location, movement became impossible, or progression was blocked due to an error.</p>

<p>‘Can I leave right now?’</p>

<p>But before hope could swell within me, Samra immediately poured cold water over it.</p>

<p>—Escape can only be used when the trial causes a system-level error.</p>

<p>That made sense.</p>

<p>Using something as powerful as an escape function without restrictions would be unreasonable.</p>

<p>Even in games, escape was a function meant only for errors.</p>

<p>“If it’s a system-level error, does that mean something like the letters in the system window becoming corrupted?”</p>

<p>—That is correct.</p>

<p>It meant I had to wait until the system window displayed a new error.</p>

<p>—Also, in order to use this function, the communication must be terminated for stability.</p>

<p>The connection to the outside world that had barely been restored would have to be cut again.</p>

<p>But there was no need to mention which was more important.</p>

<p>“Understood.”</p>

<p>After giving a short reply, I asked the question I had wanted to ask from the very beginning but had hesitated to bring up.</p>

<p>“Clean3…”</p>

<p>—It has been closed.</p>

<p>Samra gave me the answer I wanted before I even finished my sentence.</p>

<p>I felt relief mixed with a small sense of loneliness.</p>

<p>Samra’s voice brought me back to reality.</p>

<p>—Han Goyo-ssi. I believe you will return safely.</p>

<p>I wasn’t sure if this kind of expression was appropriate for an android, but it felt like Samra was worried about me.</p>

<p>I smiled slightly at the thought and replied,</p>

<p>“Yes. I’ll see you outside, Samra.”</p>

<p>No further voice came.</p>

<p>The communication had ended.</p>

<p>I was alone again.</p>

<p>For a while, I sat on the windowsill and sank into thought.</p>

<p>‘First, I should proceed with clearing the trial while looking for a chance to escape.’</p>

<p>The moment Dominic caused the system window to malfunction would be the most certain opportunity.</p>

<p>The system seemed to have accepted me dying and reviving in front of Dominic as normal already…</p>

<p>After roughly organizing my plan, I suddenly looked out the window.</p>

<p>A bell rang.</p>

<p>Dong, daeng….</p>

<p>It was definitely not a bell announcing the time.</p>

<p>It was not the hour, and the way the bell rang felt unnatural.</p>

<p>The loud sound seemed more like an emergency intrusion alarm.</p>

<p>‘There was nothing in CallOVo about enemies invading.’</p>

<p>I waited for the bell to stop, but then the door of the building across from me suddenly flew open.</p>

<p>Starting with that building, every door on the street opened.</p>

<p>People with hazy expressions poured outside.</p>

<p>Then they all began moving somewhere in a line.</p>

<p>I leaned farther out the window to check where the crowd was going.</p>

<p>They were heading toward the building of the Apostles of the Void.</p>

<p>Something was going terribly wrong.</p>

<p>A choice appeared.</p>

<p>Unfortunately, only the contents were strange. The shape of the letters was completely normal.</p>

<p>I chose the first option and stepped outside through the open door.</p>

<p>The building was empty.</p>

<p>I did not encounter a single person while descending to the first floor.</p>

<p>It seemed everyone from the TRA had also followed the bell toward the cult building.</p>

<p>I had experienced several abnormal events caused by NPCs or bosses during trials.</p>

<p>But this was the first time I had seen such a large number of NPCs experience abnormalities at once.</p>

<p>Although suspicious, I blended into the crowd for now.</p>

<p>It was a perfect opportunity to naturally enter the cult.</p>

<p>Everyone heading toward the cult was smiling.</p>

<p>As if they were going on a pleasant vacation.</p>

<p>I was the only person here who was not smiling.</p>

<p>Before long, the cult building came into view.</p>

<p>Music filled my ears.</p>

<p>I had never heard it in CallOVo, but I knew the song.</p>

<p>Ode to Joy.</p>

<p>A grand symphony of men and women singing together echoed loudly.</p>

<p>A festival was taking place in front of the cult building.</p>

<p>Two apostles holding enormous black swords stood on either side of the entrance, beckoning people forward.</p>

<p>Young men and women approached while smiling brightly.</p>

<p>The black blades swung.</p>

<p>Two round heads flew through the air.</p>

<p>Then they rolled across the ground.</p>

<p>They came to a stop where countless other heads had already piled up.</p>

<p>I saw countless heads.</p>

<p>Every single one wore a happy expression.</p>

<p>As I stood frozen, staring at the scene, the beheadings continued.</p>

<p>There were no screams.</p>

<p>No painful groans, no pleas for mercy.</p>

<p>Only occasional cheerful laughter could be heard amid the chorus of joy.</p>

<p>The overflowing blood seemed as if it would reach my feet.</p>
<br/>
<p><span class="flashback">“I will prepare a grand ritual, Doctor.”</span></p>
<br/>
<p>If Dominic’s words were followed and the ceremony was carried out, it should have been against the cult members…</p>

<p>The moment that thought reached me, an unbelievable conclusion surfaced.</p>

<p>‘Could it be that every citizen of Morneveil is a cult member?’</p>

<p>There was no other way to explain this scene.</p>

<p>But Edel should not have had that kind of power.</p>

<p>I knew because I had played the priest route.</p>

<p>The power of the cult depended on the outer god they summoned.</p>

<p>They started by contracting with lower-ranked outer gods and gradually grew stronger by making contracts with more powerful ones.</p>

<p>If the current priestess Edel had made a contract with an outer god powerful enough to use this kind of strength, there was no way I would not have known.</p>

<p>‘Those who contract with outer gods have their bodies altered.’</p>

<p>In the priest route, Edel made contracts with multiple outer gods, causing her body to become distorted and mixed together.</p>

<p>By the late game, she became something close to a chimera.</p>

<p>When I played CallOVo, I promised Edel that I would release her contracts by making a pact with the highest being, and I inherited the position of priest.</p>

<p>Then the summon failed because of a game error.</p>

<p>Where had everything become tangled?</p>

<p>Blood seeped beneath my shoes.</p>

<p>I looked down at the ground.</p>

<p>My reflection appeared in the red pool of blood.</p>

<p>I had a rather foolish expression on my face.</p>

<p>“……”</p>

<p>Fragments of thoughts poured through my mind.</p>

<p>I pieced the puzzles together one by one.</p>

<p>Everything strange from the moment I entered CallOVo until now…</p>

<p>And soon, I realized.</p>

<p>From the very beginning, this world of CallOVo had been clearly telling me what it wanted.</p>

<p>The moment I realized it, I kicked off the ground and ran forward.</p>

<p>The realization had come far too late.</p>

<p>The apostles noticed me and shouted happily.</p>

<p>“Little Lamb-nim!”</p>

<p>Their pure white apostle robes had been completely dyed red.</p>

<p>The two apostles smiled brightly while holding their swords.</p>

<p>“We have been desperately searching for you. The priestess is waiting for Little Lamb-nim.”</p>

<p>As expected, they did not block my way.</p>

<p>I ran into the cult building.</p>

<p>The hymn about everyone becoming one through the power of God endlessly drilled into my ears.</p>

<p>My breathing grew ragged, but I did not slow down.</p>

<p>I rushed all the way to the underground ritual chamber of the cult building.</p>

<p>The underground chamber was brightly lit by hundreds of candles.</p>

<p>Wax had melted and hardened messily across the floor, and enormous shadows danced across the walls following the flickering flames.</p>

<p>On the white marble altar stood Edel, wearing more extravagant clothing than ever before.</p>

<p>She looked up at the ceiling, where not even a ray of sunlight could enter, with an ecstatic expression.</p>

<p>“Edel!”</p>

<p>Edel turned toward me with her thin veil covering her eyes.</p>

<p>“Little Lamb-nim!”</p>

<p>Even though the lights made it impossible to see ahead, she recognized me only by my voice.</p>

<p>Her lips, smiling brightly, were bright red.</p>

<p>“Thank goodness. Truly, thank goodness. You arrived before it was too late. I was just about to begin the ritual.”</p>

<p>I asked her while gasping for breath.</p>

<p>“A ritual for the God of the Void?”</p>

<p>It was a meaningless question.</p>

<p>I already knew the answer that would come.</p>

<p>And she knew that I had realized it as well.</p>

<p>“Yes, Little Lamb-nim!”</p>

<p>Edel held an obsidian dagger and moved as if dancing lightly.</p>

<p>“The eternal abyss, the source of chaos, the absolute void! The terror that creates and destroys, the Lord of All Things!”</p>

<p>She looked at me and shouted.</p>

<p>“It is a ritual for Doctor Dominic!”</p>
