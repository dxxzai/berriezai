<h1>Chapter 144</h1>

<p>I froze, clutching my phone.</p>

<p>In reality, only a few seconds had passed.</p>

<p>A static glitch flickered across the screen, and the messages began to erase themselves.</p>

<p>They vanished in a flash, until the entire chat thread with Reporter Sa Gongyeon had disappeared.</p>

<p>Staring at the empty screen, I hurriedly took out the phone Samra had given me.</p>

<p>I was about to ask him right away if he knew Reporter Sa Gongyeon from Day News, but I paused.</p>

<p>There was no way Samra hadn't searched through my phone.</p>

<p>Samra had already thoroughly scoured all my personal information from the moment I was first dragged to the TRA.</p>

<p>If he had seen Reporter Sa Gongyeon's messages, he would have undoubtedly conducted an investigation regarding them.</p>

<p>However, the fact that there had been no mention of it until now meant Samra was unaware of it.</p>

<p>All electronic data existing on Earth was practically Samra's property.</p>

<p>It was illogical to miss a message transmitted through the communication network.</p>

<p>Just like the Creator Samra is currently tracking, it was highly likely that this was a problem involving Outer Gods.</p>

<p>‘Is Reporter Sa Gongyeon of Day News also connected to Outer Gods?’</p>

<p>Given that she mentioned the title ‘Little Lamb,’ there was no other answer.</p>

<p>A chill ran down the back of my neck.</p>

<p>My retirement coincided with the time the Trial Meteorites fell to Earth.</p>

<p>However, Reporter Sa Gongyeon began contacting me immediately after I retired.</p>

<p>It was highly probable that she had known me since before then.</p>

<p>In other words, it meant there was a Outer God that had been watching Earth and me even before the Trial Meteorites fell.</p>

<p>Since when, exactly?</p>

<p>Why me of all people?</p>

<p>My mind felt like it was being violently jumbled by confusion.</p>

<p>‘First, I should tell Samra.’</p>

<p>It seemed it might help in tracking the Creator.</p>

<p>I composed my ragged breathing. It was information that was difficult to handle, but I was able to remain more composed than I had anticipated.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh’s declaration that he was “not infected” felt like a preventive shot.</p>

<p>Thanks to that, my mind began to function rationally.</p>

<p>Suddenly, the name “Day News” struck me as familiar.</p>

<p>The newspaper the butlers in Clean3 had shown me, and the one I found in CallOVo, were also called “Day News.”</p>

<p>In CallOVo, Black Block-ssi had helped me through the “Life Advice” section of Day News.</p>

<p>'Come to think of it, since when has Black Block-ssi been watching me?'</p>

<p>It was probably since I closed DeZonDeal.</p>

<p>Come to think of it, this was also strange.</p>

<p>How did he notice my existence and keep an eye on me? Especially since he called himself a fan every time he contacted me.</p>

<p>'Should I make a call via Interstellar Communication to ask?'</p>

<p>However, I soon gave up on the idea.</p>

<p>I might unnecessarily raise his expectations regarding a sponsorship contract by communicating with him.</p>

<p>It seemed best to avoid associating with a <em>pervert</em> as much as possible.</p>

<p>In any case, I couldn't afford to waste any more of Je Hyun-oh's time, which was worth more than gold.</p>

<p>I took a deep breath and gathered my belongings.</p>

<p>I packed just a few clothes into a large sports duffel bag and put my old cell phone in as well.</p>

<p>Even after packing small items like my seal, bankbook, ID, and cards, the bag was still spacious.</p>

<p>Since there was nothing else to pack, I just needed to go visit CEO Cha in the hospital.</p>

<p>But I... kept hesitating.</p>

<p>I couldn't bring myself to leave, as if I were tied up with a rope.</p>

<p>I lingered aimlessly in the bedroom before finally heading to the living room.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh happened to be standing in front of the display cabinet, so I thought it was a good opportunity and asked him for a favor.</p>

<p>“Could you help me for a moment? I’d like to take the cloth off this.”</p>

<p>Since he was a human, he was in the perfect condition to help me.</p>

<p>With Je Hyun-oh’s help, I lifted the cloth covering the display cabinet.</p>

<p>And for the first time in four years, I faced the glass display cabinet.</p>

<p>Inside the large cabinet, which reached from floor to ceiling, were stored gifts and letters received from fans during my career as an pro gamer.</p>

<p>I took in the gifts one by one: paintings, character dolls, keyrings, photo books, and so on.</p>

<p>I couldn't help but chuckle at some of the gifts, like a doll modeled after the champion character I used to play, or a photo that superimposed my childhood face onto an athlete's uniform.</p>

<p>Everyone else must have forgotten them, but I was the only one still clinging to them, harboring lingering attachments.</p>

<p>Then I should have at least looked at them proudly, but I was too scared to do so, so I kept the cloth covering them.</p>

<p>I intended to finally sort them out this time... but it seemed I wouldn't be able to.</p>

<p>I wanted to remember them, even if it was just for me.</p>

<p>'Can I take everything with me?'</p>

<p>I had no idea if there would be enough space in the new accommodation.</p>

<p>If it was the size of the room in the quarantine room I used to stay in, it certainly wouldn't fit.</p>

<p>If it didn't work out, I figured I would just have to leave it here and keep paying the rent.</p>

<p>As I looked inside the display cabinet, I discovered an item placed in the very bottom corner.</p>

<p>It was the championship ring I had received at the last competition.</p>

<p>I had thrown away all the trophies and plaques, but I had kept that one after much deliberation.</p>

<p>However, I felt I could finally throw it away now.</p>

<p>I opened the cabinet and took out the ring. Its heavy weight felt like it was pressing down on my hand.</p>

<p>I held it for a moment, then threw the box and the ring into the trash can.</p>

<p>I felt Je Hyun-oh staring intently at me, but I pretended not to notice and slung my duffel bag over my shoulder.</p>

<p>&quot;I think we can go now.&quot;</p>

<p>I was about to leave first, but I stopped short of grabbing the front door handle.</p>

<p>I stood frozen in place in front of the entrance.</p>

<p>Long fingers gripped the back of my neck.</p>

<p>I flinched in surprise at the light grip, as if carefully controlling the force.</p>

<p>I looked up at the owner of the hand. Dark purple eyes were staring back at me.</p>

<p>Only then did I realize that my breathing sounded strange.</p>

<p>My body was trembling slightly as well.</p>

<p>I pressed my hand firmly against my chest and caught my breath.</p>

<p>Once I calmed down, I belatedly noticed the gaze observing me.</p>

<p>It was an eye that seemed to be gauging something.</p>

<p>I felt like I had made a slight mistake.</p>

<p>What Je Hyun-oh wanted was a strong being to enter &lt;Zero&gt; with him.</p>

<p>Recalling his preference for the young god, I opened my mouth as calmly as possible.</p>

<p>“...I forgot something, so I’ll just grab it and come out.”</p>

<p>I went back to the bedroom, put on a baseball cap and a mask, and stepped out.</p>

<p>A familiar sense of stability washed over me as my vision was obscured.</p>

<p>I was finally able to go outside. Having been at the TRA for so long, it seemed I had definitely lost my sense of reality.</p>

<p>When I stepped into my studio apartment, I felt as if I had woken from a dream.</p>

<p>Fidgeting with the brim of my hat, I wanted to return to the TRA as soon as possible.</p>

<p>Closing the front door and leading the way down the hallway, I asked.</p>

<p>“Lieutenant Colonel Je. Is it alright if we walk to Paseong? I have something to buy on the way.”</p>

<p>Mok Saehwa had sent me the location in advance. The excuse was a hospital visit, but the designated meeting spot was, as if it were only natural, the Paseong headquarters.</p>

<p>It wasn't that far from where I used to live.</p>

<p>Since it was about a 20-minute walk, I suggested walking to buy a hospital gift as well.</p>

<p>It meant leaving the car behind.</p>

<p>I figured if I asked the TRA, they would take care of collecting the car left in front of the apartment building.</p>

<p>I wanted to take a taxi on the way back.</p>

<p>Just as I was about to turn around because I hadn't received a reply from Je Hyun-oh, my bag strap was tugged.</p>

<p>Since I was carrying it diagonally, my body swayed.</p>

<p>Taking a step back, I looked at Je Hyun-oh with a puzzled expression.</p>

<p>“Goyo.”</p>

<p>“Yes.”</p>

<p>“Can you walk?”</p>

<p>“Huh? Of course...”</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh scanned me with his eyes. I continued speaking, still dazed.</p>

<p>“...I can walk...?”</p>

<p>His gaze turned to the hat and mask I was wearing, and then back to my eyes.</p>

<p>“Alright, then.”</p>

<p>Then, letting go of the bag strap with a casual flick, he said.</p>

<p>“Walk.”</p>

<p>I replied “Yes” and hurried my steps, afraid he might change his mind.</p>

<p>As I walked down the street, I suddenly realized that it had been a very long time since I had walked like this.</p>

<p>This was because I usually only went back and forth between my studio apartment and the nearby convenience store.</p>

<p>And after joining the TRA, I had only been wandering between my room and the Trial sites.</p>

<p>I glanced at Je Hyun-oh, thinking he might be feeling similar.</p>

<p>He seemed somehow on edge.</p>

<p>It wasn't outwardly obvious, but having become so accustomed to reading his moods, I got that impression for some reason.</p>

<p>Was he bothered by the crowds?</p>

<p>I kept an eye on Je Hyun-oh as I hurried my steps and quickly bought the get-well gift.</p>

<p>Once I bought the gift, Je Hyun-oh, who had remained silent until then, spoke for the first time.</p>

<p>“Are you buying that?”</p>

<p>“Yes. I think this will do.”</p>

<p>It might not be the most suitable get-well gift, but I was sure CEO Cha would like it.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh seemed to approve of my choice as well.</p>

<p>I tucked the large box under my arm and headed back toward Paseong.</p>
