<h1>Chapter 24</h1>

<p>Mo Haein didn't answer.</p>

<p>Maybe I'd been too vague. I made it crystal clear.</p>

<p>"I'm asking you to turn me into a zombie."</p>

<p>Finally, Mo Haein's lips parted. But instead of a reply, she let out a long sigh.</p>

<p>I'd expected that reaction. I pressed on.</p>

<p>"Zombies don't attack preemptively. That goes for people in the middle of zombification too. Once three hours pass after infection, the System classifies you as a zombie."</p>

<p>I'd learned this from doing every crazy thing imaginable to speed-clear the game.</p>

<p>An infected Player won't be attacked by zombies as long as they don't strike first.</p>

<p>The catch was that there was no cure — so it was only viable right before reaching the ending.</p>

<p>I'd even done multiple playthroughs where I deliberately got bitten before the finale.</p>

<p>This is the best option right now.</p>

<p>It would prevent Mo Haein from attacking her own teammates, and since I was the weakest link, it also meant I could avoid the boss zombie. A solid plan.</p>

<p>I'd repair the generator with Mo Haein while Kwak Hanmuk handled the boss zombie.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk listened quietly to my explanation, then asked calmly.</p>

<p>"So we ask the Lieutenant Colonel to take the boss, and you and I do the repairs?"</p>

<p>"We need the delivery items urgently, and the emergency generator could get damaged."</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh's attacks had a wide area of effect.</p>

<p>Since the boss fight would happen right next to the generator, a stray shot could destroy the machinery.</p>

<p>For similar reasons, we couldn't leave the repairs to Je Hyun-oh either. When it came to aggression, he was in a league of his own.</p>

<p>There was no way he'd sit still with a boss zombie lurking nearby.</p>

<p>The odds of him blowing up the entire underground section were roughly one hundred percent.</p>

<p>"Hmmmm..."</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk nodded deeply, as if he fully understood.</p>

<p>When Mo Haein shot him an uneasy look, he said exactly what she didn't want to hear.</p>

<p>"Then I should go zombie too."</p>

<p>"Kwak Hanmuk!"</p>

<p>"What? It's a solid plan."</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk nonchalantly twisted the knife in her gut.</p>

<p>"If the whole TRA special team turns into infected, they won't be able to execute us. They can't afford to lose two team leaders at once, can they?"</p>

<p>"That's nonsense...!"</p>

<p>"You can't die."</p>

<p>Mo Haein froze. She dropped her head low.</p>

<p>After a long silence, she called out to me like she was talking to herself.</p>

<p>"...Trainee."</p>

<p>"Yes."</p>

<p>"If we can't get the True Ending—"</p>

<p>"We will. Yi Gaon confirmed she can produce the vaccine within three days as long as the power supply is stable."</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>Mo Haein had two days left.</p>

<p>She'd have to spend about a day as a zombie, whether we liked it or not.</p>

<p>We could keep her under control for a day, but any longer than that wouldn't be good for her either.</p>

<p>You can't stay as a zombie too long.</p>

<p>Because of the nature of zombies — they don't feel pain. If she lost her reason, she might mutilate her own body without realizing it.</p>

<p>Even if the True Ending cured the infection, damaged bodies didn't regenerate.</p>

<p>My goal was to produce both the vaccine and the cure within three days and clear DeZonDeal.</p>

<p>"Captain. Trust me on this."</p>

<p>"You're as crazy as Kwak Hanmuk."</p>

<p>"Thank you."</p>

<p>"That wasn't a compliment."</p>

<p>"Sorry."</p>

<p>I corrected myself quickly, and Mo Haein let out a short laugh. Finally, she turned to Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>"Lieutenant Colonel."</p>

<p>The final decision was his. Je Hyun-oh stared at me in silence.</p>

<p>I tensed up naturally.</p>

<p>He was unpredictable — and with his goggles and gas mask hiding his face, I couldn't read his expression at all.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh was exactly the kind of person who might suddenly smash me into pieces with his metal hand without warning.</p>

<p>I was bracing myself for whatever emoticon he'd flash when his voice came through.</p>

<p>"Goyo."</p>

<p>Whenever Je Hyun-oh started a sentence by calling my name, it had never led to anything good.</p>

<p>I waited for what came next.</p>

<p>"If you wanted to die, you should've just said so."</p>

<p>His metal hand spread wide. The sharp tips glinted in the early morning light.</p>

<p>"I could've done it for you."</p>

<p>"I! I absolutely want to live."</p>

<p>At my quick response, Je Hyun-oh clacked his metal fingers together.</p>

<p>I swallowed dryly as he tapped his digits against each other.</p>

<p>His voice, like scraping the bottom of an abyss, drilled into my eardrums.</p>

<p>"Don't any of you die while I'm gone."</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk, Mo Haein, and I were reflected in his black goggles.</p>

<p>I could feel murderous intent radiating from him as he kept clacking his hand. It was aimed at the Trial. At the Adaptees.</p>

<p>"I went through the trouble of not killing you, keeping you alive..."</p>

<p>It wasn't an empty threat. No one would mistake Je Hyun-oh's words for a bluff.</p>

<p>"We'll manage."</p>

<p>"We'll be careful."</p>

<p>But the Captains answered with practiced nonchalance, as if they'd already sworn to die only by Je Hyun-oh's hand.</p>

<p>Something about their replies made me think Je Hyun-oh might have smiled briefly.</p>

<p>Of course, since his face was completely hidden, it was just a guess.</p>

<p>"Goyo. Tell me what to do."</p>

<p>"I'll organize the item list and locations for you to farm. One of the items is zombie blood — not from Dead Zombies, but regular zombie blood."</p>

<p>I scribbled the list on paper and pen I'd brought from the lab, and also sent him a Message.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>I'll send you the list through the System window too.</p></div>

<p>Given his physical structure, checking paper would be inconvenient.</p>

<p>I also had another motive: showing him how useful I could be.</p>

<p>Please don't kill me.</p>

<p>If I proved my worth now, maybe he'd spare me when he eventually left DeZonDeal.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh took the item list and the blood collection kit I'd brought from the lab, then headed for the emergency stairs.</p>

<p>The fifth floor, where the lab was located, had windows that couldn't be opened, so he'd need to go up to the rooftop to fly out.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk offered to open the door for him and followed along to see him off.</p>

<p>Right before opening the emergency stairwell door, Je Hyun-oh looked back at me.</p>

<div class="hyunoh"><p>~ ~</p></div>

<p>What the hell was that now.</p>

<p>I told myself not to get caught up in Je Hyun-oh's emoticons, but every time a new one appeared on his goggles, I ended up puzzling over its meaning.</p>

<p>The wave symbol probably meant he'd be back quickly.</p>

<p>And while I was still deliberating, Je Hyun-oh disappeared into the stairwell.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk went with him, leaving me alone with Mo Haein for a moment.</p>

<p>We sat down in a corner of the hallway.</p>

<p>"Should I let you bite me before Captain Kwak gets back?"</p>

<p>At my suggestion, Mo Haein let out a disbelieving laugh and grabbed my arm.</p>

<p>"Don't regret this, Trainee. I'm going to bite down hard."</p>

<p>"...Ah, could you use this hand instead?"</p>

<p>"Aren't you right-handed?"</p>

<p>I smiled sheepishly at her concern. Fortunately, she switched hands without protest.</p>

<p>She gripped my forearm and met my eyes.</p>

<p>"Here I go."</p>

<p>Mo Haein opened her mouth. Her neat teeth had sharpened at the tips.</p>

<p>Then she bit down gently. The sensation of her teeth touching my skin was ticklish.</p>

<p>When I flinched, she quickly pulled away.</p>

<p>"Does it hurt?"</p>

<p>I shook my head.</p>

<p>"Just ticklish. I'm fine."</p>

<p>"Okay."</p>

<p>She took a breath, then brought her lips back to my forearm.</p>

<p>And this time, without warning, she bit down.</p>

<p>CRUNCH. The sound of flesh being pierced rang clear.</p>

<p>At the same time, my heart hammered wildly.</p>

<p>Thump, thump — the pulse pounded in my ears like my heart was beating right next to my eardrums.</p>

<p>My vision darkened, and the System window appeared.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>You have been infected!</p></div>

<div class="systembox"><p>Zombification in progress. (Current zombification progress: 10%)</p></div>

<p>The fever hit immediately — or at least, that's what it felt like. Heat radiated from my body.</p>

<p>It really was different from the game. The process of becoming a zombie wasn't easy.</p>

<p>I breathed calmly and waited for my vision to return.</p>

<p>Fortunately, the world brightened again quickly.</p>

<p>But the pain in my forearm was still there.</p>

<p>Mo Haein was still biting my arm, breathing raggedly. She was fighting the urge to tear off the flesh. The veins standing out on her neck were visible.</p>

<p>"Captain."</p>

<p>"Hnngh... huuuh..."</p>

<p>"Captain Mo. It's okay. Take your time."</p>

<p>"Nngh..."</p>

<p>She squeezed her eyes shut. A short groan escaped her, and then — very, very slowly — she opened her mouth.</p>

<p>With a wet sound, Mo Haein pulled away from my arm.</p>

<p>Her mouth was covered in blood. She muttered, her voice thick with self-loathing.</p>

<p>"I almost actually ate you..."</p>

<p>"You did well."</p>

<p>I staunched the bleeding and quickly moved away from her. If I stayed close, she might actually take a bite.</p>

<p>I was clumsily wrapping a bandage around myself when Kwak Hanmuk appeared.</p>

<p>"What, already bit?"</p>

<p>He sauntered over and held out his arm to Mo Haein. "Me too."</p>

<p>"AAARGH!"</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk screamed. Mo Haein had bitten down without mercy, hard and fast.</p>

<p>Apparently it really hurt — Kwak Hanmuk's eyes welled up as he whined.</p>

<p>"Captain Mo... that fucking hurts..."</p>

<p>"Drama queen."</p>

<p>Mo Haein licked her bloody lips and shot back curtly. Then she looked at me steadily.</p>

<p>"...I'll go bandage up."</p>

<p>I quietly retreated further into the corner.</p>
<hr class="calibre3"/>

<p>Before entering the underground section, I stopped by to see Yi Gaon.</p>

<p>She tried to scream the moment she saw me, so I quickly said.</p>

<p>"I got bitten on purpose."</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>Yi Gaon stared at me, speechless, then lowered her voice to a shout.</p>

<p>"Why would you do that!"</p>

<p>I explained it was to avoid zombie attacks.</p>

<p>And before she could really get angry, I offered a bribe.</p>

<p>"Here."</p>

<p>"This is..."</p>

<p>It was the portable cassette tape player Kwak Hanmuk had given me.</p>

<p>I remembered Yi Gaon complaining to Yi Seon in the game that she wanted to listen to music, so I'd brought it along.</p>

<p>Since she was an NPC of this place, listening to music through a Player shouldn't cause any problems.</p>

<p>"You might get bored during your breaks."</p>

<p>Yi Gaon clutched the portable player tightly. Her knuckles went white, her hand trembling.</p>

<p>"If Roasted Chestnut doesn't come back..."</p>

<p>She made a terrifying threat.</p>

<p>"I'll release every virus in the lab."</p>

<p>"I... I'd rather you didn't."</p>

<p>"Then come back quickly."</p>

<p>"Yes. I'll be going."</p>

<p>If Tak Jucheol and Paeng Sangdo found out I'd been bitten, it would be a headache. I needed to keep it a secret from them for now, so I finished my greeting and disappeared.</p>

<p>But that wasn't the real reason I'd fled.</p>

<p>It was the thought that had surfaced in my mind.</p>

<p>I wanted to bite Yi Gaon.</p>

<p>I could still suppress it, but the moment that thought crossed my mind, I panicked — worried I might slip up — and got out of there.</p>

<p>The aggression zombies showed toward humans was an instinct, like hunger.</p>

<p>So this is the zombie heart.</p>

<p>A zombie's state of mind — something I'd never been able to feel in the game.</p>

<p>My heart pounding slightly, I pushed open the emergency stairwell door.</p>

<p>"You're here?"</p>

<p>Mo Haein, who'd been sitting on the stairs, stood up.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk, who'd been leaning against the wall, straightened up too.</p>

<p>He was carrying a small sling bag diagonally — inside were the items I'd asked him to bring.</p>

<p>The three zombies descended the stairs.</p>

<p>Emergency stairwells had fewer zombie spawns, but they weren't safe zones.</p>

<p>Starting from the second-floor landing, which Mo Haein and Kwak Hanmuk hadn't used, zombies appeared.</p>

<p>We passed them quietly and soon reached the first basement level.</p>

<p>The stairs leading to the second basement were blocked by rubble, so we'd have to go through the first basement interior to reach B2.</p>

<p>I planned to use the freight elevator.</p>

<p>Normally, you had to struggle through ventilation ducts, but now that I was a zombie, I could use the elevator.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk opened the door and stepped inside first, letting out a low whistle.</p>

<p>"Oh, a cafeteria."</p>

<p>The first basement emergency stairs opened into a cafeteria. Since we were zombies now, it seemed like an easy enough area to push through.</p>

<p>"We could farm supplies from the cafeteria while we progress. Let's take it slow and check other areas too. There's a freight elevator connected to the second basement. Inside that elevator—"</p>

<p>Zzt — a noise crackled.</p>

<p>I stopped mid-sentence and looked around.</p>

<p>That came from the PA speakers, didn't it?</p>

<p>I was wondering if I'd misheard when—</p>

<p>WHEEEEEOOOOM—!</p>

<p>A deafening siren started blaring.</p>

<p>The zombies that had been dormant in the cafeteria area all opened their eyes at once and rose.</p>

<p>Through the growls and groans of the zombies, a recorded female voice cut through the underground section.</p>

<p>An emergency warning broadcast, automatically triggered.</p>
<br/>
<em><p>"Attention. All researchers must evacuate the laboratory immediately. Current emergency generator output is below 3%. Estimated remaining power: 15 minutes."</p></em>
<br/>
<p>Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein both looked at me.</p>

<p>The broadcast continued.</p>
<br/>
<em><p>"After 15 minutes, isolation shutters will activate across all sectors, sealing the building. I repeat. All researchers must evacuate—"</p></em>
<br/>
<p>"Captains."</p>

<p>I gave them the new objective.</p>

<p>"We need to repair the emergency generator within the next fifteen minutes."</p>
