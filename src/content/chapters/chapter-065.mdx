<h1>Chapter 65</h1>

<p>I had no idea that a system window would appear for someone who was registered.</p>

<p>Not only was it not anonymous, it was a real-name system.</p>

<p>‘Wait, I don’t think the registration window appeared when it was Park Seonggyeon.’</p>

<p>Since he hadn’t shown any particular reaction, the registration notification probably hadn’t appeared.</p>

<p>But even if I assumed the registration window didn’t appear because Park Seonggyeon was only a sample, when I registered Je Hyun-oh….</p>

<p>“……”</p>

<p>I stopped thinking about it halfway through.</p>

<p>No matter what, Je Hyun-oh had to be treated as an exception.</p>

<p>‘And honestly, registration isn’t the only problem.’</p>

<p>In Clean3, I had used the Divine Protection ability to stop the battle with the butlers.</p>

<p>I had already prepared a lie to explain that.</p>

<p>“Well… I’m not completely sure either, but I think it has something to do with the bound item I recently obtained.”</p>

<p>I rummaged through my inventory and took out the Happy Smile Candy.</p>

<p>Then I solved both the registration and Divine Protection issues at once.</p>

<p>“It turns out this item can use a defensive ability. When I tried to activate it, it said I had to register the target first…. I was in a hurry, so I did it without asking for permission. I’m sorry.”</p>

<p>Since it was a bound item, the captains wouldn’t be able to check it anyway.</p>

<p>‘Sorry, Captains.’</p>

<p>It had been fine when the person involved was Dominic, but I felt a little guilty doing this to the captains.</p>

<p>Even after my explanation, Mo Haein and Kwak Hanmuk still seemed suspicious.</p>

<p>However, they didn’t press any further, as if they were willing to let it go.</p>

<p>I lowered my eyebrows and asked while pretending to look pitiful.</p>

<p>“Would it be okay if I use Divine Protection on the captains in the future as well…? I want to be helpful, even if it’s only something like this.”</p>

<p>Whatever part of my words he found amusing, Kwak Hanmuk smiled with his eyes narrowed.</p>

<p>“Chestnut is pretty admirable.”</p>

<p>Mo Haein didn’t smile. Instead, she gave me a strict warning.</p>

<p>“Only use it when you truly judge the situation to be dangerous. No matter how good it is, an item is still something from a Trial. There’s nothing good about using it frequently. Especially since you, trainee, tend to cause abnormal phenomena often.”</p>

<p>“Yes, I’ll be careful.”</p>

<p>After answering politely, I quietly shifted my gaze.</p>

<div class="hyunoh"><p>^ ^</p></div>

<p>By then, Je Hyun-oh had approached and displayed a smiling emoticon on his goggles.</p>

<p>He clearly knew that what I was saying right now was a lie.</p>

<p>Of course, like Je Hyun-oh, he didn’t bother correcting the truth.</p>
<div class="systembox"><p>Thank you, Lieutenant Colonel Je.</p></div>

<p>When I sent him a message with my gratitude, the emoticon changed.</p>

<div class="hyunoh"><p>ㅇ &lt;b</p></div>

<p>…Was that lowercase letter supposed to be a thumbs-up?</p>

<p>I wasn’t worried that Je Hyun-oh would reveal the truth to the captains, but I was worried he might bother me in some other way.</p>

<p>For example, throwing me at a monster whenever I used Divine Protection.</p>

<p>I would have to be even more careful from now on.</p>

<p>The captains seemed like they wanted to ask me about what happened after Clean3 as well, but right now clearing Sla2Plate was the urgent matter.</p>

<p>So they decided to put the questioning aside for now and proceed with the strategy.</p>

<p>“What jobs did everyone choose?”</p>

<p>The originally planned job choices were:</p>

<div class="fieldset"><p>Je Hyun-oh: Swordsman<br/>
Kwak Hanmuk: Mage<br/>
Mo Haein: Paladin<br/>
Won Taebeom: Berserker<br/>
Han Goyo: Chef</p></div>

<p>However, Dominic joined in.</p>

<p>Duplicate choices weren’t allowed, and we couldn’t discuss it with each other, so everything became completely tangled.</p>

<p>As a result, the final jobs were a mess.</p>

<div class="fieldset"><p>Je Hyun-oh: Berserker<br/>
Kwak Hanmuk: Paladin<br/>
Mo Haein: Swordsman<br/>
Dominic: Mage<br/>
Han Goyo: Chef</p></div>

<p>Everyone’s jobs except mine had changed.</p>

<p>When I learned that Je Hyun-oh had chosen Berserker, the captains and I couldn’t hide our troubled expressions.</p>

<p>Someone who was already not exactly sane choosing Berserker as his job as well.</p>

<p>It wasn’t a great start.</p>

<p>However, I didn’t bring up anything negative and only checked on the captains.</p>

<p>“Are you both okay with your jobs?”</p>

<p>“The Black Moon Blade is the most comfortable, but an ordinary sword is no problem either. Well, a glaive is also a bladed weapon.”</p>

<p>“…Wasn’t that a spear?”</p>

<p>Mo Haein gave a small laugh and explained the difference between a glaive and a spear.</p>

<p>I had thought they were similar because they were both polearms, but there was a difference.</p>

<p>I had played games with Eastern-style settings before, but I had never used a glaive, so this was something I was learning for the first time.</p>

<p>While storing this new piece of information about her away, I wondered if there were other things everyone else might have misunderstood as well.</p>

<p>I had always only watched them from the perspective of a game, after all.</p>

<p>Anyway, she could handle an ordinary sword well too.</p>

<p>As expected of Captain Mo, I thought as I looked at Kwak Hanmuk.</p>

<p>“Ah, I really wanted to play a mage. I even decided what spells I was going to use.”</p>

<p>Since he was talking nonsense as usual, he seemed fine too.</p>

<p>After organizing a rough plan in my head, I checked the time.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>8:00 AM</p></div>

<p>Quite a lot of time had passed while we were entering and discussing various things.</p>

<p>I quickly assigned everyone their tasks.</p>

<p>“Lieutenant Colonel Je, please take care of clearing the area around the restaurant. Captain Kwak, please gather materials around the village, and Captain Mo, you and I will clean the inside of the restaurant—”</p>

<p>“I’ll clean.”</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk shrugged.</p>

<p>“Captain Mo isn’t good at that kind of thing.”</p>

<p>“What? What are you—”</p>

<p>Mo Haein reacted as if she couldn’t believe it, then shook her head.</p>

<p>“Do whatever you want. I don’t care.”</p>

<p>I had no choice but to clean the inside with Kwak Hanmuk.</p>

<p>Since I wanted to delay telling Kwak Hanmuk about the slime for as long as possible, it felt like I had been assigned to a slaughterhouse.</p>

<p>Mo Haein headed toward the village, and Je Hyun-oh stood in the very center of the restaurant’s front yard.</p>

<p>He looked at me and waved his hand, telling me to move away.</p>

<p>I ran to the restaurant entrance so I wouldn’t be caught within his attack range, but I didn’t go inside yet and watched for a moment.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh lightly swung his metal hand.</p>

<p>The sight of a black trail following the movement of his hand was impressive enough to steal my gaze.</p>

<p>It would have been even cooler if the result wasn’t just cutting grass.</p>

<p>Rustle.</p>

<p>The weeds in the front yard were swept away in a rush.</p>

<p>I had already experienced how excellent his metal hand was for gardening during Clean3.</p>

<p>I thought he would be good at mowing grass too, and I was right.</p>

<p>I clapped and added another task for Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>“Lieutenant Colonel Je! Please do the backyard too!”</p>

<p>Then I gathered up the grass he had cut from the ground.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Grass Leaf: A useless piece of grass. Burn it away.</p></div></div>

<p>After picking up around 100 pieces and putting them into my inventory, I entered the restaurant.</p>

<p>Bbyorong, a cheerful notification sound rang out.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Ugh! It’s covered in dust. Let’s clean the inside of the restaurant too!</p></div></div>

<p>Looking at the system window reminded me of something I had forgotten.</p>

<p>‘Come to think of it, it’s quiet.’</p>

<p>It used to constantly chatter about this and that, but it had been quiet since Clean3. Even now that the system window had recovered normally, it still wasn’t saying anything.</p>

<p>There had been more than enough time for it to make useless comments.</p>

<p>I felt the soft sensation of the slime resting on top of my head.</p>

<p>‘Don’t tell me… is it being careful because of Dominic?’</p>

<p>The moment I decided that was a fairly convincing guess, the system window suddenly appeared with a pop.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>You reflect on your own reckless actions. Despite committing something extremely dangerous, you feel deep relief at the fact that you survived.</p></div>

<div class="systembox"><p>This was because all of the power was exhausted for your sake—</p></div>

<p>It poured out letters as if it were angry, then suddenly cut off.</p>

<p>I called for the system window in my mind.</p>

<p>‘Why did you stop talking?’</p>

<p>However, there was no response.</p>

<p>I thought back over what the system window had said.</p>

<p>What did it mean that it exhausted its power for me?</p>

<p>I couldn’t guess, since I didn’t remember receiving any particular help.</p>

<p>I didn’t think I had done anything especially reckless either.</p>

<p>The one thing that was certain was that it didn’t have enough strength to nag at me for the time being.</p>

<p>I whispered to the pudding slime on my head.</p>

<p>“Dominic-ssi. Did you see that?”</p>

<p>“Do you mean the dust? I saw it clearly. This place certainly requires cleaning.”</p>

<p>Dominic’s ears drooped as he looked disgusted in a dignified manner from atop my head.</p>

<p>‘Can he only see quest windows?’</p>

<p>It seemed like he couldn’t see things like the system window speaking on its own or notifications that appeared as the Trial progressed.</p>

<p>“Goyo! I’ll clean the second floor!”</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk suddenly poked his head out from the stairs and shouted.</p>

<p>“Yes! I’ll do the first floor!”</p>

<p>I shouted back to him.</p>

<p>‘That startled me.’</p>

<p>Because I had something to feel guilty about, I was getting surprised every time Kwak Hanmuk said something.</p>

<p>I wanted to ask Dominic about Kwak Hanmuk, but I didn’t know when he might come back down, so it seemed too risky to talk.</p>

<p>I decided to be patient and focus on cleaning first.</p>

<p>Before properly starting the cleaning, I chose the table that looked the least damaged and cleaned it thoroughly.</p>

<p>I also found a large glass measuring cup and washed it until it was spotless.</p>

<p>“There’s going to be a lot of dust, so please excuse me for a moment.”</p>

<p>Then I turned the measuring cup upside down on the table and trapped Dominic inside.</p>

<p>The measuring cup was exactly the right size for Dominic.</p>

<p>Seeing the pudding slime trapped helplessly inside the glass measuring cup gave me an incredible sense of security.</p>

<p>Dominic drooped his ears inside the measuring cup.</p>

<p>“This place is…. a little uncomfortable, but I will wait.”</p>

<p>After opening the windows and shaking out the dust inside first, I searched around and found a bundle of paper cups.</p>

<p>I filled the paper cups with tap water, lined them up on the table, and dropped two pieces of grass into each one.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Made Grass Leaf Tea.</p></div>

<p>I checked the item description.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Grass Leaf Tea (0G): Tea made by adding two pieces of grass to plain water.
(Review: Ugh…. Who would drink this?)</p></div></div>

<p>How dare it call my masterpiece “ugh”!</p>

<p>‘They don’t know how precious drinks are.’</p>

<p>I looked proudly at the 50 cups of Grass Leaf Tea.</p>

<p>Dominic, who had been quietly wriggling inside the measuring cup while watching me, called out to me.</p>

<p>“Roasted Chestnut….”</p>

<p>The tone of his voice as he stared up at me was unusual.</p>

<p>Wondering what was wrong, I jumped in surprise and waved my hands around.</p>

<p>“D-Dominic-ssi, it’s not that! Absolutely not! I only made this as practice. Do you really think I would give something like this to you, Dominic-ssi? To my precious and only contract partner?”</p>

<p>I even stumbled over my words because I was answering so hurriedly.</p>

<p>I repeatedly reassured Dominic.</p>

<p>“I’ll make you a proper, delicious meal, Dominic-ssi, so please wait just a little longer.”</p>

<p>“Yes, Roasted Chestnut. I will look forward to it.”</p>

<p>Dominic replied while flicking his ears.</p>

<p>The gesture was so cute that I wanted to bite him, but it also sent chills down my spine.</p>

<p>Taking care of a pudding slime was not easy.</p>

<p>The 50 cups of Grass Leaf Tea were priced at 0 gold because there were no customers who would pay money to buy them.</p>

<p>‘But if there are no customers who would buy it, then I’ll just make them myself.’</p>

<p>Just as I equipped myself with the mindset of a merchant, Kwak Hanmuk came down to the first floor.</p>

<p>His gaze locked onto the table.</p>

<p>…Oh no.</p>

<p>I had forgotten to release Dominic.</p>

<p>I hurriedly pretended to organize the Grass Leaf Tea and stood the glass measuring cup upright, allowing the pudding slime to escape.</p>

<p>Dominic, who had been stuck to the glass, flipped over along with the measuring cup before slowly wriggling his way out.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk’s eyes followed Dominic exactly.</p>

<p>In a voice that sounded like he was questioning his own sanity, he pointed at the slime and asked.</p>

<p>“Chestnut-ah. Am I the only one who can see this?”</p>

<p>He had seen the pudding slime since the briefing, but it seemed like he had stayed silent because no one else was showing any strange reaction.</p>

<p>At that moment, Dominic crawled up onto me.</p>

<p>“Crazy! That thing again!”</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk hurriedly reached out and tried to grab the slime climbing over me.</p>

<p>It was such a quick movement that I didn’t even have time to stop him.</p>

<p>My heart dropped.</p>

<p>But then—</p>

<p>“Huh?”</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk’s hand passed straight through the slime’s body and touched my forearm.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk waved his hand around several times, but every time, he failed to grab anything and only brushed against my arm.</p>

<p>As I flinched in surprise, Dominic calmly explained.</p>

<p>“Roasted Chestnut. You do not need to worry about me. Unless someone is my contractor, they cannot touch me carelessly.”</p>

<p>‘You should have told me that sooner.’</p>

<p>I couldn’t bring myself to get angry at Dominic, so I only cursed internally.</p>

<p>At least, I confirmed two things.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk couldn’t touch Dominic.
He couldn’t hear Dominic’s voice either.</p>

<p>I immediately discarded the excuses I had been thinking of: “A popular squishy toy was newly released as a Trial item,” and “I couldn’t abandon a baby slime that lost its parents in an accident, so I decided to raise it.”</p>

<p>Then I quickly created a new explanation.</p>

<p>“Why are you doing that?”</p>

<p>I looked at Kwak Hanmuk with trembling eyes, pretending to be worried.</p>

<p>He blinked in confusion.</p>

<p>“No, don’t you see this squishy thing?”</p>

<p>“No. I don’t see anything.”</p>

<p>I asked back with concern.</p>

<p>“Captain Kwak…. Are you okay?”</p>
