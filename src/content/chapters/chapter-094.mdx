<h1>Chapter 94</h1>


<p>Jang Seokyun looked out the window.</p>

<p>Outside the Director's office window at the Trial Response Agency, the view was always the same.</p>

<p>Since this wasn't the main headquarters building, the familiar scenery he always looked upon wasn't there.</p>

<p>Even so, Jang Seokyun had no trouble picturing it in his mind.</p>

<p>With the main headquarters currently partially destroyed, they were temporarily using the Gyeonggi Regional Office building in Gwacheon.</p>

<p>The damaged headquarters was scheduled to be restored as quickly as possible with Samra's technical guidance.</p>

<p>At the same time, they were also handling the reconstruction of the buildings CEO Cha and Je Hyun-oh had destroyed, erasing civilians' memories, and controlling the spread of information.</p>

<p>As the Agency's workload had increased dramatically, Samra's workload had become equally overwhelming.</p>

<p>If Samra had been human, it would have been a workload no one could have endured.</p>

<p>Because Samra could process hundreds of tasks simultaneously through parallel computation, the Agency had been able to weather this terrorist attack without suffering major damage.</p>

<p>And that wasn't all. If Samra were ever to stop functioning, humanity's defense against the trials would immediately be compromised.</p>

<p>CEO Cha likely knew that as well, which was why he'd torn apart the Agency's buildings while deliberately avoiding inflicting serious damage on Samra.</p>

<p>Jang Seokyun turned away from the window.</p>

<p>His eyes met Samra's as the android stood before him with impeccable posture.</p>

<p>Even now, while reporting to him, Samra was simultaneously handling countless other tasks.</p>

<p>"Samra."</p>

<p>Looking into the holographic-colored eyes before him, Jang Seokyun asked,</p>

<p>"Do you have some objection to my decision?"</p>

<p>An AI shouldn't possess subjective emotions.</p>

<p>And yet, at this moment, he couldn't help but ask.</p>

<p>"I do not."</p>

<p>Samra continued in an even voice.</p>

<p>"My earlier recommendation was merely based on the judgment that your actions could provoke unnecessary resentment from Han Goyo-ssi."</p>

<p>"I trust you, Samra, but when it comes to Trainee Han, I have no choice. Haven't you lost your objectivity?"</p>

<p>Samra displayed an unusual tendency to favor Han Goyo alone.</p>

<p>Because of that, whenever the matter concerned Han Goyo, they deliberately discounted part of Samra's judgment.</p>

<p>Samra did not answer, but Jang Seokyun calmly continued as though accustomed to the silence.</p>

<p>"Besides, Lieutenant Colonel Je clearly called him 'the clue.'... Samra, I have no choice but to secure Trainee Han."</p>

<p>It wasn't only Han Goyo's ability to close trials.</p>

<p>There were simply too many things connected to him.</p>

<p>Jang Seokyun absolutely could not afford to let him slip away.</p>

<p>That was why he had deliberately targeted what was likely Han Goyo's greatest weakness.</p>

<p>The reason he'd first placed him on a one-month team hadn't been solely because of the trial closures.</p>

<p>When Han Goyo had first been brought into the Agency under suspicion of being a national-level disaster, Jang Seokyun had received a report on him.</p>

<p>An orphan, a career cut short by injury, years spent in complete isolation afterward.</p>

<p>It only took a few lines of that report to understand what a twenty-four-year-old in his circumstances would long for most.</p>

<p>Believing Han Goyo needed to become part of the Agency, he had formed the team and intentionally given him time to grow attached to it.</p>

<p>Years of observing every type of person imaginable had given him confidence.</p>

<p>Han Goyo would never abandon the Special Mission Team.</p>

<p>"Surely the most important thing is making sure Trainee Han remains with the Agency. Even if I have to resort to underhanded methods."</p>

<p>"……."</p>

<p>"And that's what you want as well, Samra."</p>

<p>Instead of answering, Samra gazed into empty space.</p>

<p>Recognizing it as the behavior Samra displayed while processing important information, Jang Seokyun waited.</p>

<p>"Director."</p>

<p>A moment later, Samra slowly lowered his gaze to meet Jang Seokyun's eyes.</p>

<p>Then he said something Jang Seokyun had never anticipated.</p>

<p>"It appears Han Goyo-ssi intends to sign with Paseong."</p>

<p>"...What?"</p>

<p>Jang Seokyun's expression froze instantly.</p>

<p>"Han Goyo-ssi is currently using Director Ham Jiwol's device to contact Director Mok Saehwa of Paseong. I intercepted the call. Han Goyo-ssi informed Director Mok Saehwa that he wishes to enter into a contract with Paseong."</p>

<p>Jang Seokyun already knew that Ham Jiwol and Mok Saehwa were close.</p>

<p>What bewildered him was how Han Goyo had managed to obtain Ham Jiwol's phone in the first place.</p>

<p>He had watched Han Goyo break down with his own eyes.</p>

<p>That had been exactly his intention, which was why he had deliberately chosen such cruel words.</p>

<p>Jang Seokyun had used similar methods in the past to recruit compatible Adaptees into the Agency.</p>

<p>No matter how favorable the contract offered, someone would eventually defect if another organization offered better terms.</p>

<p>So instead, he had shaken them psychologically until they bound themselves to the Agency.</p>

<p>Han Goyo was only in his early twenties, with almost no real experience in society.</p>

<p>After applying that much pressure, Jang Seokyun had been certain Han Goyo would willingly sign the Agency's contract.</p>

<p>That assumption had proven to be a serious miscalculation.</p>

<p>Even so, he quickly composed himself.</p>

<p>'Han Goyo would never abandon the Special Mission Team.'</p>

<p>He didn't know how Han Goyo had persuaded Ham Jiwol, but it had to be some clumsy attempt at resistance.</p>

<p>"It seems I'll have to go speak with Trainee Han."</p>

<p>Samra, who had once again been staring into empty space, lowered his gaze.</p>

<p>"I do not recommend it. The call has ended, and Director Mok Saehwa has contacted CEO Cha. I predict CEO Cha will arrive at Han Goyo-ssi's hospital room within one minute."</p>

<p>"...Ha."</p>

<p>A short laugh escaped Jang Seokyun.</p>

<p>There was nothing to gain from letting CEO Cha witness him forcing Han Goyo into a contract.</p>

<p>"Samra. What would you advise?"</p>

<p>As always, Samra's judgments were those of an AI seeking the optimal outcome for humanity.</p>

<p>Whether to accept those judgments was ultimately Jang Seokyun's decision, but in practice he relied on them heavily.</p>

<p>With an expressionless face, Samra answered,</p>

<p>"I recommend negotiating with CEO Cha of Paseong, rather than with Han Goyo-ssi."</p>

<hr class="calibre3"/>

<div class="fieldset"><p>♡♡Director Mok Saehwa♡♡</p></div>

<p>That was how Mok Saehwa's contact was saved in Ham Jiwol's phone.</p>

<p>The unusual contact name made me briefly wonder about the relationship between the two women, but I quickly dismissed the thought.</p>

<p>The farther I stayed from Ham Jiwol, the better.</p>

<p>Heart pounding, I pressed the call button.</p>

<p>The call connected before the ringtone had even finished sounding.</p>

<p>—Directooor Haaam!</p>

<p>The overly cutesy voice nearly made me drop the phone.</p>

<p>Holding it carefully with both hands, I said,</p>

<p>"Director Mok. This is Han Goyo."</p>

<p>—...Goyo-nim?</p>

<p>Mok Saehwa called my name as though she couldn't believe it.</p>

<p>—Is this really Goyo-nim? You didn't have to cut off an arm for Director Ham just to borrow her phone, did you...?</p>

<p>She was so shocked by the fact that I was using Ham Jiwol's phone that she immediately assumed I'd paid an unbelievable price for it.</p>

<p>Just borrowing the phone cost an arm?</p>

<p>Suddenly, giving up only 400 milliliters of blood felt like an extremely reasonable deal.</p>

<p>I assured Mok Saehwa that both of my arms were still attached.</p>

<p>Then I told her I wanted to discuss signing with Paseong and asked if I could meet CEO Cha in person.</p>

<p>Mok Saehwa immediately let out a cheer.</p>

<p>Even over the phone, I could vividly hear how delighted she was.</p>

<p>—Of course! I'll send the CEO over right away!</p>

<p>Both she and I knew CEO Cha was currently being detained by the Agency.</p>

<p>Neither of us acted as though that was any sort of obstacle.</p>

<p>Before I could change my mind, Mok Saehwa hurriedly ended the call.</p>

<p>—Just wait a moment, Goyo-nim.</p>

<p>After ending the call with Mok Saehwa, I really did wait "just a moment."</p>

<p>Not long afterward, the hospital room door slid open.</p>

<p>The fact that it opened immediately without so much as a knock told me exactly who it was, though I was still surprised.</p>

<p>'That was fast.'</p>

<p>The Agency's original building had been damaged, so they were temporarily operating out of another facility.</p>

<p>Naturally, security wasn't as tight as before.</p>

<p>Samra had probably helped to some extent, but even then, I hadn't expected him to arrive this quickly.</p>

<p>In truth, even before calling Mok Saehwa, I'd already contacted CEO Cha with Samra's help.</p>

<p>The call to Mok Saehwa had simply been a performance for the Director's benefit.</p>

<p>CEO Cha had agreed to hear my proposal in detail, and that was why he'd come to my hospital room.</p>

<p>I found myself wondering how he'd managed to get here so quickly.</p>

<p>'Did he beat up every Agency soldier on the way over? Ah... or was it the perception-suppression mask?'</p>

<p>The level of perception suppression could be adjusted, and at 100%, one's presence disappeared entirely.</p>

<p>I honestly hoped he'd show me that trick someday.</p>

<p>Keeping my noisy thoughts to myself, I greeted my guest while sitting on the bed as the patient I was supposed to be.</p>

<p>After thinking it over until the very last moment, I offered what I felt was the most polite and appropriate greeting.</p>

<p>"Thank you for coming, CEO Cha."</p>

<p>I'd tried to keep a straight face, but I worried my eyes might still have revealed just how happy I was to see him.</p>

<p>I'd already acted suspiciously friendly toward him before, so I was trying to be more careful from now on.</p>

<p>CEO Cha was still wearing his tracksuit.</p>

<p>The headphones that had been around his neck, however, were gone.</p>

<p>Apparently they'd been confiscated when he'd allowed himself to be detained.</p>

<p>'Daeil Yeorae is probably stored away in his inventory.'</p>

<p>Instead of returning my greeting, CEO Cha pulled over a folding chair and sat down beside the bed.</p>

<p>It happened to be the exact same spot where Director Jang Seokyun had been sitting earlier, giving me an odd feeling.</p>

<p>"……."</p>

<p>CEO Cha folded his arms and silently stared at me.</p>

<p>As I looked back at the white mask, I remembered what Samra had told me.</p>

<p>Apparently, after I'd coughed up blood and collapsed, Je Hyun-oh and CEO Cha had immediately called a truce.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh was one thing, but I'd never imagined CEO Cha would quietly allow the Agency to detain him.</p>

<p>Had he simply decided saving my life came first?</p>

<p>'...No. More importantly, a corpse can't sign a contract.'</p>

<p>Correcting my thoughts in a more realistic direction, I opened my mouth.</p>

<p>"About the proposal I made..."</p>

<p>"How's your body?"</p>

<p>I was so startled that my words stopped on their own.</p>

<p>For a moment, I wondered if I'd heard him correctly.</p>

<p>'Did he just ask how I was doing...?'</p>

<p>As I merely stared at him in disbelief, CEO Cha leaned back in his chair.</p>

<p>The chair creaked beneath him as he spoke quietly.</p>

<p>"Did your eardrums stop working?"</p>

<p>I answered immediately.</p>

<p>"They're perfectly fine. Ah, both my eardrums and the rest of me."</p>

<p>Then I once again corrected my thoughts in a more realistic direction.</p>

<p>'Right. I'd need to be healthy enough to sign the contract.'</p>

<p>Thinking back to the Body, Mind, and Soul Waiver Contract I'd glimpsed before, it made perfect sense.</p>

<p>Having convinced myself of that, I finally relaxed—</p>

<p>"You've been acting like you've known me for a long time."</p>

<p>CEO Cha's next remark made me tense up all over again.</p>

<p>He looked at me from an angle, one hand tucked into his windbreaker's pocket.</p>

<p>I quickly put on my best business smile.</p>

<p>"CEO Cha is extremely famous."</p>

<p>"For someone who's just heard of me, you're awfully enthusiastic."</p>

<p>"...Well."</p>

<p>I'd thought I'd hidden it, but apparently it had still shown.</p>

<p>CEO Cha questioned me as though the slightest hint of suspicion might make him split me in half with Daeil Yeorae.</p>

<p>"Well?"</p>

<p>"The truth is... I..."</p>

<p>With no other choice, I told the lie that was closest to the truth.</p>

<p>"The moment I met you, I became a fan at first sight! You're just that cool."</p>

<p>CEO Cha said nothing.</p>

<p>It felt less like he believed me and more like he'd decided to let it slide because it was too absurd.</p>

<p>Still, he didn't seem to dislike hearing it.</p>

<p>'Maybe... he secretly likes being complimented.'</p>

<p>In any case, the atmosphere wasn't bad, so I cautiously brought up the real subject.</p>

<p>"As I mentioned before, I'd like to draw up a 'Temporary Personnel Dispatch Agreement' with Paseong."</p>
<br/>
<ol>

  <li><p>I agree to be dispatched on behalf of Paseong for a set number of missions.</p></li>

  <li><p>I inform Director Jang Seokyun that I intend to sign with Paseong.</p></li>

  <li><p>Feeling the pressure, Jang Seokyun is lured into negotiating with CEO Cha through Samra.</p></li>

  <li><p>In exchange for stepping aside so that Han Goyo can sign with the Agency, CEO Cha demands immunity from punishment for this terrorist attack, along with the 'Temporary Personnel Dispatch Agreement.'</p></li>
  </ol>



<p>The agreement will follow the terms I laid out in step one.</p>


<ol start="5">
  <li><p>After CEO Cha and Jang Seokyun complete their agreement, I sign with the Agency.</p></li>
</ol>


<br/>
<p>I'll be staying with the Agency from here on anyway, so there was nothing to gain by becoming openly hostile with Jang Seokyun.</p>

<p>If things went according to plan, I could avoid a major conflict with Jang Seokyun while CEO Cha would also receive immunity.</p>

<p>'The compensation owed to the Agency can be handled by the wealthy CEO Cha.'</p>

<p>On top of that, by showing Jang Seokyun—who was convinced I'd ultimately sign with the Agency—that another possibility genuinely existed, I'd be able to negotiate far more favorable terms for myself.</p>

<p>'From that point on, I'd be the one with the upper hand.'</p>

<p>As Director of the Trial Response Agency, Jang Seokyun would be forced to negotiate with me respectfully rather than resorting to threats.</p>

<p>And since my Epic Quest required me to enter as many trials as possible anyway, a dispatch agreement wasn't a bad deal for me.</p>

<p>Even if the Special Mission Team remained together, entering seven trials in the span of two months would be difficult.</p>

<p>I intended to borrow Paseong's strength to make that happen.</p>

<p>The only uncertain part of my plan was CEO Cha.</p>

<p>"The condition is that I'll enter and close whatever trials Paseong chooses. How does five deployments sound?"</p>

<p>I couldn't help worrying whether he'd agree so readily.</p>

<p>As I anxiously waited for his answer, CEO Cha spoke about something entirely different.</p>

<p>"The last day of the broadcast."</p>

<p>CEO Cha, who had been lounging casually in his chair, leaned toward me.</p>

<p>"The one talking to the Creator... that was you, wasn't it?"</p>
