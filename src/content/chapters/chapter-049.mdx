<h1>Chapter 49</h1>

<p>I shouted desperately.</p>

<p>If Dominic joined the cult, he'd climb straight to Cult Leader.</p>

<p>If he summoned an Outer God — I didn't want to imagine what would happen.</p>

<p>I had to take him as a companion. No matter what.</p>

<p>Dominic's eyes widened slightly at my shout.</p>

<p>But then he readily accepted the offer.</p>

<p>"Very well."</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>'Dr. Dominic' has joined as a companion.</p></div>

<p>He agreed without a second thought, which caught me off guard instead.</p>

<p>He had no idea what I might make him do.</p>

<p>"Aren't you going to ask what it involves?"</p>

<p>"It doesn't matter."</p>

<p>A choice appeared.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Explain what mission you will assign to Dr. Dominic.<br/>
&#45; Tell him to just follow along quietly.<br/>
&#45; Tell him he won't come to any harm.<br/>
&#45; Tell him he might have to kill someone.<br/>
&#45; Tell him to join the Cult of the Void Apostles with you.</p></div></div>

<p>I picked the third.</p>

<p>"Even if it involves killing?"</p>

<p>Dominic smiled as if amused by the question.</p>

<p>"Because the only thing that matters to me here is you, Roasted Chestnut."</p>

<p>A vague answer, yet clear in its meaning.</p>

<p>The being before me was merely imitating humans through learned behavior.</p>

<p>Having lost his memories, Dominic didn't know what he truly was. He simply copied human conduct wholesale — expressions, gestures, habits, turns of phrase, even how to react in any given situation.</p>

<p>But none of that touched his essence.</p>

<p>And Dominic felt a certain fondness for me — the only entity in CallOVo who recognized his peculiarity.</p>

<p>It was a basic, shallow sort of liking. But even that was enough to make him prioritize me over every other NPC in CallOVo.</p>

<p>'Then maybe I can use him for combat?'</p>

<p>Originally, Dr. Dominic was a support-type companion NPC.</p>

<p>But the current Dominic possessed tremendous power and had no qualms about killing. If I asked him to eliminate someone, he'd do it.</p>

<p>If Dominic fought well under my direction, I could cut down one of the companion NPCs needed for clearing the game.</p>

<p>Originally, I was supposed to recruit a retired soldier NPC as a combat companion — but convincing him was a nightmare. If I could skip that step entirely, my clear time would drop dramatically.</p>

<p>"What do we need to do?"</p>

<p>Lost in thought about how best to utilize Dominic, I snapped back at his question and began my belated explanation.</p>

<p>"I came to Morneveil to investigate a cult called the Cult of the Void Apostles."</p>

<p>As I lectured him about how evil the Morneveil cult was, Dominic listened quietly.</p>

<p>Not because he was outraged by atrocities like human sacrifice — his eyes simply showed curiosity about what his role would be.</p>

<p>When I explained that the final goal was to investigate the cult, gather evidence, and expose their brutal reality, Dominic gave a light round of applause.</p>

<p>I put on my most solemn expression.</p>

<p>"This could be extremely dangerous. So, Dr. Dominic — how about staying with me at the hotel while I'm in Morneveil?"</p>

<p>Naturally, I wasn't worried about Dominic getting hurt. I needed him for protection.</p>

<p>And I figured it was better to keep him close where I could watch him rather than leave him to his own devices.</p>

<p>"I'd be happy to. But as you know, I can only stay in spaces that accommodate me."</p>

<p>Dominic pointed at himself with a joking gesture.</p>

<p>A 3-meter body didn't exactly have many comfortable options.</p>

<p>But I had a solution.</p>

<p>"Dr. Dominic."</p>

<p>"Yes, Roasted Chestnut?"</p>

<p>"Do you have any money?"</p>

<p>I squeezed Dominic for funds to use as operating capital.</p>

<p>He might kill me later when he got his memories back.</p>

<p>But then again —</p>

<p>'Given everything I've done so far, he'd probably kill me even if I didn't take his money.'</p>

<p>So I figured I might as well commit to the con and take his cash.</p>

<p>Dominic readily agreed to become my wallet.</p>

<p>As Morneveil's only psychiatrist, Dominic's wealth was considerable.</p>

<p>I wondered if it was money the real Dominic had accumulated — but no.</p>

<p>"My patients tend to rely on me quite a bit."</p>

<p>The patients who'd fallen for the fake Dominic had showered him with all kinds of offerings.</p>

<p>He'd even received a car as a gift….</p>

<p>I stared blankly at the gleaming black sedan in his garage.</p>

<p>Considering that cars were exorbitantly expensive luxury items in CallOVo's world, it was no exaggeration to say his patients were giving him their everything, heart and soul.</p>

<p>He was already living like a cult leader in all but name.</p>

<p>If a Dominic like this joined the cult, he'd spread his wings and soar.</p>

<p>The future where he rocketed straight to Cult Leader was crystal clear.</p>

<p>'No way in hell.'</p>

<p>I moved to the hotel with Dominic.</p>

<p>It was still auto-movement, but thanks to recruiting Dominic as a companion, this time I didn't have to walk — we took his car.</p>

<p>Arriving at the hotel in supreme comfort, I walked boldly up to the check-in counter.</p>

<p>And made my demand to the employee.</p>

<p>"I'd like to change rooms. To the most expensive suite you have. Oh, and since we're adding one more guest, please include an extra breakfast."</p>

<p>The employee's face registered bewilderment.</p>

<p>Since this hotel was run by the cult, they knew full well I was a broke journalist.</p>

<p>So when I suddenly asked to switch to the priciest suite, he looked completely thrown.</p>

<p>But I needed the hotel's suite.</p>

<p>It had multiple rooms so several people could stay comfortably, and crucially, the suite had higher ceilings than the other rooms — Dominic wouldn't have to hunch over.</p>

<p>The employee was eyeing me suspiciously when —</p>

<p>He suddenly burst out.</p>

<p>"Oh, my God! Dr. Dominic!"</p>

<p>Spotting Dominic entering late after parking the car, the employee brightened up.</p>

<p>He leaned almost half his body over the counter to greet him.</p>

<p>Dominic accepted the employee's fawning welcome with gentlemanly poise.</p>

<p>"It's been a while. Have you been well?"</p>

<p>"Of course! I can't believe Dr. Dominic remembers me… I thought you'd have forgotten someone like me…"</p>

<p>The employee's gaze turned somewhat dreamy. A wide grin stretched across his face.</p>

<p>"So you know Little Lamb. What a remarkable connection."</p>

<p>When the employee heard that Dominic would be staying with me during my time in Morneveil, he gave me the room upgrade at half price.</p>

<p>Delighted at the massive savings, I happily moved to the new room.</p>

<p>This was a suite I normally wouldn't have been able to access until the very late game, so staying here from the early stages felt incredibly luxurious.</p>

<p>"Thank you, Dr. Dominic. I owe you."</p>

<p>"Not at all."</p>

<p>Dominic surveyed the living room — spacious enough to host a sports day — then asked my permission.</p>

<p>"Would it be alright if I changed a few pieces of furniture?"</p>

<p>Ah.</p>

<p>I'd been so fixated on the ceiling height that I hadn't thought about the furniture.</p>

<p>There wasn't a single piece in the suite that Dominic could use.</p>

<p>"Of course. But even if we order, it'll take a few days, so for now we could connect the sofas and—"</p>

<p>I was about to suggest using the sofas as a temporary solution when I couldn't finish my sentence.</p>

<p>Creeeak, creeeak, creeeeak.</p>

<p>The furniture groaned as it twisted and grew.</p>

<p>Some pieces even changed material.</p>

<p>Within moments, the suite was filled with furniture transformed to match Dominic's size and taste.</p>

<p>Having casually accomplished the impossible, Dominic sat down on the sofa with satisfaction.</p>

<p>"I can't change everything — only things with simple structures."</p>

<p>As he spoke, his blue eyes sank with a dangerous glint.</p>

<p>"I feel like I might have been able to do even more in the past…"</p>

<div class="systembox">
<div class="systembox_wrap">
<p>
Dr. Dominic is recalling the past.<br/>
&#45; Tell him the past means nothing.<br/>
&#45; Compliment him — he's already amazing.<br/>
&#45; Ask if he remembers anything about the past.<br/>
&#45; <em>Tell him to join the Cult of the Void Apostles with you.</em>
</p>
</div>
</div>

<p>"You're already more than amazing as you are!"</p>

<p>I picked the second option, terrified he might start remembering the past, and praised Dominic with everything I had.</p>

<p>Even after the choice's dialogue, I kept heaping on compliments about his incredible talent until my mouth went dry. Dominic seemed pleased enough with my praise to offer a small smile.</p>

<p>"Well then, I'll tidy up my things and turn in early."</p>

<p>"Today was your first day in Morneveil. You must be exhausted. Get some rest, Roasted Chestnut."</p>

<p>Forced a smile at his gentle words, I grabbed my bag and fled to my bedroom.</p>

<p>Even after closing the bedroom door, I couldn't breathe easy for fear he might hear.</p>

<p>'Just how much can he do?'</p>

<p>He wasn't just a being with raw strength — that made him all the more terrifying.</p>

<p>I wanted to seal him away in CallOVo forever.</p>

<p>Once my pounding heart settled, I opened my bag, pulled out the contents, and scattered them around in a pretense of organizing.</p>

<p>Then I sat on the small sofa in the bedroom and did what I needed to do out of Dominic's sight.</p>

<p>'Can I check my own status window too?'</p>

<p>I'd been looking at everyone else's statuses all this time, but hadn't thought to check my own.</p>

<p>I called up my status window.</p>

<p>'Han Goyo status.'</p>

<p>A system window appeared before my eyes.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<div class="systembox_wrap">
<p>
&#45; Han Goyo (Little Lamb) ♡ x 1&lt;<br/>
&#45; Journalist<br/>
&#45; Acquired Items: Gold (9,999,999G).<br/>
&#45; Held Items: Happy Smile Candy, Portable Music Player, Signet Ring That Lost Its Master, Dark Star Fragment (1), Hologram Badge (1).<br/>
&#45; Current Status: Fear.
</p>
</div>
</div>
<p>CallOVo always gave you one extra life when you started.</p>

<p>You obtained additional hearts as you progressed through the game.</p>

<p>Right now, my respawn point was probably set to the hotel.</p>

<p>When I'd played CallOVo on the Cult Leader route, I'd set my respawn point to the cult's altar.</p>

<p>And I'd performed a miracle befitting a cult leader — dying and coming back to life.</p>

<p>'Anyway, I only have one heart now. I need to be careful.'</p>

<p>I skimmed through — there was no ability list.</p>

<p>It seemed I couldn't use Message, Execution, Prohibition, or Divine Protection on myself.</p>

<p>I'd been hoping a little for the Divine Protection ability, so I was disappointed. I checked the rest.</p>

<p>There was a new entry called Current Status, and the item list…</p>

<p>'What the hell is this gold amount.'</p>

<p>My eyes went wide.</p>

<p>I'd spent a decent chunk upgrading the hotel room, yet my gold was maxed out.</p>

<p>Which meant my holdings exceeded the display limit.</p>

<p>'No way… Is this because of Dominic?'</p>

<p>When he'd agreed to become my wallet, it seemed Dominic's gold had merged with mine.</p>

<p>All this absurd amount of money came from his patients — I had no idea what he'd been doing in CallOVo.</p>

<p>I wouldn't be surprised if he'd already been running his own cult.</p>

<p>Checking the Held Items below, I spotted an unfamiliar item.</p>

<p>It was the ring the butlers had given me.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Signet Ring That Lost Its Master: A signet ring once used by someone noble. Those who are not the owner feel a deep sadness when wearing this ring.</p></div></div>

<p>It was called a signet ring, but there was no engraving on it.</p>

<p>Even after reading the item description, I couldn't figure out its purpose.</p>

<p>I studied the plain ring for a moment, then put it back in my inventory.</p>

<p>Sorry, butlers.</p>

<p>Your Master isn't coming home. He'll be living in CallOVo forever.</p>

<p>I apologized silently to the Cat Butler and Rabbit Butler, vowing never to take out the signet ring again.</p>

<p>Then I pulled out the hologram badge Samra had given me.</p>

<p>It had been an inactive item, but once I entered the Trial, it became usable.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Hologram Badge: All things in this world are connected as one.</p></div></div>

<p>I activated it and read the description, but it was equally incomprehensible.</p>

<p>Giving up on deciphering it, I attached the badge to the inside of my clothes where it wouldn't be visible.</p>

<p>I had no idea what it was for, but wearing it couldn't hurt.</p>

<p>Deciding I'd sorted things out well enough, I wrapped up the first day.</p>

<p>I changed into pajamas and lay down on the bed. A system window appeared.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Would you like to sleep?<br/>
&#45; Yes.<br/>
&#45; No.</p></div></div>

<p>I selected 'Yes,' and my vision went dark.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Day 1 in Morneveil has ended.</p></div>

<p>It had been eventful, but I felt like I could manage somehow.</p>

<p>I ended the first day on a positive note.</p>

<p>And then, on the second day.</p>

<p>I died.</p>
