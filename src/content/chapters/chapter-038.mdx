<h1>Chapter 38</h1>

<p>'What the hell? Did the item not work?'</p>

<p>Startled, I turned the Good-Looking over in my hands.</p>

<p>The single-use item rusted blue before my eyes, its purpose spent.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Rusted Bronze Mirror: A hand mirror that has rusted over. Its surface needs to be polished.</p></div></div>

<p>Seeing the item description change, it had definitely been used.</p>

<p>'Did it break or something?'</p>

<p>My plan had gone sideways, but I could still get the True Ending without the Good-Looking.</p>

<p>'It'll take way longer than I expected, though...'</p>

<p>I'd just have to move as efficiently as possible.</p>

<p>I put the Good-Looking away and looked around.</p>

<p>The cleaners who'd been dragged to the mansion were slowly starting to accept reality.</p>

<p>They'd realized that resisting meant getting a ray gun hole through their body, so they stayed quiet, only rolling their eyes.</p>

<p>The cat butler waved his hand.</p>

<p>A roulette appeared before the cleaners' eyes.</p>

<p>"A bunch of rags wearing rags. At least show some basic decency! I'll only let those with fundamentals into the mansion."</p>

<p>Before entering the mansion, you had to spin the roulette three times.</p>

<p>In order: clothing, cleaning tools, and weapons.</p>

<p>The colored slots were divided into different sizes — the worse the item, the wider its slot.</p>

<p>For the clothing roulette, the Clean3 standard uniform — a tattered jumpsuit — sleepwear, and a large black garbage bag took up most of the space.</p>

<p>For the cleaning tool roulette, a straw, a lollipop, and an old feather pen.</p>

<p>But the weapon roulette was the real joke.</p>

<p>A bath duck, a puppy-shaped balloon, and a water gun took up two-thirds of it.</p>

<p>Decent weapon items barely had any space at all, and the best weapon — the ray gun — was just a hairline crack.</p>

<p>Even if you could get something usable from the clothing and cleaning tool roulettes, pulling something good from the weapon roulette was tough.</p>

<p>After checking my surroundings, I approached Mo Haein, who was closest.</p>

<p>"Captain Mo."</p>

<p>I called out in a whisper. She'd been frowning deeply at the roulette, but turned to me and whispered back.</p>

<p>"Trainee. Are you good at spinning the roulette?"</p>

<p>"I can get the items I want."</p>

<p>"...That's possible?"</p>

<p>I gave her a small nod, her expression skeptical, then spun my own roulette first to show her.</p>

<p>A cheerful jingle played as the roulette whirred.</p>

<p>The first spin was for clothing.</p>

<p>All kinds of clothes flashed by as the wheel spun, leaving rainbow-colored afterimages.</p>

<p>I pressed the stop button not long after.</p>

<p>The roulette slowed. The clustered colors separated, and the slots began to appear.</p>

<p>Tick, tick, tick...</p>

<p>The needle crossed slot after slot.</p>

<p>Finally, the roulette came to a complete stop.</p>

<p>It was a slot so thin it wasn't even a tenth the size of the others.</p>

<div class="fieldset"><p>Suit</p></div>

<p>Mo Haein stared at me, startled.</p>

<p>In front of her incredulous face, I spun the cleaning tool and weapon roulettes in order.</p>

<div class="fieldset"><p>Wide-Area Scan Vacuum</p></div>

<div class="fieldset"><p>Ray Gun</p></div>

<p>The roulette stopped exactly on the items I wanted.</p>

<p>Honestly, with a bit of visual acuity and reaction speed, it wasn't that hard.</p>

<p>The roulette wasn't incredibly fast, and each item was marked with a different color.</p>

<p>Plus, the time it took for the wheel to stop after pressing the button was always the same.</p>

<p>All you had to do was memorize which color matched which item and figure out the pattern of how many seconds it took to fully stop.</p>

<p>I explained the method to Mo Haein. She listened quietly, then muttered.</p>

<p>"I'll just wear the maid outfit..."</p>

<p>Turns out she'd already tried something similar.</p>

<p>She hadn't known about the stopping time like I did, but she'd tried to match the colors.</p>

<p>Problem was, hitting a slot smaller than the maid outfit was hard.</p>

<p>"Want me to try spinning yours?"</p>

<p>Just to see, I tried spinning Mo Haein's roulette.</p>

<p>I thought it wouldn't work, but somehow, the roulette whirred.</p>

<p>The stop button even pressed down.</p>

<p>Both of us stared at the roulette with the same expression: why does this work?</p>

<p>Anyway, I spun Mo Haein's roulette three times.</p>

<p>Thanks to that, she ended up in a clean suit with the latest cleaning tools and weapons, her expression noticeably brighter.</p>

<p>"Where's Captain Kwak? Did he already spin?"</p>

<p>We looked around for Kwak Hanmuk together, but he came sauntering over to us first.</p>

<p>He was already fully equipped with clothes and weapons.</p>

<p>'A shirt and pants set, a high-end broom, and a ray sword?'</p>

<p>They weren't as good as the items I'd pulled for Mo Haein, but they were still excellent.</p>

<p>More than enough to clear without any issues.</p>

<p>Mo Haein looked at Kwak Hanmuk with surprise and asked.</p>

<p>"Did you spin the roulette?"</p>

<p>"No."</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk raised an eyebrow and said matter-of-factly.</p>

<p>"I took it from someone else."</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>Apparently, when a fight broke out over items, he'd jumped right in and won.</p>

<p>As long as you got the items, it didn't matter how. Good that it worked out.</p>

<p>But something caught my eye.</p>

<p>'The Bead Rosary...?'</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk had the Zelkova wood Bead Rosary wrapped around his wrist.</p>

<p>There shouldn't have been any reason to take it out this early. Strange.</p>

<p>Come to think of it, he'd had it on his wrist from the start when we entered DeZonDeal too.</p>

<p>It was a bit odd, but I figured the Captain had taken it out because he needed it.</p>

<p>Finally, I looked for Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>"You!"</p>

<p>The cat butler shouted.</p>

<p>I snapped my head around — he was pointing at Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh hadn't spun the roulette yet, it seemed; his clothes were unchanged.</p>

<p>"Do you have a cleaning tool?"</p>

<p>He hadn't even spun the clothing roulette, so of course he wouldn't have a cleaning tool.</p>

<p>But Je Hyun-oh shamelessly raised his metal hand.</p>

<p>A tool about as far from cleaning as you could get.</p>

<p>'Is he about to get a hole in his forehead?'</p>

<p>I watched the scene with a bit of concern.</p>

<p>"Good. Perfect."</p>

<p>'Wait, what.'</p>

<p>The cat butler clapped his hands.</p>

<p>"You may enter the mansion. Go and receive your assignment."</p>

<p>I felt a deep sense of betrayal at the cat butler's words.</p>

<p>'Anyone can see he doesn't fit the mansion!'</p>

<p>A cleaner in techwear with a metal hand, cleaning a stately old mansion. Ridiculous.</p>

<p>'And how the hell do you clean with a metal hand in the first place?!'</p>

<p>The butlers must have no eyes, because they clearly couldn't see properly.</p>

<p>But whether I was baffled or not, Je Hyun-oh — passed by the cat butler — slipped into the mansion alone.</p>

<p>I never knew the exception due to BT-Z virus infection would apply to something like this.</p>

<p>Cursing the eyeless butlers under my breath, I watched Je Hyun-oh's back — and then narrowed my eyes.</p>

<p>'But Lieutenant Colonel... there's something about you that feels similar to the butlers...'</p>

<p>The cat butler's and rabbit butler's metal heads felt similar to the metal that made up Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>I'd need to see it closer to be sure, but the material felt so alike you could call it the same metal.</p>

<p>I took just a few steps closer to get a better look at the butlers.</p>

<p>And then — my eyes met the rabbit butler's.</p>

<p>Well, they don't have eyes, so that's not quite the right expression, but it felt like that.</p>

<p>I quickly put on a smile.</p>

<p>The most important thing in Clean3 wasn't the roulette draw, the cleaning, or even catching creatures.</p>

<p>It was getting on the butlers' good side.</p>

<p>Offend them, and it was a straight shot to the ray gun hole ending.</p>

<p>I smiled at the rabbit butler with a cultured expression, signaling that I'd already fully adapted to this mansion.</p>

<p>I figured that would be enough to pass.</p>

<p>"...!"</p>

<p>The moment I felt his gaze linger longer than expected, the rabbit butler charged toward me at full speed.</p>

<p>A 2.5-meter living creature barreling through the cleaners blocking his path, slamming into them — my skin crawled.</p>

<p>Thoughts raced through my head.</p>

<p>'Should I run?'</p>

<p>But then I'd have to give up on the True Ending.</p>

<p>'I've never done anything to upset the butlers.'</p>

<p>I'd played Clean3 plenty of times. That much I was sure of.</p>

<p>I was wearing the best clothing item — the suit — and hadn't caused any major disturbance.</p>

<p>'They definitely won't kill me.'</p>

<p>If I was wrong, I'd end up with a hole in my head, but I was certain.</p>

<p>I pushed down my immediate fear and gambled on holding my ground.</p>

<p>Then the rabbit butler's mouth stretched into a wide grin.</p>

<p>"Roasted Chestnut!"</p>

<p>He shouted joyfully, grabbed me, and lifted me up in one motion.</p>

<p>My view suddenly shot upward.</p>

<p>The rabbit butler held me up and turned to the cat butler, calling out.</p>

<p>"Look at this! It's Roasted Chestnut!"</p>

<p>"No way!"</p>

<p>The cat butler immediately strode over in quick steps.</p>

<p>He checked my face and let out an exclamation.</p>

<p>"Well, well. If it isn't Roasted Chestnut!"</p>

<p>The cat butler, who'd been carrying himself with dignity until now, started rambling in a rather excited and chatty tone.</p>

<p>"Have you been well? I was worried when there was no word from you. I missed you too. You could have at least sent a message! But a sudden visit makes the reunion all the more joyful and delightful, I suppose. Haha, Roasted Chestnut, if you planned this, I must say you're quite clever."</p>

<p>Then he grabbed my limp hand and shook it politely, asking.</p>

<p>"You've come to become the mansion's butler this time, haven't you?"</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>I couldn't answer.</p>

<p>My mind felt frozen solid.</p>

<p>The rabbit butler and cat butler stared at me, unresponsive.</p>

<p>The rabbit butler turned to the cat butler and said.</p>

<p>"Work talk the moment we meet. The worst."</p>

<p>"Ah... My apologies, Roasted Chestnut. I got ahead of myself."</p>

<p>The cat butler offered an elegant apology and led me along.</p>

<p>"Let me make it up to you. Instead of this, let's go inside and talk. For today, I'll leave the cleaner selection to the underlings."</p>

<p>The cat butler looked toward the mansion and called out.</p>

<p>"Alpha!"</p>

<p>Sparks crackled in the air, and a man with a metal human head appeared.</p>

<p>Like the butlers, he had no eyes — only a mouth full of sharp teeth. He was the mansion's footman.</p>

<p>"You called, Head Butler?"</p>

<p>"Take over here. I must entertain an old friend who's come to visit after a long time."</p>

<p>Having handed the work off to the footman, the cat butler headed toward the mansion alongside the rabbit butler, who was still holding me.</p>

<p>I slowly turned my gaze.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein were staring at me in shock.</p>

<p>Looking at their pale faces, I realized something belatedly.</p>

<p>From the moment I'd entered the Trial until now — not a single system window had appeared.</p>

<p>No Clean3 entry notification. No Main Quest notification.</p>

<p>Nothing except the system window I'd called up to read the item description.</p>

<p>It was like I'd stepped into a game where all the endings had already been cleared...</p>

<p>'But this isn't a game.'</p>

<p>I tried to steady my increasingly ragged breathing.</p>

<p>'This is reality.'</p>

<p>Not a dot-graphic world inside a computer — this was the real world I lived in.</p>

<p>'Then why.'</p>

<p>My heart kept pounding wildly at the incomprehensible situation.</p>

<p>"Roasted Chestnut?"</p>

<p>The rabbit butler called my name, sensing something was off.</p>

<p>Nothing would be stupider than showing my fear and anxiety here.</p>

<p>I forced a smile.</p>

<p>And squeezed my voice out.</p>

<p>"It's been a while, Head Butler, Vice Head Butler."</p>

<p>The creatures, who didn't understand human emotions well, were delighted even by my terribly shaky voice.</p>

<p>"That's right. It's been a while. Let's prepare a feast for Roasted Chestnut tonight."</p>

<p>"A feast. An excellent idea."</p>

<p>"Roasted Chestnut. What do you want to eat? I recommend lamb rib steak."</p>

<p>"You enjoyed the lamb rib steak before, didn't you! Ah, and Roasted Chestnut, the mansion has become quite decent while you were away. We've hired a good chef and several footmen."</p>

<p>The rabbit butler and cat butler chattered on excitedly, telling me all sorts of things.</p>

<p>I kept my stiff smile plastered on as I looked up at the mansion.</p>

<p>It was a colossal estate, the perfect size for beings standing about three meters tall.</p>

<p>Even though I'd been in and out of this place countless times in the game, the mansion filling my view felt endlessly foreign.</p>

<p>The rabbit butler and cat butler grinned in unison, their mouths stretching wide.</p>

<p>"There's so much I want to talk about."</p>

<p>"I feel the same! Let's enjoy our reunion all through the night."</p>

<p>I was dragged into the grand mansion by the butlers.</p>
