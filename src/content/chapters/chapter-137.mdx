<h1>Chapter 137</h1>

<p>Cease to exist...?</p>

<p>Forgetting the pain for a moment, I looked at Dominic.</p>

<p>He slowly released the heart he had been clutching.</p>

<p>My body trembled at the sensation of his hand slipping out.</p>

<p>When the tip of his last finger slipped out, I couldn't hold back and gagged right then and there.</p>

<p>Dominic held my forearm to prevent me from falling.</p>

<p>Since I hadn't eaten anything, nothing came out.</p>

<p>I rubbed my wet lips with the back of my hand.</p>

<p>To think he put his hand into a living person's body.</p>

<p>It was something that far exceeded the limits of what I could understand.</p>

<p>It was even more horrifying that, despite feeling the sensation of him digging in, not a single scar remained on my chest.</p>

<p>However, I couldn't protest to Dominic.</p>

<p>It was because he looked angrier than I had been.</p>

<p>Dominic seemed genuinely angry about me being hurt and wounded.</p>

<p>I was speechless, having seen myself in a state that looked like broken pottery pieced back together.</p>

<p>Furthermore, having heard the phrase 'cease to exist,' I was honestly terrified.</p>

<p>I hesitated for a moment before asking him.</p>

<p>&quot;Please explain in detail what you mean.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Do you remember the day we first met?&quot;</p>

<p>Of course I did. It was a memory I could never forget.</p>

<p>At that moment, Dominic had <em>looked into</em> me.</p>
<br />
<p><span class="flashback">&quot;Little Lamb?&quot;</span></p>

<p><span class="flashback">&quot;If you'll pardon me, but that name doesn't suit you.&quot;</span></p>
<br />
<p>And he had seen right through my real name and mentioned it.</p>

<p>The memory of how I had tried so hard back then to somehow frame Dominic as a human was vivid.</p>

<p>&quot;Even back then, Roasted Chestnut was already in this state.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You mean I was this broken...?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yes. There were many wounds not only on the outside but also on the inside. It got worse after that.&quot;</p>

<p>He mentioned it briefly, but I could immediately recognize the cause of the worsening condition.</p>

<p>It was because he had repeatedly killed and revived me in the ending of CallOVo.</p>

<p>However, I later made a contract with Dominic, and he wanted to help me in various ways as his contractor.</p>

<p>‘So that’s why he fed me Infernum.’</p>

<p>He had attempted to heal me with the flames of Infernum.</p>

<p>Thanks to that, my wounds were slowly healing, but it seemed I had completely messed things up by using Resurrection.</p>

<p>“Punishment wouldn’t have wounded Roasted Chestnut, and it is an ability that would have been useful in the future. The same goes for Blind Faith and Summon.”</p>

<p>Seeing Dominic summon me here, it certainly looked convenient.</p>

<p>Punishment was a cool ability, too.</p>

<p>Honestly, I needed at least one offensive skill to help me survive.</p>

<p>Blind Faith…</p>

<p>‘I’m sorry, Lieutenant Won.’</p>

<p>I felt like I would really have to bow my head in apology once I got outside.</p>

<p>Although, I’d probably be dragged away in a straitjacket before that.</p>

<p> “To think you chose Resurrection for the sake of such insignificant creatures.”</p>

<p>Dominic seemed very displeased that I had erased my other abilities to create Resurrection.</p>

<p>But I had no choice.</p>

<p>At that moment, what I wanted most was Resurrection.</p>

<p>I wasn't entirely sure, but it seemed that to possess the ability of Resurrection, I had no choice but to erase something else.</p>

<p><em>I hadn't grown enough yet to hold multiple abilities simultaneously.</em></p>

<p>As I remained silent, having nothing to say to Dominic, he rose to his feet.</p>

<p>“Things like this will happen again in the future.”</p>

<p>Dominic looked up at the sky.</p>

<p>The world had changed.</p>

<p>“…!”</p>

<p>I was in space.</p>

<p>Countless stars embedded in pitch-black darkness, incomparable to the night sky.</p>

<p>As the massive band of the Milky Way cut across the darkness, the stars shone in various colors and sizes.</p>

<p>In the distance, nebulae and star clusters emitted a mysterious light.</p>

<p>As I gazed at the wondrous beauty, I discovered a pale blue planet.</p>

<p>It was Earth.</p>

<p>Dominic reached out his hand toward me. As I took his hand, a new sight unfolded before me.</p>

<p>I widened my eyes.</p>

<p>Meteorites appeared around Earth.</p>

<p>As if summoned from somewhere, dozens, even hundreds, of meteorites appeared in sequence and fell toward Earth.</p>

<p>The Trial Meteorites were falling.</p>

<p>The endless barrage of falling stars left me breathless.</p>

<p>“It is the not-too-distant future. I sped time a little faster for your understanding, Roasted Chestnut, but… the flow of events will likely be similar.”</p>

<p>There were already countless Trial Meteorites existing on Earth.</p>

<p>Although the majority were unhatched, humans were unable to properly deal with even the tiny fraction of Trials that had hatched.</p>

<p>But so many more meteorites going to fall?</p>

<p>If they hatch the moment they collide with the surface, just like Cell-X…</p>

<p>Nothing came to mind except the destruction of the Earth.</p>

<p>&quot;The gods from the outside will come to Roasted Chestnut's planet. There will be some even stronger than the god Roasted Chestnut faced this time.&quot;</p>

<p>Dominic asked me a question.</p>

<p>&quot;Can you protect them? With an ability like Resurrection?&quot;</p>

<p>I could not answer.</p>

<p><em>I have to protect them.</em></p>

<p><em>I have to protect my subordinates, and my domain.</em></p>

<p>While I was lost in thought watching the meteors fall, the scene before my eyes changed.</p>

<p>Before I knew it, I was back at the Black Sea.</p>

<p>&quot;Roasted Chestnut.&quot;</p>

<p>Listening to the sound of the rough waves, I stared blankly up at Dominic.</p>

<p>&quot;If you wish to protect Roasted Chestnut's planet even without Punishment, it is time to make a choice.&quot;</p>

<p>He placed the bead in my hand once again.</p>

<p>&quot;Whether to grow and move forward, or to stop and cease to exist. I leave the choice to Roasted Chestnut.&quot;</p>

<p>Dominic's words ended there.</p>

<p>He remained silent, as if to say he would not intervene any further.</p>

<p>I gazed at the candy-shaped orb resting on my palm.</p>

<p>“…….”</p>

<p>I had a strong feeling that eating this would surely change something.</p>

<p>Though it wasn't really a feeling.</p>

<p>‘It would be stranger if nothing happened.’</p>

<p>What would the system have said?</p>

<p>Seeing how quiet it was, it seemed its power had run out again.</p>

<p>Or perhaps it couldn't interfere because this was Dominic's domain.</p>

<p>I had to make a choice entirely on my own.</p>

<p>Suddenly, I remembered the system window that had appeared when I cleared CallOVo and left.</p>
<br />
<p><span class="flashback">[You are the Young God of the planet ‘Earth’.]</span></p>
<br />
<p>Back then, I found the title of 'young god' absurd.</p>

<p>I regarded it as some kind of second class advancement and firmly defined myself as a human.</p>

<p>Besides, being in charge of Earth? For me, an unemployed person struggling just to manage a single studio apartment, it was a very vague statement. But now I know.</p>

<p>That I had become a being beyond human.</p>

<p>Every time I recalled the memories of speaking and acting as a <em>young god</em>, it was unbelievably chilling.</p>

<p>I also had a vague sense that the Earth was <em>my domain</em>.</p>

<p>Why me, of all people?</p>

<p>I was most curious about why the Creator chose me.</p>

<p>The ongoing Epic Quests and the system were also questionable.</p>

<p>The system offered a perfect body as the final reward for the epic quest, while threatening limb amputation and eye gouging for failure, all while leading me along.</p>

<p><em>I did not feel</em> that the system was the same entity as the Creator.</p>

<p>Amidst complex thoughts, I opened my mouth.</p>

<p>“If I eat this… will I drastically change?”</p>

<p>I wanted to live.</p>

<p>I wanted to eat the orb, if only to avoid ceasing to exist, but there was something weighing on my mind.</p>

<p>It was the fact that I did not know what kind of being I would become.</p>

<p>I did not want to think and act like a young god.</p>

<p>Consuming this would undoubtedly bring me… a step closer to becoming that sort of young god.</p>

<p>My hand trembled slightly. It was because thoughts I had blocked out suddenly flooded in.</p>

<p>I barely managed to push them back.</p>

<p>Dominic did not answer immediately.</p>

<p>Looking at the way he silently gazed at me, I felt for some reason that he was trying to understand me.</p>

<p>It must have been a frustrating situation for Dominic.</p>

<p>After all, I was choosing the difficult path when there was clearly an easy one available.</p>

<p>But I did not want to become a god with a hierarchy mindset like Dominic.</p>

<p>Whenever I recalled the moments I had recklessly pried into another's mind, or the sensation of trampling on No. 1 with my foot, I felt deeply... replused.</p>

<p>If I absolutely had to become a god, I wanted to be a god for the sake of those I cherished.</p>

<p>Dominic finally spoke after a long pause.</p>

<p>“There will be parts that won’t change even if you try.”</p>

<p>It was vague, but it seemed like the best answer for now.</p>

<p>It wasn't just <em>my subordinates and my domain</em> that I needed to protect.</p>

<p>I had to protect myself, too. </p>

<p>Looking at the orb once again, I let out a chuckle.</p>

<p>‘A choice, indeed.’</p>

<p>Wasn't there only one path from the very beginning?</p>

<p>After all, the alternative meant the Earth shattering and my own life ending.</p>

<p>But since they say there is a possibility that I won't go crazy, it was worth taking a gamble.</p>

<p>I can do it.</p>

<p>“Dominic-ssi.”</p>

<p>“Yes, Roasted Chestnut.”</p>

<p>“I want to ask just one last thing. Could you answer me honestly?”</p>

<p>“Go ahead.”</p>

<p>I asked the question I had been hesitating to ask.</p>

<p>“I want to know why you are so kind to me.”</p>

<p>“Roasted Chestnut, you are my contractor, and you are still young, aren't you?”</p>

<p>“Is that all?”</p>

<p>Dominic smiled silently.</p>

<p>As if he knew everything I feared and was afraid of.</p>

<p>He asked back in a whisper.</p>

<p> “Is that not enough?”</p>

<p>“…….”</p>

<p>It seemed he didn’t want to tell me yet.</p>

<p>When will I ever be able to understand him?</p>

<p>I had a feeling that it wouldn’t be easy, even in the future.</p>

<p>I brought the orb to my mouth.</p>

<p>However, I didn’t have the courage to eat it right away, so I just held it against my closed lips for a moment.</p>

<p>It clearly looked like candy, but when I touched it, I felt a damp sensation.</p>

<p>I lost the desire to eat it...</p>

<p>I let out a deep sigh inwardly.</p>

<p>“Roasted Chestnut.”</p>

<p>His prompt came—gentle, yet firm.</p>

<p>I parted my lips under his blue gaze. And I pushed the orb into my mouth.</p>

<p>The round shape touched my tongue, and soon it melted thickly.</p>

<p>In the midst of that, the sweet taste made me smile slightly.</p>

<p>I realized that he had truly made it easy for me to eat.</p>

<p>The candy melted quickly.</p>

<p>As I swallowed the last bit of sweetness, Dominic gently praised me.</p>

<p>“You worked hard.”</p>

<p>I replied, a little awkwardly.</p>

<p>“Thank you.”</p>

<p>His blue eyes gleamed as he reached out his hand to me.</p>

<p>Sensing what he intended to do, I turned my head slightly to avoid his touch.</p>

<p>Dominic gently coaxed me.</p>

<p>“Didn’t I promise it wouldn’t hurt? I’ll finish it while you’re asleep.”</p>

<p>“No, it’s fine. Just do it.”</p>

<p>Dominic looked at me as if I were a stubborn child.</p>

<p>But I was sick and tired of things I couldn't remember.</p>

<p>Dominic reached out his hand again.</p>

<p>“Then I will just cover your eyes.”</p>

<p>The moment his fingertips touched my eyelids, my vision went black. A low voice warned me.</p>

<p>“It will be quite long and painful.”</p>

<p>And so, I went through a terrible ordeal.</p>

<hr />

<p>Thunder roared across the black sky.</p>

<p>A blinding flash screamed through the night sky, dyeing the world blue.</p>

<p>Blue veins, tangled like balls of yarn, stretched out across the sky.</p>

<p>As the lightning struck the ground, fragments of deep blue lightning shot up in all directions like flames, accompanied by dazzling smoke.</p>

<p>Amidst the burning blue heat, Kwak Hanmuk sat slumped on the ground.</p>

<p>Warm blood flowing from his eyes and ears mixed with the pouring rain, staining him completely red.</p>

<p>In the past, and even now, as he relived it in his dreams.</p>

<p>He always had the same thought.</p>

<p>I want to die.</p>

<p>Yet, even death was a luxury denied to him.</p>

<p>Because he knew what would happen after he died.</p>

<p>There was nothing he could do.</p>

<p>Just helplessly crushed by an overwhelming, unknown entity.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk smiled bitterly even in his dream.</p>

<p>Although it was always the same nightmare, a feeling that never became familiar, no matter how many times he experienced it, weighed heavily on him.</p>

<p>However, it was bound to end soon.</p>

<p>As always, he waited to either wake up from the dream or repeat it over from the beginning.</p>

<p>Strangely, the dream did not end.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk looked up at the sky.</p>

<p>As if someone were calling him.</p>

<p>Even though the only thing continuously etched into the black sky was a deep blue flash, he could not take his eyes off it.</p>

<p>And finally.</p>

<p>He saw a radiant golden light.</p>

<p>A golden meteor shower with a long tail cut powerfully through the black sky and the blue lightning.</p>

<p>The moment he saw the falling meteor, he realized.</p>

<p>It was a star that had come to find him.</p>

<p>“…Gasp!”</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk snapped open and sat up.</p>

<p>His entire body was drenched in cold sweat. He covered his face with trembling hands.</p>

<p>It was a dream he always had. A memory of being trapped in a hellish place.</p>

<p>But the memory and the dream always ended in the same place...</p>

<p>For the first time, the dream continued. And an unfamiliar scene began.</p>

<p>It was a memory that did not exist for him.</p>

<p>Recalling the golden light etched into his mind, he muttered softly.</p>

<p>“Fuck.”</p>

<p>I just want to smoke one cigarette.</p>

<p>Ignoring the intense urge rising within him, he rubbed his face with his hand.</p>

<p>Then he looked around.</p>

<p>It was the infirmary of the TRA. It seemed that Cell-X had been cleared safely.</p>

<p>‘Goyo….’</p>

<p>The last thing he remembered was being wrapped up by a tentacle bastard.</p>

<p>The moment he saw that thing, he had instinctively known it was the same kind of entity as the one inside him.</p>

<p>He had lost consciousness feeling like his brain was being crushed, but when he actually woke up, his body was perfectly fine.</p>

<p>In fact, he felt better than he had in a long time.</p>

<p>It didn't feel like much time had passed since it had been cleared.</p>

<p>He decided he needed to check if Han Goyo was alright first.</p>

<p>It was just as he threw off the covers and stepped out of the hospital bed...</p>

<p>A screen appeared before his eyes.</p>

<p>—Captain Kwak Hanmuk.</p>

<p>The holographic-colored man on the screen stared at Kwak Hanmuk expressionlessly.</p>

<p>“Samra?”</p>

<p>He was puzzled; he knew Samra wasn’t the type to offer a warm greeting just because he had regained consciousness.</p>

<p>—Please come to the Research and Analysis Headquarters immediately. </p>

<p>As Kwak Hanmuk looked on, wondering what could be the matter right after waking up, Samra spoke with a hint of urgency.</p>

<p>—Han Goyo-ssi has been pronounced dead.</p>
