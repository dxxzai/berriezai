<h1>Chapter 68</h1>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Day 2 at the restaurant!<br/>
How about starting a new adventure tonight? Make sure to stay up late.</p></div></div>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Someone has come to the restaurant! They don't seem to be a customer, though....<br/>
Let's head down to the first floor.</p></div></div>

<p>I opened my eyes to the shrill notification sound.</p>

<p>'They're here.'</p>

<p>The moment I saw the system window in front of me, I knew the time had finally come.</p>

<p>Once you reach Level 10, the restaurant owner visits the next morning and grants you permission to enter the dungeon.</p>

<p>That's also when the daily quota system unlocks.</p>

<p>Just thinking about having to hand over my precious gold to the owner already made my heart ache.</p>

<p>If it weren't for the quota, there were countless things I could do with that money.</p>

<p>I was getting out of bed, still thinking about money.</p>

<p>"...?"</p>

<p>Dominic was nowhere to be seen.</p>

<p>After glancing around for a moment, I immediately sprang out of bed.</p>

<p>Then I tore through every corner of the room.</p>

<p>"Dominic-ssi? Where are you? Please answer me, Dominic-ssi...."</p>

<p>I muttered Dominic's name while searching everywhere, but he was nowhere to be found.</p>

<p>At least he wasn't in the room.</p>

<p>I hurriedly threw open the door and ran down the hallway.</p>

<p>'He's downstairs, right? He has to be.'</p>

<p>Dominic had never once left my side until now.</p>

<p>A terrible possibility filled my mind.</p>

<p>'If he got his memories back....'</p>

<p>I'd turned him into pudding, but he was still the most terrifying calamity of them all.</p>

<p>He would regain his memories someday—but not now.</p>

<p>I still hadn't come up with any countermeasures.</p>

<p>At this rate, I'd be the one who unleashed a calamity upon the world.</p>

<p>'And I already destroyed the place he could return to.'</p>

<p>Samra had said Clean3 had been shut down.</p>

<p>The butlers were ultimately beings bound by the Trial as well, so after pushing me into CallOVo after the ending, they had most likely disappeared along with Clean3.</p>

<p>If his memories returned now...</p>

<p>He wouldn't leave me alone. Or the Special Mission Team. Or the Trial. Perhaps not even the planet Earth itself.</p>

<p>The "Master" had made it abundantly clear in CallOVo what kind of being he truly was.</p>

<p>The sight of him slicing open his own palm with his own hand.</p>

<p>That moment when death and resurrection repeated over and over....</p>
<br/>
<em><p><span class="redtextitty">No! Don't think about it! No! No! No!</span></p></em>
<br/>
<p>Halfway down the stairs, I braced myself against the wall.</p>

<p>My vision swam, and I couldn't place my feet properly.</p>

<p>I desperately tried to steady my breathing, but my legs refused to cooperate.</p>

<p>"...!"</p>

<p>My body lurched.</p>

<p>Just as I was about to tumble down the stairs—</p>

<p>"My, my! Careful now."</p>

<p>Someone caught me.</p>

<p>"Your footing is rather unsteady."</p>

<p>I immediately froze with a foolish expression.</p>

<p>"Roasted Chestnut! Are you alright?"</p>

<p>A deep, pleasant voice asked in urgent concern.</p>

<p>Dominic was...</p>

<p>...on top of a table on the first floor.</p>

<p>Still in the form of a pudding slime.</p>

<p>The moment I saw his soft, squishy body, relief washed over me so strongly that my strength nearly gave out.</p>

<p>'There's still time.'</p>

<p>Only after regaining my composure did I look at the person supporting me.</p>

<p>A man with long, blazing crimson hair cascading down to his waist helped straighten me up.</p>

<p>Dark, sturdy horns rose from both sides of his head.</p>

<p>"Hoh. So you're the new chef. You're quite the young little one."</p>

<p>The man with reptilian eyes bared his teeth in a smile as his arrogant voice rang out.</p>

<p>"I am the master of this place, Infernum."</p>

<p>I stared blankly up at the owner of the restaurant.</p>

<p>This wasn't the worst-case scenario I'd feared.</p>

<p>But it wasn't what I'd expected either.</p>

<p>A slime and a fire dragon together... I never imagined I'd witness a combination like this in my life.</p>

<p>For now, I greeted him almost by reflex.</p>

<p>"O sovereign of the great fire dragons. This mortal humbly offers you greetings. I am Little Lamb."</p>

<p>The line had been drilled into me through endless playthroughs of the game.</p>

<p>Originally, it was dialogue you only learned late in Sla2Plate.</p>

<p>Only after completing various side quests and learning the proper etiquette for dealing with dragons would you obtain it. Yet I recited it the moment we met.</p>

<p>Satisfaction spread across Infernum's face.</p>

<p>He openly displayed his approval as he praised me.</p>

<p>"Impressive. You are one with knowledge."</p>

<p>His brilliant crimson eyes, like gemstones set into his face, rested upon me.</p>

<p>"But you are no mortal, are you? You are but a newborn child."</p>

<p>Calling a twenty-four-year-old grown man a newborn child didn't quite fit.</p>

<p>And then there was what he'd said about me not being mortal.</p>

<p>Infernum clearly perceived me as something other than human.</p>

<p>I recalled the new title I had received.</p>

<p>Young God....</p>

<p>Personally, it still didn't feel real to me, so I'd just been thinking of it as something like a second class advancement.</p>

<p>'Come to think of it, why haven't I gotten an Epic Quest yet?'</p>

<p>Surely closing three Trials wasn't the end of it, yet nothing else had happened.</p>

<p>Maybe it had something to do with the system window mentioning that its power had been exhausted.</p>

<p>Hoping the Epic Quest would arrive before I cleared Sla2Plate, I mulled over Infernum's words again.</p>

<p>Calling someone a Young God wasn't something an ordinary NPC could do.</p>

<p>'Then again, if he were an ordinary NPC, Dominic wouldn't have any reason to be here with him.'</p>

<p>In CallOVo, Dominic had treated NPCs as beings beneath even insects.</p>

<p>There had to be a reason he'd left my side and come downstairs to meet Infernum.</p>

<p>I voiced what seemed like the most reasonable conclusion.</p>

<p>"Pardon my impertinence, but... Your Majesty, are you perhaps a god?"</p>

<p>The moment he heard my question, Infernum burst into hearty laughter.</p>

<p>His flame-like, wavy hair swayed along with it.</p>

<p>"Hahaha! I am no god. But I am a being infinitely close to one."</p>

<p>Was it because he possessed a certain 'rank' that he could see Dominic?</p>

<p>I still didn't fully understand what rank actually meant.</p>

<p>The first thing that came to mind was something obvious and visible.</p>

<p>I compared Kwak Hanmuk's red hair to Infernum's crimson locks, but the feeling was completely different.</p>

<p>'Then again, Captain Kwak dyes his hair.'</p>

<p>I remembered him complaining that he needed to touch up his roots.</p>

<p>In any case, aside from having red hair, I couldn't think of any obvious similarities between the two.</p>

<p>At least, none that I knew of.</p>

<p>"Little one. How did you come to my restaurant?"</p>

<p>I pushed thoughts of Kwak Hanmuk aside and focused on Infernum again.</p>

<p>"I wished to become a chef, and so I came to reside within Infernum-nim's domain."</p>

<p>"Hmm."</p>

<p>Infernum narrowed his eyes before snapping his fingers.</p>

<p>Whoosh.</p>

<p>Flames burst into the air, and a contract appeared.</p>

<p>It was the Restaurant Lease Agreement.</p>

<p>Beneath the large text reading Monthly Rent: 1G, there was a line of tiny fine print.</p>

<p><div class="fieldset"><span class="smaller1">However, once business operations begin, both the payment schedule and amount of rent may change, and all such changes shall be determined solely by the owner.</span></div></p>

<p>The monthly rent of 1G only applied before the restaurant officially opened.</p>

<p>The moment I entered the dungeon, gathered ingredients, and began business, the monthly rent would become a daily quota, and that amount would continue increasing as more business days passed.</p>

<p>From then on, the true hellish business would begin.</p>

<p>If you failed to meet the daily quota, you had to offer one of your teammates as food for the monsters.</p>

<p>And in a five-person party, losing even a single member made clearing the upper floors of the dungeon impossible.</p>

<p>The moment someone died, the ever-increasing quota would become impossible to pay.</p>

<p>One by one, your teammates would be devoured by monsters until total annihilation became inevitable.</p>

<p>And that monster...</p>

<p>...was the fire dragon Infernum sitting before me.</p>

<p>Sla2Plate was, in truth, one enormous restaurant built for Infernum alone.</p>

<p>"Little one. Have you looked over the contract carefully?"</p>

<p>With a heavy heart, I answered.</p>

<p>"Yes...."</p>

<p>"Starting today, I intend to grant you access to a new domain. But, little one... it would be best if you stayed away from such dangerous places."</p>

<p>Instead of advice, couldn't I get some kind of children's special discount? Maybe a massive discount on the daily quota?</p>

<p>Suppressing the nonsense threatening to escape my mouth, I smiled instead.</p>

<p>"Thank you for your concern. But I wish to fulfill my dream of becoming a chef."</p>

<p>Infernum clicked his tongue.</p>

<p>Just then, Dominic, who had been quietly listening to our conversation, crossed the table and approached me.</p>

<p>"You were sleeping so soundly that I couldn't bring myself to wake you, Roasted Chestnut."</p>

<p>"Dominic-ssi...."</p>

<p>My heart had nearly stopped first thing in the morning.</p>

<p>Next time, I honestly wished he'd wake me even if he had to beat me awake.</p>

<p>And if being a slime made physical violence difficult, then at least he could block my airway.</p>

<p>I looked back and forth between Infernum and Dominic before cautiously asking,</p>

<p>"If I may ask... what were the two of you discussing so early this morning...?"</p>

<p>At the same time, I checked the time.</p>

<p>It felt strange that Je Hyun-oh and the captains hadn't come downstairs despite all this commotion.</p>

<p>The moment I looked at the system window, I froze.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>5:30 AM</p></div>

<p>In Sla2Plate, everyone goes to sleep at midnight, and the day officially begins at 6:00 AM.</p>

<p>Sleeping and waking are forcibly controlled by the system.</p>

<p>Which meant I had woken up before the system's designated wake-up time.</p>

<p>'Wait... why?'</p>

<p>I had no idea what kind of bug had caused this.</p>

<p>Dominic answered politely.</p>

<p>"I entertained our guest in your stead while you slept, Roasted Chestnut. Wouldn't you agree that the finer the restaurant, the more attentive the service should be? Infernum-ssi possesses remarkably broad knowledge, so it was a thoroughly enjoyable conversation."</p>

<p>"Indeed, little one. I sensed an unusual aura and came early to investigate. Fortunately, this gentleman welcomed me in your place."</p>

<p>Infernum looked at Dominic with a hint of respect in his eyes.</p>

<p>"I heard the two of you are traveling together. It is unfortunate that you happened to enter my domain."</p>

<p>It didn't seem like Infernum knew Dominic's true identity.</p>

<p>It felt more as though Dominic had deliberately concealed himself.</p>

<p>Either way, if Dominic had been entertaining the guest in my place, there wasn't much I could say.</p>

<p>'I'm sure he has some purpose.'</p>

<p>I couldn't ask him outright in front of Infernum, so I changed the subject instead.</p>

<p>"Um, Infernum-nim. I have one request."</p>

<p>"What is it?"</p>

<p>"Would you permit us to enter the dungeon during the daytime as well?"</p>

<p>"The daytime dungeon?"</p>

<p>Infernum asked in surprise.</p>

<p>Infernum looked genuinely surprised.</p>

<p>The reason players entered the dungeon at night in Sla2Plate was because of the fire aura.</p>

<p>During the day, when the sun was up, the dungeon's fire aura intensified, greatly increasing the stats of the monsters under the fire dragon's dominion.</p>

<p>A monster on the first floor of the daytime dungeon possessed combat power comparable to a monster on the ninety-ninth floor of the nighttime dungeon.</p>

<p>Because of that, the daytime dungeon was originally additional content that players tackled only after seeing the ending.</p>

<p>Yet here I was, asking Infernum to unlock an area that was normally entered only after reaching level 99.</p>

<p>Infernum clicked his tongue and tried to dissuade me.</p>

<p>"Little one. The daytime dungeon is too dangerous for one such as you."</p>

<p>He wasn't wrong.</p>

<p>If you died inside the dungeon, you were immediately swallowed by it and became food for Infernum.</p>

<p>That was one of the reasons Sla2Plate was so difficult.</p>

<p>Outside the dungeon, no matter how many times you died, you'd revive at the restaurant lodging.</p>

<p>But in the most dangerous place of all—the dungeon itself—the moment you died, you became a monster's meal.</p>

<p>The daytime dungeon was especially deadly.</p>

<p>But the greater the danger, the greater the reward.</p>

<p>"Come, have a cup of tea with me instead. I heard you brew excellent tea."</p>

<p>Surely he wasn't talking about the leaf tea.</p>

<p>It was the only tea I'd brewed since arriving in Sla2Plate.</p>

<p>I looked at Dominic.</p>

<p>He simply wiggled his ears as if to say he knew nothing about it.</p>

<p>I addressed Infernum once more, doing my best to remain respectful.</p>

<p>"It's only simple leaf tea, so it isn't worthy of serving to Infernum-nim. I hope to make this restaurant flourish even further and one day serve you truly wonderful dishes."</p>

<p>"Hmm."</p>

<p>"My own skills are still lacking, but fortunately my companions are exceptionally capable. I believe we'll be able to safely gather rare ingredients even from the daytime dungeon. I ask for your permission, Infernum-nim."</p>

<p>Infernum gazed steadily at me.</p>

<p>"Little one. Do you know what happens if you die in the dungeon?"</p>

<p>"I do."</p>

<p>"So you wish to become my food?"</p>

<p>His pupils narrowed even further.</p>

<p>Infernum kept his eyes fixed on me for a long while.</p>

<p>When I didn't answer, he slowly narrowed his eyes and murmured,</p>

<p>"Yes... if I could eat you, it would be enjoyable for the first time in a long while...."</p>

<p>His blunt admission of his appetite was unsettling, but I wasn't particularly afraid.</p>

<p>I'd already experienced true terror the moment I woke up that morning.</p>

<p>And besides, this was exactly what I'd expected.</p>

<p>I knew Infernum's true nature.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_pixel"><p>"Ah.... No matter how much I consume, my hunger will not be satisfied. Roasted Chestnut. Bring me the supreme delicacy."</p></div>

<p>In the game, Infernum would stop at nothing in pursuit of the finest cuisine.</p>

<p>When I remained standing there calmly, Infernum let out a faint chuckle.</p>

<p>"Do as you please."</p>

<p>He extended a finger toward me.</p>

<p>A thin strand of flame drifted from his fingertip and touched the back of my hand.</p>

<p>A sharp sting ran through me as a crimson crest was branded onto my skin.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>The Fire Dragon's Seal has been engraved upon you. You may now enter the dungeon.</p></div>

<p>Without thinking, I glanced over at Dominic.</p>

<p>His body was shimmering as though he were in a foul mood.</p>

<p>Since he and I were bound by contract, I wondered if he disliked me forming a connection with another being.</p>

<p>Judging by his reaction, he seemed exactly as displeased as I'd expected.</p>

<p>Still, he also seemed to understand that it was necessary to clear the Trial, so he let it pass.</p>

<p>"Little one. I look forward to the day you find your way onto my dining table."</p>

<p>Flames erupted around Infernum as he disappeared.</p>

<p>Only Dominic and I remained on the restaurant's first floor.</p>

<p>I held out my hand toward the pudding slime sitting atop the table.</p>

<p>But instead of climbing onto my palm, he simply looked up at me.</p>

<p>The pudding slime stared at me intently before speaking.</p>

<p>"The dish you promised to serve me, Roasted Chestnut."</p>

<p>The brilliant gleam radiating from him sent a chill down my spine.</p>

<p>Dominic made his request with impeccable politeness.</p>

<p>"Could you cook that Fire Dragon for me?"</p>
