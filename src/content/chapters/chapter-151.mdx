<h1>Chapter 151</h1>

<p>I would completely transform all of <em>my subordinates</em>.</p>

<p>If I gouge out their eyes and make them look only at their master, they would no longer hide anything from me.</p>

<p>In truth, I had been constantly anxious.</p>

<p>It was because, despite them being <em>my subordinates</em>, I didn't feel like I fully possessed them.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk had another god inside his body, and Mo Haein had a Trial Meteorite embedded in her stomach.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh was even already in a state of physical transformation.</p>

<p>Yet, I didn't even know why they had become that way.</p>

<p>The fact that I knew nothing always made me anxious and uneasy.</p>

<p>But now, it's okay.</p>

<p>Because I had realized the method.</p>

<p>I just need to make them completely <em>mine</em>, even now.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>You recognize that your current state is abnormal.</p></div>

<div class="systembox"><p>You believe the judgments of the Young God might be flawed.</p></div>

<p>I was acting based on my own thoughts and decisions, not the mindset of a social misfit.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>You and the Young God are one, but you must not be composed solely of the Young God.</p></div>

<div class="systembox">
<p>
You recognize that you have become intermingled with the Young God and strive to reclaim yourself, even if only a little.<br />

This is because you know you will regret your current actions.
</p>
</div>

<p>It was a ridiculous remark.</p>

<p>On the contrary, I was regretting having neglected my subordinates until now.</p>

<p>I needed to take care of <em>my subordinates</em> immediately.</p>

<p>I ignored the system window that kept popping up.</p>

<p>‘I’ll deal with Captain Kwak first, and then summon Captain Mo and Lieutenant Colonel Je via message.’</p>

<p>Since Je Hyun-oh has entered the trial, I’ll have him come as soon as he returns, and then Mo Haein...</p>

<p>Click.</p>

<p>My thoughts were cut short by the sound of the door opening.</p>

<p>“What? You said you were going to bed, Chestnut-ie, but you’re already bored....”</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk, who had appeared with a playful smile, stopped mid-sentence and his face hardened.</p>

<p>“It’s not that I’m bored; I have something urgent to tell you.”</p>

<p>I quickly reported my purpose, but for some reason, Kwak Hanmuk did not reply.</p>

<p>He narrowed his eyes slightly and crossed his arms. Then, leaning his shoulder against the doorframe, he looked at me.</p>

<p>Normally, he would have responded by asking what was wrong, but he just stared at me in silence.</p>

<p>Since I was the one feeling impatient, I asked him again.</p>

<p>&quot;May I come into the room?&quot;</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk stared at me intently for a while before answering.</p>

<p>&quot;Come in.&quot;</p>

<p>He took a step back, and I finally entered the room.</p>

<p>As soon as we entered, I pointed to the bed.</p>

<p>&quot;Please sit here.&quot;</p>

<p>I thought it would be best to extract his eyes while he was in a place that was comfortable and familiar to him.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk obediently followed my instructions and sat on the bed.</p>

<p>He placed both hands on the mattress and looked up at me.</p>

<p>Standing before my seated <em>subordinate</em>, I examined his eyes with a serious gaze.</p>

<p>As expected, I didn't like the gray pupils. I wanted to gouge them out and replace them with gold.</p>

<p>I was staring at them for a while when Kwak Hanmuk spoke.</p>

<p>&quot;Goyo. What do you intend to do?&quot;</p>

<p>Only then did I realize that I hadn't offered any explanation.</p>

<p>I spoke to him in a serious tone.</p>

<p>&quot;I am going to gouge out Captain Kwak's eyes right now.&quot;</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk let out a scoff and hollow laugh, then asked back.</p>

<p>&quot;My eyes?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yes.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;So, are you going to gouge out both of them?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Taking just one out just one wouldn't look very good aesthetically, would it? Your vision shouldn't be affected.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Why are you going to gouge them out?&quot;</p>

<p>I hesitated for a moment, wondering how to explain it.</p>

<p>I was worried that if I told the truth, I might look too petty.</p>

<p>But since I didn't really have an excuse, I just spoke honestly.</p>

<p>&quot;It seems like you are hiding too much from me.&quot;</p>

<p>“So, because I have too many secrets, you’re going to pluck out my eyes?”</p>

<p>“Yes. So, from now on, I intend to look after you as your master.”</p>

<p>“As my master...”</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk muttered, repeating my words, and raised his eyebrows slightly.</p>

<p>“Sounds like it would hurt a lot. I’d have to trust the master’s skills for that.”</p>

<p>It seemed like it would be fine if I just <em>looked into</em> him a little.</p>

<p>If I gently manipulated his mind so he wouldn't feel the pain before extracting it, it wouldn't hurt much.</p>

<p>I tried my best to soothe my apprehensive <em>subordinate</em>.</p>

<p>“I will make it as painless as possible.”</p>

<p>“But you know, there’s no benefit for me if you gouge out my eyes.”</p>

<p>“What? That’s not true.”</p>

<p>Of course, there was a tremendous benefit.</p>

<p>I smiled slightly and said to him.</p>

<p>“You will become completely <em>mine</em>.”</p>

<p>That was a joyful thing for subordinates as well.</p>

<p>Because a life looking only at their master was the happiness they desired most.</p>

<p>Pleasant thoughts followed.</p>

<p>I imagined changing my subordinates to only look at me.</p>

<p>‘Come to think of it, how do I alter their bodies? Should I just buy an item?’</p>

<p>I had seen several body alteration items in the subordinate section of the Galactic Trading Post.</p>

<p>I hadn't put them in my cart thinking I wouldn't need to buy them, but since I remembered all the names, I would be able to find them quickly.</p>

<p>As for the financial issues, I could simply resolve them through a sponsorship contract.</p>

<p>Even though my account was frozen, I wasn't particularly worried since I could receive the payment in goods rather than currency.</p>

<p>Dominic would do everything I wanted.</p>

<p>And if Dominic refused, there was Black Block-ssi.</p>

<p>Black Block-ssi might make somewhat unreasonable demands in exchange for the sponsorship, but...</p>

<p><em>Still, it was for the sake of my subordinates.</em></p>

<p>It was truly a perfect plan.</p>

<p>I smiled and reached out my hand. Just as my fingertips were about to touch the corners of Kwak Hanmuk’s eyes...</p>

<p>“...!” </p>

<p>I caught the prayer beads he had thrust toward me.</p>

<p>Why is he trying to bind me?</p>

<p>As I looked at him with a questioning gaze, Kwak Hanmuk smirked.</p>

<p>“Looks like you don’t want to be tied up.”</p>

<p>Then he slammed into me immediately. In the blink of an eye, I tumbled to the floor.</p>

<p>I engaged in a brief hand-to-hand struggle with him on the ground, but I was quickly subdued.</p>

<p>Lying face down on the floor, my back was crushed against his knees.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk immediately twisted both my arms behind my back and grabbed them.</p>

<p>He tried to bind me with the prayer beads, but because I resisted, they wouldn't easily wrap around me.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk spat out a curse, then forcibly yanked the struggling beads, binding my arms tight like a rope.</p>

<p>Lying face down on the floor, I struggled to turn my head back.</p>

<p>“Captain Kwak...”</p>

<p>I asked, my eyes softening into a gentle, drooping expression.</p>

<p>“Are you scared? Just bear with it a little longer, and I’ll pull them out right away. It will be over in no time.”</p>

<p>I had even intended to praise him once it was all over.</p>

<p>It was pitiful to see him so frightened by pain that would last only an instant. </p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk swept his disheveled hair back with one hand.</p>

<p>“Wow... Crazy Chestnut-ie is driving me crazy.”</p>

<p>In the darkened room, the only light was coming in from outside the window.</p>

<p>But now, it wasn’t dark at all; it was brilliantly bright. It was because of the light flowing from my eyes.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk muttered with eyes steeped in complex thoughts.</p>

<p>“So this is what a god is...”</p>

<p>His words were tinged with bewilderment. It was as if the reality differed from what he had known.</p>

<p>A creeping sense of unpleasantness rose up.</p>

<p>‘Surely he isn’t comparing me to the god inside Captain Kwak?’</p>

<p><em>I don’t like that.</em></p>

<p>At that moment, a blue spark flew from the prayer beads.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk frowned slightly and pressed his hand over his heart.</p>

<p>I flinched, too.</p>

<p>“Captain Kwak, this hurts a little. It’s hard to breathe right now, too...”</p>

<p>Actually, it didn’t hurt that much. It was just a stinging sensation, but I spoke up hoping he might untie the prayer beads.</p>

<p>However, Kwak Hanmuk didn’t budge at all.</p>

<p>“Suck it up even if it hurts. You’re the one who tried to gouge out someone's eyeballs.”</p>

<p>“.......”</p>

<p>Having nothing to say, I quietly shut my mouth.</p>

<p>Still, I could feel the weight of his knee pressing against my back adjusting to make it easier for me to breathe.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk sighed, then called out to me.</p>

<p>“Goyo.”</p>

<p>“Yes.”</p>

<p>“Even if you gouge out my eyeballs, it won’t go your way.”</p>

<p>“Why won’t it?”</p>

<p>“Because I won’t tell you anything, I’ll keep hiding, concealing, and keeping secrets.” </p>

<p>&quot;That shouldn't be possible.&quot;</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk seemed to be under the delusion that he could maintain his sense of self even after having his eyes gouged out.</p>

<p>After all, he was being reconstructed into a being meant only for me.</p>

<p>However, I did not tell him the truth.</p>

<p>It just felt like... I shouldn't.</p>

<p>&quot;But if you still want to gouge out my eyeballs...&quot;</p>

<p>Crackle, crackle, a blue light flashed continuously from the prayer beads.</p>

<p>As sparks flew, a faint convulsion traveled through our touching bodies.</p>

<p>I felt puzzled by the trembling; it seemed like a reaction to pain.</p>

<p>But this wasn’t pain I had inflicted on him.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk spoke in a voice tinged with amusement.</p>

<p>&quot;Gotta rebel against my master a little, right?&quot;</p>

<p><em>Against me...?</em></p>

<p>Just as I felt a strong sense of revulsion, a golden light burst forth.</p>

<p>The prayer beads that had been binding me finally came loose, and a blue light flashed.</p>

<p>As the golden and blue lights mingled, the pressure pressing down on my back weakened for a moment.</p>

<p>I pushed Kwak Hanmuk away and slipped out from beneath him.</p>

<p>He and I found ourselves facing each other.</p>

<p>The room wasn't very spacious, so we were positioned so close that we could touch if we reached out.</p>

<p>I glared at Kwak Hanmuk with cold eyes.</p>

<p>Light swirled beneath his sleeveless shirt. His fractal lightning tattoo emitted a blue glow.</p>

<p>That wasn't all. A deep blue light seeped into his gray pupils as well.</p>

<p>Sparks were constantly flying up around him.</p>

<p><em>I wanted to sweep away every single one of those blue lights without exception.</em></p>

<p>“Damn it, Lieutenant Colonel Je... You said he was young and weak...”</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk looked at me, gasping for breath. He smiled with trembling lips and said, “This is quite violent, isn't it?”</p>

<p>Dominic, who had been watching from above my head, chimed in.</p>

<p>“Roasted Chestnut. There is no need to listen to every single word a subordinate says.”</p>

<p>He admonished me, saying that since subordinates don’t truly know what they want, I, as their master, must lead and teach them.</p>

<p>I agreed with him to some extent.</p>

<p>He was so rebellious that it seemed I would have to crush his mind first.</p>

<p>Originally, I intended to only look into him enough so that he wouldn’t feel pain during the eye removal.</p>

<p>But if I <em>looked</em> deeper than that...</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>He would be ruined.</p></div>

<p>“.......”</p>

<p>I was about to look into Kwak Hanmuk when I froze, as if someone had pressed a stop button.</p>

<p>The paranoid researcher came to mind.</p>

<p>The one whose mind I had crushed...</p>

<p>At the same time, my wrist was grabbed.</p>

<p>The moment a tingling sensation seeped in, the prayer beads wrapped around me with all their might, and Kwak Hanmuk spat out blood, bursting with a blue light.</p>

<p>The sound of thunder striking the cloudless night sky was heard.</p>

<p>A deep blue light was seared into my vision.</p>

<p>I realized...</p>

<p>“...Captain Kwak!”</p>

<p>What I had been about to do.</p>

<p>The rosary beads slid out, and Kwak Hanmuk’s body staggered.</p>

<p>I hurriedly supported him and sat him on the bed.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk began to laugh as he watched me trembling. Wiping the blood trickling down his chin with the back of his hand, he said:</p>

<p>“Being in charge of Crazy Chestnut-ie is f***ing difficult...”</p>

<p>For a fleeting moment, I had <em>looked into</em> him.</p>

<p>That is why I clearly understood what state Kwak Hanmuk was in right now.</p>

<p>To subdue me, Kwak Hanmuk had partially released the seal that had imprisoned the spirit.</p>

<p>In that situation, I tried to <em>look in</em> him, and I collided with the spirit within his body.</p>

<p>If I hadn't stopped, Kwak Hanmuk would have lost his mind just like the paranoid researcher.</p>

<p>‘Why did I do that?’</p>

<p>I considered the <em>young god</em> and myself to be separate beings.</p>

<p>Yet, a moment ago, the two had been intermingled.</p>

<p>I thought like the <em>young god</em> while simultaneously making judgements as ‘me.’</p>

<p>I hadn't realized at all that this was strange.</p>

<p>‘Since when has it been like this?’</p>

<p>Upon realizing this sense of dissonance, I retraced the actions I had taken since consuming ■■■.</p>

<p>The more I replayed my memories, the more I felt that I had been mistaken.</p>

<p>I resolved to protect myself while eating the ■■■, diving into the gamble despite knowing the odds of success were slim.</p>

<p>And I thought I had been fighting well in my own way until now.</p>

<p>That was purely my mistake, and I might have already been defeated.</p>

<p>“Goyo.”</p>

<p>“.......”</p>

<p>With trembling eyes, I fixed my gaze on him, who was covered in blood.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk looked up at me and spoke.</p>

<p>“It's okay.”</p>

<p>It was not okay at all.</p>

<p>When he asked me to be his teammate, and when he released me from the quarantine room as the person in charge...</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk could never have imagined that something like this would happen.</p>

<p>I had nearly ruined him.</p>

<p>Come to think of it, ever since I registered him as a subordinate, he had done nothing but get hurt.</p>

<p>The fact that I was his god was of no advantage to Kwak Hanmuk.</p>

<p>On the contrary, it was nothing but a massive disaster.</p>

<p>“...I’m sorry, Captain Kwak. I... don’t know how to unregister... because of me...”</p>

<p>When I opened my lips, the only words that came out were pathetic excuses.</p>

<p>I was frozen in shock over an irreversible mistake.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk called out to me again.</p>

<p>“Chestnut-ie.”</p>

<p>“Yes...”</p>

<p>“I’m sorry.” </p>

<p>I stared blankly, not knowing why he was apologizing.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk met my gaze and continued speaking.</p>

<p>“You must be feeling very frustrated given the situation, and I’ve made things even harder for you by bringing up something I can’t properly explain. But, humanely speaking, okay? Let’s not gouge out my eyes. Just give me some time...”</p>

<p>He paused for a moment. He hesitated for quite a while, as if he needed to judge for himself whether what he was about to say was right.</p>

<p>And finally, he spoke to me.</p>

<p>“I’ll tell you everything.”</p>

<p>Then he reached out and grabbed my forearm.</p>

<p>I felt a slight weight pulling me down.</p>

<p>He spoke to a trainee as a captain belonging to the same team.</p>

<p>“I can’t speak right now because there are many things I don’t know myself.”</p>

<p>“.......”</p>

<p>“Well, probably... Captain Mo is similar. If you wait, she’ll tell you everything. Ah, I don’t know about Lieutenant Colonel Je. That guy is just too far gone in his own brand of madness.”</p>

<p>I just quietly stared at Kwak Hanmuk, who was speaking in a playful tone.</p>

<p>As I stood there lost in thought, he lightly shook the arm he was holding to get my attention.</p>

<p>“About the registration. Does that mean I belong to you?”</p>

<p>“...Yes.”</p>

<p>“Since I’ve agreed to take charge, it’s only natural that I’d be registered.”</p>

<p>Then, as a subordinate speaking to his god, he added:</p>

<p>“Don’t even think about kicking me out.”</p>

<p>I felt a throbbing pain in my heart.</p>

<p>A strange sensation I had never experienced before enveloped me.</p>

<p>I... wanted to answer firmly.</p>

<p>That as a team member, and as a god, I would not be a calamity to him.</p>

<p>Just then, a system window popped up before my eyes.</p>

<p>
‘Blossom’ acquired!
</p>
</div>
