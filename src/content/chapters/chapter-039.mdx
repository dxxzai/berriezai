<h1>Chapter 39</h1>

<p>When they came out after clearing DeZonDeal, Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein had been unconscious for over half a day.</p>

<p>They'd cleared it with the True Ending, but the BT-DZD virus infection had affected them physically.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk regained consciousness before Mo Haein. By then, the TRA was in utter chaos.</p>

<p>Han Goyo had gone into shock from massive blood loss and fallen into a coma.</p>

<p>And over Han Goyo's disposition, Ham Jiwol and Samra had clashed head-on.</p>

<p>Ham Jiwol had stormed in with her researchers, insisting that before his heart stopped, they needed to halt Han Goyo's treatment and begin biological preservation.</p>

<p>Samra had come out strongly against it. Neither side would back down, and they were locked in a tense standoff.</p>

<p>Director Jang Seokyun was also in favor of continuing treatment, but he couldn't simply ignore the Research and Analysis Division's vehement arguments.</p>

<p>Especially when Ham Jiwol was this unhinged about something — she was nearly impossible to stop.</p>

<p>That was when Kwak Hanmuk regained consciousness and joined the fray.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk, who'd rushed over after hearing all hell had broken loose, was dumbfounded.</p>

<p>"What the fuck."</p>

<p>It was a temporary team, sure — only a month — but he had enough of a conscience not to hand over a teammate, a kid who was barely twenty-four, to Ham Jiwol.</p>

<p>And besides, since when did the Research Division get to lay a finger on Field Operations personnel whenever they pleased?</p>

<p>He went off.</p>

<p>"We bust our fucking asses in the field to shut down a Trial, and you're telling me you want to shove a kid who isn't even dead yet into a preservation tank while he's still alive? This is some bullshit. I can't fucking deal with this."</p>

<p>"Captain Kwak Hanmuk."</p>

<p>Samra placed a hand on Kwak Hanmuk's shoulder. But instead of pulling him back, he subtly nudged him forward.</p>

<p>The message was clear: keep going.</p>

<p>"Did I say anything wrong?"</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk swatted Samra's hand away — the man was only pretending to hold him back — and turned to Jang Seokyun.</p>

<p>"Transfer Chief Ham to the Special Mission Team. Right now. Let her run around in a Trial with us. She can get all that data she's soooooo fucking fond of. How about it?"</p>

<p>"Captain Kwak. That's enough."</p>

<p>As if his head was throbbing, Jang Seokyun pressed his fingers firmly against his temples.</p>

<p>"Han Goyo's treatment will continue."</p>

<p>Later, when Mo Haein woke up, Kwak Hanmuk told her exactly what had happened.</p>

<p>After hearing the story, Mo Haein quietly reached a conclusion.</p>

<p>"We shouldn't mention the eye thing for now."</p>

<p>They'd already filed a report about the discoloration of his eyes once before, after HapFactory.</p>

<p>But this time in DeZonDeal had been different.</p>

<p>If Ham Jiwol got word of what happened in DeZonDeal, she would absolutely lose her mind.</p>

<p>She was the kind of person who wouldn't bat an eye at dissecting Han Goyo alive.</p>

<p>They hadn't been able to get on the same page with Je Hyun-oh, who'd entered his next Trial immediately upon returning, but that wasn't something to worry about.</p>

<p>Because he was never the one to file a report first.</p>

<p>As a rule, Je Hyun-oh didn't submit reports on the Trials he entered.</p>

<p>Occasionally, if Samra or Director Jang Seokyun asked him directly, he'd give a short answer. That was the extent of it.</p>

<p>"Captain Mo. We're telling the Lieutenant Colonel about this one."</p>

<p>At Kwak Hanmuk's sulky remark, Mo Haein let out a small laugh.</p>

<p>"We should. Chief Ham crossed a line. This needs to be a fight between division chiefs."</p>

<p>Director Jang Seokyun had sided with Field Operations, but that alone wasn't enough.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh, whatever else you could say about him, did enjoy making the Research Division's life hell.</p>

<p>That was because, a long time ago, Research had hounded him relentlessly, trying to get samples.</p>

<p>Normally he was a pain in the ass, but when he was on your side, Je Hyun-oh was a reliable goddamn lunatic.</p>

<p>They agreed: they'd send Je Hyun-oh in to stomp on the Research Division so hard they'd never spout that nonsense again.</p>

<p>Having resolved to tattle to Je Hyun-oh, Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein focused on handling their respective team duties.</p>

<p>They'd expected that Han Goyo wouldn't be getting up for a while, and that their next Trial entry would be postponed.</p>

<p>But then something astonishing happened.</p>

<p>After receiving treatment, Han Goyo's body began recovering at an unbelievable rate, and in just two days, he regained consciousness.</p>

<p>They wanted to visit him the moment they heard he'd woken up, but visits weren't permitted.</p>

<p>All they got were secondhand reports through Samra that he was fine. They didn't see his face until the briefing.</p>

<p>As if the whole coma had been a lie, Han Goyo greeted them looking perfectly healthy.</p>

<p>Neither of them said it out loud, but Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein both thought the same thing.</p>

<p>He really wasn't normal.</p>

<p>At this point, nothing that happened to Han Goyo in a Trial would surprise them.</p>

<p>And then they entered Clean3.</p>

<p>"Shit…"</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk swore first. No — he didn't know what else to say besides swearing.</p>

<p>They'd finished the early item farming decently, and now it was time to be selected by the butlers and enter the mansion.</p>

<p>As long as you were careful during the initial Selection, you didn't run into Clean3's butlers much after entering the mansion.</p>

<p>But since the goal this time was the True Ending, Han Goyo was planning to make contact with the butlers inside the mansion.</p>

<p>The endings Kwak Hanmuk had seen in Clean3 so far were all just surviving seven days and returning.</p>

<p>The Roulette was infuriating, but as long as you survived somehow, you got an ending.</p>

<p>So all he'd ever done was clean and hunt creatures like a madman.</p>

<p>He'd had no idea you could build affinity with the butlers.</p>

<p>He wasn't confident he could avoid losing his temper with those arrogant bastard butlers, so he'd been approaching this with the mindset of diligently supporting Goyo-hyung.</p>

<p>Then, out of nowhere, the Rabbit Butler came running toward Han Goyo.</p>

<p>Tensing at the anomaly, but sensing no hostile intent, they held back and watched.</p>

<p>And Kwak Hanmuk regretted it.</p>

<p>He should've just grabbed Han Goyo and bolted.</p>
<br/>
<p><span class="flashback">"Roasted Chestnut!"</span></p>
<br/>
<p>The Rabbit Butler grinned wide enough to show all his sharp teeth and called out Han Goyo's Trial name.</p>

<p>He lifted the grown man like a child, delighted, and then even called over the Cat Butler.</p>
<br/>
<p><span class="flashback">"Look who it is! Isn't that Roasted Chestnut!"</span></p>
<br/>
<p>The Cat Butler was overjoyed to see Han Goyo as well.</p>

<p>The two butlers began chatting away warmly, as if they'd run into a dear old friend. Kwak Hanmuk shot Mo Haein a bewildered look.</p>

<p>But there was no way Mo Haein knew anything either.</p>

<p>In the meantime, the Cat Butler even handed off the Cleaner Selection process — something he had never once delegated — to a footman.</p>
<br/>
<p><span class="flashback">"Take over here. I must entertain a friend who's come to visit after so long."</span></p>
<br/>
<p>It was easy to see this wasn't a situation Han Goyo had anticipated.</p>

<p>They'd never heard about anything like this in the briefing, and more importantly —</p>
<br/>
<p><span class="flashback">"It's been a while, Butlers."</span></p>
<br/>
<p>— Han Goyo's face had gone pale as he greeted them.</p>

<p>And there wasn't a damn thing they could do.</p>

<p>Han Goyo was dragged straight into the mansion.</p>

<p>And now, only Mo Haein and Kwak Hanmuk were left outside.</p>

<p>"Selection will now begin! Those properly attired, step forward!"</p>

<p>The footman called Alpha shouted loudly.</p>

<p>Avoiding Alpha's gaze, Kwak Hanmuk whispered rapidly.</p>

<p>"Captain Mo. I think we're fucked."</p>

<p>Having entered Clean3 multiple times, he knew better than anyone just how insane this situation was.</p>

<p>The butlers didn't treat Cleaners as people.</p>

<p>To them, a Cleaner was about the same as filth in a trash can.</p>

<p>The Rabbit Butler in particular often displayed strong hostility toward Cleaners, making him an NPC you absolutely had to avoid when tackling Clean3.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk would turn and go the other way if he spotted rabbit ears even a hundred meters off.</p>

<p>Those same butlers hadn't just shown Han Goyo overflowing affection — they'd gone so far as to dump the Cleaner Selection, which they treated as critically important, onto an underling, all so they could entertain a guest.</p>

<p>Receiving more attention than necessary from an NPC was never a positive sign.</p>

<p>They could end up forcibly infected and trapped inside the Trial.</p>

<p>"This doesn't seem connected to the True Ending either, does it?"</p>

<p>But no answer came back.</p>

<p>Mo Haein, her face drained of color, was staring in the direction Han Goyo had vanished.</p>

<p>Her wavering eyes had lost all focus.</p>

<p>"Haein."</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk grabbed her forearm tightly.</p>

<p>"Snap out of it, Haein. We're in Clean3."</p>

<p>He gripped hard enough to bruise and spoke low and firm.</p>

<p>Fortunately, Mo Haein came back to herself.</p>

<p>"…We're in Clean3."</p>

<p>She repeated Kwak Hanmuk's words in a daze, then clenched her jaw and shook her head sharply.</p>

<p>"Sorry."</p>

<p>Mo Haein took a sharp breath, then spoke rapidly.</p>

<p>"The butlers were friendly. I don't think they're going to do anything to the trainee right away. First, we get inside the mansion and regroup with the Lieutenant Colonel. And then…"</p>

<p>Her words, which had been flowing without pause, came to a halt. Kwak Hanmuk finished for her.</p>

<p>"We grab Goyo and get the hell out of Clean3."</p>

<p>Even if they couldn't clear it with the True Ending, the point was to get out first.</p>

<p>Mo Haein gave a small nod, then stepped forward with Kwak Hanmuk to undergo Selection.</p>

<p>The Cleaners who failed Selection died on the spot.</p>

<p>A few fled into the forest behind the mansion or dove into the lake, barely escaping with their lives.</p>

<p>But surviving seven days would be difficult.</p>

<p>At last, it was Mo Haein and Kwak Hanmuk's turn.</p>

<p>"Not bad. You may enter the mansion."</p>

<p>Alpha looked satisfied and declared them passed.</p>

<p>Now it was time to be assigned their cleaning zones inside the mansion.</p>

<p>Both of them unconsciously quickened their steps. Their hearts were anxious and urgent.</p>

<p>And when they entered the mansion —</p>

<p>"Sand will take the West Wing, second floor. Blue Thunder, the East Wing, second floor."</p>

<p>— they came face to face with Han Goyo, who had become a butler.</p>

<p>Ding!</p>

<p>A System Window appeared.</p>

<p>The interior of the great mansion was overwhelming in a way that felt entirely different from the outside.</p>

<p>It was a space roughly suited to the height of the butlers and footmen, but for a human of ordinary build, even climbing the stairs was a struggle.</p>

<p>The ceiling, bizarrely high, was carved into a grid like a chessboard, each square fitted with gold ornamentation, and the hanging crystal chandelier was dazzling and enormous.</p>

<p>The light falling from the chandelier illuminated the marble floor, which was engraved with geometric patterns, making it gleam brilliantly.</p>

<p>The walls, fitted with pilasters, were also painted with beautiful patterns, but I couldn't take any of it in.</p>

<p>"Roasted Chestnut. Do you remember? The mural painted on that wall — you fixed it for us."</p>

<p>I don't know how to paint.</p>

<p>I'd simply obtained a paint item to complete a cleaning quest in the game and slathered it on.</p>

<p>"I also recall the memory of Roasted Chestnut giving us wonderful gifts! One every single day. Our days were joyful thanks to you."</p>

<p>I don't even know what those gifts were.</p>

<p>I'd just farmed Gift Box items in the game and handed them over, box and all.</p>

<p>The Gift Box item, which restored a player's health, could also be given to a butler NPC once a day to raise affinity.</p>

<p>"It was also remarkable how Roasted Chestnut defeated that enormous creature. The basement has been clean ever since."</p>

<p>That, too, was just defeating a Boss Monster.</p>

<p>Because it was a game, of course I… acted that way.</p>

<p>While I was half out of my mind, the Rabbit Butler and the Cat Butler had at some point brought me to the parlor.</p>

<p>The Rabbit Butler sat me down on a sofa.</p>

<p>The sofa was so large that even seated, my feet didn't touch the floor.</p>

<p>As I sat there, my whole body sinking into the sofa, the Cat Butler handed me a teacup.</p>

<p>I took the large, gold-rimmed teacup with both hands, struggling with its weight, and he poured tea for me.</p>

<p>The tea, a deep red like pomegranate, filled the cup.</p>

<p>The warmth against my palms helped me regain a little of my senses.</p>

<p>"So, how have you been? I've been terribly curious. It's so difficult to exchange news with your planet, Roasted Chestnut."</p>

<p>I tilted the teacup.</p>

<p>I made a show of taking a sip, but in reality, I didn't even wet my lips.</p>

<p>Swallowing saliva instead of tea, I tried to regain my composure in the brief time I'd bought.</p>

<p>Clean3's NPCs remember me.</p>

<p>Figuring out how this had happened could wait.</p>

<p>The immediate problem in front of me was this.</p>

<p>'Can we still clear the True Ending?'</p>

<p>I was a player who had already cleared every ending in &lt;Clean! Clean! Clean!&gt;.</p>

<p>I'd even seen the hidden ending. There was no chance an additional ending existed.</p>

<p>It was also impossible for the Lieutenant Colonel or the Captains to see the True Ending in my place.</p>
<br/>
<p><span class="flashback">"It was also remarkable how Roasted Chestnut defeated that enormous creature. The basement has been clean ever since."</span></p>
<br/>
<p>Because the Boss Monster — the condition for reaching the True Ending — wouldn't be there.</p>

<p>And that wasn't all.</p>

<p>Even in the chaos of being brought to the parlor, I'd kept an eye on my surroundings. The Gift Box items were nowhere to be seen.</p>

<p>Normally, they were basic items scattered throughout the mansion, easy to find.</p>

<p>There would be many other elements that had changed besides this. Enough to make normal gameplay progression impossible…</p>

<p>'Clearing it with a normal ending won't be easy either.'</p>

<p>I know the games of the Archive fairly well — most of them, at least.</p>

<p>But I don't know how to play a game whose ending is already over.</p>

<p>'Still, I can't just sit here trapped.'</p>

<p>I didn't enter alone. Je Hyun-oh and the Captains would be trapped here too.</p>

<p>'Because of me.'</p>

<p>The tea in the teacup trembled faintly.</p>

<p>This wasn't the time to be sitting here.</p>

<p>I had to do something.</p>

<p>What I needed most right now was information.</p>

<p>The main NPCs who controlled Clean3 were favorably disposed toward me. I had to get even a small hint from them.</p>

<p>I set the teacup, its contents not diminished in the slightest, on my lap.</p>

<p>And forced my lips apart.</p>

<p>"…I should have contacted you first."</p>

<p>"It's quite all right. Roasted Chestnut is still young, is he not! The younger you are, the busier you're bound to be."</p>

<p>The Cat Butler let out a hearty laugh.</p>

<p>"How have you both been?"</p>

<p>"We've managed the mansion as always. But we're curious about Roasted Chestnut's stories."</p>

<p>"Nothing much has happened with me either."</p>

<p>Then, as if the thought had just occurred to me, I added a question.</p>

<p>"Oh, right — has the Master returned?"</p>
