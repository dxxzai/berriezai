<h1>Chapter 54</h1>

<p>It was an unbelievable combination of words.</p>

<p>The mayor of Morneveil was dead.</p>

<p>And not only that—</p>

<p>I had killed him?!</p>

<p>It was so absurd that I couldn’t even feel wronged.</p>

<p>I just stood there with my mouth hanging open when Dominic’s voice reached me.</p>

<p>“Roasted Chestnut…”</p>

<p>He was staring at me with shocked eyes.</p>

<p>For some reason, it felt like I could hear the words behind his gaze.</p>

<p>“When you said you were going to persuade the mayor… did you mean murder?”</p>

<p>‘It wasn’t me, Dominic-ssi!’</p>

<p>Even if it was late, I looked at him with all the injustice and sorrow I could muster.</p>

<p>A choice appeared.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<div class="systembox_wrap">
<p>
You have been arrested by the Morneveil Police!<br/>

&#45; 	Surrender quietly.<br/>
&#45; 	Protest fiercely and escape.<br/>
&#45; 	Ask Doctor Dominic for help.<br/>
&#45; 	Ask Cult Leader Edel to come.
</p>
</div>
</div>

<p>There was nothing to consider.</p>

<p>I immediately chose the first option.</p>

<p>Even if I escaped right now, my murder charge would not disappear.</p>

<p>If I didn’t want to spend my entire time in Morneveil being chased, I needed to be arrested first and clear my name afterward.</p>

<p>Asking Dominic for help was no different from choosing the escape option.</p>

<p>Later, during interrogation, I could ask him to testify about my whereabouts over the past few days, but now was not the time.</p>

<p>I surrendered quietly.</p>

<p>And now—</p>

<p>I was sitting on a mattress stuffed with straw inside a jail cell.</p>

<p>With my handcuffed hands, I held my forehead.</p>

<p>“Ha…”</p>

<p>A deep sigh escaped me naturally.</p>

<p>Throughout the entire interrogation at the police station, I sincerely explained my innocence.</p>

<p>I even presented Dominic, who had been with me during that time, as a witness, but it was completely useless.</p>

<p>They politely deprived Dominic of the chance to speak and simply continued treating me as the culprit.</p>

<p>It felt like they were forcing every detail to fit a conclusion that had already been decided.</p>

<p>And after hearing how the mayor of Morneveil had been killed, I was even more dumbfounded.</p>

<p>The mayor of Morneveil had died last night in his bedroom after being stabbed in the back of the neck with an extremely thin and sharp awl.</p>

<p>He died instantly without even having the chance to scream, and there were almost no visible wounds on the corpse.</p>

<p>It was a murder method created by someone with extensive anatomical knowledge.</p>

<p>‘How am I supposed to do something like that?’</p>

<p>I wanted to grab the collar of the officer accusing me of murder and shake him violently.</p>

<p>But by that point, I had an idea of who had framed me.</p>

<p>After realizing who was behind it, I stopped trying to explain myself.</p>

<p>I didn’t admit guilt.</p>

<p>I simply gave up on further explanations.</p>

<p>And that was how I ended up locked in the holding cell.</p>

<p>‘This is insane.’</p>

<p>The mayor being dead had completely thrown off my playthrough.</p>

<p>Listening to the snoring of the old man locked in the cell across from me, I sat on the worn mattress and thought about how I was going to overcome this obstacle.</p>

<p>The rescue of the sacrifices was the biggest problem.</p>

<p>Even if I managed to save them, it would be meaningless if there was no force capable of protecting them.</p>

<p>If they stayed inside Morneveil, the Apostles of the Void would simply drag them away and use them as sacrifices again.</p>

<p>‘Were there three sacrifices used for the ritual on the third day?’</p>

<p>While I was thinking about where to hide the three men, the sound of rusty hinges creaking echoed.</p>

<p>I glanced over, wondering if another new criminal had been brought into the holding cells.</p>

<p>A long shadow stretched across the floor.</p>

<p>A shadow longer than any human could possibly have.</p>

<p>The owner of the shadow reached my cell.</p>

<p>Perhaps he was shocked by what he saw, because Dominic couldn’t say anything.</p>

<p>A choice appeared.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<div class="systembox_wrap">
<p>
Doctor Dominic has arrived at the holding cell!<br/>

&#45; 	Pretend to be pitiful.<br/>
&#45; 	Get angry and ask why he allowed you to be arrested.<br/>
&#45; 	Ask him to get you out immediately.<br/>
&#45; 	Ask him to call Cult Leader Edel.
</p>
</div>
</div>

<p>Now the choices were pushing 'Call Edel' again.</p>

<p>After a brief moment of thought, I chose the first option.</p>

<p>Immediately, my face became sorrowful on its own, and warmth gathered around my eyes.</p>

<p>A trembling voice escaped.</p>

<p>“Dominic-ssi…”</p>

<p>To anyone watching, I looked exactly like someone who had suddenly been imprisoned, grown anxious, and finally relaxed after seeing someone familiar.</p>

<p>‘Not bad.’</p>

<p>Following the choice’s direction, I continued acting pitiful.</p>

<p>“Good heavens, Roasted Chestnut…”</p>

<p>Dominic placed his hand against the black iron bars.</p>

<p>“To think they locked Roasted Chestnut in a place like this. I cannot believe it. I will release you immediately.”</p>

<p>Although I hadn’t selected the third option, he said something very similar.</p>

<p>If it was Dominic, he was certainly capable of carrying out what he said.</p>

<p>But I had no choice but to refuse.</p>

<p>“It’s fine.”</p>

<p>“Why do you not ask me, your companion, for help?”</p>

<p>“Even if I leave this place, my charges won’t disappear. In fact, I would only add the crime of escaping.”</p>

<p>I stepped closer to the bars and looked up at Dominic.</p>

<p>Then I stated who I believed was behind this.</p>

<p>“The person who framed me for murder is Lady Edel. She must have been the one who killed the mayor as well.”</p>

<p>Edel was someone skilled at preparing sacrifices, having offered countless victims for human sacrifice rituals.</p>

<p>I was certain this was her work.</p>

<p>Dominic narrowed his eyes.</p>

<p>“Miss Edel… did this to Roasted Chestnut?”</p>

<p>“Yes. It seems she wants to force me into joining the cult.”</p>

<p>The moment Dominic heard my words, he immediately understood the situation.</p>

<p>She intended to push me into a dead end and then make me join the cult in exchange for clearing my murder accusation.</p>

<p>I knew Edel had an obsessive and persistent personality.</p>

<p>But I never expected things to go this far after only two days.</p>

<p>‘Technically, it’s only one day.’</p>

<p>I wondered if showing her the spectacle of my resurrection right in front of her had been a mistake.</p>

<p>From her perspective, she probably couldn’t bear leaving someone who had received a miracle from a god outside the cult.</p>

<p>“Wasn’t Miss Edel supposed to be your companion? Like me?”</p>

<p>“That’s correct.”</p>

<p>To Dominic, who seemed unable to understand, I gave a hollow laugh.</p>

<p>“Lady Edel probably believes this is something she is doing for my sake.”</p>

<p>It was a trap within CallOVo’s companion system.</p>

<p>NPCs who became companions were extremely favorable toward the player and used their abilities to the fullest for the player’s benefit.</p>

<p>But the standard for “benefiting the player” was based on the NPC’s own perspective.</p>

<p>If an NPC believed something was right, they would not hesitate to take actions that led the player toward a bad ending.</p>

<p>Just like now.</p>

<p>Regardless, one thing was certain.</p>

<p>The situation was ruined.</p>

<p>I had been arrested many times in the reporter route, but this was the first time I had ever been accused of murdering the mayor of Morneveil.</p>

<p>“Dominic-ssi. Even if I get out of here, the problem comes afterward. Originally, I planned to use the mayor’s authority to safely protect the sacrifices I rescued, but that’s no longer possible.”</p>

<p>There was no way to escape the cult’s influence.</p>

<p>Sending them outside Morneveil would also be difficult.</p>

<p>The city was surrounded by valleys, meaning we had to use the train.</p>

<p>And we would be caught at the station.</p>

<p>At that moment, Dominic calmly asked:</p>

<p>“If the problem of protecting the sacrifices is solved, can Roasted Chestnut leave this place?”</p>

<p>I quickly turned the idea over in my head.</p>

<p>Certainly, if that could be resolved, escaping from prison was acceptable.</p>

<p>I could gather the remaining evidence while being wanted.</p>

<p>After all, I had previously collected evidence while being hunted as a fugitive during the reporter route.</p>

<p>“Yes, it would be fine to lea—”</p>

<p>I stopped mid-sentence.</p>

<p>Before I could even finish speaking, the iron bars Dominic had been touching began violently shaking.</p>

<p>They repeatedly stretched out into sharp, thorn-like spikes and then shrank back down.</p>

<p>Then suddenly—</p>

<p>Bang!</p>

<p>One spike shot out.</p>

<p>“...!”</p>

<p>The tip of the spike grazed my cheek.</p>

<p>A sharp pain flashed.</p>

<p>Slowly.</p>

<p>The spike returned.</p>

<p>With blood staining its tip.</p>

<p>The iron bars finally changed shape.</p>

<p>They spread apart, creating an opening large enough for me to walk through.</p>

<p>“I apologize, Roasted Chestnut. This was slightly different from changing size… I was in a hurry and made a mistake.”</p>

<p>Dominic repeatedly apologized while examining me.</p>

<p>“No, it’s fine. This isn’t even an injury. Thank you for getting me out.”</p>

<p>I rubbed my cheek while pretending to wipe away the blood with the back of my hand.</p>

<p>I was actually trying to remove the goosebumps that had risen on my skin.</p>

<p>Just as I was about to quietly escape the holding cell—</p>

<p>Dominic suddenly pulled me backward.</p>

<p>At the same time, a second visitor arrived.</p>

<p>A woman entered the holding area with small, quick steps.</p>

<p>She wore a high-neck dress that covered her neck, a wide-brimmed hat with a silk veil hanging from it, silk gloves on her hands, and carried a parasol.</p>

<p>She gently lifted the veil of her hat and looked up at Dominic with red eyes.</p>

<p>“Doctor Dominic?”</p>

<p>Then she noticed the empty jail cell.</p>

<p>“Oh.”</p>

<p>Edel made a surprised sound and asked anxiously:</p>

<p>“Doctor! Where did Little Lamb go?”</p>

<p>Her expression, as though she had rushed here worried about me, was touching.</p>

<p>If she weren’t the very person who had put me in prison.</p>

<p>When Dominic did not answer, she nervously bit her lip.</p>

<p>“Where did he go…? It’s dangerous outside… Where could Little Lamb have gone alone…? Little Lamb… I need to protect him…”</p>

<p>Her quiet muttering carried a subtle horror.</p>

<p>Watching from behind Dominic like I had front-row seats, I felt slightly afraid.</p>

<p>Dominic simply allowed her to continue until she began biting her silk glove.</p>

<p>Edel, who had shredded the end of her glove instead of her fingernails, finally regained her senses and glanced behind Dominic.</p>

<p>“Oh, but… Doctor Dominic, behind you…”</p>

<p>She noticed.</p>

<p>The jail was dark, but considering how poorly I was hiding, it would have been strange if she hadn’t.</p>

<p>I had already been preparing excuses and lies for what I would say to Edel.</p>

<p>I tried to step forward, but Dominic’s hand stopped me.</p>

<p>“Miss Edel.”</p>

<p>“Y-yes?”</p>

<p>Edel flinched and looked up at Dominic.</p>

<p>Dominic slowly bent down.</p>

<p>Edel’s eyes gradually widened.</p>

<p>Meeting her gaze, Dominic spoke calmly.</p>

<p>“There is no need to feel anxious or impatient.”</p>

<p>His blue eyes reflected Edel.</p>

<p>Edel slightly parted her lips.</p>

<p>“Excitement only clouds judgment. Miss Edel, you remember when you received treatment, correct?”</p>

<p>“…Yes, Doctor.”</p>

<p>The eyes that had been shining with something close to madness moments ago became dull and empty.</p>

<p>“It is unfortunate that there is no chair here where you can rest comfortably.”</p>

<p>“……”</p>

<p>“Would you like to come a little closer to me?”</p>

<p>“…Yes.”</p>

<p>As if entranced, Edel stepped forward.</p>

<p>The distance between her and Dominic was now less than the length of a hand.</p>

<p>“Roasted Chestnut… No, Little Lamb, will be found. Miss Edel always does her best. However, he is not here right now. He will not be at my clinic either.”</p>

<p>Dominic continued speaking slowly.</p>

<p>“How about focusing on preparing today’s ritual? Performing the ritual slightly differently than usual may help relieve stress.”</p>

<p>His voice slowly sank deeper.</p>

<p>Through her eardrums.</p>

<p>Further inside.</p>

<p>“For example… offering believers as sacrifices would also be good. Becoming a sacrifice for the god is the believers’ ideal. Miss Edel, you are the cult leader, so you should fulfill the wishes of your faithful followers.”</p>

<p>Dominic asked in a dreamy, languid voice.</p>

<p>“Do you understand what I mean, Miss Edel?”</p>

<p>Edel answered yes and smiled without hesitation.</p>

<p>“I will prepare a grand ritual, Doctor.”</p>

<p>She looked infinitely loyal.</p>

<p>Perhaps she would have accepted anything Dominic told her.</p>

<p>Even if that thing was…</p>

<p>To die right here.</p>

<p>A chill ran through me.</p>

<p>Even though I conveniently used Dominic’s abilities, I couldn’t help the instinctive resistance I felt whenever I saw something like this.</p>

<p>I watched Edel’s back as she left.</p>

<p>Dominic turned toward me and his expression changed in surprise.</p>

<p>“Roasted Chestnut, are you alright? You are sweating heavily. Here, use this handkerchief.”</p>

<p>I vigorously wiped my face with the handkerchief, which was practically the size of a towel.</p>

<p>My mind slowly returned.</p>

<p>“Miss Edel seems to have frightened you greatly. However, something like this will not happen again. I have spoken with Miss Edel properly.”</p>

<p>“…Thank you.”</p>

<p>My cracked voice expressed my gratitude.</p>

<p>Dominic smiled.</p>

<p>He seemed to think I was simply exhausted from all of this.</p>

<p>Thankfully, I had just escaped prison.</p>

<p>“But, Roasted Chestnut.”</p>

<p>Dominic folded the handkerchief I returned and asked:</p>

<p>“What is the badge you have attached inside your clothes?”</p>
