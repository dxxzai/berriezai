<h1>Chapter 123</h1>

<p>While playing the archive games, I made it my primary goal to see every ending.</p>

<p>I collected every single ending, even those I didn't like, and played meticulously to uncover all the hidden elements of the game.</p>

<p>Cell-X was no exception.</p>

<p>Neither the Warden nor the guards were to my taste at all, but I saw all the endings anyway.</p>

<p>I remembered the things I had done as a guard.</p>

<p>I carried out quests involving cruel acts without any emotion.</p>

<p>There were also many quests that made the Chief of Security happy, causing my affection level to skyrocket.</p>

<p>I was the Chief of Security's most loyal and trustworthy subordinate and colleague.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_pixel"><p>“Roasted Chestnut. You are a perfect guard. I cannot imagine the prison without you.”</p></div>

<p>So much so that he promised to keep me by his side if he ever became the Warden.</p>

<p>If you were to ask if I was always miserable throughout that entire playthrough…</p>

<p>That was not the case.</p>

<p>There were certainly moments when I felt joy.</p>

<p>Because I still vividly remember the thrill I felt from gameplay that unfolded exactly as I had planned and from quests that were completed flawlessly.</p>

<p>But that was a game.</p>

<p>It was enjoyable precisely because it wasn't reality.</p>

<p>Even that brief moment of enjoyment vanished completely once I discovered the game's hidden story.</p>

<p>I caught my breath, which was racing with tension.</p>

<p>Reading the emotions on my face, the Chief of Security repeatedly displayed notifications that my favorability rating was rising.</p>

<p>He was becoming convinced that his dream was not a vain delusion.</p>

<p>I felt I understood why the Chief of Security and the other guards had raised their favorability ratings the moment they saw my face.</p>

<p>Just like the Chief of Security, the other guards had raised theirs because they had dreamt of a 'Prison Guard Roasted Chestnut.'</p>

<p>It felt like everything was going black, but I couldn't just meekly admit it.</p>

<p>I opened my mouth to find a way out, no matter what.</p>

<p>&quot;...I didn't know you were a person with such a rich imagination, Chief of Security.&quot; </p>

<p>Ignoring the numerous notification windows obscuring my view, I asked him.</p>

<p>“Do you consider a dream to be reality?”</p>

<p>As soon as the Chief of Security heard me, he smirked. He spoke with an uncharacteristically generous attitude.</p>

<p>“I didn’t believe it at first either. It’s closer to saying I was half-doubting.”</p>

<p>Then he leaned toward me.</p>

<p>“But I heard the voice of God.”</p>

<p>Octopus Tentacle...?</p>

<p>At that moment, various thoughts flashed through my mind.</p>

<p>There had been mentions of Octopus Tentacle since the new prison guard training center.</p>
<br />
<p><span class="flashback">[Neo is a resource of limitless potential—a foundation provided by God for a new era of humanity.]</span></p>
<br />
<p>As evidenced by the presence of a prayer room, religious education was provided to the prison guards, and the Chief of Security and the officers possessed a fanatical faith in God.</p>

<p>And their god, Octopus Tentacle, was already intervening in the trial.</p>

<p>After all, it had already turned my weekly medical officer schedule upside down.</p>

<p>They were capable of exerting sufficient influence even on the Chief of Security, an NPC serving them.</p>

<p>However, a fundamental question existed before that.</p>

<p>How do NPCs remember Roasted Chestnut?</p>

<p>I tried to extend my thoughts a little further.</p>

<p>There have been three instances so far where a being of trial knew about the ‘Roasted Chestnut’ that I had played in the game.</p>

<ol>

  <li><p>The butlers of Clean3.</p></li>

</ol>

<p>In Clean3, the game's premise was that NPCs remembered the previous gameplay.</p>

<p>At the time, I was shocked by the fact that the butlers remembered Roasted Chestnut, and I could not understand how reality and the game could intersect.</p>

<p>And the master of Clean3, who commanded the mansion and butlers...</p>

<p>It is Dominic, an Outer God, a being no different from a terrible disaster.</p>

<ol start="2">

  <li><p>Edel of CallOVo.</p></li>

</ol>

<p>Just before the ending, she called me Roasted Chestnut, not Little Lamb.</p>

<p>However, she did not fully recall Roasted Chestnut.</p>

<p>She merely muttered it unconsciously.</p>

<p>It was Dominic and me who were by her side at that time.</p>

<p>Dominic killed and revived me multiple times; given that he interfered with the system, he likely used the power of a god.</p>

<ol start="3">

  <li><p>The Creatures and Guards of Cell-X.</p></li>

</ol>

<p>Here, Octopus Tentacle is worshipped as a god.</p>

<p>Unlike the butlers of Clean3, the Creatures and Guards did not remember every single one of my gameplays.</p>

<p>However, they revived parts of memories from specific gameplays through the form of dreams.</p>

<p>Although there was a difference between the roles of a medic and a guard, it was clear that both were gameplays I had performed in the game.</p>

<p>In Clean3, CallOVo, and Cell-X, the NPCs all remember Roasted Chestnut.</p>

<p>While the forms of memory differed, they shared a definite commonality.</p>

<p>It was God.</p>

<p>Based on this, I was able to deduce one fact.</p>

<p>'In trials where a god exists, there is a chance that an NPC will remember the gameplay I did.'</p>

<p>Of course, in Clean3, Dominic wasn't actually present in that space.</p>

<p>However, since the butlers clearly recognized their god and master, it seemed plausible that he “existed.”</p>

<p>Once I thought this far, the fact I had only speculated about until now suddenly loomed large.</p>

<p>If so, the gameplay I performed wasn't just illusory data...</p>

<p>Couldn't it be seen as having actually existed at certain moments?</p>

<p>Isn't that what it means to exist in someone's memory?</p>

<p>The moment my thoughts reached that point, I felt a sense of dizziness.</p>

<p>A fleeting moment where it felt like my breath was being cut off.</p>

<p><em>I stopped thinking.</em></p>

<p><em>It was because it was a ■■ I still couldn't handle.</em></p>

<p>“…….”</p>

<p>I shifted my thoughts in a different direction.</p>

<p>The Chief of Security was continuing to build affection for me by recalling memories through his dreams.</p>

<p>Nevertheless, he must have considered dreams and reality to be separate things.</p>

<p>However, this time, when Octopus Tentacle sent him the ‘Voice of God,’ I realized it completely.</p>

<p>‘Wait a moment.’</p>

<p>I slowly opened my lips.</p>

<p>“Chief of Security… you are not a cleric, so how did you hear the Voice of God? Couldn’t it be a hallucination caused by Neo?”</p>

<p>The Chief of Security stared at me blankly.</p>

<p>It was a look that seemed to know exactly what I was trying to do with a shallow trick.</p>

<p>Even though his eyes were hidden behind a metal mask, that is how it felt.</p>

<p>“They always watch over me from close by.”</p>

<p>As soon as I heard the answer, I was certain.</p>

<p>‘The Chief of Security is a minion of Octopus Tentacle.’</p>

<p>The ‘Voice of God’ he mentioned seemed to be of a similar kind to the messages I send to those I have registered.</p>

<p>Octopus Tentacle has not only changed my mission but is even sending messages to its own minion.</p>

<p>'This is driving me crazy.'</p>

<p>I didn't even know clearly what kind of being God was yet, and with a crisis suddenly looming right over my throat, it was enough to drive me insane.</p>

<p>I racked my brains quickly.</p>

<p>At least I had one precedent to compare it to.</p>

<p>It was CallOVo, where I had met Dominic.</p>

<p>Back then, I couldn't execute a proper strategy.</p>

<p>Because of Dominic's influence, the options in the system window were rearranged on their own, and the key NPCs didn't act as I expected.</p>

<p>The situation now could be considered similar, but there was one major difference from then.</p>

<p>It was that I wasn't the only one who had entered.</p>

<p>'Surely, the others haven't had their missions changed just like mine?'</p>

<p>I could hear the sound of my plan falling apart in real time.</p>

<p>Cold sweat trickled down my back.</p>

<p>&quot;Roasted Chestnut. I know what an excellent prison guard you are.&quot;</p>

<p>The Chief of Security pretended to calmly persuade me.</p>

<p>'This is really driving me crazy.' </p>

<p>It was evident that if words failed to persuade me, he intended to resolve the situation in a different way.</p>

<p>I reminded myself once again that I had not entered this place alone.</p>

<p>And also of what would happen if I failed to clear it with the True Ending.</p>

<p>Feeling the weight of the Pudding Slime resting on my shoulder, I called out to him.</p>

<p>“Chief of Security.”</p>

<p>I lifted the corners of my mouth slightly.</p>

<p>“Actually, I wanted to become a prison guard, too.”</p>

<p>The Chief of Security looked at me silently, as if daring me to say it.</p>

<p>In front of him, I spoke as if lost in sentiment.</p>

<p>“My dream… I don’t remember it clearly, but I felt a vague longing from the moment I first met you, Chief of Security. I also thought it would be nice to work under someone like you.”</p>

<p>“…….”</p>

<p>“I am also interested in the God you believe in, Chief of Security. I, too, wanted to have faith if I ever had the chance. Since God watches over me from close by, isn’t there no reason not to believe?”</p>

<p> Pausing for a moment, I took a deep breath and exhaled.</p>

<p>Letting out a heavy sigh, I continued in a rather gloomy voice.</p>

<p>“However, the reason I left the training center and acted as a medical officer… there is a reason for it.”</p>

<p>“A reason?”</p>

<p>The head of security asked back briefly.</p>

<p>I hesitated for a long time, as if I didn't know what to do.</p>

<p>“Roasted Chestnut. Speak.”</p>

<p>Only when the head of security reprimanded me did I open my mouth, as if I had no choice.</p>

<p>“Actually, I….”</p>

<p>And squeezing my eyes shut, I confessed.</p>

<p>“I am a spy planted by the newly appointed prison warden….”</p>

<p>At my declaration, neither the head of security nor the guards behind me could hide their momentary surprise.</p>

<p>I cautiously opened my eyes again and feigned glancing at them.</p>

<p>“Since the warden holds a weakness against me, I had no choice but to follow orders.”</p>

<p>The prison warden wanted the prison to be filled with his own faction.</p>

<p>I added that I had been forcibly placed in the position of medical officer, and that I had become afraid after seeing the warden's immense power and influence firsthand since arriving here.</p>

<p>This was where it got the most important part.</p>

<p>I very cautiously pulled out my provocation.</p>

<p>“The Chief of Security… well, you are the second-in-command.”</p>

<p>As soon as I finished speaking, the air around me turned cold.</p>

<p>Blinking, I quickly corrected myself as if I had made a mistake.</p>

<p>“Ah, I mean in terms of rank. I am well aware that the real power lies with you, Chief of Security.”</p>

<p>Absolutely not.</p>

<p>The Chief of Security was currently losing ground in the power struggle within the prison.</p>

<p>Despite being relegated to second place in both rank and power, I spoke as if that were not the case at all, speaking as if the Chief of Security were the best.</p>

<p>“That is why I am not in a position to become a prison guard right now. But!”</p>

<p>I smiled from within the metal masks staring at me.</p>

<p>“I have an idea.”</p>

<p>And then I told him about the so-called ‘plan for the Chief of Security’s brilliant future.’</p>

<p>It was a method I wouldn’t have used had the Chief of Security and the prison guards’ favorability ratings not been high.</p>

<p>However, at this point, it was worth taking a gamble.</p>

<p>As expected, the Chief of Security and the guards paid close attention to my remarks.</p>

<p>“I understand. It’s not a bad plan, though.”</p>

<p>The problem was that the Chief of Security’s level of suspicion was by no means low.</p>

<p>“Shouldn’t I have a some insurance, though?”</p>

<p>I heard the clatter of glass against a steel table.</p>

<p>A vial containing neon green liquid rolled in front of me and stopped.</p>

<p>I swallowed a curse under my breath.</p>

<p>The guards approached and untied my ropes.</p>

<p>I reached out and grabbed the vial.</p>

<p>Just then, a pudding slime squirmed down onto my hand holding the vial.</p>

<p>He looked up at me and said,</p>

<p>“You may drink it.”</p>

<p>I saw a blue light that sparkled even more brilliantly than Neo.</p>

<p>Feeling a bit dazed, a pleasant voice offered me calm advice.</p>

<p>“Haven’t you already done this once? The second time will be even easier. Maybe it won’t even bother you.”</p>

<p>Dominic’s voice drifted out languidly.</p>

<p>“Because the young god is growing.”</p>

<p>I gulped down the Neo in one go.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Affection reached 100!</p></div>

<p>I listened to the sound of the Chief of Security’s affection score reaching 100.</p>
