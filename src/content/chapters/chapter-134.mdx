<h1>Chapter 134</h1>

<p>No matter how deep the darkness one hides in, there is no place untouched by the light of the Daeil Yeorae.</p>

<p>Where the light descends, there is nowhere to hide, nor anywhere to flee.</p>

<p>Even shadows vanished in the light that erased the darkness.</p>

<p>It was an impeccably precise strike, cutting across the enemy's most fatal weakness.</p>

<p>However, it was the power of a human against a god.</p>

<p>Not even one-tenth of Light Alteration's true power could be unleashed.</p>

<p>It was little more than a follow-up slash, yet the wound clearly deepened.</p>

<p>Won Taebeom immediately struck the wound with his gauntlet.</p>

<p>The first strike was a golden mark, and the second was a heavy blow.</p>

<p>Boom!</p>

<p>The power was clearly weaker than the previous attack.</p>

<p>However, as the strike landed on the wound that had torn through the outer shell, the shock and vibration were fully absorbed, crushing the tissue.</p>

<p>The wound, which had been nothing more than a thin hairline crack, split open vertically.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh immediately latched onto the eyeball and tore at the wound with his metal hand.</p>

<p>The moment blue blood burst from the forcibly opened wound...</p>

<p>With a chilling clatter, all six metal wings were dismantled into blades.</p>

<p>And they were thrust into the wound.</p>

<p>The blades moved as if each possessed its own will, digging deeper and tearing apart the eyeball.</p>

<p>The sound of metal clashing echoed, and just before a mere scratch escalated into a fatal wound...</p>

<p>A gloomy wrath poured forth.</p>
