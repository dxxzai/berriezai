<h1>Chapter 83</h1>

<p>Smoke was rising from the body of the Flame Dragon that had been burned completely black.</p>

<p>I grabbed a blackened scale with my hand, then held back a groan and pulled it away.</p>

<p>Putting aside the pain from the intense heat, the dragon’s body was still incredibly sturdy, and there was nothing I could do with my own strength.</p>

<p>“Lieutenant Colonel Je! Captain Kwak!”</p>

<p>I turned around.</p>

<p>“Please help me! We have to cut open its stomach. The twins were eaten earlier…!”</p>

<p>Because blood was flowing from my eyes, my vision was blurry.</p>

<p>I couldn’t tell what kind of expressions everyone was looking at me with.</p>

<p>I could only see that they were approaching me.</p>

<p>“Move aside.”</p>

<p>Mo Haein grabbed my shoulder and pushed me away, then placed one foot on the Flame Dragon’s stomach.</p>

<p>Then she stabbed the Black Moon Blade into the lower abdomen where she had removed the scale earlier and widened the opening.</p>

<p>The dragon’s flesh split apart.</p>

<p>However, because the body was so enormous, it seemed like it would take a long time to find the twins even if we crawled inside.</p>

<p>How could we rescue them faster?</p>

<p>It was at that moment, while desperately thinking and trying to widen the opening further.</p>

<p>A hand reached out from inside the stomach.</p>

<p>It wasn’t a child’s hand, but an adult’s hand.</p>

<p>A strange hand I had never seen before.</p>

<p>However, I grabbed the hand extended toward me.</p>

<p>It was an instinctive action.</p>

<p>The moment our skin touched, an incredibly strong heat was transmitted to me.</p>

<p>I felt relieved by a body temperature that wasn’t human.</p>

<p>I gripped the hand tightly and put strength into my legs. Leaning my weight backward, I pulled with all my strength.</p>

<p>Someone drenched completely in a sticky liquid revealed his upper body.</p>

<p>A man with long, flame-red hair hanging down looked at me.</p>

<p>When I met his red eyes, I thought of Infernum for a moment.</p>

<p>But they were completely different.</p>

<p>The red eyes beneath lowered eyelids did not contain hunger directed toward me.</p>

<p>And his face had….</p>

<p>After looking at me for a moment, the man completely pulled himself out.</p>

<p>His torn pieces of clothing barely covered his body, but he did not bother fixing his clothes.</p>

<p>Instead, he immediately reached his hand into the split opening and grabbed something, pulling it out.</p>

<p>Following him, a woman with equally long red hair revealed her face from inside the stomach.</p>

<p>With the man’s help, she completely emerged outside.</p>

<p>Then they suddenly rushed toward me.</p>

<p>“Ugh…!”</p>

<p>They hugged me tightly from both sides, colliding against me with all their strength, and even used their wings to completely cover me.</p>

<p>The Flame Dragon twins, who had grown into adults, looked down at me.</p>

<p>Their height, easily surpassing 2 meters, was slightly surprising.</p>

<p>“Little lamb!”</p>

<p>Ella shouted angrily.</p>

<p>“You, you…! You are foolish and stupid! You idiot!”</p>

<p>Ellun rubbed his face against me and muttered as if talking to himself.</p>

<p>“Why are you such a mess…. You’re hurt so much….”</p>

<p>Only then did I check my own condition.</p>

<p>I was definitely a mess. It seemed to be because I had suffered both frostbite and burns, and because I shed some blood tears at the end.</p>

<p>It didn’t hurt much.</p>

<p>The twins, who must have been slowly melting inside the Flame Dragon’s stomach, had probably been in much more pain and fear than me.</p>

<p>Just as the Flame Dragon had burned from the heat explosion caused by the Ice Dragon’s horn, the twins were also left with burn scars.</p>

<p>Across their entire bodies, including their faces.</p>

<p>However, the scar connecting the twins together looked like the shape of flames.</p>

<p>I smiled slightly and said.</p>

<p>“Ella-nim, Ellun-nim. You’ve become adults. I almost didn’t recognize you.”</p>

<p>“We merely extinguished Infernum’s flames.”</p>

<p>Ella answered curtly, but she moved her body slightly away so that I could see her transformation.</p>

<p>Ellun also followed Ella and gently released me from the tight hug before stepping back.</p>

<p>Neither of them had simply grown in body alone.</p>

<p>Their horns had become larger and harder, and their wings and tails had also grown.</p>

<p>Ellun’s injured wing had healed perfectly and become a new form.</p>

<p>Especially, I liked the horns that had newly grown on top of their heads like crowns.</p>

<p>They were incomparably more magnificent and elegant crown-shaped horns than the horns that appeared on Infernum’s head when he entered his berserk state.</p>

<p>Even though I had played Sla2Plate so many times, the twins had never once grown into adults.</p>

<p>Even when they drove away Infernum in the True Ending and became the owners of the dungeon, they were still children.</p>

<p>But now they had become dignified adult dragons.</p>

<p>A Flame Dragon perfectly suited to the title of lord.</p>

<p>I praised them with all my sincerity.</p>

<p>“You’ve become even more amazing.”</p>

<p>I wanted to compliment them with an incredible expression, but that was the only thing that came to mind.</p>

<p>While regretting my lack of eloquence, Ellun asked.</p>

<p>“Why did you come to save us? It was dangerous.”</p>

<p>I looked around.</p>

<p>Before I knew it, everything had disappeared. Only me, the twins, and the massive body of the Flame Dragon remained.</p>

<p>It was a sign that the ending was approaching.</p>

<p>Only when I had reached the moment of farewell did I answer honestly.</p>

<p>“For a very long time… I wanted to do that.”</p>

<p>Even though I had seen the happy True Ending in the game and finished it, I kept thinking about the Bad Ending where I failed to save them.</p>

<p>Even knowing that a player couldn’t change the predetermined ending of a game, I searched everywhere for even the slightest possibility.</p>

<p>Only now did I feel like I had finally reached the proper True Ending.</p>

<p>Ella and Ellun stared at me blankly.</p>

<p>Their reptilian eyes, with pupils narrowed like blades, stared at me without even blinking once.</p>

<p>The twins reached their hands toward their own heads.</p>

<p>I had been watching without much thought, but I screamed in shock at what they did next.</p>

<p>“Ella-nim! Ellun-nim!”</p>

<p>Crack.</p>

<p>The twins each broke off one of their horns.</p>

<p>Then they pulled my hand over and dropped their horns into my palm.</p>

<p>The two horns placed in my hand became engulfed in flames and transformed into large red jewels.</p>

<p>Unable to move because my hand was being held, I watched the sight helplessly and checked the item.</p>

<p>After looking at the jewel resembling the twins’ flames, I suddenly raised my head.</p>

<p>Because I was afraid that I would part with them again without even saying goodbye.</p>

<p>Fortunately, the twins were still there.</p>

<p>Ella and Ellun looked at me and asked as if they were puzzled.</p>

<p>“Why are you like this? Your face is pale.”</p>

<p>“Ah…. No, it’s nothing.”</p>

<p>I smiled gently and said.</p>

<p>“Thank you for the gift. I’ll treasure it.”</p>

<p>The twins looked satisfied and released my hand.</p>

<p>Ella and Ellun spoke excitedly about this and that.</p>

<p>“Listen, little lamb. We are now the lords of the Flame Dragons. We can do anything we desire.”</p>

<p>“We can visit little lamb’s restaurant whenever we want.”</p>

<p>“That’s right! Little lamb, we won’t even collect rent for your restaurant. Run the store however you wish. No, that’s right, we should make it entirely yours.”</p>

<p>They told me about the things they would do, and wanted to do, as the lords of the Flame Dragons.</p>

<p>I listened to the twins’ words while holding the jewel in my hand.</p>

<p>Then I suddenly thought that I should give them the remaining dishes in my inventory as gifts.</p>

<p>The Phoenix Meringue Cookies should still be left.</p>

<p>Since Ella and Ellun liked them….</p>

<p>“…….”</p>

<p>I let out a powerless laugh as I tried to open my inventory.</p>

<p>A large hand gently touched my back.</p>

<p>All unnecessary emotions and thoughts melted away.</p>

<p>My trembling body, as if struck by a chill, calmed down in an instant.</p>

<p>Dominic smiled while looking at me.</p>

<p>“You really do only like small things.”</p>

<p>I felt gentle warmth from his touch. His quiet voice reassured me even more.</p>

<p>“It’s alright. You’ll become used to it soon.”</p>

<p>He was right. I really did become fine.</p>

<p>With a peaceful heart, I followed Dominic and walked forward.</p>

<p>The place I visited with him was, surprisingly, my restaurant.</p>

<p>It was definitely the restaurant I had worked hard to run in Sla2Plate, but even the exterior had changed greatly.</p>

<p>Unlike before, when I had filled it with expensive decorations that increased reputation points, it had a much more restrained feeling.</p>

<p>The elegant yet luxurious exterior showed the refined taste of its owner.</p>

<p>Owner?</p>

<p>But the owner of the restaurant was….</p>

<p>I had defeated Flame Dragon Infernum, didn’t I?</p>

<p>So the twins became the new owners.</p>

<p>The moment I had that question, something enormous placed behind the restaurant building caught my eye.</p>

<p>The blackened thing, as if it had been burned by flames, was releasing smoke, and it was much larger than the restaurant, looking like a small mountain.</p>

<p>However, it had lost its original shape and was torn apart.</p>

<p>As if someone had ripped it apart like a toy.</p>

<p>Looking at that horribly fragmented thing, a strange thought came to mind.</p>

<p>As if that thing was….</p>

<p>“I had to prepare it quickly, so it is lacking in many ways. I should have removed it beforehand.”</p>

<p>When Dominic gestured, the black hill became dyed with blue light.</p>

<p>It was dyed like a galaxy full of stars, then disappeared in an instant.</p>

<p>As if it had been sucked into a black hole.</p>

<p>“Still, you should like the food.”</p>

<p>At Dominic’s words, I looked at the cleaned backyard.</p>

<p>What had originally been there?</p>

<p>Even though I had just seen it, I couldn’t remember.</p>

<p>As I tried to bring back the memory, Dominic led me forward.</p>

<p>“Shall we go inside? I prepared delicious food for you, Roasted Chestnut.”</p>

<p>Since it refused to come to mind this much, it probably wasn’t an important memory.</p>

<p>And right now, dinner with Dominic was the most important thing.</p>

<p>“Yes, Dominic-ssi.”</p>

<p>I obediently answered and entered the restaurant together with him.</p>
