<h1>Chapter 44</h1>

<p>"Roasted Chestnut! Hey, Chestnut!"</p>

<p>No answer, no matter what name he called. He was cycling through every chestnut-related nickname under the sun.</p>

<p>Before he could summon every creature in the mansion, I hurried outside.</p>

<p>"Tasty Chestnut... oh!"</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk, who'd been shouting with both hands cupped around his mouth like a megaphone, spotted me — then stopped mid-step.</p>

<p>He stood at a distance, eyeing me up and down with suspicion.</p>

<p>I lifted the lantern closer to my face.</p>

<p>"It's me."</p>

<p>But Kwak Hanmuk's suspicion didn't fade easily.</p>

<p>"Question. What was the name of the Trial that Blue Thunder and Chestnut cleared earlier?"</p>

<p>"...Wait, Chestnut?"</p>

<p>"Answer."</p>

<p>"...."</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk was being needlessly suspicious.</p>

<p>There were creatures in the mansion that could mimic voices, but none that could copy appearances.</p>

<p>Not to mention I had no idea where he'd even gotten that nickname.</p>

<p>I swallowed a sigh and answered.</p>

<p>"DeZonDeal. Dead Zone Delivery."</p>

<p>"Hmph. You really are Tasty Chestnut."</p>

<p>Only then did Kwak Hanmuk amble over.</p>

<p>I looked up at him.</p>

<p>"Anything unusual happen?"</p>

<p>"Yeah."</p>

<p>I blinked in surprise, but he didn't tell me what happened — he just asked a question back.</p>

<p>"Kill many Cleaners?"</p>

<p>"Only about six so far..."</p>

<p>I caught on belatedly.</p>

<p>"Did Lieutenant Colonel Je stop by?"</p>

<p>"Uh-huh."</p>

<p>"...Did you also hear him say I could reach the True Ending?"</p>

<p>"Heard it."</p>

<p>Watching him answer so casually made my chest tight.</p>

<p>"Then why last night did you..."</p>

<p>"Wondering why we didn't kill the Cleaners?"</p>

<p>What kind of question was that?</p>

<p>But there wasn't a trace of playfulness on Kwak Hanmuk's face.</p>

<p>Meeting his gray eyes — cold energy swirling in them — I answered.</p>

<p>"Of course I'm curious. And is Captain Mo okay?"</p>

<p>Mo Haein was what worried me most.</p>

<p>I hoped she wasn't suffering alone in the basement, cut off from sunlight.</p>

<p>I'd followed the Cat Butler through the entire East Wing today, but I hadn't even gotten close to the basement.</p>

<p>"Instead of this, why don't we go find Captain Mo right now—"</p>

<p>"Goyo."</p>

<p>"Yes?"</p>

<p>"Better if you don't meet Captain Mo."</p>

<p>"Is she in really bad shape?"</p>

<p>"Actually, Captain Mo—"</p>

<p>Captain Mo was...?</p>

<p>I waited, completely tense, for what would come next.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk said, with an extremely serious face:</p>

<p>"—has a butler allergy."</p>

<p>"...Captain Kwak."</p>

<p>I called his name through gritted teeth, the words practically ground out.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk cackled, then clapped me on the shoulder.</p>

<p>My body swayed under the "encouraging" blow.</p>

<p>"No need to find Captain Mo. You're sticking with me tonight, Goyo hyung-nim. I'll fucking destroy those Cleaners for you."</p>

<p>"Technically, we're feeding the Cleaners to the creatures."</p>

<p>"Right, feeding the creatures. I make a mean ramyun too."</p>

<p>Ramyun had nothing to do with anything, but I gave up on further thought.</p>

<p>At any rate, I was glad to have met up with Kwak Hanmuk.</p>

<p>Unlike Je Hyun-oh, he seemed willing to cooperate. I figured we could hunt Cleaners together first.</p>

<p>"By the way, what's Lieutenant Colonel Je doing?"</p>

<p>"Oh, him? His infection flared up and he went on a solo rampage, so we sent him to the basement. Told him to only kill creatures over there."</p>

<p>That was an extremely plausible statement.</p>

<p>I nodded in understanding. Kwak Hanmuk suddenly spoke.</p>

<p>"Let me ask you one thing."</p>

<p>"Go ahead."</p>

<p>"Did the Rabbit and Cat threaten you? Hit you a few times?"</p>

<p>The butlers hate being called that.</p>

<p>I glanced around, worried someone might be listening.</p>

<p>Fortunately, no Footmen, creatures, or Cleaners were nearby.</p>

<p>"Nothing like that at all. The butlers have been really good to me."</p>

<p>While I was at it, I passed along the news about the spaceship being shot down.</p>

<p>I didn't forget to mention that the butlers had decided to secure a transport ship for me.</p>

<p>"Ha..."</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk let out a long sigh.</p>

<p>"You were better off when your eyes were just glowing..."</p>

<p>Muttering this strange lament, he pressed his hand to his forehead for a moment — then abruptly lifted his head.</p>

<p>"Tasty Chestnut. Want me to hit you once? If I hit you hard as fuck on the head, maybe you'll come to your senses."</p>

<p>"...."</p>

<p>I stared at him, thinking.</p>

<p>Wasn't this the guy in front of me who needed his head hit?</p>

<p>No, actually, on second thought, I didn't think he needed it.</p>

<p>'This is just normal Captain Kwak, isn't it.'</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk must have read the pity in my eyes, because he finally said something normal.</p>

<p>"Fine. I'll help you catch Cleaners."</p>

<p>I started patrolling the mansion with Kwak Hanmuk.</p>

<p>Walking side by side, I gave him a brief briefing.</p>

<p>"Today I plan to feed at least thirty Cleaners to the creatures, then gather information on the third floor of the main building."</p>

<p>"Yeeeah."</p>

<p>"Oh, and if you stay with me, creatures won't attack you."</p>

<p>At that, Kwak Hanmuk tried to hide behind my back.</p>

<p>He crouched down hard, but no matter how much he scrunched, his sides and top were still sticking out.</p>

<p>He placed his hands on my shoulders and declared solemnly:</p>

<p>"Let's go, Goyo hyung-nim."</p>

<p>"...Yes."</p>

<p>I decided to just go along with Kwak Hanmuk's rhythm.</p>

<p>We walked a bit with him on my back — then I spotted a tall figure moving silently.</p>

<p>A metal face turned toward me.</p>

<p>It was a Footman.</p>

<p>"Butler."</p>

<p>The Footman approached me the moment he spotted me, then tilted his head at Kwak Hanmuk hiding behind me.</p>

<p>He looked puzzled about why a Cleaner was with me.</p>

<p>And with his hands on my shoulders, no less — it was understandable that he found it strange.</p>

<p>The only part of the Footman's face that could show emotion — his mouth — twitched.</p>

<p>His lips pulled downward as he glared at Kwak Hanmuk, not hiding his displeasure.</p>

<p>He looked like he wanted to tear Kwak Hanmuk off me immediately.</p>

<p>The Footman bent at the waist and brought his face close to mine.</p>

<p>"If you'll pardon my impertinence — may I dispose of that Cleaner for you?"</p>

<p>"It's fine, Alpha."</p>

<p>When I called his name, the Footman's mouth corners turned up slightly.</p>

<p>I jerked my chin toward Kwak Hanmuk.</p>

<p>"He's my lunchbox."</p>

<p>"...!"</p>

<p>A look of realization crossed the Footman's face, and he immediately apologized.</p>

<p>"Please forgive my rudeness, born of ignorance."</p>

<p>I generously forgave him, then did some traffic control to avoid unnecessary encounters.</p>

<p>"I'll check the third floor of the main building myself. Pass that on to the other Footmen."</p>

<p>I patted his shoulder, telling him to work hard. The Footman bowed his head repeatedly, looking honored.</p>

<p>I started walking again.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk, who'd been clinging to me like a turtle shell the whole time, finally spoke.</p>

<p>"You're practically a butler now."</p>

<p>"I'm still an Apprentice Butler."</p>

<p>I was just acting according to my role.</p>

<p>Just then, I spotted a Cleaner in the distance.</p>

<p>It was stumbling around, not yet adjusted to the darkness — seemed easy to lure.</p>

<p>I was about to shake the lantern to call it over when Kwak Hanmuk grabbed my wrist.</p>

<p>"What now?"</p>

<p>I asked curtly. Kwak Hanmuk whispered:</p>

<p>"But why the third floor of the main building? What info are you gathering?"</p>

<p>"...."</p>

<p>"You ignoring your lunchbox? Spill it."</p>

<p>"...It's not that I'm ignoring you."</p>

<p>I didn't want to tell him because he'd probably make a fuss about it.</p>

<p>But if I didn't, he'd probably keep this up all night.</p>

<p>"I'm trying to gather information about the Master of the mansion."</p>

<p>"The Master...?"</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk reacted as if he'd never heard the term before.</p>

<p>He chewed on my words, then muttered:</p>

<p>"Well, I guess if there are butlers, there's a master."</p>

<p>"Yes. The butlers of the mansion have been waiting for the Master all this time. I think the Master is connected to the True Ending."</p>

<p>"Then we should head to the main building first."</p>

<p>He argued that since the Footmen were patrolling today and Lieutenant Colonel Je was locked in the basement, the Cleaner supply wouldn't be a problem.</p>

<p>He was right — getting information about the Master was urgent too.</p>

<p>I decided to go to the third floor of the main building first.</p>

<p>On the way, we ran into several creatures, but we just exchanged brief glances and passed by.</p>

<p>We also encountered Cleaners, but they didn't notice my presence unless I shook the lantern to call them.</p>

<p>This seemed to be another ability I'd gained from becoming an Apprentice Butler.</p>

<p>I wondered what abilities I'd get if I became a full Butler.</p>

<p>If I could shoot laser beams, the first thing I'd do is fire one at Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>I was indulging in this silly fantasy when we reached the first floor of the main building.</p>

<p>GRRRRR...</p>

<p>Suddenly, the mansion rumbled lowly.</p>

<p>The tremor felt like a shallow earthquake. I stopped in my tracks, eyes wide.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk cursed from behind me.</p>

<p>"Ah, fuck. Lieutenant Colonel Je couldn't hold it in."</p>

<p>Something had clearly happened to the mansion.</p>

<p>My heart plummeted.</p>

<p>I tried to run forward.</p>

<p>But I couldn't move the way I wanted. Kwak Hanmuk's hand pressed down hard on my shoulder.</p>

<p>"Captain... What are you doing? Let me go."</p>

<p>"Nah, don't feel like it."</p>

<p>We started wrestling.</p>

<p>I swung my elbow back, aiming for his solar plexus.</p>

<p>But close-quarters combat against him was hopeless.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk grabbed my forearm, impressed.</p>

<p>"Whoa, Goyo hyung-nim can throw a punch?"</p>

<p>Then I nearly headbutted his chin.</p>

<p>As we tussled, the mansion rumbled again.</p>

<p>This time it was stronger, longer.</p>

<p>The chandeliers on the ceiling swung in uneasy arcs, crystals clattering against each other —</p>

<p>CRASH!</p>

<p>Every window in the mansion shattered at once.</p>

<p>Shards of glass poured inside, and moonlight flooded in.</p>

<p>The darkness that had shrouded the mansion grew hazy.</p>

<p>The bright moonlight touched me too.</p>

<p>I looked up at the night sky.</p>

<p>Countless glittering stars, an unrealistically huge moon — and...</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh, wings spread wide.</p>

<p>He had attacked the mansion.</p>

<p>While I was distracted by Je Hyun-oh for a moment, Kwak Hanmuk wrapped me in his Zelkova wood bead rosary.</p>

<p>Bound from head to toe, I stared at him in disbelief.</p>

<p>"What the hell are you doing?! If you do this, the True Ending— ugh!"</p>

<p>And then my mouth was covered too.</p>

<p>I thrashed wildly. A bead flew off and smacked me in the forehead.</p>

<p>I was glaring at Kwak Hanmuk when the next sight made me jolt.</p>

<p>BOOM!</p>

<p>A small Cleaner kicked the front door open and burst inside.</p>

<p>'Captain Mo?'</p>

<p>It was Mo Haein, holding the Black Moon Blade.</p>

<p>The shattered front door scattered at her feet.</p>

<p>Without a single disrupted breath, she swept her sharp gaze across the interior — then found me.</p>

<p>For an instant, Mo Haein's face twisted.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk hurried toward her, me still bound in the rosary, dragged through the air.</p>

<p>Mo Haein called out urgently:</p>

<p>"What's the Trainee's status!"</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk answered quickly.</p>

<p>"No aggression. At least not toward us. Definitely not a kill order — with some luck, he might not even need the special isolation facility."</p>

<p>From my perspective, this was incredibly unfair.</p>

<p>'So now I'm being treated as infected?'</p>

<p>I'd become an Apprentice Butler, but I definitely wasn't infected. I was still perfectly lucid.</p>

<p>But wrapped in the rosary, I had no way to prove I was normal.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein dragged me outside the mansion.</p>

<p>"Lieutenant Colonel Je!"</p>

<p>Mo Haein shouted.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh immediately swung his metal hand toward the mansion.</p>

<p>CLANG!</p>

<p>Sparks flew.</p>

<p>An invisible barrier blocked him.</p>
<br/>
<em><h3>"How dare you...!"</h3></em>
<br/>
<p>A voice so enormous it felt like it would tear my eardrums pressed down on my whole body.</p>

<p>Mo Haein and Kwak Hanmuk coughed up blood.</p>
<br/>
<em><h3>"You lowly wretches dare...!"</h3></em>
<br/>
<p>Sparks crackled in the empty sky.</p>

<p>Between the flashing lights, the butlers appeared.</p>

<p>The Cat Butler, holding a long spear with a sun-shaped circular blade.</p>

<p>And the Rabbit Butler, holding a long spear with a crescent moon-shaped blade.</p>

<p>The Cat Butler raised his sun spear, erupting in fury.</p>
<br/>
<em><h3>"You will not die an easy death!"</h3></em>
<br/>
<p>As if delivering a final judgment, his stern voice made my head ring —</p>
<br/>
<em><h3>"...Roasted Chestnut?"</h3></em>
<br/>
<p>The Rabbit Butler had spotted me.</p>
