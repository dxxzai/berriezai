<h1>Chapter 71</h1>

<p>I look at the scene unfolding before my eyes.</p>

<p>A city street at night with Christmas carols playing.</p>

<p>Two men in casual clothes are walking together.</p>

<p>'I' open my mouth, letting out a puff of white breath.</p>

<p>The one walking beside me with his hands shoved into his pockets speaks.</p>
<br/>
<em><p>"Hey, they're selling Roasted Chestnut over there."</p></em>
<br/>
<p>Then he nudges 'me' with his elbow.</p>
<br/>
<p><em>"Hyung, wanna get some Roasted Chestnut?"</em></p>

<p><em>"Aren't you sick of them already?"</em></p>
<br/>
<p>Even while saying that, 'I' am already walking toward the roasted chestnut truck.</p>
<br/>
<em><p>"Hyung, isn't practice brutal?"</p></em>

<em><p>"Not really. It's fun, actually.... Are you having a hard time?"</p></em>
<br/>
<p>"Haha."</p>

<p>A hearty laugh rings out. He grins and shoots a question right back.</p>
<br/>
<em><p>"I get to play games with you, don't I, Hyung?"</p></em>
<br/>
<p>At the remark asking how it could be hard when I win all the time, 'I' can't help laughing too.</p>
<br/>
<em><p>"Ah, I want to debut already and play in an official match."</p></em>

<p><em>"Me too. I wish we had a match tomorrow."</em></p>

<em><p>"That's a bit much."</p></em>
<br/>
<p>The two of them chat about nothing in particular as they stop in front of the roasted chestnut truck.</p>

<p>I watch the smiling 'me.'</p>

<p>Continuing to stare.</p>

<p>Unable to look away.</p>

<p>"Roasted Chestnut."</p>

<p>"......"</p>

<p>"Roasted Chestnut."</p>

<p>"......"</p>

<p>"Roasted Chestnut."</p>

<p>"...Yes."</p>

<p>"When was this?"</p>

<p>"Before I debuted as a pro player... back when I was still training."</p>

<p>"I see. Could you tell me more about your past, Roasted Chestnut?"</p>

<p>"......"</p>

<p>"Roasted Chestnut."</p>

<p>"...Ah, sorry. Just for a moment."</p>

<p>"Why are you shaking like this?"</p>

<p>"......"</p>

<p>"Shall we leave now? Learning about Roasted Chestnut is enjoyable, but it seems to be painful for you."</p>

<p>"...I want to leave. Please...."</p>

<p>"Of course, Roasted Chestnut. Let's go outside together. Close your eyes. Slowly breathe in and out."</p>

<p>"......"</p>

<p>"You're doing well. We'll be outside soon, so just keep your eyes closed and wait a little longer. Just a little. You can do that, can't you, Roasted Chestnut?"</p>

<p>"Yes...."</p>

<p>"Excellent. Then."</p>
<hr class="calibre3"/>
<p>I slowly opened my eyes.</p>

<p>My face was soaked in cold sweat. I tried to wipe away the beads running down my face, but my hands were shaking too much.</p>

<p>Forcing strength into them, I rubbed my face roughly.</p>

<p>Only then did I finally regain my senses.</p>

<p>"Roasted Chestnut. Are you all right?"</p>

<p>Something soft pressed against my cheek. I thanked the slime clinging to the side of my face.</p>

<p>"Dominic-ssi.... Thank you for getting me out...."</p>

<p>'That was close.'</p>

<p>Gorgos, the hidden boss on the 88th floor of the daytime dungeon, was a troublesome enemy who specialized in illusion magic.</p>

<p>He wasn't friendly toward humans, making him especially cruel.</p>

<p>Normally, you were supposed to challenge him after eating monster cuisine to gain a mental resistance buff, but he had suddenly appeared on the 45th floor, leaving me no chance to prepare.</p>

<p>In the game, being caught in Gorgos's illusion meant staring at a black screen for sixty seconds while gentle, soothing music played.</p>

<p>When the sixty seconds ended, a system window would appear.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_pixel"><p>Everything you have ever wanted exists in this eternal world.</p></div>

<p>At that moment, flowers would begin falling across the screen, and you had to figure out the pattern and press the correct directional keys.</p>

<p>If you failed, the duration of the illusion doubled each time, and remaining trapped for over thirty minutes resulted in death.</p>

<p>The game gave players a key-pressing mechanic.</p>

<p>Reality gave me nothing.</p>

<p>I had to break free on my own.</p>

<p>If Dominic hadn't helped me, I didn't think I could have escaped.</p>

<p>Why had Gorgos appeared on the 45th floor?</p>

<p>Suddenly, something that had happened in DeZonDeal came to mind.</p>

<p>It felt strangely similar to when the emergency generator had suddenly broken down.</p>

<p>But that wasn't my priority right now.</p>

<p>I filed the uncertain connection away for later and turned my attention to Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>He was still standing there like a statue.</p>

<p>'I have to defeat Gorgos.'</p>

<p>Unless I defeated the hidden boss, there was probably no way to free Je Hyun-oh from the illusion.</p>

<p>While playing the Archive game, I'd explored both the game's version of Korea and the Agency of Extraordinary Affairs, uncovering plenty of information about the characters.</p>

<p>But compared to everyone else, there was surprisingly little information about Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>Just what kind of illusion had trapped him so completely?</p>

<p>One thing was certain.</p>

<p>I had to get him out before thirty minutes passed.</p>

<p>I looked at the goat-headed creature before me.</p>

<p>'Can I beat it by myself?'</p>

<p>The answer came immediately.</p>

<p>Impossible.</p>

<p>I knew Gorgos's skills and every strategy to beat him by heart, but the limitations of my class were simply too great.</p>

<p>A chef was a support class.</p>

<p>Maybe if I'd come fully prepared...</p>

<p>But in a situation like this, I needed a damage dealer.</p>

<p>'Wait.'</p>

<p>I called to the slime that had climbed back down from my cheek to my shoulder.</p>

<p>"Dominic-ssi."</p>

<p>"Yes, Roasted Chestnut."</p>

<p>"Could you fight that goat-headed thing together with me?"</p>

<p>"Of course. I'll defeat it immediately, Roasted Chestnut."</p>

<p>I hurriedly stopped him.</p>

<p>"No. You can only use magic. Not magic you create yourself—the skills this place gives you."</p>

<p>"Hm. Understood."</p>

<p>The hidden boss from the 88th floor had appeared on the 45th.</p>

<p>If Dominic used his own power during this boss fight, it might create an even worse error than the current one.</p>

<p>And he couldn't contribute too much, either.</p>

<p>If he ended up with the highest contribution, explaining things later would become impossible.</p>

<p>"What level are you?"</p>

<p>"Fifty-one."</p>

<p>Thanks to today's daytime dungeon run, everyone's levels had skyrocketed.</p>

<p>Even so, we were still far below what was recommended for the hidden boss on the 88th floor.</p>

<p>Still...</p>

<p>I felt like we might somehow manage.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Supreme Rock Salt: A high-purity chunk of salt ore with no impurities. Perfect for high-grade cooking.</p></div></div>

<p>I pulled out the rock salt I'd obtained from the hidden boss on the 33rd floor.</p>

<p>During Gorgos's first phase, he used illusion magic to create dozens of clones identical to himself.</p>

<p>Anyone holding the rock salt would always be targeted by the real body.</p>

<p>Back in the game, every time Gorgos filled the arena with illusions, the party would inevitably be wiped out.</p>

<p>I'd challenged him around seventy times trying to find a countermeasure before finally discovering this trick.</p>

<p>Apparently, despite having a goat's head, Gorgos still retained some beastly instincts, making him react to the scent of the rock salt.</p>

<p>"Dominic-ssi. Learn the Blazing Beam skill, and level it to the maximum."</p>

<p>A strange light flickered within the goat's crimson eyes.</p>

<p>Like the conductor of a choir, Gorgos swept his thorn-covered staff through the air.</p>

<p>The skulls inside the net bag let out horrifying screams as the boss battle music began.</p>

<p>The shrill cries of the skulls and the orchestra clashed together into a dreadful harmony.</p>

<p>The moment the screaming reached its peak, black hazard zones spread across the floor.</p>

<p>I knew exactly when and where they would appear.</p>

<p>Dodging them was easy, even with a chef's stats.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh, trapped in the illusion, continued losing health, but that alone wasn't enough to kill someone like him.</p>

<p>Once I avoided every hazard zone, Gorgos's eyes flashed crimson.</p>

<p>Dozens of illusions filled the cramped room.</p>

<p>Being surrounded by countless goat heads wasn't exactly pleasant.</p>

<p>Every Gorgos charged at me simultaneously.</p>

<p>Gripping the rock salt tightly, I ran and shouted,</p>

<p>"Dominic-ssi! Fire the beam at the one that's actually swinging its staff!"</p>

<p>"This spell is... rather old-fashioned. But if that's what Roasted Chestnut wants."</p>

<p>A small magic circle appeared before the slime.</p>

<p>A deep hum echoed as magical energy condensed.</p>

<p>Then came a sharp screech like glass being scraped.</p>

<p>A beam of blue mana struck the goat-headed monster trying to bind me with thorny vines.</p>
<br/>
<em><p>"Kyaaaagh!"</p></em>
<br/>
<p>All the illusions vanished at once as Gorgos crashed onto the floor.</p>

<p>Blood-like tears streamed from his eyes as he ripped open the net bag.</p>

<p>The trapped skulls burst free, screaming.</p>

<p>The tails of his black-flame tuxedo stretched outward, covering the entire floor.</p>

<p>Black flames engulfed the entire 45th floor, continuously draining my health.</p>

<p>I stared at Gorgos in shock.</p>

<p>'That's the phase three mechanic.'</p>

<p>A single Blazing Beam had dealt enough damage to skip phase two entirely.</p>

<p>That wasn't supposed to happen.</p>

<p>Just as the thought crossed my mind—</p>

<p>The skulls shrieked, launching black fireballs from every direction.</p>

<p>Quickly dodging them, I looked back toward Gorgos.</p>

<p>Like a mad conductor, he swung his staff.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Status Ailment: 'Charmed'<br/>
Duration: 30 seconds</p></div></div>

<p>It was a spell that captivated the victim's mind, forcing them to approach him.</p>

<p>My body stiffened.</p>

<p>My limbs moved on their own.</p>

<p>A smile spread across my face against my will.</p>

<p>As I walked toward Gorgos against my own intentions, I silently counted the seconds.</p>

<p>The moment I got close under the effects of Charm would be my opportunity.</p>

<p>"Dominic-ssi. After Charm ends, there will be a 1.7-second window where the staff glows just before the goat head casts illusion magic again. Hit its glass horn with the beam then."</p>

<p>The glass horns normally had complete invulnerability.</p>

<p>But during the instant before Gorgos cast a spell, an attack delivered from extremely close range could damage them.</p>

<p>Destroying the glass horns caused the black smoke sealed inside them to surge backward.</p>

<p>The skulls' funeral dirge would stop.</p>

<p>Gorgos would become temporarily incapacitated.</p>

<p>That was the opening.</p>

<p>If we focused our attacks then, even my current level and class could defeat him.</p>

<p>The timing required incredible precision.</p>

<p>But if it was Dominic, he could do it.</p>

<p>"Understood, Roasted Chestnut. But what is Charm? Is that why you're walking toward the goat head?"</p>

<p>I wanted to explain.</p>

<p>But the closer I got to Gorgos, the stronger Charm's influence became.</p>

<p>I could barely speak.</p>

<p>"Dominic-ssi.... Please...."</p>

<p>The moment I reached Gorgos, thorny vines burst from his staff and wrapped around me.</p>

<p>The thorns dug painfully into my skin.</p>

<p>Blood seeped from every puncture wound, staining my clothes red.</p>

<p>Then—</p>

<p>Gorgos suddenly trembled.</p>

<p>He violently shook his head before staring straight at me with those crimson eyes glowing strangely.</p>

<p>I had no idea what was wrong with him.</p>

<p>I just wished he'd hurry up and cast the illusion spell.</p>

<p>Maybe it was my imagination, but the thorny vines seemed to multiply, wrapping even tighter around my body.</p>

<p>Suppressing a groan, I waited.</p>

<p>Then—</p>

<p>A high-pitched whine.</p>

<p>The sound of mana gathering.</p>

<p>Gorgos's staff hadn't begun glowing yet.</p>

<p>But Charm left me unable to say anything.</p>

<p>The sound this time was different from before.</p>

<p>It was threatening.</p>

<p>Something that awakened instinctive fear deep inside me.</p>

<p>My body froze.</p>

<p>Then came a piercing sound.</p>

<p>A beam fired.</p>

<p>But it wasn't Blazing Beam.</p>

<p>A beautiful ribbon of light streaked through the air.</p>

<p>Nebulas shimmered within it like the Milky Way itself.</p>

<p>The birth and death of stars flowed together inside that beam.</p>

<p>It struck the goat-headed monster.</p>

<p>Gorgos began to crumple.</p>

<p>As though an invisible giant hand had seized him, his entire body compressed inward.</p>

<p>Bones folded and snapped.</p>

<p>Crunch.</p>

<p>Crack.</p>

<p>The sound of his skeleton collapsing echoed horribly.</p>

<p>Just as Gorgos's body reached its limit—</p>

<p>A blinding blue explosion erupted.</p>

<p>"...!"</p>

<p>I squeezed my eyes shut.</p>

<p>When I opened them again—</p>

<p>Gorgos was gone.</p>

<p>Only cold stardust drifted through the air before slowly settling.</p>

<p>At the very center of where he had stood, a pitch-black hole remained.</p>

<p>Countless eyes stared out from within it.</p>

<p>The distortion, like a miniature black hole, swallowed every last speck of stardust before quietly disappearing.</p>

<p>A corrupted system window appeared.</p>

<div class="systembox glitch_b3"><p><span class="glitch_t2">The hidden boss of the 88th floor, Gorgos, has died!</span></p></div>

<p>A hidden boss that was supposed to flee...</p>

<p>Had died.</p>

<p>As I stood there in stunned silence, Dominic apologized politely.</p>

<p>"I'm sorry. Roasted Chestnut was injured, so I panicked and made a mistake."</p>

<p>His quiet apology—that he hadn't been able to use the designated spell—drifted into my ears.</p>

<p>If he made another mistake like that...</p>

<p>I had a feeling the dungeon itself would disappear.</p>

<p>Before I could calm my racing heart—</p>

<p>"By the way, Roasted Chestnut."</p>

<p>Dominic floated close to my face.</p>

<p>His little ears brushed against my cheek.</p>

<p>"That berserker seems a little strange. Shouldn't we restrain him?"</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh, finally freed from the illusion, was staring directly at me.</p>
