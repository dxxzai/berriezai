<h1>Chapter 152</h1>

<p>That night, I lay awake all night.</p>

<p>It wasn't just me; Kwak Hanmuk did the same.</p>

<p>At my request, he tied my wrists with his prayer beads.</p>

<p>It was a restaint I could undo if I wanted to, but I still wanted to have a safety net in place.</p>

<p>After binding my wrists, Kwak Hanmuk escorted me to my room.</p>

<p>“Crazy Chestnut, stop thinking useless thoughts and go to bed early. Otherwise, you won't grow tall.”</p>

<p>He made sure I was tucked into bed before returning to his own room, where he spent a long time talking to someone on the phone.</p>

<p>It seemed to be Samra.</p>

<p>Since the sleepless android collects information 24 hours a day, he must have realized the situation immediately.</p>

<p>I looked up at the ceiling alone, trying to sort out the clutter in my head.</p>

<p>“You’ve gained the 'Blossom' ability.”</p>

<p>The Pudding Slime on my chest spoke in a tone that didn't seem particularly pleased.</p>

<p>“It would be better to cultivate divine power rather than your subordinates.”</p>

<p>While he spoke as if he had no choice but to yield to my wishes, he subtly emphasized the uselessness of Blossom.</p>

<p>I let Pudding Slime’s words go in one ear and out the other.</p>

<p>I didn’t want to listen to the slime’s advice to gouge out eyes right now.</p>

<p>Judging by the current situation, cultivating divine power carried a significant risk.</p>

<p>As I am now, there was a high probability that I would be unable to handle the power and would lose my humanity.</p>

<p>Furthermore, the threat of the Outer Gods had now appeared, alongside Trial Meteorites.</p>

<p>To counter this, it was clearly necessary for my subordinates to become stronger than they were now.</p>

<p>Therefore, Blossom was the most appropriate and necessary ability for my current situation.</p>

<p>However, even though I thought that, I could not bring myself to use the ability immediately.</p>

<p>It was because I was afraid.</p>

<p>At least for tonight, I did not want my eye color to change again.</p>

<p>I slowly lowered my gaze. I felt a little relieved as the prayer beads wrapped around my wrist came into view.</p>

<p>I spoke to the system inwardly.</p>

<p>‘Isn’t there a way to do this? I’m completely outmatched right now.’</p>

<p>The system remained silent once again.</p>

<p>After all, if the system had known how I could coexist with my divine self, it would have told me long ago.</p>

<p>It seems that becoming a god while maintaining my sanity is exactly what the system wants.</p>

<p>I looked up at the ceiling again.</p>

<p>“.......”</p>

<p>Come to think of it, things had been strange for a while.</p>

<p>I had been abnormal since I began discussing the contract with Director Jang Seokyun regarding the TRA.</p>

<p>According to my original plan, I intended to calmly threaten him using the contract.</p>

<p>However, at that time, I was steeped in arrogance.</p>

<p>I scoffed at the act of a mere human attempting to bind a god, and I showed Jang Seokyun what kind of being a god truly is.</p>

<p>I didn't even feel wary about exposing the young god; I simply used it as a convenient means to an end.</p>

<p>It was the same from then on.</p>

<p>I was not in my right mind even when I went to Paseong to find CEO Cha.</p>

<p>Nevertheless, completely unaware of it, I unhesitatingly looked into the paranoid researcher in PTB and destroyed his mind.</p>

<p>It seemed that from the moment Je Hyun-oh entered the Trial, I had been completely consumed by the ego of a young god.</p>

<p>I focused all my attention solely on the subordinate who came to find me.</p>

<p>I had even entertained the dangerous notion of tossing CEO Cha aside like a toy to vent my frustration.</p>

<p>Nothing changed even after the Trial was closed.</p>

<p>Even seeing CEO Cha so severely injured that a pool of blood was forming, I merely thought of it as him paying the price.</p>

<p>I only schemed to find out what CEO Cha was hiding.</p>

<p>Perhaps CEO Cha... might have noticed my condition.</p>

<p>Given his trait as a Non-believer and his sharp intuition, the possibility was quite high.</p>

<p>Furthermore, he had said things that were particularly provocative to me.</p>

<p>It was the same after that. CEO Cha directly dug into the parts I had kept hidden.</p>
<br />
<p><span class="flashback">“Playing god seems to suit you. You haven't even bothered to look for a way to return to being human.”</span></p>
<br />
<p>It wasn't just about wanting to prevent Earth's destruction.</p>

<p>Everything I possessed right now...</p>

<p>...was something I could only have obtained because I had become a god.</p>

<p>I did not want to return to being human.</p>

<p>Even though I tried to ignore it, CEO Cha kept provoking me, and eventually, everything I had been holding back exploded all at once.</p>

<p>I had already defined him as a stranger in Cell-X.</p>

<p>I even tried to subtly test if he remembered playing with me, but I concluded that he was a different being from the CEO Cha I used to like.</p>

<p>That is why I wanted to understand him, even just a little bit.</p>

<p>I wanted to know why on earth he was acting that way toward me, and why he wanted me to be human.</p>

<p>But CEO Cha told me nothing.</p>

<p>His attitude was that I shouldn't try to find out the reason, but just accept whatever he gave me.</p>

<p>While he was relentlessly haranguing me, he was also subjecting himself to electric shocks as if his own body didn't matter...</p>

<p>At that moment, I really couldn't hold back my anger.</p>

<p>‘And even after that, he was severely injured by Lieutenant Colonel Je.’</p>

<p>The fleeting moment our eyes met kept coming back to me.</p>

<p>I couldn't shake the feeling that I was the reason CEO Cha had been so clearly overpowered in his battle against Je Hyun-oh. </p>

<p>Lost in thought for a while, I picked up the cell phone I had placed beside me.</p>

<p>Although my wrists were tied, I could at least browse through the call log.</p>

<p>I stopped at one of the few entries on the list.</p>

<div class="fieldset"><p>Director Mok Saehwa of Paseong</p></div>

<p>And I stared at the only Paseong contact I knew for a long time.</p>

<hr />

<p>The next day, I reported the Special Mission Team office at the TRA headquarters.</p>

<p>The building had been completely restored, as if the gaping hole torn in its side had never existed.</p>

<p>I had heard that Samra had provided significant technical assistance with the restoration of the TRA, and the building seemed to have improved compared to before.</p>

<p>In particular, the Special Mission Team office felt strangely dazzling, even though it looked exactly the same as before.</p>

<p>Excluding Je Hyun-oh, who had not yet emerged from the Trial, I briefly explained the concept of gods and their subordinates to Samra and the Captains.</p>

<p>Mo Haein was flustered at first, but she understood the situation well.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk summarized it in a single sentence, as if it were no big deal.</p>

<p>&quot;So, it just means we have to serve you well as our Master, right?&quot;</p>

<p>I felt my ears turning red from the shock of what I had done to him last night.</p>

<p>I earnestly tried to explain myself to Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein.</p>

<p>&quot;No, that is absolutely not the case. It is just that I can exert influence over you Captains in some way...&quot;</p>

<p>When I emphasized that a dangerous situation could arise, Mo Haein slowly nodded.</p>

<p>Since Mo Haein had also been informed of the events of the previous night, she understood the implications.</p>

<p>Next, I confided in them in some detail about the situation I was currently facing.</p>

<p>I had to coexist with the <em>young god</em>, but at this rate, I was on the verge of being consumed.</p>

<p>I had to find a way to regain my balance somehow.</p>

<p>I had come to my senses after seeing the blue lightning Kwak Hanmuk created, but that was not a means I could use repeatedly.</p>

<p>Merely keeping a god sealed within a physical body was already pushing the limits.</p>

<p>The situation was so unmanageable that prayer beads and tattoos had to be used, so there was nothing to be gained by repeatedly tampering with the seal.</p>

<p>In fact, Kwak Hanmuk had coughed up blood. If I pushed it any further, it might be a truly serious matter.</p>

<p>“For now, I intend to try my best to prevent my eye color from changing. If I start acting strangely, please smack some sense into me.”</p>

<p>And I, too, planned to find my own way to coexist with the <em>young god</em> within me.</p>

<p>When it came to matters concerning the god, seeking an answer from a god was the most correct course of action.</p>

<p>However, Dominic was out of the question, and needless to say, the Administrator was a bit of a stretch.</p>

<p>It seemed they wouldn't understand the concept of wanting to maintain a human ego.</p>

<p>Then, the only one left was Black Block-ssi...</p>

<p>Although it felt a bit iffy, he seemed like a decent target.</p>

<p>After all, he was interested by my very existence.</p>

<p>He would likely view my attempt to coexist with the <em>young god</em> most positively.</p>

<p>I planned to cook a meal and try to communicate with Black Block-ssi once I entered a trial soon.</p>

<p>While I was lost in thought, Kwak Hanmuk rustled around, trying to open a bag of snacks.</p>

<p>Mo Haein reached out and snatched the bag away.</p>

<p>Whenever he tried to open one, he’d end up bursting the bag about one-third of the time.</p>

<p>As she placed the opened bag of snacks in front of him, Kwak Hanmuk stopped picking up the potato chips and glanced at the Pudding Slime.</p>

<p>Perhaps because I had introduced it as my pet... He seemed curious about what the Pudding Slime ate.</p>

<p>Fortunately, he only showed curiosity and didn't poke him with the snack he was holding.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk lamented while eating his snack.</p>

<p>“Captain Mo. You know, last night... do you know how hard it was for me to serve Master Crazy Chestnut?”</p>

<p>“Captain Kwak...!”</p>

<p>“Crazy Chestnut...he's become a real handful.”</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk teased me while giggling. Mo Haein brushed off Kwak Hanmuk’s words and called out to me.</p>

<p>“Trainee.”</p>

<p>She stared at me blankly and asked.</p>

<p>“Are you okay?”</p>

<p>“...Ah, I’m fine.”</p>

<p>I didn’t expect to hear a question expressing concern for me, so my reaction was a little delayed.</p>

<p>I swallowed the words that were tickling deep in my throat.</p>

<p>I wanted to ask Mo Haein about the meteorite.</p>

<p>However, since this must be a sensitive matter for her, I shouldn't mention it rashly.</p>

<p>I was waiting for the most natural opportunity to arise.</p>

<p>Mo Haein looked at me for a moment, then spoke calmly.</p>

<p>“It looks like Lieutenant Colonel Je will be coming out of the Trial sometime this afternoon, so let’s all get together briefly this evening to celebrate the move.”</p>

<p>With those words, the meeting ended, and Mo Haein stood up first.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk also disappeared with her, saying he would finish his work quickly and come back later this evening.</p>

<p>It seemed they had been so busy dealing with Geumcheon lately that they couldn't even enter the Trial.</p>

<p>Since Je Hyun-oh had to enter the Trial more frequently, he, too, was constantly busy without a break.</p>

<p>Only I, the trainee, was free.</p>

<p>To be precise, I was someone who would become a trainee, though.</p>

<p>“Here is the Trial Response Agency contract.”</p>

<p>As soon as we were left alone, Samra handed me the contract.</p>

<p>Samra had drafted the contract on behalf of Director Jang Seokyun, who was on an overseas business trip for the establishment of an international organization.</p>

<p>The contents of the contract were exactly as I had wanted.</p>

<p>There were even some parts that were slightly more favorable, and it seemed Samra had adjusted those details.</p>

<p>The continued existence of the Special Mission Team was a basic condition, and it guaranteed a reasonable salary and a reasonable amount of freedom, among other things.</p>

<p>Furthermore, I could terminate the contract at any time if I wished.</p>

<p>I intended to maintain the rank of trainee.</p>

<p>I was already an outlier to begin with, so I didn't want to stand out even more by suddenly becoming a Captain as Jang Seokyun had suggested.</p>

<p>Besides, I actually needed to undergo training.</p>

<p>I still didn't know how to salute properly, nor did I know how to shoot a gun.</p>

<p>I wanted to receive formal training, complete my trainee period, and then be promoted sequentially.</p>

<p>Samra watched intently as I signed the contract for the TRA.</p>

<p>And as soon as I finished signing, he calmly and swiftly gathered the contract.</p>

<p>“I look forward to working with you, Samra.”</p>

<p>Samra smiled gently at my greeting.</p>

<p>It was a smile so natural that, were it not for his holographic coloring, no one would have guessed he was an android.</p>

<p>However, Samra’s smile vanished quickly.</p>

<p>It was because I had said I would complete the training just like the other trainees.</p>

<p>“Han Goyo-ssi, you do not need to complete the trainee training course.”</p>

<p>Samra objected, saying it was unnecessary, but for me, I wanted to receive training.</p>

<p>The creator provided me with only filtered information.</p>

<p>Since training for trainees will start with the very basics, I might learn facts I didn't know while attending the lessons.</p>

<p>Samra eventually agreed to my wishes, and we continued our conversation.</p>

<p>“We are tracking the connection between the Outer Gods and the Trial Meteorites.”</p>

<p>In response to the meteorite from Cell-X, existing meteorites that had fallen to Earth nearly hatched or exploded.</p>

<p>Fortunately, we averted the crisis by shutting down Cell-X, which halted the chain reaction.</p>

<p>However, it was highly likely that similar incidents would occur in the future.</p>

<p>Samra concluded that the meteorite chain reaction was related to the Outer Gods.</p>

<p>While there is certainly a difference between trials where a god exists and trials where one does not...</p>

<p>I couldn't figure out why it was affecting other meteorites as well.</p>

<p>Suddenly, a question I had in PTB came to mind.</p>

<p>A being that has escaped <em>the cage</em>.</p>

<p>Since he is no longer an NPC, I thought he might not know, but I asked him first.</p>

<p>“Samra. Do you happen to know anything about <em>the cage</em>?”</p>

<p>Samra answered immediately.</p>

<p>“I do not.”</p>

<p>I blinked for a moment.</p>

<p>Strangely enough, it seemed like Samra was lying.</p>

<p>He wasn’t the kind of being who would lie.</p>

<p>Just as I was gripped by a strange unease, Samra asked me a question.</p>

<p>“What was the reason you purchased the keychain when you visited Paseong?”</p>

<p>“...Ah.”</p>

<p>“I judged it to be an unnecessary item that deviated from your usual spending patterns, Han Goyo-ssi.”</p>

<p>“Well...”</p>

<p>I felt at a loss.</p>

<p>Samra was likely asking because he considered it a peculiar detail, but the reason for the purchase was simply too trivial.</p>

<p>In the end, I fumbled through my pocket and pulled out the keychain.</p>

<p>It was a keychain made of mother-of-pearl.</p>

<p>“I bought it because it reminded me of you, Samra. But it seems so useless...”</p>

<p>I bought it on impulse because it caught my eye while I was buying a get-well gift for CEO Cha. It was because the shimmering mother-of-pearl resembled Samra's holographic light.</p>

<p>However, after buying it, I realized I had no use for it.</p>

<p>It was completely useless to a high-tech android, and it was an awkward gift for an adult man to receive, even if he wasn't a machine.</p>

<p>I had packed it while getting ready for work today, but I had just decided to keep it sealed in my pocket.</p>

<p>Samra did not take the mother-of-pearl keychain I offered, but simply looked down at it.</p>

<p>“What is the reason you are giving me this gift?”</p>

<p>“Is a reason really necessary to give a gift?”</p>

<p>Feeling needlessly embarrassed, I ran my hand across the back of my neck.</p>

<p>“According to statistics, there is a purpose and a reason for every gift.”</p>

<p>“I just bought it because it reminded me of Samra.”</p>

<p>As I awkwardly tried to put the keychain back in the holder, he reached out and took it.</p>

<p>Then, he held it up to the light.</p>

<p>Samra, who had been watching the light reflection of the mother-of-pearl, soon looked back at me.</p>

<p>The sight of the android holding the keychain was so amusing that I couldn't help but laugh.</p>

<p>I smiled and joked with him.</p>

<p>&quot;It's a secret, since I'm only giving it to Samra.&quot;</p>

<p>For a while, Samra remained silent, collecting only my physical reactions and storing them as data.</p>

<p>Then, he spoke in a quiet voice.</p>

<p>&quot;So, it isn't for humanity.&quot;</p>

<p>I thought it was too grandiose to claim that a single mother-of-pearl keychain was for the sake of humanity.</p>

<p>I quickly promised him a better gift.</p>

<p>&quot;Next time, I will give you an item that Samra can actually use.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;This is enough for now.&quot;</p>

<p>And Samra slowly curved his lips into a smile.</p>

<p>&quot;Because Goyo-ssi gave it to <em>me</em>.&quot;</p>

<p>I paused for a moment.</p>

<p>&quot;Samra, just now...&quot;</p>

<p>I continued speaking slowly to the holographic eyes that were looking at me as if asking what was wrong.</p>

<p>&quot;...No. I was mistaken for a moment.&quot;</p>

<p>It seemed I had misheard.</p>

<p>Anyway, it was a relief that Samra seemed to like the mother-of-pearl keychain.</p>

<p>Next time, I wanted to give him a gift that was truly useful.</p>

<p>‘I need to earn some Star Fragments quickly.’</p>

<p>Just before the recruit training course began...</p>

<p>I received a shocking message.</p>

<p style="font-size:21.6px">—Hey kiddo!!</p>

<p>The administrator, who had suddenly called via video call, shoved a newspaper in my face while looking pale.</p>

<p>Pressing the newspaper close to the screen, they shouted.</p>

<p style="font-size:21.6px">—You-didn't-agree-to-this, did-you? Day-News-!?</p>

<p>I stared at the newspaper in a daze.</p>

<p>My photo was attached to the newspaper.</p>

<p>It was a picture of me, wearing the test subject outfit in PTB, glaring straight ahead with a flushed face.</p>

<p>It looked like the moment right after I had yelled at CEO Cha.</p>

<p>And below the photo was a huge article.</p>

<p>It was the very front page of the newspaper...</p>
