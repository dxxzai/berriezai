<p>Lord Octopus Tentacle is such a magnificent deity.</p>

<p><em>“■■■■■■■■■?”</em></p>

<p>It was like a funeral dirge sung from deep within the ocean.</p>

<p>The muffled sound pounded against my brain, but I could not understand a single word.</p>

<p>As I looked up at him with a bewildered expression, his laughter deepened.</p>

<p><em>“You…ng."</em></p>

<p>I could hear it this time, but I didn’t understand the meaning. It didn’t seem like he intended to call me a lamb.</p>

<p>Does he mean I am young?</p>

<p>After a moment of contemplation, I thought of Dominic.</p>

<p>Dominic always used to speak to me in a way that implied I was young, too.</p>

<p>‘Come to think of it, Lord Octopus Tentacle should be able to see Dominic.’</p>

<p>Even Infernum, who wasn't a god, saw Dominic.</p>

<p>Lord Octopus Tentacle would surely be aware of Dominic.</p>

<p>Since they are fellow gods, they should at least exchange greetings, but it was strangely quiet.</p>

<p>I glanced sideways at Pudding Slime.</p>

<p>Lord Octopus Tentacle followed my gaze, but showed no particular reaction.</p>

<p>As if he did not recognize Dominic.</p>

<p>Could it be that Dominic had hidden himself?</p>

<p>I couldn't understand why he had gone out of his way to disappear out of sight of Lord Octopus Tentacle.</p>

<p>As I pondered for a moment, Lord Octopus Tentacle called out to me again.</p>

<p><em>“Little… Lamb.”</em></p>

<p>His shadow cast at my feet squirmed, even though there wasn't a single breeze.</p>

<p>What had been human-shaped gradually split into strands of tentacles.</p>

<p>Then, it broke free from the shadow and took on a three-dimensional form.</p>

<p>The pungent smell of the sea pierced my sense of smell.</p>

<p>Tentacles oozing sticky mucus squirmed and wrapped around me.</p>

<p>I smiled pleasantly at the sensation climbing up from my feet.</p>

<p>It felt as if I was slowly drawing closer to the deep sea.</p>

<p>Ah… I wanted to cut off my useless limbs and attach Lord Octopus Tentacle’s…</p>

<p>It was just then, while I was immersed in a hazy state.</p>

<p>I felt a stinging sensation.</p>

<p>A small blue spark was flying from the wooden bracelet wrapped around my wrist.</p>

<p>Seeing the bracelet chain stretch long behind me, I turned my head to look.</p>

<p>Before I knew it, the surroundings were covered in tentacles.</p>

<p>The waves of the tentacles rippled like the crashing sea.</p>

<p>I spotted a red head in a gap between the tentacles.</p>

<p>The man, slowly being crushed to death, called out to someone.</p>

<p>“Goyo... Han Goyo…”</p>

<p>He was bleeding from his eyes.</p>

<p>Speaking with tears of blood, as if he had seen something he should never have seen, he said:</p>

<p>“That’s a god… f*ck… snap out of it…”</p>

<p>Crackle.</p>

<p>Sparks flew repeatedly from the wooden bracelet, and the red-haired man spat out a gush of blood.</p>

<p>For some reason, I couldn't take my eyes off the sight.</p>

<p>It was when I was staring intently at the man.</p>

<p><em>“■■■■■■….”</em></p>

<p>A faint hint of displeasure mingled in the voice of Lord Octopus Tentacle.</p>

<p>It seemed as though I had been staring at others too much. Even though I was standing right in front of God.</p>

<p>A sticky tentacle touched my cheek.</p>

<p>The Lord Octopus Tentacle made me look at him and smiled softly.</p>

<p>But...</p>

<p>I glanced at the Lord Octopus Tentacle for a moment, then turned my head back to the man.</p>

<p>He was dying, crushed by the power of God.</p>

<p>Just as the man was about to move his lips...</p>

<p>“…!”</p>

<p>He let out a pained groan.</p>

<p>The Lord Octopus Tentacle’s tentacles tightened as if to crush him to death, and the man vanished in an instant into the waves of tentacles.</p>

<p>Like being swept away by the deep sea.</p>

<p>Now, the only thing in my sight was the endlessly undulating tentacles.</p>

<p>The wooden bracelet wrapped around my wrist lost its grip and slid off.</p>

<p>Instantly, the wooden bracelet, too, was buried in the waves of tentacles.</p>

<p>I… <em>felt sick.</em></p>

<p>Along with that, I felt a cool breeze.</p>

<p>I looked up.</p>

<p>White and black light seemed to flash by briefly, and then the ceiling disappeared.</p>

<p>Not even a rockfall occurred. As if literally erased, the ceiling was opened, revealing the night sky.</p>

<p>In the dry sky, which had been nothing but hazy with sea fog...</p>

<p>Followed by ear-splitting thunder, the sound shook everything around.</p>

<p>The moment I heard the roar, the sensations that had been nothing but ecstasy vanished as if washed away.</p>

<p>I felt a burning sensation in my heart.</p>

<p>Thump, thump, my pulse quickened, and heat welled up in my eyes.</p>

<p>Amidst the rapidly rising heat, I <em>became angry</em>.</p>

<p><em>“Get out of here.” </em></p>

<p>Lying on the floor, he was covered in blood.</p>

<p>My vision narrowed, and my breathing quickened. With trembling hands, I checked his pulse first.</p>

<p>Fortunately, he had only lost consciousness; his heart was still beating.</p>

<p>He is alive.</p>

<p>At that moment, a part of the prayer beads, which had been hanging limply, returned to its owner.</p>

<p>I watched the beads slowly wrap around Kwak Hanmuk's wrist, then gritted my teeth.</p>

<p>With a violent crunch and crackle, steel and concrete crumpled wildly, and the vast prison building was instantly crushed.</p>

<p>It was enough to make Je Hyun-oh scramble just to spread his metal wings to block the falling rocks.</p>

<p>A sound like a scraping sensation was heard, and tentacles struck the island once again.</p>

<p>The ground crumbled, and everything began to fall.</p>
