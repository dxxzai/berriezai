<h1>Chapter 7</h1>

<br />
<p><em>"....."</em></p>
<br />

<p>Smiley was silent.</p>

<p>Its black plastic eyes simply held me still, as if examining something unfamiliar.</p>

<p>A moment later.</p>

<p>Smiley's pincer hand lowered me to the ground.</p>

<p>The moment my feet touched the floor, a pounding drum and bass BGM kicked in. The music that signaled the start of HapFactory's final boss fight.</p>

<p>A System window rose before me.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<div class="systembox_wrap_j">
<p>
<span class="glitch_t2">◆Mai?n Que?st??:</span> 
Grant Smiley Eternal Rest.<br/>
You have promised Smiley death.<br/>
If you fail to keep that promise, you and your <span class="glitch_t2">c?mpanions?will?di?die?di?die?</span><br/>
You believe you must win the battle against Smiley.
</p>
</div>
</div>

<p>The System window was corrupted too — probably because this wasn't normal progression.</p>

<p>I exhaled softly.</p>

<p>The truth was, I still had plenty of stages to clear and toy affinities to earn before the final boss fight.</p>

<p>I didn't even have a weapon item.</p>

<p>In short, I was charging in headfirst with nothing but bare hands.</p>

<p>But the situation was already bugged. Even if I wanted to follow the proper order, I couldn't.</p>

<p>And sure, it wasn't a HapFactory item, but...</p>

<p>I had managed to get one decent thing.</p>

<p>Good Smiley was waiting for me to strike first. I pulled Bolt Candy from my inventory and tore open the wrapper.</p>

<p>Bolt Candy's attack power and body enhancement buff lasted 180 seconds.</p>

<p>I had to clear all three of Smiley's phases in that time.</p>

<p>Thirty-five seconds each for Phase 1 and Phase 2, sixty seconds for Phase 3, and twenty-five seconds for the event scenes between phases.</p>

<p>A tight time budget.</p>

<p>As I put the Bolt Candy in my mouth, a jolt of electricity tingled across my tongue.</p>

<p>"Ngh..."</p>

<p>It stung like static electricity, my face scrunching up involuntarily.</p>

<p>But the sensation of tasting something I'd only ever seen as lines of text in a System window was strangely pleasant.</p>

<p>I sprang lightly into the air.</p>

<p>The moment I confirmed my body moving beyond what I'd expected, several workable strategies flooded my mind.</p>

<p>I picked one from among the thoughts that surfaced and looked at Smiley.</p>

<p>Smiley, still waiting for my first move, silently tracked my movements with its eyes.</p>

<p>Sorry, Smiley, but in Phase 1, you don't get a turn.</p>

<p>"Captain Mo Haein!"</p>

<p>I wrenched my cuffed hands back as hard as I could.</p>

<p>"Eat the Bolt Candy!!"</p>

<p>I brought the Hinge Handcuffs slamming down on Smiley's left eye.</p>

<p>CRACK!</p>

<p>The plastic eye split open.</p>

<p>'That actually worked.'</p>

<p>A TRA special-issue item made to restrain Adaptees.</p>

<p>I'd figured it could be used as a weapon since its durability was far higher than most items, but it was still a gamble.</p>

<p>The thrill of the bet paying off heated my brain.</p>

<p>Counting seconds in my head, I grabbed the cracked plastic fragments and ripped them off.</p>

<p>Rainbow-colored blood burst out as Smiley thrashed its body.</p>

<p>The recoil sent me flying, and I landed far away.</p>

<p>I noticed my movements felt freer and looked down — the hinge connecting the handcuffs had broken.</p>

<p>Having unintentionally gained freedom for my hands, I checked the music.</p>

<p>A crackling static was bleeding into the background track.</p>

<p>The sound of the fight transitioning to Phase 2.</p>

<p>'Saved four seconds.'</p>

<p>Smiley, rainbow liquid streaming from one eye, slammed its pincer hand into the ground.</p>

<p>An earthquake rippled through the floor as it split open.</p>

<p>Small toys crawled up through the cracks.</p>

<p>Unfinished toys the employees must have been making also broke free from the assembly line and came running.</p>

<p>They looked like they wanted to play together, but getting hit just twice by those toys meant instant death.</p>

<p>Block bullets flew from between the toys pouring in from all sides. Shots fired by soldier toys.</p>

<p>A bullet I couldn't dodge in time seared across my thigh.</p>

<p>As I staggered from the pain, a fish toy lunged at my face, mouth wide open.</p>

<p>But its sharp teeth never reached me.</p>

<p>"You crazy bastard!"</p>

<p>The Black Moon Blade chopped the fish to pieces. Fish toy sashimi scattered everywhere.</p>

<p>Mo Haein's neck veins bulging as she shouted.</p>

<p>"This guy out of his damn mind!"</p>

<p>"Sorry, Captain."</p>

<p>But I'm alive, right?</p>

<p>I kept the second part to myself.</p>

<p>Fortunately, Mo Haein didn't have the energy to stay mad at me — she immediately turned her head.</p>

<p>Using the Smiley eyeball fragment I'd ripped off earlier as a shield, I spoke quickly.</p>

<p>"To finish Phase 2, we need to attack the left eye."</p>

<p>Mo Haein and I sprinted forward. The impact of toys and bullets slamming into the shield rattled my body.</p>

<p>Then Smiley's pincer hand came down where I stood.</p>

<p>The ground surged upward, launching Mo Haein and me into the air.</p>

<p>In a game, this would've been a simple obstacle mechanic, but here, we were stranded on top of a suddenly risen pillar of earth.</p>

<p>Mo Haein snatched the eyeball fragment I'd been using as a shield and planted it as a foothold.</p>

<p>"Hold on!"</p>

<p>I grabbed Mo Haein's waist.</p>

<p>The moment we touched the risen ground, we went sliding down on the fragment like a sled.</p>

<p>The toys that had been charging at us couldn't keep up with the sled's speed, crashing into each other and tumbling down in a chaotic mess.</p>

<p>The moment we hit the bottom, I hurled the shattered fragment aside and ran for Smiley again.</p>

<p>Its pincer hand kept slamming the ground, trying to catch us, but it wasn't hard to dodge.</p>

<p>The moment we were in range, Mo Haein leaped high.</p>

<p>The Black Moon Blade sank into Smiley's left eye.</p>

<p>Its outer shell was gone, leaving only a pool of rainbow liquid — the blade's tip dug deep into it.</p>

<p>Even though it was a plastic toy, it felt like piercing soft flesh.</p>

<p>A mechanical sound of a child giggling and shouting repeated from Smiley.</p>
<br/>
<em><p>"Die!-Kill-me!-Die!-Kill-me!-Die!"</p></em>
<br/>
<p>Mo Haein pulled the spear free and landed on the ground. The fifteen-meter Smiley began to shrink, creaking and cracking.</p>

<p>Back down to one meter, Smiley stood there with rainbow liquid dripping from its eye, frozen like a broken machine.</p>

<p>The air filled with hazy rainbow-colored mist. A tearing noise, almost painfully loud, bled into the BGM.</p>

<p>Phase 3 had begun.</p>

<p>'Lost about five seconds.'</p>

<p>I'd lost time when I couldn't dodge the rising ground at the start.</p>

<p>But there was still room.</p>

<p>I sharpened the senses heightened by Bolt Candy. With visibility limited by the mist, reaction speed was the key mechanic.</p>

<p>BOOM — the ground trembled as everything shook. The sound of the toy factory collapsing.</p>

<p>Debris began to fall.</p>

<p>Heavy objects emerging from the mist meant instant death on impact, and even if dodged, they became terrain obstacles.</p>

<p>The floor was quickly buried under huge stone fragments, machine parts, and broken toys.</p>

<p>Once the terrain change finished, Smiley began moving in earnest.</p>

<p>A cheerful laugh echoed behind me, accompanied by the clanking of a plastic chain drawing closer.</p>

<p>Along with joyful laughter, the sound of a plastic chains clattering approaching from behind.</p>

<p>Thump, thump, thump, thump—heavy footsteps pounding down.</p>
<p>But I still couldn't move.</p>

<p>Mo Haein, pressed close beside me, asked in a low voice.</p>

<p>"Anything for this part?"</p>

<p>"We just have to run, straight up. But at the very end..."</p>

<p>I told Mo Haein what I planned to do at the finish. She frowned but didn't argue further.</p>

<p>The factory speakers that had been playing the BGM crackled, then a signal tone dropped.</p>
<br/>
<em><p>"One, two, Happy Smile!"</p></em>
<br/>
<p>The bindings on our feet released. Mo Haein and I sprinted forward like mad.</p>

<p>Smiley's final Phase 3 was a chase.</p>

<p>Unlike Smiley, which smashed through everything in its path to pursue us, the Player had to run while dodging obstacles on the ground.</p>

<p>Dodging the pincer hands that lunged out from behind large obstacles.</p>

<p>Even with my physical abilities enhanced by Bolt Candy, my breath was climbing up to my chin.</p>

<p>An announcement played through the tearing BGM.</p>
<br/>
<em><p>"Announcement. For the. Attention. Of. Happy Smile Factory's Roa-sted Chestnut. Roa-sted Chestnut employee. Roa-sted Chestnut employee, please stop moving immediately. Immediately. Stooooop."</p></em>
<br/>
<p>They were drawing out the name "Roasted Chestnut" so long it almost sounded like they were actually roasting a chestnut.</p>

<p>They kept calling out to me, but I never looked back.</p>

<p>The rainbow mist began to thin. It meant the virtual space Smiley had created was ending.</p>

<p>But the remaining seconds were razor-thin. The chase had gone on longer than expected.</p>

<p>'Eight seconds.'</p>

<p>The tension that it might stretch even longer made my skin prickle.</p>

<p>'Five seconds.'</p>

<p>Finally, I could see the edge of the mist.</p>

<p>I squeezed out the last of my strength.</p>

<p>'Three seconds.'</p>

<p>Right before I broke free of the mist.</p>

<p>A sharp, stabbing pain shot through my thigh.</p>

<p>I hadn't seen the rebar jutting out from between the stone debris. And of all places, it hit the same spot I'd already injured.</p>

<p>The buff time ran out. The enhanced physical abilities faded.</p>

<p>My body suddenly felt impossibly heavy, and I stumbled, tumbling to the ground.</p>

<p>My hand parted the mist uselessly.</p>

<p>'Just one more step and I'd be outside.'</p>

<p>Then a pincer hand grabbed me by the scruff of the neck.</p>

<p>It wasn't Smiley. Mo Haein had extended her toy arm and hooked me.</p>

<p>The moment I was dragged out, the mist and obstacles covering everything vanished.</p>

<p>I was outside the factory.</p>

<p>The collapsing toy factory came into view. And Smiley, still chasing after me with its body mangled and broken.</p>

<p>Mo Haein hurled me to the ground, gripped the Black Moon Blade, and pulled her arm all the way back.</p>

<p>The spear shot forward like a javelin, piercing straight through Smiley.</p>

<p>Smiley crumpled to the dirt floor, letting out a child's cry.</p>
<br/>
<em><p>"No---!"</p></em>
<br/>
<p>The ear-splitting background music cut out. HapFactory's final boss fight was over.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>You have won the battle against Smiley!</p></div>

<p>"Hah... hah..."</p>

<p>Mo Haein sheathed the Black Moon Blade and collapsed to the floor, gasping for breath.</p>

<p>I steadied my own breathing, then staggered to my feet.</p>

<p>Mo Haein's gaze flickered briefly. Her eyes showed conflict — whether she should let me do what I was about to do.</p>

<p>"Captain..."</p>

<p>At my call, laced with a pained groan, she muttered resentfully.</p>

<p>"What the hell are you going to do by healing the boss."</p>

<p>But she didn't stop me.</p>

<p>I limped toward Smiley alone.</p>

<p>I tore open a Nut Candy wrapper and placed it where its mouth should have been. The candy dissolved like light, seeping into Smiley.</p>

<p>Smiley's broken body repaired itself as it slowly rose.</p>

<p>It stood there, looking up at me.</p>

<p>"I came to keep my promise."</p>

<p>The only thing that could kill Smiley was something that shared its blood and flesh.</p>

<p>A twin, separated in childhood after losing their memories.</p>

<p>That was the Player's true identity.</p>

<p>Smiley had gained power along with eternal life, remembered the Player, and called the only being who could kill it to the factory.</p>

<p>I genuinely wanted to give Smiley peace.</p>

<p>Death itself was the happy ending for Smiley.</p>

<p>Its black plastic eyes held me for a long moment. Then a quiet voice came out.</p>

<p>"Why-did-you-come-only-now?"</p>

<p>The drawn-out, childish voice wasn't mechanical.</p>

<p>'But that's not the original line...'</p>

<p>Something felt deeply twisted, but it seemed like we were still heading toward the true ending, so I apologized without arguing.</p>

<p>"Sorry."</p>

<p>I hesitated, then placed my hand on top of Smiley's head.</p>

<p>As I gently stroked the smooth plastic, Smiley said another strange line.</p>

<p>"Don't-wanna-die."</p>

<p>Then it slowly extended its pincer hand and grabbed the edge of my workwear sleeve.</p>

<p>"Wanna-go-with-Roasted-Chestnut-together-forever."</p>

<p>It sounded like a child wheedling. My heart wanted to take it right away, but it wasn't an item — there was no way.</p>

<p>Then a System window appeared.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<p>Smiley and the toys wish to become ■■ ■■ ■■.<br/>
You willingly wish to accept their request to become ■■ ■'s ■ and ■.</p>
</div>

<p>The text was covered in strange ■ characters, making it impossible to read properly.</p>

<p>And the options that followed were ridiculous.</p>
<div class="systembox">
<div class="systembox_wrap_j"><p>
&#45; Yes!<br/>

&#45; Absolutely. <br/>

&#45; YEEES!
</p></div>
</div>

<p>Seriously... hah.</p>

<p>If I didn't accept, I wouldn't see the ending, so I had no choice.</p>

<p>I picked 'YEEES!' A cheerful notification rang out as the System window appeared.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<p>
&lt;Happy Smile Factory&gt; Cleared! <br/>
&#45; ■■ ■■ ■■ Banquet Ending.
</p>
</div>

<p>Announcing an ending I'd never seen before.</p>
