<h1>Chapter 19</h1>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk, Mo Haein, Samra, and the others all matched the feeling I'd imagined while watching dot characters.</p>

<p>Their features had been rendered well in pixels, and I found myself quietly impressed.</p>

<p>But Je Hyun-oh had never once shown his face in the game.</p>

<p>He'd never even taken off his goggles.</p>

<p>Now, across both game and reality, I was seeing part of Je Hyun-oh's face for the very first time.</p>

<p>And with his goggles off, Je Hyun-oh looked nothing like I'd imagined.</p>

<p>I pictured a tall, well-built handsome guy, but he's more...</p>

<p>Beneath thick lashes, dark violet eyes set in horizontally elongated sockets stared back at me.</p>

<p>His irises and pupils were different colors — the pupils were metallic gold.</p>

<p>The discoloration from the infection made it unmistakably clear that he was no longer fully human.</p>

<p>Unlike me, who stood frozen with wide eyes, Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein looked slightly surprised but otherwise unfazed.</p>

<p>Their reaction was more along the lines of "So he finally took off his goggles."</p>

<p>They'd seen his face plenty of times, so there was nothing new to be shocked about.</p>

<p>I tried not to stare too obviously, but Je Hyun-oh's eyes crinkled.</p>

<p>Only then did I realize I'd stepped several paces closer.</p>

<p>I must have moved toward him unconsciously, trying to observe him.</p>

<p>Since we're here, I wish he'd take off the gas mask too.</p>

<p>Maybe once we got closer, I'd ask if he could.</p>

<p>"Trainee."</p>

<p>Lost in my face-gazing, I snapped back to reality at Je Hyun-oh's voice.</p>

<p>"What's your name?"</p>

<p>"Han Goyo."</p>

<p>"Goyo."</p>

<p>His impossibly low voice didn't match his smiling eyes at all.</p>

<p>Like two completely different things had been glued together.</p>

<p>"If you do that one more time, you'll really die."</p>

<p>...A chill ran down my spine.</p>

<p>"I'm sorry."</p>

<p>I apologized immediately.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein probably thought he was warning me about the dangerous stunt I'd pulled — using him to kill zombies.</p>

<p>But I knew.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh was talking about &lt;Zero&gt;.</p>

<p>He's telling me not to act like I know anything.</p>

<p>Despite holding the rank of Lieutenant Colonel, Je Hyun-oh was the youngest team leader at the TRA.</p>

<p>Unlike Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein, who were in their thirties, he was only twenty-eight.</p>

<p>The turning point that led to his absurd string of special promotions was &lt;Zero&gt;.</p>

<p>Originally a promising talent, everything changed for Je Hyun-oh after he entered &lt;Zero&gt;.</p>

<p>Becoming a sentient infected, he became the TRA's most powerful weapon.</p>

<p>It wasn't an exaggeration to say that South Korea — pushed to the brink of national collapse by the Trials — had been able to recover thanks to Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>There were even Adaptees who entered &lt;Zero&gt; willingly, hoping to gain power as strong as his.</p>

<p>But none of them came out alive.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh was &lt;Zero&gt;'s sole survivor.</p>

<p>The TRA wanted to know how he'd cleared &lt;Zero&gt;, but Je Hyun-oh never filed a report.</p>

<p>Director Jang Seokyun himself had pressed him on it, and he couldn't even get a single word out of Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>After that, countless attempts were made to extract information about &lt;Zero&gt; from him.</p>

<p>In response, Je Hyun-oh started attacking anyone who brought up &lt;Zero&gt; in his presence.</p>

<p>After one researcher from the Research and Analysis Division died as a result, it became an unspoken taboo to mention &lt;Zero&gt; around Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>To this day, the TRA had no idea what kind of Trial &lt;Zero&gt; actually was.</p>

<p>And I'd poked that bear.</p>

<p>But I had my own argument to make.</p>

<p>"So does this mean you'll accept me as a team member now?"</p>

<p>Frankly, Je Hyun-oh was the one who'd tried to kill me the moment we met.</p>

<p>As violent as he was toward Trials and Adaptees, he'd never been this aggressive toward a TRA soldier without reason.</p>

<p>Instead of answering my question about whether he'd kill me, Je Hyun-oh turned his gaze to Mo Haein.</p>

<p>"Captain Mo. What's this?"</p>

<p>"A newly enlisted trainee. Short-term, one month. A Special Mission Team has been newly formed under the Director's direct command, centered around Trainee Han Goyo, who has specialized knowledge of the Trials."</p>

<p>"Mm. And I'm assigned to it."</p>

<p>"Yes."</p>

<p>Mo Haein paused briefly, then looked Je Hyun-oh straight in the eye and added.</p>

<p>"...He's a teammate, Lieutenant Colonel-nim."</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh slowly closed his eyes, then opened them.</p>

<p>"Trainee Crazy Chestnut shut down HapFactory. He has considerable knowledge about the Trials. He'll be useful as a teammate."</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk chimed in diligently, advertising Trainee Han Goyo's usefulness.</p>

<p>And while the two Captains were working hard, I sent out a Message.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Please trust me.</p></div>

<p>Je Hyun-oh's eyes flicked to the System window floating in front of him, then back to me.</p>

<p>An instinctive chill ran through me.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh still wanted to kill me. I had no idea why — the murderous intent made no sense.</p>

<p>But even as I felt that killing intent, I was certain he would never actually kill me.</p>

<p>At least not while we were here in DeZonDeal.</p>

<p>If he was going to kill me, he would have done it already.</p>

<p>There was no way Je Hyun-oh couldn't move just because I'd rolled him across some asphalt.</p>

<p>Even after his final attack was blocked by the Prohibition, he'd had countless opportunities to take my head off.</p>

<p>Yet he'd let me live.</p>

<p>Probably... because he doesn't know exactly what I can do.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh didn't know that my abilities were still pathetic.</p>

<p>All he knew was that I could send System windows and place brief restrictions on him.</p>

<p>It was only natural for him to assume I was hiding something bigger.</p>

<p>And I did have Execution, technically. With him registered, his life was in my hands.</p>

<p>As the silent stare-down dragged on, Mo Haein grew visibly uneasy.</p>

<p>Only after a fairly long pause did Je Hyun-oh finally speak.</p>

<p>"Can you get a True Ending here too?"</p>

<p>The meaning was clear.</p>

<p>If I couldn't prove my worth in DeZonDeal, he'd dispose of me regardless of my weird ability to summon System windows.</p>

<p>The truth was, I hadn't even confirmed whether a True Ending was possible yet.</p>

<p>But that's my problem.</p>

<p>Either way, if I didn't want to lose my limbs, I had to make it happen whether it was possible or not.</p>

<p>I answered him with full conviction.</p>

<p>"I will. No matter what."</p>

<p>Something amused him. Laughter spread through Je Hyun-oh's eyes.</p>

<p>His eyes curved into crescents as he abruptly called my name.</p>

<p>"Goyo."</p>

<p>"Yes!"</p>

<p>"You're going to die young."</p>

<p>"...Excuse me?"</p>

<p>After dropping that sudden curse, he put his goggles back on.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein stood there dumbfounded before speaking up for me.</p>

<p>"Come on, Lieutenant Colonel-nim. What kind of talk is that to the team's youngest? Goyo's twenty-four, you know. Twenty-four. Two. Four."</p>

<p>"Lieutenant Colonel-nim. That was a bit much."</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh didn't answer, of course. Instead, he just displayed a smug emoticon on his goggles.</p>

<div class="hyunoh"><p>&gt; &lt;</p></div>

<p>What the hell was that supposed to mean...</p>

<p>I gave up trying to interpret the emoticon. That wasn't what mattered right now anyway.</p>

<p>Finally — Je Hyun-oh had joined the Special Mission Team!</p>

<p>My heart swelled as I looked at Je Hyun-oh, Mo Haein, and Kwak Hanmuk.</p>

<p>To think I'd be on a team with the people I'd considered the TRA's best members while playing the Archive's games.</p>

<p>This was the best moment I'd had since becoming the System and ending up on the verge of losing all four limbs.</p>

<p>"I look forward to working with you, Lieutenant Colonel-nim."</p>

<p>I bowed to Je Hyun-oh while secretly entertaining a small hope.</p>

<p>Maybe I'll get to see my favorite character too?</p>

<p>I liked the TRA people, but my favorite character from the Archive wasn't part of the organization.</p>

<p>Still, since they were an Adaptee, I figured I'd meet them eventually.</p>

<p>Of course, surviving the month and keeping my limbs intact came first.</p>

<p>Just then, zombie sounds drifted through the quiet night air. The remaining Dead Zombies seemed to have tracked our presence.</p>

<p>"The jeep!"</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk went for the jeep first.</p>

<p>"I'll go hide it."</p>

<p>There was no mode of transport more useful than the jeep, so it'd be a waste to use it once and abandon it here.</p>

<p>But if we hid it poorly, the Dead Zombies would tear it apart.</p>

<p>Dead Zombies were more destructive than regular zombies.</p>

<p>That was why the Dead Zone was nothing but ruins — not a single intact building compared to other areas.</p>

<p>We'd need to get at least to the Red Zone to find a safe place to hide the jeep, but by then it'd defeat the purpose of having transport.</p>

<p>"Captain Kwak. Moving the jeep somewhere safe would take too long."</p>

<p>I pointed to the laboratory rooftop.</p>

<p>"What if we put it on the roof?"</p>

<p>Mo Haein looked at me like this crazy trainee is at it again.</p>

<p>She asked, exasperated but curious.</p>

<p>"And how exactly do you plan to get it up there?"</p>

<p>"Fly it."</p>

<p>A moment later.</p>

<p>We were successfully flying through the night sky.</p>

<p>Or rather, Je Hyun-oh was carrying the jeep — with me, Kwak Hanmuk, and Mo Haein inside.</p>

<p>The cold metallic sound of his wings fluttered softly like background music.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk, sitting in the driver's seat, rested his arm on the wide-open window, enjoying the flight.</p>

<p>"Lieutenant Colonel-nim is really convenient."</p>

<p>He was thoroughly satisfied with the performance, letting the cool night breeze hit his face.</p>

<p>"Captain Mo. What if we made Goyo hyung-nim the team leader? Then we could ride this plane every time, right?"</p>

<p>Unlike Kwak Hanmuk, who was joking and laughing cheerfully, Mo Haein in the passenger seat still looked like she couldn't believe we were flying in a jeep.</p>

<p>"Shut up with the nonsense, Kwak. I'll bite you."</p>

<p>She had Hinge Handcuffs on her wrist.</p>

<p>A precaution in case her aggression suddenly spiked.</p>

<p>She was in the passenger seat for the same reason — if she attacked from behind, it'd be dangerous, so Kwak needed her in his line of sight.</p>

<p>Which meant I, the youngest, got to sit in the back.</p>

<p>Listening to Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein bicker, I glanced down through the window.</p>

<p>Dead Zombies were already swarming around the laboratory. But none of them could climb up to the rooftop.</p>

<p>If we can just keep going like this.</p>

<p>If we could clear the True Ending quickly too, that would be perfect.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh flew up to the rooftop, circled the lab once to gauge the spot, then set the jeep down in a suitable place.</p>

<p>Thump. The jeep landed on the rooftop with a slight jolt.</p>

<p>A few Dead Zombies that had been hiding in the rooftop corners attacked immediately, but there was no need for us to step in.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh dealt with them in an instant.</p>

<p>While Kwak Hanmuk unlocked Mo Haein's cuffs, I read the System window floating before me.</p>

<p>Finding the researcher to develop the vaccine was a quest I'd received in the game too.</p>

<p>The wording was a bit strange, but the context wasn't far off.</p>

<p>I started a brief briefing.</p>

<p>"As soon as we enter the laboratory, the first thing we—"</p>

<p>"Trainee. Hold on."</p>

<p>But Mo Haein cut me off before I could even start.</p>

<p>"Your hunger and thirst are fully recovered, right?"</p>

<p>"Yes! All filled up now. I'll be careful not to let that happen again."</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>Mo Haein frowned. Then Kwak Hanmuk suddenly threw an arm around my shoulder.</p>

<p>I looked up at him, pinned under his weight.</p>

<p>"We, you know, take Trial closures reeeeally seriously. Not those bastards, is what I'm saying."</p>

<p>"Right, Trainee. You're only a trainee by rank, and you haven't even had formal training."</p>

<p>Mo Haein looked at me steadily.</p>

<p>"To be precise, right now you're a civilian, not a soldier."</p>

<p>"Ah... I'm sorry."</p>

<p>I was about to take a step back, thinking I'd been too forward.</p>

<p>Then Mo Haein pulled out a piece of cloth from who knows where, wet it, and handed it to me.</p>

<p>When I just stood there holding it, she gestured to her face.</p>

<p>Only then did I understand. I wiped my face — the cloth came away bright red.</p>

<p>Mo Haein sighed and murmured to me.</p>

<p>"...I meant pace yourself."</p>
