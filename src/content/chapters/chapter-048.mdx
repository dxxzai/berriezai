<h1>Chapter 48</h1>

<p>The towering man looked utterly out of place against the cozy surroundings.</p>

<p>He was like something that had been jarringly dropped into this world, the only one of his kind.</p>

<p>There was no doubt about who he was.</p>

<p>The Master.</p>

<p>"…"</p>

<p>I swallowed without meaning to.</p>

<p>Where had things gone wrong?</p>

<p>Why was this being — the Master — pretending to be Dr. Dominic?</p>

<p>My tongue felt frozen with tension.</p>

<p>No, not felt. It was literally frozen.</p>

<p>A system window appeared before me.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<div class="systembox_wrap">
<p>
You have encountered <span class="glitch_t2">Dr. Do?mi?nic???</span> <br/>

&#45; Greet him warmly.
&#45; Scream and run away, only to get hit by a car and die!<br/>
&#45; Fall to your knees and beg for mercy, then hang yourself from a willow tree!<br/>
&#45; Bash your head against the floor pleading for your life until you bleed out!<br/>
&#45; Swear to become His Excellency's loyal servant!
</p>
</div>

<p>The options were completely busted…</p>

<p>At this rate, there was really only one viable choice.</p>

<p>While I was staring at the options, Dominic saw off a patient named Mary.</p>

<p>As her car drove away, Dominic turned back to me.</p>

<p>I picked the first one.</p>

<p>"Nice to meet you. I'm Little Lamb. I work as a journalist in London, and I came to Morneveil today."</p>

<p>My voice came out bright and cheerful, betraying none of the tension coiled inside me.</p>

<p>I was tearfully grateful for CallOVo's gameplay system — outputting pre-written dialogue through the choices.</p>

<p>"Little Lamb?"</p>

<p>Dominic repeated my name, studying me closely.</p>

<p>He bent down, casting a shadow over me.</p>

<p>With the tension easing just slightly, I could finally get a proper look at his face.</p>

<p>He… isn't human.</p>

<p>He was clearly wearing human skin, but calling him the same species felt wrong.</p>

<p>It wasn't just because he was three meters tall.</p>

<p>It was a beauty too grotesque for any human to bear.</p>

<p>Perfect platinum hair, and blue eyes that seemed to hold the entire cosmos.</p>

<p>Those eyes were like a star cluster in the night sky.</p>

<p>Layers of rich blue rippled within them, shifting into strange violet hues depending on the angle, sometimes brushed with a cold, icy white.</p>

<p>Across his irises, tiny golden fragments like shattered stars were scattered irregularly, glittering in an unreal way.</p>

<p>But despite such brilliant radiance, the longer I met those eyes…</p>

<p>The more I realized they had no end.</p>

<p>The deepest abyss.</p>

<p>Like a void.</p>

<p>Boundless.</p>
<br/>
<em><p>AH!HOWCOULDITBEDEGRADINGTOKNEELBEFOREHISEXCELLENCYANDBOWMYHEAD!ITISTHEONLYCOURTESYAPATIFULWRETCHLIKEMECANOFFER!THEGLORYOFALIFETIME!</p></em>
<br/>
<p>"…!"</p>

<p>I gasped sharply.</p>

<p>Before I could even process the horrible thought that had flashed through my mind, Dominic spoke.</p>

<p>"If you'll pardon me, but that name doesn't suit you."</p>

<p>He hummed lightly, a soft sound, then asked.</p>

<p>"How about the name Goyo?"</p>

<p>And smiled as he added.</p>

<p>"Han Goyo. A name like that."</p>

<p>…At this point, I genuinely wanted to run away.</p>

<p>The problem was, there was nowhere to go.</p>

<p>To leave CallOVo, I needed Dr. Dominic's role.</p>

<p>Besides, even if I took the Cult Leader route, I'd end up crossing paths with the doctor.</p>

<p>Either way, I was stuck dealing with this 'Master' pretending to be Dominic.</p>

<p>"How about… Roasted Chestnut? My friends call me Roasted Chestnut."</p>

<p>I desperately offered another name.</p>

<p>Dominic's brow furrowed slightly, clearly not pleased, but he accepted my suggestion with composure.</p>

<p>"If that's what you wish to be called, by all means."</p>

<p>He adjusted the sleeves of his suit neatly, offering a gentlemanly smile.</p>

<p>"So, Roasted Chestnut. What brings you to the clinic?"</p>

<p>"Well… you see…"</p>

<p>"First, let's go inside. We can talk slowly."</p>

<p>He seemed to have recognized me as a patient.</p>

<p>Either way, the conversation would be long, so I had to go inside the clinic.</p>

<p>I steeled myself and stepped through the door.</p>

<p>Dominic's clinic had an open ceiling on the first floor, making it unusually tall.</p>

<p>Thanks to that, the fake Dominic could move around freely without hitting his head.</p>

<p>The office was finished in mahogany wood, paired with a corner fireplace that gave it a warm atmosphere.</p>

<p>Dominic sat me down in a leather wingback chair.</p>

<p>"Please, have a seat. Would you like some warm tea?"</p>

<p>"No, thank you. I already had some."</p>

<p>Your butlers served me black tea, and it didn't end well.</p>

<p>…I wanted to say that, but I held back.</p>

<p>Dominic sat across from me.</p>

<p>Only then did I realize the clinic had a bizarre structure.</p>

<p>Only the furniture Dominic used was oversized to fit his frame — everything else was standard human size.</p>

<p>The long reclining chair for patients looked almost comically small next to him.</p>

<p>As Dominic prepared to listen to his patient, I struggled with how to begin.</p>

<p>Why aren't you going home? Go back already.</p>

<p>I know you're an alien.</p>

<p>Your butlers are waiting for you.</p>

<p>Several lines came to mind, but none of them were usable.</p>

<p>I didn't know why he was staying in CallOVo, so if I acted like I knew too much, I might upset him.</p>

<p>After much deliberation, I chose a safe opening.</p>

<p>"I came from outside."</p>

<p>"Another city?"</p>

<p>"No. Outside this world."</p>

<p>"I see."</p>

<p>Dominic looked at me with deeply sympathetic eyes.</p>

<p>Now that I'd said it out loud, I just sounded like a crazy person.</p>

<p>And since his specialty happened to be psychiatry, my statement carried even less credibility.</p>

<p>I tried a different angle.</p>

<p>"Have you always been here, Dr. Dominic?"</p>

<p>"Yes. I was born in Morneveil, and aside from my university years, I've stayed here ever since."</p>

<p>He seemed deeply committed to his role as Dominic.</p>

<p>Realizing this wasn't working, I finally just said it outright.</p>

<p>"You're not Dominic, are you."</p>

<p>A flicker of agitation crossed Dominic's eyes.</p>

<p>I looked away briefly — I couldn't bear to watch his emotional shift.</p>

<p>If I stared into those eyes too long, I'd get sucked in again.</p>

<p>I fixed my gaze on his chest, and a low voice flowed out.</p>

<p>"You're the first person to say that to me."</p>

<p>I lifted my gaze slightly — Dominic had suddenly stood up.</p>

<p>He walked over to me.</p>

<p>I was frozen to the sofa.</p>

<p>Dominic bent his knees to bring his eyes roughly level with mine.</p>

<p>Those star-studded blue eyes looked down at me.</p>

<p>His smooth voice dropped, heavy.</p>

<p>"The truth is, I have no memories."</p>

<p>It took me a moment to process what he'd said.</p>

<p>"…No memories?"</p>

<p>"Yes. One day I opened my eyes and I was here. Everyone called me Dominic, treated me like Dominic. So I just… went along with it."</p>

<p>This is insane.</p>

<p>I barely stopped myself from cursing out loud.</p>

<p>The situation was literally insane.</p>

<p>He'd lost his memory…</p>

<p>"Roasted Chestnut. Am I human?"</p>

<p>Dominic asked a question that could drive a person even more insane.</p>

<p>I scrambled desperately for a way to handle this.</p>

<p>"Why… would you think that…"</p>

<p>"Look at this."</p>

<p>Dominic held out his gloved hand in front of me.</p>

<p>He placed one finger on his palm.</p>

<p>Then slowly dragged it down.</p>

<p>The leather glove split along his fingertip, and the skin beneath parted with it.</p>

<p>But there was no blood — as there should have been — and no pained groan.</p>

<p>Dominic's expression didn't change as he showed me his split palm.</p>

<p>Inside it…</p>
<br/>
<em><p><span class="redtextitty">DON'T LOOK! NO! CLOSE YOUR EYES! NO! DON'T LOOK! CLOSE YOUR EYES! HELP ME! PLEASE! NO! AAAAAAAARGH!</span></p></em>
<br/>
<p>"Ghh—"</p>

<p>I clamped my hand over my mouth.</p>

<p>A searing pain twisted through my gut, and my eyes burned.</p>

<p>Blood dripped from my nose.</p>

<p>Dominic quickly pulled out a handkerchief and pressed it under my nose.</p>

<p>The clean linen cloth stained red.</p>

<p>"I'm sorry, Roasted Chestnut. That was my mistake."</p>

<p>Dominic looked at me, flustered, checking on me.</p>

<p>It took a while for the nosebleed to stop.</p>

<p>Pale-faced, I looked at Dominic.</p>

<p>The palm he'd split had already healed cleanly.</p>

<p>The skin, the leather glove — all pristine, as if they'd never been cut.</p>

<p>His expression somewhat subdued, he spoke.</p>

<p>"What am I… I don't think I'm human."</p>

<p>"No!"</p>

<p>I pressed the handkerchief to my nose and shouted, my voice muffled.</p>

<p>"You ARE human!"</p>

<p>There was only one thought in my head right now.</p>

<p>'I have to lie through my teeth.'</p>

<p>This being must never leave the Trial.</p>

<p>I had to keep him locked inside CallOVo, or at the very least — the absolute minimum — keep him in this amnesiac state so he wouldn't realize what he truly was.</p>

<p>This 'Master' was weak now because he didn't know himself.</p>

<p>But what if he did?</p>

<p>If, like the butlers who hadn't disappeared even when Clean3 was shut down, he wouldn't disappear even if CallOVo closed?</p>

<p>What if he crossed over into other Trials?</p>

<p>I was certain.</p>

<p>Any Trial he entered would become hell.</p>

<p>TRA called the Trials that fell to Earth 'calamities' and fought against them, but compared to the being in front of me, those were nothing.</p>

<p>This was pure calamity.</p>

<p>"Really. Dr. Dominic is definitely human. A little special, maybe, but human."</p>

<p>I spewed lies with all my might.</p>

<p>The Master stared at me as I desperately lied.</p>

<p>He asked.</p>

<p>"Are you human too, Roasted Chestnut?"</p>

<p>"I am. But I'm different from the people of Morneveil."</p>

<p>"Is that so…"</p>

<p>I wasn't an NPC, so that wasn't a lie.</p>

<p>Dominic seemed to think for a moment, then concluded.</p>

<p>"If Roasted Chestnut is human, then I suppose I am too."</p>

<p>"…"</p>

<p>I was alive.</p>

<p>Having somehow succeeded in my con, I forced a smile at him.</p>

<p>Dominic crinkled his nose playfully and smiled back.</p>

<p>"But I suppose I'm not really Dominic, as you said. I don't particularly like the name."</p>

<p>His words slowed.</p>

<p>"If I were to choose another name, something like Domnius would…"</p>

<p>The moment the name Domnius left his lips, those blue eyes began to glow with brilliant radiance.</p>

<p>I shouted frantically.</p>

<p>"No! Dominic! I think the name Dominic suits you perfectly. It sounds good, and it fits you well."</p>

<p>"Hmm. If you insist, Roasted Chestnut. I'll stay Dominic."</p>

<p>Dominic still seemed reluctant, but he accepted his name.</p>

<p>Having settled that, his face relaxed as he spoke.</p>

<p>"Thanks to you, some of the questions I've been carrying have been answered. I'm grateful."</p>

<p>Having narrowly escaped death in a matter of seconds, I answered with a much heavier expression.</p>

<p>"Not at all…"</p>

<p>No sooner had I gotten past one crisis than the next obstacle greeted me.</p>

<p>"I'd like to help you too, if there's anything I can do."</p>

<p>Along with Dominic's kind gratitude, a system window appeared.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Would you like to recruit 'Dr. Dominic' as a companion?</p></div>

<p>What to do about Dominic.</p>

<p>To clear CallOVo, I needed Dr. Dominic.</p>

<p>He was the only one who could undo the brainwashing on the cultists.</p>

<p>But if possible, I wanted to keep him quietly locked up in the clinic…</p>

<p>"Oh, Roasted Chestnut. I recently received an invitation to join a certain religion. It's quite interesting — if you'd like, you could come with me…"</p>

<p>"Dr. Dominic!"</p>

<p>"Yes?"</p>

<p>"PLEASE BE MY COMPANION!!"</p>
