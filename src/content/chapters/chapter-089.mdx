<h1>Chapter 89</h1>

<p>As if it had never opened its mouth in the first place, it snapped shut.</p>

<p>It didn't slash with its blade, either. The toothed sword merely brushed past me.</p>

<p>Seo Moonchan retreated, widening the distance between us.</p>

<p>"...Huh? What the..."</p>

<p>The most bewildered person there was Seo Moonchan himself.</p>

<p>As the weapon's owner, he understood better than anyone how absurd what had just happened was.</p>

<p>Mischievous Predator was an exceptionally violent weapon, true to both its name and appearance.</p>

<p>It unleashed relentless attacks, sometimes becoming so aggressive that even its owner struggled to control it.</p>

<p>If anything, it usually bit enemies far more than necessary.</p>

<p>As far as I knew, this had never happened before.</p>

<p>'Why won't it attack me?'</p>

<p>I couldn't understand it either, so I stared at the toothed sword.</p>

<p>"Huh..." Seo Moonchan muttered as he swung his sword around experimentally before charging again.</p>

<p>Won Taebeom immediately blocked with his gauntlet.</p>

<p>But with both Mok Saehwa and Seo Moonchan focusing all their attacks solely on me, he quickly reached his limit.</p>

<p>This time, Mok Saehwa's bullet succeeded in hitting me.</p>

<p>Or rather... it almost did.</p>

<p>"Wow, Goyo-nim!"</p>

<p>Mok Saehwa exclaimed in surprise.</p>

<p>I was even more shocked than before.</p>

<p>Just before the bullet touched me, it popped into a blossom with a soft poof.</p>

<p>Having somehow become someone who got hit by a flower, I instinctively caught the falling bloom.</p>

<p>Originally, the bullet should have embedded itself in my flesh before blooming and draining my life force.</p>

<p>Instead, it had simply turned into a flower and fallen to the ground.</p>

<p>Even Won Taebeom finally asked after witnessing the second anomaly.</p>

<p>"...Is it... supposed to do that?"</p>

<p>There was no way I had an answer.</p>

<p>"Uh... I... don't really know either."</p>

<p>Watching the battle from atop my head, Dominic slid down onto my shoulder and calmly explained.</p>

<p>"Roasted Chestnut. No matter how insignificant a creature may be, the instinct to revere and worship a god cannot be denied."</p>

<p>He explained as though there were nothing surprising about it at all.</p>

<p>That it was simply the natural order.</p>

<p>Of course, I was extremely surprised.</p>

<p>The idea that magical items would worship me was almost as unbelievable as me being a Young God.</p>

<p>I still had no idea what any of this meant.</p>

<p>But one thing about the current situation was clear.</p>

<p>'They can't attack me.'</p>

<p>I immediately revised the plan I'd been forming in my head.</p>

<p>"Lieutenant Won."</p>

<p>Then I raised my right hand, still wearing the gauntlet.</p>

<p>"I'll apply the mark."</p>

<p>Paladin's Oath only displayed its true power when both gauntlets were used together.</p>

<p>Its mechanism was simple.</p>

<p>Mark an enemy with the right gauntlet.</p>

<p>Then attack that marked enemy with the left gauntlet to dramatically increase the damage dealt.</p>

<p>The amount of bonus damage apparently depended on Won Taebeom's condition, though even I didn't know the exact details.</p>

<p>Right now, because he had been carrying me, Won Taebeom was only wearing the left gauntlet.</p>

<p>I intended to wear the right one and apply the mark for him.</p>

<p>Since the items were on my side, the directors couldn't attack me at all.</p>

<p>That meant I could move much more boldly.</p>

<p>It would be difficult to reach Mok Saehwa since she fought at range.</p>

<p>But Seo Moonchan would have to keep approaching Won Taebeom to attack him.</p>

<p>I planned to seize that opportunity.</p>

<p>Just from that one sentence, Won Taebeom immediately understood what I was planning.</p>

<p>His reaction, however, wasn't encouraging.</p>

<p>"Trainee Han. Is this how you behave even with the Special Mission Team?"</p>

<p>"...Huh?"</p>

<p>I thought it was a good plan, but Won Taebeom answered coldly.</p>

<p>"I gave you the gauntlet for defense. Focus only on your own safety."</p>

<p>"...Yes."</p>

<p>I immediately shrank back.</p>

<p>Still, Won Taebeom seemed willing to take advantage of our current advantage.</p>

<p>He clenched the gauntleted hand into a fist before opening it again.</p>

<p>Then he adjusted his grip on me and said,</p>

<p>"We'll move quickly."</p>

<p>He shot forward.</p>

<p>Boom!</p>

<p>His gauntlet slammed directly into Seo Moonchan's toothed sword.</p>

<p>The heavy punch made the blade vibrate violently.</p>

<p>Seo Moonchan redirected the blow and stepped backward.</p>

<p>The instant he created an opening, bullets flew through it.</p>

<p>Won Taebeom deflected the bullets aimed at him with his gauntlet.</p>

<p>The ricocheting rounds struck a nearby wall before transforming into flower petals and vanishing.</p>

<p>He then brushed aside the toothed sword lunging for his head and drove a heavy punch toward Seo Moonchan's abdomen.</p>

<p>Seo Moonchan dodged the punch effortlessly.</p>

<p>At the same time, he also avoided my outstretched hand, which had nearly touched him.</p>

<p>"Whoa there, Goyo-nim. That's cheating."</p>

<p>My fingers barely missed him.</p>

<p>I withdrew my hand with a disappointed sigh.</p>

<p>Won Taebeom shot me a glance, but the battle was too intense for him to scold me.</p>

<p>Several more exchanges followed.</p>

<p>Now that he no longer needed to devote as much attention to protecting me, Won Taebeom's movements became far smoother.</p>

<p>He still couldn't seize the advantage, but he managed to hold his ground by the narrowest margin.</p>

<p>As time passed, the situation slowly tilted in our favor.</p>

<p>Mok Saehwa cried out impatiently.</p>

<p>"Director Seo! At this rate we'll never get Goyo-nim to sign!"</p>

<p>She turned her gun toward herself.</p>

<p>Bang! Bang!</p>

<p>Successive shots rang out as flowers bloomed all over her own body.</p>

<p>Seeing what she was doing made me panic.</p>

<p>Sacrificing her own life force to increase her attack power was something Mok Saehwa only resorted to in battles she absolutely had to win.</p>

<p>Her eyes turned bloodshot.</p>

<p>Her flushed face heaved as she tore the withered flower from her magazine and threw it onto the ground.</p>

<p>Breathing heavily enough for her chest to rise and fall, she looked straight at me.</p>

<p>"Goyo-nim... haa... just... sign... the contract... please... okay?"</p>

<p>A chill ran down my spine.</p>

<p>She smiled brightly at my reaction.</p>

<p>Then...</p>

<p>Click.</p>

<p>She reloaded her weapon and aimed at Won Taebeom.</p>

<p>"Sorry, but I'm upgrading this to eight weeks of hospitalization. Please don't take it personally!"</p>

<p>Ratatatatat!</p>

<p>The gun that had been firing single shots switched to full-auto, unleashing a torrent of bullets.</p>

<p>With machine-gun-like suppressive fire filling the area, Won Taebeom's mobility was severely restricted.</p>

<p>Seo Moonchan instantly seized the opportunity and closed in with the toothed sword.</p>

<p>Won Taebeom dodged the blade.</p>

<p>But just as if it had been waiting for this exact moment, the sword split open wide and bit down on his gauntlet.</p>

<p>Clang!</p>

<p>Metal crashed against metal.</p>

<p>Neither the teeth nor the gauntlet suffered any damage.</p>

<p>However, the gauntlet's surface was now coated in thick poison.</p>

<p>If even a little of it splashed onto his skin, he would be poisoned immediately.</p>

<p>Won Taebeom tried to pull away from Seo Moonchan while shaking the poison off his gauntlet.</p>

<p>That was when a bullet finally hit him.</p>

<p>"...!"</p>

<p>Won Taebeom narrowed one eye.</p>

<p>A brilliant flower instantly bloomed from his shoulder.</p>

<p>Mok Saehwa's eyes shone even brighter.</p>

<p>After draining another person's life force, she intensified her barrage.</p>

<p>I did my best to swat away the incoming bullets, but with Seo Moonchan constantly pressing the attack, it wasn't easy.</p>

<p>The poison coating Won Taebeom's gauntlet further restricted his movements.</p>

<p>'We still have to survive for at least five more minutes.'</p>

<p>At this rate, I'd end up being forced to sign that contract.</p>

<p>I decided to rely on item power.</p>

<p>"Lieutenant Won! Just take the bullets! I have a healing item!"</p>

<p>His eyes met mine.</p>

<p>I looked at him as if begging him to trust me.</p>

<p>Mok Saehwa sounded almost disappointed.</p>

<p>"The bullets from my Blooming Funeral can't be countered with healing items, Goyo-nim!"</p>

<p>I know.</p>

<p>When someone was riddled with Blooming Funeral's bullets, the life force it stole couldn't be restored by ordinary healing items.</p>

<p>But...</p>

<p>'This isn't an ordinary healing item.'</p>

<p>A six-star Phoenix Meringue Cookie from Sla2Plate should be more than enough.</p>

<p>The question was whether Won Taebeom would trust me.</p>

<p>What I was asking was essentially for him to gamble his life on my word.</p>

<p>There was no way we shared that level of trust.</p>

<p>Honestly, it wouldn't have been strange if he simply ignored me.</p>

<p>If he did, I'd try suggesting another plan.</p>

<p>But unexpectedly, Won Taebeom immediately charged straight at Seo Moonchan.</p>

<p>Boom!</p>

<p>Seo Moonchan blocked the incoming punch with his sword and let out a dry laugh.</p>

<p>"Lieutenant Won... I didn't know you were the type to listen to your juniors so obediently."</p>

<p>Flowers bloomed all over Won Taebeom's body.</p>

<p>A bullet even embedded itself in his cheek, forming another flower bud.</p>

<p>But he never faltered.</p>

<p>Calmly deflecting the relentless barrage of punches, Seo Moonchan sneered.</p>

<p>"Even so, trying to beat me with only one arm is asking a bit much, don't you think?"</p>

<p>I agreed.</p>

<p>Which was exactly why I intended to make sure it wasn't just one arm anymore.</p>

<p>As their increasingly rapid exchange brought them completely within arm's reach...</p>

<p>I reached out.</p>

<p>Straight into the path of the toothed sword's swing.</p>

<p>Seo Moonchan's eyes widened.</p>

<p>Before he could react...</p>

<p>The toothed sword opened its mouth first.</p>

<p>Because it opened its jaws, it barely avoided cutting me.</p>

<p>But in doing so...</p>

<p>The tips of its teeth ended up pointed directly at Dominic.</p>

<p>I noticed that.</p>

<p>But I didn't care.</p>

<p>Unless it was another god, nothing could affect Dominic.</p>

<p>A mere item would simply pass straight through him.</p>

<p>Or so I thought.</p>

<p>Apparently unwilling to let something filthy pass through him, Dominic flattened himself more firmly against me.</p>

<p>He seemed unwilling even to let it phase through him.</p>

<p>The moment he squished against me in annoyance...</p>

<p>Crunch.</p>

<p>The sword's teeth shattered.</p>

<p>There was no need to wonder who the culprit was.</p>

<p>Startled by the completely unexpected turn of events, Seo Moonchan froze for just an instant.</p>

<p>I grabbed him by the collar.</p>

<p>"...Goyo-nim!"</p>

<p>Then I pulled myself straight into his arms.</p>

<p>Already thrown off balance because of the sword, Seo Moonchan instinctively caught me.</p>

<p>I slammed the right gauntlet against him, branding him with the mark.</p>

<p>"Lieutenant Won!!"</p>

<p>I didn't even have time to finish shouting before Won Taebeom was already moving.</p>

<p>Freed from restraint, he punched straight at Seo Moonchan despite being covered in flowers.</p>

<p>Seo Moonchan immediately threw me aside and raised the toothed sword to defend himself.</p>

<p>But the timing was just slightly off.</p>

<p>"Ghk!"</p>

<p>Blood burst from Seo Moonchan's mouth as the punch landed squarely in his abdomen.</p>

<p>The bespectacled man in the business suit flew through the air before crashing into a building with a tremendous bang.</p>

<p>After rolling across the ground, I couldn't stop the corners of my mouth from lifting.</p>

<p>Won Taebeom hurried over and picked me back up.</p>

<p>At the same time, he growled at me in a low voice.</p>

<p>"Are you insane?"</p>

<p>"Eat this first."</p>

<p>With one hand I plucked the flowers blooming from his body.</p>

<p>With the other, I pulled a cookie from my inventory and held it out.</p>

<p>Won Taebeom swallowed the meringue cookie in a single bite.</p>

<p>His brow furrowed.</p>

<p>Recovering almost instantly, he stared at me with even greater suspicion.</p>

<p>I hurriedly explained.</p>

<p>"It's an item from Sla2Plate. I brought it out after clearing the trial."</p>

<p>Meanwhile, Mok Saehwa lowered her gun and spoke with heartfelt admiration.</p>

<p>"Seriously... Goyo-nim really belongs in Paseong..."</p>

<p>Just as she once again began preaching the virtues of recruiting me...</p>

<p>Flash!</p>

<p>The surroundings became as bright as midday.</p>

<p>There was only one thing that kind of explosion of light could mean.</p>

<p>Daeil Yeorae.</p>

<p>The moment I worried about Samra's safety, a powerful gust of wind swept past.</p>

<p>My hair whipped violently across my face, making me squint.</p>

<p>Something wrapped around my waist.</p>

<p>I was yanked violently backward, crashing into something as solid as a wall.</p>

<p>I turned my head.</p>

<p>"...!"</p>

<p>A white mask filled my vision.</p>

<p>"Han Goyo."</p>

<p>CEO Cha spoke in his mechanical voice.</p>

<p>"Ready to sign?"</p>
