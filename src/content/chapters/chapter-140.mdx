<h1>Chapter 140</h1>

<p>As Kwak Hanmuk was standing in front of me, my vision was completely blocked, and I couldn't see anything.</p>

<p>I cautiously took a step to the side, trying to figure out the situation.</p>

<p>Despite Kwak Hanmuk's rude remarks, Ham Jiwol and the directors of Paseong showed no reaction.</p>

<p>They simply stared at me, looking utterly dazed.</p>

<p>I couldn't guess at all what was going on.</p>

<p>Judging by the fact that I had been inside a glass pillar, it seemed my condition wasn't very good.</p>

<p>I usually lost consciousness for a long time whenever I finished a Trial and emerged, but...</p>

<p>'Wasn't it 72 hours this time?'</p>

<p>If anything, I actually woke up sooner than usual.</p>

<p>As I racked my brain, I heard an ominous murmuring.</p>

<p>&quot;He's alive...&quot;</p>

<p>Suddenly, a stream of blood gushed from Ham Jiwol's nose.</p>

<p>Looking closer, I saw that the capillaries in her eyes had burst, turning the whites blood-red, while dark, bruised circles marred the skin beneath them. </p>

<p>She looked as if she hadn't slept in days, and blood trickling down her face, she cried out.</p>

<p>“He's really alive... Goyo-ssi, he's alive...!”</p>

<p>Huh?</p>

<p>I wanted to ask what she meant, but Ham Jiwol’s demeanor was so intense that I couldn’t bring myself to speak.</p>

<p>The way Ham Jiwol looked at me was more than just fervor.</p>

<p>It might be a bit of an embarrassing thought, but it was as if she had fallen in love.</p>

<p>It was a look that truly could not be described in any other way.</p>

<p>Blushing and gasping for breath, she swayed. Then, she collapsed onto the floor.</p>

<p>She had fainted.</p>

<p>I stared at the back of her head with my mouth agape.</p>

<p>Although I still knew nothing, for now, Ham Jiwol being unconcious wasn't a bad thing.</p>

<p>“Lieutenant Won Taebeom.”</p>

<p>Samra broke the silence and spoke.</p>

<p>“I request that you remove Director Ham Jiwol.” </p>

<p>It meant he wanted him to drag her outside.</p>

<p>However, Won Taebeom did not react to Samra's words.</p>

<p>He still had his eyes fixed on me.</p>

<p>It was an intense gaze, with a different meaning than Ham Jiwol's.</p>

<p>I couldn't help but be flustered by his gaze.</p>

<p>Since I had manipulated his mind, it would have been appropriate for him to feel disgust or fear of an unknown entity.</p>

<p>But such emotions were not visible in Won Taebeom's eyes.</p>

<p>I couldn't fully grasp it, but it seemed closer to confusion.</p>

<p><em>He really does like me.</em></p>

<p>...I absolutely do not agree with the young god's way of thinking.</p>

<p>But it certainly didn't seem like he disliked me.</p>

<p>‘Lieutenant Won. I will come to kneel and apologize soon.’</p>

<p>While I was diligently sending silent apologies, Samra called out to him again.</p>

<p>“Lieutenant Won Taebeom.”</p>

<p>Only then did Won Taebeom slowly take his eyes off me.</p>

<p>He seemed to be deep in thought for a moment, then without a word, he pulled Ham Jiwol over his shoulder and slung her over his shoulder.</p>

<p>It was then.</p>

<p>Amidst chilly atmosphere, a cell phone ringtone with a cute melody rang out.</p>

<p>The owner of the ringtone, a popular girl group song, was Mok Saehwa.</p>

<p>As soon as she answered the call, her face lit up instantly.</p>

<p>“CEO!”</p>

<p>Along with her, Seo Moonchan’s face also filled with joy.</p>

<p>Mok Saehwa continued speaking clearly.</p>

<p>“Yes. Goyo-nim is alive. Ah, he is right here with me, so I will hand the phone over...”</p>

<p>Slash.</p>

<p>Mok Saehwa’s cell phone was sliced in two.</p>

<p>The metallic feather that had grazed her finger returned to Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>“...I tried to, but it looks like it won’t work.”</p>

<p>Mok Saehwa sucked on the tip of her finger, from which a bead of blood was welling up, and stuffed the severed cell phone into her pocket.</p>

<p>The two directors turned to look at me at the same time. Then, suddenly, they started clapping and singing in unison.</p>

<p>“Goyo-nim! Congratulations on your resurrection!”</p>

<p>Clap, clap, clap, clap.</p>

<p>Amidst the pouring applause, I almost blurted out &quot;Thank you&quot; without realizing it.</p>

<p>While I was still dazed by the resurrection congratulations—something I had never received in my life—Seo Moonchan abruptly pulled a neatly folded handkerchief from his suit jacket.</p>

<p>He took off his glasses and made a show of wiping his eyes.</p>

<p>‘Not a single tear is falling.’</p>

<p>At this point, I waited, curious about the purpose of this performance.</p>

<p>After pretending to wipe his tears for a moment, Seo Moonchan spoke.</p>

<p>“Goyo-nim. I hear that CEO Cha has just woken up.”</p>

<p>“What?”</p>

<p>“He had been in a coma. He has barely regained consciousness, but he is still not in very good condition.”</p>

<p>It felt like my brain had shut down.</p>

<p>That can't be right.</p>

<p><em>I must have revived him, right?</em> </p>

<p>Resurrection brings the dead back to life and heals the wounds of the living.</p>

<p>Since it is an area-of-effect skill that applies to the entire space, I assumed CEO Cha would have emerged fully recovered.</p>

<p>After all, even I, having regained consciousness after 72 hours, am perfectly fine.</p>

<p>‘Could it be because of the Nonbeliever trait?’</p>

<p>I began to suspect that CEO Cha’s Nonbeliever trait might have blocked the healing.</p>

<p>My heart sank.</p>

<p>When the Trial is sealed off, the infected virus disappears, but physical injuries remain.</p>

<p>CEO Cha continued to take damage even after losing more than half of his life force to Light Alteration.</p>

<p>To think he came out in that state without receiving any healing.</p>

<p>“.......”</p>

<p>During the 72 hours I was trapped in the black space, I thought about countless things.</p>

<p>It was because there was little I could do, left all alone.</p>

<p>It was a somewhat painful time, as there were many thoughts clinging to me so persistently that I wanted to stop thinking about them.</p>

<p>Thoughts of CEO Cha were among them.</p>

<p>My heart felt heavy, as if a boulder had been placed upon it.</p>

<p>It was a time when I could not hide the darkening expression of worry.</p>

<p>“Director Seo Moonchan’s remarks were largely exaggerated.”</p>

<p>Samra spoke, his eyes flashing with light.</p>

<p>“It is true that he was unconscious, but CEO Cha’s vital signs have now recovered to perfectly normal levels. He is at a level where there would be no setbacks even if he were to enter a trial immediately.”</p>

<p>It appeared he had hacked into the hospital’s equipment to retrieve the information.</p>

<p>Samra looked at the directors of Paseong and declared.</p>

<p>“I will stop any remarks that would confuse Han Goyo-ssi.”</p>

<p>Regardless, the directors remained steadfast.</p>

<p id="samra">“It seems lying is not easy in front of Samra Mansang<sup><a href="#samrafoot">1</a></sup>.”</p>

<p>While Seo Moonchan was making a lighthearted remark and shrugging his shoulders, Mok Saehwa called out to me.</p>

<p>“Goyo-nim.”</p>

<p>She clasped her hands together and looked at me with pleading eyes.</p>

<p>“Please come visit our CEO in the hospital.”</p>

<p>At the same time, Kwak Hanmuk couldn’t hold back and stepped forward.</p>

<p>“Wow, how long do I have to wait like this?”</p>

<p>He tilted his head crookedly and raised his voice.</p>

<p>“Isn’t it time for you directors to get lost?”</p>

<p>Mok Saehwa quickly continued speaking.</p>

<p>“Of course, Paseong has suffered a huge loss! And the losses will only get bigger in the future!”</p>

<p>She smiled brightly.</p>

<p>“As long as Goyo-nim comes to visit, it doesn’t matter.”</p>

<p>“The CEO will be waiting for Goyo-nim, too.”</p>

<p>Seo Moonchan quickly threw in a remark as well.</p>

<p>The directors stood in a line, looking at me like pitiful cats.</p>

<p>It seemed like a strategy to appeal to my sympathy, but having experienced Mischievous Predator and Blooming Funeral, I felt no emotion.</p>

<p>However, I gave them the answer they wanted.</p>

<p>“Yes. I will go visit.”</p>

<p>Everyone was surprised by my answer. Even the directors looked shocked.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk looked at me with a bewildered expression.</p>

<p>“Chestnut-ie... Is it because you're still sick? You're saying strange things because you're still in a state of mental and physical weakness.”</p>

<p>“Han Goyo-ssi. That is an unnecessary action.”</p>

<p>Samra looked as if he were about to rattle off CEO Cha’s physical data right away, showing exactly how fine he was.</p>

<p>As Je Hyun-oh clattered his metal hand, Mo Haein also quietly admonished me.</p>

<p>“There is no need to do that.”</p>

<p>But... I wanted to go.</p>

<p>I felt that I could only feel at ease if I saw CEO Cha alive and moving with my own eyes.</p>

<p><em>It would have been better if I had made him my subordinate.</em></p>

<p><em>If I had, I could have verified every single detail of him right away.</em></p>

<p>I felt it once again, but the young god’s way of thinking seemed a bit unique.</p>

<p>I did not agree with this thought, either.</p>

<p>‘And it seemed like CEO Cha doesn't like gods very much.’</p>

<p>It was wrong to force someone to register against their will.</p>

<p>In any case, given that the young god had a history of causing a scene, I did have to bring up the matter.</p>

<p>I made a polite request to the directors.</p>

<p>“I will visit soon, so I would appreciate it if you could leave for now.”</p>

<p>“Of course. We will leave promptly.”</p>

<p>“Please contact us anytime, Goyo-nim!”</p>

<p>Having gotten what they wanted, the directors bowed respectfully as if nothing had happened.</p>

<p>However, before they could even disappear, my vision flickered with holographic colors.</p>

<p>Samra had formed a virtual space and summoned the entire Special Mission Team.</p>

<p>It was in the exact same form as the Special Mission Team office in the destroyed main building.</p>

<p>I wanted to feel a glimmer of joy at this familiar sight, but I could not.</p>

<p>I... found myself surrounded by the Special Mission Team...</p>

<p>'This is a bit scary.'</p>

<p>Except for Mo Haein, everyone else was taller than me.</p>

<p>As they all looked down at me, memories of Cell-X naturally came to mind.</p>

<p>It was the memory of lying face down in front of Je Hyun-oh and Kwak Hanmuk.</p>

<p>Click. Je Hyun-oh removed his goggles.</p>

<p>As he revealed his eyes and stared at me silently, I became twice as scared.</p>

<p>If he had just called my name, &quot;Goyo,&quot; I could have at least prepared myself, but the fact that he said nothing made it even more frightening.</p>

<p>I was trembling inwardly at that moment.</p>

<p>&quot;Han Goyo-ssi.&quot;</p>

<p>Samra called my name first. And what followed was unbelievable.</p>

<p>&quot;You have recovered vital signs 72 hours after being pronounced dead.&quot;</p>

<p>I was unable to react for a moment, then slowly asked back.</p>

<p>&quot;...Did I die and come back to life?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yes.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;.......&quot;</p>

<p>To think I died and came back to life... It was something that would have terrified me, too.</p>

<p>I began to understand all the reactions of the people earlier, which had been beyond strange and even bordering on bizarre.</p>

<p>I was practically going crazy because I had absolutely no idea that connecting to a physical body meant something like this.</p>

<p>And to think they proceeded with it so publicly.</p>

<p>If I had known this would happen, I would have resurrected while hiding in some corner.</p>

<p>Between this the real-name registration system for subordinates, it seemed that gods simply didn't care about privacy.</p>

<p>I felt a sense of dread regarding what was about to happen, but Samra brought up something even more daunting to me.</p>

<p>“Previously, I had confirmed the existence of transcendent beings, the Outer Gods, outside of Earth and was searching for information regarding them. Therefore, I judged that there is a sufficient possibility that such transcendent beings exist within Earth as well.”</p>

<p>Samra’s pupils moved like the aperture of a camera.</p>

<p>He looked at me without blinking and asked a question.</p>

<p>“Han Goyo-ssi, you are a god. Is that correct?”</p>

<p>I froze right there.</p>

<p>And I realized that Samra had already obtained the answer from my reaction.</p>

<p>No... Even if I had lied perfectly, it would have been meaningless.</p>

<p>Because Samra was already convinced.</p>

<p>In fact, it was a ridiculous situation to even try to make any more excuses.</p>

<p>Reluctantly, I opened my mouth with a heavy heart.</p>

<p>“Um... just as Samra said... I....”</p>

<p>Feeling my face burning, I spoke in a barely audible mumble.</p>

<p>“...am the god of Earth....”</p>

<p>It was truly not an easy task.</p>

<p>After speaking, I cautiously glanced at the others.</p>

<p>I expected them to look at me like I was a lunatic, but surprisingly, their faces were incredibly serious.</p>

<p>As if...they had exchanged some kind of conversation while I was unconscious for 72 hours.</p>

<p>Of course, Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein looked visibly surprised, but they were remarkably calm considering the shocking sentence they had just heard.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh, having known about it all along, didn't show any particular signs of agitation.</p>

<p>However, the way he had been staring at me for so long was starting to give me goosebumps.</p>

<p>I struggled to shake off the temptation to check his status window to see why he was acting that way.</p>

<p>“Trainee.”</p>

<p>Mo Haein called out to me in a stiff tone.</p>

<p>“Yes...”</p>

<p>Feeling guilty, I cautiously glanced at Mo Haein and asked.</p>

<p>“Should I get into push-up position...?”</p>

<p>At that, Kwak Hanmuk turned his gaze to the side and muttered.</p>

<p>“This is driving me crazy.”</p>

<p>It seemed this wasn't the right approach.</p>

<p>As I stood there awkwardly, Mo Haein bit her lip tightly and snapped at me.</p>

<p>“Report what happened while clearing Cell-X.”</p>

<p>She glared at me. Her eyes looked as if she were about to grab me by the collar and shake me at any moment.</p>

<p>“What did you do to be dead for 72 hours all by yourself?”</p>

<p>While I hesitated, unsure how to answer, Je Hyun-oh spoke for the first time.</p>

<p>“Goyo pushed himself too hard. Right up until the brink of death.”</p>

<p>Well, that’s true, but...</p>

<p>Still, considering the circumstances, there were parts that were unavoidable...</p>

<p>‘He was the one who happily helped me out when I was a god, too.’</p>

<p>While I mumbled, feeling a tiny bit of injustice, Kwak Hanmuk narrowed his eyes and spoke.</p>

<p>“And I suppose the fact that I’m fine is also Goyo’s doing.”</p>

<p>“That’s correct...”</p>

<p>“What do you mean by ‘turning me into a ball’?”</p>

<p>“To protect you, Captain Kwak...”</p>

<p>“So, you used some kind of divine power, and because you pushed yourself too hard, you were dead for 72 hours, right?”</p>

<p>“Yes. That is correct...”</p>

<p>With my answer, silence fell for a moment.</p>

<p>I felt a slightly strange sensation in the gazes I was receiving.</p>

<p>Even though I was clearly being scolded, I didn’t dislike it.</p>

<p>It seemed like everyone was treating me not as some alien being, but as an ordinary trainee.</p>

<p>Just as I quietly closed and opened my eyes...</p>

<p>“This won’t do.”</p>

<p>Mo Haein suddenly blurted out, strode toward me, and grabbed my wrist.</p>

<p>Then she said to Samra.</p>

<p>“Samra. Let me out.”</p>

<p>Am I being arrested?</p>

<p>I stood stiff and prepared to be handcuffed when Samra said something I couldn’t understand.</p>

<p>“After discussion, we have established the top priority measures to effectively protect Han Goyo-ssi.”</p>

<p>It was a moment when I was struggling to understand what he meant.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh finally called out to me.</p>

<p>“Goyo.”</p>

<p>I looked at him with a feeling of resignation.</p>

<p>As our eyes met, his long eyes curved slightly.</p>

<p>A low, grating voice whispered to me.</p>

<p>“I’ll make you play the role of a god properly.”</p>

<p>“Huh...?”</p>

<p>“Right, Sweet Chestnut. If you’ve caused trouble, you have to take responsibility.”</p>

<p>Leaving me flustered, Kwak Hanmuk let out a deep sigh.</p>

<p>Then, wiping away all traces of playfulness, he spoke to me with a serious look in his eyes.</p>

<p>“Let’s team up.”</p>
