<h1>Chapter 62</h1>
<p>The pudding slime was still sitting in front of the tablet.</p>

<p>Even though I was still half-asleep, the sounds reaching my ears were incredibly loud.</p>

<p>Boom! Ratatatatat—!</p>

<p>The noise was coming from the tablet.</p>

<p>On the screen, an explosion went off, and a man riddled with bullets collapsed to the ground.</p>

<p>The pudding slime, seriously watching the bloody scene, approached me and greeted me.</p>

<p>“Good morning, Roasted Chestnut.”</p>

<p>I hurriedly got up, grabbed the tablet, and checked the video rating first.</p>

<p>‘Not suitable for viewers under nineteen, with tags for sexuality, violence, horror, drugs, and imitation risk…?’</p>

<p>The imitation risk warning especially made me feel uneasy.</p>

<p>Not knowing how I felt, Dominic calmly explained.</p>

<p>“I discovered that I could receive additional materials through the electromagnetic waves here. Thanks to that, I was able to view many things.”</p>

<p>He mentioned a few works that he found enjoyable, but I had never heard of any of the movies.</p>

<p>When I checked the tablet, sure enough, it was connected to the internet.</p>

<p>I had no idea how he had done it, but I searched the movie titles anyway.</p>

<p>I learned that Dominic had extremely refined, cinephile-like tastes.</p>

<p>He seemed to have watched many art films and classic films, but they were all movies I had never seen.</p>

<p>There were even black-and-white films and silent films.</p>

<p>My first plan of trying to reform him through visual materials had failed.</p>

<p>I gave up on the plan cleanly and returned the tablet so Dominic could continue watching movies.</p>

<p>Dominic also did not forget to gently comfort me so I would not feel disappointed.</p>

<p>“The action film I watched with Roasted Chestnut last night was enjoyable as well, but I believe this side suits my preferences a little more. I have also gained a better understanding of the planet called Earth.”</p>

<p>I wondered what kind of Earth Dominic had come to understand.</p>

<p>Maybe later I should search for movie summary shorts on MTube.</p>

<p>Either way, thanks to Dominic, I was definitely awake now.</p>

<p>With my sleepiness completely gone, I ate the breakfast that had been delivered on a tray.</p>

<p>After washing up, I put on my uniform and stood in front of the mirror.</p>

<p>Today was the day I would meet Je Hyun-oh and the captains again after a long time.</p>

<p>In the afternoon, we would conduct the briefing for Sla2Plate, then immediately enter at the location where Sla2Plate was incubating with the special mission team.</p>

<p>The entire special mission team was currently out on missions.</p>

<p>So it was decided that everyone except me would travel to the Sla2Plate incubation site while conducting a video meeting.</p>

<p>The additional Adaptee from the TRA who was scheduled to enter with us was also currently carrying out a mission, so Samra would conduct the briefing separately from the special mission team.</p>

<p>‘Lieutenant Colonel Je today said he would come out of the trial this afternoon, right? The captains said they would return around then too.’</p>

<p>The captains had traveled a considerable distance to capture an Adaptee who had committed a serious crime.</p>

<p>Because Adaptees could use items obtained from trials even outside them, they occasionally abused them for criminal purposes.</p>

<p>In such cases, TRA arrested and punished the Adaptee.</p>

<p>According to TRA’s military law, captured Adaptees usually worked for the country like slaves instead of receiving fines or prison sentences.</p>

<p>However, in cases where the crime was especially severe, immediate execution was the rule.</p>

<p>Because there was no telling what kind of dangerous acts they might commit.</p>

<p>There had even been a case in the past where an Adaptee stored explosive items in their inventory and attempted a terrorist attack against TRA, making punishments even stricter.</p>

<p>Considering that South Korea technically had a death penalty but did not practically carry out executions, it was surprising.</p>

<p>However, since TRA, Adaptees, and trials were all strictly classified information, the outside world had no way of knowing.</p>

<p>If it became public knowledge, there would probably have been controversy.</p>

<p>In many ways, Adaptees had very few human rights.</p>

<p>Thinking that my current situation might not be much different from a serious criminal’s, I placed Dominic on my shoulder.</p>

<p>A slight weight settled on one side.</p>

<p>I clenched my fist tightly against the urge that kept creeping up.</p>

<p>‘I really want to squish him just once.’</p>

<p>Unaware of my thoughts, Dominic wobbled his body and asked,</p>

<p>“Roasted Chestnut. Is the &lt;Slay to Plate&gt; you are entering today a place like Morneveil?”</p>

<p>“It’s similar, but it is also a completely different space.”</p>

<p>I briefly explained trials and Adaptees to him.</p>

<p>Then a thought suddenly occurred to me.</p>

<p>Would Dominic know anything about the creator?</p>

<p>Even if he had lost his memories and did not know specifics, maybe he could provide some kind of hint.</p>

<p>“Um, Dominic-ssi. What would you think if there was an existence that predicted all of this?”</p>

<p>The answer that came back was clear.</p>

<p>“It would be a god.”</p>

<p>“Would it be an existence similar to you, Dominic-ssi?”</p>

<p>A low laugh followed.</p>

<p>“Who knows.”</p>

<p>I could understand the meaning contained in that short answer.</p>

<p>He was saying I should not compare him with such things.</p>

<p>‘Even though you lost your memories.’</p>

<p>I could only wonder how he knew he was extraordinary.</p>

<p>Perhaps it was something he instinctively felt.</p>

<p>I wanted to ask more, but Samra arrived, so I closed my mouth.</p>

<p>“Han Goyo-ssi.”</p>

<p>I was supposed to hear about the current state of trials in South Korea and notable trials in other countries from Samra that morning, but…</p>

<p>I froze mid-greeting when I saw the woman who had appeared with him.</p>

<p>“Hello…!”</p>

<p>It was Ham Jiwol, the head of the Research Analysis Division.</p>

<p>She looked extremely excited and was hugging a large bag.</p>

<p>A bag that looked like it contained various horrifying tools.</p>

<p>I immediately looked at Samra.</p>

<p>‘Please save me.’</p>

<p>Receiving my desperate plea for help, Samra let out a sigh like a human.</p>

<p>“Due to Director Jang Seokyun’s orders, Director Ham Jiwol will be checking Han Goyo-ssi’s current physical condition. We agreed to finish as quickly as possible, so I ask for your cooperation.”</p>

<p>A health examination?</p>

<p>The justification was so perfect that even Samra apparently could not stop this one.</p>

<p>I looked at Ham Jiwol approaching with wide eyes.</p>

<p>When our eyes met, she smiled brightly.</p>

<p>Adjusting her large glasses, Ham Jiwol giggled and came closer.</p>

<p>“Goyo-ssi…. I’m happy you came back safely! Last time, I didn’t get to properly introduce myself…. Today, I want to spend a little more time with you, Goyo-ssi….”</p>

<p>It was a terrifying thing to hear.</p>

<p>It was even scarier because I had no idea what she planned to do.</p>

<p>Just when I was desperately hoping that, with Samra present, it would only end with something like drawing a little blood—</p>

<p>“Director Ham Jiwol.”</p>

<p>Samra stopped her.</p>

<p>Ham Jiwol quickly turned around and answered.</p>

<p>“Yes…!”</p>

<p>“Lieutenant Colonel Je Hyun-oh is still paying attention to Director Ham Jiwol.”</p>

<p>The Ham Jiwol who had been full of thoughts about chewing, tearing apart, and savoring my body immediately became flustered.</p>

<p>“B-but…. This is different from before…. This, this time, the Director allowed it….”</p>

<p>“I am only conveying the facts. The decision belongs to Director Ham Jiwol.”</p>

<p>Ham Jiwol’s momentum instantly collapsed.</p>

<p>Regardless, Samra stood upright and waited quietly for her choice.</p>

<p>Holding her bag tightly, Ham Jiwol made a troubled expression.</p>

<p>Looking dejected, she slowly approached me.</p>

<p>“Then… just a little blood from Goyo-ssi….”</p>

<p>“You are entering a trial today, so I cannot permit actions that may affect your condition.”</p>

<p>“Ah, ahh…. But last time Goyo-ssi needed a transfusion….”</p>

<p>“Director Ham Jiwol.”</p>

<p>Ham Jiwol looked as if the world had collapsed.</p>

<p>Then suddenly, her expression became determined.</p>

<p>“T-then!”</p>

<p>As if this was the one thing she absolutely could not give up, she shouted.</p>

<p>“I’ll collect more fingernails that I couldn’t get last time…. And epithelial cells from inside your mouth…!”</p>

<p>Samra looked at me and asked,</p>

<p>“Han Goyo-ssi, would you be okay with that?”</p>

<p>I did have some willingness to cooperate with that much.</p>

<p>Though I had no idea what she had used the previous samples for.</p>

<p>“Yes, that’s fine.”</p>

<p>“Th-thank you!”</p>

<p>Ham Jiwol looked up at me with moved eyes.</p>

<p>Since she was shorter than me, I sat down on a chair so she could match my height.</p>

<p>Ham Jiwol sat me down and quickly took out a small pair of scissors.</p>

<p>I held out my right hand.</p>

<p>She grabbed my hand and began cutting my fingernails with a quiet snip, snip.</p>

<p>“Goyo-ssi…. Your fingers are pretty….”</p>

<p>It was the scariest compliment I had ever received.</p>

<p>Fortunately, Ham Jiwol didn't suddenly pull out a hacksaw to saw off my finger.</p>

<p>After neatly trimming my fingernail, Ham Jiwol toyed with my hand, her eyes filled with lingering regret.</p>

<p>As she lingered over my hand—seemingly examining the veins and bone structure—Dominic slithered down from my shoulder, moving along my forearm.</p>

<p>Just as I began to worry about what Dominic might be up to, Samra intervened.</p>

<p>“Director Ham Jiwol.”</p>

<p>“A-alright...”</p>

<p>Grumbling, Ham Jiwol quickly tucked the sample tube containing the fingernail clipping into her coat.</p>

<p>Then, she pulled something else out of her bag.
I watched tensely, wondering what strange object she was retrieving, only to see two long cotton swabs.</p>

<p>“Now, Goyo-ssi. Say ‘ah’...”</p>

<p>When I opened my mouth, she gently swabbed the inside.</p>

<p>The sensation of the swab brushing against the inside of my cheek tickled.</p>

<p>The collection was over in an instant. Ham Jiwol held the swabs with great care and gazed at me with a look of deep emotion.</p>

<p>“Goyo-ssi, you really are... so well-behaved. If only the other compatible subjects were as well-behaved as you... that would be wonderful... but everyone hates me...”</p>

<p>It seemed she lacked the self-awareness to realize why they disliked her.</p>

<p>Just then, Samra threw me a lifeline.</p>

<p>“Han Goyo-ssi. Lieutenant Colonel Je Hyun-oh has emerged from the Trial. We will be moving to the briefing for &lt;Slay to Plate&gt;.”</p>

<p>‘Lieutenant Colonel Je!’</p>

<p>I couldn't have been more relieved to hear that.</p>

<p>He had said he wouldn't be out until the afternoon, but it seemed he had cleared it early.</p>

<p>I sprang to my feet, marveling at just how amazing Je Hyun-oh was.</p>

<p>Ham Jiwol let me leave without a fuss, as she was busy drying the swab used to collect epithelial cells.</p>

<p>“See you next time…!”</p>

<p>I simply gave a quick nod in response to her farewell and hurriedly made my escape with Samra.</p>

<p>Samra offered me a comforting pat.</p>

<p>“You worked hard.”</p>

<p>“Not at all… Thank you for saving me.”</p>

<p>Dominic, who had been quiet until then, spoke up from my shoulder.</p>

<p>“She is quite an assertive lady. If necessary, I could eliminate her interest in you. Just say the word, Roasted Chestnut.”</p>

<p>I didn’t know what method he had in mind, but I had a feeling it wouldn’t align with Earth standards, so I wanted to decline.</p>

<p>After mentally turning down the offer, I chatted with Samra as we arrived at the Special Mission Team office.</p>

<p>I was glad to see the office again after a while, but then came a polite request.</p>

<p>“Roasted Chestnut, would you mind setting me down on the table?”</p>

<p>Samra had gone to the break room to get some coffee.</p>

<p>I gently placed Dominic onto the table.</p>

<p>Dominic wandered freely across the tabletop, looking at various things.</p>

<p>He showed interest in a mound of snacks but merely observed them closely without touching or handling them.</p>

<p>It seemed he wasn't particularly curious beyond that initial glance.</p>

<p>While I stole a glance at ‘The Slime’s Adventure’, Samra returned carrying a tray with two cups of coffee.</p>

<p>“Thank you. Next time, I’ll get them myself.”</p>

<p>“There’s no need for that. This, too, is part of a supporter’s duties.”</p>

<p>It certainly seemed like he only ever made coffee for me, though...</p>

<p>Recalling how surprised the captains had been by Samra’s coffee, I accepted the cup. After confirming I had taken a sip, Samra projected three screens into the air.</p>

<p>However, the first screen displayed nothing but black.</p>

<p>I realized a moment later that it was the black of a pair of goggles.</p>

<p>His 'Crow Goggles' had been snapped in half during the Clean3 mission, so it seemed he had scavenged a new pair in the meantime.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh, who had been leaning in close, slowly pulled his face back.</p>

<p>I greeted him warmly.</p>

<p>“Lieutenant Colonel Je. Congratulations on the clear.”</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh said nothing.</p>

<p>Wondering if he couldn't hear me, I called out to him again.</p>

<p>“Lieutenant Colonel Je?”</p>

<p>— Goyo.</p>

<p>“Yes!”</p>

<p>— So, you’re not dead yet.</p>

<p>“…Yes.”</p>

<p>As I hesitated between apologizing and expressing gratitude, faces appeared on the other two screens as well.</p>

<p>— Trainee!</p>

<p>Mo Haein barked the word but then bit her lip.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk also stared at me with wide eyes.</p>

<p>However, perhaps mindful of Samra’s presence, neither of them said a word.</p>

<p>It seemed I would need to find a time to speak with them privately later.</p>

<p>‘I should probably bow my head and apologize…’</p>

<p>They must have handled the aftermath of my sudden disappearance during the Clean3 mission.</p>

<p>Thanks to them smoothing things over, I had been able to enter the next Trial without being confined to a quarantine facility.</p>

<p>I looked at Je Hyun-oh and the captains, my eyes filled with genuine apology.</p>

<p>“We will begin the briefing.”</p>

<p>Samra announced the start of the briefing.</p>

<p>“The Trial rating for &lt;Slay to Plate&gt; is five stars; this is Lieutenant Colonel Je Hyun-oh’s first time entering it. Captain Mo Haein and Captain Kwak Hanmuk have extensive experience clearing this Trial as a team.”</p>

<p>Until now, the special mission team had only ever entered four-star rated zones. This was their first time entering a five-star zone.</p>

<p>After stating the standard disclaimer that there was neither a vaccine nor a cure for the BT-STP virus, Samra explained the concept of Sla2Plate.</p>

<p>I was listening intently to the briefing.</p>

<p>"...Huh?"</p>

<p>I heard the sound of Dominic laughing.</p>

<p>When I looked his way, Dominic made his way across the table toward me.</p>

<p>I placed my hand on the table to help him climb up.</p>

<p>As he scrambled up my forearm, Dominic spoke.</p>

<p>"Roasted Chestnut. There is someone here who can see me."</p>

<p>The moment I wondered who it might be...</p>

<p>I locked eyes with Kwak Hanmuk.</p>
