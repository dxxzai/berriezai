<h1>Chapter 78</h1>

<p>I tensed up, worried Dominic might recognize the currency, and answered,</p>

<p>"...Yes. He sent it as payment for the food."</p>

<p>Fortunately, he showed no sign of recognizing the coin or recalling any memories.</p>

<p>Dominic chose his words with elegance.</p>

<p>"It seems his aesthetic sense is not bad."</p>

<p>Perhaps because he had made it himself, he seemed rather pleased with it.</p>

<p>Wanting to inspect it more closely, Dominic climbed up my forearm toward the coin.</p>

<p>But there was nothing to be gained from letting him examine the Dark Star Fragment.</p>

<p>Pretending not to notice, I quickly walked over to the register and dropped the coin into the cash box.</p>

<p>At that moment, a system window appeared.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>You sold <span class="glitch_t2">?????</span> and received <span class="glitch_t2">999,999,999G</span>.</p></div>

<p>Immediately after the glitched system message, a sudden notification appeared.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Business has ended.</p></div>

<p>The daily quota window appeared right afterward.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Today's Quota: 999,999,499G</p></div>

<p>The moment I saw the amount, I cursed inwardly.</p>

<p>That damned Fire Dragon was trying to leave me with only another 500G.</p>

<p>'Black Block-ssi, I'm counting on you.'</p>

<p>After taking a deep breath, I opened the money window and checked the number.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Current Funds: 999,999,999G</p></div>

<p>It hadn't changed.</p>

<p>Just like during CallOVo, that meant my funds had exceeded the display limit.</p>

<p>No matter how much Infernum tried to gouge me through the quota, he couldn't take away what existed beyond what the system could display.</p>

<p>No matter how much quota he collected, he was only scooping handfuls out of an enormous pile of money that exceeded the display limit.</p>

<p>In other words, I was practically in an infinite gold state.</p>

<p>'Black Block-ssi!!'</p>

<p>I cheered inwardly, clenching my fist tightly before quickly fixing my expression.</p>

<p>At the same time, I became curious about the value of the Dark Star Fragment.</p>

<p>'Just how much is this worth?'</p>

<p>I didn't know exactly how the system judged value, but I'd expected Sla2Plate's currency to be extremely cheap compared to the Dark Star Fragment.</p>

<p>Even so, after almost the entire display limit had been deducted as quota, the number hadn't changed by even the slightest amount.</p>

<p>That meant the Dark Star Fragment itself was worth an astronomical sum.</p>

<p>'At the very least... it must be worth hundreds of millions of won.'</p>

<p>That thought naturally reminded me of the butlers who had casually handed me such an incredible fortune.</p>

<p>Just as a deleted game disappears from memory, a closed Trial never appears again.</p>

<p>Every time I closed a Trial, I was permanently saying goodbye to its NPCs.</p>

<p>Even knowing it couldn't be helped, I still felt a little lonely whenever I thought about it.</p>

<p>At the very least, I should take good care of the alien Pudding Slime out of gratitude toward the butlers.</p>

<p>Sneaking a glance over, I saw the Pudding Slime squishing contentedly around my wrist.</p>

<p>"Trainee! What is this?"</p>

<p>Just then, Mo Haein hurried over.</p>

<p>The quota window must have appeared for her as well. She had probably seen my current funds too.</p>

<p>"It seems an alien customer stopped by."</p>

<p>"I didn't see any strange-looking customer."</p>

<p>"Aliens visit while looking exactly like humans."</p>

<p>Since Mo Haein knew nothing about it, she had no choice but to accept my explanation.</p>

<p>"Even so... that much money..."</p>

<p>She still looked unconvinced, so I calmly added,</p>

<p>"Think of it like a game cheat. Honestly, I just got lucky. Most of the time it doesn't work."</p>

<p>I also explained that it was an anomaly, which was why I hadn't wanted to use the method in the first place.</p>

<p>Since I'd already mentioned during yesterday's meeting that I intended to create an anomaly, Mo Haein more or less accepted it.</p>

<p>Crossing her arms, she asked,</p>

<p>"So anyway, does that mean the standing restaurant is finished?"</p>

<p>"Yes, Captain Mo-nim."</p>

<p>Business had already ended, so I planned to run straight into town and go on a shopping spree.</p>

<p>I told her about the restaurant's new concept.</p>

<p>"From now on... it's fine dining."</p>
