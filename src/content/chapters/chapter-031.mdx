<h1>Chapter 31</h1>

<p>My body plummeted fast.</p>

<p>I barely managed to flail my limbs a few times.</p>

<p>The fall was over before I even fully registered what was happening.</p>

<p>Squelch.</p>

<p>A disgusting sensation enveloped my entire body.</p>

<p>"Haaah!"</p>

<p>I gasped for air.</p>

<p>The stench of rot flooded into my lungs, but I couldn't properly register it.</p>

<p>The sky above me was rapidly disappearing.</p>

<p>The mouth that had gaped wide to swallow me was closing.</p>

<p>I planted my feet on the squelching tongue and reached out. But there was nothing to grab onto.</p>

<p>My hands slipped.</p>

<p>The mucous membrane surrounding my limbs convulsed.</p>

<p>And then — gulp.</p>

<p>I was swallowed.</p>

<p>My body tumbled down a slick passage that felt like an esophagus.</p>

<p>Darkness on all sides, and the System window kept flashing like crazy.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Would you like to execute 'Je Hyun-oh'?</p></div>

<div class="systembox"><p>Would you like to execute 'Je Hyun-oh'?</p></div>

<div class="systembox"><p>Would you like to execute 'Je Hyun-oh'?</p></div>

<div class="systembox"><p>Would you like to execute 'Je Hyun-oh'?</p></div>

<div class="systembox"><p>Would you like to execute 'Je Hyun-oh'?</p></div>

<p>I was apparently pissed off from getting done in by Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>But Je Hyun-oh had shown me too much for me to execute him over something like this.</p>

<p>Like taking down a million zombies, for instance.</p>

<p>I knew he was strong, but witnessing it firsthand was overwhelming.</p>

<p>Having seen how useful he was, I couldn't bring myself to execute him.</p>

<p>Instead, I set a goal of surviving this place somehow and grabbing Je Hyun-oh by the collar — well, that might be too much, so maybe just lodging a complaint.</p>

<p>Honestly, even the System window was just saying that. It probably didn't actually want me to execute Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>It was the System window itself that had most desperately wanted him registered.</p>

<p>A new quest appeared as I kept tumbling endlessly through slimy mucus.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p><span class="glitch_t2">◆Ma!in! Que!st!!!</span>: Escape from the Nothing.</p></div></div>

<p>There was more written out, but it was hard to read while falling out of my mind.</p>

<p>How long did I fall?</p>

<p>Finally, my body stopped.</p>

<p>"Ugh..."</p>

<p>I landed in a space about the size of a small room and let out a small groan.</p>

<p>Fortunately, there was almost no impact from the fall.</p>

<p>I'd slid down something like an esophagus, and the floor I hit was soft and squishy.</p>

<p>Unlike the pitch-black esophagus, there was a faint light here, so I could see my surroundings.</p>

<p>Surprisingly, there was no stench.</p>

<p>It felt a bit ridiculous to say, but it seemed like I'd landed in the cleanest part.</p>

<p>The texture was just absolutely awful...</p>

<p>I let out a small "ugh" as I looked at the wall, which sank in the shape of my hand where I pressed against it.</p>

<p>My palm started itching from touching the wall, so I pulled my hand away — and froze.</p>

<p>An eye had appeared on the damp wall.</p>

<p>The same eyes that had grown on the Nothing's outer skin were now inside its guts.</p>

<p>A bloodshot eye stared at me.</p>

<p>As our gazes met, the inner wall squirmed, and more eyes sprouted one after another.</p>

<p>Soon I was surrounded by hundreds of eyes.</p>

<p>A physiological revulsion made my skin crawl, but my heart was pounding for another reason too.</p>

<p>I studied the eyes on the inner wall carefully.</p>

<p>Had the eyes from the outer skin been pushed inward? Or were they newly generated?</p>

<p>I'd never been inside DeZonDeal's final boss before, so I couldn't suppress my curiosity.</p>

<p>Already somewhat adapted to the disgust, I slowly reached out and touched an eye.</p>

<p>The Captains would have freaked out if they saw this, but I was alone here, so there was no one to scold me.</p>

<p>The eye was wet and slimy.</p>

<p>It didn't blink — either because it couldn't feel pain or because it had no eyelids. It just rolled its pupil, following my hand.</p>

<p>I pulled my hand away and recalled something I'd momentarily forgotten.</p>

<p>'I need to get out of here.'</p>

<p>All I had on me was the ammunition belt around my waist and a single military knife strapped to my thigh for emergencies.</p>

<p>No matter how much I swung that knife, it would be useless against a zombie that couldn't feel pain.</p>

<p>'And I can't count on the Captains to save me.'</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh had personally fished me out and dropped me onto the boss monster.</p>

<p>There was no way he'd let Mo Haein and Kwak Hanmuk rescue me.</p>

<p>But it didn't seem like he wanted me dead either.</p>
<br/>
<p><span class="flashback">"Goyo. Don't die."</span></p>
<br/>
<p>That was what Je Hyun-oh said as he dropped me.</p>

<p>He probably wanted to confirm something about me.</p>

<p>I had no idea what, though...</p>

<p>'Couldn't you have at least thrown in a gun while you were at it.'</p>

<p>Suddenly I felt like I could grab Je Hyun-oh by the collar if I got out of here.</p>

<p>First, I called up the System window I hadn't been able to read earlier.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<div class="systembox_wrap">
<p>
<span class="glitch_t2">◆Ma!in! Que!st!!!</span>: Escape from the Nothing.<br/>

You have been placed in danger by the treacherous ■■.

<span class="glitch_t2">Kill! Execute!! Kill! Execute!!!</span> Though executing them immediately would be just, mercy is also a virtue not unworthy of ■■ ■.<br/>

Rather than punishing ■■, you choose to overcome this crisis with your own strength.<br/>

You believe you can escape from the Nothing.<br/>

But the Nothing wishes to remain with you forever.<br/>

It will not let you go easily.
</p>
</div>
</div>

<p>"...?"</p>

<p>I read the whole thing, but the content didn't make sense.</p>

<p>It wants to be with me?</p>

<p>HapFactory's Smiley had tried to lure players in out of loneliness.</p>

<p>But the Nothing was a boss monster formed from dead zombies and the ruins of a city.</p>

<p>It had no ego — it just faithfully carried out its sole purpose of killing players.</p>

<p>Even as I dismissed it as System window nonsense, I felt a little uneasy.</p>

<p>Maybe it was because of the countless eyes watching me. They felt strangely expectant.</p>

<p>But expect what?</p>

<p>Like in any game, the only thing between a boss and an Adaptee in the Trial was predetermined.</p>

<p>Defeating each other.</p>

<p>All I could do now was escape this place by bursting through its guts or tearing them apart.</p>

<p>Unless a special quest was given, there was no peaceful solution.</p>

<p>I had no idea what Je Hyun-oh or the boss monster wanted from me.</p>

<p>Then I felt a sharp sting on my back.</p>

<p>I felt around and found a shallow wound through the long tear in my uniform top.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh must have scratched me slightly with his metal hand when he grabbed me.</p>

<p>The bleeding had mostly stopped, but blood still came off on my palm.</p>

<p>As I frowned at my bloodied hand, I felt eyes on me.</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>I slowly lifted my head.</p>

<p>All the pupils were fixed on the same thing.</p>

<p>My blood-stained palm.</p>

<p>Suddenly Yi Gaon's voice flashed through my mind.</p>
<br/>
<p><span class="flashback">"Roasted Chestnut-nim's blood was the cure for the Pandora virus!"</span></p>
<br/>
<p>Maybe... I could actually do something here.</p>
<hr class="calibre3"/>
<p>The boss monster opened its mouth, Han Goyo fell, and was swallowed — gulp.</p>

<p>It all happened in less than a minute.</p>

<p>Mo Haein stood frozen for a moment, then started running.</p>

<p>She threw away even the bare minimum of decorum toward a superior officer and screamed.</p>

<p>"JE HYUN-OH!!!"</p>

<p>The scream tasted like blood in her throat.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh had been excessively aggressive toward Han Goyo from the very first moment he saw him.</p>

<p>It was strange, but she'd understood it. TRA was an exception — he was fundamentally hostile to the Trial and Adaptees.</p>

<p>But now he clearly recognized Han Goyo as a team member.</p>

<p>And yet he'd thrown him to the boss monster like a toy.</p>

<p>It wasn't even an instinctive attack.</p>

<p>This was a violation of TRA regulations and a crime warranting arrest.</p>

<p>As she ran toward Je Hyun-oh, a bead rosary flew in from a distance.</p>

<p>The Zelkova wood beads, flying like a long rope, wrapped around Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>"Lieutenant Colonel! You son of a bitch!"</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk grabbed the rosary and roared.</p>

<p>"I told you not to kill him!"</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh, the one who'd caused this whole mess, remained calm. He just floated silently in the air, bound by the rosary.</p>

<p>"I didn't kill him."</p>

<p>"You son of a BITCH! Then what the hell was all that fucking around?!"</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh replied in a tone so calm it bordered on lazy.</p>

<p>"I had to see his eye color too."</p>

<p>Mo Haein felt her blood boil.</p>

<p>As she got closer, she viscerally realized how absurd the situation was.</p>

<p>The boss monster, larger than the Laboratory building, stood perfectly still in its sludgy, filth-like form. The hundreds of eyes that had covered its outer skin were gone somewhere. It just stood there dumbly, not attacking at all after swallowing Han Goyo.</p>

<p>Mo Haein fired a burst at the thing, which loomed like a small mountain, to test it.</p>

<p>But the bullets just buried themselves in its flabby flesh and were absorbed.</p>

<p>'If only I could use the Black Moon Blade...!'</p>

<p>A feeling of helplessness made her vision go red.</p>

<p>Without the Black Moon Blade, Je Hyun-oh was the only one who could cut through that massive monster in one blow.</p>

<p>There might be other ways, but who knew what would happen to Han Goyo trapped inside in the meantime.</p>

<p>Mo Haein aimed her gun directly at his gleaming goggles.</p>

<p>"Right now! Get the Trainee out RIGHT NOW!"</p>

<p>Kiiik — a metallic grinding sound.</p>

<p>It was the sound of Kwak Hanmuk tightening the rosary around Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>"Lieutenant Colonel. Get Goyo out."</p>

<p>Pajik — a brief flash of blue light sparked from the rosary.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh glanced at the rosary for a moment, then slowly called out each name.</p>

<p>"Captain Mo, Captain Kwak."</p>

<p>Then he said something strange.</p>

<p>"Can't you hold off on the mutiny for a bit? I feel like something interesting's about to happen."</p>

<p>What the hell was he talking about now. This man was impossible to reason with.</p>

<p>Just as she was about to pull the trigger —</p>

<p>"...!"</p>

<p>Mo Haein flinched and swung her gun around.</p>

<p>Squelch.</p>

<p>Something flabby fell from the boss monster.</p>

<p>What fell was a zombie corpse that had been absorbed.</p>

<p>That was just the beginning.</p>

<p>Squelch. Squelch. Squelch. The boss monster's flesh kept collapsing.</p>

<p>It was spitting back out everything it had indiscriminately absorbed.</p>

<p>Zombie corpses, rubble from collapsed buildings, city garbage...</p>

<p>As the components that made up its body crumbled, its massive size gradually shrank.</p>

<p>Mo Haein and Kwak Hanmuk could only stare blankly at the sight of the boss monster disintegrating alive.</p>

<p>It was when more than half had crumbled away.</p>

<p>A bright golden light fell over the darkening surroundings.</p>

<p>The source of the light, walking out of the dissolving boss monster, was Han Goyo.</p>

<p>His eye color had changed again.</p>

<p>Mo Haein, who'd been unconsciously spellbound by those golden eyes, suddenly widened her own.</p>

<p>Because she belatedly noticed what the golden light had been hiding.</p>

<p>Han Goyo's entire body was drenched in blood.</p>

<p>Beneath his pale face, several large, knife-like gashes pulsed open, spewing blood.</p>

<p>The blood was absorbed by the boss monster before it could pool at his feet, and wherever the blood touched, the flesh dissolved as if melting away.</p>

<p>As Mo Haein and Kwak Hanmuk stood frozen like statues, Je Hyun-oh's laughter rang out.</p>

<p>He'd taken off his goggles at some point, and he laughed as he shouted.</p>

<p>"It really is the exact same gold...!"</p>

<p>Ding — a cheerful notification sound rang out as a System window appeared.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>You have won the battle against the Nothing!</p></div>

<p>It was a victory notification announcing that Han Goyo had defeated DeZonDeal's final boss.</p>
