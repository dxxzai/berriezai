<h1>Chapter 72</h1>

<p>The black goggles displayed no emoticons.</p>

<p>They simply reflected my face across their smooth surface.</p>

<p>The face staring back at me was stiff.</p>

<p>Silence hung between us. Neither Je Hyun-oh nor I spoke.</p>

<p>As the stillness pressed down on us, a cheerful notification chimed.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>The sun is setting! The heat of the sun has vanished, and night has fallen over the dungeon.<br/>
What adventures await in the nighttime dungeon?</p></div></div>

<p>It was the notification announcing the end of the daytime dungeon. I could feel the scorching heat lingering throughout the dungeon begin to fade.</p>

<p>I slowly parted my lips.</p>

<p>“Lieutenant Colonel Je.”</p>

<p>There was no reply. Gorgos was dead, so he should have awakened from the illusion...</p>

<p>Just as I was about to call him again—</p>

<p>I instinctively threw myself backward.</p>

<p>Slash.</p>

<p>The spot where I had been standing was had a mark carved deeply into the floor.</p>

<p>The path left behind by the metal hand looked like the claw marks of a wild beast.</p>

<p>I swallowed hard.</p>

<p>I remembered what Kwak Hanmuk had said back in DeZonDeal.</p>

<p>When emoticons appeared on the goggles, it meant Je Hyun-oh was in his right mind.</p>

<p>I stared at the goggles, completely devoid of any lights, before calling him once more.</p>

<p>“Lieutenant Colonel Je, are you oka—ugh!”</p>

<p>He swung his hand again.</p>

<p>I dodged the path of the metal arm, but it didn't matter.</p>

<p>Several black crescent blades rained toward me, finally drawing blood.</p>

<p>I had barely escaped the thorn vines earlier, only to end up wounded again.</p>

<p>“Roasted Chestnut.”</p>

<p>Dominic called out to me.</p>

<p>I hurriedly whispered back.</p>

<p>“No, it's alright. I'll handle this.”</p>

<p>I absolutely couldn't ask for his help.</p>

<p>Dominic had already put on enough of a spectacular magic show.</p>

<p>Even if I only asked him to subdue Je Hyun-oh gently, he might use another spell like before and casually call it a "mistake."</p>

<p>If Je Hyun-oh took a hit from that beam, even he wouldn't come away unscathed.</p>

<p>No matter what, I had to deal with him myself.</p>

<p>The problem was that I was a Chef.</p>

<p>Naturally, I had no attack skills. I didn't even have a proper weapon.</p>

<p>I'd filled my inventory with cooking ingredients, so I hadn't brought a single recovery item. There was no way to restore my health.</p>

<p>Still, there was one possibility.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh wasn't actively trying to kill me.</p>

<p>Right now, his attacks felt more like attempts to drive me away.</p>

<p>If he'd truly intended to kill me, those black crescents from earlier would've pierced my vital points already.</p>

<p>'A scrape across the forearm is nothing.'</p>

<p>Against Je Hyun-oh, that wasn't even enough to qualify as a warning.</p>

<p>If anything, his attacks were far weaker than usual, which only made me more worried.</p>

<p>He really didn't seem to be in good condition.</p>

<p>I watched him with tense eyes.</p>

<p>Surprisingly, after launching those crescents, he made no further attacks.</p>

<p>He simply stood there, and for a brief moment, I wondered if maybe I could talk him down.</p>


<p>Clink.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh suddenly spread his wings.</p>

<p>The six metallic wings unfolded with a grinding sound, casting ominous shadows across the room.</p>

<p>If he fired every one of those metal feathers at me, I'd die on the spot and end up served on Infernum's dining table.</p>

<p>Yet, a strange certainty filled me.</p>

<p>I knew he wouldn't attack me.</p>

<p>But instead of bringing relief, that certainty made me even more uneasy.</p>

<p>Screeeech.</p>

<p>Metal scraped against metal, and at the same moment, Je Hyun-oh vanished.</p>

<p>He launched himself forward so quickly I could barely follow him with my eyes.</p>

<p>His destination wasn't me, but was the dungeon wall.</p>

<p>BOOM!</p>

<p>The wall exploded inward.</p>

<p>Because the space was maintained by magic, however, it immediately began restoring itself instead of collapsing.</p>

<p>Before the wall had even fully repaired, Je Hyun-oh slammed his head into it again.</p>

<p>Bang! Bang! Bang! Bang!</p>

<p>The repeated impacts echoed endlessly.</p>

<p>Dominic asked in confusion.</p>

<p>“Is this also an ability Berserkers use?”</p>

<p>Only then did I finally understand what was happening.</p>

<p>“Lieutenant Colonel Je!!”</p>

<p>His body was completely surrounded by razor-sharp metal wings.</p>

<p>I couldn't even get close enough to touch him.</p>

<p>A curse nearly escaped my lips.</p>

<p>I'd been so focused on avoiding him attacking me, I'd never imagined I'd end up trying to stop him from smashing his own head into a wall. I searched through my items, and there was... one thing.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Hellfire Oven Mitts: Thick cooking gloves reinforced with the hide of a Lava Crocodile. Suitable for cooking under extreme temperatures.
(Defense +70, Cooking Success Rate +5%, Fire Immunity)</p></div></div>

<p>It was a Chef-exclusive item dropped at low rates by the Floor 29 boss, the Firestarter Goblin.</p>

<p>I'd kept it as cooking equipment, but its defense stat was surprisingly good.</p>

<p>More importantly, the gloves were thick enough to block the metal feathers—at least for a little while.</p>

<p>I hurriedly equipped them and rushed toward Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>I shoved the metal wings aside with the mitts.</p>

<p>I tried to avoid touching the blades directly, but perfect defense was impossible.</p>

<p>The gloves were quickly shredded, and the blades cut into my hands.</p>

<p>The cold sensation against my palms made me flinch, but I forced myself forward.</p>

<p>I managed to grab Je Hyun-oh's forearm. Practically collapsing, I pulled him away from the wall.</p>

<p>“Lieutenant Colonel Je, please get a hold of yourself...!”</p>

<p>To my surprise, he came away from the wall as easily as I pulled.</p>

<p>He staggered back two steps before looking down at me.</p>

<p>“......”</p>

<p>My own face reflected in the smooth black goggles.</p>

<p>Then he slowly turned his head and raised his metal hand.</p>

<p>A sickening sound rang out.</p>

<p>Crunch.</p>

<p>My eyes widened.</p>

<p>The sharp scent of blood filled the air, and metal feathers clattered loudly onto the stone floor.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh was tearing off his own wings.</p>

<p>One entire wing crashed to the ground.</p>

<p>I stood frozen, staring.</p>

<p>Then, when he reached for the second wing, I shouted.</p>

<p>“Je Hyun-oh!!”</p>

<p>I immediately activated my ability.</p>

<p>'Prohibition.'</p>

<p>The metal hand gripping the wing froze.</p>

<p>With the few seconds I'd bought, I hurriedly bent down to pick up the fallen wing.</p>

<p>But there was no need.</p>

<p>The severed wing that had fallen before my eyes began moving by itself.</p>

<p>Click... clatter...</p>

<p>It broke apart into dozens of metal feathers, and covered in blood, the feathers drifted back toward their owner.</p>

<p>They attached themselves to the mangled flesh, reforming the wing.</p>

<p>As though it had never been torn away.</p>

<p>The only proof it had ever happened was the blood staining the metal feathers and the crimson liquid dripping between my own fingers.</p>

<p>The Prohibition ended.</p>

<p>The instant it wore off, Je Hyun-oh grabbed another wing and tore at it again.</p>

<p>When I cast Prohibition once more, his hand froze, but only for a brief moment.</p>

<p>The moment the restraint ended, he resumed ripping his wings apart.</p>

<p>However, no matter how many times he tore the feathers, it was no use.</p>


<p>As if mocking his resistance, each severed wing restored itself perfectly.</p>


<p>Knowing it was meaningless, he continued tearing them off anyway.</p>

<p>No matter how many times I cast Prohibition, the moment it ended he resumed mutilating himself, causing blood to pool around his feet.</p>

<p>At this rate, it would never end.</p>

<p>I clenched my teeth.</p>

<p>'Fine.'</p>

<p>I glared at Je Hyun-oh as I activated another ability alongside Prohibition.</p>

<p>'Prohibition. Divine Protection.'</p>

<p>Divine Protection wasn't an ability meant for situations like this.</p>

<p>It protected against outside attacks, it wasn't designed to stop self-harm.</p>

<p>But I had to try something, anything.</p>

<p>I just wanted him to stop.</p>

<p>Perhaps because I'd activated abilities in such rapid succession—</p>

<p>My eyes burned.</p>

<p>The reflection in his goggles showed golden light flickering within my own eyes.</p>

<p>Those unfamiliar... yet strangely familiar eyes.</p>

<p>I knew I shouldn't keep using my abilities, yet I had no intention of stopping. </p>

<em><p>I couldn't bear to watch one of my own people hurt himself.</p></em>

<em><p>That was something I absolutely couldn't allow.</p></em>

<p>Golden veils descended over Je Hyun-oh's head as Prohibition took hold.</p>

<p>The instant the veil settled, Prohibition disappeared.</p>

<p>I prepared to cast Prohibition again immediately, but, Je Hyun-oh didn't move.</p>

<p>He simply stood quietly as if still under my control, and slowly lifted his head.</p>


<p>The dungeon ceiling was solid stone.</p>

<p>Not a glimpse of the sky could be seen, yet he gazed upward, like someone searching for stars.</p>

<p>Only then did I realize he wasn't looking at the ceiling, but at Divine Protection's golden veil.</p>

<p>Bathed in gold, Je Hyun-oh remained perfectly still.</p>

<p>He didn't move until the veil slowly faded away.</p>

<p>Its golden brilliance dimmed and the veil crumbled into countless fragments.</p>

<p>Particles of light drifted gently downward.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh slowly raised his metal hand, trying to grasp those falling fragments. But the hand capable of cutting through anything couldn't hold onto even a single piece of light.</p>

<p>His arm dropped limply.</p>

<p>I watched him with anxious eyes.</p>

<p>“Excellent use of your ability, Roasted Chestnut. Thanks to you, that Berserker seems to have calmed down now. Although... keeping him as one of your subordinates will require... hmm... a bit of education.”</p>

<p>I could hear Dominic praising me, but his words barely registered.</p>

<p>I couldn't take my eyes off Je Hyun-oh, terrified he'd start smashing his head against the wall again or tearing off his wings.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh turned toward me.</p>

<p>Click.</p>

<p>He removed his goggles and pushed them up onto his head.</p>

<p>His eyes, with a distinct contrast between the color of his iris and pupil, were calm.</p>

<p>It was impossible to believe this was the same man who'd been acting insane only moments ago.</p>

<p>He reached a hand toward me.</p>

<p>The blade at the tip of his metal fingers pointed directly at my eye.</p>

<p>It stopped just in front of my face.</p>

<p>Honestly, it was a little frightening. I thought he might stab straight through my eye, but I didn't move.</p>

<p>After a long moment, he withdrew his hand.</p>

<p>“Are you back to normal now?”</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh silently looked down at me.</p>

<p>“Goyo.”</p>

<p>My lips parted.</p>

<p>“Yes, Lieutenant Colonel Je.”</p>

<p>His voice, ruined by &lt;Zero&gt; , scraped harshly against my ears.</p>

<p>“You really wanna die, huh.”</p>

<p>“Uh... I really want to live, actually...”</p>

<p>“Then why didn't you run?”</p>

<p>After I'd practically burst my eyes trying to use my abilities to save him that was the first thing he said.</p>

<p>Part of me wanted to get angry, but another part couldn't help laughing.</p>

<p>Because, I could tell he was really back.</p>

<p>“Because without you, Lieutenant Colonel Je, I don't have enough inventory space.”</p>

<p>I looked at him.</p>

<p>“So let's leave together.”</p>
