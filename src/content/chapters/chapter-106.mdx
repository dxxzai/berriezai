<h1>Chapter 106</h1>


<p>I stared at the vine plant with a look of bewilderment.</p>

<p>I couldn't fathom why it was acting this way all of a sudden.</p>

<p>It was moving as if to block me from striking it any further.</p>

<p>Nothing like this had ever happened in the game.</p>

<p>Usually, when accepting the rookie prison guard quest, the creatures' suspicion levels were already high.</p>

<p>I wondered if some anomaly had occurred when the levels spiked erratically earlier.</p>

<p>I was curious about Creature No. 1's current level.</p>

<p>However, checking its level required a face-to-face encounter; I couldn't do it by interacting with just a part of its body.</p>

<p>Escaping the training area was the priority.</p>

<p>To continue the strikes, I used my baton to gently push away the vine that had wrapped around my right hand.</p>

<p>But the vine stubbornly kept coiling around me.</p>

<p>Text appeared on the monitor.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_monitor"><p>Strike the creature model. (Current count: 6/10)</p></div>

<p>If I didn't complete the required number of strikes quickly, it would be marked as a failure.</p>

<p>After a moment of hesitation, I swung the baton again. I figured it would back off on its own after taking a single hit.</p>

<p>Thwack—a dull sound of impact rang out. The vine stiffened for a moment, then shuddered violently.</p>

<p>I waited for the stem to release my right hand.</p>

<p>But for some reason, it wrapped around me even tighter.</p>

<p>It even climbed up to my wrist, forming a thick, protective layer.</p>

<p>“…….”</p>

<p>It wasn't as if we were splitting the hits between us—what was this?</p>

<p>I couldn't understand why it was insisting on taking the blows.</p>

<p>'And I even have a way to heal my body afterward.'</p>

<p>But with no way to convey my thoughts, I had no choice but to complete the remaining strikes by hitting the vine plant.</p>

<p>As I landed the final tenth hit, the sound of applause rang out from the screen. I finished striking the creature model.</p>

<p>Frowning slightly, I wiped my damp forehead with the back of my hand and let out a sigh.</p>

<p>As the strikes concluded, the figures on the system window rose.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Becoming a new prison guard... 13%</p></div>

<p>I sheathed my baton and examined the vine plant.</p>

<p>After the final blow, the vine plant hung limp.</p>

<p>I had struck it just hard enough to register a hit, but it must have still been painful.</p>

<p>I stroked the vine plant gently.</p>

<p>At that, the stem—lifeless just moments ago—began to wriggle and climb up my forearm.</p>

<p>I gathered the vine plant into my arms.</p>

<p>It was a bit of a handful, but I managed to cradle it with one arm.</p>

<p>I intended to return the vine plant to Creature No. 1.</p>

<p>Creature No. 1 was capable of detaching and reattaching parts of its body.</p>

<p>If I brought it back, it would reconnect itself to the main body.</p>

<p>Fortunately, it wasn't particularly heavy.</p>

<p>Seeming to like the feel of my embrace, the vine plant shifted and settled into a position that was more comfortable for itself.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_monitor"><p>Let’s move to the next room and meet Neo.</p></div>

<p>The wall split open, revealing a doorway.</p>

<p>Before moving on, I glanced at Dominic. He had been unusually quiet, which made me uneasy.</p>

<p>"He really ought to have said something by now."</p>

<p>I had braced myself for a scolding the moment I struck my right hand with the baton.</p>

<p>I knew full well that Dominic didn't approve of such methods.</p>

<p>I had even imagined what he might say.</p>

<p>Are you risking injury to your hand over such a trivial creature? It’s not easy to understand the nature of Roasted Chestnut, and so on.</p>

<p>Yet, strangely, Dominic remained silent.</p>

<p>This was surprising, given that the situation would normally have elicited a sound of dissatisfaction—like a low "Hmm..."—by now.</p>

<p>I glanced at Dominic.</p>

<p>That was when I realized something was amiss.</p>

<p>Dominic was staring intently at the vine-like plant.</p>

<p>There was no squishing of the body, no twitching of ears, and no scattering of sparkles.</p>

<p>He was simply frozen in place, staring fixedly.</p>

<p>At that moment, I felt a sense of déjà vu.</p>

<p>The Dominic standing there now gave off the same vibe as Samra did when he stopped pretending to be human.</p>

<p>It was like casting aside a flimsy mask to reveal one's true essence...</p>

<p>A faint shiver ran up my spine as the realization crystallized.</p>

<p>Squish. The slime’s body shifted.</p>

<p>"Let’s move to the next room. The door is about to close."</p>

<p>At Dominic’s calm words, I quickly checked the door.</p>

<p>Failure to move within a set time after a transfer order was issued counted as insubordination.</p>

<p>One would be confined in the room until their mind was sufficiently "reconditioned," so it was crucial not to be late.</p>

<p>As I moved to the next room, I kept glancing back at Dominic.</p>

<p>The slime had gone back to its squishy state, so it seemed fine for the moment...</p>

<p>Still harboring a nagging sense of unease, I entered the next room, and sure enough, a monitor descended with a low, mechanical hum.</p>

<p>A glass vial filled with neon-green liquid appeared on the screen.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_monitor"><p>This is a fuel harvested from creatures.<br/>
It is called Neo.</p></div>

<p>The vial slowly rotated on the screen. After the container rotated enough to clearly reveal the neon-green liquid inside, words appeared.</p>
<div class="bigbox2_monitor">
<p>
Neo is a resource of limitless potential—a foundation provided by God for a new era of humanity.
</p>
</div>

<p>Illustrations depicting the uses of Neo appeared on the screen.</p>

<p>From curing diseases and enhancing the human body to even serving as fuel for spaceships.</p>

<p>It vividly demonstrated just how happy humanity had become thanks to this miraculous, magical substance.</p>

<p>Scenes of a child who couldn't walk rising from a wheelchair to embrace their parents, and of scientists—once in despair—advancing toward a new world using Neo, were truly stirring.</p>

<p>Had I not known the true nature of "Neo," I might have been moved as well.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_monitor"><p>Currently, there is only one way to produce Neo.<br/>
That is to enlist the cooperation of Creatures.<br/>
Humanity and Creatures have built a positive relationship based on mutual cooperation.</p></div>

<p>Illustrations showing humans and Creatures coexisting peacefully flooded the screen, layering over one another.</p>

<p>Then, suddenly, the image shattered.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_monitor"><p>However, there is no need to treat even the most heinous criminals as sentient beings.</p></div>

<p>The screen now displayed illustrations of Creatures committing crimes.</p>

<p>Horrific scenes of murder, arson, and looting followed one after another.</p>

<p>In these illustrations, the Creatures were depicted as grotesque monsters, while the humans—drawn with cute features—were portrayed as purely innocent victims.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_monitor"><p>They must pay the price for their sins.<br/>
Extracting Neo from criminal Creatures is akin to recycling waste.</p></div>

<p>I briefly glanced at the vine-like plant I was holding in my arms. It was a relief that the first prisoner was a plant-type creature.</p>

<p>Since it lacked vision—even if it could sense touch—it didn't have to witness that utter nonsense.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_monitor"><p>Neo is an essential fuel for us prison guards.<br/>
To deal with heinous criminals, guards have been equipped with advanced physical bodies.<br/>
Neo is required to maintain these augmented bodies.</p></div>

<p>This time, a pillar rose from the floor.</p>

<p>Resting atop the pillar was a glass vial containing the Neo I had just seen on the screen.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_monitor"><p>Neo has been prepared for the new guard.<br/>
Consume the Neo immediately.</p></div>

<p>Ugh...</p>

<p>Knowing how Neo was harvested, I felt a strong sense of revulsion.</p>

<p>But there was no way around it.</p>

<p>I had no choice but to drink it.</p>

<p>I popped the aluminum cap off the vial.</p>

<p>My right hand wasn't in great shape, so my grip slipped a bit, but I managed to open it somehow.</p>

<p>After taking a deep breath, I downed the Neo.</p>

<p>Gulp. I could clearly feel the liquid sliding down my esophagus.</p>

<p>Slightly viscous, it didn't go down smoothly; instead, it felt thick and syrupy.</p>

<p>I set the empty vial down.</p>

<em><p>It left a bad taste in my mouth...</p></em>

<p>I felt myself calming down a bit.</p>

<p>As if on cue, the sound of loud applause erupted from the screen.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_monitor"><p>Neo consumption complete.</p></div>

<p>I shifted only my eyes to check the system window.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Becoming a new prison guard... 53%</p></div>

<p>Drinking the Neo caused the progress rate to jump by 40% instantly.</p>

<p>My hand, which had been mangled by the baton, healed in the blink of an eye.</p>

<p>My right hand was back to normal.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_monitor"><p>The first ‘Basic Knowledge’ training session has concluded. <br/>Let’s move to the next room and begin the second training session.</p></div>

<p>As I started to walk, I glanced at the load resting on one of my arms.</p>

<p>The weight pressing against me felt cumbersome and uncomfortable.</p>

<p>A sudden surge of irritation rose within me, accompanied by an impulse to hurl the bundle onto the floor.</p>

<p>This is exactly why creatures are a nuisance.</p>

<p>“…Ah.”</p>

<p>I blinked rapidly.</p>

<p>I was inwardly appalled by the alien nature of the thought I’d just had.</p>

<p>Now that my "prison guard progression rate" had surpassed 50%, my mindset was undeniably changing for the worse.</p>

<p>Even in the game, I had found the process of transforming into a prison guard bizarre, but experiencing it in reality was <em>far more unpleasant.</em></p>

<em><p>I detested the sensation of something trying to alter who I was.</p></em>

<p>“…….”</p>

<p>My head felt feverish, as if my temperature were rising.</p>

<p>I realized I needed to be careful not to let the prison guard mindset consume me.</p>

<p>I would try my best to avoid raising the progression rate any further, but even so, by the time the training course concluded, it would likely reach 98%.</p>

<p>My goal was to meet Kwak Hanmuk before it hit 100%.</p>

<p>‘After all, I sent a message right before the Chief of Security dragged me away.’</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk was probably on his way to find me right now.</p>

<p>Clutching the mass of vines to my arm, I took a slow, deep breath and moved into the next room.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_monitor"><p>The second training session is ‘practical training.’</p></div>

<p>Unlike the previous rooms, this one was quite large.</p>

<p>A massive monitor was mounted on one wall, and an arena—the kind you’d see in a fighting ring—was set up in the center.</p>

<p>The ring, encircled by heavy chains instead of ropes, had a bleak, grim atmosphere.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_monitor"><p>In the first training session, the trainee known as ‘Little Lamb’ demonstrated excellent results. <br/>Now, let’s learn in greater detail how to reprogram the minds of these creatures.</p></div>

<div class="bigbox2_monitor"><p>Here, you will face virtual holographic versions of all the creatures currently held in the prison.<br/>
First, we will engage each creature, and then we will study their specific weaknesses and the methods to subdue them.</p></div>

<div class="bigbox2_monitor"><p>Trainee Little Lamb, please step into the ring when you are ready.</p></div>

<p>I set the vine plant down on the floor.</p>

<p>The plant, having left my arms, caused its stems to writhe unsteadily.</p>

<p>However, I couldn't exactly fight while cradling a plant.</p>

<p>“Wait here.”</p>

<p>Even though they couldn't hear me, I tossed out a quick remark while loosening up and checking my condition.</p>

<p>Neo not only restores the body but also enhances it.</p>

<p>It was primarily due to Neo’s influence that the prison guards were able to contend with the creatures.</p>

<p>I couldn't yet exert as much power as the guards.</p>

<p>However, even this level of strength was enough to handle mere hologram creatures without difficulty.</p>

<p>‘It’s not like I’m actually getting hurt, anyway.’</p>

<p>Normally, rookie guards face hologram creatures without any prior information, learning frustration and rage in the process.</p>

<p>They master suppression techniques only after suffering crushing defeats; the negative emotions felt earlier make them accept and execute even brutal methods as a matter of course.</p>

<p>I already understood all the creature suppression techniques.</p>

<p>There was no need to drag out the training by fighting them repeatedly.</p>

<p>I intended to subdue every creature on the first attempt and finish the practical training in record time.</p>

<p>However, I felt a rush of heat and a slight dizziness.</p>

<p>Was it because of the Neo I had consumed?</p>

<p>A strange, persistent sense of discomfort was also nagging at me. I wanted to get the training over with as quickly as possible.</p>

<p>I stepped onto the ring.</p>

<p>At that moment, hologram creatures appeared one after another in the arena.</p>

<p>Amidst the roar of the crowd, text flashed onto the screen.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_monitor"><p>Creatures to be faced by the trainee</p></div>

<p>The screen paused briefly before displaying more text.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_monitor"><p>Introducing Creature Inmates No. 1 through <span class="glitch_t2">No. 13.</span></p></div>

<p>I scanned the thirteen creatures with an expressionless gaze.</p>

<p>My eyes stopped on the hologram of Creature Inmate No. 13.</p>

<p>My lips, previously set in a straight line, parted to let out a dry, incredulous laugh.</p>

<p>“Hah…” The sense of unpleasantness that had been stirring within me all this time suddenly erupted.</p>

<p>My eyes burned with heat...</p>

<em><p>Who dares to treat my subordinate like this?</p></em>
