<h1>Chapter 9</h1>

<p>BEEP— BEEP— BEEP—.</p><p></p>

<p>An error alarm rang out in repeated bursts.</p>

<p>Researchers, fully kitted in protective suits and gear, reported in tense voices.</p>

<p>"Third attempt failed. Confirmed as an error — even forced measurement is impossible."</p>

<p>On a monitor to one side, a waveform none of them had ever seen was being drawn. Fear spread through the researchers.</p>

<p>Ham Jiwol, the Analysis Agency Chief who'd been checking the readings, quickly looked at Kwak Hanmuk.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk waved his hand to signal that he was maintaining the Bind properly.</p>

<p>Ham Jiwol cautiously approached 'it' and replaced the attached patch.</p>

<p>As if handling radioactive material.</p>

<p>'Honestly, radioactive material would've been easier.'</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk thought that as he stared through the window.</p>

<p>A murderous atmosphere churned, but the subject at its center remained calm.</p>

<p>The man, bound tightly in the bead rosary and sleeping peacefully, seemed a step removed from the chaos surrounding him.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk rolled the beads of his rosary on his wrist out of habit.</p>

<p>His rosary was a defensive-type item.</p>

<p>Made of zelkova wood and imbued with the energy of lightning, the rosary could extend freely and even detach within a certain range.</p>

<p>It was most suited for restraining and subduing a target, but Kwak Hanmuk had used it offensively through all sorts of unorthodox methods.</p>

<p>He rarely used the binding and subduing that were the zelkova wood rosary's fundamental purpose.</p>

<p>The reason was simple. It wasn't fun.</p>

<p>But what about earlier?</p>
<br/>
<p><span class="flashback">"Hanmuk. Bind."</span></p>
<br/>
<p>The moment Mo Haein's words fell, he fired off the Bind without a second thought.</p>

<p>It was a movement commanded by instinct. The feeling of that moment — moving on pure intuition without passing through reason — was still vivid.</p>

<p>"Haein. What the hell did you pick up?"</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk shook his head as he spoke, and Mo Haein immediately shot back.</p>

<p>"I didn't pick him up. He appeared in front of me."</p>

<p>Mo Haein, who had filed reports to her superiors without a break, looked haggard. But her eyes were sharp.</p>

<p>The only thing sustaining her now was the euphoria of having permanently closed a Trial with a True Ending.</p>

<p>"Is he human?"</p>

<p>"…I don't know."</p>

<p>In the moment Park Seonggyeon's head burst into golden fragments, Mo Haein had witnessed it clearly.</p>

<p>The change that occurred in 'its' pupils.</p>

<p>It was something that couldn't happen if it were human.</p>

<p>Currently, all personnel at the TRA were scrambling to investigate the being called 'Han Goyo.'</p>

<p>Samra would start their investigation soon, so that thing could be analyzed too.</p>

<p>Whether it was an infected entity contaminated by the Trial's virus, or a non-human being.</p>

<p>In truth, Mo Haein didn't care what 'Han Goyo' was.</p>

<p>Whether he could be used, or not.</p>

<p>If it helped close a Trial with a True Ending, Mo Haein was ready to accept whatever that thing was.</p>
<br/>
<p><span class="flashback">"You worked hard, Captain."</span></p>
<br/>
<p>The image of him greeting me suddenly came to mind.</p>

<p>His slightly smiling face had been that of an ordinary college student.</p>

<p>But Mo Haein pushed the memory out of her head.</p>

<p>That moment's emotion had been genuine, but for now, it would only hinder her judgment.</p>

<p>As Mo Haein stared at the sleeping Han Goyo without blinking, Kwak Hanmuk, who had only been rolling his rosary beads, suddenly spoke.</p>

<p>"You said the Trial's NPC showed a friendly attitude toward Han Goyo."</p>

<p>"Yeah."</p>

<p>"Did you ever try attacking Han Goyo with the Black Moon Blade?"</p>

<p>Mo Haein frowned at the out-of-nowhere nonsense, but Kwak Hanmuk was serious.</p>

<p>"No, I know it sounds fucking insane, but… I keep feeling it. My rosary."</p>

<p>Even he seemed to find it absurd as he let out a hollow laugh.</p>

<p>"It wants me to let him go."</p>

<p>Mo Haein's lips parted.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk pressed down on the rosary with his hand and looked at Han Goyo.</p>

<p>Since earlier, his rosary had been making his skin tingle and expressing dissatisfaction.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk voiced the impossible hypothesis pressing down on his mind.</p>

<p>"Not just the Trial's NPC…. Maybe even the items are friendly toward Han Goyo."</p>

<hr class="calibre3"/>

<p>"Ngh…."</p>

<p>I swallowed a groan and opened my eyes.</p>

<p>A white ceiling. Looked like a hospital room.</p>

<p>…I wanted to wake up thinking that, but the scene before me was nothing like it.</p>

<p>Where I opened my eyes was an interrogation room. And I was strapped to a chair in a restraint suit.</p>

<p>'I'm in serious trouble.'</p>

<p>The TRA was under the Agency of National Defense, so trials were held in a military court — but they were courts in name only.</p>

<p>Because of its special nature, it operated its own separate military court and the trial process was independent.</p>

<p>In short, if someone said, "This bastard is bad!" the TRA's military judge would just slam down a detention or punishment on the spot.</p>

<p>At least the silver lining was that my injuries had been fully treated.</p>

<p>Confirming that the pain in my thigh had lessened considerably, I suddenly grew curious.</p>

<p>'Come to think of it, why didn't Kwak Hanmuk get registered?'</p>

<p>A Captain like him was an important character with excellent abilities, so he seemed worth registering, but the System window saying "registrable character" hadn't appeared.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<p>
You believe you must proceed with character registration carefully.

Because you currently have only '3 slots' available for registration.
</p>
</div>

<div class="systembox"><p>You judge that it is still a bit early to fill a slot with 'Kwak Hanmuk.' He might escape your ■■.</p></div>

<div class="systembox">
<p>
You want to increase your abilities as a System as soon as possible.<br />

You think it would be good to try registering 'Mo Haein.'
</p>
</div>

<p>A rigid answer came back to a light question. Roughly satisfied with my curiosity, I ignored the System window out of habit.</p>

<p>Just then, the closed door opened and a man in a TRA uniform walked in.</p>

<p>"Hello, Han Goyo-ssi."</p>

<p>The man, greeting me with a friendly smile, introduced himself.</p>

<p>"I am ■■■ from the Trial Response Agency."</p>

<p>I heard his name, but it was a character I didn't know at all.</p>

<p>But what was the name again?</p>

<p>Even though I tried to recall it, the name I'd just heard wouldn't come to mind.</p>

<p>The man looked at me, tied up like a cocoon, with a sympathetic expression.</p>

<p>"Very uncomfortable, aren't you? If you cooperate well, I'll have you untied right away."</p>

<p>I was full of willingness to cooperate. That was only natural, since I had nowhere to go.</p>

<p>'My meal ticket.'</p>

<p>If I got out of here, I'd be homeless. I tried my best to look as innocent as possible as I answered.</p>

<p>"Yes. Please take care of me."</p>

<p>The man sitting across from me checked his tablet for a moment, then smiled faintly.</p>

<p>"First… what does 'Roasted Chestnut' mean?"</p>

<p>I, who had been ready to answer quickly, ended up asking back with a dumb look on my face.</p>

<p>"Huh?"</p>

<p>"Roasted Chestnut."</p>

<p>"Ah, Roasted Chestnut…."</p>

<p>I was flustered.</p>

<p>'How am I supposed to explain Roasted Chestnut?'</p>

<p>I wondered if this was some kind of interrogation technique too.</p>

<p>Not knowing what to do, I just blinked my eyes before hesitantly opening my mouth.</p>

<p>"It's just Roasted Chestnut. You know, Roasted Chestnut."</p>

<p>The atmosphere turned cold.</p>

<p>To show a more cooperative attitude, I scraped together everything I had and didn't have to explain.</p>

<p>"My name is Han Goyo. You know the carol? 'Silent Night.' At first, I was called 'Gobam.' Then, while eating roasted chestnuts with my friends, 'Gobam' turned into 'Gunbam'<sup id="uoC8iCfSUPL8VpoRTcpy9H5"><a href="#u6dyDlVl3tTOtxVzGnDsRK7">1</a></sup>…."</p>

<p>God, this was pathetic.</p>

<p>The more I explained, the deeper I felt myself digging a hole.</p>

<p>But if I stopped now, it would seem even weirder.</p>

<p>"…My nickname changed. It was a familiar word, so I used the nickname 'Roasted Chestnut' often on the internet and such."</p>

<p>When I played Archive's games, I always set my player name to Roasted Chestnut.</p>

<p>"So, 'Roasted Chestnut' is a nickname you chose yourself?"</p>

<p>"Yes."</p>

<p>"...."</p>

<p>The man was silent.</p>

<p>But I had nothing more to explain. I wanted to stop talking about Roasted Chestnut.</p>

<p>'Let's move on to the next question.'</p>

<p>I was waiting quietly when he spoke.</p>

<p>"Trial names either represent an Adaptee's characteristics or carry some symbolism."</p>

<p>The man stared at me.</p>

<p>It was a gaze trying to detect lies.</p>

<p>"And all those names are determined by the Trial."</p>

<p>I learned that for the first time.</p>

<p>I thought everyone just picked whatever name they wanted, like when starting a game.</p>

<p>I had played Archive's games quite diligently, but the world-building explanations were always a bonus that came after the game's ending.</p>

<p>As a player, there were bound to be things I didn't know.</p>

<p>"There has never been a case, not once, where a self-chosen nickname with no characteristics or meaning, like yours, Han Goyo-ssi, was used in a Trial."</p>

<p>The man pushed his tablet toward me.</p>

<p>"In this &lt;Happy Smile Factory&gt;, there was another Adaptee besides Han Goyo-ssi whose name suffered an anomaly."</p>

<p>A photo of Park Seonggyeon was displayed on the tablet.</p>

<p>"Park Seonggyeon was given a completely different name, not the one he was usually assigned by the Trial."</p>

<p>In HapFactory, Park Seonggyeon's nametag had read 'Sample.'</p>

<p>A System window immediately came to mind.</p>
<br/>
<p><scan class="flashback">['Park Seonggyeon' is a registrable Sample character. Would you like to register?]</scan></p>
<br/>
<p>Sample, and Sample.</p>

<p>A naming so blatant it was practically begging to be noticed.</p>

<p>From the moment Park Seonggyeon entered HapFactory, he had been branded as a sample for me.</p>

<p>It was a decent selection, though.</p>

<p>Because the target was Park Seonggyeon, I had been able to choose Execution without much guilt or hesitation.</p>

<p>But the situation didn't sit well with me.</p>

<p>Because I could feel the will of some entity that wanted me to develop my abilities as a System.</p>

<p>Anyway, the interrogation seemed to be a failure.</p>

<p>'I can't really say my characteristic and symbolism is "Roasted Chestnut"….'</p>

<p>Leaving me feeling complicated, the man continued his questions.</p>

<p>"Han Goyo-ssi knew how to clear &lt;Happy Smile Factory&gt; with a True Ending. Is that correct?"</p>

<p>"It was a coincidence."</p>

<p>"Han Goyo was very skilled in boss battles, and knew a new strategy that even the Trial Response Agency didn't know. He used this to achieve a True Ending."</p>

<p>The man chuckled and finished his statement.</p>

<p>"…That was Captain Mo Haein's report."</p>

<p>"It really was a coincidence. It seemed similar to a game I usually liked, so I tried it and it worked out…."</p>

<p>"Why did you blow Park Seonggyeon's head off?"</p>

<p>The man cut me off and asked. The question already assumed I was the one who did it.</p>

<p>I lied again.</p>

<p>"I don't know anything about that."</p>

<p>The man let out a long sigh.</p>

<p>"Han Goyo-ssi."</p>

<p>"Yes."</p>

<p>"I'm telling you this because you seem unaware, but torture is legal in the Trial Response Agency."</p>

<p>I know that…. I really know that….</p>

<p>But that didn't mean I could just admit it.</p>

<p>To properly confess, I'd have to say the unbelievable story that I came into the game, into the Archive world.</p>

<p>'Can't I register that guy?'</p>

<p>For a brief moment, I entertained the fantasy of registering him and threatening him with Execution.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Beings that are not ■■ cannot yet be registered.</p></div>

<p>The System window popped up abruptly.</p>

<p>To hide that I was giving an awkward look at the square blocking my vision, I lowered my eyes as if scared.</p>

<p>'That damn black box.'</p>

<p>I couldn't figure out why it was showing me something covered up. Then, as if it had been waiting, the System window added more.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Insufficient.</p></div>

<p>As if it couldn't even be bothered to type the six syllables of 'your ability is insufficient,' it sent just a single word. At this rate, it would be sending only the initial consonants next.</p>

<p>I was about to dismiss the System window when I looked at the black box with fresh eyes.</p>

<p>■■....</p>

<p>No way… a human?</p>

<p>The moment the word came to mind, a realization struck me hard.</p>

<p>One of the Trial Response Agency's main characters.</p>

<p>A being created by South Korea to respond to the Trials, using items from the Trials.</p>

<p>Not human. An AI system android.</p>

<p>I called the man's name.</p>

<p>"Samra Mansang.<sup id="samra"><a href="#samrafoot2">2</a></sup>"</p>

<p>At that moment, the man's face, which I had thought was perfectly normal until just now, came into proper view.</p>

<p>The man's face was painted black, as if someone had scribbled over it.</p>

<p>The moment I registered the dissonance, his dark TRA uniform turned white.</p>

<p>The paint covering his face peeled away smoothly, revealing the man's true face.</p>

<p>Hair and irises that shimmered in holographic colors, shifting in real-time with the light.</p>

<p>The being with colors a human could never possess burst into laughter.</p>

<p>The space, which had been a dim interrogation room until a moment ago, transformed.</p>

<p>Massive walls with no end in height sprang up.</p>

<p>Walls made of every kind of monitor panel, smartphone, and tablet connected haphazardly.</p>

<p>Before that wall, where everything in the world played on different screens, was a chair with no backrest on an abnormally high platform.</p>

<p>Samra, seated on the chair, looked down at me.</p>

<p>"How did you know, Han Goyo-ssi?"</p>
