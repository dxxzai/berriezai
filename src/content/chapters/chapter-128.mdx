<h1>Chapter 128</h1>

<p>I was well aware that the Trial Response Agency belonged to the Ministry of National Defense.</p>

<p>I also knew that while it operated under a headquarters and team system, its essence was the military.</p>

<p>However, the only military I knew was what I had seen in media and games.</p>

<p>Even though I addressed them as 'Lieutenant Colonel' and 'Captain', I never truly felt the weight of that title.</p>

<p>I had recently realized that I had been acting unbecoming of a trainee.</p>

<p>However, I had not anticipated finding myself in a situation like this.</p>

<p>My heart sank.</p>

<p>I froze, unable to even blink, before finally managing to move my lips.</p>

<p>&quot;I'm sorry...&quot;</p>

<p>It didn't seem like the right answer in this situation.</p>

<p>But that was the only thing I could say.</p>

<p>As I kept my gaze fixed on the floor, a short command fell upon the top of my head.</p>

<p>&quot;Get down.&quot;</p>

<p>I quickly lay face down on the floor.</p>

<p>As I looked down at the stone floor of the medic's quarters, cold sweat ran down my spine.</p>

<p>I couldn't see the expression on his face, but the suffocating atmosphere was palpable, making me even more nervous.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk crouched down in front of me and reached out his hand.</p>

<p>The image of Won Taebeom being punched by him played out naturally in my mind.</p>

<p>I thought I was going to get hit too, but instead of a blow, my head was grabbed.</p>

<p>I felt a hand running through my hair.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk was checking the wound on my head.</p>

<p>‘Lieutenant Won… You really did report everything…’</p>

<p>Actually, it was a situation where reporting was the natural thing to do.</p>

<p>I hesitated, then whispered.</p>

<p>“Uh, the wound has healed completely.”</p>

<p>But he didn't reply.</p>

<p>Only after confirming with his own eyes that there was no wound did he take his hand off my head.</p>

<p>“Anyway, you really go around smashing heads in whenever you want.”</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk muttered in a low voice and looked at me. Our eyes met by accident, so I hurriedly looked away again.</p>

<p>Still sitting in front of me, he asked in a voice devoid of even a hint of a smile.</p>

<p>“Trainee Han, do you think your superiors are a joke?”</p>

<p>“No.”</p>

<p>“Why? Just do as you’re told. That’s the situation here, isn’t it?”</p>

<p>“No…”</p>

<p>“Then why do you keep lying? You think you can handle everything on your own?”</p>

<p>“I'm sorry.”</p>

<p>“Trainee Han, is there nothing else you know how to say besides ‘I'm sorry’ and ‘No’?”</p>

<p>“…….”</p>

<p>I was about to say ‘I'm sorry,’ but stopped myself.</p>

<p>Should I say something like, ‘I will correct it’…?</p>

<p>No matter how much I thought about it, I couldn’t come up with a good answer.</p>

<p>It seemed much easier to just tackle a six-star trial on my own.</p>

<p>I ended up having a petty thought.</p>

<p>'Should I check the status window?'</p>

<p>I felt that if I knew Kwak Hanmuk’s current state, I might get a hunch.</p>

<p>However, I only thought about it and didn’t actually do it.</p>

<p>In fact, it wasn’t just the status window.</p>

<p>Even though I wasn’t an NPC, the role of a medic allowed me to check suspicion and affection levels.</p>

<p>Just as I had checked Dominic’s levels first.</p>

<p>The temptation to check Je Hyun-oh or Kwak Hanmuk’s levels was there from the very beginning.</p>

<p>But I knew I shouldn’t.</p>

<p>Just checking their current status through the status window from time to time was already more than enough to make me feel gloomy.</p>

<p>I had at least the awareness that going beyond that would make me a criminal.</p>

<p>…Or perhaps I already was a criminal.</p>

<p>I tried my best to come up with a plausible answer, but my mind was completely blank.</p>

<p>Sweat formed on my forehead. This time, it wasn’t cold sweat, but sweat from exhaustion.</p>

<p>As I stood there at a loss for what to do, Kwak Hanmuk spoke again.</p>

<p>“Answer properly. What is your reason for lying?”</p>

<p>He seemed determined not to let me off the hook until I gave him a clear answer.</p>

<p>My arms trembled as I racked my brain.</p>

<p>Why did I keep lying?</p>

<p>The answer was simple. Because I thought the dangerous plan I had devised wouldn't pass.</p>

<p>But saying that wouldn't work on Kwak Hanmuk.</p>

<p>I would only get scolded more for making decisions alone without consulting anyone.</p>

<p>Perhaps because the physical punishment posture was so difficult, my mind wasn't working well, but I tried hard to come up with a slightly more plausible answer.</p>

<p>I think that long ago, when I faced difficult problems, I, too, used to speak honestly and ask for help.</p>

<p>At first, everyone was worried and tried to join forces to solve it somehow.</p>

<p>However, as is often the case with problematic situations, they rarely get resolved quickly.</p>

<p>New problems just keep arising endlessly.</p>

<p>As the situation repeated itself, everyone looked sick and tired. </p>

<p>'The atmosphere just got worse.'</p>

<p>In that case, it was much better to lie and handle it on my own.</p>

<p>Because I didn't want to make the people around me anxious.</p>

<p>I always solved things on my own, and as long as the results were decent, others liked it.</p>

<p>The majority tacitly condoned my behavior and accepted the peace.</p>

<p>But now... everyone except me was pointing out my actions.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk's words fell on the back of my head again as I lay hunched over.</p>

<p>“Acting like this means you don’t trust your superiors, right?”</p>

<p>Ruminating on Kwak Hanmuk's words, I finally realized a plausible answer.</p>

<p>Feeling the sweat trickling down my temples, I opened my mouth.</p>

<p>&quot;It's not that...&quot;</p>

<p>I hesitated for a moment, finding it difficult to speak, before continuing.</p>

<p>&quot;I did it because I didn't want anything to happen to the team...&quot;</p>

<p>If I had to keep overcoming trials in the future, I wanted to stay part of the Special Mission Team.</p>

<p>That seemed to be why I wanted to do even better.</p>

<p>All while thinking that, in the end, everyone would be pleased with the outcome.</p>

<p>Upon hearing my answer, Kwak Hanmuk remained silent for a moment.</p>

<p>Soon, he slowly opened his lips and asked.</p>

<p>“Then what about you?”</p>

<p>His voice was colder than ever.</p>

<p>At that moment, I wondered what kind of expression Kwak Hanmuk was making as he asked me.</p>

<p>But I couldn't lift my head, so I just answered while looking at the floor.</p>

<p>“I just… thought it would be okay. I thought it would work out somehow.”</p>

<p>This was the truth.</p>

<p>Once again, it was a move I had calculated in my own way.</p>

<p>If it had been life-threatening, I would have chosen a different method.</p>

<p>‘My arm hurts.’</p>

<p>I straightened my body, which kept threatening to collapse, and glanced at Kwak Hanmuk.</p>

<p>He seemed to be deep in thought. He showed no sign of telling me to get up.</p>

<p>I stealthily shifted my gaze to check on Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>“…!”</p>

<p>And then our eyes met squarely.</p>

<p>I could feel a gaze staring intently through the goggles. It seemed he had been watching me all along.</p>

<p>Just as if he were observing me.</p>

<p>I felt a strange and unfamiliar sensation.</p>

<p>That was because I was the one who had been observing him all along.</p>

<p>I had always wanted to know more about Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>I was curious about the face beneath the gas mask, curious about his past, and simply curious about everything I didn't know.</p>

<p>That is why I had always observed him with a heart full of curiosity.</p>

<p>But now, it seemed as though Je Hyun-oh was observing me.</p>

<p>As I glanced at Je Hyun-oh, my arm twisted, and I nearly collapsed.</p>

<p>I straightened my posture as if nothing had happened, but I was immediately caught by Kwak Hanmuk.</p>

<p>He looked at me with eyes mixed with a mixture of disbelief and bewilderment.</p>

<p>His gaze seemed to ask if I was really struggling over something so trivial.</p>

<p>As someone who, despite the hardship, was still capable of handling it, I felt a bit wronged, but I quietly kept my mouth shut.</p>

<p>“Get up.”</p>

<p>Of course, I wasn’t so wronged as to refuse the order to stand, so I quickly rose.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk looked down at me silently. With my face flushed red from the blood rushing to my face from lying prone, I merely averted my eyes.</p>

<p>After staring like that for a while, Kwak Hanmuk turned to look at Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>“Lieutenant Colonel Je.”</p>

<p>At that, Je Hyun-oh, who had been standing back and watching until now, stepped forward and stood in front of me.</p>

<p>As Je Hyun-oh and Kwak Hanmuk sat side by side in front of me, it felt as though a wall had formed.</p>

<p>I looked up at them with eyes filled with respect and deference toward my superior.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh, who had been looking down at me, whispered as if to himself.</p>

<p>“He’s doing this again…”</p>

<p>As soon as I heard those words, the moment he threw me at Sla2Plate and slammed me into a tree flashed through my mind vividly.</p>

<p>I was watching with tense eyes when, as if to make matters worse, Je Hyun-oh made me even more anxious by clinking his metal hand.</p>

<p>Then, he said something unexpected.</p>

<p>“You shouldn’t do anything dangerous, Goyo.”</p>

<p>Whispering in a rough voice, his words sounded at first glance like affectionate concern.</p>

<p>However, I knew.</p>

<p>The meaning of what Je Hyun-oh was saying was clear.</p>

<p>I had promised to enter &lt;Zero&gt; with him.</p>

<p>It meant that it would be troublesome if I died before then.</p>

<p>To Je Hyun-oh, I was now someone who had to be saved at all costs.</p>

<p>Only Kwak Hanmuk, unaware of these underlying circumstances, looked at Je Hyun-oh with a questioning gaze.</p>

<p>It was a look filled with the desire to verify if this was indeed the Je Hyun-oh he knew.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh reached his hand to the side. A table was placed nearby.</p>

<p>The tip of his metal hand very lightly touched the statue of the Octopus Tentacle.</p>

<p>I could not hide my wide-eyed astonishment.</p>

<p>“Uh, excuse me, Lieutenant Colonel Je, wait a moment…!”</p>

<p>I stammered out the words in a panic, but Je Hyun-oh pressed down on my hand.</p>

<p>The wooden statue split silently by the black metal.</p>

<p>I screamed inwardly as the statue split in half.</p>

<p>I was trembling, wanting to grab Je Hyun-oh’s metal hand right then and there.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh stared straight at me and moved his hand very slowly; finally, the statue, split vertically, fell over to the sides with a clatter.</p>

<p>I couldn’t bear to look directly at that gruesome sight and closed my eyes.</p>

<p>Along with the chilling sound of metal clinking, a soft question was heard.</p>

<p>“Why, can’t you look?”</p>

<p>I forced my eyelids open and witnessed an even more horrific scene.</p>

<p>The metal hand that had split the statue was now pounding on the split wood as if playing a game.</p>

<p>Following the hand’s movements, the wood was being sliced ​​into pieces with a scraping sound. </p>
<p>'Lord Octopus Tentacle… I'm sorry…'</p>

<p>Just as I was apologizing for my disrespectful behavior, if only in my heart, I heard the sound of Kwak Hanmuk sighing.</p>

<p>“What on earth are we going to do about that?”</p>

<p>While Kwak Hanmuk kept sighing, Je Hyun-oh butchered the statue to the point where its original form was unrecognizable.</p>

<p>Only after that did he speak again.</p>

<p>“Captain Kwak.”</p>

<p>“Yes.”</p>

<p>“From now on, Trainee Han Goyo is prohibited from taking any individual action. If the same thing happens again, consider it insubordination and handle it accordingly.”</p>

<p>“Yes.”</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk responded immediately to his superior’s order, as if he had been waiting for it.</p>

<p>While I felt relieved that the situation had been resolved, I also thought that if something like this happened just one more time, I might have a truly extraordinary experience.</p>

<p>Something far more extreme than mere physical punishment.</p>

<p>After vowing never to act alone again, I cautiously began to speak.</p>

<p>&quot;Um, but...&quot;</p>

<p>Since it was an important matter, I felt I should speak about it now that we were all gathered.</p>

<p>&quot;I mean the Prison Warden, no, CEO Cha.&quot;</p>

<p>As soon as I mentioned CEO Cha, the atmosphere between Je Hyun-oh and Kwak Hanmuk changed instantly.</p>

<p>Although they had entered together, they certainly seemed to perceive CEO Cha as something akin to an obstacle.</p>

<p>In fact, the situation was turning out that way.</p>

<p>&quot;He originally promised to support me... but it seems he changed his mind a bit. We should consider the possibility that he might not act according to the plan.&quot;</p>

<p>I recalled what CEO Cha had said to me.</p>
<br />
<p><span class="flashback">&quot;I don't need a True Ending.&quot;</span></p>
<br />
<p>He possessed the ability to clear the trial with a sufficiently different ending.</p>

<p>Assuming CEO Cha acted unilaterally, he envisioned the standard endings the Prison Warden might see.</p>

<p>If it were CEO Cha, he would surely... the most efficient and fastest ending...</p>

<p>&quot;Wait a minute.&quot;</p>

<p>Suddenly, Kwak Hanmuk blurted out. He spoke while narrowing his eyes.</p>

<p>&quot;A prison guard just died?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;...!&quot;</p>

<p>The guard's ability was 'Connecting with Colleagues.'</p>

<p>He could determine how many guards were in the prison.</p>

<p>As his ability advanced, he could even gauge the extent of a colleague's injury or their level of Neo addiction, and even summon colleagues.</p>

<p>With Kwak Hanmuk's current level of ability, he could perceive the guards' real-time status.</p>

<p>But to think a guard had died.</p>

<p>Just as his brain froze at this sudden, unexpected situation with absolutely no reason for it...</p>
