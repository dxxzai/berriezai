<h1>Chapter 153</h1>

<p>I never thought the day would come when my photo and article would be published in an interstellar newspaper.</p>

<p>And not just any article, but a huge headline on the front page.</p>

<p>I couldn't believe it, so I read it carefully again, but it wasn't a hallucination.</p>

<p>It was my face, and it was an article about me.</p>

<p>Once I fully accepted it in my mind, only one thought came to mind.</p>

<p>'Is it really this big of a deal?'</p>

<p>The administrator had previously told me that young gods are intriguing beings.</p>

<p>They also warned me to be especially careful of the ancient gods, as I, possessing a domain, was not protected.</p>

<p>However, I never imagined I would be a being receiving enough attention to be featured on the front page of a newspaper across the entire universe.</p>

<p>'But I couldn't read the Day News in CallOVo.'</p>

<p>Everything was blocked off, so I could barely read the general knowledge section, but now I could see everything, including the photos and the article.</p>

<p>It certainly seemed that I had grown.</p>

<p style="font-size:21.6px">—Sa Gongyeon-You've-lost-your-mind-! Using-a-young-god-as-news-material-!</p>

<p>The administrator was furious, their hair sticking out in all directions as they trembled uncontrollably.</p>

<p>—Phew, kid. Sorry for yelling.</p>

<p>They finally calmed down and seemed to regret raising their voice in front of me.</p>

<p>Although I might be young by interstellar standards, as an adult by Earth's standards, I didn't think much of it even when they yelled.</p>

<p>“It’s fine. But is this article putting me in a lot of danger right now?”</p>

<p>—That’s right. Because the existence of the young god has been publicly exposed.</p>

<p>The administrator turned the page and showed me the next one.</p>

<p>It was a section that required a paid subscription to read, and it described my situation in detail from an alien's perspective.</p>

<p>Everything had been revealed, including the fact that the young god, who possessed a domain, was living in a defenseless state.</p>

<p>The only saving grace was that the planet's name or coordinates hadn't been disclosed.</p>

<p>The photos attached to the special feature on the young god were quite diverse.</p>

<p>Close-up shots of my face, full-body shots, close-ups of just my golden eyes, and so on.</p>

<p>It wasn't just PTB; there were even photos of me sitting blankly on the bed right after waking up, or pictures of me haphazardly throwing my uniform on before work.</p>

<p>‘And this is even from this morning.’</p>

<p>There was no paparazzi as crazy as this one.</p>

<p>Furthermore, the content of the article was crafted with exquisite skill.</p>

<p>It seemed to reveal everything, yet strangely omitted details, sparking a curiosity in even me to know more about the young god.</p>

<p>They had set the stage perfectly for all sorts of aliens to take an interest in me.</p>

<p>I read the article carefully and thought about it from various angles.</p>

<p>“Can you take the article down if the person involved has not consented?”</p>

<p>—I think it would be difficult since the kid isn't a protected person...</p>

<p>“Understood.”</p>

<p>After listening to the whole thing, I felt like I understood.</p>

<p>Having reached a conclusion, I told the administrator:</p>

<p>“I will handle this matter.”</p>

<p>—What? </p>

<p>The administrator asked with a serious expression.</p>

<p>&quot;Kid. You aren't trying to contact the butlers, are you?&quot;</p>

<p>I didn't expect the topic of the cat butler and rabbit butler to come up right here.</p>

<p>Perhaps because they were Dominic's subordinates, the butlers seemed to have quite a bit of influence.</p>

<p>&quot;That's not it.&quot;</p>

<p>After politely thanking the anxious administrator for letting me know, I ended the communication.</p>

<p>I let out a long sigh.</p>

<p>I was sitting in an empty conference room.</p>

<p>Training had already started some time ago.</p>

<p>To catch up on the theoretical training that had taken place earlier, I was scheduled to receive a separate crash course today.</p>

<p>So I was waiting alone in the conference room for the training when I suddenly received a call from the administrator.</p>

<p>&quot;Roasted Chestnut.&quot;</p>

<p>The Pudding Slime, who had been watching politely until then, called out to me.</p>

<p>I spoke first before Pudding Slime could say anything.</p>

<p>“Dominic-ssi. I think I can handle this.”</p>

<p>Normally, I would have asked him something.</p>

<p>But right now, I didn't feel like doing that.</p>

<p>I knew that Dominic has no ill intentions behind the advice he gives me.</p>

<p>Although he recommended gouging out eyes, that was appropriate advice by his standards.</p>

<p>It was just that for me, it was difficult to understand and accept.</p>

<p>I made a collect call to Black Block.</p>

<p>Black Block answered immediately after I started the interstellar communication.</p>

<p>—Little Lamb! To reach out to me first—what joy, what delight!</p>

<p>It was as if he had been watching me all along.</p>

<p>I called out to him in a calm tone.</p>

<p>“Black Block-ssi.”</p>

<p>—Speak!</p>

<p>“I am very disappointed.”</p>

<p>—What? Disappointed?!</p>

<p>Black Block screamed in horror.</p>

<p>Before he could let out a sob, I spoke in a cold voice.</p>

<p>“I didn’t expect you to publish an article about me in the Day News.”</p>

<p>—The Day News! It is a truly fine newspaper. It is a true press equipped with noble journalism. But Little Lamb...</p>

<p>I drove the nail in the coffin first to the Black Block, who was trying to slip away pretending not to know.</p>

<p>“Isn’t Reporter Sa Gongyeon Black Block-ssi’s subordinate?”</p>

<p>Silence.</p>

<p>In the silence that felt as if sound had been cut off, I waited expressionlessly.</p>

<p>After a few seconds, a loud laugh was heard. It was laughter loud enough to make my eardrums ring for a moment.</p>

<p>—Hahaha! That’s the correct answer, Little Lamb!</p>

<p>As expected, my hunch was right.</p>

<p>I had once received a quest at the broadcasting station in DeZonDeal, where zombies roamed.</p>
<br />
<p><span class="flashback">[★Event Quest: Exciting Broadcasting Station Exploration]</span></p>
<br />
<p>It was a quest sent by Black Block, and the quest description included the phrase, 'The broadcasting station is ■■ ■■■'s subordinate!'</p>

<p>Furthermore, in CallOVo, he had contacted me using Day News.</p>

<p>It was enough to suspect that the Black Block was an entity connected to the media.</p>

<p>That was not all.</p>

<p>Sa Gongyeon’s behavior—rushing at me claiming to be a fan and obsessing over sponsorship—had aspects similar to Black Block.</p>

<p>However, I had determined that they were not the same person, and I was merely suspecting a connection.</p>

<p>The decisive factor in my conviction was this recent front-page newspaper incident.</p>

<p>It seemed clear that Black Block was a superior being to Sa Gongyeon.</p>

<p>However, a mere reporter could not possibly have spread Black Block’s subject of interest throughout the entire universe at will.</p>

<p>The fact that Sa Gongyeon had lavishly decorated the front page of Day News with the birth of a young god meant that there had been instructions from Black Block.</p>

<p>At this point, I suspected that Sa Gongyeon was a subordinate of Black Block, and at the same time, I realized the sinister scheme hidden behind it.</p>

<p>‘Sponsorship contracts.’</p>

<p>All sorts of Outer Gods would take an interest in the young god and try to track the planet.</p>

<p>As a newcomer to Akasha, there was no way I could handle all the Outer Gods.</p>

<p>If I were cornered, I would look for someone to turn to for help, but right now, there were only two beings around me who possessed that kind of power.</p>

<p>Dominic, or Black Block.</p>

<p>Since one can have multiple sponsors, even if I were to sign a sponsorship contract with Dominic, Black Block would have been looking for an opening to intervene.</p>

<p>While showing off to me the influence he could wield over the universe media.</p>

<p>If I hadn't known the truth, I might have signed sponsorship contracts with both of them just to defend Earth.</p>

<p>‘There isn’t a single alien in this world you can trust.’</p>

<p>Pudding Slime froze my account, and Black Block sold my face to the entire universe.</p>

<p>The administrator's description of it as a &quot;lawless zone&quot; was very apt.</p>

<p>It seemed that surviving in this harsh universe, where there was not a shred of consideration for children, would not be easy.</p>

<p>—Little Lamb. Let's sign a sponsorship contract.</p>

<p>Black Block no longer hid his true intentions.</p>

<p>It wasn't surprising, as it was a development I had fully anticipated.</p>

<p>“What happens if I refuse?”</p>

<p>—Refusal, huh? It will be a bad choice.</p>

<p>Black Block's voice began to twist strangely.</p>

<p>—What can the Little Lamb <em>do on his own?</em></p>

<p>His words, mixed with laughter, continued slowly.</p>

<p>—<em>New articles are scattered every day, and more and more people take an interest... The Little Lamb's small, cute planet <b>might be crushed!</b></em></p>

<p>Just as I found myself frowning involuntarily...</p>

<p>A short laugh rang out.</p>

<p>Hovering somewhere between a dry chuckle and a sneer, it was low and quiet.</p>

<p>Nevertheless, it pierced my brain with striking clarity.</p>

<p><em>And then he spoke.</em></p>
<br />
<p style="font-size:21.6px"><span class="skew">“■■■■■■■■.”</span></p>
<br />
<p>I heard nothing of what the <em>voice</em> said.</p>

<p>However, I could clearly feel the shaking of the space that occurred at that moment.</p>

<p>If he had applied just a little, just a tiny bit more force, everything around me would have twisted and torn apart.</p>

<p>A dizzying terror swept through my body like a shiver.</p>

<p>I stood frozen, dazed.</p>

<p>Slowly regaining my breath, I confirmed that I was still here.</p>

<p>Then, very slowly, I turned my gaze.</p>

<p>On the desk, a small, soft slime was sparkling.</p>

<p>It was still the cute form I knew.</p>

<p>A polite apology came through.</p>

<p>“I’m sorry, Roasted Chestnut. I startled you.”</p>

<p>He comforted me, saying it wasn’t something a young Roasted Chestnut should have heard, that he had made a mistake, and asked if I was surprised.</p>

<p>I parted my lips and barely managed to shake my head.</p>

<p>Then, a gentle voice returned, saying, "That's a relief."</p>

<p>The other party, connected via interstellar communication, was just as frozen.</p>

<p>After a long silence, Black Block spoke.</p>

<p>—Little Lamb. I was watching you first. For a very long time, only you...</p>

<p>I don't know what Dominic had said to him, but he seemed to have lost his composure.</p>

<p>His twisted voice had returned to its normal state.</p>

<p>However, he still hadn't given up on me.</p>

<p>By then, I was slowly recovering.</p>

<p>'Now is the time.'</p>

<p>To ensure I wouldn't miss this once-in-a-lifetime opportunity, I opened my mouth quickly.</p>

<p>&quot;Black Block-ssi.&quot;</p>

<p>I gave him a carrot first.</p>

<p>&quot;I didn't know you had been watching me for so long. You really are a long-time fan of mine.&quot;</p>

<p>—Ah, Little Lamb!</p>

<p>Just as he was about to be moved, I poured cold water on him.</p>

<p>&quot;But to act like this...&quot;</p>

<p>Actually, I was the one who shouldn't have been acting this way.</p>

<p>After all, I was a low-level god, while the other party was a max-level god with a full set of gear.</p>

<p>But?</p>

<p>I was backed by Dominic, the oldest, most veteran god.</p>

<p>Therefore, I abandoned my original negotiation plan and decided to just stand my ground.</p>

<p>“I will send the food soon. Let’s leave it at that.”</p>

<p>It meant I would send the food as a farewell and that we shouldn't contact each other again.</p>

<p>Black Block went into a frenzy.</p>

<p>—Please, anything but that! I was wrong.</p>

<p>Given the current situation, I felt like just sending a meal kit and telling him to cook the promised dish himself.</p>

<p>However, I couldn't afford to completely sever ties with Black Block.</p>

<p>He was a being who had been watching me for who knows how long.</p>

<p>Furthermore, he was already extending his influence across Earth through Sa Gongyeon.</p>

<p>Even if I cast him out immediately, the probability of getting entangled with him in the end was high.</p>

<p>‘And aliens must be defeated by aliens.’</p>

<p>I felt I could vaguely understand what the Outer Gods meant by ‘interest.’</p>

<p>Judging by his actions so far, even though Black Block had driven me into a crisis, it didn't seem like he would throw me to other aliens as prey.</p>

<p>Unless he took on the role of a savior himself.</p>

<p>And crucially, he hadn't revealed the planet's name or coordinates.</p>

<p>—Little Lamb. What do I have to do to appease you?</p>

<p>“Hmm, I think there might be a way...”</p>

<p>As I slowly began to speak, the Black Block grew impatient.</p>

<p>—What is it! Please let me know immediately.</p>

<p>I waited long enough for him to become impatient before I opened my mouth.</p>

<p>“Please settle the payment for my photos.”</p>

<p>My face has already been sold; let me at least earn some Star Fragments.</p>

<p>After all, how could I possibly recall my face once it had spread to every corner of the universe?</p>

<p>Given the circumstances, it seemed best to at least collect the payment.</p>

<p>“I will continue to provide photos, so I would like to sign a formal contract.”</p>

<p>—Little Lamb...</p>

<p>The Black Block called out to me in a trembling voice.</p>

<p>Just as I was about to suspect that he wasn't acting this way because he was stingy with the Star Fragments...</p>

<p>A tremendous sound erupted from the communication.</p>

<p style="word-break:break-all">—LittleLambLittleLambLittleLambLittleLambLittleLambLittleLambLittleLambLittleLamb <span style="font-size:21.6px;letter-spacing:3px"><b>Little Lamb!</b></span></p>

<p>A voice filled with madness called out to me.</p>

<p>Beyond the crackling noise that sounded like bursting explosions, laughter erupted as if millions were screaming in unison.</p>

<p>This was followed by a sharp squeal, and before long, the ear-splitting noise vanished.</p>

<p>In the silence, the Black Block let out a sound like a maniac: &quot;Hehe, hehehe.&quot;</p>

<p>—Little Lamb is truly my star. As your longtime fan, every time this happens ■■■■■■■...</p>

<p>Once again, it was words I couldn't understand.</p>

<p>Muttering something to himself, the Black Block spoke to me.</p>

<p>—I will go prepare a contract. A contract befitting my star.</p>

<p>He promised with such emotion, yet in the blink of an eye, he lost his mind again and started trying to seduce me.</p>

<p>—But Little Lamb, wouldn't mere photos be insufficient? Little Lamb needs more star fragments. I have a good idea!</p>

<p>&quot;Black Block-ssi.&quot;</p>

<p>—Speak!</p>

<p><em>It's annoying, so stop it.</em></p>

<p>—I'm sorry, Little Lamb...</p>

<p>I hadn't actually said that out loud, but since it seemed effective at getting the creature to back off, I didn't correct it.</p>

<p>“Please contact me when the contract is ready.”</p>

<p>I intended to show the contract to the administrator as well.</p>

<p>And since things had turned out this way, I also asked about what I had been trying to find out.</p>

<p>“Black Block-ssi, you are interested in my current form, aren't you?”</p>

<p>—That is correct.</p>

<p>“Do you happen to know a way for me to maintain this state?”</p>

<p>—What state, exactly?</p>

<p>I wasn't sure how Black Block would take it, but I spoke cautiously.</p>

<p>“I... want to be a god while being human.”</p>

<p>I wanted to be a god who retained my humanity.</p>

<p>If I had to accept the ego of a <em>young god</em>, I wanted to take the initiative.</p>

<p>—That is interesting.</p>

<p>Black Block answered my dilemma cheerfully.</p>

<p>—But the Little Lamb has never been human!</p>
