<h1>Chapter 101</h1>


<p>It goes without saying that I don't enjoy getting beaten up.</p>

<p>However, when playing as the Medic, the fastest and most effective strategy was to get thrashed by the guards right from day one.</p>

<p>Cell-X featured a unique system exclusive to the Medic role.</p>

<p>It revolved around two stats: 'Suspicion' and 'Affinity.'</p>

<p>Unlike most games where you only need to manage affinity, here you had to keep an eye on the suspicion level as well.</p>

<p>These stats were tracked individually for each NPC, ranging from a minimum of 0 to a maximum of 100.</p>

<p>The events that occurred and the ending you reached depended on these figures.</p>

<p>If a guard’s suspicion level was high, there was a strong chance of triggering an event where you’d be dragged to a blind spot—away from security cameras—and beaten.</p>

<p>On the other hand, if the guards' suspicion level was too low, it became difficult to build affinity with the Creatures, no matter how well you treated them.</p>

<p>They would suspect you of being in league with the guards.</p>

<p>To achieve the True Ending as the Medic, you needed to maintain a balanced level of suspicion and affinity with the guards, while achieving zero suspicion and maximum affinity (100) with the Creatures.</p>

<p>The guards required particular caution.</p>

<p>It wasn't just high suspicion that was dangerous; scenarios where both suspicion and affinity were high—or even just affinity alone—would all lead to a Bad Ending.</p>

<p>In short, the guards were tricky figures; you couldn't treat them too well, yet you couldn't treat them poorly either.</p>

<p>"Getting beaten up right at the start is a smart move for the long run."</p>

<p>Sustaining injuries inflicted by the guards actually boosted the Creatures' affinity toward you.</p>

<p>Since gaining the Creatures' favor was difficult in the early stages, this method was the best option all around.</p>

<p>"You’re always so bold, Roasted Chestnut."</p>

<p>Dominic described my approach using polite phrasing. It certainly wasn't a compliment. But as long as I could escape this place quickly, I didn't mind taking a beating.</p>

<p>Stepping out of my quarters, I saw a dimly lit corridor.</p>

<p>Even with my flashlight on, it only illuminated the immediate area; the space didn't feel truly bright.</p>

<p>I walked quietly down the hallway, which was heavy with an eerie atmosphere.</p>

<p>The prison was divided into inmate blocks and guard blocks, and the Medic's quarters were tucked away in the farthest corner of the guard block.</p>

<p>My destination was the guards' break room.</p>

<p>Unless they were on duty, the guards usually spent late nights there playing cards or smoking.</p>

<p>I planned to go there and pick a fight.</p>

<p>Walking down the corridor with nothing but a flashlight in hand, I sent a message to Captain Kwak.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Captain Kwak, are you alright? I’m playing the role of the medic. Please come see me tomorrow.</p></div>

<p>Since I was bound to get beaten up today, I sent a message asking him to visit tomorrow instead.</p>

<p>‘Come to think of it, Dominic is a guard, too.’</p>

<p>I found myself curious about Dominic’s stats.</p>

<p>Of course, since he wasn't an NPC, his suspicion and affinity levels wouldn't affect the ending.</p>

<p>Still, thinking I might be able to see the numbers, I took a quick look at Dominic’s stats.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Dominic (Guard)<br/>
&#45; Suspicion: 999<br/>
&#45; Affinity: 999</p></div></div>

<p>…Huh?</p>

<p>I was momentarily stunned by the unexpected sequence of numbers.</p>

<p>‘The maximum is supposed to be 100, right? Why is the suspicion so high?’</p>

<p>Having checked the stats expecting zero suspicion and maximum affinity, I couldn't help but be flustered.</p>

<p>“Roasted Chestnut.”</p>

<p>Like a guilty conscience betraying itself, my heart sank the moment Dominic called out to me. I cautiously replied to ‘Suspicion 999.’</p>

<p>“…Pardon?”</p>

<p>“What are you so curious about?”</p>

<p>Could it be that checking stats also required using one’s real name?</p>

<p>I wondered if I’d been subjected to a real-name requirement for the second time—following character registration—but Dominic spoke with a hint of amusement in his voice.</p>

<p>“You seemed to want to check my stats, so I brought it up.”</p>

<p>“Well… actually, a medic can see things like that.”</p>

<p>As I honestly confessed about the numerical error, Dominic let out a low chuckle.</p>

<p>“You’d find it difficult to measure me with something like that.”</p>

<p>Dominic was an existence that defied measurement—an anomaly in himself.</p>

<p>It was presumptuous of me to try and gauge him with such superficial metrics.</p>

<p>Any lingering sense of hurt toward ‘Suspicion 999’ vanished completely.</p>

<p>Dominic soothed me as I reflected on my mistake.</p>

<p>“If you’re curious, just ask. I’ll answer whatever you like.”</p>

<p>'In that case, Dominic, exactly where do your suspicion and affection for me fall on a scale of 0 to 100?'</p>

<p>…Since I couldn't exactly ask that, I simply replied that it was fine.</p>

<p>The hallway grew gradually brighter as we neared the guards' break room.</p>

<p>I stowed my flashlight in my inventory and pondered as I walked.</p>

<p>In the game, the event would trigger the moment you entered, but that wouldn't be the case for a real-life trial.</p>

<p>I was agonizing over what lines to deliver to trigger the event correctly when I found myself near the break room.</p>

<p>Loud laughter drifted from behind the door—the sound of guards chatting while playing a game.</p>

<p>I was hovering uncertainly outside the break room, having not yet decided on my opening line.</p>

<p>“What is the matter?”</p>

<p>A cold voice rang out from behind me. Startled, I turned around, and the first thing that caught my eye was a gray metal mask.</p>

<p>The Chief of Security was looking down at me.</p>

<p>Ding—a sound signaled a change in stats.</p>

<p>Thinking, 'Suspicion must have gone up,' I glanced at the system window.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Suspicion +8</p></div>



<div class="systembox"><p>Affinity +1</p></div>

<p>“…?”</p>

<p>I witnessed the peculiar sight of both suspicion and affinity rising simultaneously.</p>

<p>It made sense for suspicion to rise; after all, I had been standing in front of the guards' break room since my very first night in prison.</p>

<p>But it was baffling as to why my affinity had gone up.</p>

<p>'Is it a glitch?'</p>

<p>Perhaps it rose because he was excited at the prospect of hitting me.</p>

<p>In any case, the affinity had only increased by one point, and getting hit was the more pressing matter right now.</p>

<p>After some deliberation, I decided to use a provocation skill.</p>

<p>“I’ve come to lodge a complaint about what happened today…!”</p>

<p>Despite the bold words, my voice trembled slightly.</p>

<p>The lips visible beneath the mask immediately curled into a sneer.</p>

<p>“You’ve come to lodge a complaint?”</p>

<p>Mockingly repeating my words, the Chief of Security flung open the break room door.</p>

<p>Then, he gave me a rough shove inside.</p>

<p>These men dealt with creatures, so their strength was immense.</p>

<p>I nearly toppled over; I stumbled and took several unsteady steps before finally regaining my balance.</p>

<p>Then, I awkwardly lifted my head.</p>

<p>“…….”</p>

<p>Every guard in the break room was looking at me.</p>

<p>Even though half their faces were hidden behind metal masks, I could feel their piercing gazes.</p>

<p>“Huh? Isn't that the doc?”</p>

<p>One of the guards tossed the playing cards he was holding onto the table and spoke up in recognition.</p>

<p>The Chief of Security, closing the break room door behind me, answered on my behalf. “So, you want to lodge a complaint about what happened today?”</p>

<p>At the Chief of Security’s words, the guards burst into laughter in unison, then stood up one by one, snickering.</p>

<p>Figures with imposing physiques loomed over me.</p>

<p>At this point, I felt a twinge of regret.</p>

<p>Unlike in the game, the realization that I would have to take the blows myself made me feel the pain before it even happened.</p>

<p>‘The restroom? Or the shower room?’</p>

<p>Since the guards' break room had surveillance cameras, I figured I’d be dragged to one of those two places for a beating; I was wondering which one it would be.</p>

<p>“Whoa, hey, guys. Hold on a sec.”</p>

<p>One of the guards suddenly sprang up from a dimly lit corner.</p>

<p>‘There was another guard?’</p>

<p>The guard, having hurriedly stubbed out his cigarette in the ashtray, strode toward me with long, confident steps.</p>

<p>He, too, had half his face concealed by a black metal mask.</p>

<p>However, I could see red hair peeking out from beneath his cap.</p>

<p>Thwack. The red-haired guard threw his arm around my shoulders, almost slamming into me.</p>

<p>“I’ll make sure this greenhorn doctor gets a proper orientation today—from one rookie guard to another. You guys just take it easy.”</p>

<p>He spoke with the swagger of a thug, then put me in a headlock and gave my head a sharp, painful rap with his knuckles.</p>

<p>It hurt enough to bring tears to my eyes; when I let out a groan, the guards laughed mockingly.</p>

<p>One guard tapped my stomach with the back of his hand and said:</p>

<p>“Doc. Pay close attention to your training. Let’s get along starting tomorrow, yeah?”</p>

<p>Amidst their snickering laughter, I was dragged away, my arm held tight by the red-haired guard. He remained silent even after stepping out of the break room.</p>

<p>We walked quite a distance away, eventually entering a dark corridor, before I finally spoke up.</p>

<p>“Captain Kwak…?”</p>

<p>Only then did Kwak Hanmuk whip around to look at me.</p>

<p>He removed his mask. His gray eyes were hard and unyielding.</p>

<p>“Goyo.”</p>

<p>“Yes?”</p>

<p>“What were you just trying to do?”</p>

<p>“…Ah, well.”</p>

<p>It had been unexpected that Kwak Hanmuk was assigned the role of a prison guard.</p>

<p>After all, roles in Cell-X were never duplicated.</p>

<p>Since Dominic had been assigned as a guard, I had naturally assumed Kwak Hanmuk would have a different role.</p>

<p>'Is it because he wasn't active?'</p>

<p>After the event scene, Dominic hadn't done a thing; he’d just stood there wearing his hat.</p>

<p>He had even teleported away from the guard's post, so it seemed likely the system had glitched.</p>

<p>In any case, things became much easier for me thanks to Kwak Hanmuk being a guard.</p>

<p>I explained my plan to him.</p>

<p>“…So, I think it would work best if you inflicted a suitable injury on me.”</p>

<p>Upon hearing this, Kwak Hanmuk pressed a hand to his forehead and muttered.</p>

<p>“Goyo, you really are… a total nutcase.”</p>

<p>Then, he rapped me on the head with his prayer beads—snap.</p>

<p>“A nutcase like you needs another flick to the forehead.”</p>

<p>Having been hit by both his hand and the beads, I felt a bit aggrieved, but I took the knocks quietly.</p>

<p>Then, a little belatedly, I told him I was glad he was safe.</p>

<p>“My sanity isn't safe thanks to a nutcase like you. A member of the TRA thinking about getting beaten up? You should be thinking about beating the hell out of others.”</p>

<p>“I’m sorry.” As I gave a polite, apologetic bow, the look in Kwak Hanmuk’s eyes finally softened a little.</p>

<p>“You promised Captain Mo you wouldn’t do anything dangerous.”</p>

<p>“What I did earlier didn’t exactly amount to risking my life...”</p>

<p>“Do you want another flick to the forehead?”</p>

<p>“No...”</p>

<p>Reading the room, I briefed him on the current situation.</p>

<p>I informed him that Je Hyun-oh, Won Taebeom, and CEO Cha had entered the area, shared news from outside, and briefly outlined the information regarding Cell-X.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk narrowed his eyes as he listened.</p>

<p>“This is definitely a five-star difficulty trial. It might even hit six.”</p>

<p>While he looked genuinely troubled, I hesitated.</p>

<p>I had been wanting to ask for a while, but I was worried it might be an ill-timed question given the serious situation.</p>

<p>“What is it?”</p>

<p>He must have noticed my hesitation, as he asked the question himself; unable to hold back any longer, I spoke up.</p>

<p>“Captain Kwak, were you a smoker?”</p>

<p>"?”</p>

<p>“No?”</p>

<p>“Then what was that earlier?”</p>

<p>“I’m an intermittent non-smoker.”</p>

<p>“…….”</p>

<p>I didn’t quite grasp the difference between that and being a smoker, but my curiosity was satisfied.</p>

<p>So that explained why he had been staring at the ashtray on the roof earlier.</p>

<p>It was a trivial detail, but since it concerned Kwak Hanmuk, I made sure to commit it to memory.</p>

<p>“Oh, look, it’s wearing a hat now, too.”</p>

<p>At Kwak Hanmuk’s remark, I glanced at the Pudding Slime.</p>

<p>The softly shimmering guard slime looked absolutely adorable.</p>

<p>“Sometimes, accessories are added like this depending on the trials.”</p>

<p>“Is that so…?”</p>

<p>After spending a moment observing the slime, Kwak Hanmuk headed to the medical staff quarters with me.</p>

<p>He ushered me into the room and spoke in a stern, almost commanding tone.</p>

<p>“Don’t pull any stunts; just sleep quietly tonight. I’ll dig up as much information on the guards as I can for you.”</p>

<p>It wasn’t really the information that mattered so much as the suspicion and affinity levels, but…</p>

<p>Still, there was no point in arguing; Kwak Hanmuk wouldn’t budge.</p>

<p>I saw Kwak Hanmuk off and reluctantly lay down on the bed.</p>

<p>The Pudding Slime naturally climbed up onto my chest.</p>

<p>Back in Sla2Plate, it had constantly slept on my chest because of the straw bed; perhaps out of habit, it always made a sleeping spot for itself right there on me.</p>

<p>Since the slime was weightless and felt pleasant to the touch, I didn’t mind it at all.</p>

<p>I drifted off to sleep with the Pudding Slime resting on my chest.</p>

<p>That was how my first day in prison—a Sunday—came to an end.</p>

<p>And the next day, Monday.</p>

<p>As I began my weekly schedule as a Medic, I ran into Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>He was there as Creature Inmate No. 13 from Zone X.</p>
