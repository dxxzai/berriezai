<h1>Chapter 105</h1>


<p>The quest that I most dislike in Cell-X has begun.</p>

<p>However, it was impossible to cancel a quest that had already started.</p>

<p>I first checked the pudding slime.</p>

<p>When I reached out to my head, a soft body came up onto my palm.</p>

<p>I quickly put my hand down and checked.</p>

<p>“…….”</p>

<p>I saw Dominic who had turned into neon green slime.</p>

<p>As soon as I saw the sight soaked in fluorescent liquid, I almost burst out laughing, but I held it back.</p>

<p>It was because Dominic seemed to be in a bad mood.</p>

<p>I looked worried and wiped the pudding slime off with my hand.</p>

<p>“Are you okay? I’m sorry, it's because of me.”</p>

<p>I fulfilled my selfish desires by pretending to clean him.</p>

<p>I massaged the soft body, and briefly took off and touched the prison guard hat that I had been curious about for a while.</p>

<p>I was curious about what material the prison guard's hat was made of, but it wasn't as soft as slime and felt like a regular hat.</p>

<p>I restored Dominic to the sparkly pudding slime as best I could and then put the hat back on.</p>

<p>Dominic, who had been holding still as I kneaded, finished by shaking out his ears to expel the last remaining liquid.</p>

<p>Then he looked up at me and said.</p>

<p>“Watching over Roasted Chestnut is not as easy as I thought.”</p>

<p>It seemed like he couldn't understand the existence of God being harassed by things that were infinitely insignificant by his standards.</p>

<p>“I’m sorry, Dominic-ssi. I will get stronger quickly.”</p>

<p>When Dominic heard my answer, he burst into laughter for some reason.</p>

<p>I don't know what's funny, but Dominic then silently jumped up and climbed onto my shoulder.</p>

<p>Actually, it wasn't the time to talk to Dominic.</p>

<p>This is because time is important here.</p>

<p>I took off my doctor's gown and twisted it like I was doing laundry.</p>

<p>I finished reading the quest window with the sound of neon green liquid dripping as background music.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>◆Sub-quest: Become a true prison guard.<br/>
The Chief of Security, who is always looking for new guards, felt both special attraction and suspicion toward you.<br/>
The Chief of Security wants to wash away the apparent distrust in order to welcome you as a colleague.<br/>
You must prove your innocence to the Chief of Security by faithfully completing the new guard training course.<br/>
But this might not be the right way.</p></div></div>

<div class="systembox"><p>Becoming a new prison guard.... 1%</p></div>

<p>“Haa….”</p>

<p>After finishing reading the quest window, I sighed again.</p>

<p>The place where the Chief of Security pushed me was the new prison guard training center.</p>

<p>I wanted to recruit him as a colleague because his favorability score was high, but if his suspicion score was also high, I would be pushed into this place.</p>

<p>Each time you complete training here, your progress as a new prison guard increases.</p>

<p>However, there was one catch.</p>

<p>Regardless of training completion, the progress of becoming a prison guard was guaranteed to reach 100% after a certain period of time.</p>

<p>It was because of the neon green liquid that I fell into when I first fell into the training room.</p>

<p>The neon green liquid, a key component of prison guards, was infectious.</p>

<p>The more you drink, the higher your starting percentage will be, and the less you drink, the lower your starting percentage will be.</p>

<p>‘Once you touch it, you will definitely be infected, but… .’</p>

<p>Still, thanks to being as careful as possible, I started with the lowest percentage, 1%.</p>

<p>I shook off as much of the neon green liquid that was left on my body.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>You feel you should not take the new prison guard training course.</p></div>

<p>That was something I knew well.</p>

<p>However, there is no way to get out if you do not take this training course.</p>

<p>‘If you don't have a solution, keep quiet. It's a nuisance.'</p>

<p>The system window no longer appeared.</p>

<p>In any case, there was nothing the system could do besides block my view.</p>

<p>And I really had no intention of becoming a prison guard anyway.</p>

<p>I walked down the dark passage. After I was walking along a corridor-like area for a while, suddenly, my vision became brighter.</p>

<p>Before I knew it, I was in a small, square room.</p>

<p>A large monitor came down with a jingling sound.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_monitor"><p>Welcome to the <span class="glitch_t2">????</span>new prison guard training course!</p></div>

<p>As the screen changed, letters appeared in abundance.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_monitor"><p>This training is a process of developing the physical strength, strong sense of duty, and strict judgment that new prison guards must possess.<br/>
It is never easy to establish order inside a prison cell and reform the minds of prisoners.<br/>
But it is an equally worthy and noble mission.<br/>
Education here will help you shed your weakness and become the prison's strongest shield and weapon.<br/>
I hope that by completing this training, you will be able to become a great new prison guard.</p></div>

<p>After the lengthy notice, an input box appeared with a short phrase.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_monitor"><p>Enter the trainee's name.</p></div>

<p>I laughed a little. The monitor was a touchpad.</p>

<p>It was a cutting-edge method that did not fit in with other objects in the prison.</p>

<p>In this way, I felt strange every time items that did not match the setting appeared in the trials.</p>

<p>I entered the name the trial gave me.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_monitor"><p>Trainee: Little Lamb</p></div>

<p>I looked at my name for a moment before hitting enter.</p>

<p>It is said that the name of the trial represents the characteristics of the suitable person or has some symbolic meaning.</p>

<p>My first name was Roasted Chestnut.</p>

<p>That was originally a nickname I used in the game, so I didn't think anything of it.</p>

<p>‘I only thought I wanted to change it into something cooler.’</p>

<p>And after being changed into Little Lamb, there was no need to worry about the name.</p>

<p>I knew that in rare cases the name given by the trial would change, so I just assumed it was something like that.</p>

<p>Why a lamb?</p>

<p>Along with the doubts came some frustration.</p>

<p>Since it was changing anyway, they should have given me a cool name; why did they give me an animal name?</p>

<p>While I was hoping they would sell me a name change ticket, there was a rattling noise.</p>

<p>It was the sound of the steel cabinet in the right corner being opened.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_monitor"><p>Trainees must change into appropriate attire.</p></div>

<p>I headed to the cabinet, and inside was a large towel and a set of prison guard uniforms.</p>

<p>I looked like a rat drowning in neon green water, so I was happy to see new clothes.</p>

<p>After drying my body with a towel, I dressed in a prison guard uniform from head to toe.</p>

<p>When I put on my hat and held my baton, I looked like I could work in a prison right away.</p>

<p>However, there was no metal mask.</p>

<p>An explanation for it had already appeared on the monitor.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_monitor"><p>Metal masks are distributed after completion of all training courses.</p></div>

<p>I didn't really want to wear a metal mask.</p>

<p>This was because I already had a great decoration item called Prison Guard Slime.</p>

<p>If it were a game, it would have been a legendary item.</p>

<p>‘In the first place, the more cute an item like this is, the harder it is to find.’</p>

<p>I put the slime down for a moment to change clothes and put it back on my shoulder.</p>

<p>Dominic complimented me.</p>

<p>“The uniform looks good on you, Roasted Chestnut.”</p>

<p>“Thank you. You too, Dominic-ssi. Your hat is really cool.”</p>

<p>After complimenting the hat, I returned to the monitor.</p>

<p>A song like a peaceful guidance sound flowed from the monitor.</p>

<p>Then a new screen appeared.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_monitor"><p>Hello, Trainee Little Lamb.<br/>
From now on, we will begin full-scale training.<br/>
The first lesson is ‘basic knowledge’.</p></div>

<p>A rippling sea was depicted on the screen.</p>

<p>It displayed text while showing a prison in the middle of a remote island.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_monitor"><p>Criminals imprisoned in this prison are divided into humans and creatures.<br/>
They have all committed heinous crimes, and the only means of reforming their minds is...</p></div>

<div class="bigbox2_monitor"><p>...is ‘power’.</p></div>

<div class="bigbox2_monitor"><p>Except for No. 6, communication with creature prisoners is impossible, so you need to use ‘force’ more actively to subdue them.</p></div>

<p>An illustration of prison guards hitting a Creature Inmate with a baton appeared on the screen.</p>

<p>A rattling sound was heard again along with an illustration that was exaggerated and humorous, even cute at first glance.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_monitor"><p>Let's move to the next room and learn how to subdue creatures.</p></div>

<p>A crack appeared on the wall and the door opened.</p>

<p>The prison guard progress rate also increased.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Becoming a new prison guard.... 5%</p></div>

<p>I moved to the next room and gritted my teeth.</p>

<p>A new monitor appeared.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_monitor"><p>Strike the creature model with force at least 10 times with the baton.</p></div>

<p>Beneath it lay a circular tangle of vines.</p>

<p>The plant with its thick stem was about the size of a large teddy bear.</p>

<p>Even though it didn't make any sound when I hit it, I knew it.</p>

<p>That was part of Creature Inmate No. 1.</p>

<p>The sense of pain is still connected, so when you hit it, it still feels the pain.</p>

<p>Nevertheless, it was brought for educational training.</p>

<p>The letters appeared on the monitor again.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_monitor"><p>Violence is the only means of reforming the minds of vicious creatures.</p></div>

<p>A deflated laugh spontaneously flowed from my mouth.</p>

<p>In the game, the player starts playing without any information about the creatures.</p>

<p>Just like the human prisoners in the prison, the Creatures are considered to be violent criminals and choose harsh options for them.</p>

<p>As you play multiple times and see the endings in different roles, you gradually gain new information, and the truth is revealed in the medic's bad ending, the creature annihilation ending.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_pixel"><p>“Roasted Chestnut. In truth, no one here has committed a crime.”</p></div>

<div class="bigbox2_pixel"><p>“I don’t have enough energy left to claim my innocence to others. I don’t think they will listen. I just wanted to tell Roasted Chestnut about our injustice.”</p></div>

<div class="bigbox2_pixel"><p>“Because we don’t want to be remembered as bad people by Roasted Chestnut.”</p></div>

<p>After listening to Creature No. 6's will and seeing the bad ending, information is unlocked little by little from then on.</p>

<p>There is no creature here who has committed a heinous crime.</p>

<p>The remote island prison that was thought to hold only the most vicious criminals was actually a detention facility for extracting biofuel from Creatures.</p>

<p>They used human criminals to block outside views and exploit creatures.</p>

<p>A total of twelve Creatures were imprisoned in this prison.</p>

<p>Except for creature No. 6, free communication with humans was impossible.</p>

<p>Creatures who could not properly protest were imprisoned, their freedom was taken away, and they were oppressed with violence.</p>

<p>And the Chief of Security was a scumbag who knew the whole truth.</p>

<p>“…….”</p>

<p>I breathed calmly.</p>

<p>I took out my baton, approached the vine, and placed my right hand on it.</p>

<p>As soon as I placed my hand, a faint spasm radiated from the plant. It was a trembling that knew the pain was about to come.</p>

<p>I raised my baton.</p>

<p>And I brought my hand down.</p>

<p>“…Ugh!”</p>

<p>Even though I was prepared, it hurt quite a bit. I swallowed my groan and brought the baton down again.</p>

<p>The '10 hit' rule had a slight loophole.</p>

<p>It was that as long as I was in contact with the creature, hitting my own body would be counted as a hit to the creature.</p>

<p>It was a way to pass the training without losing the creature's favor, even though it was a bit painful.</p>

<p>‘I only have to endure it ten times.’</p>

<p>To be counted as a hit, a certain level of force had to be applied. I swung the baton violently.</p>

<p>It was when I had hit about six times.</p>

<p>Rustling...</p>

<p>The vine suddenly began to move.</p>

<p>Just as I was startled, wondering if I had somehow missed with the baton.</p>

<p>A soft stem carefully wrapped around my right hand.</p>

<p>To prevent me from striking it with the baton any further.</p>
