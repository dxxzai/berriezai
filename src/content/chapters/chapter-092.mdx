<h1>Chapter 92</h1>

<p>I barely managed to hide my mixed feelings.</p>

<p>"Director."</p>

<p>I made a show of trying to sit up, but Jang Seokyun stopped me with practiced ease.</p>

<p>"Stay seated. You're still recovering, aren't you?"</p>

<p>I didn't argue and settled right back onto the bed.</p>

<p>Leaning against the raised backrest, I looked at him.</p>

<p>'It should've been Samra.'</p>

<p>I needed to see Samra before being left alone with Jang Seokyun.</p>

<p>If I'd heard the current situation from Samra first and gathered even a little information, I'd have had the advantage.</p>

<p>But Jang Seokyun had probably thought the exact same thing and deliberately came before giving me that chance.</p>

<p>Hiding my tension, I forced a relaxed smile.</p>

<p>Pretending to be weak wouldn't work on Jang Seokyun. If anything, he'd press even harder.</p>

<p>Against someone like him, it was better to put up a strong front, even if it was just bluffing.</p>

<p>As if he'd simply come for a routine hospital visit, Jang Seokyun pulled over a chair and sat beside my bed.</p>

<p>"You regained consciousness after three days. How are you feeling?"</p>

<p>Three days...</p>

<p>Still, I felt much better now. When I answered that I was fine, he picked up a bottle of water.</p>

<p>"Would you like a drink?"</p>

<p>"Thank you."</p>

<p>As I drank, I tried to guess how he planned to persuade me.</p>

<p>He already knew ordinary terms wouldn't be enough to keep me.</p>

<p>But the Trial Response Agency was a government organization. It couldn't possibly match the kind of offers Paseong, a private corporation, could make.</p>

<p>Just the conditions Director Seo Moonchan had rattled off with that insane contract were beyond what even major corporations could normally offer.</p>

<p>Most likely, Jang Seokyun would appeal to things only the Agency could provide—people like Samra or Ham Jiwol.</p>

<p>Ham Jiwol...</p>

<p>She was terrifying, but undeniably capable.</p>

<p>Developing vaccines and treatments for Trials... there were many things only she could do.</p>

<p>"Trainee Han."</p>

<p>Jang Seokyun began with praise.</p>

<p>"You really did close those Trials. Three of them in a single month. As Director of the Trial Response Agency—and as a citizen of the Republic of Korea—I sincerely thank you."</p>

<p>Considering his position, it was surprisingly humble.</p>

<p>He held the military rank of General, and in government terms, he was effectively a cabinet minister.</p>

<p>Yet hearing someone like him express gratitude so honestly didn't move me.</p>

<p>It scared me.</p>

<p>It only reinforced the certainty that he had absolutely no intention of letting me go.</p>

<p>I answered carefully.</p>

<p>"It would've been impossible without the Trial Response Agency. You trusted me and gave me your support, so I'd like to thank you as well."</p>

<p>After I responded with proper courtesy, Jang Seokyun let out a hearty laugh.</p>

<p>"Trainee Han."</p>

<p>"Yes, Director."</p>

<p>"I like you very much. Not because you're someone capable of closing Trials."</p>

<p>He smiled warmly.</p>

<p>"I like you as a person."</p>

<p>While I searched for an appropriate response, he continued on his own.</p>

<p>"You've got the instincts of a gambler, don't you? Even when you know it's reckless, if there's even the slightest chance of success, you'll take the bet."</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>"Even if the odds are only one percent."</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>"I imagine that's one of the reasons you've managed to close Trials. That unusual willingness to gamble."</p>

<p>His gaze said he knew everything about me.</p>

<p>He wasn't even part of the Special Mission Team.</p>

<p>How much had he really seen of me?</p>

<p>Even adding together every conversation we'd ever had face-to-face, it didn't total an hour.</p>

<p>He'd undoubtedly received reports from the task force, but he was speaking as though he'd personally watched me.</p>

<p>I understood.</p>

<p>This wasn't directed at "Trainee Han of the Trial Response Agency."</p>

<p>It was aimed at...</p>

<p>'Goyo the professional gamer.'</p>

<p>Naturally.</p>

<p>If I were Jang Seokyun, that would've been the first thing I'd investigated.</p>

<p>If he wanted, there was no shortage of information about my past.</p>

<p>Slowly, I clenched my trembling hand into a fist.</p>

<p>Whenever my old life came up, things always felt awkward.</p>

<p>Even after all this time.</p>

<p>There weren't many people left who remembered or searched for "Goyo."</p>

<p>New star players appeared every year.</p>

<p>Who would remember someone whose career had barely lasted a year?</p>

<p>At best, I was little more than old gossip.</p>

<p>Everything that remained about "Goyo" was only ever used to dig me up and tear me apart—just like now.</p>

<p>I slowly steadied myself.</p>

<p>Fortunately, the trembling in my hand subsided.</p>

<p>I could tell exactly why Jang Seokyun was acting this way.</p>

<p>He wanted to shake me from the very beginning and seize the upper hand.</p>

<p>I reminded myself not to fall for such an obvious tactic and forced another smile.</p>

<p>"...Thank you."</p>

<p>It seemed like I'd answered well enough.</p>

<p>My voice hadn't even trembled.</p>

<p>Jang Seokyun smiled back.</p>

<p>"I was the same when I was young. If there was even the slightest possibility, I'd never give up. I believed I had to keep pushing forward. Sometimes that youthful recklessness paid off. I'm older now, so I can't do things the way I used to."</p>

<p>His eyes rested on me.</p>

<p>"That's why it's enjoyable watching you."</p>

<p>After finishing a story I hadn't been interested in hearing, he finally got to the point.</p>

<p>"Trainee Han. How would you feel about officially joining the Trial Response Agency?"</p>

<p>"I'm honored that you think so highly of me, Director. But I'd appreciate some time to think. It's not a decision I can make lightly..."</p>

<p>"Of course."</p>

<p>His immediate acceptance caught me off guard.</p>

<p>I'd been expecting threats or pressure.</p>

<p>As I wondered why he was backing off so easily, he made another proposal.</p>

<p>"Goyo."</p>

<p>"Would you like to visit Captain Mo?"</p>

<p>I looked at him in confusion.</p>

<p>He explained casually.</p>

<p>"Captain Mo has been hospitalized since clearing Sla2Plate. After the Agency building was damaged, her room was moved closer to yours. I thought the two of you might like to visit."</p>

<p>A bad feeling settled over me.</p>

<p>Still, hearing that Mo Haein was hospitalized immediately made me worry more than anything else.</p>

<p>I wanted to see with my own eyes whether she'd been seriously hurt.</p>

<p>She belonged to the Agency.</p>

<p>If I refused Jang Seokyun's offer now, I had no idea when I'd get another chance to visit her.</p>

<p>It might be a very long time before I saw her again.</p>

<p>After some hesitation, I followed him.</p>

<p>Since the paralysis in my lower body had finally disappeared, I could walk on my own.</p>

<p>Dragging my IV stand out of the room, I found armed soldiers standing outside.</p>

<p>Jang Seokyun signaled to them before leading me toward the basement.</p>

<p>When we reached the deepest underground level, I looked down the unfamiliar hallway.</p>

<p>"Director Jang Seokyun! Ah... T-Trainee Goyo...!"</p>

<p>The entire floor had been converted into a laboratory packed with scientific equipment.</p>

<p>Researchers from the Research Analysis Division hurried back and forth carrying tablets.</p>

<p>Ham Jiwol came hurrying toward us, then froze the moment she saw me.</p>

<p>"Director Ham. We've come to see Captain Mo."</p>

<p>"Ah... Captain Mo...! Th-this way! I'll show Goyo where she is...!"</p>

<p>Even while speaking to Jang Seokyun, Ham Jiwol couldn't take her eyes off me.</p>

<p>And me...</p>

<p>The moment I realized Mo Haein was hospitalized inside a laboratory, a deep sense of dread settled in my chest.</p>

<p>'Why is Captain Mo in Ham Jiwol's lab...?'</p>

<p>Since the Agency headquarters had been wrecked, this had to be a temporary research facility in another building.</p>

<p>Unless it was something extremely serious, they never would've brought her here.</p>

<p>Whatever the reason, being in Ham Jiwol's lab couldn't possibly mean anything good.</p>

<p>Without realizing it, I quickened my pace until I was walking beside Ham Jiwol.</p>

<p>She looked up at me and smiled brightly.</p>

<p>"Goyo-ssi... you don't need to worry too much. I'm taking very good care of Captain Mo...!"</p>

<p>The fact that she was receiving Ham Jiwol's attention was already unfortunate enough.</p>

<p>I tried not to let my anxiety show.</p>

<p>Finally, we arrived at the place where Mo Haein was supposedly "recovering."</p>

<p>Ham Jiwol unlocked the sealed door with her researcher ID.</p>

<p>And then, I saw an enormous glass cylinder.</p>

<p>Mo Haein was suspended inside it, submerged in an unidentified liquid.</p>

<p>Ham Jiwol walked past my frozen body and tapped on a nearby console.</p>

<p>Checking the numbers on the screen, she said,</p>

<p>"Captain Mo... her readings have stabilized quite a bit... but she shouldn't enter any Trials for a while...!"</p>

<p>I couldn't take my eyes off her.</p>

<p>Inside the liquid, dressed in nothing but a tank top and shorts...</p>

<p>Embedded in her abdomen, was a small stone glowing with a faint light.</p>

<p>A Trial meteorite.</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>I couldn't breathe.</p>

<p>A ringing filled my ears as my vision narrowed.</p>

<p>I didn't even have the presence of mind to process what I was seeing.</p>

<p>My head went completely blank.</p>

<p>Jang Seokyun came to stand beside me.</p>

<p>As casually as if we were discussing the weather, he spoke.</p>

<p>"Pro gamer Goyo was known for having a tremendous sense of responsibility, wasn't he?"</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>"I watched your match recordings. Truly remarkable. Especially your final match before retirement."</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>"Did you know?"</p>

<p>He looked at me.</p>

<p>"Han Goyo is a perfect fit for the Trial Response Agency."</p>

<p>"As though Heaven itself had sent you to us."</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>"Goyo."</p>

<p>"Captain Mo's younger sibling would've been twenty-four this year."</p>

<p>"The same age as you."</p>

<p>"But Captain Mo's sibling..."</p>

<p>"...will always remain twenty-four."</p>

<p>He slowly walked forward.</p>

<p>Resting a hand against the glass, he looked up at the sleeping Mo Haein.</p>

<p>After staring at her for a long moment, he turned back toward me.</p>

<p>"If she'd simply died, Captain Mo wouldn't be suffering like this."</p>

<p>"Do you see the meteorite embedded in her abdomen?"</p>

<p>"...Director."</p>

<p>"Captain Mo's younger sibling..."</p>

<p>"...is trapped inside it."</p>

<p>"Goyo."</p>

<p>"Every time Captain Mo looks at you..."</p>

<p>"...she probably wants to fall to her knees and beg."</p>

<p>"To beg you to save her sibling."</p>

<p>"Because you're the only hope capable of closing Trials."</p>

<p>"Director Jang Seokyun!"</p>

<p>He looked at me calmly.</p>

<p>I glared back at him.</p>

<p>Without the slightest trace of respect owed to the Director of the Trial Response Agency.</p>

<p>I couldn't tolerate him exposing Mo Haein's private tragedy just to keep hold of me.</p>

<p>What if this was something she'd never wanted me to know?</p>

<p>Swallowing the curse rising in my throat, I spoke.</p>

<p>"I understand what you're trying to say."</p>

<p>"That's enough."</p>

<p>"No!"</p>

<p>Jang Seokyun suddenly shouted.</p>

<p>His sharp eyes locked onto mine as he growled,</p>

<p>"No, you don't."</p>

<p>"If you did, you would've already enlisted in the Agency instead of weighing us against Paseong."</p>

<p>"...I—"</p>

<p>"You think Captain Mo is the only one in this situation?"</p>

<p>"Lieutenant Colonel Je Hyun-oh, Captain Kwak, Samra..."</p>

<p>"They all need you, Goyo."</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>"Will you abandon every one of the people placing their hopes in you?"</p>

<p>"The Special Mission Team that was created for your sake?"</p>

<p>He slowly approached me.</p>

<p>Then he took an item from his inventory and pressed it against my chest.</p>

<p>Lowering my gaze, I looked at it before slowly accepting it.</p>

<p>An official Trial Response Agency enlistment contract.</p>

<p>Not the ordinary paper agreement I'd signed as a trainee—</p>

<p>But a true contract created from a Trial item, capable of binding an Adaptee.</p>

<p>Looking straight into my eyes, Jang Seokyun whispered,</p>

<p>"I trust you'll make the wise choice, Trainee Han."</p>
