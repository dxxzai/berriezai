<h1>Chapter 20</h1>

<p>I froze for a moment, still holding the cloth, then awkwardly thanked her.</p>

<p>"Thank you."</p>

<p>Thinking back over my actions, I had to admit I'd charged in without any restraint.</p>

<p>But I didn't feel like I was overdoing it.</p>

<p>If anything, I felt a little... happy.</p>

<p>Of course, a peaceful daily life without any incidents or accidents is the best.</p>

<p>But this wasn't so bad for me either.</p>

<p>Probably because I got to explore the world of my favorite game in real life.</p>

<p>I'm even learning things I never knew from the game...</p>

<p>If it weren't for that quest where my limbs got cut off, I'd be even happier.</p>

<p>I didn't voice these thoughts out loud — Mo Haein would probably look at me like I was even more insane.</p>

<p>Instead of speaking my honest feelings, I busied myself wiping my face with the cloth she'd handed me.</p>

<p>Seeing the red staining the fabric, I could kind of understand why Mo Haein had been looking at me like I was out of my mind this whole time.</p>

<p>While I — who'd looked worse than the zombies — cleaned myself up, Mo Haein and Kwak Hanmuk consumed some rations.</p>

<p>Well, Mo Haein only had a little. Kwak Hanmuk had quite a lot.</p>

<p>After shoving an endless amount of snacks into his stomach, Kwak Hanmuk smacked his lips.</p>

<p>Despite eating that much, he didn't exactly look full.</p>

<p>"You can have more. There's plenty of places to find food."</p>

<p>"Nah... gotta watch my figure..."</p>

<p>Muttering something vague, Kwak Hanmuk crunched down on a lollipop as his last snack.</p>

<p>I looked away for a second, and when I glanced back, only the bare stick remained.</p>

<p>Squatting there, holding the lollipop stick between his fingers and staring blankly into space, Kwak Hanmuk caught my eye and grinned before standing up.</p>

<p>Then he tossed the stick on the ground and ground it under his foot.</p>

<p>It wasn't a cigarette — what was he doing with a lollipop stick? I had no idea.</p>

<p>While the three of us filled our hunger and thirst meters, Je Hyun-oh sat on top of the jeep.</p>

<p>Since he was already infected with the BT-Z virus, he often broke the Trial's rules.</p>

<p>In DeZonDeal, he didn't need to fill his hunger or thirst meters, and getting bitten by zombies didn't infect him.</p>

<p>He probably wouldn't have been able to equip the toy limbs if we'd entered HapFactory.</p>

<p>That was why Je Hyun-oh was waiting for us to finish eating.</p>

<p>Watching him silently look down at us from above, I felt a bit like an animal in a zoo.</p>

<p>Hope you're enjoying the show.</p>

<p>Having fully appreciated Je Hyun-oh's convenience, I wanted to stay on his good side.</p>

<p>But the moment our eyes met, Je Hyun-oh clacked his metal fingers like he was playing piano, so I immediately looked down.</p>

<p>Better to stay out of his sight as much as possible.</p>

<p>After the short meal and recovery, I packed only the essentials into my backpack.</p>

<p>Once the backpack was on my back, I checked the rooftop's HVAC system.</p>

<p>Fortunately, it wasn't damaged and was running smoothly on emergency power.</p>

<p>I began the briefing for entering the lab.</p>

<p>"First objective: find the surviving researchers."</p>

<p>The DeZonDeal laboratory was a long, horizontal building with two basement floors and five above-ground floors.</p>

<p>Unlike the National Institute of Infectious Diseases where the Trial had hatched, it wasn't divided into multiple research wings — everything was in one building.</p>

<p>The fifth floor was the most important. That's where the high-risk pathogen lab was located.</p>

<p>The Biosafety Level 4 (BSL-4) lab was the critical facility for researching the DeZonDeal zombie virus vaccine.</p>

<p>"Originally, the surviving researcher would be hiding on the third floor, but since the situation has changed, they're likely in a different location now."</p>

<p>In the game, the researcher — the lab's last survivor — is found hiding in a cleaning supply closet on the third floor.</p>

<p>The player gives the dying researcher food, clears out the zombies, and helps them reach the fifth-floor lab.</p>

<p>The researcher opens the locked lab and begins vaccine research.</p>

<p>That's when the delivery quests officially start.</p>

<p>But now, there were four surviving researchers.</p>

<p>It would be hard for all of them to hide together in a cramped closet.</p>

<p>"My guess is... they're probably already in the lab. The fifth floor."</p>

<p>Since Je Hyun-oh had swept through the zombies at the start, they'd likely grabbed food and barricaded themselves in to protect the most important lab.</p>

<p>They might have run into a hidden zombie in the process and suffered casualties.</p>

<p>But that was just speculation.</p>

<p>"So if we don't find them on the fifth floor, we check the third too?"</p>

<p>Mo Haein immediately added her opinion.</p>

<p>"Yes. Just in case. And finding the researchers is important, but we also need to check if the True Ending conditions are being met as soon as possible..."</p>

<p>Since that was something I had to verify myself, I offered the most efficient suggestion.</p>

<p>"How about we split into two teams and run operations simultaneously?"</p>

<p>"Good idea. We should listen to Goyo hyung-nim."</p>

<p>Following Kwak Hanmuk, Mo Haein also nodded in agreement.</p>

<p>Riding this momentum, I tried to subtly assign the teams.</p>

<p>Me and Captain Mo in one team!</p>

<p>I already had a plausible reason — we'd worked together in HapFactory.</p>

<p>Also, Kwak Hanmuk had means to subdue Je Hyun-oh if he did anything, so putting them in the same team made sense.</p>

<p>"Then the teams are..."</p>

<p>But I couldn't finish my sentence.</p>

<p>SCREECH!</p>

<p>A horrible sound of metal scraping rang out. Je Hyun-oh was scratching the jeep with his metal hand.</p>

<p>His black wings moved slowly.</p>

<p>The feathers rustled as they folded, and the wings disappeared behind his back.</p>

<p>He hopped down from the jeep, walked over to me, and stood by my side.</p>

<div class="hyunoh"><p>O O</p></div>

<p>It seemed like he meant to come with me...</p>

<p>I looked at Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein. They were already discussing how to search on their own.</p>

<p>Apparently, they were confident that since Je Hyun-oh had come to his senses and accepted me as a teammate, he wouldn't attack anymore.</p>

<p>They must have judged that he was better suited to protect me than they were, with their depleted stamina.</p>

<p>But there was something the Captains didn't know.</p>

<p>I'd brought up &lt;Zero&gt; with Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>I'd poked at what was basically his sore spot, but I couldn't exactly confess that to Mo Haein and Kwak Hanmuk.</p>

<p>I was already standing out by mentioning Trial strategies and items.</p>

<p>If I acted any more conspicuous, the tacit tolerance I had right now might shatter.</p>

<p>At least, thankfully, I didn't have to worry about Je Hyun-oh telling them about &lt;Zero&gt; or the System window.</p>

<p>If anything, Je Hyun-oh's problem was that he didn't say things even when he needed to.</p>

<p>He wouldn't go out of his way to bother revealing that 'Han Goyo' was something out of the ordinary.</p>

<p>He'll just deal with it himself the moment he decides I'm a threat.</p>

<p>Since he recognized me as a teammate, he'd probably cut me some slack, but if I crossed the line, he'd take care of me immediately.</p>

<p>I could feel it clearly from the way he didn't bother hiding his desire to kill me.</p>

<p>Maybe even now, he was coming along to find an opportunity to do it.</p>

<p>I hope I never have to use Execution on Lieutenant Colonel Je...</p>

<p>Burying my worries, I became Je Hyun-oh's teammate.</p>

<p>We set the fifth-floor lab as our rendezvous point.</p>

<p>Oblivious to my inner turmoil, Mo Haein and Kwak Hanmuk opened the rooftop door and disappeared down the emergency stairs to carry out the operation.</p>

<p>Now only Je Hyun-oh and I remained on the rooftop.</p>

<p>I turned to look at him. His goggles still displayed the wide-eyed emoticon.</p>

<p>He gave me the feeling he'd follow along with whatever I said.</p>

<p>"...So, we need to go to the second floor."</p>

<p>Getting from the rooftop to the second floor while fighting through Dead Zombies would take quite a while.</p>

<p>But with Je Hyun-oh here, there was no reason to go on foot.</p>

<p>Before I could even bring it up, Je Hyun-oh seemed to have the same idea.</p>

<p>He pulled a leather glove from his inventory.</p>

<p>As he placed the black glove over his left metal hand, a bright light shone and it transformed into the shape of a human hand.</p>

<p>So that's the Crow Gloves.</p>

<p>With the Crow Gloves on one hand, Je Hyun-oh looked a little less threatening.</p>

<p>He grabbed the back of my neck.</p>

<p>Then he sprinted across the rooftop... and jumped down without any warning at all.</p>

<p>The sensation of falling was dizzying.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh plummeted fast, then precisely slammed his metal hand into the wall at the second floor.</p>

<p>At the same time, he kicked through the window and hurled me inside.</p>

<p>"Ugh...!"</p>

<p>I nearly tumbled through broken glass and across the floor, but barely managed to catch my balance and stand up.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh, with one human hand and one metal hand dangling at his sides, calmly climbed through the window into the hallway.</p>

<p>Emergency lights were spaced sparsely along the corridor.</p>

<p>A long shadow stretched from Je Hyun-oh's feet.</p>

<p>"KREEEEH!"</p>

<p>Hearing the window shatter, Dead Zombies' eyes flashed as they came charging.</p>

<p>He gestured at me with his human hand — the one wearing the leather glove. It meant: stay back, they're annoying.</p>

<p>The moment I pressed myself flat against the wall, Je Hyun-oh swung his metal hand horizontally.</p>

<p>A whoosh — the sound of air splitting — and five lines appeared across the zombies' bodies.</p>

<p>Then came wet thuds. The horde of zombies collapsed into pieces.</p>

<p>I immediately forgot any disrespectful thoughts I'd harbored while being thrown to the floor.</p>

<p>"Thank you, Lieutenant Colonel Je."</p>

<p>His pros and cons were very clear. His personality was a bit much, but his skills were good — like a boss who let me clock out early.</p>

<p>With a grateful heart, I dutifully attended to my superior, who'd cleared out the zombies in seconds.</p>

<p>"This way, sir."</p>

<p>Our target was the Laboratory Director's Office.</p>

<p>On the way there, I briefly stopped in an office.</p>

<p>"Excuse me... could I ask for one very tiny, very small errand?"</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh stared at me as if to say 'go ahead.' I beamed at my superior officer.</p>

<p>"Food farming."</p>

<p>Since the third floor and above were mostly research facilities like analysis rooms and labs, food was usually most abundant on the first and second floors.</p>

<p>With the number of researchers' mouths to feed going from one to four, it was a good idea to stock up on delivery food while we were on the second floor anyway.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh's goggles lit up.</p>

<div class="hyunoh"><p>O K</p></div>

<p>Wait, those things could display text too...?</p>

<p>That was something I'd never known since Je Hyun-oh barely spoke in the game.</p>

<p>I wondered inwardly if it could display longer messages, then followed Je Hyun-oh into the office.</p>

<p>Unlike the hallway, the office was dark since the emergency lights weren't on, but that wasn't a big problem.</p>

<p>I knew exactly where the food was, and Je Hyun-oh could see in the dark.</p>

<p>"Third desk on the left, bottom drawer — chocolate bars. And the desk next to it, if you move the flower pot, there's a bag of jelly."</p>

<p>I just called out coordinates, and Je Hyun-oh swiftly gathered everything and tossed it into my backpack.</p>

<p>All I had to do was hold my backpack open in the dark and follow him around.</p>

<p>This is easy. So easy.</p>

<p>I'd been nervous when we were left alone, but he was much more cooperative than I expected, so things went smoothly.</p>

<p>I made full use of Je Hyun-oh's convenience.</p>

<p>We finished looting the office and were nearing the Laboratory Director's Office.</p>

<p>Grrrk... Grrrrk...</p>

<p>Faint zombie sounds. They must have woken up from the window shattering, but strangely, the noise sounded muffled — like they were trapped somewhere.</p>

<p>But there shouldn't be any trapped zombies on the second floor.</p>

<p>Something must have gone wrong somewhere.</p>

<p>The sound was coming from the direction of the Laboratory Director's Office, so I quickened my pace.</p>

<p>The closer I got, the more the zombie noises mixed with dull thuds of something hitting a surface, and the sound of someone crying.</p>

<p>And when I reached the Laboratory Director's Office...</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>"Goyo."</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh called my name.</p>

<p>His voice was rough and beastly, laced with amusement.</p>

<p>"Can you clear this?"</p>

<p>To prove my usefulness, I should have answered yes immediately.</p>

<p>But this time, I couldn't answer.</p>

<p>THUMP. THUMP. THUMP.</p>

<p>A zombie kept slamming its head against the reinforced glass door.</p>

<p>Its arms and legs were tied. Its mouth was gagged.</p>

<p>The only free part of its body — its head — kept ramming the glass door, and around its neck, a researcher's ID card dangled and swayed.</p>

<p>I stared blankly at the zombie, then slowly lowered my gaze.</p>

<p>The ID card with its name and photo came sharply into view.</p>

<div class="fieldset"><p>Yi Seon</p></div>

<p>There was no doubt.</p>

<p>They were the researcher who was supposed to develop the vaccine.</p>
