<h1>Chapter 26</h1>

<p>DeZonDeal's Laboratory had two bosses.</p>

<p>The first was the Security Guard Zombie you ran into the moment you entered the first-floor lobby.</p>

<p>The Security Guard Zombie — its body grotesquely swollen — had injected itself with illegal steroid-based drugs to build muscle while still human.</p>

<p>As zombification progressed, the residual drug compounds accelerated, leaving it looking no different from a monster.</p>

<p>The Security Guard Zombie boasted the highest attack power in DeZonDeal, but Je Hyun-oh had taken it out early on, so I never even saw its face.</p>

<p>The second was the Chef Zombie in the underground area.</p>

<p>The backstory was that a chef who'd fled to the Generator Room when the zombie outbreak hit had turned. Trapped in a space filled with 24-hour noise, it was more vicious and had sharper hearing than any other zombie.</p>

<p>The Chef Zombie was a time-attack boss — it kept slamming into the emergency generator, lowering its operational rate.</p>

<p>If you focused too much on delivery and delayed the strategy, it would completely destroy the generator, sending you straight to a bad ending.</p>

<p>Also, once the boss fight started, it attacked indiscriminately, so keeping its attention on you to stop it from wrecking the generator was crucial.</p>

<p>If repairs weren't completed within a set time after the fight began, the generator exploded, taking the entire underground area with it — game over.</p>

<p>And if you cut the boss's HP below half before repairs finished, that was also game over.</p>

<p>Because the Chef Zombie would self-destruct, and the impact would blow up the generator along with it.</p>

<p>Without information on the boss, you were guaranteed a game over.</p>

<p>I'd caught this damn Chef Zombie so many times I was sick of it.</p>

<p>So I was a little arrogant — thinking I could handle pretty much any variable that came up.</p>

<p>But the zombification recovering... that was a variable I'd never expected.</p>

<p>The moment I let out the breath I'd been holding, the Chef Zombie lunged.</p>

<p>"KYAAAAAH—!"</p>

<p>"Goyo!"</p>

<p>"Trainee!"</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein moved to react to the sudden outbreak, but I stopped them.</p>

<p>"I'm fine!"</p>

<p>I bit down and ran as I shouted.</p>

<p>"I'll... handle the boss! Throw me the bag!"</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk immediately yanked off the sling bag and hurled it.</p>

<p>The bag arced through the air and landed right in my arms.</p>

<p>I snatched it, and Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein ran for the spare parts box.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk slammed his body into the steel cabinet. He forced the door open and swept all the parts inside into his arms.</p>

<p>'If only you'd said useful things from the start.'</p>

<p>Then I would've read the System windows properly on my own, wouldn't I?</p>

<p>This time was no different.</p>

<p>Nothing helpful — just a bunch of nagging and nonsense about how I hadn't been reading.</p>

<p>I checked the Chef Zombie chasing me.</p>

<p>'Is it just me whose zombification recovered?'</p>

<p>Judging by how the Chef Zombie was targeting only me, it was certain.</p>

<p>I had no idea why it recovered. I hadn't done anything special after Mo Haein bit me.</p>

<p>The difference from Kwak Hanmuk was...</p>

<p>That I was the System.</p>

<p>That was the only thing I could think of.</p>

<p>Either way, now that I was recognized as a target, going back to being a zombie at this point wouldn't mean anything.</p>

<p>I had to face the Chef Zombie as a human.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<p>
You believe this is truly a life-or-death crisis.<br/>

But fortunately, there IS a way to overcome it.
</p>
</div>


<div class="systembox"><p>You wish to register 'Mo Haein' and 'Kwak Hanmuk' to fill all '3 slots' and elevate the System's ability by one level.</p></div>

<div class="systembox"><p>Would you like to register 'Mo Haein' and 'Kwak Hanmuk'?</p></div>

<p>Ah, seriously.</p>

<p>'This is exactly why I don't read them.'</p>

<p>The strange sensation I'd felt when registering Je Hyun-oh was still vivid.</p>

<p>No more registrations. Absolutely not.</p>

<p>I ignored the System window and ran, scanning the Generator Room.</p>

<p>The Generator Room was a wide space with a massive engine, but not spacious enough to play cat-and-mouse with the Chef Zombie for six minutes.</p>

<p>Not that I was that agile either.</p>

<p>If I'd still been a zombie, my physical abilities would've been better than now — a shame.</p>

<p>I yanked open the zipper of the sling bag and reached inside.</p>

<p>Then I pulled out the item Kwak Hanmuk had dutifully retrieved for me, even while asking why the hell I needed something like that.</p>

<p>A plastic jackstone.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Jackstone: Only use for playing jacks.</p></div></div>

<p>Rattle rattle.</p>

<p>The tiny beads inside the jackstone made a constant noise.</p>

<p>I threw one jackstone in the opposite direction from where I was running.</p>

<p>"KREEEEEK!"</p>

<p>The Chef Zombie let out a blood-curdling cry and immediately lunged toward the jackstone.</p>

<p>'Got it.'</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk had brought the right item.</p>

<p>The jackstone was a toy a kid at the Green Zone elementary school had been playing with.</p>

<p>The Chef Zombie always reacted to the small noise coming from the jackstone.</p>

<p>Also, when it picked up and swallowed the jackstone, it got stunned for three seconds.</p>

<p>Throwing the jackstone and running was the hidden strategy for the Chef Zombie.</p>

<p>But there were only five of them, so I couldn't waste them recklessly.</p>

<p>I had to use them efficiently while the emergency generator was being repaired.</p>

<p>"Krrrk... krrrr..."</p>

<p>The Chef Zombie picked up the jackstone with its decay-rotted hand.</p>

<p>It picked it up by instinct, but seemed to have no idea what to do with it.</p>

<p>The Chef Zombie stood there blankly for a moment, then shoved the jackstone into its mouth and swallowed.</p>

<p>While the Chef Zombie was briefly stunned, I climbed up a steel shelf where the pipes were organized.</p>

<p>I climbed onto the two-meter-high shelf and checked on the emergency generator.</p>

<p>Just then, the System window rose with a ding sound.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<p>
Emergency generator repair has begun!<br/>

Repairers: Blue Thunder, Sand. (Repairers cannot be changed mid-process.)<br/>

Current repair progress: 1%
</p>
</div>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein were sitting on opposite sides of the generator, peeling off metal panels.</p>

<p>After Mo Haein sprayed the Rust Remover Spray, she hurled the can with all her strength.</p>

<p>CLANG!</p>

<p>The spray can hit the chain-link fence on the opposite side.</p>

<p>Just then, the Chef Zombie — its stun wearing off and searching for me — immediately headed that way.</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>Mo Haein checked that I was safe with trembling eyes.</p>

<p>Then she immediately turned her head back.</p>

<p>She ran to Kwak Hanmuk's side and started loosening bolts with a wrench alongside him, swapping out the damaged wires by color.</p>

<p>Both of them moved calmly, following the repair method I'd explained in the briefing.</p>

<p>It looked like they could finish the repair within the six minutes I'd estimated.</p>

<p>Thanks to Mo Haein buying me a little time, I'd made it safely onto the steel shelf.</p>

<p>"KIYAAAAH!"</p>

<p>The Chef Zombie let out a horrible scream and slammed into the shelf with a THUD.</p>

<p>The impact was strong enough to send vibrations through the steel shelf, which was firmly bolted to the floor.</p>

<p>Keeping the Chef Zombie's attention on me was the most important thing.</p>

<p>I kept the repair progress System window open in the corner of my vision and waited for the Chef Zombie to climb up the shelf.</p>

<p>Then, when it was one step away, I started running.</p>

<p>As I ran along the steel shelves that stretched across the Generator Room like a track, the Chef Zombie chased after me.</p>

<p>Its apron — full of holes — flapped loudly as it ran.</p>

<p>Right before the shelf ended, I threw the second jackstone to the opposite side.</p>

<p>The Chef Zombie predictably turned its head toward the jackstone.</p>

<p>CRASH!</p>

<p>The Chef Zombie, chasing the jackstone, fell off the shelf.</p>

<p>It lay still for a few seconds from the shock of the unprepared fall, then — crack, crack — twisted its body upright.</p>

<p>Then it ran toward where the jackstone had rolled.</p>

<p>I checked its path, then glanced at the repair progress.</p>

<p>Mo Haein had circled back to the right side, wiping off rust water and loosening the inner bolts.</p>

<p>On the left, Kwak Hanmuk had finished connecting wires and was tightening the bolts one by one.</p>

<p>The heat blasting from the emergency generator's engine sent sweat streaming down Kwak Hanmuk's temples.</p>

<p>When the Chef Zombie returned to the steel shelf, I ran toward the generator's cooling fan this time.</p>

<p>The hot blast from the cooling fan was suffocating, but since it was the noisiest spot, being here could briefly confuse the Chef Zombie.</p>

<p>Sure enough, the Chef Zombie chasing me stumbled, losing its target for a moment.</p>

<p>For the next minute, the Chef Zombie would wander around searching for a target.</p>

<p>But after that, its aggro would reset and it'd start attacking the generator.</p>

<p>I had to act before the minute was up.</p>

<p>Having secured a brief safe zone, I counted silently and checked the chain-link fence on the opposite side.</p>

<p>Beyond the fence was another generator that had stopped working.</p>

<p>I kept my eyes on the Chef Zombie, and the moment its aggro reset, I threw the third jackstone.</p>

<p>Not too hard, not too soft — the jackstone slipped through the chain-link fence and landed in the perfect spot.</p>

<p>Just out of the Chef Zombie's reach.</p>

<p>The moment it heard the sound, the Chef Zombie lunged at the fence.</p>

<p>Its hands couldn't reach through the gaps, so it started tearing at the fence with brute force.</p>

<p>Snap. Snap. The chain-link fence broke, its sharp ends piercing the back of the zombie's hand.</p>

<p>Rotten fluid dripped from the pierced hand.</p>

<p>But zombies don't feel pain.</p>

<p>The Chef Zombie mangled its own hands tearing at the fence, finally grabbed the jackstone, and shoved it into its mouth.</p>

<p>I hadn't expected that.</p>

<p>In the game, the zombie only showed the behavior of tearing at the fence — it never told me it would get injured that badly.</p>

<p>Besides, it was dot graphics, so the information was limited.</p>

<p>'Being a chef, you'd think it'd need its hands.'</p>

<p>I had to change my plan slightly. I pulled out the pistol from the sling bag.</p>

<p>The Glock 19, a compact model, held fifteen rounds in the magazine.</p>

<p>But I only planned to use one.</p>

<p>The Chef Zombie didn't have high HP, so if I misused the gun and its HP dropped below half, it'd self-destruct.</p>

<p>A convenient but dangerous gamble.</p>

<p>I'd wanted to avoid using the gun if possible, but I had no choice if I wanted to subdue it.</p>

<p>I racked the slide and aimed at the Chef Zombie.</p>

<p>I'd been a crack shot in FPS games, but in reality, it'd be different.</p>

<p>I'd even been exempt from basic military training, so this was the first time in my life I'd actually held a gun.</p>

<p>The checkering on the grip felt rough against my palm.</p>

<p>Bitingly tense, I hooked my finger over the trigger.</p>

<p>The Chef Zombie growled and turned to look at me. It caught the sound of my breath and immediately lunged.</p>

<p>I waited calmly.</p>

<p>'When it's as close as possible.'</p>

<p>It was the best way to minimize aiming error.</p>

<p>The approaching zombie felt like slow motion.</p>

<p>Even though there was no way it was actually slow.</p>

<p>The moment I focused, I felt a hot heat behind my eyes.</p>

<p>The same heat I'd felt several times recently.</p>

<p>Even though it was a bizarre phenomenon, it didn't scare me.</p>

<em><p>If anything, it felt natural.</p></em>

<p>I pulled the trigger.</p>

<p>BANG!</p>

<p>The muzzle jerked up from the sudden recoil, and the shell casing flew to the side.</p>

<p>The first shot grazed the zombie's flesh, leaving only a long gash.</p>

<p>But I grinned.</p>

<p>I pulled the trigger again immediately.</p>

<p>This time, it didn't miss. The bullet pierced the kneecap.</p>

<p>"KIYAAAAK!"</p>

<p>The Chef Zombie lost its balance and collapsed. The sound of the shell casing hitting the floor rang out sharply.</p>

<p>I lowered the muzzle immediately. Any more attacks and it'd be in self-destruct range.</p>

<p>I let out a light breath and checked on the emergency generator.</p>

<p>But Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein weren't repairing.</p>

<p>They'd stopped, staring at me with tense expressions.</p>

<p>What?</p>

<p>I looked around, wondering if there was something near me.</p>

<p>But there was nothing.</p>

<p>Instead, I saw my own face reflected in the glass of the fire extinguisher cabinet.</p>

<p>A light carved clearly even through the dirty glass.</p>

<p>My pupils were glowing gold.</p>
