<h1>Chapter 46</h1>

<p>I don't remember exactly what I was thinking the moment Rabbit Butler spotted me.</p>

<p>My consciousness had been hazy for a while by then.</p>

<p>I think I was still feeling wronged up to that point.</p>

<p>I couldn't understand why no one would listen to me, why they had to cause such a massive disaster.</p>

<p>True Ending, my ass. Now we couldn't even get out of Clean3 peacefully. Tied up in the Bead Rosary, I agonized alone over how to salvage this mess.</p>

<p>Then the East Wing exploded, and I snapped back to reality.</p>

<p>"...?"</p>

<p>The moment I realized my mind was clear, I was met with debris flying in every direction.</p>

<p>Watching the building burst apart like a brilliant firework, I finally understood how abnormal my actions and thoughts had been up until now.</p>

<p>I'd firmly believed I was fine, but at some point I'd started prioritizing the mansion.</p>

<p>Even when looking for ways to reach the True Ending, I'd tried to choose options that benefited the mansion.</p>

<p>'There was no reason for that.'</p>

<p>Just gathering information about the Master would've been enough.</p>

<p>But I'd gone out of my way to include the part about feeding the Cleaners to the mansion.</p>

<p>I'd even felt loyalty toward it.</p>

<p>'Why would I?'</p>

<p>I liked Clean3, and I liked the butlers of Clean3, but not enough to swear loyalty.</p>

<p>I had no idea why it happened.</p>

<p>As I stood there confused, a System window blinked open before my eyes.</p>

<p>It appeared so urgently that the notification sound — ding — only rang after the window was already up.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>'Han Goyo (Roasted Chestnut)' has entered &lt;Clean! Clean! Clean!&gt;!</p></div></div>

<div class="systembox">
<div class="systembox_wrap glitch_t2">
<p>
◆&lt;Clean! Clean! Clean!&gt; M!!!AIN! QU!!E!!ST!!: !!!!ESCAPE!!!!<br/>

Escape!Run!Escape!Run!Escape!Run!Escape!Run!Escape!Run!Escape!Run!Escape!Run!Escape!Run!Escape!Run!CanYouRun?Escape!Run!Escape!Run!Escape!Run!
</p>
</div>
</div>

<p>It had been a while since I'd seen a System window.</p>

<p>Not that I was glad to see it. It was just spouting useless nonsense again, as usual.</p>

<p>'If I could, I would've done it already.'</p>

<p>If escape or running were possible, I'd have done it ages ago.</p>

<p>Giving me a quest to do the impossible.</p>

<p>'Throw in some exclamation marks and weird formatting and that's it? Disappointing.'</p>

<p>I silently cursed the System window, but no additional windows appeared.</p>

<p>It gave me a quest, but apparently no real solution.</p>

<p>I hadn't expected one anyway, so I just let it go.</p>

<p>I wanted to tell Captain Kwak that I was back to normal, but the Bead Rosary had my mouth sealed shut. There was nothing I could do.</p>

<p>Anxious, I watched the butlers and Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>The butlers were using a weapon I'd never seen before, firing a massive beam of light at Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>Even to the naked eye, that beam was clearly no joke — and yet Je Hyun-oh took it from both sides and survived.</p>

<p>His shattered Goggles rolled across the floor.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh's eyes flashed wildly. He was just as out of his mind as the butlers.</p>

<p>'We're screwed.'</p>

<p>At this rate, it wouldn't end until either Je Hyun-oh or the butlers died.</p>

<p>Even if Je Hyun-oh won, he'd take enough damage to be immobilized for a while.</p>

<p>And there was no guarantee the Captains would come out unscathed in the process.</p>

<p>With a large-scale battle like this, they could easily get caught in the crossfire and injured.</p>

<p>Honestly, "injured" was putting it lightly. If we were unlucky, they'd just die.</p>

<p>I thought and thought.</p>

<p>Even if I stepped in to mediate, there was no way Je Hyun-oh — let alone the butlers, whose mansion had just been destroyed — would listen to a word I said.</p>

<p>No matter how much I thought about it, I couldn't come up with a way to turn this around.</p>

<p>Except one.</p>

<p>I chose the last option, the one I really didn't want to take.</p>

<p>'If I fill all three Registration slots, will I get a useful ability?'</p>

<p>The moment it read my thoughts, the System window frantically popped up.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<p>
In the face of absolute crisis, you have had a positively genius idea!<br/>

Your judgment is beyond words — astonishing, magnificent, and ice-cold!<br/>

This is because proceeding with the 'Registration' you have been putting off will bring about remarkable progress.
</p>
</div>

<p>'What kind of ability will I get?'</p>

<div class="systembox">
<p>
You feel anxious, not knowing what the future holds.<br/>

However, I am certain that if you proceed with 'Registration' and fill all three slots, you will surely gain a good ability to resolve this predicament.
</p>
</div>

<p>It didn't seem to want to tell me. Or maybe it didn't know itself.</p>

<p>I hesitated one last time.</p>

<p>But there really was no other way.</p>

<p>'Fine. I'll do the Registration on the off chance it works.'</p>

<p>If filling all three slots, which it had been screaming about, didn't produce any meaningful change, I planned to permanently revoke the System window's right to speak.</p>

<p>I threatened it internally — saying I was giving it its first and last chance — and the System window, as if afraid I might change my mind, quickly threw up a square window.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Would you like to register 'Mo Haein' and 'Kwak Hanmuk'?</p></div>

<p>I hesitated for a moment.</p>

<p>I had no idea what would happen once I registered them, and doing it while tied up in the Bead Rosary felt a bit off.</p>

<p>But then the Rosary that had been sealing my mouth loosened and slid off.</p>

<p>I thought Captain Kwak had untied it for some reason, but my body was still bound.</p>

<p>"Captain Kwak..."</p>

<p>The moment I called him, he flinched — so it didn't seem intentional.</p>

<p>After Captain Kwak confirmed I was back to normal and I was safely released from the Bead Rosary prison, I started with an apology.</p>

<p>"I'm sorry, Captain Kwak."</p>

<p>It was a decision I had to make, but I still felt bad.</p>

<p>Especially because I had no desire to hold the Captains' lives in my hands.</p>

<p>I registered both of them at once.</p>

<p>'Register Mo Haein, Kwak Hanmuk.'</p>

<p>I thought it might take a while since there were two people, but the Registration completed instantly.</p>

<p>As if it had been waiting for this moment.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Registration complete!</p></div>

<p>A strange feeling washed over me.</p>

<p>It was different from when I'd registered Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>Back then, it felt like a kind of liberation.</p>

<p>Now, it felt like...</p>

<em><p>An omnipotent sensation, as if I could do anything.</p></em>

<div class="systembox"><p>'Han Goyo (Roasted Chestnut)' has completed the tutorial. A new name is granted.</p></div>

<div class="systembox"><p>'Han Goyo (Little Lamb)'</p></div>

<p>The System window cascaded open.</p>

<p>I stared at it, eerily calm.</p>

<p>A massive golden roulette appeared.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>You acquire a new ability.</p></div>

<p>The roulette began to spin.</p>

<p>It was fast enough that following it with my eyes was difficult.</p>

<p>But I'd never failed to draw what I wanted.</p>

<p>'What I need most right now.'</p>

<p>The roulette's needle stopped exactly on the slot I wanted.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>You have acquired 'Divine Protection'!</p></div>

<p>The moment I got the ability, I used it immediately.</p>

<p>'Divine Protection on Mo Haein, Kwak Hanmuk, Je Hyun-oh.'</p>

<p>A translucent veil of dazzling gold draped over Kwak Hanmuk.</p>

<p>He flinched at the sudden veil settling over his head.</p>

<p>The same golden veil covered Mo Haein, and Je Hyun-oh too.</p>

<p>As soon as Je Hyun-oh saw the veil falling over his head, he moved quickly, trying to dodge.</p>

<p>No chance.</p>

<p>'Prohibition on Je Hyun-oh.'</p>

<p>I immobilized him and forced the veil over his head.</p>

<p>Despite his resistance, once the veil was on, he went quiet.</p>

<p>He tried to touch the veil covering him with his metal hand.</p>

<p>But the veil, made of light, wouldn't be caught by metal.</p>

<p>His sharp metal blade sliced through the veil a couple of times — futilely.</p>

<p>The veil rippled on its own even though no wind was blowing, like an aurora, or a halo.</p>

<p>Once I'd cast Divine Protection on all three, the strength drained from my entire body.</p>

<p>The intense euphoria that had been dominating me vanished, leaving me with a slight chill.</p>

<p>My body swayed a little, but unlike last time, my nose wasn't bleeding and I was relatively okay.</p>

<p>Panting, I looked at the butlers.</p>

<p>'I wonder if it'll have any effect.'</p>

<p>There were buff-type abilities that temporarily increased the damage of registered individuals, but I'd chosen the defensive-type ability, 'Divine Protection.'</p>

<p>I couldn't very well give a buff to Je Hyun-oh, who was on the verge of rampaging, nor could I buff the Captains and send them into a dangerous fight.</p>

<p>Most of all, I didn't want to watch the butlers die right in front of me.</p>

<p>So I chose 'Divine Protection.'</p>

<p>My reasoning was that if their attacks were blocked by my ability, the butlers' attention would shift from Je Hyun-oh to me.</p>

<p>'At least I might get a chance to talk, even once.'</p>

<p>Of course, they might shoot a beam at me instead of talking.</p>

<p>But I was sure they wouldn't.</p>

<p>'Because they want something from me.'</p>

<p>Their reaction, however, was far from what I'd expected.</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>The butlers stopped attacking Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>They lowered their spear blades and just stared blankly at the golden veil.</p>

<p>Why?</p>

<p>At that moment, I felt a clear emotion from them.</p>

<p>Nostalgia.</p>

<p>It didn't fit them at all, and I couldn't understand it, but...</p>

<p>That's definitely what it felt like.</p>

<p>As their gazes lingered, the eerie aura radiating from them subsided.</p>

<p>Their floating bodies slowly descended to the ground.</p>

<p>A short spark flickered, and the spears in their hands vanished.</p>

<p>Rabbit Butler and Cat Butler, having completely dropped their combat stance, looked at me.</p>

<p>I was thrown off.</p>

<p>Because the 'chance to talk' I'd braced myself for was supposed to be against bloodthirsty butlers.</p>

<p>Facing butlers whose killing intent had completely drained away was completely unexpected.</p>

<p>With the butlers' hostility gone, the golden veil over Je Hyun-oh and the Captains faded and disappeared.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh tracked the fading traces of light with his eyes.</p>

<p>He, too, had gone quiet.</p>

<p>"...Butlers."</p>

<p>Neither Rabbit Butler nor Cat Butler said anything — they just stared at me — so I spoke first.</p>

<p>"I'll find the Master for you."</p>

<p>Their metal mouths parted, revealing the inside slightly.</p>

<p>With their mouths hanging open in a daze, I said it again.</p>

<p>"I'll find the Master and bring him home. So please let me and the Cleaners out."</p>

<p>Now that I'd regained my senses, I could judge the situation objectively.</p>

<p>The butlers had recognized me even as the mansion was being attacked.</p>

<p>Putting me above the mansion, which should have been their top priority — that couldn't be explained by simple favorability.</p>

<p>It meant I was connected to something more important than the mansion.</p>

<p>And to the butlers, there was only one thing more important than the mansion.</p>

<p>Their 'Master,' who hadn't come home for a long time.</p>

<p>An abnormal absence.</p>

<p>It was clear something had happened to whoever this Master was.</p>

<p>After a long search, the butlers had finally located their Master, but they were bound to the mansion and couldn't leave.</p>

<p>They couldn't just ask anyone for help, not knowing what had happened to their Master.</p>

<p>And then I appeared.</p>

<p>Someone the butlers could trust, with the ability and freedom to find their Master.</p>

<p>From the butlers' perspective, they must have decided they absolutely couldn't let me go.</p>

<p>"...Can you really find him?"</p>

<p>Rabbit Butler asked.</p>

<p>I answered without hesitation.</p>

<p>"Yes."</p>

<p>Though there was a slight flaw in that answer.</p>

<p>I could find him, but I hadn't set a deadline for 'by when.'</p>

<p>I was going to keep entering new Trials, so eventually I might reach a Trial I hadn't played in Archive's games.</p>

<p>If the butlers' Master was there, I could tell him to go home.</p>

<p>It was a vague, distant future.</p>

<p>But either way, the mansion and the butlers were beings that would disappear once the Trial closed.</p>

<p>Even if I never ended up meeting their Master, there would be no way to verify the promise.</p>

<p>My promise was essentially a white lie.</p>

<p>Still, there was one reason I made it sound like I'd definitely keep it.</p>

<p>Because in our final moments, I wanted to part ways seeing them with hope — at least a little — instead of anger and curses.</p>

<p>"...It was the same when we first met, but you."</p>

<p>Cat Butler gave a faint smile.</p>

<p>"You never cease to amaze us."</p>

<p>The butlers approached me.</p>

<p>Then, in front of me, they knelt on one knee.</p>

<p>"We will not forget this kindness."</p>

<p>"For as long as the universe exists. Eternally."</p>

<p>Cat Butler held out a ring to me.</p>

<p>The moment I took it, a System window appeared.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<p>
&lt;Clean! Clean! Clean!&gt; Cleared!<br/>

&#45; Eternal Memory Ending.
</p>
</div>

<p>I knew it instinctively.</p>

<p>This was the True Ending.</p>

<p>With a heavy heart, I looked at the butlers.</p>

<p>At those who would soon disappear.</p>

<p>But.</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>Why.</p>

<p>Why?</p>

<p>I hurriedly looked around.</p>

<p>There was nothing.</p>

<p>By the time I noticed, there was only darkness.</p>

<p>Everything had vanished. But the butlers remained.</p>

<p>Something was wrong.</p>

<p>A chill raced through my body.</p>

<p>"Roasted Chestnut. No — should I call you Little Lamb now?"</p>

<p>Rabbit Butler slowly stretched his mouth into a grin.</p>

<p>"You'll definitely be able to find the Master."</p>

<p>Cat Butler grinned the same way.</p>

<p>"We'll be waiting for the day you return home with the Master."</p>

<p>"I'll be waiting, Little Lamb."</p>

<p>The world, filled with darkness, melted away into neon.</p>

<p>I was pushed into a new Trial.</p>
