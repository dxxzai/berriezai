<h1>Chapter 87</h1>

<p>My heart pounded at the arrogant remark.</p>

<p>Before I knew it, my mouth almost opened to greet him, but I barely held myself back.</p>

<p>Ever since I learned that the world of Archive was real, I'd imagined meeting my favorite character.</p>

<p>Hello, I'm Han Goyo, a trainee at the Trial Response Agency. I've heard a lot about you, CEO Cha. It's a pleasure to finally meet you. Could I perhaps get your autograph...? Ah, I figured not. Yes. Sorry.</p>

<p>I'd imagined something ordinary like that.</p>

<p>I never imagined our first meeting would be like this.</p>

<p>But the moment I met someone I'd only ever seen in pixel graphics in person was overwhelming.</p>

<p>Half-dazed, I watched my favorite character, alive and moving before me.</p>

<p>“......”</p>

<p>Receiving my gaze, CEO Cha silently looked back at me for a moment.</p>

<p>Then he lightly jumped down from the vehicle.</p>

<p>Standing upright, the man was tall.</p>

<p>I knew he was tall, but seeing him in person made him seem even taller.</p>

<p>He looked only a little shorter than Captain Kwak Hanmuk, so he had to be around 190 centimeters.</p>

<p>He had a lean build, and with the tracksuit he was wearing, he looked a bit like a mixed martial arts fighter.</p>

<p>'If he stood next to Captain Kwak, they'd look like a gathering of athletes.'</p>

<p>While I was busily looking him over, the metallic click of a weapon snapped me back to reality.</p>

<p>CEO Cha had pulled a large cannon-like firearm from his inventory and rested it on his shoulder.</p>

<p>At the center of the gun was a transparent chamber where blue electrical currents swirled.</p>

<p>Startled, I slapped Samra's shoulder.</p>

<p>“Samra!”</p>

<p>The item CEO Cha had taken out was an EMP Shockwave Cannon.</p>

<p>I'd used it in Archive before.</p>

<p>It released an immense electromagnetic pulse in an instant, overloading and melting the internal circuitry of machines.</p>

<p>It was a deadly weapon against an android like Samra.</p>

<p>I was about to say we needed to run immediately, but before I could speak, Samra answered first.</p>

<p>“There is no need for concern, Han Goyo-ssi.”</p>

<p>Even with the muzzle pointed directly at him, he didn't move an inch.</p>

<p>“A primitive method like that cannot damage me.”</p>

<p>The most advanced thing I knew was an EMP Shockwave Cannon, so hearing him call it primitive left me speechless.</p>

<p>Samra's holographic eyes and hair shimmered brilliantly.</p>

<p>Even when standing still, his holographic colors emitted a gentle glow, but now they blazed openly, illuminating the surroundings.</p>

<p>Taking careful aim at Samra, CEO Cha pulled the trigger.</p>

<p>Kiiiiiiing!</p>

<p>With a piercing high-frequency whine, the blue current swirling inside the chamber compressed into a single point.</p>

<p>Facing a situation where he could become nothing more than a heap of scrap metal at any moment, Samra spoke in an emotionless voice.</p>

<p>“From this moment, I will generate an inverse-phase electromagnetic field for fifteen minutes and thirty-seven seconds.”</p>

<p>The next instant, a blinding blue flash erupted from the muzzle.</p>

<p>A massive electromagnetic pulse burst forth, ionizing the atmosphere into plasma.</p>

<p>At the same time, a transparent wave spread outward from Samra.</p>

<p>The space along its advancing boundary warped for an instant, rippling like a heat haze.</p>

<p>An invisible sphere collided with the EMP in midair.</p>

<p>There was no explosion.</p>

<p>Instead, something even stranger occurred.</p>

<p>The space where they met twisted and shimmered, like oil spreading across the surface of water.</p>

<p>The enormous electromagnetic energy—powerful enough to paralyze an entire city—melted away the moment it touched Samra's transparent sphere and vanished as though it had never existed.</p>

<p>As I stared with my mouth hanging open, Samra explained what had just happened.</p>

<p>“It is destructive interference. Opposing waveforms cancel one another.”</p>

<p>“Huh?”</p>

<p>When I failed to understand immediately, he offered a much simpler comparison.</p>

<p>“It operates on the same principle as noise cancellation. I generated a waveform opposite to the noise.”</p>

<p>That made a little more sense.</p>

<p>But after hearing the rest of the explanation, I was completely lost again.</p>

<p>“To counter an instantaneous EMP with inverse-phase waves, the frequency, waveform, and output velocity must all be analyzed in real time. I performed several billion calculations per second to perfectly analyze the EMP waveform and generated an inverse-phase wave with a synchronization rate of one hundred percent.”</p>

<p>It somehow felt like Samra was showing off a little.</p>

<p>Though maybe that was just my imagination... or maybe it wasn't.</p>

<p>Either way, it was obvious he'd done something incredible.</p>

<p>I'd been nothing more than a spectator on his back this whole time, so I praised him.</p>

<p>“That's amazing. I had no idea you could do something like that. Your explanations are really easy to understand too.”</p>

<p>You're like a premium AI.</p>

<p>Not the free version—the kind you have to pay hundreds of thousands of won a month to subscribe to.</p>

<p>I wondered if calling an android a premium AI counted as an insult, so I kept that thought to myself.</p>

<p>Not long afterward, Samra's inverse-phase field completely swallowed the EMP and dominated everything within a one-kilometer radius.</p>

<p>Then an astonishing sight unfolded.</p>

<p>Every surrounding light and electronic display left behind a bluish afterimage before going dark.</p>

<p>At the same time, a silence as sharp as a blade settled over the area.</p>

<p>Every electronic device had stopped, and all the faint background noises disappeared at once.</p>

<p>It felt as though I'd sunk deep underwater.</p>

<p>An oppressive silence made my ears ring.</p>

<p>My heartbeat and breathing sounded unnaturally loud, and I felt slightly dizzy.</p>

<p>I hadn't said a word, yet Samra immediately assessed my condition.</p>

<p>“Because the human nervous system operates through minute electrical signals, humans experience an instinctive sense of danger while inside an inverse-phase electromagnetic field. I will relocate us immediately.”</p>

<p>“Where are you going, Samra?”</p>

<p>CEO Cha casually tossed the EMP cannon behind him.</p>

<p>Then he tapped the white scabbard at his waist with a finger.</p>

<p>“You came all the way here to see me. At least let us say hello.”</p>

<p>My gaze was drawn to his sword.</p>

<p>'Daeil Yeorae!'</p>

<p>The white sword whose name meant Great Sun Tathāgata had an unforgettable item description.</p>

<p>I recalled what it had said in the game.</p>
<div class="bigbox2_pixel">
<p>Daeil Yeorae: Though the sun of the mortal world sets into darkness, the light of Daeil Yeorae knows neither day nor night.</p>
</div>

<p>He might have failed to turn the android into scrap metal, but CEO Cha was still an opponent no one could afford to underestimate.</p>

<p>If Lieutenant Colonel Je was the unrivaled master of large-scale battles against multiple enemies, then CEO Cha was the absolute pinnacle of one-on-one combat.</p>

<p>His fighting style specialized in explosive burst damage delivered in an instant.</p>

<p>And Daeil Yeorae was the perfect foundation for that overwhelming power.</p>

<p>The white sword seemed as though it had been forged for CEO Cha alone...</p>

<p>“Han Goyo-ssi.”</p>

<p>“...Yes?”</p>

<p>“We're moving.”</p>

<p>Hadn't Samra already said that a moment ago?</p>

<p>I wondered why he was repeating himself.</p>

<p>Only then did I notice the looks on Won Taebeom's and CEO Cha's faces.</p>

<p>Won Taebeom had been carefully watching the exchange between Samra and CEO Cha without letting his guard down.</p>

<p>But now he was staring at me with an even more baffled expression than when he'd first seen me riding on Samra's back.</p>

<p>He wasn't someone who showed much emotion.</p>

<p>For him to look at me like that...</p>

<p>'Did I spend too much time staring at CEO Cha?'</p>

<p>CEO Cha's expression looked similar.</p>

<p>Though there was one difference...</p>

<p>He seemed to be in a slightly better mood.</p>

<p>I'd honed my ability to read expressions by observing Lieutenant Colonel Je's goggles and gas mask, so I was fairly confident in my judgment.</p>

<p>Shrrng.</p>

<p>CEO Cha drew Daeil Yeorae.</p>

<p>The dazzling white blade emerged.</p>

<p>Surrounded by darkness after every other light had vanished, the sword alone shone brilliantly.</p>

<p>It was breathtaking.</p>

<p>“Looks like Han Goyo has a lot he wants to say to me.”</p>

<p>Hearing that, I hurriedly tried to explain myself to Samra.</p>

<p>“No, Samra. I was just fascinated for a moment.”</p>

<p>But the android who knew every one of my biological signals didn't even respond.</p>

<p>Instead, he addressed Won Taebeom.</p>

<p>“Lieutenant Won Taebeom. I'm entrusting Han Goyo-ssi to you.”</p>

<p>Samra handed me over.</p>

<p>Now I found myself riding on someone else's back.</p>

<p>Even if I'd only just awakened after sleeping for so long, it was still incredibly embarrassing for a grown man to keep getting carried around on other people's backs.</p>

<p>Thanks to Samra carrying me this whole time, I felt much better now.</p>

<p>“I can walk.”</p>

<p>But Samra immediately brought me back to reality.</p>

<p>“That is impossible. Han Goyo-ssi is currently experiencing paralysis of the lower body. Full recovery is expected to require at least one more day.”</p>

<p>So that's why I couldn't put any strength into my legs.</p>

<p>Everything suddenly made sense.</p>

<p>Dominic, perched atop my head, gently admonished me as well.</p>

<p>“Roasted Chestnut. It is also important to know how to accept the service of your subordinates.”</p>

<p>There wasn't anyone here who ranked below me, but I nodded as though I understood.</p>

<p>CEO Cha let out a crooked smile.</p>

<p>“The Trial Response Agency still works people to the bone, I see.”</p>

<p>Samra's face became expressionless.</p>

<p>Even when he stopped pretending to be human, CEO Cha showed not the slightest hint of unease.</p>

<p>Slowly, he turned his head and fixed his gaze on me.</p>

<p>“Han Goyo. Come to Paseong.”</p>

<p>Really?</p>

<p>...I almost answered that before barely stopping myself.</p>

<p>CEO Cha was famous not only for his terrible personality but also for his deep distrust of people.</p>

<p>There were only a handful of individuals he truly trusted.</p>

<p>Because of that, I'd heard that every mercenary recruited by Paseong was scouted by the company's two directors—his closest confidants.</p>

<p>I was probably the first person in Paseong's history to receive a recruitment offer directly from its CEO.</p>

<p>It really seemed like I was incredibly useful to him.</p>

<p>'Would it really hurt just to hear why he wants me so badly?'</p>

<p>Of course I couldn't trust CEO Cha.</p>

<p>I wasn't going to sign with Paseong.</p>

<p>Maybe I'd just hear him out for a moment...</p>

<p>“CEO Cha. I can no longer permit further conversation.”</p>

<p>A holographic-colored mist flowed from Samra.</p>

<p>Within the dreamlike mist, Samra tore away both sleeves of his uniform.</p>

<p>Smooth artificial skin was revealed as the synthetic flesh imitating a human body split apart along geometric lines.</p>

<p>Mechanical components writhed like living creatures, rearranging themselves into silver gun barrels.</p>

<p>His left arm became a railgun.</p>

<p>His right arm transformed into a plasma cannon.</p>

<p>Even then, Samra took the time to explain.</p>

<p>“I selected relatively simple weapon systems in order to simultaneously operate nanomachines and inverse-phase waves.”</p>

<p>In other words, he was saying he could transform into something much cooler, but since he had too many things to manage right now, he'd settled for an early-model configuration.</p>

<p>'Didn't you say you only had defensive combat functions?'</p>

<p>Apparently his definition of defense differed somewhat from a human's.</p>

<p>Perhaps noticing my expression, Samra added,</p>

<p>“Because of the condition that I must not harm humanity, a significant portion of my functions has been restricted. Completely defending against CEO Cha would be difficult, but I estimate I can buy sufficient time.”</p>

<p>Looking directly at CEO Cha, Samra declared,</p>

<p>“Lieutenant Colonel Je Hyun-oh is returning. I will defend this position until then.”</p>

<p>Whiiiiing.</p>

<p>Holographic energy began gathering inside the plasma cannon.</p>

<p>Samra gave his order.</p>

<p>“Lieutenant Won Taebeom. Withdraw immediately.”</p>

<p>Boom!</p>

<p>The instant those words fell, brilliant white light collided with holographic radiance.</p>

<p>Samra had intercepted CEO Cha as he rushed toward me.</p>

<p>Their exchange unfolded so quickly it was difficult to follow with the naked eye.</p>

<p>The white sword cleanly split apart an energy shell that Samra had fired by twisting his arm into a shape no human body could possibly achieve.</p>

<p>Fragments of energy scattered in every direction as CEO Cha charged straight toward him.</p>

<p>The barrel of Samra's left-arm railgun spun rapidly, unleashing a barrage of electromagnetic projectiles.</p>

<p>The bullets tore through the air toward CEO Cha's chest.</p>

<p>Yet CEO Cha casually twisted his body in midair, deflected the rounds with his blade, and immediately swung the white sword.</p>

<p>The swarm of nanomachines surrounding Samra instantly shifted form.</p>

<p>The holographic mist condensed into a defensive barrier composed of interconnected hexagons.</p>

<p>Boom!</p>

<p>The barrier and the white blade collided, sending showers of sparks cascading like a waterfall.</p>

<p>The dim surroundings became bright as daylight for an instant.</p>

<p>I wanted to keep watching the battle between CEO Cha and Samra, but Won Taebeom started running, and the distance between us naturally widened.</p>

<p>Glancing back over my shoulder, I finally understood why I'd felt those tremors back in the underground passage.</p>

<p>The side of the Trial Response Agency building had been completely torn open.</p>

<p>I'd asked Samra what kind of urban warfare he thought would happen in Seoul.</p>

<p>He had already foreseen this outcome.</p>

<p>“Trainee Han.”</p>

<p>At the blunt voice, I looked at the back of Won Taebeom's head.</p>

<p>“Are you planning to remain with the Trial Response Agency?”</p>

<p>If I answered no, it felt like he might throw me onto the ground, so I gave a vague reply.</p>

<p>“For now... I think so.”</p>

<p>“Do you know how to handle a firearm?”</p>

<p>“I know the basics.”</p>

<p>I didn't bother mentioning that the first time I'd fired one was in DeZonDeal.</p>

<p>“My apologies.”</p>

<p>Won Taebeom shifted me from his back to the front, supporting me with one arm.</p>

<p>His strength matched his muscular physique.</p>

<p>He effortlessly repositioned me, handed me a pistol, and then took an item from his inventory for his now-free hand.</p>

<p>The silver gauntlet looked like something a medieval knight would wear.</p>

<p>It was Won Taebeom's weapon item, Paladin's Oath.</p>

<p>After equipping the gauntlet, Won Taebeom stared ahead.</p>

<p>The night covered by Samra's inverse-phase field was unnaturally silent.</p>

<p>Yet I immediately sensed what Won Taebeom was watching for.</p>

<p>Under the moonlight, two figures slowly emerged from the shadows.</p>

<p>A man wearing glasses and a woman with her hair tied in a high ponytail.</p>

<p>Both wore office attire, making them look at first glance like ordinary employees working late into the night.</p>

<p>If not for the Trial items they carried in their hands.</p>

<p>Holding a sword lined with teeth, the man smiled amiably and greeted us with the polished tone of a salesman.</p>

<p>“Lieutenant Won. It's been a while. You're looking as impressive as ever.”</p>

<p>The woman carrying a gun covered in blooming flowers widened her eyes innocently, as though she wasn't holding a weapon at all.</p>

<p>“Lieutenant, you're working awfully late. Shouldn't you clock out already?”</p>

<p>“Move.”</p>

<p>“Haha, we'd love to. But what can we do? When the boss tells us to jump, we jump.”</p>

<p>“We only need a few minutes to talk with Goyo-nim.”</p>

<p>The woman smiled brightly.</p>

<p>“If Goyo-nim just signs the contract, we'll send him right back.”</p>

<p>They were Seo Moonchan and Mok Saehwa, the two directors of Paseong and CEO Cha's closest confidants.</p>
