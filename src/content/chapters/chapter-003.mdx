<h1>Chapter 3</h1>

<p>'Maybe Smiley could help with recovery too, since it's a toy.'</p>

<p>It was a kind of insurance.</p>

<p>I was in the middle of fulfilling the true ending conditions — I couldn't afford to make enemies of the toys.</p>

<p>"What are you doing?"</p>

<p>"Ah, sorry. Just tying my shoelaces."</p>

<p>I hurried after Park Seonggyeon, glancing back.</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>The candy on Smiley's palm was still there.</p>

<p>The only difference was that the mechanical sound had stopped.</p>

<p>Looked like it hadn't done anything.</p>

<p>We passed a long corridor painted with murals introducing HapFactory's toys and came to a large plastic door.</p>

<p>Dozens of small levers in various colors lined the walls and ceiling on both sides of the door.</p>

<p>"Lieutenant Park. Right side."</p>

<p>Mo Haein reached out with her forceps. A plastic chain unspooled with a rattle and grabbed a lever on the ceiling.</p>

<p>She pulled down the colored levers on the left wall and ceiling in rapid succession.</p>

<p>Park Seonggyeon took the right wall and ceiling, lowering his levers.</p>

<p>Normally you'd look at the color pattern on the floor to figure out which levers to pull, but they'd already memorized the pattern. They were clearly familiar with HapFactory's layouts.</p>

<p>Click. The last lever fell into place, cheerful music played, and the plastic door swung open.</p>

<p>Mo Haein and Park Seonggyeon strode inside without hesitation.</p>

<p>This was where players started their first shift as factory workers.</p>

<p>Sorting toys that came down the conveyor belt — an easy task for a first job.</p>

<p>But unlike the two leading the way, I slowed down.</p>

<p>Because.</p>

<p>"C-Captain."</p>

<p>"...The pattern's broken."</p>

<p>Clank. Clank. Clank. The lights came on from the outside in, gradually revealing the dark interior.</p>

<p>The music that wrapped around the space was cheerful but somehow sinister.</p>

<p>The toys that should have been spinning quietly on the conveyor belt were all on the floor.</p>

<p>Staring at Mo Haein and Park Seonggyeon with their glossy eyes.</p>

<p>Ding!</p>

<p>A notification sound rang out as a system window appeared.</p>

<div class="systembox">
    <div class="systembox_wrap">
        <p>
            ◆Main Quest: Survive the Murderous Toys.<br />
            The toys will not forgive the adult who attacked Smiley.<br />
            You decide you must survive, evading the enraged toys.
        </p>
    </div>
</div>

<p>'Why'd you have to go and beat up Smiley...'</p>

<p>I swallowed a sigh internally and crept toward the safe zone.</p>

<p>The Trial Response Agency, being a government organization, applied strict rules to Trials.</p>

<p>The most important rule was 'no unnecessary contact with NPCs.'</p>

<p>It was to minimize the risk of viral infection through contact.</p>

<p>The Trials that came from space carried a peculiar virus.</p>

<p>If infected, the body would mutate and develop a strong aggression toward humans.</p>

<p>Mo Haein, a soldier of the Trial Response Agency, followed the rules meticulously.</p>

<p>Today was probably the first time she'd preemptively attacked an NPC. She might have expected some story changes, but not to this extent.</p>

<p>The route Mo Haein was currently on was the Collapse Ending route — the hardest difficulty.</p>

<p>By attacking Smiley, the first NPC to appear, she'd made every single toy in the factory hostile to the players.</p>

<p>The players would be attacked by toys in every stage, and at the end, they'd escape the burning factory for the finale.</p>

<p>The text that appeared at the end of the Collapse Ending was especially chilling.</p>
<div class="bigbox2_pixel">

<p>&lt;Happy Smile Factory&gt; has collapsed.<br />
Smiley was hacked to pieces and thrown into the flames, but even so, it could not die.<br />
Smiley will become a terrible nightmare and come for the player again...</p>
</div>

<p>I still remembered that ending vividly.</p>

<p>'But the Collapse Ending route connects to the True Ending route, too.'</p>

<p>If I played it right, I could maintain my position as a clueless civilian and still see HapFactory's true ending.</p>

<p>As I recalled the Collapse Ending, a child's voice rang out over the music as a guide announcement.</p>

<p><br />
<em></em></p>

<p><em>"It's the adult who bullied Smiley. A-dult. The boring adult."</em></p>

<p><br />
</p>

<p>Hundreds of toys stared at Mo Haein and Park Seonggyeon, swaying their bodies in time with the music like they were warming up.</p>

<p>Left, right, left, left, right...</p>

<p><br />
<em></em></p>

<p><em>"We want to make the adult fun."</em></p>

<p><em>"But already adult. A-dult. A-a-a-dult. No. Don't like. Not fun."</em></p>

<p><em>"Aha!"</em></p>

<p><br />
</p>

<p>Cymbals crashed and children cheered.</p>

<p><br />
<em></em></p>

<p><em>"We found the answer. Make the adult small!"</em></p>

<p><em>"Small. Small. Small. Like a child. Like a-a-a-a child. Small. Small."</em></p>

<p><em>"Pieces. Pieces. Pieces. Pieces. Pieces."</em></p>

<p><br />
</p>

<p>Mo Haein shouted.</p>

<p>"Civilian!"</p>

<p>Park Seonggyeon, who'd been swaying his head left and right like the toys, snapped back to attention.</p>

<p>He swore and reached out with his forceps, grabbing my waist and lifting me onto the conveyor belt.</p>

<p>"Stay put if you don't want to die!"</p>

<p>Meaning he wouldn't save me if something happened.</p>

<p>"Yes sir!"</p>

<p>I answered Park Seonggyeon with vigor and swallowed a sigh internally.</p>

<p>The conveyor belt registered players as toys. It was a safe zone used to avoid attacks in HapFactory.</p>

<p>But there was something neither Park Seonggyeon nor Mo Haein knew.</p>

<p>On the Collapse route, the conveyor belt was useless.</p>

<p><br />
<em></em></p>

<p><em>"Turning the adult into a child, staaaart!"</em></p>

<p><br />
</p>

<p>The guide announcement ended with the sound of children's laughter. The toys that had been swaying shot up like popcorn and lunged.</p>

<p>Mo Haein extended a toy arm toward the ceiling.</p>

<p>Hanging from a pipe running across the ceiling, she sliced the air with her human hand.</p>

<p>A blade made of obsidian appeared.</p>

<p>Mo Haein's item — the Black Moon Blade.</p>

<p>The Trial Response Agency recommended clearing a Trial using only items obtained from that Trial.</p>

<p>Using items from other Trials had a high chance of causing variables.</p>

<p>But this was an emergency, so she'd pulled out the Black Moon Blade.</p>

<p>'Whoa...!'</p>

<p>Even in the midst of the tension, I was awed internally.</p>

<p>I was seeing Mo Haein's Black Moon Blade with my own eyes. Right in front of me.</p>

<p>I watched her fight like I was spellbound.</p>

<p>Park Seonggyeon swept toys away with his forceps while shining a flashlight on them with his human hand.</p>

<p>The toys caught in the light stopped moving for a moment.</p>

<p>Mo Haein didn't miss the opening. She cut the toys down with the Black Moon Blade.</p>

<p>The toys sliced by the long blade screamed sounds like children's cries and bled rainbow-colored blood.</p>

<p>I wanted to keep watching, but unfortunately, I didn't have time.</p>

<p>I tore my eyes away from Mo Haein with effort. Then I ran across the conveyor belt, dodging the lunging toys.</p>

<p>I had no items, but avoiding attacks wasn't hard. I knew the attack patterns.</p>

<p>The way the toys swayed their bodies when the music first started — that was the hint for the attack pattern.</p>

<p>Having seen every ending in HapFactory, I had all the patterns memorized like the rotten veteran I was.</p>

<p>I sprinted across the conveyor belt, quickly scanning the toys rampaging all around me.</p>

<p>New toys were being added from the production pipes — it was a total mess.</p>

<p>But finding what I needed wasn't hard.</p>

<p>A 15-centimeter mass-produced Smiley doll. Black Edition.</p>

<p>Among all the garish primary colors and fluorescent toys, the pitch-black doll was hard to spot.</p>

<p>But it was essential — I had to get it for the true ending.</p>

<p>I reached my arm through the toys.</p>

<p>"Ghk...!"</p>

<p>A hot sensation grazed my arm. An apron-wearing pony doll had cut my forearm with a plastic kitchen knife.</p>

<p>But I managed to snatch the black Smiley.</p>
<div class="systembox">
<div class="systembox_wrap">
<p>Smiley Black Edition: The one and only Sad Smiley in the world.</p>
</div>
</div>

<p>I ran across the conveyor belt again and arrived at a pile of package boxes stacked like scenery behind the production machines.</p>

<p>Among the boxes packaging various toys, one was particularly large.</p>

<p>It was the package box that should have contained the NPC Smiley.</p>

<p>Now it was empty, thanks to Mo Haein attacking Smiley earlier.</p>

<p>The box was meant for a meter-tall doll, so it could fit a full-grown adult.</p>

<p>I crammed myself into the package box.</p>

<p>It looked a bit pathetic...</p>

<p>It was fine when it was dot graphics, but actually doing it was a little embarrassing.</p>

<p>Anyway, settled in the package box, I watched Mo Haein and Park Seonggyeon through the transparent vinyl.</p>

<p>They were almost done cleaning up the toys.</p>

<p>If I delayed any longer here, the true ending might be out of reach. I quickly checked the Smiley doll.</p>

<p>Even though every second counted, I froze.</p>

<p>'It's smiling?'</p>

<p>The Sad Smiley doll was smiling.</p>

<p>Like its name, Sad Smiley was supposed to have a crying expression.</p>

<p>But there was no way I'd grabbed the wrong doll.</p>

<p>In this garish toy factory, Sad Smiley was the only achromatic toy.</p>

<p>As I examined the doll, I noticed my blood had gotten on it.</p>

<p>When my forearm was cut, blood had splattered — but the black color hid it, so I hadn't noticed.</p>

<p>I wiped the blood off belatedly, but Sad Smiley was still smiling.</p>

<p>It was suspicious, but I had no time to deliberate.</p>

<p>I pressed Sad Smiley's belly. A recorded, melancholy child's voice came out.</p>

<p><br />
<em></em></p>

<p><em>"Please make Sad Smiley happy."</em></p>

<p><br />
</p>

<p>In the original game, two choices would appear.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_pixel">
<p>—Ignore it.<br />
—Ask what to do.</p>
</div>

<p>But there were no choices here, so I had to say it out loud.</p>

<p>"What do I have to do to make you happy?"</p>

<p>Then Sad Smiley would give me a clue to the toy factory's secret...</p>

<p><br />
<em></em></p>

<p><em>"Become Sad Smiley's owner."</em></p>

<p><br />
</p>

<p>...it said.</p>

<p><br />
<em></em></p>

<p><em>"Stay together forever. Together is fun. Fun is happy. Forever. For-ev-ever. Forever. Forever. Foreverrrrrr..."</em></p>

<p><br />
</p>

<p>CRACK!</p>

<p>The Sad Smiley in my hand split in half. Black blood splattered across my face.</p>

<p>"I told you to stay put, civilian."</p>

<p>Mo Haein stared at me, the Black Moon Blade stained with rainbow blotches hanging at her side.</p>

<p>I stepped out of the box, holding the half of the Sad Smiley doll that was left.</p>

<p>The vinyl of the package box I'd been hiding in was also cut in half, so I could get out easily.</p>

<p>Everything around me was rainbow-colored.</p>

<p>"I told you not to move on your own!"</p>

<p>Park Seonggyeon scowled and shouted.</p>

<p>I'd been dazed for a moment by Sad Smiley's strange behavior, so I was a beat late in responding.</p>

<p>"Sorry. I was on the conveyor belt like you said, but the toys were attacking, so I had to dodge..."</p>

<p>"You were attacked even on the belt?"</p>

<p>Mo Haein and Park Seonggyeon's expressions turned serious immediately.</p>

<p>While they conferred briefly, I found the other half of the Sad Smiley that had been knocked away.</p>

<p>I placed the two halves together and set a Nut Candy on top.</p>

<p>I stared blankly down at Sad Smiley's smiling face.</p>

<p>Oh dear, an unexpected situation. Truly...</p>

<p>My heart was pounding from sheer excitement...!</p>
