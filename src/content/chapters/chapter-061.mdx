<h1>Chapter 61</h1>

<p>There was one way to avoid getting a game over when the required amount wasn’t met.</p>

<p>It was to offer one of the party members as food for the monsters.</p>

<p>After sacrificing people one by one like that, if even the final remaining person failed to meet the required amount, it would be a complete game over.</p>

<p>‘But doesn’t this require five people?’</p>

<p>The Sla2Plate I knew required a party of five before the game could begin.</p>

<p>There were parts of the structure that made progressing with fewer people impossible.</p>

<p>The trial would probably require five people to enter as well.</p>

<p>As I waited quietly, Samra explained exactly as expected.</p>

<p>“Because this trial requires five people to enter, we will add one suitable person and enter the trial.”</p>

<p>After saying he would bring an appropriate person from among the suitable candidates of the Trial Response Agency, Samra looked at me silently.</p>

<p>“Why did you not use Escape?”</p>

<p>“Ah, that… I thought I could clear it, so I saved the item.”</p>

<p>“Items can be created as many times as necessary. From now on, please make sure to use them. As I said before, there is no one more important than Han Goyo-ssi in the current Republic of Korea.”</p>

<p>The wall of electronic devices behind him moved like a wave, surrounding me from all directions.</p>

<p>Inside the countless screens were horrifying disasters.</p>

<p>Scenes created by trials that had hatched around the world.</p>

<p>Samra, who had approached me before I knew it, quietly added,</p>

<p>“Now, it would not be an exaggeration to say the entire world.”</p>

<p>“……”</p>

<p>“In fact, countries around the world have begun to recognize Han Goyo-ssi’s existence. From now on, you will need to be even more careful about your safety.”</p>

<p>“Yes… I’ll be careful.”</p>

<p>“What happened to the NPC that caused the system error?”</p>

<p>He is currently moving energetically from my left shoulder to my right shoulder.</p>

<p>I made sure not to look at Dominic and answered with something different from what I was thinking.</p>

<p>“He seems to still be trapped inside CallOVo.”</p>

<p>I could hear Dominic laughing.</p>

<p>He did not interrupt my conversation with Samra, as befitting a gentleman, but he was clearly enjoying watching.</p>

<p>Actually, I had considered telling Samra that I had made a contract with Dominic.</p>

<p>However, I couldn’t completely trust Samra enough to do that.</p>

<p>He could not perform actions that harmed humanity.</p>

<p>But Dominic was obviously an existence harmful to humanity from anyone’s perspective.</p>

<p>If Samra decided to eliminate Dominic immediately, it would become troublesome.</p>

<p>And if Dominic ended up being taken away to the research division…</p>

<p>I could easily imagine Dominic being shocked by the rudeness of Earth’s people and wiping out the Trial Response Agency.</p>

<p>‘If he got a little more shocked after that, he’d probably destroy Earth too.’</p>

<p>I had gone through so much trouble to turn him into a pudding slime. That couldn’t happen.</p>

<p>For now, the proper order was to try and reform Dominic as much as I could myself, and if I truly couldn’t handle him, ask Samra for help as a last resort.</p>

<p>“Han Goyo-ssi.”</p>

<p>“Yes…?”</p>

<p>The problem was that Samra had recorded all of my physical reactions, making it far too easy for him to detect lies.</p>

<p>When he stared at me again with his holographic eyes shining, I had no choice but to add,</p>

<p>“Samra. Please trust me.”</p>

<p>Hearing the same words he had once said to me, Samra narrowed his eyes.</p>

<p>He let out a quiet sigh.</p>

<p>“The Trial Response Agency is observing the continuous occurrence of abnormal phenomena surrounding Han Goyo-ssi.”</p>

<p>It was also a warning that I wouldn’t be able to hide these abnormalities forever.</p>

<p>And that was a problem I had already considered.</p>

<p>Especially the incident that occurred in Clean3 was at a level where I wouldn’t have been able to say anything even if I had been dragged into an interrogation room immediately.</p>

<p>‘So that’s why he didn’t remove the restraints.’</p>

<p>Perhaps Samra was protecting me from suspicion as much as he could.</p>

<p>Showing obvious favoritism toward me would not be beneficial, so he was probably deliberately acting according to the rules.</p>

<p>‘Lieutenant Colonel Je and the captains too… did they take my side?’</p>

<p>If they had reported everything that happened, I wouldn’t be discussing entering another trial right now.</p>

<p>I would already have been locked inside a special containment facility.</p>

<p>The question came first.</p>

<p>Samra had a clear reason for protecting me.</p>

<p>Even setting aside Je Hyun-oh, who never said anything anyway, I couldn’t understand why the captains would support a one-month trainee like me.</p>

<p>I slowly opened my mouth.</p>

<p>“…If I close Sla2Plate this time.”</p>

<p>And then I explained my plan to Samra.</p>

<p>“I’m going to negotiate with the Trial Response Agency. I’ll properly establish my position and share the information I have in more detail.”</p>

<p>At my determination to escape the status of a one-month trainee, Samra finally relaxed his expression.</p>

<p>In a surprisingly gentle voice for an android, he said,</p>

<p>“All members of the Special Mission Team were worried about Han Goyo-ssi’s safety.”</p>

<p>“I’m sorry…”</p>

<p>After hearing my apology, Samra stared at me silently, as if something still bothered him.</p>

<p>Then he added one more thing.</p>

<p>“Han Goyo-ssi. I am also part of the Special Mission Team.”</p>

<p>I looked at him, wondering why he was stating something so obvious.</p>

<p>Samra repeated himself more clearly.</p>

<p>“That means I was also worried about Han Goyo-ssi.”</p>

<p>It seemed like he was telling me not to do anything reckless and to stay safe, so I apologized again.</p>

<p>Samra still looked dissatisfied, but he promised me his support.</p>

<p>“For now, focus only on closing the trial. Do not worry about anything else. I will assist you.”</p>

<p>Samra really was a kind android.</p>

<p>Of course, he was probably being kind because I was a necessary existence to him, but results mattered more than the process.</p>

<p>After adding another lie—that I had not received the item rewards from CallOVo because it was an unhatched trial—I finally managed to move on to another topic.</p>

<p>When we had finished discussing the current situation of the Trial Response Agency, Samra asked,</p>

<p>“Is there anything you need?”</p>

<p>It was the same as when I had requested a neat appearance during Clean3. He was asking whether there was any item I needed.</p>

<p>However, I asked for something else.</p>

<p>After hearing my request, Samra’s expression became strange, but he promised to provide the item.</p>
<hr class="calibre3"/>
<p>After leaving Samra’s virtual space, I moved to the quarters inside the Trial Response Agency.</p>

<p>Even while being escorted to the containment area, I was still wearing the restraints, and they were only removed once we reached the door.</p>

<p>Leaving behind the soldiers who looked at me unfavorably, I entered the room.</p>

<p>Maybe I had gotten attached after only using it for a few days. Even though it was called a containment room, it was simply comfortable and pleasant.</p>

<p>I placed the pudding slime on the table and took off my uniform first.</p>

<p>Dominic crawled around on top of the table and asked,</p>

<p>“What kind of existence is Han Goyo-ssi here?”</p>

<p>I was about to take off my shirt, but I hesitated for a moment.</p>

<p>I understood the meaning behind his question.</p>

<p>It seemed Dominic had noticed that the door to my containment room could only be opened from the outside.</p>

<p>This time, I had no excuse.</p>

<p>After thinking for a moment, I honestly explained my situation.</p>

<p>“Actually, I… am considered a dangerous existence.”</p>

<p>I confessed that although I was a young god here, I was not in a position where I was worshipped.</p>

<p>Dominic crawled over to the edge of the table.</p>

<p>Even if he fell, nothing would happen to him.</p>

<p>Even if he broke, it would probably just be the floor of the containment room that broke, but I instinctively held out my hand.</p>

<p>It was a side effect of making him look far too cute.</p>

<p>Dominic seemed to interpret it as an invitation and slowly climbed onto my palm.</p>

<p>“……”</p>

<p>I held my breath.</p>

<p>The sensation was too pleasant.</p>

<p>If I didn’t put strength into controlling myself, I felt like I might just start squishing him without thinking.</p>

<p>‘I heard playing with soft, squishy things like this is popular these days.’</p>

<p>For a moment, I enjoyed the latest trend.</p>

<p>“Han Goyo-ssi.”</p>

<p>The pudding on my palm spoke to me.</p>

<p>“Please do not be too discouraged. Their actions are an entirely normal defensive mechanism.”</p>

<p>A faint glow flowed from the pudding.</p>

<p>The impossible, unrealistic sparkle that resembled starlight shining during the day created a comforting feeling.</p>

<p>“Humans cannot endure Han Goyo-ssi’s true nature. It is an enormous amount of information that they cannot comprehend, so they instinctively feel fear toward you.”</p>

<p>The voice that reached me was low and deep, making me feel as if I had sunk into a comfortable bed in a quiet counseling room.</p>

<p>I looked down at Dominic for a while before calling his name.</p>

<p>“Dominic-ssi.”</p>

<p>“Yes, Han Goyo-ssi.”</p>

<p>Dominic seemed like he was about to continue speaking, but he stopped himself and waited for me.</p>

<p>His advice was helpful.</p>

<p>Helpful enough that I felt like I could rely on him.</p>

<p>But I recalled the deranged patients I had seen in CallOVo and reminded myself to stay cautious.</p>

<p>‘Be careful.’</p>

<p>Before Dominic could continue his counseling, I quickly offered him another distraction.</p>

<p>“I brought something interesting. It’s something that will let you learn about this place’s culture.”</p>

<p>It was the tablet PC I had asked Samra for.</p>

<p>It did not have an internet connection, but it contained a wealth of data.</p>

<p>The audiovisual materials I prepared so Dominic would not be bored while I slept were various transformation hero shows.</p>

<p>The reason I chose them was simple.</p>

<p>Most of them were stories about fighting for dreams, love, friendship, and justice.</p>

<p>Either way, Dominic regaining his memories was an unavoidable disaster waiting to happen.</p>

<p>Until then, I planned to guide Dominic toward more positive thinking as much as possible.</p>

<p>Additionally, my goal was to make him develop a favorable impression of Earth.</p>

<p>‘So that when Dominic’s memories return, he won’t destroy Earth.’</p>

<p>“Please watch this for a while. I’m going to wash up.”</p>

<p>“Understood, Han Goyo-ssi.”</p>

<p>I left Dominic on the bed and set up the tablet for him.</p>

<p>After taking a leisurely shower and coming back out, I found Dominic watching the audiovisual educational material with great interest.</p>

<p>When I sat on the bed wearing my pajamas, Dominic greeted me.</p>

<p>“You came at a good time. I have many questions.”</p>

<p>With his soft body, Dominic tapped the tablet and moved the scenes to the parts he wanted.</p>

<p>Even though I had never taught him how to use it, he was already skilled at handling machines.</p>

<p>After playing a scene where the heroes transformed, Dominic asked,</p>

<p>“Han Goyo-ssi. Why do their enemies wait while they are changing forms?”</p>

<p>“It’s something like a rule. Attacking someone while they are introducing themselves is considered a very dishonorable act.”</p>

<p>“Courtesy, then. In that case, Han Goyo-ssi, why do they fight while wearing such inconvenient clothing? It does not seem suitable as attire for soldiers in battle.”</p>

<p>“The more flashy the design of the clothing, the stronger the attack power becomes. If you look there, the more like a supporting character someone is, the more ordinary their clothes are, right?”</p>

<p>“Hmm, I understand. Then, Han Goyo-ssi.”</p>

<p>The slime threw itself against the tablet again.</p>

<p>This time, it was the scene after the transformation where they struck a pose and shouted their lines.</p>

<p>Pointing at the colorful heroes, Dominic asked,</p>

<p>“Are their personalities determined by their colors?”</p>

<p>“Uh… I think their colors are determined based on their personalities.”</p>

<p>“Is that so? Heroes are beings formed with fixed personalities.”</p>

<p>I answered Dominic’s questions while watching the video together for a while.</p>

<p>‘Aren’t villains way too cool these days?’</p>

<p>I worried that Dominic might end up finding the villains more appealing than the heroes.</p>

<p>But I couldn’t take the tablet away from the slime that was watching so attentively.</p>

<p>And if I went down to children’s videos, I felt Dominic would probably find them boring.</p>

<p>While glancing at the slime, I eventually fell asleep at some point.</p>

<p>And the next morning.</p>

<p>I woke up to the sound of gunfire and explosions.</p>
