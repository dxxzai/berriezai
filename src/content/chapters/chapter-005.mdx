<h1>Chapter 5</h1>

<p>Park Seonggyeon's lips trembled as he let out a terrified murmur.</p><p></p>

<p>"Wha-what... Why is it doing this..."</p>

<p>He tried to back away from the System Window shoved in his face, but his back hit the wall almost immediately.</p>

<p>He must have known physical force wouldn't work, but he still swatted at the air, trying to make the window disappear.</p>

<p>I approached Park Seonggyeon, pretending I had no idea what was happening.</p>

<p>"Are you okay?"</p>

<p>"Hk!"</p>

<p>Park Seonggyeon, trembling violently, flinched and swung his index finger at me.</p>

<p>I'd already anticipated his reaction — I dodged his attack by a hair's breadth.</p>

<p>"Lieutenant Park!"</p>

<p>Mo Haein's voice cracked like fire, her eyes blazing.</p>

<p>But Park Seonggyeon was already out of his mind. He charged at Mo Haein, screaming.</p>

<p>"AAAAARGH!!"</p>

<p>Normally, Mo Haein would have dodged him easily, but right now she was busy dodging Tarantella's attacks and the note gimmick.</p>

<p>"Ngh...!"</p>

<p>She gritted her teeth. Red blood seeped through her clenched jaw, trickling down the corner of her mouth.</p>

<p>Mo Haein, her abdomen impaled by one of Tarantella's toy legs, suppressed the pain and swung her Black Moon Blade.</p>

<p>A long gash opened across Park Seonggyeon's chest.</p>

<p>He crumpled to the floor, then took a barrage of hits from the note toys pouring from the ceiling — his body caved in all over.</p>

<p>A dissonant chord rang out as Park Seonggyeon's screaming body shimmered into a patchwork of rainbow colors.</p>

<p>Then — crash.</p>

<p>He collapsed into a pile of toy blocks. Dead.</p>

<p>'Three hearts. He'll revive.'</p>

<p>We hadn't hit any save points on the way here, so he'd probably respawn in the lobby.</p>

<p>"Damn it..."</p>

<p>Mo Haein looked at me, her eyes wavering. She'd be judged dead soon too, and lose a heart.</p>

<p>Despair, frustration, and helplessness filled her eyes as she looked at the civilian who'd be left alone before the boss.</p>

<p>Mo Haein spat out the words like rapid fire.</p>

<p>"Listen carefully, civilian! Dying here doesn't mean you're really dead. You'll revive, so stay put wherever you wake up, and I'll — !"</p>

<p>But she couldn't finish. She crumbled into toy blocks.</p>

<p>Seeing her worry about me made guilt prickle in my chest.</p>

<p>Sorry, Captain Mo Haein. I'll make it up to you when you get back.</p>

<p>When she revived, she'd prioritize protecting the civilian over tracking down Park Seonggyeon.</p>

<p>I calculated how long it would take for Mo Haein — who'd be waiting in the lobby for me to respawn — to realize something was wrong and come back to the lab. Then I looked up.</p>

<p>Princess Tarantella was staring down at me.</p>

<p>Ding! A System Window appeared.</p>
<div class="systembox">
<div class="systembox_wrap">
<p>◆ Main Quest: Soothe the Angry Princess Tarantella.<br/>
All your companions have died. You are alone.<br/>
Only you remain to face the angry spider.<br/>
You wish to soothe Princess Tarantella. To do so, you must win the battle.</p>
</div>
</div>

<p>The boss's target had shifted to me now.</p>

<p>Notes poured from the ceiling as all eight legs lunged at once.</p>

<p>The attacks came in a chaotic flurry, but they weren't hard to track with my eyes. This was a section I'd cleared without a single death in the game too.</p>

<p>'Movement feels fine.'</p>

<p>The Trial granted you the minimum physical ability needed to clear it as a baseline.</p>

<p>It was strange how easily my body moved exactly as I willed it.</p>

<p>"——!"</p>

<p>Tarantella, who hadn't landed a single hit, let out a high-pitched shriek.</p>

<p>Phase 2 had begun.</p>

<p>The cascading notes vanished, and Tarantella started spewing yarn webs in every direction.</p>

<p>The webs severely restricted the player's movement — get caught, and you'd be helpless as the boss killed you.</p>

<p>'Wonder if the glitch strategy works too.'</p>

<p>I didn't have much time. I was thinking of trying a bug exploit to clear it fast.</p>

<p>Of course, the success rate was low.</p>

<p>It'd worked in the game, but I had no idea if it'd work here.</p>

<p>I charged straight at Tarantella. The key was moving before the webs were fully in place.</p>

<p>I stepped on each of the eight legs, leaping upward until I reached the top of Tarantella's head.</p>

<p>Ugh.</p>

<p>From where I'd planted my feet, a maw full of densely packed teeth was visible just a handspan away.</p>

<p>If I failed, I'd fall straight into that mouth.</p>

<p>'But I still have hearts left, right?'</p>

<p>I pushed off with all my strength, leaping toward the darkness of the hole in the ceiling.</p>

<p>Everything was pitch black.</p>

<p>My body, which had risen to its limit, began to fall.</p>

<p>A faint chill crept over my skin.</p>

<p>The thought of the pain I'd endure if I failed sent a cold shiver down my spine.</p>

<p>A short fall.</p>

<p>My feet touched ground.</p>

<p>"Haaah..."</p>

<p>I let out the breath I'd been holding. I looked down.</p>

<p>Tarantella, having lost its target, floundered for a moment, then shot out a web.</p>

<p>The glitch only available in Phase 2 was hiding on the foothold in the ceiling hole.</p>

<p>If you waited on the ceiling foothold — a bug that shouldn't exist — Tarantella would lose track of its target, act erratically, and eventually award you a victory.</p>

<p>Tarantella, now spewing webs in only one direction like a broken machine, lifted all eight legs at once.</p>

<p>Then it coughed up a cocoon, and its four-part mouth closed back into a smiling face.</p>

<p>"——..."</p>

<p>A child's crying sound emerged as the spider toy collapsed to the floor.</p>

<p>The boss fight was over.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>You have won the battle against Princess Tarantella!</p></div>

<p>I used Tarantella as a platform and climbed back down.</p>

<p>The cheerful chiptune music had shifted to a melancholy tune.</p>

<p>The cocoon Tarantella had coughed up crumbled away, revealing doll parts.</p>

<p>They were the limbs of the prince doll.</p>

<p>I collected the doll parts and put them in my inventory.</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>Tarantella hadn't moved a muscle the whole time.</p>

<p>I took out a Nut Candy and slid it toward Tarantella.</p>

<p>Suddenly, Tarantella's leg twitched.</p>

<p>The pincer at the end of its leg clicked weakly, trying to grab the candy, but kept missing.</p>

<p>I unwrapped the Nut Candy and placed it where I assumed its mouth was.</p>

<p>The smiling face split open slightly, and it swallowed the candy. Tarantella, which had been slumped over until a moment ago, seemed to regain its strength and lifted itself up.</p>

<p>The chiptune music had shifted to a gentle, soothing melody.</p>

<p>Tarantella looked at me and stretched a leg upward.</p>

<p>The spider climbed the wall with its toy legs, slowly disappearing into the darkness of the ceiling.</p>

<p>All that remained was a yarn cocoon.</p>

<p>'I already got the doll parts, though?'</p>

<p>I picked up the cocoon, puzzled. The yarn unraveled, revealing a small candy.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Bolt Candy: Click-click, upgrading toy...</p></div></div>

<p>It was HapFactory's hidden item — Bolt Candy.</p>

<p>Bolt Candy temporarily boosts attack power and physical abilities significantly.</p>

<p>Unlike Nut Candy, it can be used without wearing toy limbs.</p>

<p>It could only be obtained once in HapFactory, and only through a secret quest.</p>

<p>It was also the hidden item I'd been planning to find before Mo Haein returned to the lab.</p>

<p>But for Tarantella to give me the Bolt Candy...</p>

<p>This was the first time a boss had handed over a hidden item.</p>

<p>HapFactory's toys hate adults.</p>

<p>In the true ending route, the toys do develop some fondness for the player, but it's more like 'not hating them.'</p>

<p>Something like this — giving gifts — shouldn't have been possible.</p>

<p>'Come to think of it, Sad Smiley also said something about wanting me to be its owner.'</p>

<p>The whole "let's be together forever" thing was creepy, but technically it was a statement of affection.</p>

<p>I pocketed the Bolt Candy, picked up the prince doll that had fallen to the floor, and reattached its limbs.</p>

<p>I put the completed doll in my inventory, then looted a triangular flask filled with rainbow-colored liquid.</p>

<p>I started walking, lost in thought.</p>

<p>'What if there's another ending besides the true ending?'</p>

<p>When I reviewed my playthrough, the only difference was one thing.</p>

<p>My blood had gotten on Sad Smiley.</p>

<p>In the game, unless it was a specific event, getting wounded didn't cause items to get blood on them.</p>

<p>'First things first.'</p>

<p>This was an opportunity I'd worked hard to create — to get separated from Park Seonggyeon and Mo Haein. I couldn't afford to get lost in contemplation.</p>

<p>I left the lab and headed for the next stage: the warehouse.</p>

<p>I scanned the prince doll's eyes to unlock the warehouse door and stepped inside.</p>

<p>There was a hidden space in the warehouse.</p>

<p>A space that could only be found by collecting the sketchbook papers scattered throughout HapFactory, crafting an item, and solving the clues based on that item.</p>

<p>The contents of the sketchbook papers were as follows:</p>

<div class="bigbox2_pixel">
<p>Unknown Paper #1 <br/>
To save the princess who fell ill, the prince summoned an evil demon.</p>
</div>

<div class="bigbox2_pixel">
<p>Unknown Paper #2 <br/>
The princess, having gained the demon's power, grew healthy! But she became a terrible monster.</p>
</div>

<div class="bigbox2_pixel">
<p>Unknown Paper #3<br/>
The princess, trapped in the tower, lives an eternal life. The princess hates becoming a monster and wants to die.</p>
</div>

<div class="bigbox2_pixel">
<p>Unknown Paper #4<br/>
But no matter what she does, she cannot die. Forever...</p>
</div>

<div class="bigbox2_pixel">
<p>Unknown Paper #5<br/>
The enraged princess pushed the prince from the tower.<br/>
The prince shattered into pieces and died.<br/>
The princess envied the prince's fragmented death.</p>
</div>

<p>When all five papers were collected, they transformed into a storybook item.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_pixel"><p>Unknown Storybook: A crude storybook made by drawing pictures in a sketchbook with crayons. It seems to have been made by a young child.</p></div>

<p>And at the end of the storybook, one more page was added.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_pixelR"><p>WANNADIEWANNADIEWANNADIEWANNADIEKILLMEKILLMEKILLMEKIEDIEKILL</p></div>

<p>This was the core of HapFactory's main story.</p>

<p>But since I already knew all the clues the storybook would provide, I didn't need to wander around the factory collecting papers.</p>

<p>Instead, I looted tarot cards — marked with the crown and tower symbols from the storybook — inside the warehouse.</p>

<p>Then I approached an old cabinet tucked away in a corner, barely visible.</p>

<p>I pulled on the door of the long metal cabinet. Clunk. It didn't open.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_pixel"><p>Seems to be locked...</p></div>

<p>In the game, a System Window would pop up here.</p>

<p>But if you stubbornly tried to open the door over ten times —</p>

<div class="bigbox2_pixel"><p>The cabinet door broke!</p></div>

<p>A chime would sound, and the door would swing open.</p>

<p>Instead of a chime, I heard the sound of a lock breaking.</p>

<p>Doll parts came tumbling out of the opened cabinet. All of them were dismembered prince dolls.</p>

<p>I emptied out the doll parts and instead placed the completed prince doll and the items I'd gathered from the lab and warehouse inside the cabinet, then closed the door.</p>

<p>A flash of light seeped through the cracks in the cabinet. I waited for the light to settle, then opened the door.</p>

<p>Inside, all the other items had vanished. Only a single Bolt Candy sat there, alone.</p>

<p>'Two Bolt Candies now.'</p>

<p>This was an item I'd never managed to get two of before. I pondered the best use for them and called up the System Window.</p>

<p>'Park Seonggyeon's status window.'</p>

<p>I checked his status — nothing had changed except that he'd used one Nut Candy in the meantime.</p>

<p>Come to think of it, when I'd sent him that Message earlier, I'd been able to see his System Window.</p>

<p>Normally, you couldn't see other people's System Windows, but since Message was something I'd created, I guess it was visible.</p>

<p>'But if I'm the System, shouldn't I be able to see everything from the start?'</p>

<p>As soon as I raised the valid question, a square popped up as if it had been waiting.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Your lack of ability.</p></div>

<p>It seemed too lazy to say the same thing twice — a pretty half-hearted response.</p>

<p>I'd gotten what I wanted to know, so I let the System Window's apathy slide.</p>

<p>I started prepping while I waited for Mo Haein, who should be arriving soon.</p>

<p>There was a running gimmick from the warehouse leading into the next stage.</p>

<p>You had to dodge falling debris from a collapsing building, so I cleared the obstacles in advance to prepare.</p>

<p>Just as I moved the last chair, Mo Haein appeared.</p>

<p>"Hah... you... how..."</p>

<p>She was panting, like she'd run here at full sprint.</p>

<p>Mo Haein, gripping the warehouse doorframe, stared at me blankly, scanning me from head to toe.</p>

<p>Having confirmed I was unharmed, she clenched her jaw and walked toward me — Black Moon Blade still in hand.</p>

<p>The black obsidian blade pressed close under my chin. Mo Haein looked up at me and asked.</p>

<p>"Civilian."</p>

<p>"Yes."</p>

<p>"You can see the system windows, can't you?"</p>
