<h1>Chapter 23</h1>

<p>Before leaving the second floor, Yi Gaon approached the Laboratory Director's Office.</p>

<p>Yi Seon was hidden behind the barricade, out of sight. Not that it would have mattered much even if she could see him.</p>

<p>The emergency light cast a faint glow over Yi Gaon's face.</p>

<p>She whispered in a small voice.</p>

<p>"I'll be back."</p>

<p>A zombie, hearing a human voice, let out a low gurgle.</p>

<p>Yi Gaon didn't hesitate any longer. She turned around.</p>

<p>To join up with Mo Haein and Kwak Hanmuk, we headed to the fifth floor of the Laboratory.</p>

<p>We didn't take the stairs — we used the same method of transport as before.</p>

<p>But since Yi Gaon was with us, instead of smashing the window, we quietly opened it.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh pulled out the metal wings he'd folded away.</p>

<p>Yi Gaon stared at the wings, her face going blank.</p>

<p>The goggles and the metal hand she could somehow rationalize as auxiliary weapons, but the wings seemed to be far beyond her comprehension.</p>

<p>I wished she'd just accept the wings along with everything else, but since she couldn't, I gave her an honest explanation.</p>

<p>"It's a new weapon the military secretly developed."</p>

<p>Yi Gaon still didn't seem entirely convinced, but she nodded — stiffly, but she nodded.</p>

<p>"I... I see."</p>

<p>In a world where zombies roamed around, what was a pair of wings on someone's back?</p>

<p>Yi Gaon would get used to it in a few hours.</p>

<p>"Excuse me for a moment."</p>

<p>I picked Yi Gaon up. She awkwardly placed her hands on my shoulders.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh stared at me.</p>

<p>He's not going to throw me again, is he?</p>

<p>I had no trust in Je Hyun-oh, so I quickly sent a Message.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>I'm currently carrying the vaccine and cure.</p></div>

<p>Je Hyun-oh clicked his metal hand together.</p>

<p>It looked like a gesture of disappointment that he couldn't throw us.</p>

<p>Fortunately, Je Hyun-oh behaved himself and lifted both me and Yi Gaon, moving us to the rooftop.</p>

<p>Once on the roof, I set down the trembling Yi Gaon and checked the HVAC system again.</p>

<p>The HVAC system was a crucial device that kept the viruses inside the Laboratory contained.</p>

<p>If the HVAC system shut down, all kinds of viruses would spread outside, leading straight to a bad ending.</p>

<p>Since it had to be maintained at all costs, most of the emergency power went to the HVAC system.</p>

<p>That meant additional power was needed for vaccine development.</p>

<p>Right now, the emergency generator inside the Laboratory was broken, so its operation rate was probably around 20%.</p>

<p>We had to repair the emergency generator and restore the operation rate.</p>

<p>We'd already collected most of the delivery items.</p>

<p>A few items from the Yellow Zone, Red Zone, and Dead Zone were still missing, but I figured I could have Je Hyun-oh go farm those.</p>

<p>If he flies there and back, it'll be quick.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh could handle zombies easily, so there was nothing to worry about.</p>

<p>The most urgent task was repairing the emergency generator.</p>

<p>As I was thinking about how to do it, Yi Gaon let out a small sound.</p>

<p>"Ah —"</p>

<p>I turned to look at her. She smiled, a little embarrassed.</p>

<p>"The sun is rising."</p>

<p>The sky was growing pale. A day had passed.</p>

<p>Yi Gaon was lost in the sight of a sunrise she hadn't seen in a long time. I grew a little more anxious.</p>

<p>Day two of entry.</p>

<p>A day had passed since infection. Mo Haein's aggression would be much stronger now.</p>

<p>I took Yi Gaon and moved to the fifth floor.</p>

<p>I didn't forget to hold the door open for Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>Right now, with the Crow Gloves deactivated, he could only smash through doors.</p>

<p>When we reached the fifth floor, the hallway was full of zombie corpses.</p>

<p>Their heads were crushed, as if someone had beaten them in — definitely Kwak Hanmuk's handiwork.</p>

<p>He really is strong. Thinking this, I approached the sealed laboratory door with Yi Gaon.</p>

<p>The security sliding door was designed to open only with an ID card.</p>

<p>Yi Gaon looked at me and nodded.</p>

<p>With trembling hands, she took out her ID card and scanned it.</p>

<p>At the sound of the door opening, the researchers inside the lab jumped out in surprise.</p>

<p>Then they saw Yi Gaon and let out nervous, hollow laughs, as if they'd been worried for nothing — until —</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>They saw me and Je Hyun-oh, and quietly shut their mouths.</p>

<p>I checked the names on the ID cards hanging around the researchers' necks.</p>

<p>Tak Jucheol. Paeng Sangdo.</p>

<p>Both were names I recognized. In the game, they died early on — but they'd survived this far.</p>

<p>As I looked at them with mild curiosity, Paeng Sangdo stammered.</p>

<p>"Are you... soldiers? Are you with those people from earlier? Same group, I mean... same team?"</p>

<p>"Yes."</p>

<p>I had no reason to exchange pleasantries with them. I got straight to the point.</p>

<p>"Help Yi Gaon make the vaccine."</p>

<p>I deliberately didn't mention the cure.</p>

<p>And the moment I gave the order to the researchers, a System window appeared.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<div class="systembox_wrap_j">
<p>
<span class="glitch_t2">◆Ma?in??! Q?es!t!</span>: Develop the vaccine and cure.<br/>

You are once again challenging an impossible feat!<br/>

You believe that a vaccine alone cannot make the zombie world a happy place.<br/>

Therefore, you wish to develop a cure and move toward a <span class="glitch_t2">new!new!new</span>! world.<br/>

Your reckless challenge is admirable in its attempt alone.<br/>

But sometimes, choosing the safe path isn't a bad idea.<br/>

Perhaps that is the true courage.<br/>

You believe this is a moment where it's not too late to change your choice.
</p>
</div>
</div>

<p>It was blatantly telling me not to do it.</p>

<p>I dismissed the System window blocking my view and looked at the researchers.</p>

<p>Tak Jucheol and Paeng Sangdo were the kind of villain characters you see in zombie stories.</p>

<p>The kind who prioritize their own survival above all else and don't hesitate to sacrifice others for it.</p>

<p>In the game, those two researchers died because they threw Yi Seon to the zombies and tried to escape somewhere else — only to run into a boss zombie.</p>

<p>Looks like they survived this time.</p>

<p>Since things had turned out this way, I wanted to put them to good use.</p>

<p>I planned to squeeze them dry and make them contribute to the world of DeZonDeal.</p>

<p>Whether my intentions showed in my eyes or not, Paeng Sangdo, who kept glancing at me, was looking increasingly uncomfortable.</p>

<p>I pulled out the bait to handle them effectively.</p>

<p>"I know the location of a survivor shelter."</p>

<p>"...!"</p>

<p>Tak Jucheol and Paeng Sangdo held their breath, focusing on my words.</p>

<p>"It's a place with plenty of food and hardly any zombie activity. But it's a long way from the Laboratory."</p>

<p>There was no way weak researchers could make it to a shelter in the Green Zone.</p>

<p>Even this Laboratory was swarming with zombies.</p>

<p>Tak Jucheol and Paeng Sangdo knew that well.</p>

<p>"If this research is completed successfully, I'll escort you to the shelter."</p>

<p>Threats alone wouldn't extract wholehearted cooperation.</p>

<p>Since I had to leave them with Yi Gaon, I needed to make sure they didn't get any funny ideas.</p>

<p>They were driven by survival instinct. If I dangled the shelter in front of them, they'd go wild and throw themselves into vaccine production.</p>

<p>Sure enough — just moments ago they'd been full of reluctance, but now their eyes had changed.</p>

<p>Paeng Sangdo sniffled, then exchanged glances with Tak Jucheol several times.</p>

<p>Then he cautiously spoke up.</p>

<p>"W-well, of course we want to make the vaccine. Who wouldn't? But the core material..."</p>

<p>"I have the meteorite."</p>

<p>At my words, Yi Gaon flinched and clutched the pocket of her lab coat.</p>

<p>So that's where she put it.</p>

<p>Having confirmed the meteorite's location, I looked back at Tak Jucheol and Paeng Sangdo.</p>

<p>Tak Jucheol asked in a cracked voice.</p>

<p>"Do you know about Pandora?"</p>

<p>The DeZonDeal virus started with a meteor shower that fell to Earth one day.</p>

<p>A new virus was discovered in the fallen meteorite, and researchers named it 'Pandora.'</p>

<p>Pandora was a zoonotic virus that infected both humans and animals, but it was judged to be vulnerable to heat — making infection unlikely in mammals with high body temperatures.</p>

<p>That conclusion, however, was the result of rushed research.</p>

<p>Because the meteorite had fallen on the development zone, pressure was applied from all sides.</p>

<p>In the end, the Laboratory failed to warn about the virus's potential danger and gave the corporation the answer it wanted.</p>

<p>Development proceeded as planned. Meanwhile, Pandora evolved at an unbelievable speed and adapted to high temperatures.</p>

<p>Then a worker on the development zone construction site was bitten by a wild dog infected with Pandora — and the disaster began.</p>

<p>Yi Seon was a researcher who had continuously warned about the dangers of Pandora.</p>

<p>He secretly smuggled out part of the meteorite that was slated for disposal and hid it at home.</p>

<p>The meteorite Yi Gaon had risked going to the Yellow Zone to retrieve in order to save her brother — that was it. The core material for the vaccine.</p>

<p>"How did you know?"</p>

<p>Before I could answer Tak Jucheol's question, Yi Gaon cut in.</p>

<p>She was shaking even harder than when she'd been afraid of Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>"That the meteorite caused the virus... that my brother and I had it..."</p>

<p>Before the trust I'd built could crumble, I answered simply.</p>

<p>"I'm not actually a regular soldier. I work for a secret government agency."</p>

<p>"Ah...!"</p>

<p>Yi Gaon let out an exclamation as if everything finally made sense.</p>

<p>"The National Intelligence Service... something like that, right? I understand."</p>

<p>Close enough.</p>

<p>There wasn't a single lie in what I'd just said. I'd only told the truth. I nodded.</p>

<p>While Yi Gaon was convincing herself, Tak Jucheol and Paeng Sangdo were busily whispering to each other.</p>

<p>Tak Jucheol stood with his hands clasped behind his back in a dignified pose, while Paeng Sangdo stepped forward to speak.</p>

<p>"We have conditions for cooperating. If you procure the supplies we need for the research..."</p>

<p>It wasn't worth hearing the rest. They wanted to live in luxury throughout the research.</p>

<p>I turned to look at my reliable superior.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh handled it appropriately.</p>

<p>"WAAAAAH!"</p>

<p>"EUUUUGH!"</p>

<p>He grabbed both researchers by the scruffs of their necks and lifted them into the air.</p>

<p>Their lab coats, pierced by his metal hands, tore with a long ripping sound.</p>

<p>The researchers, who had been drooling and flailing, dropped to the floor one by one — thud, thud — their backs exposed and shredded.</p>

<p>Just as I was about to extract a loyalty oath from them to become vaccine development slaves, the Captains arrived.</p>

<p>"Whoa, hyung-nim, having fun without me?"</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk hurried over, his eyes gleaming. He looked like he wanted a piece of the action, but there was nothing to give.</p>

<p>If I threatened them any more, they'd be too scared to do any research.</p>

<p>I calmed Kwak Hanmuk down and approached Yi Gaon.</p>

<p>"Yi Gaon. Can you do this?"</p>

<p>Yi Gaon looked around the lab. For a moment, she seemed to think of Yi Seon, her eyes welling up — but she didn't cry.</p>

<p>Instead, she answered with determination.</p>

<p>"I have to."</p>

<p>Yi Gaon gripped the pocket of her lab coat and looked up at me.</p>

<p>"There are some things I need for the research..."</p>

<p>"I'll get everything you need by the end of today. I'll also repair the emergency generator by today. You don't need to worry about food either."</p>

<p>I answered before she could even say what she needed. Yi Gaon faltered, then her expression shifted to understanding.</p>

<p>I could practically see the words 'National Intelligence Service' passing through her mind.</p>

<p>"Thank you! The emergency generator was really urgent. Thank you so much."</p>

<p>Yi Gaon thanked me repeatedly, then suddenly pulled me aside.</p>

<p>Once we were outside the lab, far from Tak Jucheol and Paeng Sangdo, she whispered.</p>

<p>"The vaccine. My brother already finished the blueprint, so if we just have the materials and power, it's possible in three days. It's molecular synthesis, not cultivation — once the power's on, we can extract the raw material from the meteorite and run the sequence right away. Of course, we'll have to skip clinical verification."</p>

<p>Yi Gaon watched my reaction.</p>

<p>She seemed worried that I'd notice she'd lied earlier about not knowing about the vaccine — when actually, it was nearly complete.</p>

<p>But I already knew.</p>

<p>In the game too, Yi Seon met the player with the vaccine research almost finished.</p>

<p>The siblings were the same.</p>

<p>Just like Yi Gaon, Yi Seon couldn't find a reason to develop the vaccine when his little sister might already be dead.</p>

<p>He kept failing to complete it. Only when his sister Yi Gaon was rescued did he finally produce the vaccine.</p>

<p>"You said you'd get the materials and repair the emergency generator by today, so I'll finish the setup in the meantime. The problem is the cure..."</p>

<p>Yi Gaon's calm voice trailed off into a sigh.</p>

<p>"I tried researching it before, but the screening results for effective candidate substances weren't good. Even using the meteorite as a control, there was no reaction. Same with existing antiviral compounds. It felt so hopeless that..."</p>

<p>"Focus on the vaccine for now. I'll look into the candidate substances."</p>

<p>Yi Gaon looked at me as if she couldn't believe it.</p>

<p>This time, I lied.</p>

<p>"I have some information on that. It's not certain, but it seems worth checking."</p>

<p>I didn't actually know the candidate substance.</p>

<p>But there were a few clues I'd seen like easter eggs in the game. I planned to track them down.</p>

<p>Yi Gaon looked up at me for a long moment, then whispered.</p>

<p>"I'll trust you."</p>

<p>I sent her back to Tak Jucheol and Paeng Sangdo in the lab, then stepped into the hallway with Je Hyun-oh and the Captains.</p>

<p>"We'll start vaccine production now. Lieutenant Colonel, please farm the remaining items. Captains, you should help me repair the emergency generator."</p>

<p>At my briefing, Mo Haein and Kwak Hanmuk each spoke up.</p>

<p>"Trainee. I've never fixed a machine in my life."</p>

<p>"I've repaired a car before, but a generator? No clue."</p>

<p>"I know how to repair it."</p>

<p>Based on the repair method from the game, it didn't require anything too extreme.</p>

<p>"The problem is the boss zombie guarding the emergency generator in the underground area."</p>

<p>Two people need to repair the generator. One needs to handle the boss.</p>

<p>In the game, the siblings Yi Seon and Yi Gaon repaired the generator while I fought the boss.</p>

<p>But now Yi Seon was a zombie, and Yi Gaon had to make both the vaccine and the cure.</p>

<p>Excluding Je Hyun-oh, who needed to go gather research materials, that left me, Kwak Hanmuk, and Mo Haein. The three of us had to handle this — but...</p>

<p>The problem was Mo Haein's aggression as her zombification progressed. The chance of her suddenly attacking us during the operation was sky-high.</p>

<p>As I explained the plan, Mo Haein and Kwak Hanmuk seemed to be thinking the same thing.</p>

<p>Mo Haein's eyes darkened. She was already using a lot of energy just to suppress the aggression rising inside her.</p>

<p>"I do have an operation in mind, though."</p>

<p>I paused, not speaking right away. I could already picture Mo Haein calling me crazy again.</p>

<p>And indeed, she was already looking at me like I was insane.</p>

<p>Captain Kwak. I trust you.</p>

<p>Hoping Kwak Hanmuk would support my plan, I opened my mouth.</p>

<p>"Captain Mo."</p>

<p>"Say it."</p>

<p>"Please bite me."</p>
