<h1>Chapter 125</h1>

<p>I reached out my hand to touch it.</p>

<p>The pungent smell of the sea enveloped the surroundings, and hundreds of tentacles turned toward me in unison.</p>

<p>Just before I touched the tentacles...</p>

<p>“Roasted Chestnut.”</p>

<p>A large hand reaching out from behind pressed down on my forearm.</p>

<p>I tilted my head back.</p>

<p>A 3-meter-tall man standing behind me looked down and said.</p>

<p>“No.”</p>

<p><em>Why?</em></p>

<p>“You cannot digest something that size.”</p>

<p><em>I can eat it.</em></p>

<p>His blue eyes curved into a round shape.</p>

<p>“A young god, yet so greedy. Even though you are still so small.”</p>

<p>He tried to dissuade me, saying that even ■■■■ couldn't fully digest it.</p>

<p>I was displeased by the way they spoke to me, as if I were a small child.</p>

<p><em>I am not a child.</em></p>

<p>Upon hearing my words, Dominic immediately burst out laughing.</p>

<p>His smiling eyes seemed to be gazing at something truly young and tiny.</p>

<p>I softened slightly.</p>

<p>It was because I knew what kind of being Dominic was.</p>

<p>In his presence, everything would surely seem merely childish.</p>

<p>“Let’s see. Shall we do this?”</p>

<p>Dominic remained behind me, wrapping his arms around both of my shoulders.</p>

<p>I looked at the thing I had momentarily forgotten.</p>

<p>Located at my feet, it continued to extend its tentacles toward me.</p>

<p>However, as if blocked by something, it repeatedly slipped away mid-air.</p>

<p>Its squirming movements were disgusting.</p>

<p>As I watched it, a question suddenly struck me.</p>

<p>Why did I want to eat that?</p>

<p>I simply wanted to punish the one who coveted my subordinate.</p>

<p>It was something I could have done without necessarily eating it.</p>

<p>But my thoughts did not continue.</p>

<p>A gentle voice, like an ecstatic dream, tickled my ears.</p>

<p>“That thing is neither small, nor weak, nor benevolent. It is a suitable being for you to deal with, Roasted Chestnut.”</p>

<p>He encouraged me, saying that I could handle it if I just learned a little trick, and that it was possible because I possessed strong subordinates.</p>

<p>“Once you’ve played with it to your heart’s content, I’ll cut it into pieces you can eat.”</p>

<p><em>I don’t want to be in pain like with ■■■■.</em></p>

<p>“It won’t hurt this time.”</p>

<p><em>Really?</em></p>

<p>“Because it will help cool you down. Digesting ■■■■ will be easier.”</p>

<p>…….</p>

<p>“I promise.”</p>

<p>In that case, it seemed like it would be fine. I nodded slightly.</p>

<p>Dominic moved his fingers lightly. He laughed as he playfully tapped me.</p>

<p>“Once you’ve digested it all, you’ll be able to take your first steps.”</p>

<p>Then, he covered my eyes with one hand. A huge hand seemed to engulf my entire face.</p>

<p>“Let’s go back. There is no benefit in staying in a place like this any longer.”</p>

<p>In the pitch-black darkness, I heard a call.</p>

<p>It was a mass of corrupted sound, mixed with grotesque, distorted syllables.</p>

<p>It was calling me.</p>

<p>It tried to call my name several times, but it was futile.</p>

<p>Neither Roasted Chestnut nor Little Lamb are names that hold my true essence.</p>

<p>Leaving the discordant noise behind, I slipped outside.</p>

<hr class="calibre3"></hr>

<p>As soon as I opened my eyes, the dream scattered.</p>

<p>No matter how hard I tried to hold onto it, it slipped through my grasp like grains of sand, leaving nothing behind.</p>

<p>I felt my eyes burning.</p>

<p>They were probably glittering with gold again.</p>

<p>“The Octopus Tentacle has approached Roasted Chestnut.”</p>

<p>The Pudding Slime was guarding my bedside.</p>

<p>In a half-dazed state, I asked.</p>

<p>“Did it enter my dream…?” </p>

<p>“It is common for a god to dwell in a dream.”</p>

<p>Dominic added the advice that it would be best not to try to recall the dream.</p>

<p>He meant that although I had successfully chased away Octopus Tentacle, if I remembered the dream, the severed connection might be re-established.</p>

<p>I slowly sat up.</p>

<p>And I waited until the heat in my throbbing eyes subsided.</p>

<p>A god interfering with the human mind.</p>

<p>I thought it was peculiar ever since I watched the religious broadcasts here, but I never expected it to approach me in this way.</p>

<p>I could guess why Octopus Tentacle were able to enter my dream.</p>

<p>Unlike before, I had drunk three bottles of Neo in a row.</p>

<p>It seemed that had an effect in some way, allowing Octopus Tentacle to find me.</p>

<p>I didn't remember the dream clearly.</p>

<p>But… the feeling of enjoyment remained.</p>

<p>It was the joy of finally finding something I had been searching for all along.</p>

<p>I rubbed my eyes with my hand and flinched at the dry, crumbly blood.</p>

<p>It was because I had fallen asleep looking grimy.</p>

<p>I got up thinking I needed to wash up quickly, but I heard footsteps outside.</p>

<p>I hadn't closed the door properly earlier because I had practically rolled in.</p>

<p>The footsteps I heard were heading precisely toward the medical officer's quarters.</p>

<p>As someone whose head had been smashed just a few hours ago, I couldn't help but feel wary.</p>

<p>I clutched the iron pipe I had carefully kept under the bed.</p>

<p>This time, I intended to smash his head myself.</p>

<p>Holding the pipe and widening my eyes, I parted my lips and said, &quot;Uh.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Lieutenant Won?&quot;</p>

<p>It wasn't an intruder; it was Won Taebeom.</p>

<p>I had sent a message to Kwak Hanmuk asking him to explain the general situation to CEO Cha.</p>

<p>I also asked him to send Lieutenant Won to the medical officer's quarters if possible.</p>

<p>However, I didn't expect him to come all the way here right away.</p>

<p>As I was putting down the iron pipe, I realized something was off.</p>

<p>Won Taebeom was standing stiffly in the doorway, looking down at me.</p>

<p>'Is it because I'm dirty?'</p>

<p>I was a mess. My face and clothes were soaked with blood from the head injury I sustained earlier.</p>

<p>It even seemed like the smell of cigarettes had permeated me, as the Chief of Security had smoked so much.</p>

<p>As I cautiously sniffed my sleeve, Won Taebeom spoke in a stiff voice.</p>

<p>&quot;Report the situation, Trainee Han.&quot;</p>

<p>I quickly assumed a respectful posture.</p>

<p>I wasn't entirely sure, but I stood there looking as soldier-like as I could, and answered.</p>

<p>&quot;There was a conflict with the Chief of Security.&quot;</p>

<p>I reported that he had kidnapped me to turn me into a prison guard, and that I had escaped by betraying CEO Cha.</p>

<p>&quot;Why didn't you treat yourself?&quot;</p>

<p>“I’m fully recovered.”</p>

<p>I replied that I had been forced to drink Neo by the Chief of Security, but resolved the issue by using the antidote I had on hand.</p>

<p>Of course, I hadn’t touched a single drop of the antidote, but Won Taebeom had no way of knowing the truth.</p>

<p>Just as I was about to explain why I had called him over...</p>

<p>Something very embarrassing happened.</p>

<p>Grumble.</p>

<p>My hunger had exceeded its limits and triggered a warning.</p>

<p>Come to think of it, I hadn't eaten anything since the cereal yesterday morning.</p>

<p>“Lieutenant Won… I’m really sorry, but…”</p>

<p>I spoke, feeling my ears burn with embarrassment.</p>

<p>“Do you have any leftover food? If so, could you give me just a little?”</p>

<p>Won Taebeom looked at me with disbelief.</p>

<p>I would try to hold out if I could, but since starving too much leads to a bad ending, I needed to eat right now.</p>

<p>Fortunately, Won Taebeom immediately took some food out of his inventory and gave it to me.</p>

<p>“Thank you!”</p>

<p>I bowed politely and accepted the food.</p>

<p>It was none other than a peanut butter jelly sandwich.</p>

<p>Having eaten nothing but expired cereal for the past few days, I took the time to savor the taste of heaven.</p>

<p>I devoured it with gusto, but my eating speed gradually slowed.</p>

<p>It was because I could feel Won Taebeom staring at me intently.</p>

<p>I might have eaten too greedily. I chewed the sandwich as quietly as possible.</p>

<p>After finishing, I gently brushed the bread crumbs off my lips with my hand.</p>

<p>“Trainee Han.”</p>

<p>“Yes?”</p>

<p>I jumped in surprise, as if I had committed a crime, but he spoke with a sigh.</p>

<p>“Please go wash up first. We will discuss the operation after that.”</p>

<p>“Uh, but...”</p>

<p>“It is an order.”</p>

<p>“Yes.”</p>

<p>Actually, since I really wanted to wash up, I was extremely happy about Won Taebeom’s order.</p>

<p>I quickly took a shower in the small bathroom attached to the medical officer's quarters.</p>

<p>After changing my clothes, I felt like I had been reborn.</p>

<p>I put on the slime I had left outside for a moment and hurried back to Won Taebeom.</p>

<p>Won Taebeom spoke curtly.</p>

<p>“Dry your hair.”</p>

<p>“Yes.”</p>

<p>Water droplets kept rolling down my wet hair.</p>

<p>I was in such a hurry that I had only roughly rubbed it with a towel, leaving my hair looking like wet seaweed.</p>

<p>Following my superior’s order, I took out another dry towel and vigorously shook my hair.</p>

<p>Won Taebeom waited patiently for me.</p>

<p>When I finally faced him again, Won Taebeom had a complicated look in his eyes.</p>

<p>I wondered if I had been too arrogant, unbefitting of a trainee, once again.</p>

<p>I was taking a moment to reflect when he called out to me.</p>

<p>“Trainee Han. How have you been with your meals?” </p>

<p>“I ate cereal in small portions. But yesterday morning, I ran out of food… I survived thanks to you, Lieutenant Won.”</p>

<p>“…….”</p>

<p>After a long silence, he handed me five energy bars.</p>

<p>“If any food-related problems arise in the future, consult your superior immediately. Understood?”</p>

<p>“Yes.”</p>

<p>I quietly accepted them and put them in my inventory.</p>

<p>Feeling like a rich man, I began to think seriously about the operation.</p>

<p>My original plan was a ‘prison rebellion centered on creatures.’</p>

<p>Set a date to overturn the prison all at once, pour in Neo to turn the guards into scraps, and then feed them to the creatures.</p>

<p>This was the standard strategy for the True Ending.</p>

<p>I had already discussed this with Orca and hidden Creature No. 1 underground.</p>

<p>I asked Kwak Hanmuk and CEO Cha to complete quests to maximize their respective guard and warden abilities.</p>

<p>I had assigned the underground maze to Je Hyun-oh, but since the situation had changed drastically, I had to shift my focus.</p>

<p>The new operation I devised was a 'prison rebellion exploiting the Chief of Security's coup.'</p>

<p>Thinking hard, I asked Won Taebeom.</p>

<p>&quot;Lieutenant Won, have you completed the cleric quest?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yes. I have raised the ability to level 3.&quot;</p>

<p>The cleric's ability is brainwashing.</p>

<p>One can use brainwashing on a target of their choosing to turn them into a fanatic.</p>

<p>Human inmates who become fanatics see a massive increase in labor productivity, while creatures see a massive increase in Neo production.</p>

<p>It is an ability that encapsulates the effects of religious broadcasting.</p>

<p>As the level increases, the duration of the brainwashing effect grows; at level 3, it lasted about two days.</p>

<p>I nodded and checked something else.</p>

<p>&quot;Did you notice any anomalies on the schedule?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;The mission assigned to today's cleric schedule was to brainwash the medic.&quot;</p>

<p>This crazy octopus tentacle.</p>

<p>I expected it to reach out to the cleric first, and sure enough, that was the case.</p>

<p>“The missions of everyone else have all changed as well. What is going on?”</p>

<p>“Well… the god here wants me.”</p>

<p>Since Won Taebeom’s role was that of a cleric, it was difficult to lie about this part.</p>

<p>When I spoke honestly, his eyes narrowed.</p>

<p>“Are you saying that a being called ‘God’ actually exists?”</p>

<p>“You can just think of him as the boss.”</p>

<p>It was actually quite similar.</p>

<p>I cursed Octopus Tentacle inwardly as I recalled my weekly schedule, which had become quite bizarre.</p>

<p>At this rate, no matter what plan I devised, it was bound to fail because of the missions.</p>

<p>Failing the same mission twice would result in a bad ending.</p>

<p>Even if I managed to get away with a mission failure today, if the exact same mission was assigned tomorrow, I would be helpless against it.</p>

<p>In the end, Octopus Tentacle was targeting me.</p>

<p>After pondering a solution, I came up with a plausible plan.</p>

<p>I mean, a really good way to stop the tyranny of the Octopus Tentacle and reduce the Chief of Security's suspicion level to zero.</p>

<p>However, there is something I want to ask Dominic…</p>

<p>I opened my lips without thinking.</p>

<p><em>“Dominic-ssi.”</em></p>

<p>Gasp.</p>

<p>I flinched, startled by the voice I had just spoken.</p>

<p>The moment I thought I wanted to talk to Dominic, a strange voice suddenly came out.</p>

<p>When I flinched, Won Taebeom looked at me with a puzzled expression.</p>

<p>Dominic smiled and reassured me.</p>

<p>“It’s fine. Your eyes haven’t changed; they're still the same. You can handle everyday conversations comfortably now, so you don’t need to worry.”</p>

<p>He nuzzled against me, as if finding me endearing.</p>

<p>“Lowly creatures can't hear you, so speak with peace of mind.”</p>

<p>Won Taebeom across from me certainly didn’t notice my voice.</p>

<p>It felt somewhat like the whisper function often used in online games.</p>

<p>Using a strange but useful voice, I cautiously asked Dominic.</p>

<p><em>“Does Octopus Tentacle need to make a contract to take me as its subordinate?”</em></p>

<p>“Yes. Taking a god as a subordinate is quite tricky.”</p>

<p><em>“Then it would be alright if I praised Octopus Tentacle for a few days, right?”</em></p>

<p>Dominic, realizing what I was trying to do, replied as if speaking to a troublemaker.</p>

<p>“Roasted Chestnut, really… I must not take my eyes off you.”</p>

<p>In any case, it meant it was fine.</p>

<p>I came to a conclusion.</p>

<p>“Lieutenant Won.”</p>

<p>“Speak.”</p>

<p>“Please brainwash me.”</p>

<p>To become a fanatic of Octopus Tentacle.</p>
