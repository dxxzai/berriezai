<h1>Chapter 148</h1>

<p>I knew a day like this would come someday.</p>

<p>Just as he had seen the holographic badge in CallOVo, I expected the time would come when Dominic would notice the signet ring hidden in my inventory.</p>

<p>But I never imagined it would be now.</p>

<p>I felt as if every nerve had stiffened.</p>

<p>I didn't want to return the ring.</p>

<p>However, contrary to my thoughts, my body was already moving beyond my will.</p>

<p>As if obeying an irresistible command, I pulled the ring out of my inventory.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Signet Ring That Lost Its Master: A signet ring once used by someone noble. Those who are not the owner feel a deep sadness when wearing this ring.</p></div></div>

<p>I knew it from the moment I received the ring from the butlers. This was not an item meant for me.</p>

<p>Dominic had already reclaimed a part of his existence. I could clearly <em>feel</em> that he was different from before.</p>

<p>If he were to change even more after receiving the signet ring...</p>

<p>I gasped for breath.</p>

<p>I barely managed to clutch the ring with my convulsing hand.</p>

<p>Not handing this to him right now was the best I could do.</p>

<p>Seeing my state, Dominic asked softly,</p>

<p>&quot;Are you scared?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;.......&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;It is merely an object. Giving it to me won't result in what Roasted Chestnut is worried about.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;.......&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;That is because it truly means nothing to me. However, it will be meaningful to Roasted Chestnut. If you return the ring, I will be able to do a few things for you.&quot;</p>

<p>He whispered softly, suggesting he could help with things I wanted, such as opening an Interstellar Account.</p>

<p>&quot;If you're still scared, you can put the ring back. Let's just take it out when you want to give it to me.&quot;</p>

<p>His voice, tinged with a faint smile, was not as entirely affectionate as before.</p>

<p>Dominic spoke like someone who already held everything in his grasp.</p>

<p>“You were going to return it to me anyway, weren’t you?”</p>

<p>I realized it then.</p>

<p>Even though it seemed like I had a choice, there was ultimately only one answer.</p>

<p>It was pointless to hold out here.</p>

<p>Since the owner had recognized his property, it was bound to be reclaimed sooner or later.</p>

<p>In reality, from Dominic’s perspective, he could have forcibly taken it back, but he was merely letting me off the hook.</p>

<p>Returning it now would be advantageous for our future relationship.</p>

<p>Also, as Dominic said, I couldn't possibly find his essence in a single ring.</p>

<p>I opened my lips.</p>

<p>“...Dominic-ssi.”</p>

<p>“Yes, Roasted Chestnut.”</p>

<p>“The ring... I should have returned it long ago. I’m sorry.”</p>

<p>“No problem. You must have had a hard time.”</p>

<p>Dominic actually comforted me.</p>

<p>He even spoke in a tone that suggested he was proud of me for keeping such an object safe all this time, noting that I must have been scared to possess it. It was as if he were comforting a child who had confessed honestly without lying.</p>

<p>Feeling like I was becoming endlessly younger, I just listened quietly.</p>

<p>After reassuring me for a while, Dominic said.</p>

<p>“Then, I will take it.”</p>

<p>Even though I tried not to be nervous, my body froze involuntarily.</p>

<p>The ring on my palm vanished as if melting away.</p>

<p>When the ring had completely disappeared...</p>

<p>A chilling silence descended.</p>

<p>Every sound in the world was cut off.</p>

<p>Even the light was sucked in, and absolute darkness enveloped the space.</p>

<p>A darkness composed solely of pitch black enveloped my body, and pure disorder and chaos crushed my mind.</p>

<p>The remnants of light from a bursting supernova flashed.</p>

<p>Billions of galaxies swirling as they vanished into nothingness.</p>

<p>The death of countless stars.</p>

<p>The screams of stars trampled by <em>Him</em> poured down upon me.</p>

<p><em>Scary scary scary scary scary scary scary scary scary scary scary</em></p>

<p>I wanted to run away.</p>

<p>But amidst primal fear and terror, all I could do was endure.</p>

<p>I wished this terrible catastrophe would end.</p>

<p>But... does an end exist?</p>

<p><em>Is there an end to the infinite void?</em></p>

<p>“Roasted Chestnut.”</p>

<p>“...!”</p>

<p>I woke up with a start.</p>

<p>It took a moment to realize that I had escaped the endless abyss of emptiness, and that I was inside a small room.</p>

<p>A moment that must have been nothing more than a fleeting instant had clawed my mind to shreds.</p>

<p>I stared at Dominic, frozen in place.</p>

<p>He didn't grow huge, nor did he give orders as a master.</p>

<p>He spoke to me while still remaining a small, soft slime.</p>

<p>“Look. I'm exactly the same, Roasted Chestnut.”</p>

<p>I felt like I knew what Dominic wanted.</p>

<p>When he retrieved the signet ring, I could clearly feel the vast gap between Dominic and me.</p>

<p>However, Dominic did not want me to be afraid or scared.</p>

<p>He still wanted to pretend to be a friendly Pudding Slime.</p>

<p>And that was exactly what I wanted as well.</p>

<p>After taking a deep breath, I reached out my hand to him.</p>

<p>His soft body touched mine. Only then could I feel a little relieved by the familiar touch.</p>

<p>“Would you wait a moment?”</p>

<p>Having asked for my understanding, the Pudding Slime stayed quietly on my hand for a while, as if checking something.</p>

<p>As I waited for him, I thought to myself.</p>

<p>What did it mean that he had gone around my planet?</p>

<p>It seemed unlikely he had stayed away from me simply for that reason.</p>

<p>Because Dominic was interested in the young god, not <em>my domain</em>.</p>

<p>It seemed there was some other purpose as well...</p>

<p>It was clear that even if I asked Dominic, he wouldn't tell me.</p>

<p>If he could have told me, he would have already told me.</p>

<p>While I was speculating about his whereabouts, Dominic finished his check.</p>

<p>He let out a light &quot;hmm&quot; and said, &quot;This should be enough.&quot;</p>

<p>I hesitated, then asked him.</p>

<p>&quot;What do you mean by 'enough'?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I meant that it would be enough to become a patron of Roasted Chestnut.&quot;</p>

<p>The signet ring was likely related to the cosmic account he owned.</p>

<p>Dominic asked me politely,</p>

<p>&quot;May I check Roasted Chestnut's Galactic Trading Post?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Please do.&quot;</p>

<p>Then he smeared glitter on me. Starlight seeped into me.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Entering the Galactic Trading Post.</p></div>

<p>As I entered the Galactic Trading Post, Dominic, who was sharing my vision, looked at the trading post system window and said a word.</p>

<p>“I see that the feature for young gods is enabled. The Akasha System will be considerate in many ways, so it would be best to keep it enabled.”</p>

<p>Then, as soon as he saw my shopping cart, he let out a laugh.</p>

<p>I felt a little embarrassed by the sound of his laughter.</p>

<p>Since I had nothing to do while trapped for 72 hours, I had put everything I wanted to buy into my cart.</p>

<p>Even though I tried to be careful in my selection, the list was endless.</p>

<p>The total of my cart exceeded trillions and reached quadrillions.</p>

<p>However, this was partly because the items at the Galactic Trading Post were so expensive.</p>

<p>If something seemed decent, it was at least billions, and if it was decent, tens of billions was nothing.</p>

<p>There were plenty of items I really wanted that cost hundreds of billions.</p>

<p>Dominic, having scanned my cart, made a proposal.</p>

<p>“How about signing a sponsorship contract with me? I could buy everything in your cart, Roasted Chestnut. And I would be able to do even more for you.” </p>

<p>It was the second sponsorship offer, following Black Block-ssi's.</p>

<p>Both of them acted as if they had an abundance of Star Shards, readily offering to buy me whatever items I wanted.</p>

<p>I recalled the time I spent happily emptying Dominic's wallet in CallOVo.</p>

<p>I asked cautiously.</p>

<p>“Is the sponsorship contract separate from the current contractual relationship with Dominic-ssi?”</p>

<p>“To freely transfer goods or currency from the Galactic Trading Post to Roasted Chestnut, a different type of contract than the current one is required.”</p>

<p>Apparently, it was necessary to utilize the Akasha system.</p>

<p>He also explained that under the current contract, it was difficult to give me anything without receiving something in return.</p>

<p>Come to think of it, during the Infernum event, I received quests from Dominic to hunt, and during the ■■■ event, my power was used in the annihilation, albeit only partially.</p>

<p>‘A sponsorship contract, huh.’</p>

<p>The concept of a sponsor was familiar.</p>

<p>But why would they sponsor me when I am not even playing with the Pudding Slime logo attached? I couldn't help but ask.</p>

<p>“I am curious what benefit the sponsoring god gains if we sign a contract.”</p>

<p>“It varies depending on the form of the contract, but the contract I intend to make with Roasted Chestnut offers no benefit whatsoever.”</p>

<p>“What? Then...”</p>

<p>“It simply extends the relationship a little longer. Our current contract has a set term.”</p>

<p>Until Dominic regains his memory.</p>

<p>That was the contract between me and him at the moment.</p>

<p>However, to think that signing a sponsorship contract would extend my relationship with Dominic in exchange for massive financial support...</p>

<p>It felt like a contract so advantageous to me that I was suspicious.</p>

<p>Furthermore, I couldn't understand what it meant for the relationship to be extended.</p>

<p>In the end, I ended up declining the sponsorship offer once again.</p>

<p>“For now... I will try doing it on my own.”</p>

<p>“Understood.”</p>

<p>As always, Dominic let me do as I wished.</p>

<p>He also agreed to help me open an interstellar account.</p>

<p>I watched intently, hoping to learn the method of how on earth it was made, but it was a futile effort.</p>

<p>A glitch flashed and bounced in the air, and suddenly, an account had appeared.</p>

<div class="systembox glitch_t2 glitch_b3"><p>An account dedicated to Akasha has been created for ‘Han Goyo’ of Planet ‘Earth’.</p></div>

<p>I was just wondering if this was okay, as it felt somewhat abnormal.</p>

<div class="systembox glitch_t2 glitch_b3"><p>Dark Star Fragment has been deposited into the account.</p></div>

<p>The Dark Star Fragment, which had been sitting quietly in my inventory, was automatically deposited into the account.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Deposit Alert: 10,000,000,000 Star Shards</p></div>

<p>As soon as I saw the number of Star Shards displayed in the system window, I opened my eyes wide.</p>

<p>Ten billion...?</p>

<p>My shock at the unbelievable number lasted only a moment.</p>

<p>Wreeee!</p>

<p>Suddenly, a warning alarm went off, and an emergency system window popped up.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<p>
&lt;Notice of Abnormal Transaction Detection and Account Freeze Measures&gt;
</p>

<div class="systembox_wrap">
<p>
Emergency security measures have been implemented as an inflow of unauthorized, high-value funds into your account was detected, deviating drastically from normal transaction patterns.
</p>

<p>- Inflow Amount: 10,000,000,000 Star Fragments</p>

<p>- Recipient Code: Unidentified (Unauthorized Means)</p>

<p>- Measures Taken: Planetary Assets frozen and withdrawals, transfers, and payments completely blocked</p>

<p>
Please explain the source of the funds to lift these emergency security measures.
</p>
</div></div>

<p>... Account frozen?</p>

<p>The Pudding Slime I trusted has shot me in the foot.</p>

<p>I called out to Dominic, who was sharing my vision.</p>

<p>“Dominic-ssi...?”</p>

<p>I think I’m doomed. What should I do?</p>

<p>I called out to him with that meaning in mind, and Dominic spoke as if he had absolutely no idea.</p>

<p>“I didn’t know Roasted Chestnut had this much cash. You won’t be able to use it for the time being.”</p>

<p>Unless otherwise set up, creating an account automatically deposits the funds from your inventory.</p>

<p>His tone, implying it was his own oversight, was highly suspicious.</p>

<p>I could only suspect that he deliberately didn’t tell me because I hadn’t signed a sponsorship contract.</p>

<p>My 10 billion Star Shard......</p>

<p>“Still, it’s not a bad thing for the Administrator to know. Especially for someone as young as Roasted Chestnut.”</p>

<p>Dominic said something, but it didn’t register well.</p>

<p>Star shards were flying right before my eyes.</p>

<p>At that moment, I felt like I could just squash a pudding slime.</p>

<p>While I was staring blankly at the account freeze notification window...</p>

<p>A new square window popped up, and a strange presence appeared. A video call was forcibly connected even though I hadn't accepted.</p>

<p>Startled, I looked at the screen to see a strange alien appear.</p>

<p>The alien, with long hair that mingled turquoise and purple like a nebula and swaying in the air, had white skin.</p>

<p>Its androgynous appearance made it difficult to determine its gender.</p>

<p>It wore a stifling uniform coat that reached up to its neck, and the hands exposed beneath the sleeves were made of a transparent, crystal-like mineral, constantly shimmering with light like fiber optics.</p>

<p>Its eyes, with their diamond-shaped, angular pupils, moved ceaselessly as it checked something.</p>

<p>—This is the Galactic Trading Post Administrator. We have connected to proceed with an explanation process regarding an abnormal transaction detected in your account. We ask for your sincere cooperation.</p>

<p>Hearing the voice, I lowered my guard slightly.</p>

<p>The stiff, dry voice sounded like that of a weary office worker.</p>

<p>'Do I just need to explain myself to that alien?'</p>

<p>A glimmer of hope welled up that I might not lose ten billion won.</p>

<p>Since this was my first time meeting an alien other than Dominic or Black Block-ssi, I decided to start by greeting them.</p>

<p>“Hello.”</p>

<p>As I greeted them politely, the administrator, who had been staring blankly into the air, finally looked at me.</p>

<p>They looked slightly surprised.</p>

<p>After a moment of silence, the administrator spoke to me in a somewhat softer voice.</p>

<p>—Kid. Is there an adult on this planet? Could you go call one?</p>

<p>I almost instinctively turned to look at Dominic, who was perched on my shoulder.</p>

<p>Having barely managed to hold back, I replied to the administrator.</p>

<p>“I am the owner of this planet.”</p>

<p>The administrator did not seem to recognize Dominic.</p>

<p>They seemed to be a trading post administrator, but they didn't appear to be of low rank; it seemed Dominic had simply disappeared.</p>

<p>I didn't know the reason, but Dominic seemed to have decided that it was better for him not to be seen.</p>

<p>As soon as they heard my answer, the administrator leaned their face close to the screen.</p>

<p>Their diamond-like pupils dilated wide, and their hair rippled in all directions.</p>

<p style="font-size:21.6px">—<em>You-are-the-planet's-owner-? All-alone-without-a-guardian-?!</em></p>

<p>As if they was very surprised, their voice, which had been drooping with fatigue, became incredibly loud.</p>

<p>I answered, feeling a bit intimidated.</p>

<p>“Yes... I’m sorry, but... how do I unfreeze the account?”</p>

<p>However, the administrator wasn't listening to me at all.</p>

<p>They frantically moved his hair like hands and repeatedly tapped the air with their crystal hands.</p>

<p>Wherever they touched, flashes of light erupted in the air.</p>

<p>—Planet Earth, just joined Akasha too... Child filter applied... To think they entrusted a planet to this baby. They must be out of their minds. Young gods are so precious, after all...</p>

<p>Muttering to themself while checking, they addressed me.</p>

<p>—Kid. Let me take a look at the Galactic Trading Post transaction history.</p>

<p>And before they could even hear my answer, they arbitrarily accessed the transaction history.</p>

<p>Of course, there was nothing to see in my transaction history.</p>

<p>—First transaction... 10 Event Star Fragments...</p>

<p>The administrator’s hair hung low. They looked at me with a somewhat miserable expression.</p>

<p>As I stared blankly, the administrator sighed and asked in a rather gentle tone.</p>

<p>—Where did you get the Dark Star Fragment? Did anyone force the kid to do anything bad?</p>

<p>I pondered for a moment.</p>

<p>How should I answer?</p>

<p>First of all, a problem arose because the Dark Star Fragment currency was a much larger sum than I had anticipated.</p>

<p>Since a large amount was deposited immediately upon account creation without any proper transaction history, it was likely to cause a problem within the system.</p>

<p>If I failed to explain myself properly here, it seemed highly likely that my 10 billion Star Fragments would turn into cosmic dust.</p>

<p>I wished Dominic would whisper a hint, but he just sat on my shoulder, looking squishy and unhelpful.</p>

<p>I slowly opened my mouth.</p>

<p>“I received this for doing cleaning work. My employers thought highly of me.”</p>

<p>I answered honestly, fearing that getting caught lying would make the situation even worse.</p>

<p>Then the administrator looked at me with even more pity.</p>

<p>It seemed the gods of other planets didn't do cleaning part-time jobs...</p>

<p>—The Dark Star Fragment is a rare currency, so we need to verify it. Where did you do the cleaning, kid?</p>

<p>I learned for the first time that it was a rare currency.</p>

<p>I thought it was a widely used currency since it was said to be usable throughout the universe.</p>

<p>‘How am I supposed to explain that I obtained this through a trial?’</p>

<p>I doubt the administrator would know if I mentioned the already closed Clean3, but I tried telling them the truth for now.</p>

<p>“I cleaned at a mansion...”</p>

<p style="font-size:21.6px">—<em>A-mansion-?</em></p>

<p>At that moment, a huge reaction came back. The administrator buried their face in the screen again.</p>

<p>And asked an unexpected question.</p>

<p>—Kid, did you get it from the cat and rabbit butlers?</p>
