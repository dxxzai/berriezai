<h1>Chapter 145</h1>

<p>Including those that had not yet hatched, there were hundreds of thousands of Trial Meteorites that had fallen to Earth.</p>

<p>It was only natural that I didn't know about all the Trials.</p>

<p>It was also only natural that CEO Cha knew about Trials I didn't.</p>

<p>This was because CEO Cha was continuously entering Trials in the real world as the representative of Paseong.</p>

<p>Unlike me, who only entered domestic Trials, CEO Cha also received many requests from abroad.</p>

<p>But to think that there was a Trial where Sa Gongyeon appeared as an NPC.</p>

<p>As someone who had received a text message sent by Sa Gongyeon, I felt more bewildered than happy to have found a clue.</p>

<p>‘A Trial NPC that can influence reality?’</p>

<p>I quickly chased away the uncertain thought.</p>

<p>Nothing was confirmed yet. I needed to hear more from CEO Cha.</p>

<p>“Let’s go inside and talk.”</p>

<p>CEO Cha turned around immediately. Having achieved her goal, Kang Cheonseo smiled and let go of my arm. </p>

<p>She walked alongside CEO Cha, who was leading the way, and winked at me.</p>

<p>I flashed a business smile at the Chairman and followed her, keeping an eye on Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>After confirming that he appeared fine, I approached Kang Byeokrin.</p>

<p>When we had briefly met at Chairman Cheongang’s residence before, Kang Byeokrin had fallen and sprained her leg.</p>

<p>It bothered me that we hadn't been able to talk at the time because she was supposedly getting her leg treated.</p>

<p>Although it was in the past, we had received a great deal of help thanks to Cheongang becoming the team's sponsor.</p>

<p>Since I felt nothing but gratitude toward Cheongang in many ways, I wanted to treat her well as well.</p>

<p>“How have you been?”</p>

<p>For some reason, Kang Byeokrin was startled the moment I spoke to her.</p>

<p>Her face, which had been subtly flushed for a while, turned even redder, and she suddenly covered her mouth with her hand.</p>

<p>She answered only after some time had passed. </p>

<p>“Yes... Um, Goyo...-ssi, have you been doing well?”</p>

<p>I knew that Kang Byeokrin, befitting her status as Kang Cheonseo’s granddaughter, possessed a formidable personality, yet strangely, she gave off a very gentle impression.</p>

<p>I couldn’t judge if I was mistaken, as I had already overheard what she said as she got out of the car during our first meeting.</p>

<p>‘I thought she disliked commoners.’</p>

<p>I also wondered if she was being nice to me because of Kang Cheonseo.</p>

<p>“I’ve been doing well. I felt like I didn’t get to properly greet you back then.”</p>

<p>It was a relief that the leg she had fallen on seemed to have fully healed.</p>

<p>Just as I was about to bring up a casual topic like the weather, Kang Byeokrin asked me first.</p>

<p>“Have you... eaten?”</p>

<p>“Yes. I've already eaten.”</p>

<p>Just as I was about to continue the conversation by asking if she had eaten, a barrage of questions suddenly poured in.</p>

<p>“You’re eating three meals a day, right? You need to make sure you eat properly every single day. Are you getting enough sleep? You’re sleeping at least seven hours, right?”</p>

<p>My brain froze for a moment at the sudden barrage of words.</p>

<p>I didn't know how to answer. Kang Byeokrin's gaze was too serious to simply brush it off as a joke.</p>

<p>After a moment of hesitation, I gave the safest possible answer.</p>

<p>“I am taking good care of my health. Thank you for your concern.”</p>

<p>However, Kang Byeokrin’s questions still hadn’t ended.</p>

<p>“What about nutritional supplements? You don’t take any herbal medicine, do you? Maybe could I have some tonics prepared...”</p>

<p>“Rin.”</p>

<p>Kang Cheonseo called her name without even turning around, as if giving a warning.</p>

<p>Kang Byeokrin’s lips parted as if to speak, but she ultimately fell silent and pressed her lips firmly together.</p>

<p>Even so, she looked at me with pleading eyes.</p>

<p>As someone who had been subjected to a health survey out of the blue, I felt a bit awkward.</p>

<p>I couldn't understand why Kang Byeokrin was looking out for me to this extent.</p>

<p>However, it didn't seem like she asked with bad intentions, and for some reason, it seemed like an important matter to her.</p>

<p>I felt that I shouldn't answer carelessly.</p>

<p>First, I lowered the corners of my eyes and put on the kindest smile I could.</p>

<p>Then, I lowered my voice and whispered softly.</p>

<p>“I will make sure to take my nutritional supplements and get some herbal medicine.”</p>

<p>Upon hearing my reply, Kang Byeokrin’s eyes widened.</p>

<p>Then, her face looked as if she were about to cry.</p>

<p>I, who had tried my best to answer nicely, was startled and flustered.</p>

<p>I felt like I had made a mistake, but I hesitated because I didn't know what it was, until Kang Byeokrin immediately composed herself.</p>

<p>Kang Byeokrin spoke to me seriously.</p>

<p>“That's a promise.”</p>

<p>...I had intended to play dumb.</p>

<p>I’d thought I could just ignore it, since I’d likely have little contact with her in the future, but I realized I couldn't simply brush it off.</p>

<p>Taking herbal medicine might be too much trouble, but I should take my nutritional supplements.</p>

<p>As we arrived at the Paseong CEO's office, the conversation with Kang Byeokrin naturally came to a halt.</p>

<p>The Paseong directors, who were waiting outside the office, bowed in greeting.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh held out the coffee and dessert he was holding to Mok Saehwa, who was standing nearby.</p>

<p>Mok Saehwa looked at Je Hyun-oh with a look that seemed to say, &quot;What is this?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I bought these for you directors.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;It was Goyo-nim? Thank you.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Shall I hold your bag for a moment?&quot;</p>

<p>Mok Saehwa asked me kindly, but I replied that it was fine.</p>

<p>The duffel bag was light, and since the box on the side was a gift, I wanted to deliver it personally.</p>

<p>The CEO’s office, decorated in a clean, minimalist style, reflected CEO Cha’s own personality.</p>

<p>I spotted a pair of white headphones on the desk inside.</p>

<p>It seemed he had been wearing them until the guests arrived.</p>

<p>“We’re both busy, so I’ll get straight to the point and leave quickly.”</p>

<p>Kang Cheonseo said, sitting on the sofa as if it were her own home.</p>

<p>“What Geumcheon wants is 'Good-Looking'.”</p>

<p>'Good-Looking' was a small bronze hand mirror item.</p>

<p>It transformed the user’s appearance to match the preference of the NPC presiding over the current Trial.</p>

<p>It could only be used once per trial, and it was an item I had used in Clean3 but hadn't seen any change in my appearance.</p>

<p>Since there was no better item for safely gaining an NPC's favor, it was understandable that Geumcheon would covet it.</p>

<p>“Why would we strike a deal with criminals? I refused immediately. And then they approached Rin.” </p>

<p>Geumcheon proposed to Kang Byeokrin that they would give her a certain item in exchange for 'Good-Looking.'</p>

<p>However, surprisingly, that proposal cut through a secret that Cheongang had been hiding.</p>

<p>Cheongang was searching for the insider who had leaked the information, but they were facing more difficulties than expected.</p>

<p>“Geumcheon found out about something we only shared among people we truly trusted; it’s terrifying, so we can’t live like this.”</p>

<p>“What kind of item was it?”</p>

<p>At CEO Cha’s question, Kang Cheonseo gestured to Kang Byeokrin.</p>

<p>Kang Byeokrin slowly opened her mouth to answer.</p>

<p>“They said it was an item that perfectly restores a damaged body.”</p>

<p>Does Kang Byeokrin need a body recovery item?</p>

<p>‘She doesn’t look injured.’</p>

<p>Regardless of my confusion, Geumcheon’s proposal was a lie.</p>

<p>Recovery items themselves could certainly exist.</p>

<p>However, to receive such an item as a clear reward, one would have to be the top contributor in achieving the True Ending.</p>

<p>Even as the top contributor, you couldn't obtain a restoration item like that from a normal ending.</p>

<p>‘But what kind of Trial is it, anyway?’</p>

<p>I was curious. If it were a Trial I had already played through in the game...</p>
