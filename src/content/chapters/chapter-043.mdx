<h1>Chapter 43</h1>

<p>The moment I finished reading the article, only one thought came to mind.</p>

<p>'How do I get back?'</p>

<p>Every ending I knew in Clean3 always had you leaving the mansion on a spaceship.</p>

<p>You'd watch the ending credits roll with the mansion shrinking into the distance, and that was that.</p>

<p>I stood there holding the newspaper, dazed, when the Cat Butler spoke up.</p>

<p>"Don't worry too much, Roasted Chestnut. I'll find us a transport ship. Getting one person like yourself back home is no trouble at all!"</p>

<p>As I listened to the Cat Butler telling me to leave it all to him, Je Hyun-oh's voice flickered through my mind.</p>
<br/>
<p><span class="flashback">"You trust that?"</span></p>
<br/>
<p>But still… anyone could see he was trying to help me right now.</p>

<p>"Though as you're aware, Roasted Chestnut, we have no occasion to go outside, so securing a transport ship will take some time. It will likely run past the one week you originally agreed to stay."</p>

<p>"Roasted Chestnut. Stay with us."</p>

<p>The Cat Butler and the Rabbit Butler looked down at me.</p>

<p>I stood still, clutching the newspaper, then looked up at them and asked.</p>

<p>"Butlers… are you all right with this?"</p>

<p>"Of course."</p>

<p>"What could we possibly dislike about it? If Roasted Chestnut stays with us, it's nothing but a delight."</p>

<p>I folded the newspaper neatly and handed it back to the Cat Butler.</p>

<p>"Then, if you'll forgive me, I'll impose on you for a few more days."</p>

<p>"Impose? You'll be working hard, Roasted Chestnut! You are a proper Apprentice Butler, are you not?"</p>

<p>The Cat Butler patted my head like he found me admirable.</p>

<p>Then he startled himself and quickly pulled his hand away.</p>

<p>"My apologies. I treated you like a child."</p>

<p>"It's fine."</p>

<p>I didn't know how old the butlers were, but they'd certainly lived longer than me.</p>

<p>Something like this barely registered.</p>

<p>"Roasted Chestnut. I want to do it too."</p>

<p>The Rabbit Butler placed his hand on top of my head.</p>

<p>He didn't ruffle my hair — he tidied up the mess the Cat Butler had made, smoothing it back into place.</p>

<p>It felt like being a beast getting its fur groomed.</p>

<p>I thought to myself as the Rabbit Butler combed through my hair.</p>

<p>'How long is it going to take to get that transport ship?'</p>

<p>Still, the butlers would get it as fast as they could, so it wouldn't be too late.</p>

<p>That meant the clear time would be delayed, but a few days was fine.</p>

<p>When the ship arrived, I could just bring Je Hyun-oh and the Captains aboard with me.</p>

<p>I'd have to tell Je Hyun-oh about this first thing when he came by tonight.</p>

<p>"I've delivered the urgent news, so would you wait just a moment? Let me finish one bit of business, and I'll brew you some black tea with the new tea leaves that just came in."</p>

<p>I was supposed to be working, though….</p>

<p>Calling myself an Apprentice Butler while lazing around like this was embarrassing.</p>

<p>I was debating whether to tell the Cat Butler we could have tea later, once his work was done, when the air around us suddenly went cold.</p>

<p>The Cat Butler had his arms crossed, looking thoroughly displeased as he stared at the Footmen.</p>

<p>Every Footman had their head bowed low, bodies trembling.</p>

<p>Come to think of it, he'd assembled all the Footmen since morning — something must have blown up.</p>

<p>I watched the scene, worried some problem had arisen in the mansion.</p>

<p>The Cat Butler spoke up, his voice laced with irritation.</p>

<p>"Only four Cleaners died last night."</p>

<p>Excuse me?</p>

<p>My eyes flew wide open, but I immediately remembered the Rabbit Butler beside me and composed my expression.</p>

<p>'That can't be right.'</p>

<p>If Je Hyun-oh had properly relayed my message to the Captains last night and acted on it, the Cleaners should have been slaughtered en masse by the Creatures.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh was doing the exact opposite of what I'd suggested.</p>

<p>Saving the Cleaners and slaughtering the Creatures instead.</p>

<p>A curse rose up in my throat, but I couldn't let it out of my mouth.</p>

<p>'No — why?'</p>

<p>All I felt was confusion.</p>

<p>Even with all of us pooling our strength, it was uncertain whether we'd reach the True Ending. So why was he sabotaging it?</p>

<p>He hadn't even passed my opinion along to the Captains, it seemed.</p>

<p>There was no other explanation for only four Cleaners dying.</p>

<p>If Je Hyun-oh had been standing in front of me, I'd have abused my authority as Apprentice Butler and cracked him on the head.</p>

<p>While I was inwardly screaming Je Hyun-oh's name and trembling with rage, the Cat Butler spoke coldly.</p>

<p>"To think I brought such useless wretches on as Footmen. The fault is entirely mine."</p>

<p>That wasn't the butler's fault.</p>

<p>It was all Je Hyun-oh's — that was what I was thinking when it happened.</p>

<p>A beam of light shot out.</p>

<p>Two Footmen with holes bored through their heads melted on the spot.</p>

<p>They turned into liquid metal, like mercury, pooling on the floor.</p>

<p>The other Footmen pulled out buckets and swept the shimmering puddles away with brooms, swift and practiced.</p>

<p>They moved as naturally as if this had happened many times before.</p>

<p>The Cat Butler muttered, displeased.</p>

<p>"I'd wanted to fill out thirteen, though…. I'll have to find more new Footmen."</p>

<p>Then he gave an order to the Footmen, now ten in number.</p>

<p>"Tonight, all of you are to patrol the mansion and check on the Creatures and Cleaners! Dismissed."</p>

<p>The Footmen bowed in unison and vanished with a crackle of sparks.</p>

<p>The Cat Butler shook his head, then suggested we go have some tea and tugged me along.</p>

<p>I joined him for teatime, if only to help soothe his mood.</p>

<p>The Cat Butler lamented to me about how utterly hopeless young people were these days, while the Rabbit Butler listened in silence.</p>

<p>I chimed in at the right moments and waited for my chance.</p>

<p>And at the opportune moment, I asked.</p>

<p>"Would it be all right if I roamed the mansion tonight as well?"</p>

<p>The Cat Butler set down his teacup and asked back, surprised.</p>

<p>"Roasted Chestnut, you mean to check on the Cleaners and Creatures yourself?"</p>

<p>"Yes. I know I'm only an Apprentice Butler, but I haven't been doing much real work…. I'd like to help out with something like this. And I'm worried about the mansion, too."</p>

<p>The moment I finished speaking, the Cat Butler threw his arms wide and cried out.</p>

<p>"If only every Footman were like you, Roasted Chestnut!"</p>

<p>He was so deeply moved that he showered me with praise.</p>

<p>He told me I could do whatever I liked, so long as I didn't overdo it.</p>

<p>I answered with a humble smile, saying I'd be careful.</p>

<p>At last, I'd legally obtained the right to roam the mansion at night.</p>

<p>Relying solely on Je Hyun-oh was a path to ruin.</p>

<p>I needed to step in myself.</p>

<p>'I have to meet the Captains first.'</p>

<p>On my way to Kwak Hanmuk's and Mo Haein's cleaning zones, I figured I might toss a few Cleaners to the Creatures while I was at it.</p>

<p>And I planned to search the third floor of the Main Building.</p>

<p>That was where the Master's bedroom, study, and the like were gathered — I might be able to find some information there.</p>

<p>I drank the deep red tea as I laid out my plans for the night.</p>

<p>"Have some more, Roasted Chestnut! Eat some snacks, too."</p>

<p>The Cat Butler diligently pressed tea and treats on me.</p>

<p>I'd been sure I was full, but once I started eating, it all went down fine.</p>

<p>I didn't refuse a thing and accepted it all.</p>
<hr class="calibre3"/>

<p>The work of an Apprentice Butler was nothing much.</p>

<p>I was too much of a trainee to be entrusted with anything important. The tasks I got were so light, I almost felt guilty taking pay.</p>

<p>What I did during the day today was inspect whether the East Wing Cleaners had properly cleaned their assigned zones.</p>

<p>Cleaning assignments in Clean3 were decided each day by spinning the Roulette.</p>

<p>The Roulette result determined the contamination level of your assigned zone.</p>

<p>The worst possible outcome, 100% contamination, turned the place into a garbage pit, and in that case, you could never finish cleaning before inspection without high-grade items.</p>

<p>Failing the cleaning inspection meant immediate death.</p>

<p>But the Captains had good items, so even if they'd gotten 100% contamination, they'd have managed the cleaning without trouble.</p>

<p>'Lieutenant Colonel Je is the last person I need to worry about.'</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk was in charge of cleaning the floor of the East Wing's second-floor corridor, and Je Hyun-oh was in charge of the landscaping trees in the East Wing garden.</p>

<p>But even if I went to their assigned zones, I wouldn't be able to meet them.</p>

<p>It was because of a rule you could call the fundamental principle of clearing Clean3.</p>

<p>During the day, clean your assigned zone thoroughly, but take care not to cross paths with the butlers.</p>

<p>You had to be careful not to run into a butler for no reason and get hit with a beam of light.</p>

<p>They'd probably hidden somewhere else the moment they sensed a butler's presence from a distance.</p>

<p>In the midst of all this, Je Hyun-oh had trimmed the trees so perfectly that it actually pissed me off a little….</p>

<p>"The Cleaner assigned here is reasonably competent."</p>

<p>The Cat Butler, looking at the neatly rounded boxwoods, praised Je Hyun-oh's skill and built up some goodwill.</p>

<p>He was satisfied with Kwak Hanmuk's zone too, which made me feel oddly proud to myself.</p>

<p>And at last, night came.</p>

<p>I'd eaten dinner early and was resting in my room when I lit the lantern.</p>

<p>The lantern the Rabbit Butler had prepared for me in advance was just the right size.</p>

<p>It was shaped to mimic a gas lantern, but it actually ran on some unknown fuel and operated by touch, of all things.</p>

<p>I picked up the lantern and was about to head outside when I stopped short.</p>

<p>'Feel like I'm forgetting something.'</p>

<p>I thought hard, and then I remembered Samra's item.</p>

<p>The holographic color badge was sitting quietly in my Inventory.</p>

<p>But there was no particular reason to check it right this moment.</p>

<p>Butler work came first. I decided I'd look at it later when I had time, and stepped out into the corridor.</p>

<p>The moment I stepped into the pitch-black corridor, a scream rang out.</p>

<p>"GYAAAH!"</p>

<p>I saw a Cleaner fleeing in a panic.</p>

<p>I swung my lantern and shouted.</p>

<p>"This way!"</p>

<p>The Cleaner came running toward me in a frantic rush.</p>

<p>"I'm — alive — haah!"</p>

<p>When the Cleaner saw my butler uniform and startled, I made a shushing gesture.</p>

<p>A voice called out from the distance.</p>

<p>"■■, where are you? It's too dark here, I can't tell…. ■■, you didn't abandon me, did you…?"</p>

<p>The Cleaner's eyes wavered — maybe it was the voice of the colleague they'd been fleeing with.</p>

<p>I handed them the lantern.</p>

<p>The Cleaner, who'd been looking at me with unease, snatched the lantern and ran toward where their colleague was.</p>

<p>They must have figured that as long as they drove away the darkness, they could handle a Creature somehow.</p>

<p>But before long, a wretched plea rang out.</p>

<p>"AAAAH! PLEASE, SAVE ME!"</p>

<p>I walked slowly.</p>

<p>When I reached the place the light had led to, the Cleaner was nowhere to be seen.</p>

<p>Instead, a gentleman in a crisp tailcoat was strolling down the corridor, opening the doors to rooms one by one.</p>

<p>From the gentleman in the white smiling-face mask came the voice of the Cleaner who had fled moments ago.</p>

<p>"AAAAH! PLEASE, SAVE ME! AAAAH! PLEASE, SAVE ME! AAAAH! PLEASE, SAVE ME!"</p>

<p>The gentleman approached me.</p>

<p>He came closer, the tails of his coat fluttering, and politely held out the lantern the Cleaner had taken.</p>

<p>"You've worked hard."</p>

<p>When I offered him encouragement, the gentleman trembled all over as if delighted.</p>

<p>Then he left, motivated, to find other prey.</p>

<p>I picked up the lantern again and moved on.</p>

<p>I went down to the second floor of the East Wing and first scanned the corridor.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk, the one I was looking for, was nowhere to be seen. Instead, a group of Cleaners came bursting out from around a corner, all clatter and chaos.</p>

<p>As they fled in a panic, I quickly swung my lantern to show them.</p>

<p>"Over here! This way!"</p>

<p>The Cleaners who saw the lantern came running like moths to a flame.</p>

<p>Five of them in total.</p>

<p>'That's a decent number.'</p>

<p>In that case, the dance studio would do nicely.</p>

<p>I opened the door to a room a few steps away and went inside, and the Cleaners who'd come running from afar all followed me in.</p>

<p>Every one of them was pale-faced — they'd probably been chased by a Creature.</p>

<p>"Huff, haah…. You're…!"</p>

<p>One of them recognized me.</p>

<p>"You damn — you came here as a Cleaner, so why the hell are you the only one in a butler uniform!"</p>

<p>But the others, instead of getting angry, tried to win me over.</p>

<p>"Hey, he was a Cleaner anyway."</p>

<p>"You're still trying to save us now, right?"</p>

<p>I left them to their forced smiles and sidelong glances and turned on the light in the room.</p>

<p>The dim room lit up with a click.</p>

<p>It was the spotlight on the small stage installed at the very back of the dance studio.</p>

<p>On the stage, where a circle of light fell, stood a ballerina made of white metal.</p>

<p>With her smooth, featureless face, she gave us an elegant bow.</p>

<p>I requested a song from her.</p>

<p>"There are a lot of people, so allegro, please."</p>

<p>The clear sound of an oboe flowed out.</p>

<p>It was the Black Swan dance from Swan Lake.</p>

<p>Normally, the slow adagio version and the fast allegro version played at random, but at my request, it proceeded as allegro.</p>

<p>The metal ballerina in the black tutu danced to the fast, dynamic music.</p>

<p>The Cleaners stared at the metal ballerina, frozen stupidly, even though they knew they had to run right then.</p>

<p>I, too, fell under the spell of the metal ballerina's movements along with them.</p>

<p>While she stepped lightly and flew about, the Cleaners gradually drew closer to the metal ballerina.</p>

<p>It was an act they performed without conscious awareness.</p>

<p>The music reached its climax, and the metal ballerina looked at me.</p>

<p>She began the fouetté section — thirty-two consecutive turns.</p>

<p>Turn after turn followed, balancing straight on one leg while whipping the other around.</p>

<p>Every time she swung her leg, the Cleaners' bodies twisted.</p>

<p>Screech, screek. The sound of entire bodies contorting lay beneath the beautiful melody.</p>

<p>When she finally finished the fouetté.</p>

<p>Clap clap clap clap!</p>

<p>I gave her an enthusiastic round of applause.</p>

<p>The metal ballerina returned a delighted bow to me.</p>

<p>At her feet lay the Cleaners, their bodies twisted like screws, scattered haphazardly.</p>

<p>But neither she nor I paid them the slightest attention.</p>

<p>The Creatures were all fitting for a beautiful, elegant mansion.</p>

<p>The only ugly filth here was the Cleaners.</p>

<p>'I completely understand why the butlers hate Cleaners so much.'</p>

<p>I felt my shoulders swell with the pride of being a butler of this mansion.</p>

<p>I pretended not to see the ballerina ravenously tearing into the Cleaners.</p>

<p>A lady's meal was not to be disturbed.</p>

<p>I was about to step back out into the corridor when I heard a voice outside calling for me.</p>

<p>"Goyo! Han Goyo! Roasted Chestnut! Sweet Chestnut!!"</p>

<p>It was Kwak Hanmuk's voice.</p>
