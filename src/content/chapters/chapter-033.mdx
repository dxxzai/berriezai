<h1>Chapter 33</h1>

<p>I stared at the system window announcing the closure.</p>

<p>Then slowly turned my gaze, scanning my surroundings.</p>

<p>No one was there.</p>

<p>Yi Seon, who had been shedding black tears of blood — gone. Tak Jucheol and Paeng Sangdo, who had howled like a choir — wiped clean away.</p>

<p>'I didn't even get to say a proper goodbye.'</p>

<p>Smiley flashed through my mind. The one who'd turned into an item right before my eyes.</p>

<p>Having the same thing happen twice made it feel... hollow, somehow.</p>

<p>Even knowing they were just NPCs, I never really thought about it while inside the Trial. They were right there, alive and moving, so I kept forgetting they were meant to disappear. I'd catch myself vaguely thinking I could meet them again anytime.</p>

<p>Regardless of how I felt, the system window diligently kept flashing noisy notifications.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<div class="systembox_wrap">
<p>
◆ &lt;Han Goyo&gt; Epic Quest: Close 3 Trials within one month.<br/>

Trials currently closed (1/3)<br/>

(Warning! Quest failure will result in limb amputation! Success will preserve limbs.)
</p>
</div>
</div>


<div class="systembox">
<p>
'Han Goyo' has made a name known in Akasha!
</p>
</div>

<div class="systembox"><p>■■ ■■■ is watching you.</p></div>

<div class="systembox"><p>Current Recognition: 0.05%</p></div>

<p>I shook off the loneliness and read the system window.</p>

<p>Whoa, Recognition.</p>

<p>'Last time it only gave me 0.01%.'</p>

<p>This time it went up by a whopping 0.03% — a very generous increase.</p>

<p>Still had no idea what I was supposed to use it for, though.</p>

<p>'And I still don't know what "Black Block is watching you" means either.'</p>

<p>I shifted my gaze to the cassette tape player in my hand.</p>

<p>The system window popped up again.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>You feel you absolutely must listen to this music.</p></div>

<p>It was similar to the message I'd gotten when I received the Happy Smile Candy before.</p>

<p>I was planning to check it out anyway.</p>

<p>I was curious why the contents had changed, and I wanted to hear the music inside.</p>

<p>'I'll ask Samra to listen to it for me.'</p>

<p>Since it might be dangerous, I planned to have Samra — who'd probably be fine even if he listened to the music — handle it instead.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<div class="systembox_wrap">
<p>
The Trial &lt;Dead Zone Delivery&gt; has ended.<br/>

The Trial will be permanently closed in 80 seconds.
</p>
</div>
</div>


<div class="systembox">
<div class="systembox_wrap">
<p>
You are the Top Contributor of &lt;Dead Zone Delivery&gt;.<br/>

You may take 2 items.
</p>
</div>
</div>

<p>As expected, the Top Contributor notification appeared.</p>

<p>I'd already picked one item. The portable cassette tape player.</p>

<p>The second item was the problem.</p>

<p>I wanted to ask the Captains if there was anything the TRA needed, but they weren't around, so I had to choose alone.</p>

<p>Turns out I didn't need to worry.</p>

<p>"...Ah."</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Item selection complete.</p></div></div>

<p>Because I couldn't choose any more items.</p>

<p>The player and the cassette tape inside it were apparently recognized as separate items, counting as two selections.</p>

<p>'Can I change it?'</p>

<p>I regretted my choice as I faced the prospect of walking out with just a cassette tape player.</p>

<p>But there was no cancel button in sight.</p>

<p>"Cancel. Change. Undo...?"</p>

<p>I tried saying a few things despite the embarrassment, but the system window remained silent.</p>

<p>Looked like I was going to have to become someone who really loved music.</p>

<p>'It came from a Trial, so maybe it has music that doesn't exist in human history.'</p>

<p>Maybe it was a new cultural artifact that could bring great progress to the music world.</p>

<p>Hoping that was the case, I packed the cassette tape player.</p>

<p>Eighty seconds later, the world crumpled into a kaleidoscope of colors.</p>

<p>I emerged from the Trial.</p>
<hr class="calibre3"/>
<p>It felt like I'd come back after a very long time.</p>

<p>In reality, probably about five or six days had passed.</p>

<p>Before I could even open my eyes on the cold laboratory floor, my entire body ached.</p>

<p>"Ugh..."</p>

<p>A groan escaped me involuntarily. I swallowed the pain while still lying face-down.</p>

<p>It was because all the thread and bandages I'd used to treat my wounds inside DeZonDeal had disappeared.</p>

<p>Having paid the price of being Top Contributor, I curled up motionless for a while.</p>

<p>'It didn't hurt this much inside DeZonDeal, did it?'</p>

<p>It seemed the painkiller effect had vanished along with everything else.</p>

<p>After groaning for a bit, I finally lifted my head and looked around.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein came into view first. They didn't seem to have regained consciousness yet.</p>

<p>I'd been alone at the end, so I'd worried a little, but it was a relief.</p>

<p>'What about the Lieutenant Colonel?'</p>

<p>He wasn't someone I needed to worry about, but since I was checking on the Captains, I looked for him too.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh was holed up in the farthest corner.</p>

<p>From the way he sat with his back against the wall, he seemed to already be conscious.</p>

<p>His outfit was exactly the same as inside the Trial.</p>

<p>Goggles and gas mask, hoodie and combat uniform.</p>

<p>With his metal hand dangling loosely, he looked like a big toy robot that had run out of power.</p>

<p>Not that he was ever the kind of person you'd attach a cute description like "toy robot" to, but...</p>

<p>I felt a little strange.</p>

<p>I'd seen everything Je Hyun-oh did inside the Trial. How he rampaged.</p>

<p>The sight of him gleefully slaughtering zombies right in front of me was vivid in my memory.</p>

<p>But back in reality, he seemed to have no will, no drive at all.</p>

<p>I called out softly to him as he sat there without even a twitch.</p>

<p>"Lieutenant Colonel Je."</p>

<p>His face, which had been staring at the floor, moved very slowly.</p>

<p>My reflection appeared in his goggles.</p>

<p>"Uh..."</p>

<p>I'd called him, but now I had nothing to say. I brought up a safe topic.</p>

<p>"Nothing unusual happened? When we cleared, I wasn't with—"</p>

<p>I was speaking with only my head raised while still lying down, when I flinched.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh had suddenly stood up.</p>

<p>His goggles flickered to life.</p>

<div class="hyunoh"><p>*^ ^*</p></div>

<p>Symbols appeared on either side of the goggle's smiley emoticon.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh changed both his metal hands into Crow Gloves and walked toward me.</p>

<p>He bent down right in front of me, then tapped me with his hand.</p>

<p>The gesture seemed to check if I was really alive, so I spoke in a dying voice.</p>

<p>"I'm alive..."</p>

<p>A corpse couldn't exactly talk. Je Hyun-oh withdrew his hand.</p>

<p>"Lieutenant Colonel Je."</p>

<div class="hyunoh"><p>? ?</p></div>

<p>"Are you satisfied now?"</p>

<p>I didn't know what he wanted from me, but since he told me not to die, I'd come back alive even from inside a boss monster's guts.</p>

<p>'Isn't this enough?'</p>

<p>From now on, I just wanted to focus on clearing Trials in a peaceful team atmosphere.</p>

<p>'Please.'</p>

<p>I looked up at Je Hyun-oh with earnestness.</p>

<p>"Yeah."</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh answered quietly, moving his gloved fingers like he was playing the piano.</p>

<p>Since they weren't metal hands, there was no clicking sound.</p>

<p>He looked at me and spoke.</p>

<p>"Goyo, live long."</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>When he was throwing me at the boss monster, it was one thing — now he was telling me to live long.</p>

<p>But I reminded myself again that this was Je Hyun-oh, and quickly resigned myself.</p>

<p>'At least he's not telling me to die.'</p>

<p>Anyway, since he'd told me to live long, I figured I didn't need to worry about my life from now on.</p>

<p>Felt like I'd been through one hell of an initiation.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh pressed down on the earpiece in my ear.</p>

<p>Immediately, Samra's voice came through.</p>

<p>—Han Goyo-ssi.</p>

<p>I reported to him.</p>

<p>"&lt;Dead Zone Delivery&gt; has been closed. All Special Mission Team members will return to the TRA."</p>

<p>No sound came from the earpiece.</p>

<p>Just as I wondered if the connection had cut out, a reply came.</p>

<p>—Yes, Goyo-ssi. I'll be waiting.</p>

<p>It might be an unusual expression for an android, but I felt like I could sense joy in his voice.</p>

<p>Samra announced my success in a gentle voice.</p>

<p>—Special Mission Team's first mission complete.</p>
<hr class="calibre3"/>
<p>As soon as I returned to the TRA, I received emergency treatment.</p>

<p>The wounds that had been stitched up inside DeZonDeal had reopened and started bleeding — since I'd already lost a lot of blood, it almost got dangerous.</p>

<p>Mo Haein and Kwak Hanmuk were also unconscious for a while, due to the aftereffects of the zombification that had progressed.</p>

<p>Mo Haein especially had been a full zombie for a while, so she was out longer.</p>

<p>Fortunately, there were no other major issues.</p>

<p>As soon as he returned to the TRA, Je Hyun-oh was dispatched to a short Trial that could be finished quickly before the next Special Mission Team assignment.</p>

<p>It was a nominal punishment for attacking a team member.</p>

<p>He probably wanted to enter a Trial anyway, and he'd adjust his schedule to return in time for the Special Mission Team mission, so I wasn't particularly worried.</p>

<p>After receiving treatment, I woke up having been unconscious longer than even the Captains.</p>

<p>"...?"</p>

<p>As soon as I opened my eyes, I was experiencing something strange.</p>

<p>Snip snip.</p>

<p>Snip snip snip.</p>

<p>Someone was cutting my nails with small scissors...</p>

<p>I was so startled that I couldn't even signal that I was awake, just watched the scene.</p>

<p>The person who had been deeply focused on cutting my nails belatedly felt my gaze and looked up.</p>

<p>"Hyaaack!"</p>

<p>And screamed.</p>

<p>I wondered if I should be the one screaming here, but I stayed calm and tried to identify her first.</p>

<p>"Who are you?"</p>

<p>The chubby-cheeked woman wore large round glasses and a white coat — she looked more like a researcher than a doctor.</p>

<p>The way she looked at me was similar to Yi Gaon when she'd realized I was the cure.</p>

<p>"Ah, hello... I'm, um, from Division 2..."</p>

<p>"You mean the Research and Analysis Division?"</p>

<p>"Yes, yes."</p>

<p>The woman suddenly bowed her head in greeting.</p>

<p>All the while, she clutched the sample tube containing my nails as if it were precious.</p>

<p>In a very small voice, she announced her name.</p>

<p>"My name is Ham Jiwol... I'm the Chief...!"</p>

<p>As soon as I heard the name, I quickly clenched my fist to hide my nails.</p>

<p>Ham Jiwol made a sound of dismay, her eyes and mouth rounding in surprise.</p>

<p>It seemed she hadn't collected as many of my nails as she wanted.</p>

<p>'Thank goodness... No, wait — is that unlucky?'</p>

<p>Research and Analysis Division Chief, Ham Jiwol.</p>

<p>She wasn't someone I encountered often while playing the Archive's games.</p>

<p>That was because meeting Ham Jiwol came with a 100% probability of...</p>

<p>'A bad ending.'</p>

<p>Only endings where the player was dragged off as a lab experiment subject and experienced horrific things.</p>

<p>Knowing how brutal Ham Jiwol's human experiments were, I couldn't help but feel terrified.</p>

<p>I was trembling internally when, by the greatest stroke of luck, a savior appeared.</p>

<p>"Han Goyo-ssi. You're awake?"</p>

<p>The one who opened the hospital room door and entered was Samra. I'd never been so glad to see him.</p>

<p>"Yes! I'm perfectly fine. I can be discharged right now."</p>

<p>"Ah, ah...!"</p>

<p>Ham Jiwol fidgeted in front of me, but I desperately pretended not to notice.</p>

<p>Meanwhile, I shot Samra a pleading look.</p>

<p>Samra took his time collecting data on my physical reactions before finally rescuing me.</p>

<p>"Chief Ham Jiwol. Could you step aside? I need to discuss the next mission with Han Goyo-ssi."</p>

<p>"Ah... Samra, um, I... I only got 0.06 grams of nail... There's other stuff too..."</p>

<p>"Sample data collection is already sufficient. Anything beyond that is Chief Ham Jiwol's personal greed."</p>

<p>"...That's—"</p>

<p>Ham Jiwol closed her mouth without further argument, and Samra expertly shooed her out of the hospital room.</p>

<p>"Thank you."</p>

<p>"So you know about Chief Ham Jiwol as well."</p>

<p>"Not much."</p>

<p>Samra stood by the hospital bed and looked at me. It wasn't easy looking up at the tall android.</p>

<p>When I tilted my head way back, Samra obligingly sat down on a chair.</p>

<p>"Are the Captains alright?"</p>

<p>"Captain Kwak Hanmuk and Captain Mo Haein are currently attending to their respective team duties."</p>

<p>Fortunately, they seemed fine. As I felt relieved, Samra informed me of the next mission.</p>

<p>"It's still under discussion, but the Special Mission Team's second mission will likely be assigned &lt;Clean! Clean! Clean!&gt;."</p>

<p>We were scheduled to enter in a week, and if I wanted to adjust the timing, I should let him know.</p>

<p>But I was the one in a hurry.</p>

<p>'Limb amputation...'</p>

<p>I said there was no need.</p>

<p>Honestly, for Clean3, entering in a week was fine. It was a relatively easier Trial than DeZonDeal.</p>

<p>Instead, I asked for something else.</p>

<p>"There's an item I'd like to acquire before the mission."</p>

<p>"I'll submit a request to the Asset Management Division."</p>

<p>"Well, the thing is, the TRA doesn't have this item... There should be exactly one in Korea, and as far as I know, the owner is the Chairman of the Cheon-gang Group."</p>

<p>The moment he heard my words, Samra immediately knew what item I meant.</p>

<p>"'Good-Looking'?"</p>

<p>"Yes. Having that item would be a huge help in clearing the Trial. Is there any way we could ask the Chairman for cooperation?"</p>

<p>"It's possible."</p>

<p>"...!"</p>

<p>Seeing Samra answer so confidently, it really did seem possible.</p>

<p>But before I could feel happy, Samra added something puzzling.</p>

<p>"However, Han Goyo-ssi must personally meet the Cheon-gang Chairman and persuade them."</p>

<p>"What?"</p>

<p>"The Cheon-gang Chairman will absolutely not hand over the item to anyone but Han Goyo-ssi."</p>

<p>It didn't make sense. If persuasion was needed, wouldn't a high-ranking TRA official be much better suited?</p>

<p>I'd never even seen the Cheon-gang Chairman from a distance—</p>

<p>'No, wait.'</p>

<p>Had I... seen the Chairman before?</p>
