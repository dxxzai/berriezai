<h1>Chapter 53</h1>

<p>Ever since I entered Morneveil and met Dominic, the choices had been strange.</p>

<p>But I had been too busy worrying about Dominic to properly think about it.</p>

<p>In truth, the choice errors were something I couldn’t solve myself, so I had simply ignored them.</p>

<p>However, one thing was clear.</p>

<p>The choices being repeatedly pushed in front of me were guiding me in a specific direction.</p>

<p>The choices wanted me to proceed with the cult leader route.</p>

<p>Because they kept presenting options to join the cult and wanted me to become interested in the cult’s god.</p>

<p>And now, they had finally gone so far as to present me with the choice to use the cult’s sacred relic to summon a god.</p>

<p>I knew the God of the Void.</p>

<p>It was the god whose summoning had failed due to a game error in the cult leader route of CallOVo.</p>

<p>I was convinced that the God of the Void was the true ending of CallOVo.</p>

<p>The game’s title itself was The Call of the Void, wasn’t it?</p>

<p>Besides, the descriptions used to refer to him had been extraordinary.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_pixel"><p>He is the eternal abyss, the origin of chaos, and the absolute void! A terror that creates and destroys!</p></div>

<p>I recalled the most extravagant title given to the God of the Void.</p>

<p>‘The Lord of All Things… that was it.’</p>

<p>It was a title that clearly belonged to a final ending.</p>

<p>But now I was being told to attempt the summoning that had failed back then.</p>

<p>In the reporter route.</p>

<p>And in front of Dominic, of all people.</p>

<p>As I stared at the system window in front of me, I moved my gaze.</p>

<p>The person I expected to be looking at the Shining Scalene Polyhedron was looking at me instead.</p>

<p>As if he was waiting to see what choice I would make.</p>

<p>My heart began beating faster from the tension.</p>

<p>I absolutely must not perform the summoning ritual in front of Dominic.</p>

<p>A primal instinct screamed a warning throughout my entire body.</p>

<p>But the only choice available to me was the summoning.</p>

<p>Like someone standing before a time bomb moments away from exploding, cold sweat formed on my skin.</p>

<p>I checked the choices again.</p>

<p>‘As expected, this is the only one.’</p>

<p>I selected the final option.</p>

<p>“Dominic-ssi. Let us summon the God of the Void together.”</p>

<p>My body moved against my will and spoke the predetermined line.</p>

<p>Holding the metal box containing the Shining Scalene Polyhedron carefully with both hands, I shouted with a face filled with ecstasy.</p>

<p>“If it is Dominic-ssi and me, we can surely summon the god. The Lord of All Things shall descend upon this land!”</p>

<p>It was a perfectly cult-like line.</p>

<p>I tried to say something else afterward, but just as expected, actions and dialogue that completely contradicted the previous statement were impossible.</p>

<p>That was only natural.</p>

<p>This was a trial based on a choice-selection game.</p>

<p>I had no choice but to stand stiffly and wait for Dominic’s reaction.</p>

<p>Unlike the outer shell of myself, which looked like a fanatic ready to begin the summoning ritual at any moment, my true thoughts were being crushed by anxiety.</p>

<p>Before I realized it, Dominic’s eyes had become calm.</p>

<p>He slowly opened his lips.</p>

<p>“Roasted Chestnut.”</p>

<p>Please.</p>

<p>Please…</p>

<p>“Weren’t you trying to expose the cult’s crimes as a reporter?”</p>

<p>Yes!</p>

<p>I resisted the urge to hug Dominic and celebrate.</p>

<p>Dominic reacted exactly as I wanted him to.</p>

<p>Honestly, it had been a gamble.</p>

<p>While the only available choice was one that demanded summoning the god, the final option was the only one that mentioned Dominic.</p>

<p>The text was larger and looked the most suspicious, but I had bet everything on the phrase 'together with Dominic.'</p>

<p>I judged that Dominic would notice the inconsistency between my previous behavior and the actions forced by the choice system.</p>

<p>Because he was an existence outside the system.</p>

<p>And there was one more reason.</p>

<p>“Besides, ‘the Lord of All Things’? What a ridiculous expression. I have never placed anyone above myself.”</p>

<p>Dominic’s true nature was not that of a doctor.</p>

<p>It was that of a ‘master.’</p>

<p>Therefore, I assumed he would find the very concept of summoning an existence far above himself unpleasant.</p>

<p>The ‘master’ of the mansion must possess an overwhelmingly high status.</p>

<p>So even if he had lost his memories, I predicted that he would not appreciate me saying there was someone above him.</p>

<p>Fortunately, Dominic acted exactly as I expected.</p>

<p>With tears of emotion in my eyes, I showered Dominic with praise.</p>

<p>“I’m sorry. I think I momentarily had a misguided thought. I’m truly fortunate that someone like you, Dominic-ssi, who always guides me down the right path, is my companion.”</p>

<p>I continued praising him so that if something similar happened again, Dominic would once again rescue me from the hell of forced choices.</p>

<p>I quickly blamed my sudden behavior on the insane item.</p>

<p>“Actually, Dominic-ssi. About what I just said and did… I know it may be hard to believe, but it was not my own will. I think it was because of the power of this object.”</p>

<p>I hurriedly placed the metal box on the floor and quickly took out my camera to take pictures.</p>

<p>The photo came out blurry, so I took another one before closing the box.</p>

<p>I wanted to leave this place as soon as possible.</p>

<p>However, unlike me, who stood up, Dominic remained sitting with his knees bent.</p>

<p>“Roasted Chestnut.”</p>

<p>Our eyes were at nearly the same height.</p>

<p>Only slightly lower than mine.</p>

<p>I answered nervously.</p>

<p>“Yes…?”</p>

<p>“May I examine this object a little longer?”</p>

<p>I couldn’t say no.</p>

<p>So I answered timidly.</p>

<p>“It seems like a dangerous object.”</p>

<p>“But when I look at it, I feel as though something is about to come back to me.”</p>

<p>The inside of the secret room was dark.</p>

<p>And yet Dominic’s blue eyes shone brightly, like stars emitting their own light.</p>

<p>I looked into his eyes while being careful not to be swallowed by the abyss pretending to be starlight.</p>

<p>“Dominic-ssi.”</p>

<p>“Yes, Roasted Chestnut?”</p>

<p>“I would prefer if you did not.”</p>

<p>I placed a hand over my chest, and gently—very gently—placed my other hand on Dominic’s shoulder as I pleaded.</p>

<p>“I’m afraid something might happen to you, Dominic-ssi.”</p>

<p>There wasn’t a single lie in those words.</p>

<p>Because if something truly happened to Dominic, my life would simply become dust scattered across the universe.</p>

<p>Whether he summoned the God of the Void or recovered his memories, either way, I was dead.</p>

<p>“You don’t need to push yourself like this. I’m sure you will recover your memories eventually.”</p>

<p>I absolutely did not say that I would help him recover his memories.</p>

<p>I was already atoning in real time after receiving a harsh lesson from the butlers.</p>

<p>At my sincere words, Dominic stared at me silently for a moment.</p>

<p>Then he repeated one part of what I had said.</p>

<p>“Eventually…”</p>

<p>I had no idea why that was the only part that left an impression on him.</p>

<p>Fortunately, Dominic gave up on the box and stood.</p>

<p>“Let us leave this place.”</p>

<p>Having successfully convinced him, I quickly left with the hunched-over Dominic.</p>

<p>Just before leaving, I glanced back.</p>

<p>The metal box containing the Shining Scalene Polyhedron emitted a faint glow even in the darkness.</p>
<br/>
<p><span class="flashback">“Something I have seen before in the past.”</span></p>
<br/>
<p>If I had my way, I would have interrogated him thoroughly.</p>

<p>When?</p>

<p>Where?</p>

<p>How had he seen it?</p>

<p>But doing so might have triggered him to remember his past.</p>

<p>I was doing my best to avoid anything related to his past.</p>

<p>From now on, I had no intention of saying a single word about anything connected to his memories.</p>

<p>What kind of existence was Dominic?</p>

<p>Carrying an unanswered question that I would never be able to solve, I tore my gaze away from the metal box.</p>

<p>With eight pieces of evidence collected and two companions recruited, I greeted the morning with a satisfied feeling.</p>

<p>Yesterday, after finishing my evidence collection inside the cult, I barely managed to shake off Edel, who kept trying to stop me, and found the train station.</p>

<p>I sent the Breath of Mist item to London to request an analysis of its ingredients, and afterward, I wandered around Morneveil, expanding the available movement options.</p>

<p>I traveled together with Dominic, and there was not a single person in Morneveil who didn’t know him.</p>

<p>It had reached the point where I wondered whether Edel’s statement that Dominic had treated every person in Morneveil was not an exaggeration, but the truth.</p>

<p>Before meeting Dominic, who would be in the living room, I took a moment alone in the bedroom to calm myself by taking deep breaths.</p>

<p>Then, I bravely opened the bedroom door.</p>

<p>“Dominic-ssi, good morning—what is this?”</p>

<p>I had been about to greet him energetically, but I froze in shock.</p>

<p>The enormous living room was filled with flowers.</p>

<p>In a place covered with all kinds of flowers, Dominic, who had been sitting there pretending to read the newspaper and pretending to drink tea, greeted me.</p>

<p>“Good morning, Roasted Chestnut.”</p>

<p>Ridiculously enough, Dominic sitting among the flowers actually looked quite fitting.</p>

<p>I muttered in confusion.</p>

<p>“No… Why are there flowers…”</p>

<p>Then, before finishing my sentence, something suddenly came to mind.</p>

<p>“Could it be Lady Edel?”</p>

<p>Dominic gave a faint smile and answered.</p>

<p>“How did you know? Miss Edel sent these flowers to Roasted Chestnut.”</p>

<p>“……”</p>

<p>There was a reason I immediately guessed Edel.</p>

<p>It was because something similar had happened when I recruited Edel as a companion while playing the cult leader route in the game.</p>

<p>At that time, since we were staying together inside the cult, Edel would come looking for me whenever she felt like it.</p>

<p>Giving me mountains of flowers and gifts was the mildest thing she did.</p>

<p>I would wake up in the morning only to find her singing and dancing in my bedroom.</p>

<p>Or she would create a performance praising me and show it to me together with the apostles.</p>

<p>Or she would suddenly burst into tears and say how fortunate she was to be able to serve Roasted Chestnut-nim.</p>

<p>She did everything a devoted cult follower could possibly do.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_pixel"><p>“Ahhh, Roasted Chestnut-nim. My beloved prophet. I can do anything for Roasted Chestnut-nim!”</p></div>

<p>In the game, from the moment she gave me her heart, she passionately followed me and expressed her affection.</p>

<p>Naturally, I couldn’t help but grow attached to Edel as well.</p>

<p>Though I never expected things would turn out like this while playing the reporter route.</p>

<p>‘When I was the cult leader, it was nice being able to change the apostles’ clothes.’</p>

<p>Remembering how I had changed everyone into more modest outfits, I checked the card that had arrived along with the flower explosion.</p>
<div class="card_wrapper">
<p>Please accept these as repayment for the flowers you gave me yesterday.
I really want to show Little Lamb the ritual that will be held tonight.
Please come meet me.</p>

<p>— With love, Edel.</p>
</div>
<p>We’ve only known each other for two days. Isn’t it a little early to be talking about love already…?</p>

<p>Her aggressive letter left me flustered.</p>

<p>“Are you going?”</p>

<p>Dominic asked with amusement.</p>

<p>I quietly shook my head.</p>

<p>“I won’t be able to.”</p>

<p>Seeing the curiosity in his expression, I explained the reason.</p>

<p>“Because today’s ritual will not happen.”</p>

<p>“Are we the ones making that happen?”</p>

<p>“That’s right.”</p>

<p>The third day in Morneveil.</p>

<p>Today, I had to rescue the people being held as sacrifices for the cult’s human sacrifice ritual.</p>

<p>It was an important event in the reporter route, where I could stop the human sacrifice while also obtaining evidence by eliminating the sacrifices themselves.</p>

<p>After explaining this plan to Dominic, I told him the first thing we needed to do.</p>

<p>“First, we need to visit City Hall and persuade the mayor of Morneveil.”</p>

<p>He was someone who had become accustomed to the benefits provided by the Apostles of the Void.</p>

<p>Since he was in league with the cult, persuading him would not be easy, so I needed to prepare thoroughly beforehand.</p>

<p>After listening to my plan, Dominic asked:</p>

<p>“What comes next?”</p>

<p>“We’ll go to where the sacrifices are being held and rescue them.”</p>

<p>The plan was to receive official support from the mayor so that the rescued people could be safely protected.</p>

<p>It was just as I was about to explain the operation to Dominic.</p>

<p>Bang. Bang. Bang.</p>

<p>Someone knocked on the door.</p>

<p>It was far too rough to be a housekeeper.</p>

<p>I opened the door with a puzzled expression.</p>

<p>Police officers were standing in the hallway.</p>

<p>A man holding a baton looked at me with a suspicious, guarded expression.</p>

<p>“Morneveil Police Department. Are you Little Lamb?”</p>

<p>“Yes, that’s me…?”</p>

<p>Click.</p>

<p>Cold metal handcuffs were locked around my wrists.</p>

<p>A shout so loud it made my ears ring rang out.</p>

<p>“Little Lamb! You are under arrest for the murder of the mayor of Morneveil!”</p>
