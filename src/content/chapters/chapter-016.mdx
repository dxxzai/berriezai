<h1>Chapter 16</h1>

<p>Je Hyun-oh wasn't my first Registration. I had already registered Park Seonggyeon as a Sample before him.</p>

<p>But this time was completely different.</p>

<p>A searing, boiling heat tore through my entire body.</p>

<p>It was unmistakably excruciating pain. And yet, a strange sensation that arrived at the same time made me feel wrong.</p>

<p>Liberation? Relief? Catharsis…?</p>

<p>A feeling I couldn't put into words pressed down on the pain and drove itself deep into my brain.</p>

<p>A surging sense of elation curled the corners of my mouth on its own.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Registration complete!</p></div>

<p>I already knew what I had to do.</p>

<p>It came as naturally as a newborn lamb seeking its mother's first milk.</p>

<p>'Prohibition. Message.'</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Prohibiting 'Je Hyun-oh'.</p></div>

<div class="systembox"><p>You formulate a Message to send to 'Je Hyun-oh'.</p></div>

<p>My nose throbbed. It felt as if the heat behind my eyes had traveled downward.</p>

<p>I licked the metallic taste from my lips and sent the Message. I could see the message window appear in front of Je Hyun-oh, blocking his path.</p>

<p>"Captain, don't attack!"</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk, who had been preparing a strike, faltered.</p>

<p>"Just ram him!"</p>

<p>If Je Hyun-oh counterattacked, both of us would be torn to pieces on the spot.</p>

<p>But there was no time to deliberate.</p>

<p>"Captain Kwak!!"</p>

<p>The moment I called out to him, Kwak Hanmuk burst into laughter like a madman.</p>

<p>Instead of attacking, the Bead Rosary whipped around and bound Kwak Hanmuk and me together. So tightly that nothing could separate us, no matter what happened.</p>

<p>"Fuck— Lieutenant Colonel, sir! Incoming rookie!"</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk's maniacal laughter mixed with the ripping roar of the engine. The motorcycle charged straight ahead.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh, immobilized by the Prohibition, filled my vision.</p>

<p>And the System window floating in front of him.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Stay still.</p></div>

<p>The gaze that had been fixed on the System window turned toward me.</p>

<p>My face reflected on its smooth surface, unlit by any LED glow.</p>

<p>But the face behind the goggles remained unknowable.</p>

<p>Just before the motorcycle crumpled.</p>

<p>The Bead Rosary completely covered our view. Kwak Hanmuk and I were encased within a cocoon it had spun.</p>

<p>CRASH!</p>

<p>An explosion of noise, like ramming headlong into a steel wall. The brutal impact felt like it rattled my internal organs.</p>

<p>The Bead Rosary unraveled, and Kwak Hanmuk and I tumbled messily across the floor.</p>

<p>"Guh…!"</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk coughed up a mouthful of blood. The Bead Rosary had absorbed a significant portion of the impact, but it couldn't block one hundred percent of it.</p>

<p>The alien metallic substance that made up Je Hyun-oh was fundamentally incompatible with the Zelkova wood Bead Rosary, which didn't mix well with metal to begin with.</p>

<p>Despite the pool of blood forming beneath him, Kwak Hanmuk checked on Je Hyun-oh before his own condition.</p>

<p>A brief silence passed. Then a slightly bewildered voice crept out of him.</p>

<p>"…Lieutenant Colonel?"</p>

<p>At the end of a long skid mark on the floor, Je Hyun-oh lay sprawled out.</p>

<p>As limp as a doll with its strings cut.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk spoke, his voice laced with wishful thinking.</p>

<p>"He dead?"</p>

<p>Of course, he said it knowing full well there was no way that was true.</p>

<p>I staggered to my feet and made my way over to Kwak Hanmuk.</p>

<p>"Right now… I think we should move. Quickly."</p>

<p>We couldn't afford to waste this opportunity while Je Hyun-oh was immobilized.</p>

<p>But then Kwak Hanmuk suddenly grabbed my face with both hands.</p>

<p>He stared at me, still and silent.</p>

<p>My cheeks squished in his grip, I quietly protested.</p>

<p>"That hurts…."</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk didn't even flinch at the complaint. After peering at me intently for a long moment, he muttered.</p>

<p>"It came back."</p>

<p>"What came back?"</p>

<p>"Nah. You okay?"</p>

<p>"I'm fine. What about you, Captain Kwak?"</p>

<p>"I'm fucking fantastic."</p>

<p>…Kwak Hanmuk answered with a grin, blood streaming down from the corners of his mouth.</p>

<p>I probably didn't look much better. I tugged at my sleeve and roughly wiped under my nose and along my chin.</p>

<p>"We need to move."</p>

<p>"Yeah, let's go."</p>

<p>He rummaged through the backpack and duffel bag that had been flung far away.</p>

<p>He tossed the burst triangle kimbap, crushed cans, plastic bottles, and wrecked firearms, then repacked only the food that was still intact, along with the axe, a knife, and the like.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk slung the duffel bag over his shoulder, positioned me behind him, and approached Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh was still lying there, perfectly still.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk flicked his hand, and I limped forward.</p>

<p>It was just as I was passing beside Je Hyun-oh. The goggles lit up.</p>

<div class="hyunoh"><p>X X</p></div>

<p>I thought I understood why they called him a certified maniac….</p>

<p>The moment Kwak Hanmuk saw Je Hyun-oh's goggles, he spat out a string of curses. But he didn't waste any more time than that.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk and I left Je Hyun-oh behind and entered the Yellow Zone.</p>

<p>Despite it being the middle of the night, the Yellow Zone was quiet, without a single zombie in sight.</p>

<p>That was thanks to every zombie in the vicinity swarming the bridge and getting themselves drowned en masse.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk found an old Jeep, ripped open the ignition box, and started it with a screwdriver.</p>

<p>"Goyo, you know how to drive?"</p>

<p>"I have a license, but I've only driven manual in games."</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk snorted at my answer and took the wheel.</p>

<p>"Should we head to the base first?"</p>

<p>"Yes. Good idea."</p>

<p>We needed to regroup before going to find Mo Haein. In this state, searching was out of the question — we were in no shape to take down even a single zombie.</p>

<p>While Kwak Hanmuk drove, I decided to check on Je Hyun-oh, plastered to the passenger seat like a rice cake stuck to the pan.</p>

<p>'Je Hyun-oh Status Window.'</p>

<div class="systembox">
<div class="systembox_wrap">
<p>
&#45; Je Hyun-oh (Manifest)<br/>
&#45; Soldier<br/>
&#45; Acquired Items: None.<br/>
&#45; Held Items: Crow Hood Jumper (1), Crow Combat Uniform (1), Crow Goggles (1), Crow Mask (1), Crow Gloves (1), Crow Walkers (1).
</p>
<p>
&gt; Message<br/>

&gt; Prohibition<br/>

&gt; Execution
</p>
</div>
</div>

<p>There were additions that hadn't been there with Park Seonggyeon. I examined each entry one by one.</p>

<p>Manifest seemed to be the name Je Hyun-oh had been granted in the Trial.</p>

<p>His occupation was simply listed as Soldier, with no rank — likely because DeZonDeal didn't have detailed settings for Player occupations.</p>

<p>Items were divided into Acquired and Held.</p>

<p>'Acquired means items obtained in the current Trial, and Held is… items he already possessed, like Mo Haein's Black Moon Blade?'</p>

<p>I waited a moment.</p>

<p>No System window popped up to contradict me, so my guess seemed to be correct.</p>

<p>The Held Items list was a full-on crow party.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh always wore his own items in Trials, but I'd never realized they were all part of the same set.</p>

<p>'And Prohibition.'</p>

<p>That was the ability I had used on Je Hyun-oh earlier.</p>

<p>Prohibition temporarily restricts a registered individual from taking any action whatsoever.</p>

<p>But the effective duration was extremely short. Three seconds at most, by feel?</p>

<p>That seemed to be the current limit of my ability.</p>

<p>The reason Je Hyun-oh hadn't moved even after those three seconds passed was because of the Message I had sent right after, telling him to stay still.</p>

<p>From Je Hyun-oh's perspective, an inexplicable phenomenon had occurred, so he would have followed the System window's instructions for the time being.</p>

<p>'He doesn't know I was the one who sent it.'</p>

<p>I could probably get a few more uses out of it against him.</p>

<p>The problem was that this was nothing more than a stopgap.</p>

<p>With my current abilities, I couldn't fully control Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>Using Prohibition consecutively was also impossible.</p>

<p>Considering the pain I'd experienced when I used the ability, it was clearly an act that put significant strain on my body.</p>

<p>I might end up dying while trying to capture Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>'Are there no attack skills?'</p>

<div class="systembox">
<p>
You feel a great sense of accomplishment at your further-developed self.<br/>

You consider it a truly successful first Registration and resolve to strive for even greater growth in the future.
</p>
</div>


<div class="systembox">
<p>
You think it would be best to fill all three Character Registration slots first, in order to achieve rapid growth and obtain new abilities.
</p>
</div>
<p>The System window looked pleased.</p>

<p>Normally it would have just sent a single word like 'insufficient,' but this response was fairly elaborate.</p>

<p>Registering Je Hyun-oh seemed to be the reason.</p>

<p>But I wanted to avoid Registration if at all possible.</p>

<p>It had already made me uneasy before, and after registering Je Hyun-oh this time, I'd realized something more clearly.</p>

<p>It was burdensome enough to have a complete leash on another human being, but the decisive thing was….</p>

<p>I felt like I was becoming strange.</p>

<p>The emotions and mindset I had experienced the moment I registered Je Hyun-oh were abnormal.</p>

<p>I felt like I'd had some bizarre thoughts too, but only a vague impression remained — I couldn't properly remember.</p>

<p>As if I'd fallen into some kind of trance and then woken up.</p>

<p>I was someone who pursued a quiet life, true to my name.</p>

<p>Just becoming Earth's System was already more than I could handle.</p>

<p>Adding another factor that could negatively affect my mind was too much.</p>

<p>Was it impossible to upgrade my System abilities just by clearing Trials?</p>

<p>I would find out once I cleared DeZonDeal with the True Ending this time.</p>

<p>'Let's try to handle things on my own if I can.'</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh was already registered, so there was nothing I could do about him.</p>

<p>Having roughly sorted out my thoughts, I turned my attention to Kwak Hanmuk.</p>

<p>He was the supporter who was making it possible for me to move proactively right now.</p>

<p>Even when things seemed questionable, he trusted me completely and followed my lead.</p>

<p>What had happened earlier was only possible because Kwak Hanmuk had rammed into Je Hyun-oh based on my opinion.</p>

<p>Of course, he might have just gone along with it because doing insane shit was fun.</p>

<p>"What?"</p>

<p>Maybe I'd been staring a little too long. Kwak Hanmuk glanced at me. Since it had come to this, I formed a question.</p>

<p>"Captain Kwak, may I ask what name you received in the Trial?"</p>

<p>I was curious about information on him that I hadn't been able to learn in the game. Kwak Hanmuk answered readily.</p>

<p>"Blue Thunder."</p>

<p>"...."</p>

<p>"You just thought, 'His hair is red, so why Blue Thunder?'"</p>

<p>"…How did you know?"</p>

<p>"Haha!"</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk burst into refreshing laughter.</p>

<p>Clutching my wounded pride, I envied him.</p>

<p>I should've picked a cooler name when I was playing the game. The price of picking something half-assed stung.</p>

<p>'Can I not change my name?'</p>

<p>If there was something like a name change ticket, I wanted to buy one.</p>

<p>While I was wallowing, I wondered why Kwak Hanmuk had been given the name Blue Thunder. His eyes were gray, too.</p>

<p>Curious, but not quite able to press further, I watched as Kwak Hanmuk held the wheel with one hand and handed something to me.</p>

<p>An exclamation slipped out before I could stop it.</p>

<p>"Wow…."</p>

<p>It was a portable cassette tape player with wired headphones connected.</p>

<p>"It was in the glove box. First time seeing one, Goyo?"</p>

<p>"I've seen them in archive footage and stuff."</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk muttered "archive footage…" to himself. There was clearly a bit of a generation gap between us.</p>

<p>I examined the cassette player with curious eyes.</p>

<p>This was an item I'd never seen in the game either. That was because vehicle glove boxes couldn't be opened in the game.</p>

<p>The item description was simple.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Portable Music Player: Contains a cassette tape with six exciting songs.</p></div></div>

<p>"Don't listen to the music."</p>

<p>I had been about to put the headphones on. "Ah," I said, and lowered my hands.</p>

<p>Not using Trial items recklessly was a TRA rule.</p>

<p>It was only natural, for safety — you never knew what kind of infection might occur.</p>

<p>'I want to listen to it, though.'</p>

<p>It didn't seem like I could right now. I tucked the cassette player into the duffel bag.</p>

<p>Come to think of it, the triangle kimbap and fire chicken ramen from the convenience store were recent, but the firearms, the Jeep, and the cassette were all old models.</p>

<p>It felt like the timeline had been mixed up at random.</p>

<p>When I'd roamed DeZonDeal as a game, I hadn't noticed it, but now that I was confronting it as reality, the sense of dissonance was considerable.</p>

<p>It was a little too unsettling to dismiss as just another quirk of the Trial.</p>

<p>Assuming the Trials were based on a game the Creator had made.</p>

<p>I knew the Creator wasn't the type to botch details like this.</p>

<p>Meticulous worldbuilding was one of the reasons I'd loved his games.</p>

<p>If the scrambled timeline had been the setting all along, and the game had simply hidden it….</p>

<p>"AAGH!"</p>

<p>SCREEEECH—!</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk suddenly yelled and slammed on the brakes.</p>

<p>Jolt. My body lurched forward, barely held back by the seatbelt.</p>

<p>I'd nearly smashed my forehead into the windshield. Eyes wide, I stared ahead.</p>

<p>"Captain Mo…?"</p>

<p>Mo Haein, turned into a zombie, was hitchhiking.</p>
