<h1>Chapter 40</h1>

<p>At the mention of the owner of the vast mansion — the butlers' employer — Cat Butler lit up.</p>

<p>"Oh! He hasn't returned yet. But there was good news!"</p>

<p>Rabbit Butler smiled broadly along with him.</p>

<p>Throughout the entire game of Clean3, the master of the mansion was mentioned as something important.</p>

<p>The butlers waited for their master, who had gone on a long journey, working to keep the mansion in the most perfect condition possible.</p>

<p>They regularly hired cleaners to clean the place. The reason for going through such trouble was simple:</p>

<p>The cleaners were fresh sacrifices to be offered to the mansion.</p>

<p>Whether in the garden, the hallways, the bedrooms, the bathrooms, the basement — no matter where they died, the cleaners' bodies were absorbed completely, leaving not a drop of blood, flesh, or bone behind.</p>

<p>And they became fuel to keep the mansion at its most brilliant state.</p>

<p>That was why the butlers personally selected which cleaners to bring inside.</p>

<p>Though if someone managed to survive seven days, they were let out alive.</p>

<p>They always brought in more than enough cleaners, so they had no need for the lives of those who managed to survive.</p>

<p>Occasionally, a cleaner who showed exceptional skill would be formally hired by the mansion.</p>

<p>When a player showed good behavior toward the butlers during the game, they would occasionally offer praise and suggest becoming a mansion employee.</p>

<p>But there was one ultimate praise line that only appeared when both butlers' affection was maxed out.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_pixel"><p>"Roasted Chestnut. I shouldn't say this, but… I'm sure the Master will take a liking to you too."</p></div>

<div class="bigbox2_pixel"><p>"If it's Roasted Chestnut, the Master would be pleased. One shouldn't presume to guess His Excellency's intentions, but I can be certain about you!"</p></div>

<p>Praise that mentioned the Master.</p>

<p>After hearing this praise, stories about "the Master" would unlock, allowing you to find bits of information scattered around the mansion.</p>

<p>The Master, who had supposedly gone on a long journey, had actually disappeared suddenly.</p>

<p>Currently missing, but as a figure of extremely high rank and influence, calls and visits inquiring about him never ceased.</p>

<p>They'd set him up as a very important character within the world, yet during the entire game, the Master never showed so much as a single strand of hair.</p>

<p>Even after clearing all endings, it was the same.</p>

<p>The Master never returned to the mansion.</p>

<p>It wasn't just a simple MacGuffin — even in the hidden ending where the player was treated to a feast, the butlers were still waiting for their vanished master.</p>

<p>Even after the ending, every time I thought of Clean3, I was haunted by a lingering unease that never resolved.</p>

<p>And now there was good news regarding that "Master."</p>

<p>I had a gut feeling this would be important information.</p>

<p>"What news? Did they set a return date?"</p>

<p>"Unfortunately, no."</p>

<p>Cat Butler bent forward, his metal face drawing closer.</p>

<p>"I'll tell you specially, Roasted Chestnut."</p>

<p>Lowering his voice to a near whisper, he spoke secretively.</p>

<p>"We've found out where the Master is!"</p>

<p>Please don't tell me it's Earth…?</p>

<p>I wanted desperately to ask where, but held back.</p>

<p>I couldn't act rashly.</p>

<p>With patience, I simply reacted like a friend hearing good news.</p>

<p>"That's a relief. He'll be back soon, then."</p>

<p>But the butlers didn't answer. They just looked at each other.</p>

<p>Then Cat Butler said something strange.</p>

<p>"Maybe so. Now that you're here, Roasted Chestnut."</p>

<p>The topic didn't continue — it shifted to something else.</p>

<p>Rabbit Butler tapped the air.</p>

<p>"Roasted Chestnut. Give me your code."</p>

<p>A translucent blue screen, similar to the System window, appeared.</p>

<p>Rabbit Butler grumbled as he scrolled down.</p>

<p>"Only yours I don't know."</p>

<p>With a flick of his long fingers, the blue screen slid across and stopped in front of me.</p>

<p>The screen was filled with unknown characters that seemed to be a list of codes.</p>

<p>In Clean3's world, there was a concept called a "code," similar to a phone number.</p>

<p>It was used for interstellar communication — and of course, I didn't have one.</p>

<p>I furrowed my brows and put on my most apologetic expression.</p>

<p>"I don't have a code. Sorry."</p>

<p>"Good heavens!"</p>

<p>Cat Butler was horrified, and even Rabbit Butler was so startled his ears twitched.</p>

<p>Visibly shaken, Cat Butler asked politely.</p>

<p>"If it's not too rude, may I create a code for you?"</p>

<p>I laughed awkwardly.</p>

<p>"It costs to maintain one. And it's not commonly used on my planet."</p>

<p>"Oh dear…"</p>

<p>Cat Butler sighed. After a short cough, he scolded me in a rather stern voice.</p>

<p>"Roasted Chestnut. Sometimes you need to accept help from those around you."</p>

<p>Meanwhile, I clenched my sweat-slicked hands into fists, hiding them.</p>

<p>In Clean3, the player applies to a cleaning company because they're poor.</p>

<p>Not knowing any better, they apply to an infamous agency and get forcibly dragged to the mansion that all cleaners fear.</p>

<p>So using money as an excuse for this conversation wouldn't seem strange.</p>

<p>"Don't tell me you came as a cleaner for financial reasons too?"</p>

<p>"Partly."</p>

<p>"I see…"</p>

<p>I naturally linked the reason I'd come through the agency to financial problems.</p>

<p>Cat Butler nodded gravely, and Rabbit Butler cut in.</p>

<p>"Roasted Chestnut. Don't work as a cleaner."</p>

<p>"Huh?"</p>

<p>"Roasted Chestnut works as a butler."</p>

<p>"Exactly right! Roasted Chestnut, how about working as a butler?"</p>

<p>Cat Butler responded enthusiastically, saying it was an excellent idea.</p>

<p>I tried to decline carefully.</p>

<p>"I appreciate the offer, but…"</p>

<p>"Listen, Roasted Chestnut. I know you're hesitant about formal employment. I won't push for that. How about working as an apprentice butler here for just seven days, until the agency comes back?"</p>

<p>Cat Butler's voice dropped low.</p>

<p>"How could we ever make you work as a cleaner? If you consider us friends, don't make us do such a cruel thing."</p>

<p>I had no excuse to refuse.</p>

<p>I was going to stay in Clean3 for seven days anyway.</p>

<p>And being an apprentice butler rather than a cleaner seemed more advantageous for gathering information and supporting the Captain and Lieutenant Colonel.</p>

<p>"Then, I'll leave it to you."</p>

<p>"Good thinking! We'll give you a generous allowance on top of what the agency pays. Yes, I should pay in advance. I'll deposit it right—"</p>

<p>Cat Butler summoned a translucent window like Rabbit Butler had, his fingers moving busily — then he stopped.</p>

<p>He stared at me sitting there blankly, his mouth falling open.</p>

<p>Realizing belatedly that it had been rude, he closed his mouth again, but his gaze was still fixed on me.</p>

<p>"You have nowhere to receive a deposit, do you."</p>

<p>Cat Butler's muttering voice was full of pity.</p>

<p>It felt like he was treating me like an extreme poverty case from a barbarian planet outside civilized society.</p>

<p>'I do have an account, you know. Just not an interstellar one.'</p>

<p>I wasn't sure if this was a Trial or another space entirely.</p>

<p>The possibility was slim, but it might be connected to reality.</p>

<p>I knew better than to casually give out personal information to NPCs in this situation.</p>

<p>And if I gave the wrong info, they'd probably come find me on a spaceship…</p>

<p>"Pay me in cash."</p>

<p>"I suppose so."</p>

<p>Accepting Rabbit Butler's suggestion, Cat Butler pulled something out of thin air.</p>

<p>"This is currency created by our Master. Usable anywhere in the universe!"</p>

<p>Cat Butler handed me a translucent coin with pride thick in his voice.</p>

<p>It was about the size of my palm.</p>

<p>I had no idea what it was worth, but the butlers didn't seem like the type to give small amounts.</p>

<p>I acted surprised.</p>

<p>"This is too much…"</p>

<p>"Roasted Chestnut! Put it away. Please."</p>

<p>Rabbit Butler leaned his face close to mine. Black filled my vision.</p>

<p>"Roasted Chestnut. Take it. It's a gift."</p>

<p>I could see sharp teeth inside his mouth as he spoke.</p>

<p>"We haven't given Roasted Chestnut any gifts. We've only received."</p>

<p>"…Thank you so much."</p>

<p>It's not like I accepted because I was scared or anything.</p>

<p>I pocketed the coin and quickly checked the item description.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Dark Star Fragment: Currency made from carving the core of a dead star. Usable throughout the entire universe. <em>In His Excellency's glorious name!</em></p></div></div>

<p>After reading the description, I felt deeply uneasy about carrying the coin.</p>

<p>But with the butlers watching right in front of me, I had no choice but to put it in my inventory.</p>

<p>'If the System window were still here, it would've definitely said something about this coin.'</p>

<p>Now that I was desperate for any information I could get, I felt its absence keenly.</p>

<p>When I had it, it was annoying.</p>

<p>'So this is what they mean by a chicken rib — can't eat it, can't throw it away.'</p>

<p>I was led by the two butlers to a guest room on the third floor of the East Wing.</p>

<p>The butlers' rooms were on the first floor of the main building.</p>

<p>A guest room for an apprentice butler seemed too luxurious, but I figured asking would just earn me a scolding, so I accepted quietly.</p>

<p>The guest room wasn't built for human height either — the doorknob was at about face level.</p>

<p>Cat Butler placed a small ladder next to the door for me.</p>

<p>I could open the door without stepping on the ladder, but I gratefully accepted his consideration.</p>

<p>"Don't forget the evening feast! See you later, Roasted Chestnut!"</p>

<p>Apparently both butlers couldn't leave their posts when the cleaners arrived, so Cat Butler left first.</p>

<p>Rabbit Butler stayed behind to teach me butler duties.</p>

<p>"Roasted Chestnut. Here, butler uniform."</p>

<p>The clothes Rabbit Butler handed me were identical to what the butlers wore.</p>

<p>I wondered if they'd fit, but since they counted as an item, they adjusted to my size when equipped.</p>

<p>Rabbit Butler looked down at me and clapped.</p>

<p>Then we headed to the main building.</p>

<p>When we arrived, another footman was assigning cleaning zones to the cleaners.</p>

<p>"Zeta."</p>

<p>"Yes, Butler-nim."</p>

<p>"You do something else."</p>

<p>Pushing Zeta aside, Rabbit Butler positioned me where he'd been standing.</p>

<p>"Roasted Chestnut does it."</p>

<p>I looked at the cleaners before me, feeling slightly awkward.</p>

<p>The ones who'd ridden the same spaceship here looked bewildered to see me as a butler now.</p>

<p>Feeling sheepish, I started my first butler duty.</p>

<p>It seemed I'd been granted some authority by becoming a temporary butler of the mansion.</p>

<p>Strangely, just by looking at the cleaners' faces, I could tell their names.</p>

<p>I fluently pronounced a language I'd never seen before, one I didn't know.</p>

<p>"■■■■■, go to the West Wing garden. ■, go to the East Wing first floor."</p>

<p>I assigned them weed removal and chandelier polishing respectively.</p>

<p>The ones who looked ill-tempered I sent to the basement.</p>

<p>Since I already knew what to do and how in each zone, assigning areas was easy.</p>

<p>Rabbit Butler stood a step behind, watching me and praising me constantly.</p>

<p>"Roasted Chestnut. Good job. Perfect butler."</p>

<p>While I was handling work amid the praise, the Captains finally appeared.</p>

<p>'Captain Mo! Captain Kwak!'</p>

<p>Both froze on the spot the moment they saw me.</p>

<p>Mo Haein's expression was especially bad — she seemed really shocked.</p>

<p>I was overjoyed to see them, but kept my face expressionless.</p>

<p>Then I assigned them the zones I'd saved.</p>

<p>"Mo-rae, go to the West Wing second floor. Blue Thunder, go to the East Wing second floor."</p>

<p>Both were the safest zones in the mansion.</p>

<p>I gave them the easiest task too — cleaning the hallway floors.</p>

<p>The hallways were strategic points where you could run into a room or jump out a window into the garden if something happened.</p>

<p>And they were the most convenient for me to make contact with the Captains when I got the chance.</p>

<p>I'd separated them to search for Je Hyun-oh's location.</p>

<p>I couldn't ask the butlers or footmen where he'd been assigned.</p>

<p>'It'd be weird for a butler to take interest in a mere cleaner's assignment.'</p>

<p>Anyway, I'd done my best with what I could.</p>

<p>Just as I was about to shift my gaze to the next cleaner —</p>

<p>Mo Haein covered her mouth with her hand and staggered.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk sensed something wrong and quickly grabbed her, but it was too late.</p>

<p>Mo Haein's body crumpled forward as she retched.</p>

<p>"Ughk…!"</p>

<p>The sound of stomach acid hitting the marble floor echoed starkly through the space.</p>

<p>A voice came from behind me.</p>

<p>"A defective one got in."</p>

<p>Rabbit Butler reached out.</p>

<p>"Roasted Chestnut. Don't worry."</p>

<p>His fingertips extended past my shoulder, aimed precisely at Mo Haein.</p>

<p>"I'll take care of it."</p>

<p>Fweee!</p>

<p>A sharp sound rang in my ears.</p>

<p>A beam shot forward.</p>
