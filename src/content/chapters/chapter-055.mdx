<h1>Chapter 55</h1>

<p>The hologram badge Samra gave me was attached inside my clothes.</p>

<p>It wasn’t somewhere visible, and of course, I had never shown it in front of Dominic.</p>

<p>“…The badge.”</p>

<p>“The badge?”</p>

<p>“I received it as a gift. From a friend.”</p>

<p>“Ah, a friend from London?”</p>

<p>“…Yes. But how did you know I was wearing the badge? I hid it inside my clothes.”</p>

<p>I asked while laughing as if I was making a joke.</p>

<p>Hearing my question, Dominic answered as if he found it fascinating.</p>

<p>“From some point onward, I could simply feel it.”</p>

<p>It seemed like he had only just realized that the badge had been hidden.</p>

<p>Judging from the way he spoke, it didn’t seem like he had known from the moment I started wearing it.</p>

<p>For some reason, I had the feeling it was after he saw the Shining Irregular Polyhedron.</p>

<p>And the reason he recognized the badge was probably…</p>

<p>‘Is it because it isn’t an item from CallOVo?’</p>

<p>The most plausible theory was that he sensed something strange because it was an item from outside.</p>

<p>“First, let’s leave this place.”</p>

<p>I walked forward first.</p>

<p>“We still need to collect the remaining evidence, and we also need to rescue the people kidnapped as sacrifices. It would probably be more efficient if we split up.”</p>

<p>I spoke to Dominic as he followed behind me.</p>

<p>“I’m short, so it’s easier for me to hide and move around. I’ll go collect the evidence. Dominic-ssi, would you take care of the sacrifices?”</p>

<p>I had never once thought of myself as short, but those words came out naturally when I was standing beside Dominic.</p>

<p>“That is a good idea. I will protect those we rescue at the clinic. Edel-yang will not be able to approach that place.”</p>

<p>I briefly explained where the people who were going to become sacrifices were being held.</p>

<p>But then, I realized I could no longer hear footsteps following me.</p>

<p>I stopped while opening the door leading out of the holding cell and looked at Dominic.</p>

<p>“…Dominic-ssi?”</p>

<p>“Roasted Chestnut. I know this is an unreasonable request, but…”</p>

<p>He placed a hand against his face for a moment. He seemed almost surprised by his own actions.</p>

<p>“No. Forget it. Please ignore me.”</p>

<p>Was he doing this intentionally?</p>

<p>When someone started saying something and then stopped, didn’t that only make it more curious?</p>

<p>It probably wasn’t going to be something pleasant, but my curiosity kept bothering me.</p>

<p>And if I didn’t ask now, I had no idea what kind of bomb it might become later.</p>

<p>‘Wouldn’t it be better to make it explode sooner?’</p>

<p>I approached him with the mindset of a bomb disposal expert.</p>

<p>“Please tell me, Dominic-ssi.”</p>

<p>After hesitating for a while, Dominic carefully asked me.</p>

<p>“Could you give me the badge?”</p>

<p>The request itself wasn’t cautious at all.</p>

<p>I still didn’t know what the badge was for, but seeing how much it bothered Dominic, it seemed like it was an important item.</p>

<p>I couldn’t casually hand such an item over to Dominic.</p>

<p>It wasn’t only mine. It also belonged to Samra.</p>

<p>I had no choice but to tell another lie.</p>

<p>“I’m sorry. It’s a keepsake from a deceased friend…”</p>

<p>After sending Samra, who was perfectly alive and working fine, to his grave, I put on the same pitiful expression I had shown earlier in the cell.</p>

<p>“My benefactor and companion, Dominic-ssi… I want to give you anything I can, but this one thing…”</p>

<p>Dominic became flustered and immediately apologized.</p>

<p>“It was your friend’s keepsake… Roasted Chestnut, I’m sorry. I acted on an impulse just now. I don’t know why I behaved that way either.”</p>

<p>I shook my head as if telling him it wasn’t his fault.</p>

<p>“I think it’s because of that strange metal box we saw yesterday.”</p>

<p>“…!”</p>

<p>“It really is a dangerous box. Let’s never go near it again.”</p>

<p>“I suppose we should. So yesterday, Roasted Chestnut experienced this same impulse?”</p>

<p>Dominic looked at me with a new expression.</p>

<p>It was the look of someone checking once again whether I was truly alright.</p>

<p>I smiled as if nothing was wrong and asked:</p>

<p>“Shall we go now?”</p>

<p>“Yes, Roasted Chestnut.”</p>

<p>Dominic obediently followed me out.</p>

<p>Then we separated to carry out our assigned tasks, and finally, when I was alone—</p>

<p>“Phew…”</p>

<p>I let out the breath I had been holding.</p>

<p>While I was together with Dominic, it felt like my heart was constantly on the verge of giving out.</p>

<p>It was even worse because I had lied to Dominic so many times.</p>

<p>The reason was that I had already deceived Dominic more than once.</p>

<p>I had intentionally told Dominic the wrong location where the kidnapped people were being held.</p>

<p>I wanted the rescue to take a long time, so he would have to spend the entire day focused only on that.</p>

<p>After all, today’s ritual would not use the kidnapped people as sacrifices. The cult members themselves would be offered instead, so there was no need to rescue them early.</p>

<p>‘Originally, I planned to stay as close to Dominic as possible.’</p>

<p>I had intended to keep an eye on whether he caused any trouble while also making use of him for various purposes.</p>

<p>But now, I was starting to think I should keep him hidden away somewhere.</p>

<p>Things had become tangled because Dominic had replaced the sacrifices with cult members.</p>

<p>The people who were supposed to be sacrificed had been rescued, but the ritual itself was still going to happen.</p>

<p>Of course, I could infiltrate the cult together with Dominic and stop the ritual.</p>

<p>That would be much easier…</p>

<p>But Dominic’s strange reaction after seeing the Shining Irregular Polyhedron, combined with everything else, made me decide that I should avoid showing him anything related to summoning an Outer God as much as possible.</p>

<p>Perhaps Dominic was an existence summoned here by the cult.</p>

<p>That thought came to me because I knew the rituals performed by the cult could summon various Outer Gods.</p>

<p>I didn’t know whether he was some kind of divine being, but regardless, he was certainly not ordinary, so the possibility existed.</p>

<p>But if that were true, there was another problem.</p>

<p>The trials were only a space that repeated the same world.</p>

<p>The fact that a trial could summon and imprison an existence like Dominic…</p>

<p>I couldn’t believe the trial possessed that much power.</p>

<p>In any case, it was a question I couldn’t answer right now.</p>

<p>Because of that, I decided I would handle today’s ritual by myself.</p>

<p>I went around to the back of the police station and peeked inside through an open window.</p>

<p>NPCs had set schedules, and at this time, nobody was behind the police station.</p>

<p>I reached inside and took the hat hanging on the wall.</p>

<p>‘I’m only taking one hat.’</p>

<p>Since they had dragged an innocent person here, I felt like the police station could at least provide a souvenir.</p>

<p>The atmosphere was quiet, so it seemed my escape from the holding cell had not been discovered yet.</p>

<p>If they came to deliver dinner to the prisoners, they would notice.</p>

<p>So I had to finish collecting evidence and enter the cult before then.</p>

<p>I quietly entered an alleyway where no pedestrians ever went.</p>

<p>A plump mouse squeaked and quickly disappeared in front of me.</p>

<p>After walking deep into the dead end, a system window appeared.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Entered the back streets of Morneveil. Where would you like to go?</p></div></div>

<p>There were more than ten movement options unlocked, more than eight pieces of evidence collected, and more than two companions recruited.</p>

<p>A shortcut that became available once certain conditions were fulfilled.</p>

<p>All of those were conditions I had prepared yesterday.</p>

<p>I selected my destination.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Move to City Hall.</p></div>

<p>My body stepped onto a trash can and effortlessly jumped onto the top of the wall.</p>

<p>In an instant, I climbed up to the rooftops and began running.</p>

<p>The moment of racing across the roofs of the low buildings was precarious.</p>

<p>With my original physical abilities, it might have been difficult.</p>

<p>However, because I was moving through automatic movement, my actions were almost no different from those of a movie stunt performer.</p>

<p>Leaping across gaps between rooftops, I reached my destination, City Hall, in no time.</p>

<p>After checking the time on the clock tower, I looked around the area.</p>

<p>The cobblestone plaza was filled with a restless atmosphere.</p>

<p>Police officers stood around with serious expressions.</p>

<p>The reason was that the mayor’s residence, located two blocks away from City Hall, was where the murder had occurred.</p>

<p>The biggest advantage of using the back streets was that they automatically connected to hidden routes.</p>

<p>Strictly speaking, they weren’t roads, but places that could be used like roads.</p>

<p>I climbed up to the mayor’s office by grabbing onto a drainage pipe attached to the wall.</p>

<p>Thanks to all the personnel being gathered around the mayor’s residence, the mayor’s office was quiet.</p>

<p>I took out the wire I had collected yesterday.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Old Wire: An essential tool for a reporter obsessed with investigation! It can be used in various ways. (Remaining uses: 5)</p></div></div>

<p>I unlocked the window latch and pushed it open.</p>

<p>As soon as I entered, a system window appeared.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>You have entered the Morneveil mayor’s office.</p></div>

<p>An ominous presence can be felt.</p>

<p>It was a notification that an event related to the main story was about to occur.</p>

<p>It was a notification that an event related to the main story was about to occur.</p>

<p>The event that was supposed to happen originally was the Morneveil mayor discovering me, an intruder, and becoming furious.</p>

<p>He was under a weak form of brainwashing, and if I failed to persuade the mayor, he would transform into a monster and a boss battle would begin.</p>

<p>Entering the mayor’s office without preparation meant I would immediately lose one heart.</p>

<p>But right now, the mayor was gone, so I was able to enter without any problems.</p>

<p>Since I had become someone breaking into an empty house, I looked around the mayor’s office.</p>

<p>I was searching for the documents related to the cult that the mayor had hidden.</p>

<p>In the game, I either defeated the mayor after he became a boss or succeeded in persuading him and obtained the item.</p>

<p>I knew the documents were definitely somewhere in the mayor’s office, but since this was my first time stealing them like this, I had to search for their location.</p>

<p>I was about to start by checking the desk when something caught my eye.</p>

<p>A newspaper neatly folded on top of the desk.</p>

<p>It must have been delivered before the news of the mayor’s death spread.</p>

<p>It was today’s newspaper.</p>

<p>I looked at it without much thought, but then something strange happened.</p>

<p>The name of the newspaper, which had been written as 'Morneveil Herald,' slowly changed.</p>

<p>And the new name was one I knew.</p>

<p>“Day News…?”</p>

<p>It was the same newspaper that the cat butler in Clean3 had given me.</p>

<p>The newspaper that contained the article about the spaceship explosion.</p>

<p>There was no way it was a coincidence.</p>

<p>I immediately picked up the newspaper.</p>

<p>However, the entire thing was covered in square colored blocks.</p>

<p>Previously, there had been at least one article I could read.</p>

<p>This time, there was nothing.</p>

<p>I carefully checked even the very last page.</p>

<p>That was when I noticed a small lifestyle advice section tucked into the empty space left after the articles had been arranged.</p>

<p>It was only a short piece consisting of a couple of lines, but I could read it.</p>
<div class="news_wrapper">
<p>
■■ ■■■’s Simple Life Advice!<br/>

For items that cannot be used even after activation, try using the 'Appraisal' ability!
</p>
</div>
<p>“I don’t have that ability.”</p>

<p>It felt similar to being told to add ice to a beer that had gone lukewarm.</p>

<p>I had searched desperately only to find some meaningless piece of advice like this.</p>

<p>As I cursed the useless newspaper, a line suddenly appeared over the words.</p>
<div class="news_wrapper">
<p>
■■ ■■■’s Advanced Life Advice!<br/>

For items that cannot be used even after activation, try using the 'Appraisal' ability! Try putting a little blood on it! It is a primitive method, but surprisingly effective!
</p>
</div>
<p>“……”</p>

<p>What was this?</p>

<p>It felt as if something was watching what I was doing.</p>

<p>I stared at the squares silently.</p>

<p>I remembered seeing similar arrangements of squares several times before.</p>

<p>Every time I cleared a trial, those same square patterns appeared with messages that seemed to be paying attention to me.</p>

<p>I had also seen the same squares during DeZonDeal’s event quest.</p>

<p>The information written in that event quest had actually been helpful.</p>

<p>After thinking for a moment, I decided to try it, even if it was only on the off chance.</p>

<p>Letting a drop or two of blood fall was not difficult.</p>

<p>It was worth trying.</p>

<p>I removed the badge from inside my clothes and lightly pricked my fingertip with the sharp needle-like part.</p>

<p>Then I rubbed the drop of blood that formed on my fingertip onto the badge.</p>

<p>Just as I thought nothing would happen—</p>

<p>The drop of blood absorbed into the badge without a trace.</p>

<p>Then a holographic light began to shimmer.</p>

<p>I widened my eyes at the unusual sight.</p>

<p>Along with a crackling noise like static, a faint voice came from the badge.</p>

<p>I grabbed the badge and brought it close to my ear.</p>

<p>—…Goyo-ssi!</p>

<p>“Samra?”</p>

<p>It was Samra’s voice.</p>

<p>He had connected communication from outside the trial to the inside.</p>

<p>Hearing his voice after such a long time made me incredibly happy.</p>

<p>While I was still amazed by the unbelievable advanced technology, Samra hurriedly continued speaking.</p>

<p>—Where are you right now?</p>

<p>“I’m in &lt;Call of the Void&gt;. I was forcibly transferred from Clean3 directly into CallOVo by an NPC.”</p>

<p>—You mean you entered a trial?</p>

<p>“Yes.”</p>

<p>I felt a slight sense of discomfort.</p>

<p>Samra never said unnecessary things.</p>

<p>The fact that he specifically asked whether it was a trial felt strange.</p>

<p>—Han Goyo-ssi…</p>

<p>After a short silence, Samra spoke.</p>

<p>—&lt;Call of the Void&gt; is a trial that has never hatched before.</p>

<p>“A trial that has never hatched…”</p>

<p>I repeated his words.</p>

<p>—It means it is a trial that does not exist.</p>
