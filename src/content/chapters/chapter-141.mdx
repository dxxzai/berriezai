<h1>Chapter 141</h1>

<p>Mo Haein looked up at the figure encased in the glass pillar.</p>

<p>It was an unfamiliar experience.</p>

<p>After all, she was the one who was usually asleep.</p>

<p>Whenever she opened her eyes, Ham Jiwol was always recording data, and sometimes Kwak Hanmuk was with her.</p>

<p>Entering the trial, surviving, entering again, surviving.</p>

<p>Sometimes, when she felt like she couldn't endure it anymore, she would fall asleep and wake up.</p>

<p>And then the cycle repeated. It was a monotonous life, if you could call it that.</p>

<p>She lived holding onto the hope that if she kept entering Trials like this, she might one day find a way to end them for good.</p>

<p>She knew all too well that it was a futile endeavor, yet she did not let go of that hope.</p>

<p>Since nothing would change even if she let go, she believed it was better to hold on.</p>

<p>Then, a meteorite named Han Goyo crashed into that life of hers.</p>

<p>Mo Haein, who had been gazing up at the glass pillar for a long time, lowered her head.</p>

<p>After taking a light breath in and out, she turned around.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh, Kwak Hanmuk, and even Samra.</p>

<p>They were all staying put in the laboratory where the glass pillar stood.</p>

<p>The directors of Paseong had decided to stand by the immediate vicinity of the TRA until 72 hours had passed.</p>

<p>They hadn't wanted to leave willingly; it had taken the drafting of contracts exclusive to Adaptees to finally get them out.</p>

<p>Ham Jiwol was wandering the research department area with eyes gleaming like a zombie, but Samra had sealed the entrance to prevent her from entering without permission.</p>

<p>The Special Mission Team was also in a situation where it wouldn't matter if one or two members were absent until the promised three days had passed.</p>

<p>However, none of them could bring themselves to leave and remained in front of the glass pillar.</p>

<p>Samra could handle his duties from anywhere, but it was a different story for Je Hyun-oh, who had to enter Trials.</p>

<p>The losses incurred by him remaining here were immeasurable in monetary terms.</p>

<p>The same was true for Mo Haein and Kwak Hanmuk, so the TRA tried their best to mobilize them.</p>

<p>However, the attempts at persuasion did not last long.</p>

<p>As soon as Je Hyun-oh flapped his metal wings a couple of times, everyone who had come to try and persuade him fled.</p>

<p>Surprisingly, Director Jang Seokyun did not step forward; it seemed he had decided it was best not to provoke Je Hyun-oh any further.</p>

<p>The commotion subsided to some extent, and finally, only the Special Mission Team remained.</p>

<p>In the quiet, Kwak Hanmuk plopped down on the floor haphazardly.</p>

<p>He ruffled his hair wildly and asked Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>&quot;How did we get out?&quot;</p>

<p>The sound of metal wings folding echoed throughout the room.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh, with his wings hidden, answered while clacking his metal hands.</p>

<p>&quot;Thanks to the efforts of Trainee Han.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Lieutenant Colonel Je... Han Goyo has just been pronounced dead. Is that really the end of it?&quot; </p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk asked back in a somewhat fierce tone, but Je Hyun-oh did not reply.</p>

<p>Mo Haein carefully observed the silent Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>She knew well that he was the type of person who wouldn't say things even when he should.</p>

<p>The TRA also left Je Hyun-oh alone, refraining from asking him what needed to be asked.</p>

<p>He was a man who always walked a precarious line.</p>

<p>Although everyone took special care to avoid accidentally provoking him, Je Hyun-oh would occasionally show signs of his condition flaring up even when left alone.</p>

<p>He might have simply shut his mouth because of his mental condition, but it felt a little different this time.</p>

<p>It seemed as though he was intentionally hiding something.</p>

<p>But for what purpose? Was there really a need keep secrets, even in a situation like this?</p>

<p>It didn't feel like a relapse either; Je Hyun-oh appeared to be in much better condition than usual.</p>

<p>It was just as she was glaring at him to figure out his intentions...</p>

<p>“I have received a report from Lieutenant Won Taebeom regarding the specific circumstances of the trial.”</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein looked at Samra in unison.</p>

<p>Samra had long since given up hope on Je Hyun-oh and was in contact with Won Taebeom.</p>

<p>“However, it was a report that lacked significant objectivity and credibility. Captain Kwak Hanmuk, please report everything you remember.”</p>

<p>Won Taebeom had been rambling incoherently... Even though it was Samra’s statement, it was hard to believe.</p>

<p>‘His condition was strange earlier, too.’</p>

<p>While Mo Haein thought about Won Taebeom for a moment, Kwak Hanmuk still hadn't begun his report.</p>

<p>It was uncharacteristic of him.</p>

<p>He finally started his report, but his words were unclear.</p>

<p>He faltered and stopped speaking several times while describing the final moments.</p>

<p>“How on earth did we deal with that tentacle bastard...?”</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk muttered, his eyes narrowing in a grimace.</p>

<p>“It was a similar being to the one inside me.”</p>

<p>“...!” </p>

<p>Mo Haein knew the reason why Kwak Hanmuk had a tattoo.</p>

<p>However, she only knew that some supernatural being had been imprisoned; she did not know exactly what it was.</p>

<p>That was because Kwak Hanmuk had never revealed that specific detail to anyone.</p>

<p>Samra’s eyes gleamed even more brilliantly.</p>

<p>With eyes gleaming so brightly that one wondered if he was going to overload, he spoke.</p>

<p>“Before entering this trial, Han Goyo-ssi defined himself as a ‘System’ in the conversation he had with Captain Kwak Hanmuk.”</p>

<p>Since he was carrying an electronic device at the time, their conversation was transmitted to Samra in its entirety.</p>

<p>Upon hearing those words, Mo Haein could understand, to some extent, the parts that had seemed strange until now.</p>

<p>It seemed that the abilities Han Goyo had recklessly unleashed on the Special Mission Team during the boss battle in Sla2Plate were entirely possible if he was indeed a System.</p>

<p>However, Samra was formulating a different hypothesis.</p>

<p>“However, there are still many unexplained aspects. Captain Kwak Hanmuk. Is the prediction that he will be revived after 72 hours something Han Goyo-ssi conveyed using his abilities?”</p>

<p>“...That is correct.”</p>

<p>“Understood.”</p>

<p>The light slowly subsided from the holographic eyes that had been flashing continuously until now.</p>

<p>As if he had finished his calculations.</p>

<p>“Considering the current situation, and all the abilities Han Goyo-ssi possesses, the trials, and the fact that he can interfere with the Adaptees. The definition of a ‘transcendent being’ is inadequate. The most appropriate definition of Han Goyo-ssi is...”</p>

<p>Samra declared, his eyes radiating only a faint light.</p>

<p>“...a god.”</p>

<p>A chilling silence enveloped the laboratory.</p>

<p>A god...?</p>

<p>Mo Haein was speechless at the word, which seemed so distant.</p>

<p>But Samra’s statement was not yet over.</p>

<p>“If Han Goyo-ssi is truly resurrected, we have no choice but to definitively confirm him as a god.”</p>

<p>Samra concluded firmly.</p>

<p>“This is the judgment reached by Samra Mansang.”</p>

<p>At that moment, Mo Haein felt a surge of emotion.</p>

<p>It was a sense of revulsion toward something that was incomprehensible to a human.</p>

<p>In fact, while the concept of the 'System' was difficult to accept, it did not feel entirely detached.</p>

<p>This was because it was an entity one always came into contact with whenever entering a trial.</p>

<p>An Adaptee was bound to look at the system window when accepting quests or using items.</p>

<p>From the very beginning, the Trial Response Agency was a state institution established to respond to national disasters and threats caused by superior beings.</p>

<p>Han Goyo was also initially suspected of being a transcendent being and was brought to the Agency.</p>

<p>Therefore, if the 'System' were described as a transcendent being similar to the trials or the NPCs within them, it made more sense.</p>

<p>But the concept of a god... felt completely different.</p>

<p>It was an unfathomable unknown—a concept so vague it stirred a lingering sense of dread.</p>

<p>“What kind of bullshit is this, you son of a bitch?”</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk expressed Mo Haein’s feelings with rough language.</p>

<p>He, who had been sitting on the floor, sprang to his feet.</p>

<p>“Samra. I wasn’t cursing at you. Nor was I doubting your judgment.”</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk took a few deep breaths, as if trying to calm his emotions.</p>

<p>Nevertheless, the words that followed were incoherent.</p>

<p>“How is Han Goyo a god? It doesn’t make sense. I mean, what abilities? Yeah, he did use them a little. But even then, it wasn’t anything incredibly impressive... It was just sending messages and putting veils over heads, things like that. Besides, he’s only twenty-four. To be a god, don’t you have to be thousands of years old?”</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk, who had been rambling on in a rapid and rough tone, swallowed a curse once again.</p>

<p>“Calculate it again, Samra.”</p>

<p>“It is unnecessary. I have already reviewed every possibility.”</p>

<p>“Haa...”</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk strode toward the laboratory door.</p>

<p>It seemed as though he intended to go outside for a moment to clear his head.</p>

<p>However, he stood motionless in front of the door for a while before eventually turning back.</p>

<p>Even in this situation, he could not bring himself to leave.</p>

<p>Mo Haein, who had been standing there blankly, looked up once again at the being she had been looking at just a few minutes ago.</p>

<p>Naturally, nothing had changed.</p>

<p>Twenty-four years old, Trainee Han Goyo of the TRA.</p>

<p>He was exactly the same.</p>

<p>Just then, Je Hyun-oh spoke.</p>

<p>“Captain Kwak, Captain Mo.”</p>

<p>He lifted his goggles to reveal his eyes. And he met their gazes with with golden pupils.</p>

<p>It was an unfamiliar situation.</p>

<p>This was because Je Hyun-oh rarely went out of his way to show his eyes unless he was in front of Han Goyo.</p>

<p>Slow words flowed from beneath the gas mask.</p>

<p>“Is it normal for the dead to come back to life?”</p>

<p>It was only after hearing those words that Mo Haein realized.</p>

<p>To be certain that the dead could come back to life was not normal in the first place.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh lightly tapped the glass pillar with his metal hand.</p>

<p>“It’s not that big of a deal, so don’t worry. It’s nothing special yet.”</p>

<p>Speaking as if it were nothing, he said in a voice tinged with amusement.</p>

<p>“Look at him now. He’s just trapped inside the glass pillar. If it weren’t for us, he would have been cut into pieces. Weak, young....”</p>

<p>Screech, sharp fingertips scratched the glass pillar.</p>

<p>Mo Haein tensed up for a moment without realizing it. It felt as though he might split the pillar open and pull Han Goyo out at any moment.</p>

<p>Leaving a long scratch behind, Je Hyun-oh spoke softly.</p>

<p>“He is a trainee that the captains need to protect.”</p>

<p>Mo Haein felt as if she were being split in half.</p>

<p>The moment she heard Samra's words, she felt a strong sense of revulsion toward the very concept of a  'God.'</p>

<p>However, at the same time, she also felt a protective instinct, believing she had to protect Han Goyo by any means necessary.</p>

<p>As if it were a completely separate issue.</p>

<p>The source of her irrational state was easy to guess.</p>
<br />
<p><span class="flashback">['Han Goyo' has registered 'Mo Haein.']</span></p>
<br />
<p>Something had gone wrong from the moment the system window popped up announcing that Han Goyo had registered her, and she began to wear a golden veil.</p>

<p>Of course, she had been concerned since he was first brought to the TRA, but it had never been this intense.</p>

<p>If so, were all these emotions due to divine power?</p>

<p>But...</p>

<p>Mo Haein knew Han Goyo.</p>

<p>The face that looked at him with eyes full of affection, dripping with the delight of being on the same team.</p>

<p>The strange kid who even liked Je Hyun-oh, someone everyone else shunned and avoided.</p>

<p>He was a bit foolish and often did things that seemed like he wasn't in his right mind.</p>

<p>A trainee who found surprisingly bold and original breakthroughs, and who, when driven into a crisis together, was the first to think of sacrificing himself.</p>

<p>Thanks to Han Goyo, she had been able to overcome trials. That was an undeniable truth.</p>

<p>Because of Han Goyo, Mo Haein was slowly finding light in the hope she had once thought was futile.</p>

<p>Lost in thought, Mo Haein snapped back to reality at the sound of a voice.</p>

<p>“The trainee was declared dead trying to protect his seniors.”</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh muttered, looking at Kwak Hanmuk with deep regret.</p>

<p>“You two are being pretty heartless.” </p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk let out a hollow laugh as he clutched his left shoulder, which bore a tattoo.</p>

<p>Mo Haein could tell that he, too, was wavering between revulsion and protective instinct, much like herself.</p>

<p>Amidst the confusion, 72 hours passed.</p>

<p>In the slow flow of time, both Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein agonized.</p>

<p>No matter how much they thought about it, no clear answer could be reached.</p>

<p>And Je Hyun-oh and Samra began to drop hints to persuade them.</p>

<p>“We suspect that there are beings outside of Earth that must be defined as gods. Han Goyo-ssi likely did not come into existence without any cause and effect.”</p>

<p>“.......”</p>

<p>“Han Goyo-ssi is absolutely necessary.”</p>

<p>Samra continued to throw out objective information, emphasizing Han Goyo’s importance.</p>

<p>In contrast, Je Hyun-oh acted as if it were a simple matter.</p>

<p>“It all comes down to Han Goyo.”</p>

<p>He spoke as though the fact that he was a god was a mere trifle, acting as if he couldn't fathom why anyone would agonize over it.</p>

<p>“It’s not a choice between one or the other; it’s all Han Goyo. What’s the problem?”</p>

<p>The more he tried to persuade her rationally, the more suspicious she became.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk acted as if he were half-crazed.</p>

<p>Sometimes he would simply stand there with his head pressed against the glass pillar; at other times, he would sit blankly for hours, rolling his prayer beads.</p>

<p>However, Mo Haein was also in poor condition, so she couldn’t look after him.</p>

<p>Just processing the information given to her was overwhelming.</p>

<p>And when the remaining time dwindled to single digits.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk was the first to ask Je Hyun-oh a question.</p>

<p>“Lieutenant Colonel Je. Please tell me everything you know. As for Han Goyo... what is going to happen to him now?”</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh let out a low laugh, as if he had heard an amusing question.</p>

<p>“I don’t know either. He’s still young, so wouldn’t he be heavily influenced by his surroundings?”</p>

<p>At that ambiguous answer, Mo Haein furrowed her brows.</p>

<p>It was an odd way to describe a grown man.</p>

<p>It sounded as if his divine self existed separately.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh, who had been leaning against the glass pillar, took out only one of his metal wings and wrapped it around the pillar.</p>

<p>“If the captains object, I’ll stay behind alone.”</p>

<p>At that moment, Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein ended up looking at each other.</p>

<p>They hadn't fully understood or accepted the situation yet.</p>

<p>At the very least, it was certain that Han Goyo should not be left in Je Hyun-oh’s hands.</p>

<p>“Captain Kwak Hanmuk, Captain Mo Haein.”</p>

<p>At the same time, Samra called out their names.</p>

<p>“I propose a plan to protect Han Goyo-ssi.”</p>

<p>Realizing that their hearts were leaning to one side, he delivered the proposal at the most opportune moment.</p>

<p>“The continued existence of the Special Mission Team.”</p>

<p>“.......”</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk did not answer. Instead, he looked at Han Goyo inside the glass pillar.</p>

<p>The man who had been gazing up at the being defined as a god suddenly spat out a curse.</p>

<p>“Ha, fine, fuck. This time, I’ll lock him up on the right.”</p>

<p>Then, turning to look at Mo Haein, he asked.</p>

<p>“Captain Mo. Are you okay?”</p>

<p>“...I.”</p>

<p>Mo Haein slowly opened her mouth.</p>

<p>She had been tormented by countless thoughts for 72 hours, but in the end, she had returned to the beginning.</p>

<p>It was an irrational, illogical, and unscientific conclusion.</p>

<p>“I just want to stay by the trainee’s side.”</p>
