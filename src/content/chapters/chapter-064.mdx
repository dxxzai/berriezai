<h1>Chapter 64</h1>

<p>“Captain Mo seems to have taken a liking to one particular trainee.”</p>

<p>Won Taebeom stood expressionlessly and looked at Director Jang Seokyun.</p>

<p>With his hands clasped behind his back, Jang Seokyun continued speaking while looking out the window of the Chief’s office.</p>

<p>“As you already know, Trainee Han has shown remarkable results alongside the Special Mission Team over the past month or so. He is a talent our Trial Response Agency cannot afford to lose.”</p>

<p>Jang Seokyun slowly turned around.</p>

<p>With his sharp gaze, Jang Seokyun sometimes gave the impression that he was glaring simply by looking at someone.</p>

<p>“Lieutenant Won Taebeom. Make absolutely certain that the Special Mission Team does not close this trial.”</p>

<p>It was an unreasonable order that directly opposed the goals pursued by the Trial Response Agency.</p>

<p>However, given the current circumstances, it was also the most reasonable order.</p>

<p>Won Taebeom silently saluted.</p>

<p>As he left the Chief’s office and walked down the hallway, Samra’s voice came through his earpiece.</p>

<p>—Lieutenant Won Taebeom. Report your current location.</p>

<p>“I am still at the Agency. I will move to the Sla2Plate incubation site immediately.”</p>

<p>Director Jang Seokyun had probably called him there knowing everything.</p>

<p>There was no need to give an order.</p>

<p>He likely knew that Won Taebeom would act according to his wishes on his own.</p>

<p>Won Taebeom thought of ‘Han Goyo’.</p>

<p>To the Trial Response Agency, he was nothing less than an abnormal phenomenon.</p>

<p>Han Goyo, who appeared suddenly one day, had changed many things.</p>

<p>A being capable of closing trials.</p>

<p>At first glance, he seemed like hope itself, but he also carried the terrifying possibility of becoming a disaster at any moment.</p>

<p>And to Won Taebeom, Han Goyo was unquestionably a disaster.</p>

<p>The shock of seeing Mo Haein return from Clean3 was still vivid.</p>

<p>As a member of Field Team 2, he had watched Mo Haein for a long time, but that was the first time he had ever seen her like that.</p>

<p>Extreme hyperarousal caused by a severe trauma response.</p>

<p>Unable to endure the extreme state of alertness, Mo Haein had eventually gone to the special isolation facility herself and requested confinement.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk and Je Hyun-oh, who had closed Clean3 together, also displayed abnormal reactions.</p>

<p>After returning to the Agency, they went to the Chief’s office together with Samra.</p>

<p>At the time, Won Taebeom had been assigned as Jang Seokyun’s security detail in case of an emergency, so he was able to hear part of their conversation.</p>

<p>The Special Mission Team’s request was simple.</p>

<p>They wanted permission to use the Agency’s personnel to search for the missing Han Goyo.</p>

<p>However, this was a request Jang Seokyun could not accept.</p>

<p>An eligible individual disappearing or dying inside a trial was something that happened far too often.</p>

<p>Even Samra had been unable to determine Han Goyo’s whereabouts.</p>

<p>The only conclusion they could make was that Han Goyo was in a state close to death.</p>

<p>No matter how important Han Goyo was, wasting the Agency’s manpower in such a situation made no sense.</p>

<p>The Special Mission Team should have understood that fact as well, yet they were making an irrational demand.</p>

<p>Samra mentioned Han Goyo’s uniqueness, but Jang Seokyun cut off his statement by arguing that Han Goyo’s trait merely caused NPCs and items to react favorably toward him.</p>

<p>Then Kwak Hanmuk stepped forward and tried to persuade Jang Seokyun.</p>
<br/>
<p><span class="flashback">“We closed it, but he disappeared right in front of us. As his team members, we want to take responsibility and find him.”</span></p>

<p><span class="flashback">“Captain Kwak. It is only a disappearance. For now, waiting is also an option. Since Trainee Han causes abnormal phenomena, there is no telling how the situation may change.”</span></p>
<br/>
<p>Jang Seokyun attempted to refuse by appealing to principles.</p>
<br/>
<p><span class="flashback">“Director Jang.”</span></p>
<br/>
<p>That was when Je Hyun-oh spoke.</p>

<p>A rough, low voice that was not entirely human scraped across the room like something dragging against the floor.</p>
<br/>
<p><span class="flashback">“I found him. Han Goyo is the clue.”</span></p>
<br/>
<p>It was a statement that made no sense at all.</p>

<p>Yet Jang Seokyun’s expression immediately hardened.</p>

<p>After letting out a deep sigh, Jang Seokyun eventually gave in and approved the search for Han Goyo.</p>

<p>It should never have happened.</p>

<p>The opportunity cost of wasting time and manpower just to search for one eligible individual was impossible to calculate.</p>

<p>The most significant loss was Samra.</p>

<p>True to the name Samramansang<sup id="samra"><a href="#samrafoot">1</a></sup>, he gathered information from around the world and served as one of the main pillars of the Trial Response Agency.</p>

<p>That Samra pushed himself to the verge of overload, repeatedly searching and searching.</p>

<p>For a single person.</p>

<p>To scan the entire Earth down to the second, Samra stopped imitating a human and functioned purely as a machine.</p>

<p>The sight of him physically connecting with the Agency’s supercomputer network was disturbing.</p>

<p>And it was not only Samra.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk and Je Hyun-oh continued carrying out Agency missions according to their agreement with Jang Seokyun, but they refused any mission that would take longer than a day.</p>

<p>They needed to respond immediately if any clue about Han Goyo appeared.</p>

<p>Director Ham Jiwol collapsed the moment she heard the news.</p>

<p>Then, as soon as she regained consciousness, she broke down crying and demanded that they bring back even Han Goyo’s corpse.</p>

<p>Unfortunately, Kwak Hanmuk witnessed that scene, and a major incident nearly occurred.</p>
<br/>
<p><span class="flashback">“Director Ham. Are you treating another team’s member, who is still alive and well, like a cadaver? Keep acting like this and see what happens, you damn—”</span></p>
<br/>
<p>Kwak Hanmuk overturned the entire research department.</p>

<p>After Je Hyun-oh, the Special Mission Team had destroyed the research department for the second time, but the department still could not file a complaint.</p>

<p>Because they knew what the word ‘team’ meant to Kwak Hanmuk.</p>

<p>The Trial Response Agency only began returning to normal after Samra finally succeeded in locating Han Goyo.</p>

<p>Only then did everything slowly, very slowly, begin returning to its original place.</p>

<p>After witnessing that entire process, Won Taebeom was able to reach an even clearer conclusion.</p>

<p>Han Goyo had to be made subordinate to the Trial Response Agency.</p>

<p>If necessary, he would put Han Goyo in the Agency’s isolation facility again and again.</p>

<p>“Special Mission Team trainee Han Goyo.”</p>

<p>That thought did not change even when he came face-to-face with Han Goyo.</p>

<p>However, it left a bitter taste in his mouth.</p>

<p>The boy looked unmistakably young.</p>

<p>The fact that he had to do something unpleasant to someone ten years younger than himself was not something Won Taebeom enjoyed.</p>

<p>But that was merely personal sentiment.</p>

<p>Once inside the trial, he intended to focus only on completing his mission.</p>

<p>…If he entered the trial.</p>

<p>“Lieutenant Colonel Je Hyun-oh, Lieutenant Won Taebeom has joined. We will enter.”</p>

<p>Mo Haein reported to Samra while placing her hand on the Sla2Plate meteorite.</p>

<p>At that exact moment, Won Taebeom realized something was wrong.</p>

<p>Because the world before his eyes was still completely normal.</p>

<p>Only the members of the Special Mission Team were dissolving into fluorescent colors.</p>

<p>He watched as the Special Mission Team disappeared right before him.</p>

<p>Samra’s voice came through his frozen earpiece.</p>

<p>—Lieutenant Won Taebeom. Did you fail to enter?</p>

<p>“…Yes. I do not know the cause.”</p>

<p>—I will investigate the matter.</p>

<p>“Understood.”</p>

<p>The voice did not disappear like he expected.</p>

<p>Instead, Samra continued.</p>

<p>—I have one more question.</p>

<p>“Please ask.”</p>

<p>—What order did Director Jang Seokyun give you?</p>
<hr class="calibre3"/><br/>
<div class="systembox"><p>‘Han Goyo (Little Lamb)’ has entered &lt;Slay to Plate&gt;!</p></div>

<p>A system window appeared before me.</p>

<p>However, I could not properly read it.</p>

<p>I only stared blankly with my mouth open in disbelief.</p>

<p>Completely unaware of my thoughts, Dominic sparkled happily and asked:</p>

<p>“Roasted Chestnut, what is your class? Ah, allow me to guess.”</p>

<p>Even if the four-person entry could somehow be dismissed as an abnormal phenomenon, the dungeon was the real problem.</p>

<p>There were sections that required five people.</p>

<p>‘No, but the slime was counted as a single participant.’</p>

<p>Somehow, it should work out.</p>

<p>And if it was Dominic, perhaps he could use something similar to magic inside the dungeon.</p>

<p>I briefly imagined a pudding slime casting magic before finally reading the system window.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<div class="systembox_wrap">
<p>
◆ &lt;Slay to Plate&gt; Main Quest: Create the greatest restaurant and conquer the dungeon.<br/>

You, who have chosen the Chef class, enter the trial with a solemn determination.<br/>

This is because it will be your first achievement presented to Akasha as a young god.<br/>

You must prove and grow your abilities as a young god by clearing the True Ending.<br/>

Quest Success: Survival!<br/>
Quest Failure: Decreased Recognition! Explosion of &lt;Slay to Plate&gt; !
</p>
</div>
</div>

<p>I reread the final sentence several times.</p>

<p>Decreased recognition could be ignored since I still did not know what it was used for.</p>

<p>But—</p>

<p>‘Explosion of Slay to Plate?’</p>

<p>If an incubated trial was left uncleared for a certain amount of time, it would explode.</p>

<p>By transforming the surrounding area into the same form as the trial and spreading the virus on a massive scale, it was the situation the Trial Response Agency feared the most.</p>

<p>I imagined the Gangnam area, including Dosan Park, being transformed into Sla2Plate.</p>

<p>A chill naturally ran through me.</p>

<p>‘We absolutely have to clear the True Ending.’</p>

<p>I had already intended to do so, but now I felt even more desperate.</p>

<p>At that moment, Dominic’s ears fluttered as he spoke.</p>

<p>“Oh, my. I accidentally saw the answer. Roasted Chestnut, you are the Chef.”</p>

<p>It took me a moment to understand what he meant.</p>

<p>Dominic had been looking at my system window….</p>

<p>It was unsettling, but not something completely surprising.</p>

<p>If it was Dominic, it was certainly possible.</p>

<p>I took a slow breath and calmed myself.</p>

<p>‘But in CallOVo, I don’t think Dominic could see the system window.’</p>

<p>I would have to confirm whether he could only see it after entering Sla2Plate, or if there was some specific condition.</p>

<p>I quietly looked around.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh and the captains who entered with me were checking their own system windows while standing some distance away.</p>

<p>After confirming that there was enough distance between us, I quickly whispered.</p>

<p>“Yes, Dominic-ssi. I chose Chef.”</p>

<p>Since this was a five-person entry, we had decided on everyone’s classes beforehand during the briefing.</p>

<p>I volunteered for Chef.</p>

<p>Not because I was good at cooking.</p>

<p>It was because it seemed like the most convenient role for managing a restaurant.</p>

<p>When I played Sla2Plate as a game, I always chose Chef as well.</p>

<p>After hearing my answer, Dominic politely asked:</p>

<p>“If it is not too much trouble, may I request a dish prepared by Roasted Chestnut?”</p>

<p>In CallOVo, Dominic only pretended to eat food or drink tea.</p>

<p>I had no idea why he suddenly became interested in cooking.</p>

<p>“Of course, I should prepare something for you too, Dominic-ssi. I’ll make something delicious.”</p>

<p>Since he had been trapped in a trial with a British setting, I assumed he would probably enjoy anything I made.</p>

<p>With my thoughts filled with prejudice, I promised Dominic I would cook for him.</p>

<p>Around that time, Mo Haein and Kwak Hanmuk finished checking their system windows and approached me.</p>

<p>Mo Haein looked at me and muttered in disbelief.</p>

<p>“We really entered with only four people…. This is the first time I’ve seen an abnormal phenomenon like this.”</p>

<p>She had checked several times and confirmed there were only four participants.</p>

<p>Beside her, Kwak Hanmuk stared at the pudding slime.</p>

<p>Under his suspicious gaze, I quietly pretended not to notice.</p>

<p>“The dungeon is the real issue, but we’ll figure that out later.”</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk exchanged a glance with Mo Haein.</p>

<p>“Goyo.”</p>

<p>“Trainee.”</p>

<p>Hearing them call me, I cautiously answered.</p>

<p>“Yes, Captains.”</p>

<p>Mo Haein spoke first.</p>

<p>After hesitating slightly, she said in a very quiet voice:</p>

<p>“First… I’m glad you came back safely.”</p>

<p>Immediately after saying that, she moved on to the next topic as if it was nothing.</p>

<p>“And there’s something I want to ask.”</p>

<p>“Yes!”</p>

<p>I waited, prepared to answer sincerely and do my best no matter what the question was.</p>

<p>But I could not immediately answer.</p>

<p>“What is registration?”</p>

<p>“What?”</p>

<p>Mo Haein looked up at me with her arms crossed.</p>

<p>“In Clean3, a system window appeared saying that you registered me.”</p>

<p>…Crazy.</p>
