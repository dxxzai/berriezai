<h1>Chapter 80</h1>

<p>I could no longer even use the flimsy excuse of the true ending.</p>

<p>However, after finally saying what I honestly felt, I calmed down a little.</p>

<p>Once I came to my senses, I could realize just how reckless I had been acting.</p>

<p>“Captain Kwak, but….”</p>

<p>I confessed the truth that it was still too early to fight the Fire Dragon.</p>

<p>After listening to my explanation that we had not fulfilled the prerequisite conditions required to defeat Infernum, the final boss of Sla2Plate, Kwak Hanmuk raised his eyebrows and said,</p>

<p>“Well, but it’d be a waste to miss the true ending after coming all the way here. We busted our asses in the dungeon, and we’re supposed to get the normal ending? Fuck, absolutely not.”</p>

<p>That was… a fair point too.</p>

<p>The true ending was truly right in front of us.</p>

<p>“And I want to try fighting the Fire Dragon.”</p>

<p>After answering that there was no proper fight against the Fire Dragon in the normal ending, Kwak Hanmuk turned his head toward Mo Haein.</p>

<p>“Captain Mo, are you okay with it?”</p>

<p>I looked toward Mo Haein with discouraged eyes.</p>

<p>Mo Haein stared straight at me.</p>

<p>She looked at me without even blinking and gave me a very clear and simple condition.</p>

<p>“Promise me you won’t do something like what you just did ever again.”</p>

<p>“Yes. I’m sorry.”</p>

<p>I answered without making any excuses, filled with sincere regret.</p>

<p>Then I looked at Je Hyun-oh….</p>

<p>He was walking toward me while replacing one of his metal hands with the Crow Gauntlet.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh lightly moved the hand he had changed into the Crow Gauntlet.</p>

<p>Then he stopped in front of me.</p>

<p>The moment I saw my reflection in his black goggles, his hand immediately grabbed me by the collar and lifted me up.</p>

<p>My feet left the ground as my airway was compressed.</p>

<p>“Cough…!”</p>

<p>A groan escaped naturally as I struggled to breathe. Before I could even try to struggle.</p>

<p>My body was thrown through the air.</p>

<p>BANG!</p>

<p>My back slammed into the ornamental trees Je Hyun-oh had carefully trimmed, and leaves scattered down.</p>

<p>“Ahhh! Lieutenant Colonel Je!”</p>

<p>“Trainee!”</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein tried to rush over in surprise, but Je Hyun-oh raised a hand to stop them.</p>

<p>Step, step, step.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh approached with slow steps.</p>

<p>“Roasted Chestnut.”</p>

<p>Dominic called out to me. He had clung tightly to me while my body was flying through the air and asked,</p>

<p>“Roasted Chestnut, are you alright? Should I help?”</p>

<p>I shook my head while pretending to gather my thoughts.</p>

<p>“Hmm, Roasted Chestnut is… too lenient with his subordinate.”</p>

<p>The Pudding Slime expressed its dissatisfaction, but I did not have the mind to pay attention right now.</p>

<p>Meanwhile, Je Hyun-oh reached me and crouched down.</p>

<p>He sat down and stared silently at me, who was collapsed and gasping for breath.</p>

<p>“Goyo.”</p>

<p>A throbbing pain rose from my back where I had struck the tree.</p>

<p>I was definitely bruised.</p>

<p>Swallowing the pain, I barely lifted my gaze.</p>

<p>And seeing my own face reflected in his black goggles, I answered.</p>

<p>“Yes….”</p>

<p>“You said you wanted to live.”</p>

<p>He tilted his head slightly while continuing to look at me.</p>

<p>“But why do you keep doing this?”</p>

<p>It was a mutter filled with genuine confusion.</p>

<p>I hurriedly tried to bow down and apologize.</p>

<p>“I’m sorry, I….”</p>

<p>Crack.</p>

<p>But before I could even finish speaking, a sharp blade grazed right beside my face.</p>

<p>The tree where the metal hand had been embedded split apart with a cracking sound and fell backward.</p>

<p>The tree crashed down directly behind me, but I could not even look away.</p>

<p>Frozen stiff, I stared at Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>The person who held my entire attention asked again.</p>

<p>“You do want to live, right, Goyo?”</p>

<p>“Yes…. I really want to live.”</p>

<p>The person with one hand lowered wearing the Crow Gauntlet and the other lowered as a metal hand moved his fingers out of habit.</p>

<p>It was a movement that seemed as if my answer did not matter much.</p>

<p>After silently weighing something about me for a while, Je Hyun-oh finally spoke.</p>

<p>“Yeah.”</p>

<p>A quiet whisper followed.</p>

<p>“Because I haven’t decided yet either.”</p>

<p>“What?”</p>

<p>I asked again because I did not understand, but he never answered.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh slowly stood up.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein quickly approached me.</p>

<p>“Good grief, you’ve become a beaten-up chestnut.”</p>

<p>“Are you okay?”</p>

<p>The captains helped me up and protested toward Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>“Why do you keep throwing the kid around?”</p>

<p>“Lieutenant Colonel Je. You didn’t have to go this far.”</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh answered simply.</p>

<p>“Goyo deserved it.”</p>

<p>After that, no one could refute him.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk awkwardly cleared his throat and brushed the dirt off my clothes.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh watched that for a moment before speaking.</p>

<p>“Captain Kwak, Captain Mo.”</p>

<p>The pitch-black figure, with the afternoon sunlight behind him, looked down at me and said,</p>

<p>“Let’s go hunt the Fire Dragon.”</p>
<hr class="calibre3"/>
<p>The dungeon ends at the 99th floor.</p>

<p>However, the tower continues upward, and the unfinished number becomes complete as the 100th floor.</p>

<p>At the very top of the tower was the nest of the Fire Dragon Infernum.</p>

<p>The twins were probably being held there.</p>

<p>I took a deep breath in front of the dungeon entrance and slowly let it out.</p>

<p>To achieve the true ending, the decisive battle with the Fire Dragon was essential.</p>

<p>The standard strategy originally required completing two conditions.</p>
<br/>
<ol>
<li><p>Achieve a restaurant reputation score of 1,000 points to obtain the title ‘Best Restaurant.’</p></li>
<li><p>Conquer up to the 99th floor of the dungeon to obtain the ‘Flame Truffle.’</p></li>
</ol>
<br/>
<p>Equipping the ‘Best Restaurant’ title obtained from the first condition created fire immunity for all party members.</p>

<p>It allowed them to nullify the continuous damage caused by the endless heat inside the Fire Dragon’s nest.</p>

<p>Additionally, they could ignore the fire attribute bonus gained by the Fire Dragon when attacking from its own nest.</p>

<p>And the second condition: conquering up to the 99th floor of the dungeon.</p>

<p>In this case, along with the ‘Dungeon Conqueror’ title, they would receive the Flame Truffle, the only ingredient in the entire Sla2Plate world.</p>

<p>The Dungeon Conqueror title only increased tip probability, so it was not very useful. The important thing was the Flame Truffle.</p>

<p>Cooking with this produced food that greatly increased attack power specifically against the Fire Dragon.</p>

<p>However, right now, neither the first nor second condition was possible.</p>

<p>The restaurant score had not yet reached 1,000 points, and there was no time to conquer the 99th floor of the dungeon.</p>

<p>Because the twins had suddenly been captured, we had to fight Infernum without being fully prepared.</p>

<p>Infernum was the boss of a five-star trial. Even with complete preparation, he was not an opponent we could face carelessly.</p>

<p>Even so, I did not think the strategy was impossible.</p>

<p>There were Je Hyun-oh and the captains, and above all else….</p>

<p>I knew Infernum well.</p>

<p>Entering the dungeon, I took out the food I had prepared in my inventory and placed it in the lobby.</p>

<p>“Everyone, please eat this first. I’ll brief you on the Fire Dragon strategy while you eat.”</p>

<p>It was the Fire Flame Course Meal.</p>

<p>It was not a perfect combat meal, but since it was a course consisting of six-star dishes, the effects it provided were considerable.</p>

<p>The captains quickly ate the dishes while listening to the Fire Dragon strategy I had planned.</p>

<p>They had only eaten one or two types of food until now, so when they tried the entire course meal for the first time, their eyes widened.</p>

<p>“Crazy. I think I got fucking strong.”</p>

<p>While Kwak Hanmuk marveled, Mo Haein also checked her stats and could not hide her surprise.</p>

<p>The dishes greatly increased attack power, defense, critical hit chance, and critical damage, but the most important part was the buff that appeared after eating the entire course meal.</p>

<p>“Trainee, does this have fire immunity too?”</p>

<p>“Yes. It lasts for 20 minutes.”</p>

<p>The continuous damage from the Fire Dragon’s nest was quite painful, so it was best to defeat it before the buff ended.</p>

<p>I explained that if 20 minutes passed, they would have to endure by restoring their health with recovery potions or food.</p>

<p>After that, I used the chef class skill, ‘À la carte.’</p>

<p>Red spark-like lights swirled around the captains’ bodies before disappearing.</p>

<p>À la carte was a buff skill obtained when a chef reached level 80 and 85% proficiency.</p>

<p>Party members who ate the food could choose one stat they needed most and increase it.</p>

<p>Naturally, the stat the captains chose was attack power.</p>

<p>However, I chose a different stat.</p>

<p>“This is also only 20 minutes, so keep the buff duration in mind.”</p>

<p>À la carte’s cooldown was one hour, meaning it could effectively only be used once per battle.</p>

<p>It would have been best to apply the buff after entering the Fire Dragon’s nest, but since we did not know what could happen inside, I safely used the skill beforehand.</p>

<p>After that, I handed out small cookie-like foods that could be eaten quickly for recovery.</p>

<p>“Food and recovery potions can be used at the same time.”</p>

<p>“Wow, being a chef was a fucking insane class.”</p>

<p>While listening to Kwak Hanmuk’s admiration, I made one final request to Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>Perhaps because of our previous experience, he accepted the request more easily this time.</p>

<p>After finishing preparations, everyone stepped onto the teleportation magic circle.</p>

<p>Mo Haein took out her Black Moon Blade and gently stroked it for a moment.</p>

<p>I had always thought it looked like a spear no matter how I looked at it, so I turned my gaze toward Kwak Hanmuk.</p>

<p>Come to think of it, Kwak Hanmuk had continued wearing rosary beads even inside Sla2Plate.</p>

<p>I had never actually seen him use them.</p>

<p>Since it did not fit the Trial Response Agency’s tendency to avoid using items, I was curious.</p>

<p>I decided to ask him later and waited for Mo Haein’s signal to enter.</p>

<p>After confirming everyone was standing on the magic circle, she spoke briefly.</p>

<p>“We’re entering.”</p>

<p>The available floor number changed to 100.</p>

<p>Normally, it was only possible after conquering up to the 99th floor and seeing the event scene, but because we had received the Fire Dragon’s invitation, we could enter immediately.</p>

<p>The moment we reached the Fire Dragon’s nest through the teleportation magic circle, intense heat rushed toward us.</p>

<p>Unlike the dungeon, an enormous open space spread out before us.</p>

<p>Amazing! You have entered the Fire Dragon’s nest!<br/>
What kind of adventure awaits here?<br/>
Be careful not to offend the Fire Dragon’s mood!</p>

<p>The enormous space, large enough for a Fire Dragon to lie down comfortably, was covered with smoothly polished white marble and obsidian.</p>

<p>One wall was lined with display cases.</p>

<p>When people thought of a dragon’s nest, they usually imagined shining gold and mountains of jewels.</p>

<p>However, the Fire Dragon’s display cases were filled with finely crafted wine glasses, silverware, various alcohols and spices, and rare ingredients.</p>

<p>All of them were ingredients preserved by magic at the exact moment they were at their freshest and best for cooking.</p>

<p>And in the center was a vast dining table.</p>

<p>The table, carved from obsidian, had a delicate red magic circle engraved on it. When infused with magic, it maintained the food placed on the table at the most appropriate temperature.</p>

<em><p>“Little one.”</p></em>

<p>A rumbling voice echoed through the space.</p>

<p>I looked upward.</p>

<p>Very far upward.</p>

<p>A massive ball of flame appeared in the darkness.</p>

<p>It was the Fire Dragon’s eye.</p>

<p>Infernum had the form of a dragon, but his true body could not be properly seen because it was hidden in darkness.</p>

<p>Only his vivid red eyes were visible, staring at me.</p>

<em><p>“You have come to become my cuisine.”</p></em>

<p>Then, after a slight pause, he continued.</p>

<em><p>“…Is the end of your journey coming as well?”</p></em>

<p>The added words were likely directed at Dominic.</p>

<p>However, Dominic above my head did not respond at all.</p>

<p>Even though it was no different from saying he would die alongside me, he seemed not to consider it worth answering.</p>

<p>The being before us was called the Fire Dragon’s monarch and was of a level capable of seeing Dominic’s existence, but that was all.</p>

<p>At most, he had briefly caught Dominic’s interest, and now he had fallen into the same meaningless existence as any other NPC.</p>

<p>If there was any meaning to it, he was merely an ingredient I would soon catch and cook.</p>

<p>A question suddenly occurred to me.</p>

<p>To Dominic, even someone as great as Infernum was insignificant.</p>

<p>I wondered why someone like that had found meaning in me and even made a contract with me.</p>

<p>It was certainly strange enough that simply saying he was being lenient because I was a young god did not explain it.</p>

<p>Putting the thought aside, I calmly looked up at Infernum and asked.</p>

<p>“Where are Ella-nim and Ellun-nim?”</p>

<p>I had searched for them first the moment we entered the 100th floor, but they were nowhere to be seen.</p>

<p>At my question, a huge laugh echoed.</p>

<em><p>“If you wish to hear the answer, come onto my table.”</p></em>

<p>The red eyes disappeared, and Infernum, now transformed into a human, walked out from the darkness.</p>

<p>The final boss battle’s background music began to play.</p>

<p>Sharp, mechanical digital drum beats pounded steadily, raising the tension as if announcing the disaster that would soon descend.</p>

<p>The low bass sounds and unknown mechanical noises sent an unpleasant chill through the air.</p>

<p>The music suddenly stopped, and Infernum pulled out a chair and sat down before the table.</p>

<p>Sitting there elegantly, he gave a relaxed declaration.</p>

<p>“Now, it is time for the feast.”</p>

<p>A rough and destructive lo-fi sound, as if a monster’s roar had been distorted, struck my ears once.</p>

<p>Then, fast electronic sounds that seemed to tear the ears apart layered over a rhythm shifting between off-beat and on-beat, announcing the true opening of hell.</p>

<p>Among the chaotic music, a sparkling sound could be heard.</p>

<p>Ignoring the system window, the first phase began.</p>

<p>The first phase attacks of the Fire Dragon Infernum were extremely simple yet deadly.</p>

<p>Following his gesture, the empty space above our heads became densely filled with silver.</p>

<p>The identity of the silver objects that would rain down across the entire area after a few seconds was all forks and knives.</p>

<p>However, the reason the first phase’s attack was deadly was not because of the sharp utensils.</p>

<p>It was because one randomly selected party member would be sent onto Infernum’s dining table.</p>

<p>The selected party member became unable to act during the first phase.</p>

<p>Additionally, when attacking Infernum, the selected party member’s health would decrease instead.</p>

<p>However, this time, there was no random selection.</p>

<p>“Little one. Come to me.”</p>

<p>A hook attached to a chain shot forward.</p>

<p>The hook caught my waist and, hanging from the ceiling, suspended me like a piece of meat.</p>

<p>Infernum laughed while looking at me hanging over the table, folded in half.</p>

<p>“Meat tastes bad if it gets damaged.”</p>

<p>As expected, things were going exactly as predicted.</p>

<p>I twisted my lips into a smile and smiled back.</p>

<p>“You’re speaking as if you’ll actually get the chance to eat me.”</p>

<p>The moment the Fire Dragon raised his eyebrows.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh, with his wings spread, flew toward me while taking the full rain of forks and knives.</p>

<p>The silver utensils striking his metal wings created a loud clattering noise.</p>

<p>BANG!</p>

<p>Landing on the table, he grabbed me before Infernum could react and threw me toward the floor.</p>

<p>Then immediately, he swung a hook—not his metal hand—toward Infernum.</p>
