<h1>Chapter 102</h1>


<p>Monday morning.</p>

<p>As soon as I opened my eyes, I checked my weekly schedule.</p>

<p>The Medic’s Monday schedule was as follows:</p>

<div class="fieldset"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>▸ Monday <br/>
☐ Check and organize medication inventory.<br/>
☐ Examine at least six human inmates during face-to-face consultations.<br/>
☐ Conduct rounds in Zone X.<br/>
☐ Check the infirmary’s CCTV.</p></div></div>


<p>It looked simple at a glance, but there wasn't enough time to get it all done in a single day.</p>

<p>For starters, taking stock of the medication wasn't easy.</p>

<p>Hundreds of different drugs were jumbled together haphazardly.</p>

<p>Back when I first organized them in the game, I once spent the entire day just sorting them.</p>

<p>"I remember getting dragged off and beaten by the guards for failing the mission on my very first day."</p>

<p>Fortunately, the inventory remained the same every time; once memorized, the task became much easier.</p>

<p>If it was identical to the game, I should be able to finish it within thirty minutes.</p>

<p>I decided to hurry to the infirmary.</p>

<p>Since consultations for human inmates began at 9:00 AM, I had to get there before then.</p>

<p>"I need to find something to eat, too."</p>

<p>There was a cafeteria, but if I went there to eat too early—without reading the room—I’d immediately get beaten up by the guards.</p>

<p>I had to scavenge for food little by little while avoiding the guards' eyes, which was no easy task.</p>

<p>I looked in the mirror and neatly put on my doctor's coat.</p>

<p>Prison Medics usually wear scrubs, but in Cell-X, I was issued a white coat.</p>

<p>Standing out in a prison that was otherwise entirely pitch-black, the white coat made me feel even more conspicuous.
</p><p>'I’ve heard that white animals in the wild stand out and easily fall prey to attacks.'</p>

<p>Recalling a factoid from an animal-themed short video—and pondering the correlation between wearing a doctor’s coat and being bullied—I fidgeted with the garment before stepping out of my quarters.</p>

<p>The medical room, the medic's primary workspace, was located at the outermost edge of the prisoner block.</p>

<p>"Roasted Chestnut, do problems arise if you don't stick to your set schedule every day?"</p>

<p>I gave an honest answer to the slime guard's question.</p>

<p>"Yes. If I fail a task, the guards harass me."</p>

<p>"Oh dear... Does that mean I have to harass you, too?"</p>

<p>If a slime guard were to resort to violence, how would they do it?</p>

<p>Smacking me with a squishy body, pressing down hard, rubbing vigorously...</p>

<p>None of those options sounded too bad.</p>

<p>Indulging in the idle fantasy that all the guards might transform into soft slimes, I replied:</p>

<p>"Unless you're carrying out a guard's duty, there's no need for that."</p>

<p>"That's a relief."</p>

<p>In fact, the very act of walking to the medical room with me right now was already far removed from a guard's official duties.</p>

<p>Upon arriving at the medical room, I took a look around the interior.</p>

<p>True to a prison housing heinous criminals, the atmosphere inside was incredibly bleak and dreary.</p>

<p>Most of the fixtures were made of stainless steel with a dull sheen, creating a cold, stifling vibe.</p>

<p>At a glance, it looked more like a dissection room than a medical facility.</p>

<p>The small window was covered with closely spaced iron bars, making it difficult to see outside. The space was small, containing only a single medical examination bed, but the thick restraint straps attached to it were the first thing that caught my eye.</p>

<p>The bed’s legs were bolted firmly to the floor; the structure was so solid that it wouldn't budge even if several strong men hung off it.</p>

<p>I lifted my gaze to check the ceiling.</p>

<p>I spotted a CCTV camera mounted in the corner.</p>

<p>CCTV cameras, which monitored every move of both the doctor and the inmates, were a staple of the infirmary.</p>

<p>Having once been beaten severely in the infirmary after a CCTV camera malfunctioned in the game, the camera's operational status was of utmost importance to me.</p>

<p>"I have to keep it working at all costs."</p>

<p>Firmly resolving to do so, I stepped onto a chair to inspect the camera.</p>

<p>The indicator light showed it was functioning properly, and there was no visible damage.</p>

<p>After confirming it was in good working order, I organized the medicine cabinet.</p>

<p>Since the layout was identical to the game, the task was easy.</p>

<p>Once I had neatly arranged the last of the medicine bottles, I checked the daily schedule.</p>

<p>Next to the completed tasks, short handwritten notes had been added.</p>

<div class="fieldset"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>▸ Monday <br/>
☑ Check and organize medication inventory. <i>(Keep the key safe.)</i> <br/>
☐ Examine at least 6 human inmates during face-to-face consultations.<br/>
☐ Conduct rounds in Zone X.<br/>
☑ Check the infirmary CCTV. <i>(Ensure it is operational.)</i></p></div></div>

<p>These notes confirmed the accuracy of the operational manual.</p>

<p>It was a smooth start.</p>

<p>After quickly cleaning the infirmary, I discovered a box of expired cereal tucked away in the corner of a desk drawer.</p>

<p>It was emergency food stashed there by the previous Medic.</p>

<p>Judging by the way food was hidden in various spots around the infirmary, that person must have been subjected to quite a lot of harassment. I crunched on my cereal and tried offering some to the pudding slime, but—as expected—it politely declined.</p>

<p>"I can probably scrape by for about two days on the food my predecessor left behind."</p>

<p>After gathering and stashing away all the food, I checked the time.</p>

<p>8:50 AM.</p>

<p>Soon, human inmates would be flooding in for medical consultations under the escort of prison guards.</p>

<p>Conducting face-to-face consultations with human inmates was a real hassle.</p>

<p>I had to weed out the fakers to find the genuine patients and examine at least six people; perhaps because they were violent criminals, they had nasty tempers.</p>

<p>They all insisted they were sick, demanding prescriptions for narcotic painkillers and hospitalization to get out of forced labor.</p>

<p>If things didn't go their way, they would often fly into a rage and cause a scene.</p>

<p>Although a guard was always present, they were no help at all—they’d just chuckle while watching the Medic get harassed by the inmates.</p>

<p>"It's a good thing I can handle it all myself."</p>

<p>Since I knew exactly who was truly sick, along with their diagnoses and treatments, I figured I could pull off playing the doctor.</p>

<p>The crucial question now was which guard would show up.</p>

<p>It would be easiest if Kwak Hanmuk came, but that wouldn't allow me to gauge the situation inside the prison.</p>

<p>I just hoped for the most indifferent guard among them to be assigned.</p>

<p>Just before 9:00 AM, I decided to use my ability for the first time in a while to check Kwak Hanmuk's status window.</p>

<p>Seeing his current status made me curious about his situation.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>&#45; Current Status: Irritated.</p></div></div>

<p>I wondered what was going on to make his status show "Irritated."</p>

<p>Amidst that, I chuckled a little when I noticed my inventory was packed with food and snacks I’d picked up. I checked Je Hyun-oh’s status window as well, but it didn’t appear—likely because he hadn’t entered the Trial yet.</p>

<p>Then, nine o’clock struck.</p>

<p>Bang!</p>

<p>The infirmary door flew open without a knock. A uniformed prison guard stomped in, his heavy boots thudding against the floor.</p>

<p>My eyes widened the moment I saw the gray metal mask.</p>

<p>“Chief of Security?”</p>

<p>It was far too high a rank for a mere guard duty assignment—specifically, supervising the Medic.</p>

<p>The Chief of Security was the real power in the prison.</p>

<p>Every guard obeyed his orders absolutely.</p>

<p>They would follow the Chief’s commands even if it meant ignoring the Warden’s words.</p>

<p>To the inmates, he was the most feared figure of all.</p>

<p>That was because he would instantly punish anyone who stepped out of line.</p>

<p>Countless inmates had met their end at his hands—thrown into the sea, with their deaths staged to look like accidents or illnesses.</p>

<p>Honestly, since they were all heinous criminals, no one cared if they ended up as fish food.</p>

<p>The problem was that his violent nature was directed at me, too.</p>

<p>It was no exaggeration to say that the guards' harassment of the Medic was a practice instigated by the Chief of Security.</p>

<p>With his tacit approval and encouragement, the guards had gradually taken to tormenting the Medic.</p>

<p>It had reached the point where no doctor wanted to take the position here.</p>

<p>A vacant Medic post was exactly what the Chief of Security wanted.</p>

<p>Yet, I had stepped in to fill the role that had been empty since the previous doctor left.</p>

<p>That was precisely why I had been relentlessly harassed in the game, too.</p>

<p>In any case, the Chief of Security rarely stepped in personally. He usually had his subordinates do the harassing... </p>
<p>‘Why did he come here?’</p>

<p>I offered an awkward greeting, feeling uneasy.</p>

<p>“Hello…”</p>

<p>“Little Lamb. Begin the examination.”</p>

<p>Instead of returning the greeting, the Chief of Security issued an immediate order.</p>

<p>His tone was polite in words, but his attitude was effectively condescending.</p>

<p>I began the examination under his watchful eye.</p>

<p>The human inmates all had menacing appearances.</p>

<p>Although their hands and feet were restrained, preventing them from physically assaulting me, they had no trouble shouting with fierce, angry expressions.</p>

<p>“What does a young punk like you know! Just admit me to the infirmary!!”</p>

<p>Knowing that the guards were incredibly lenient when it came to inmates harassing the Medic, the prisoners expressed their rage quite aggressively.</p>

<p>However, I had been through too much to be fazed by mere shouting, threats, or profanity.</p>

<p>Compared to the Pudding Slime currently sitting on my shoulder, these guys felt like nothing more than insignificant specks of dust.</p>

<p>In any case, the status of the human inmates had no bearing on the game's ending.</p>

<p>I went through the motions of a cursory examination and wrote a perfunctory prescription.</p>

<p>“There’s nothing wrong. Drink plenty of water and get some rest.”</p>

<p>“You quack! You didn't even check my temperature!”</p>

<p>“Next patient, please.”</p>

<p>Then, when the "real" patients—whose cases I had memorized beforehand—appeared, I put on a show of examining them with the utmost dedication.</p>

<p>“Good heavens! This is a Methicillin-resistant <em>Staphylococcus aureus</em> (MRSA) infection!”</p>

<p>“…Medic. I haven’t even sat down in the chair yet.”</p>

<p>“Ah, I can tell just by looking at your face. There’s a risk of contagion, so I’m requesting immediate isolation.”</p>

<p>“Ahem. Well, if it calls for isolation... I suppose I do have that... Methi... what was it? Yeah, that sounds about right.” </p>
<p>The inmates kept getting suspicious because I acted like I knew too much too soon, so I threw in some performative touches—pretending to take their temperatures and applying a stethoscope.</p>

<p>I managed to conduct in-person consultations right up until lunchtime, successfully completing my assigned tasks.</p>

<div class="fieldset"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>▸ Monday<br/>
☑ Check and organize medication inventory. <i>(Keep the key safe.)</i><br/>
☑ Examine at least six patients during in-person consultations. <i>(Creature Inmate No. 13)</i><br/>
☐ Conduct rounds in Zone X.<br/>
☑ Check the infirmary CCTV. <i>(Ensure it is operational.)</i></p></div></div>

<p>"So, the only thing left is rounds in Zone X."</p>

<p>The truth or falsehood of the items in the work manual changes depending on the schedule.</p>

<p>Since the note about "Creature Inmate No. 13" had been added, I had to act under the assumption that the 13th inmate was present in Zone X today.</p>

<p>I hesitated just as I was about to re-read the previous Medic's work manual one last time before starting my rounds.</p>

<p>The Chief of Security was still in the infirmary.</p>

<p>'Why is he still here? Is he planning to hit me?'</p>

<p>I tensed up, wondering if he intended to get physical, when suddenly, a chime sounded.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Suspicion +5</p></div>



<div class="systembox"><p>Affinity +2</p></div>

<p>Once again, both suspicion and affinity rose simultaneously.</p>

<p>I could understand why the 'Suspicion' stat might rise—after all, I was observing the patients very closely.</p>

<p>However, I couldn't for the life of me figure out why 'Affinity' was increasing.</p>

<p>I checked the Chief of Security's current stats.</p>
<div class="systembox">
<div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Chief of Security<br/>
&#45; Suspicion: 13<br/>
&#45; Affinity: 3</p></div>
</div>
<p>"Suspicion is still far higher, though..."</p>

<p>Even though the numbers were within a safe range, the inability to understand the reason behind the fluctuations made me uneasy.</p>

<p>Just then, the Chief of Security's radio crackled with static.</p>

<p>—Security Department, Zone X, Code Red.</p>

<p>The Chief of Security responded to the clipped, professional voice on the other end.</p>

<p>"Acknowledged. I'm heading there now."</p>

<p>He immediately turned to me and issued an order.</p>

<p>"Little Lamb-nim. Please follow me."</p>

<p>"Pardon?"</p>

<p>"It seems a newly arrived creature inmate in Zone X is causing trouble. Please come with me to check the situation."</p>

<p>I looked at the Chief of Security in surprise.</p>

<p>I was flustered because I had never encountered a creature rampage event on the very first day of the schedule.</p>

<p>A 'Code Red' meant a violent incident had occurred.</p>

<p>It felt surreal to hear 'Code Red' here in the Trial, having heard the term in the real world not long ago.</p>

<p>"And to think a new creature inmate has arrived..."</p>

<p>In the game, a creature inmate had never been added before.</p>

<p>I was curious to see this creature I didn't know about.</p>

<p>I followed the Chief of Security to Zone X.</p>
