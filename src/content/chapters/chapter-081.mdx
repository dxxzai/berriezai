<h1>Chapter 81</h1>


<p>Infernum tried to dodge the hook, but it was useless.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh had already calculated even the movements he would make to avoid it.</p>

<p>“Kuaaaak!”</p>

<p>Infernum, pierced by the hook, screamed and stood up.</p>

<p>As he staggered backward while blood poured from his body, red scales began to grow across his skin.</p>

<p>When Infernum transferred damage to the person he selected, he entered a temporary invincible state.</p>

<p>The only tool capable of attacking him during that time was the hook.</p>

<p>During the briefing, I had clearly asked Je Hyun-oh to swing the hook because I would definitely be the selected target, and the plan proceeded perfectly.</p>

<p>After confirming that the hook had pierced Infernum, the captains immediately increased their distance.</p>

<p>Infernum, who had lost a huge amount of health in one blow, let out a vicious roar.</p>

<p>Having lost his human voice, he began transforming into a Fire Dragon.</p>

<p>The beginning of Phase 2.</p>

<p>I checked the remaining buff time.</p>

<p>‘15 minutes and 34 seconds.’</p>

<p>Because we had not been able to start the battle immediately after entering, a little more time than expected had been consumed during Phase 1.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh quickly equipped the Crow Gauntlet, lifted me up with one hand, and flew as far away from the Fire Dragon as possible.</p>

<p>This was because if we were nearby while Infernum transformed into a dragon, more than 90% of our health would be lost without fail.</p>

<p>Pillars of flame shot upward, completely covering Infernum.</p>

<p>The intense flames spread all the way to the ceiling, enough to completely erase the gentle darkness that had hung over the area.</p>

<p>As the flames that seemed capable of devouring everything around them died down, the true form of the Fire Dragon appeared.</p>

<p>A massive dragon that looked as if it had been made from black volcanic rock and red lava.</p>

<p>A being like a mountain looked down from an impossibly high place.</p>

<em><p>“Insignificant creatures dare to disturb my sacred feast.”</p></em>

<p>The dragon’s voice, filled with anger, caused the entire space to tremble, and the chandelier hanging from the ceiling fell.</p>

<p>Getting hit by the falling chandelier was an instant-death attack, so we immediately avoided it.</p>

<p>The loud background music became even more violent.</p>

<p>The melody broke apart, and metallic drum sounds mixed with shattered noise erupted explosively, making the song rampage chaotically.</p>

<p>The Fire Dragon opened its mouth.</p>

<p>All the air in the area began being violently sucked in, and a red glow gathered inside the dragon’s mouth.</p>

<p>The light changed from red to blue, and then into a pure, brilliant white of extreme heat.</p>

<p>Heat haze violently distorted around the wide-open jaws.</p>

<p>The space itself was burning from the heat of the concentrated magic power.</p>

<p>The Fire Dragon’s breath.</p>

<p>“Roasted Chestnut. Is it polite to wait until it fires that?”</p>

<p>Hanging from Je Hyun-oh, I ignored Dominic’s question and shouted.</p>

<p>“Captains!!”</p>

<p>Hearing my shout, Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein ran toward Infernum this time.</p>

<p>A stream of flame erupted from the Fire Dragon’s mouth.</p>

<p>The ultra-high-temperature flames shooting straight forward spread out in a fan shape following the Fire Dragon’s head, covering the entire space.</p>

<p>Touching it meant immediate death.</p>

<p>However, there was one tiny blind spot where it could be avoided.</p>

<p>Right beside the Fire Dragon’s feet.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh and I took position by the dragon’s left foot, while the captains positioned themselves by the right foot.</p>

<p>The blind spot was only large enough for one person to barely stand.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh continued holding me under his arm, while Kwak Hanmuk lifted Mo Haein up.</p>

<p>The brilliant white flames grazed dangerously close to the tips of their feet.</p>

<p>And the moment the breath passed, they immediately rushed out again.</p>

<p>Because the stomp was coming next.</p>

<p>The Fire Dragon, enraged that it had failed to defeat even a single person despite firing its breath, stomped its foot.</p>

<p>KUUUNG!</p>

<p>A heavy sound erupted, and the ground split apart.</p>

<p>Boiling lava was revealed between the cracks in the floor.</p>

<p>If we had been close to the Fire Dragon, we would have fallen directly into the lava.</p>

<p>The bubbling lava rose up and covered the entire floor.</p>

<p>To stay safe, we had to stand on broken stone fragments. Touching the lava caused health to rapidly decrease.</p>

<p>It was a mechanic that completely restricted our range of movement.</p>

<p>From the gaps where the lava flowed out, Hellfire Lizards and Hellfire Hounds crawled out.</p>

<p>Unlike ordinary monsters, these direct subordinates of the Fire Dragon had bodies black like charcoal, with lava dripping from their eyes and mouths.</p>

<p>The Hellfire Lizards used ranged attacks, firing fireballs from a distance, while the Hellfire Hounds rushed forward at high speed with close-range attacks.</p>

<p>Monsters that were individually equivalent to dungeon bosses charged toward us.</p>

<p>Mo Haein swung the Black Moon Blade toward the black hounds.</p>

<p>The Black Moon Blade flashed as it drew a massive half-circle.</p>

<p>The heavy crescent blade struck the hound’s head.</p>

<p>With a rough crushing sound of breaking bone, the hound scattered into flames.</p>

<p>Mo Haein did not stop and immediately rotated her body.</p>

<p>The Black Moon Blade, carrying centrifugal force, cut through the waists of two hounds charging from the side.</p>

<p>KWADEUDEUK!</p>

<p>The hounds’ tough volcanic-rock-like hides were sliced apart, and they scattered into flames as well.</p>

<p>Mo Haein caught her breath for a moment and showed a faint smile.</p>

<p>She did not express it, but she seemed frustrated from having to use an ordinary sword all this time.</p>

<p>“Hyaaap, saving Captain Mo!”</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk swung his greatsword and knocked away a hound flying toward Mo Haein’s back.</p>

<p>When the greatsword infused with the white light of holy power touched it, the hound exploded.</p>

<p>Mo Haein, avoiding the flames from the explosion, glared at Kwak Hanmuk for a moment.</p>

<p>It meant he was making things unnecessarily distracting.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk chuckled and struck the fireballs shot by the lizards with the broad side of his greatsword.</p>

<p>Swinging as if hitting a baseball, the fireball bounced away and fell onto the hounds.</p>

<p>While the hound staggered after being hit by the fireball, he drove the greatsword vertically downward.</p>

<p>The hound exploded, and flames burst outward.</p>

<p>“Hyaaah, Holy Judgment!”</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk used the Paladin’s Judgment skill.</p>

<p>The sword of light, boosted by the level increase and buff effects, multiplied into ten swords, spread across the sky, and rained down.</p>

<p>Watching the hounds pierced like skewers, Mo Haein asked while still busy swinging the Black Moon Blade.</p>

<p>“Why do you shout the skill name?”</p>

<p>“It feels different when you do. Captain Mo, try it too.”</p>

<p>“…….”</p>

<p>She looked like she regretted asking and focused on the battle again.</p>

<p>Thanks to both of them eating the course meal, their various stats had increased greatly, making the battle much easier.</p>

<p>In Kwak Hanmuk’s case, because of the holy bonus, he was using a Paladin’s greatsword instead of rosary beads, and his swordsmanship was skilled.</p>

<p>Feeling reassured, I looked forward again.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh was flying upward while holding me under his arm.</p>

<p>He intentionally slowed down to lure the Hellfire Lizards into targeting him.</p>

<p>The lizards all raised their heads toward the slow-moving target, and blazing red fireballs were fired directly at Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>Amid the pouring fireballs, he folded his wings and flew like an acrobat.</p>

<p>I curled my body as much as possible.</p>

<p>A fireball narrowly grazed my right cheek, and I smelled the ends of my hair burning.</p>

<p>Then Je Hyun-oh twisted his body in midair and rotated, avoiding the projectiles attacking simultaneously from the left and below.</p>

<p>The high-temperature masses exploded behind him by a paper-thin margin, scattering volcanic ash.</p>

<p>Another fireball flew toward Je Hyun-oh’s front a moment later.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh only tilted his head slightly, letting it pass beside his ear.</p>

<p>After avoiding every fireball, he threw me downward before the lizards could fire another round.</p>

<p>While my body flew through the air, I did not take my eyes off Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>The moment he threw me, Je Hyun-oh folded his wings and rapidly descended.</p>

<p>BANG!</p>

<p>The metal hand he slammed down shattered the lizard’s hard scales and tore deeply into its flesh.</p>

<p>Instead of hot blood, bright red lava poured out.</p>

<p>Before the lizard could even collapse while screaming, Je Hyun-oh swung his lava-covered metal hand toward the next target.</p>

<p>I turned my gaze away from Je Hyun-oh and prepared to land.</p>

<p>The place I had asked Je Hyun-oh to throw me toward was Infernum’s ingredient storage.</p>

<p>I planned to obtain an item there to fight Infernum, and also, perhaps….</p>

<p>Check whether there was a small Fire Dragon horn.</p>

<p>My body gradually approached the ground.</p>

<p>THUD!</p>

<p>I rolled across the floor messily.</p>

<p>I wanted to land stylishly with a breakfall, but I had never learned how.</p>

<p>‘Though I did it a lot in the game.’</p>

<p>If I had been a different class instead of a chef, I might have had basic martial arts techniques or passive skills that allowed me to perform a breakfall.</p>

<p>Thinking regretfully, I took out food for recovery. Since chefs were physically weak, a single fall caused a considerable loss of health.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Phoenix Meringue Cookie (1100G): A dessert made by selecting only the whites from phoenix eggs. It maintains its crispness forever even at room temperature. (Review: Happiness that breaks apart crisply and melts softly. A pleasant warmth spreading through the esophagus. The greatest luxury for myself. Rating: ★★★★★★.)</p></div></div>

<p>When I put it in my mouth and chewed, it shattered with a crisp sound and melted like cotton candy.</p>

<p>Along with the rich sweetness, warmth filled my body.</p>

<p>It was especially effective for restoring health, and its recovery speed was faster than a potion.</p>

<p>I swallowed the sticky meringue crumbs left in my mouth and ran toward the ingredient storage.</p>

<p>This time, there was something I could ask Dominic for help with at the perfect moment.</p>

<p>“Dominic-ssi! Could you perhaps find the Fire Dragon horn with magic?”</p>

<p>However, Dominic, who I expected would immediately agree, said something unexpected.</p>

<p>“Did you not say you would serve me using only your own strength, Roasted Chestnut?”</p>

<p>His question left me speechless.</p>

<p>The words I had said to Dominic came back with perfect clarity.</p>
<br/>
<p><span class="flashback">“Isn’t this the first dish I’m making for Dominic-ssi, my most trusted and relied-upon companion? I wanted to serve you entirely with my own strength. Please wait just a little longer.”</span></p>
<br/>
<p>I opened my mouth, then closed it.</p>

<p>And answered with a smile.</p>

<p>“…Yes. You’re right. I was just saying it. It’ll be soon, so please wait.”</p>

<p>I knew where the item I was looking for was.</p>

<p>The ninth storage case from the left.</p>

<p>When I ran there, an unusually cold chill swept out compared to the other storage cases.</p>

<p>Inside the enormous storage case was a single item.</p>

<p>The horn of an Ice Dragon.</p>

<p>The transparent, ice-like horn shimmered with a sapphire glow resembling the deep ocean.</p>

<p>I put on one of the Crow Gauntlets I had borrowed from Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>At that moment, a dragon’s roar was heard.</p>

<p>KIAAAAA!</p>

<p>Along with the roar that shook the entire space, an enormous pressure pressed down on my entire body.</p>

<p>It felt as if the air had become thin and gravity had grown heavier.</p>

<p>A paralysis state caused by the roar.</p>

<p>Once the debuff was applied, I could not move. It was even more dangerous because a chef’s physical abilities were poor.</p>

<p>I clung to the glass of the ingredient storage case while gasping for breath.</p>

<p>After waiting briefly, I heard Kwak Hanmuk shout from far away.</p>

<p>“Holy Purificationnn!”</p>

<p>Then white light surrounded my body. Thanks to the Paladin skill Purification, the debuff was removed.</p>

<p>The paralysis suddenly disappeared, and I almost fell before barely regaining my balance.</p>

<p>Fortunately, the glass of the ingredient storage case was weak enough that even a chef’s abilities could break it.</p>

<p>I took out a meat tenderizer from my inventory, smashed the glass, and obtained the desired item.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Blue Horn of the Ice Dragon (Large): An old horn of the Ice Dragon Tribe. When ground into powder and sprinkled onto food, it adds ultimate flavor. Due to its large size, it is especially valuable.</p></div></div>

<p>It was an essential item for defeating the Fire Dragon.</p>

<p>I held and hugged the Ice Dragon horn, which was as large as my torso, with one hand.</p>

<p>The moment it touched my body, a chill cold enough to make my hair stand on end spread through me, but the surrounding heat made it bearable.</p>

<p>Pressing the cold horn against my pounding heart, I ran again.</p>

<p>I quickly scanned the endless rows of ingredient storage cases, but I could not find the Fire Dragon horn.</p>

<p>The twins seemed to be safe.</p>

<p>I then checked the battle situation.</p>

<p>The captains were skillfully stepping across the small platforms above the lava while dealing with the lizards and hounds.</p>

<p>And Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>He was tearing apart the Fire Dragon like a mad crow.</p>

<p>Moving his six metal wings freely, he attacked and retreated swiftly, leaving the massive Fire Dragon helpless as its health continued to fall.</p>

<p>The endless attacks created a constant clanging sound.</p>

<p>The sound of the metal hand targeting the dragon’s scales.</p>

<p>The Fire Dragon was bleeding from various places.</p>

<p>Since its scales were too hard to easily break even with the metal hand, it seemed he had simply ripped several off.</p>

<p>By relentlessly attacking the exposed areas where the scales had been torn away, the Fire Dragon let out painful groans.</p>

<p>The number of hounds and lizards endlessly created from the lava began to decrease.</p>

<p>It seemed we would soon enter Phase 3.</p>

<p>‘6 minutes and 12 seconds.’</p>

<p>The remaining buff time was dangerously close.</p>

<p>If things went wrong, we would have to endure being roasted by the heat of the Fire Dragon’s nest for several minutes.</p>

<p>It seemed we had to finish it as quickly as possible.</p>

<p>It was while I was running toward the position we had agreed on beforehand.</p>

<p>I heard the sound of chains being released.</p>

<p>There are no hook mechanics in Phase 2, I thought.</p>

<p>Then, I saw two hooks descending from the ceiling.</p>

<p>The fluttering pale pink hair shone under the light.</p>

<p>The ones caught on the hooks were the twins.</p>

<p>I stepped forward to run toward them.</p>

<p>However, the two small bodies immediately fell.</p>

<p>Into the wide-open mouth of the Fire Dragon.</p>
