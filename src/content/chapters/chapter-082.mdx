<h1>Chapter 82</h1>

<p>It was the light signaling the recovery of its health.</p>

<em><p>“Hahahaha! Little one!”</p></em>

<em><p>“Don’t you want to see my underlings?”</p></em>

<em><p>“Meet them inside my stomach.”</p></em>

<p>I stared blankly at the Flame Dragon.</p>

<p>Strangely, I wasn’t angry. My mind was endlessly cold, and I was in an unusually calm state.</p>

<em><p>Only my eyes were unbearably hot….</p></em>

<p>The heat surging through my eyes made my head throb.</p>

<p>But I took my first step more calmly than ever before.</p>

<p>I ran again.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh and Captain Kwak were retreating while putting away their weapons.</p>

<p>They were trying to assess the battle situation after the Flame Dragon’s sudden recovery.</p>

<p>The berserk Flame Dragon roared, shaking the space itself.</p>

<p>When the Flame Dragon spread its wings and flapped them once, a powerful gust of wind erupted and pushed my body backward.</p>

<p>Following the wind, flames spread across the entire floor. There were no longer even any footholds left to avoid them.</p>

<p>A system window appeared.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Due to the roar of the berserk Flame Dragon, all buff effects have disappeared.</p></div>

<p>Not only the attack power increase buff, but even the fire immunity had completely disappeared.</p>

<p>I had been thrown into the flames and was continuously suffering from burns.</p>

<p>Defeating a Flame Dragon that had entered a berserk state and regained all of its health was objectively impossible.</p>

<em><p>However, I thought it was possible.</p></em>

<p>‘Divine Protection.’</p>

<p>A golden veil covered Je Hyun-oh and Captain Kwak.</p>

<p>After making them safe, I immediately sent a message.</p>

<p>Captain Kwak stared at the system window that appeared before his eyes with a confused expression, then turned to look at me.</p>

<p>There was no time to explain everything one by one.</p>

<p>I kept running.</p>

<p>I heard the sound of metal wings flapping. Je Hyun-oh grabbed me and soared into the sky.</p>

<p>Soon, Captain Kwak also began acting according to my message.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk extended the rosary beads.</p>

<p>The rosary beads that spread out into several strands multiplied into dozens, then hundreds, and covered the Flame Dragon.</p>

<p>The Flame Dragon, tangled in the rosary beads like a spider web, let out a boiling laugh.</p>

<em><p>“How foolish! Your end has already been decided.”</p></em>

<p>His words clearly revealed the confidence that something like this would never be able to restrain him.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk, sweating profusely from the intense heat, burst into laughter and cursed roughly.</p>

<p>“Bullshit, fuck…!”</p>

<p>The Flame Dragon’s flames swept across the Byeokjomok rosary beads.</p>

<p>However, the rosary beads did not burn despite the flames and endured without damage.</p>

<p>When Kwak Hanmuk pulled the rosary beads tightly, the Flame Dragon twisted its body.</p>

<p>Mo Haein, holding the Black Moon Blade, leapt up nimbly.</p>

<p>Instead of running across the floor filled with lava and flames, she ran along the long-stretched rosary beads.</p>

<p>She moved without any shaking, as if she were running on flat ground despite standing on thin strands made of rosary beads.</p>

<p>It looked as if she had jumped into boiling lava, but the flames had no effect on her wrapped in Divine Protection.</p>

<p>Mo Haein swung the Black Moon Blade toward its lower abdomen.</p>

<p>It was the area where the scales were the thinnest, making it a vital point.</p>

<p>Clang! The scales crumpled under the Black Moon Blade, then soon became ragged and fell away.</p>

<p>The Flame Dragon tried to twist its body as if annoyed, but it couldn’t move at all because it was bound by the rosary beads.</p>

<p>Mo Haein grabbed the fallen scale, crossed over the lava, and shouted toward me.</p>

<p>“Catch!”</p>

<p>Then she threw the scale like a spear.</p>

<p>Because it was the thinnest scale, his metal hand was enough to crush it.</p>

<p>I placed the broken scale fragments over the Ice Dragon’s horn.</p>

<p>When it encountered the heat, the sapphire-colored magic surrounding the horn began trembling unstably.</p>

<p>However, the coldness on the surface weakened, enough that I could touch it with my bare hand without wearing the Crow Gloves.</p>

<p>I firmly gripped the lower part of the horn. Then I took out the Chinese cleaver and sharpened the upper part.</p>
