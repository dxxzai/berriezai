<h1>Chapter 4</h1>

<p>My heart raced, and the corners of my mouth kept lifting on their own.</p>

<p>An Easter egg? Or an ending route I'd never seen before?</p>

<p>Either way, this was a new &lt;Happy Smile Factory&gt;.</p>

<p>There was still something I didn't know about the game I'd played until I was sick of it!</p>

<p>I wanted to tear it apart and dig through every corner right this instant.</p>

<p>I knew my reaction wasn't exactly normal-civilian behavior, so I pressed my lips together to hide my smile.</p>

<p>Right on cue, Mo Haein finished her discussion and spotted the Sad Smiley I'd assembled from the pieces.</p>

<p>I put on my dumbest face and explained to her, whose look clearly said what the hell did you do now.</p>

<p>"Well, uh, when I picked up this candy, it said I could repair toys with it. Even if they're just toys, I felt bad leaving them broken."</p>

<p>At my words, Mo Haein's face went blank. Park Seonggyeon burst out laughing.</p>

<p>"Haha! You're a soft one, aren't you? Wanting to fix them up."</p>

<p>He patted my shoulder.</p>

<p>"But no being soft here, alright? Gotta be careful. Can't explain in detail, but this place is dangerous as hell."</p>

<p>He tried to scare me, saying the toys might get back up and attack.</p>

<p>I apologized to him again.</p>

<p>Satisfied that he'd properly shaken me up, Park Seonggyeon's gaze drifted to the air, and his eyes went wide.</p>

<p>"Oh, Captain. This item description's a bit weird."</p>

<p>He'd read the Sad Smiley's item description through the System window.</p>

<p>"Um, sorry, but can we hurry up? I want to get out."</p>

<p>I pretended to be scared and urged Mo Haein and Park Seonggyeon to move on.</p>

<p>I was worried they might touch the Sad Smiley doll.</p>

<p>I had no idea if Nut Candy could actually restore a broken toy, but since things seemed to be progressing along a different route, I planned to keep leaving candy out.</p>

<p>Of course, Sad Smiley wasn't an NPC — it was just a doll item with a recorded voice. But I left the candy there anyway.</p>

<p>"It might mean something, but we already cut it in half. We've got a civilian with us — let's clear this first."</p>

<p>Luckily, Mo Haein focused on a quick clear.</p>

<p>She'd already pulled out the Black Moon Blade, so it seemed like she planned to brute-force her way through and end this in no time. A fitting approach for the Collapse ending.</p>

<p>Vwoom — Mo Haein swung her spear in a wide arc, flicking off the rainbow blood, then glanced at me one last time before turning away.</p>

<p>"Let's go."</p>

<p>"Yes ma'am!"</p>

<p>Park Seonggyeon was in a good mood again. He kept checking his hand — he'd already swapped his left arm for a toy one.</p>

<p>Mo Haein still had only one toy arm, because she needed the other for the Black Moon Blade.</p>

<p>If not for the Blade, she'd have equipped items on both arms first.</p>

<p>I watched Park Seonggyeon carefully as he celebrated.</p>

<p>In the Trials, item farming was directly tied to survival, so being happy about grabbing extra gear fast was natural.</p>

<p>'But something felt off.'</p>

<p>Mo Haein and Park Seonggyeon had cleared HapFactory multiple times.</p>

<p>There was a variable now, sure, but with Mo Haein pulling out the Black Moon Blade, clearing wouldn't be hard.</p>

<p>No — it was guaranteed.</p>

<p>So was getting one extra toy arm really worth that much excitement?</p>

<p>"You look young, Goyo. College student?"</p>

<p>I set aside my suspicion and answered Park Seonggyeon's question.</p>

<p>"Yeah."</p>

<p>Twenty-four was usually college age, right?</p>

<p>Explaining that I was a former pro gamer who retired due to injury and was currently unemployed would've been way too long-winded.</p>

<p>"Military?"</p>

<p>"Not yet."</p>

<p>Actually, I'm exempt...</p>

<p>No reason to get into that either, so I let it slide.</p>

<p>"How'd you end up in here?"</p>

<p>"I just fell asleep and woke up here... Can I get back home?"</p>

<p>"'Course you can. Just trust your hyung here."</p>

<p>He clicked his pincer fingers together. The guy looked ready to cause an accident any second, and I didn't trust him at all.</p>

<p>Luckily, we entered the second zone — the lab — before the conversation could continue.</p>

<p>Unlike the sparkling, fantastical space outside, the lab was a ruin of shattered stone and exposed steel beams.</p>

<p>Various devices that looked more at home in a biology lab than a toy factory were scattered around.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<div class="systembox_wrap">
<p>◆Main Quest: Wake the Prince in Pieces.<br />
In the secret lab of the toy factory, the Prince in Pieces sleeps.</p>
</div>
</div>

<p>I pretended to look around the lab while glancing at the System window.</p>

<p>I needed to find the prince doll pieces hidden around the room, but to get the last piece, I'd have to fight the first boss.</p>

<p>Park Seonggyeon used his extendable toy arm to move rubble and found the doll pieces in no time. Mo Haein solved a puzzle on a computer screen and obtained a key.</p>

<p>She picked up the limbless prince doll and used the key to open the locked lab door.</p>

<p>Beyond the door marked with a large "RESTRICTED AREA" sign, there was a shattered glass tank.</p>

<p>The break looked recent — rainbow-colored liquid was still gushing out of the cracked tank.</p>

<p>The same color as the toys' blood.</p>

<p>Drip. A droplet fell from the ceiling.</p>

<p>I wiped my wet cheek with the back of my hand and considered what gesture would properly convey surprise. Then I slowly looked up.</p>

<p>"Gasp—!"</p>

<p>I stumbled back, and Park Seonggyeon let out a snort of laughter.</p>

<p>"Scared? Don't worry, we'll take that thing out in no time."</p>

<p>A massive hole of unknown depth gaped in the ceiling.</p>

<p>From the darkness, a giant spider toy descended on a yarn web.</p>

<p>All eight of its legs were made of toy blocks, and its smiling face had four pairs of eyes.</p>

<p>A spider doll that was definitely not suitable for selling as a children's toy.</p>

<p>HapFactory's first boss — Princess Tarantella.</p>

<p>"Our Captain'll swing that Black Moon Blade and it's done."</p>

<p>I glanced at Park Seonggyeon. His eyes were full of greed as he stared at Mo Haein's Black Moon Blade.</p>

<p>That glint triggered a memory I'd forgotten.</p>

<p>'Ah... Park Seonggyeon. I remember now.'</p>

<p>A new System window appeared.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<p>You think it would be nice to have an example to figure out what 'System' and 'Registration' mean. </p>
</div>

<div class="systembox">
<p>And lo and behold — you've found a perfect example right in front of you! </p>
</div>

<div class="systembox">
<p>'Park Seonggyeon' is a registrable sample character. Would you like to register?</p>
</div>

<p>The timing was perfect, as if it had read my mind. This time, I didn't hesitate.</p>

<p>'Register.'</p>
<div class="systembox">
<p>Registering 'Park Seonggyeon (Sample)'...</p>
</div>

<p>When you clear an Archive game, a cookie video plays after the ending.</p>

<p>The post-credits scenes usually show a short conversation between the player who escaped the Trial and an Archive character, but sometimes the location changes too.</p>

<p>In those cases, you get to walk around South Korea within the Archive.</p>

<p>I usually visited the Trial Response Agency.</p>

<p>I'd stopped by Mo Haein's office there once and read an official document about a verdict on an incident that happened inside a Trial.</p>

<p>A team member who'd entered the Trial with her had attacked Mo Haein for the Black Moon Blade, resulting in a death.</p>

<p>Mo Haein had cut the attacking team member in half with the Blade.</p>
<div class="news_wrapper">
<p>...This incident was triggered by Lieutenant Park Seonggyeon's unilateral attack, and the counterattack by the attacked party was confirmed as an immediate response to the preemptive strike.</p>

<p>1) The incident occurred during a boss fight.</p>

<p>2) Due to the chaos at the scene, the attacked party lacked the time to make a judgment call.</p>

<p>3) The attacked party was also seriously injured.</p>
</div>

<p>Considering the above factors comprehensively, the attacked party's use of force is judged as self-defense, not excessive response.</p>

<p>Even though it was a death, self-defense was recognized, and the punishment was light — just filing a report and a short suspension.</p>

<p>The team member who'd attacked Mo Haein was Park Seonggyeon.</p>

<p>He'd only appeared in one line in the document, so it made sense I hadn't remembered.</p>

<p>'So he caused an accident in HapFactory.'</p>

<p>The toy limbs you could get in HapFactory granted superhuman strength and incredible range.</p>

<p>'And today looks like the day he's going to cause that accident.'</p>

<p>Park Seonggyeon seemed to be planning to get the toy arm before Mo Haein, steal the Black Moon Blade, then kill her or turn her into a factory worker for the perfect crime.</p>

<p>His actions had a high chance of interfering with the True Ending.</p>

<p>And I didn't like that he was messing with Mo Haein, one of my favorite characters.</p>

<p>'Besides, if he kills Mo Haein, there's no way he'd leave a witness like me alive.'</p>

<p>So I wasn't going to leave Park Seonggyeon alive either.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<p>Registration complete!</p>
</div>

<div class="systembox">
<p>You feel like checking the sample's status window.</p>
</div>

<p>I'd been thinking about this since earlier — the System window's tone was weird.</p>

<p>In the original game, the System window had a perfectly normal tone.</p>

<p>Just your standard system message guiding the player, like in any game.</p>

<p>But now it was acting like it was speaking my thoughts for me.</p>

<p>'Is this related to me becoming the System?'</p>

<p>For now, our goals aligned, so I decided to go along with it.</p>

<p>I checked Park Seonggyeon as the System window instructed.</p>

<p>'Park Seonggyeon status window.'</p>

<div class="systembox">
<div class="systembox_wrap">
<p>— Park Seonggyeon (Sample) ♥x3<br />
— Employee of &lt;Happy Smile Factory&gt;<br />
— Items: Cherry Picker (3), Nut Candy (12), Flashlight (1).</p>
</div>
</div>

<p>The information I could check through the System was very simple and sparse.</p>

<p>The moment I felt dissatisfied, another System window popped up.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<p>You are dissatisfied with your own simple and sparse abilities.</p>
</div>

<div class="systembox">
<p>You decide you must hone your abilities as the System.</p>
</div>

<p>It seemed like I needed to grow my stats before more categories would unlock.</p>

<p>I examined the meager list more closely.</p>

<p>The heart times three was probably a life counter. The items were...</p>

<p>"...?"</p>

<p>The Cherry Picker count — the toy limb item — was three. But Park Seonggyeon had only gotten two so far.</p>

<p>There was only one explanation.</p>

<p>He'd brought items from a previous HapFactory clear.</p>

<p>Unless there was a special reason, the TRA banned using items from previous runs of the same Trial.</p>

<p>Same reason they banned using items from other Trials — to minimize variables.</p>

<p>Also, the TRA checked all items acquired after clearing a Trial, and those items were basically TRA property.</p>

<p>There was no reason to hand Cherry Pickers over to Park Seonggyeon, so he must have gotten them illegally.</p>

<p>'He's doing something dangerous because of Mo Haein.'</p>

<p>I was certain now — Park Seonggyeon was going to cause an accident in HapFactory today.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<p>You are curious about what influence you can exert over 'Park Seonggyeon (Sample)' as the System.</p>
</div>

<div class="systembox">
<p>You want to check the additional categories below the items.</p>
</div>

<p>I did as instructed and checked the categories beneath the item list.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<div class="systembox_wrap">
<p>&gt; Message<br />
&gt; Execution</p>
</div>
</div>

<p>Just two. And I had no idea what either of them meant. I picked the less intimidating one first — Message.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<p>You think of a message to send to 'Park Seonggyeon (Sample).'</p>
</div>

<p>Wait — could I actually pop up a System window in front of Park Seonggyeon?</p>

<p>As I was guessing at the function, the old computer sitting in the corner of the lab flickered to life, and a cheerful chiptune melody played.</p>

<p>The 8-bit BGM announced the start of the boss fight with Princess Tarantella.</p>

<p>Tarantella's smiling face split into four directions, revealing rows of packed teeth.</p>

<p>"——!"</p>

<p>An incomprehensible roar burst from its gaping mouth, tearing at my eardrums.</p>

<p>At the same time, note-shaped toys began falling from the hole in the ceiling, keeping pace with the rapidly intensifying music. The note toys were a gimmick that only appeared in the Collapse route.</p>

<p>Mo Haein sliced through the notes and blocked Tarantella's eight attacking legs.</p>

<p>Park Seonggyeon watched the Black Moon Blade move as if dancing. He spread both pincer hands wide, then slowly closed them.</p>

<p>Click. A faint sound cut through the chiptune.</p>

<p>While I checked the System window, Park Seonggyeon had equipped the third Cherry Picker on his leg.</p>

<p>He leaped into the air using his right toy leg.</p>

<p>He jumped between the falling notes, reaching out his pincer hand — just as he was about to attack Mo Haein.</p>

<p>I sent a message to Park Seonggyeon.</p>

<p>A System window appeared in front of him.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<p>lol</p>
</div>

<p>The sudden window blocking his vision made Park Seonggyeon's hand waver.</p>

<p>His pincer missed Mo Haein, only grazing her shoulder.</p>

<p>Mo Haein, who'd nearly been thrown into Tarantella's mouth, shouted.</p>

<p>"Park Seonggyeon! What the hell are you doing?!"</p>

<p>Stumbling as he landed, Park Seonggyeon wore a dumbfounded expression.</p>

<p>He cursed under his breath and tried to swing his toy arm again.</p>

<p>But his hand froze mid-lift.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<p>LMAO</p>
</div>

<div class="systembox">
<p>LMAOOOO</p>
</div>

<div class="systembox">
<p>LMAOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO</p>
</div>

<p>Park Seonggyeon's face turned blue as the System windows flooded his vision.</p>

<p>They were all messages from me.</p>
