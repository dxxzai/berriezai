<h1>Chapter 136</h1>

<p>It was a blue light that seemed to spread out like a star cluster.</p>

<p>I felt as if I were blinded by the feast of stars, which twinkled distantly yet brilliantly.</p>

<p>When the light finally faded...</p>

<p>I was on a beach where dark blue waves crashed.</p>

<p>The dazzling emerald water was nowhere to be seen, and dark, heavy waves filled my vision.</p>

<p>The sky was full of storm clouds, looking as if rain would fall at any moment, and the beach was made up of sharp rocks and pebbles.</p>

<p>Instead of soft, warm white sand, the hard texture piercing my feet woke me up.</p>

<p>It was an unfamiliar space, completely different from the peaceful place where I had been just moments ago.</p>

<p>Yet, I felt a strange sense of déjà vu. As if I had been here in a dream.</p>

<p>A man stood on the beach.</p>

<p>His towering stature, visible at a glance even from a great distance, made him look inhuman.</p>

<p>He was gazing at the sea.</p>

<p>Even though he couldn't see anything except the fierce waves, he seemed to see something clearly.</p>

<p>His blue eyes scattered a brilliant radiance.</p>

<p>He slowly raised his hand.</p>

<p>Then the sea parted.</p>

<p>Something. Huge. Appeared. Overwhelming. Terrifying.</p>

<p><em>Huh?</em></p>

<p>I flinched in surprise and closed my eyes.</p>

<p>The moment my vision was blocked, a belated terror washed over me. It was the terror of having seen something a human should not have seen.</p>

<p>In the pitch-black darkness, I heard the grim scream it let out.</p>

<p>It was a language I could not understand at all.</p>

<p>However, for some reason, I thought it sounded as if it were pleading.</p>

<p>It sounded like a voice confessing its sins and begging for its life.</p>

<p>I impulsively opened my eyes.</p>

<p><em>I thought it would be okay to see this much.</em></p>

<p>And I witnessed...</p>

<p>The moment a distant being was annihilated by an even more terrible catastrophe.</p>

<p>A cosmic nebula covered the dark blue sea. The being with an impure form desperately resisted the starlight engulfing it.</p>

<p>Its final struggle shook the sea, but it was futile.</p>

<p>Before that being, even the sea was nothing more than a single drop of water.</p>

<p>Its movements, stained with blue starlight, subsided.</p>

<p>Then, it was sucked into the black void that appeared in the air.</p>

<p>I gazed at the black space.</p>

<p>It contained a universe filled with beautiful stars.</p>

<p>However, looking into its essence, it was ultimately a void where nothing existed.</p>

<p>The sea, which had split apart, tangled back together as one, and soon the waves surged as if nothing had happened.</p>

<p>I looked at the being that had accomplished all of this.</p>

<p>“…….”</p>

<p>I tried to call its name, but I could not.</p>

<p>It was because I could not tell if it was the being I knew.</p>

<p>I felt no fear or terror. It was because it had transcended the realm of perception.</p>

<p>I stared at it motionless, as if my brain were paralyzed. He slowly turned toward me.</p>

<p>Our eyes met.</p>

<p>A voice resembling a well-tuned double bass called out to me.</p>

<p><em>“Roasted Chestnut.”</em></p>

<p>Just as the rabbit butler and the cat butler would call out to me…</p>

<p>As I stood there motionless, he quietly watched me.</p>

<p>He let out a faint sigh and spoke sternly, as if addressing a misbehaving child.</p>
<br />
<p style="font-size:21.6px;"><em>“Come here.”</em></p>
<br />
<p>I had no choice but to approach him.</p>

<p>It was not by my own will.</p>

<p>My body moved forcibly, even though I did not want to. It was akin to a survival instinct.</p>

<p>I staggered toward him. And I lifted my head.</p>

<p>The moment our eyes met was also not something I had wanted.</p>

<p>“Were you surprised?”</p>

<p>His tone was affectionate.</p>

<p>He soothed me as if he were still the person I knew and secretly relied on.</p>

<p>“I am sorry. If I had known you would be this surprised, I wouldn’t have given you the order.” </p>

<p>I opened my lips very slowly.</p>

<p>“Dominic-ssi…”</p>

<p>I thought there might not be a reply.</p>

<p>But he answered.</p>

<p>“Yes, Roasted Chestnut.”</p>

<p id="ssi">The way he addressed me had changed slightly from before.<sup><a href="#ssifoot">1</a></sup></p>

<p>He quietly looked down at me.</p>

<p>He was waiting for me to speak, but I… didn’t know what to say.</p>

<p>There were countless questions I wanted to ask, but I couldn’t bring myself to blurt anything out.</p>

<p>As our gazes met, I felt as if I were being slowly strangled.</p>

<p>The moment I thought I was suffocating from the gradually tightening sensation,</p>

<p>I managed to speak.</p>

<p>“Has your memory… returned?”</p>

<p>“Ah, memory.”</p>

<p>A smile flickered in his eyes, as if he found my question amusing.</p>

<p>He bowed his head toward me.</p>

<p>A vast, blue cosmos filled my field of vision.</p>

<p>“Not yet. Look, isn’t it Dominic?”</p>

<p>He was right.</p>

<p>It was Dominic, for now.</p>

<p>Dominic gave me a gentle smile.</p>

<p>“Now that Roasted Chestnut has grown a bit, I suppose you can understand. Although I called it ‘memory’ according to human standards, it is closer to something else.”</p>

<p>He explained it to me step by step.</p>

<p>“It is about reclaiming one’s <em>existence</em>. Because I lost a part of myself when Roasted Chestnut called me.”</p>

<p>It was a statement I would never have understood in the past.</p>

<p>But now, I felt I understood what it meant.</p>

<p>Back when I was playing the game, I summoned him, and he responded to my call and came to CallOVo.</p>

<p>However, he was an existence that could not be contained by mere trials.</p>

<p>By being forcibly fitted into it, he lost his memory—a part of his existence.</p>

<p>I parted my lips.</p>

<p>“I… am sorry… because of me….”</p>

<p>“What?”</p>

<p>“This, what you lost… If I hadn’t summoned you...”</p>

<p>Dominic let out a low laugh as I stammered my apology. Although warnings of danger filled my mind, I was unable to do anything.</p>

<p>I could only stare at Dominic as if I were possessed.</p>

<p>“Actually, I’m curious, too.”</p>

<p>Dominic leaned toward me.</p>

<p>Due to the difference in our physiques, even though he had only leaned his upper body slightly, it created the illusion that he was about to topple into me.</p>

<p><em>“No being would dare summon me...”</em></p>

<p>A voice so affectionate it was almost deceptive asked me.</p>

<p>“How did Roasted Chestnut summon me?”</p>

<p>I blinked blankly. My tongue moved on its own to answer him.</p>

<p>“I… don’t really know either…”</p>

<p>If I knew anything, I would surely have confessed every detail to Dominic.</p>

<p>But truly, I have no idea.</p>

<p>To think that such a formidable being was summoned and trapped in a trial by a mere game playthrough.</p>

<p>I was the one who wanted to know how such a thing could have happened.</p>

<p>Dominic <em>looked into</em> me.</p>

<p>I found myself staring directly into his eyes.</p>

<p>Blue eyes where stars floated.</p>

<p>His pupils, resembling the universe, were so incredibly deep that the longer I gazed, the more I could not escape.</p>

<p>I felt suffocated by the infinite emptiness...</p>

<p>After a very long time, Dominic averted his gaze.</p>

<p>Dominic looked at me with amusement as I gasped for breath, like someone who had been submerged in deep water and barely pulled out.</p>

<p>&quot;Fascinating, it is certainly curious.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;.......&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;We might find out the reason once you grow a little more, Roasted Chestnut.&quot;</p>

<p>He brushed back my sweat-soaked hair.</p>

<p>He reassured me that there was no need to worry about that, as regaining his existence was something that would eventually happen.</p>

<p>Then, he spoke of something else.</p>

<p>&quot;Now, Roasted Chestnut. There is a more important matter than such trivialities right now.&quot;</p>

<p>Dominic asked with a subtle smile on his lips.</p>

<p>“Was ■■■ okay to play with? I couldn’t stay by your side because I was cutting it into tiny pieces to make it easier for you to handle.”</p>

<p>You mean that was just 'a tiny piece’?</p>

<p>The thing that turned the world upside down, melted and swallowed every being, and plunged them into illusion?</p>

<p>“Ah, what I just dealt with was the rest of ■■■. Thanks to you, I, too, have regained a little of what I lost.”</p>

<p>He continued by saying that while he would have regained more if he had come into contact with a larger entity, but this wasn’t bad.</p>

<p>He also didn’t forget to praise me, a young novice, for having dealt with ■■■—even if it was just a tiny fraction—a great feat.</p>

<p>His explanation, tailored to my level, was kind.</p>

<p>When I failed to react, he lightly placed his hand on my shoulder.</p>

<p>“Roasted Chestnut.”</p>

<p>I flinched and shook his hand off.</p>

<p>Stepping back, I looked at Dominic with eyes full of wariness.</p>

<p>“What did you just do?” </p>

<p>I felt the exact same sensation that I had when ■■■ tried to infiltrate my mind.</p>

<p>Dominic replied softly, as if to signal that I was not in danger.</p>

<p>“I was just trying to ease your tension, Roasted Chestnut.”</p>

<p>“I’m fine, so I’d like to just talk right now.”</p>

<p>Only after saying it did I feel belatedly scared.</p>

<p>I wondered if I had been too arrogant, but strangely, Dominic seemed amused.</p>

<p>“Understood, Roasted Chestnut. I won’t lay a hand on you.”</p>

<p>He smiled as he readily promised me. His blue gaze met mine.</p>

<p>It was a silent command to come closer.</p>

<p>I wanted to run far away, but there was nowhere to escape.</p>

<p>I also knew that running away wouldn't solve the problem.</p>

<p>I gritted my teeth and approached Dominic with cautious steps.</p>

<p>He knelt down. He kept his gaze slightly apart and held something out to me.</p>

<p>The orb, about the size of a candy, looked terribly harmless. However, the moment I saw that neon green thing, it reminded me of an eyeball.</p>

<p>“You had trouble eating it before, so I made it a little differently this time.”</p>

<p>Staring at the round orb for a long time, I finally realized.</p>

<p>“…Dominic-ssi.”</p>

<p>And I asked him.</p>

<p>“Did I eat the heart and eyeball of Infernum?”</p>

<p>“Yes.”</p>

<p>I felt deflated by his quick answer, without even a single word of denial.</p>

<p>Since Sla2Plate, I had been tormented by heat.</p>

<p>I had always felt heat whenever my eye color changed.</p>

<p>But this time was completely different. It was a flame that burned me from the very core.</p>

<p>Caught up in the heat, I committed violent thoughts and actions.</p>

<p>I recalled the time when I was a young god, engulfed in intense heat.</p>

<p>Filled only with the thought of burning my opponent to ashes, I created abilities akin to punishment.</p>

<p>I paid no heed to the warnings the system window gave me. I was in an extremely irrational state.</p>

<p>I concluded that the cause of the flaming heat, which I could not handle, was because I ate Infernum.</p>

<p>What would happen if I ate this thing Dominic is handing me right now—presumably made of ■■■?</p>

<p>It didn't seem like anything good would happen.</p>

<p>At the same time, a fundamental question arose.</p>

<p>What was Dominic trying to do?</p>

<p>He had fed me Infernum, and this time, he was going to the trouble of preparing ■■■ for me.</p>

<p>I could clearly feel the goodwill Dominic had for me.</p>

<p>He wanted to help me grow as a god.</p>

<p>But why?</p>

<p>Because I was a young god and his contractor? Or to satisfy his curiosity?</p>

<p>Various thoughts crossed my mind, but none seemed to be the right answer.</p>

<p>I chose my words very carefully, selecting the ones that would least not offend Dominic.</p>

<p>“Dominic-ssi… Thank you for your concern, but I am fine this time.”</p>

<p>I had almost lost my reason to the flames of Infernum, and I felt I shouldn't accept something new here.</p>

<p>At my refusal, Dominic opened his eyes slightly, looking surprised.</p>

<p>“It won’t hurt. Didn’t I promise?”</p>

<p>I had no memory of him making a promise to me.</p>

<p>I answered him, explaining that I wasn't refusing because of the pain.</p>

<p>“I’m afraid consuming it might make me... strange.”</p>

<p>“I see.”</p>

<p>Dominic lowered his gaze for a moment and rolled the orb back and forth in his hand.</p>

<p>The orb in his large hand looked incredibly small.</p>

<p>“Roasted Chestnut.”</p>

<p>“…Yes.”</p>

<p>“You are badly hurt.”</p>

<p>“…….”</p>

<p>At his reproachful words, I bit my lip.</p>

<p>I had felt it to some extent, too. The pain was still rising.</p>

<p>When I didn’t answer, Dominic slowly blinked.</p>

<p>He took my hand and placed the orb on my palm.</p>

<p>Then, clasping my hand over the orb, he spoke.</p>

<p>“Look. You’ll be able to see it now, Roasted Chestnut.”</p>

<p>Confused and not understanding what he meant, I felt a shock that sent shivers down my spine.</p>

<p>It was because I had <em>looked into</em> myself.</p>

<p>A bewildered voice stammered out.</p>

<p>“What, what is this….”</p>

<p>Golden lines were haphazardly etched all over my body.</p>

<p>The irregular lines looked precarious, as if my body had been shattered into pieces and barely pieced back together.</p>

<p>It seemed as though the slightest touch would send me scattering back into fragments.</p>

<p>“It has been like this since the first time I <em>looked into</em> Roasted Chestnut. It’s getting worse and worse. You must have sustained serious internal injuries while using your powers this time… Let me just check for a moment.”</p>

<p>“…!”</p>

<p>He reached out his other hand. I screamed.</p>

<p><em>No! It hurts!</em></p>

<p>The hand that had dug into my chest caressed my heart. The touch, as if checking something, was rather rough.</p>

<p>I struggled, trying to break free somehow, but I couldn’t escape because his hand was gripping me tightly.</p>

<p>Dominic frowned as he watched me in pain.</p>

<p>But he did not stop his hand.</p>

<p>“As expected… to think you're this broken.”</p>

<p>He rummaged through me with a look of blatant displeasure.</p>

<p><em>Stop it!</em></p>

<p>A low voice fell repeatedly into my ears.</p>

<p>&quot;You have done something truly foolish. Why did you choose Resurrection? You are now in a more dangerous state.&quot;</p>

<p>A large hand clutched my heart.</p>

<p>Thump, thump—I could feel my heartbeat trapped in his grip.</p>

<p>Dominic spoke as if delivering a stern sentence.</p>

<p>&quot;If things continue like this, Roasted Chestnut will cease to exist.&quot;</p>
