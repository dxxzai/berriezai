<h1>Chapter 8</h1>

<p>I squinted at the system window, wondering what kind of ending this was supposed to be.</p><p></p>

<p>No miracle happened. The ■ didn't suddenly turn into letters.</p>

<p>The next system window that popped up was just as incomprehensible.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Han Goyo has made his name known to Akasha!</p></div>

<div class="systembox"><p>Current Recognition: 0.02%</p></div>

<p>Recognition?</p>

<p>Who exactly had heard my name to raise my Recognition — and by a measly 0.01%, no less.</p>

<p>I was still picking through the system window when Smiley suddenly began to melt into a shimmering rainbow liquid.</p>

<p>There was no time to react.</p>

<p>The moment it dissolved, streaks of light shot out from the collapsed factory — multiple beams, like a meteor shower, converging into the rainbow liquid.</p>

<p>And then Smiley was gone.</p>

<p>Leaving only a strange candy behind.</p>

<p>I bent my knees slowly and picked it up.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Happy Smile Candy: Eating it will fill you with laughter and happiness.</p></div></div>

<p>The rainbow candy was shaped like Smiley's face.</p>

<p>As I stood there holding it, another system window appeared.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>You feel that you absolutely must consume this.</p></div>

<p>When I'd forced myself to accept that earlier system window, I'd had a secret hope — that maybe Smiley and the toys would turn into items I could take outside.</p>

<p>But I never imagined it would play out like this.</p>

<p>I stared at the candy for a long moment, then carefully placed it in my inventory.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<div class="systembox_wrap_j">
<p>
The Trial &lt;Happy Smile Factory&gt; has ended.<br/>

The Trial will be permanently closed in 80 seconds.
</p>
</div>
</div>

<p>"Th-this... this is the True Ending, right?"</p>

<p>I turned to look at Mo Haein. She'd read the system window too — her trembling eyes met mine as she asked.</p>

<p>"It's the True Ending, isn't it...? My eyes aren't playing tricks on me, right...? Permanent closure..."</p>

<p>The True Ending of a Trial carried special meaning.</p>

<p>Permanent closure of the Trial. And the return of all survivors.</p>

<p>A Trial cleared via True Ending would never regenerate. It would also spit out everyone trapped inside — even those who'd been imprisoned in it before, their mutated bodies restored to normal.</p>

<p>In HapFactory's case, the employees who'd been working at the plant would be rescued.</p>

<p>The True Ending was the only way to save survivors trapped inside a Trial, but its difficulty was near-impossible. Success stories were vanishingly rare.</p>

<p>When I first started the Archive's games, an NPC told me there had been exactly nine successful cases worldwide.</p>

<p>"Yes, it's the True Ending."</p>

<p>I gave Mo Haein a small smile and offered my congratulations.</p>

<p>"Good work, Captain."</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>Mo Haein stared at me blankly. Then her face crumpled, and she turned her head away roughly.</p>

<p>The back of her neck was flushed red from emotional agitation.</p>

<p>Mo Haein wasn't normally the type to show her feelings this openly.</p>

<p>I looked away until she'd calmed down a little.</p>

<p>Fortunately, she soon returned to her usual self. Regaining her composure, she spoke slowly, in a near-whisper.</p>

<p>"...Thank you."</p>

<p>I had no idea how she'd explain me once we got out of here, but at least it probably wouldn't be as a smuggler anymore.</p>

<p>Just then, a system window appeared.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<div class="systembox_wrap_j">
<p>
You are the Top Contributor of &lt;Happy Smile Factory&gt;.<br/>

You may take '1' item.
</p>
</div>
</div>

<p>When a True Ending — or certain specific endings — was cleared, those with high Contribution could take an item from the Trial. In HapFactory's case, only the Top Contributor could acquire one.</p>

<p>Without hesitation, I chose the Happy Smile Candy.</p>

<p>Mo Haein looked curious about what I'd picked, but didn't ask. She seemed to figure she'd have plenty of time to interrogate me later anyway.</p>

<p>Eighty seconds passed. The system window flashed its closure notification, and the scenery before me crumpled, washing over with fluorescent colors.</p>

<p>I emerged from the Trial.</p>

<hr class="calibre3"/>

<p>The place where HapFactory had hatched was an abandoned factory site.</p>

<p>Police lines stretched wide around the perimeter. Scattered across the factory grounds were people of all kinds, unconscious and sprawled out.</p>

<p>The wind was cold. Wearing only a white short-sleeved tee and gray track pants, I went to rub my arms — then winced and swallowed a groan.</p>

<p>My injured thigh ached, and small wounds dotted my body everywhere.</p>

<p>If this had been a game, it would've been a no-hit clear.</p>

<p>There was a massive difference between clicking with a keyboard and mouse and actually moving your body. Even though I'd exercised diligently every day without fail, my physical limits were glaring. A no-hit clear with the basic physical stats the game gave you was impossible.</p>

<p>The TRA will treat me when they drag me in, right...?</p>

<p>Since I was already in this situation, I planned to let the TRA figure out my current status in more detail and mooch off their food and shelter for a while.</p>

<p>I looked around and spotted Mo Haein waking up at a distance. In her TRA uniform, she stood out sharply among the civilians.</p>

<p>I was about to limp over to her when —</p>

<p>"Don't move."</p>

<p>A cold sensation touched my neck. I rolled my eyes to check — a military fixed-blade knife.</p>

<p>Ah. Park Seonggyeon.</p>

<p>I'd forgotten about this bastard.</p>

<p>I couldn't help it — I let out a short, hollow laugh.</p>

<p>"You're laughing? The fuck, Goyo. You think this is funny?"</p>

<p>"Should I cry instead?"</p>

<p>"...What?"</p>

<p>My informal tone caught him off guard. Park Seonggyeon faltered for a moment.</p>

<p>I looked down at him. He still seemed to think of me as an obedient, mild-mannered college student.</p>

<p>"Park Seonggyeon!"</p>

<p>Mo Haein had fully come to her senses now. She aimed her gun at him and warned, "Last chance. Back off."</p>

<p>But her gun was trembling — barely.</p>

<p>She was the one who'd taken the brunt of the attacks during the boss fight in HapFactory. She should have been on her way to an ambulance right now.</p>

<p>Park Seonggyeon, on the other hand, was perfectly fresh. Naturally — he'd hidden away while Mo Haein and I ran ourselves ragged, just waiting for the clear.</p>

<p>"Captain Mo Haein!"</p>

<p>Well aware of this, Park Seonggyeon sneered openly.</p>

<p>"I'll give you a chance too. Hand over the Black Moon Blade."</p>

<p>"Aren't you afraid of the TRA?"</p>

<p>"Nope. Not scared at all."</p>

<p>"How much did they offer you? If it's money, I can pay — "</p>

<p>"Ha. Mo Haein. Mo Haein."</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>"You deaf or what? I said hand over the Black Moon Blade!"</p>

<p>The blade sliced across my neck. A thin red line appeared.</p>

<p>And while Mo Haein and Park Seonggyeon were locked in their standoff — I was reading the system windows that kept popping up without pause.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<p>
You are aware that 'Park Seonggyeon (Sample)' will not spare the hostage.
</p>
</div>

<div class="systembox">
<p>
You are enraged by his insolence.<br/>
You consider how to educate a dog that dares to bite without recognizing its master.
</p>
</div>

<div class="systembox">
<p>
A suitable method comes to mind!<br/>

You want to check 'Execution.'
</p>
</div>

<p>I had no idea what Execution was, or why the system was throwing such a fit about it.</p>

<p>The vibe wasn't great.</p>

<p>But if things stayed like this, I'd lose the Black Moon Blade and bleed out. The wound on my thigh was still bleeding.</p>

<p>'Execute Park Seonggyeon.'</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>You execute 'Park Seonggyeon (Sample).' This decision is irreversible and will cause a permanent change to Akasha.</p></div>
<br/>
<div class="systembox"><p>Would you like to execute 'Park Seonggyeon (Sample)'?</p></div>

<p>Choices appeared.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<div class="systembox_wrap_j">
<p>
&#45; Yes.<br/>
&#45; Execute.<br/>
&#45; Dooo it!
</p>
</div>
</div>

<p>Even through the blur creeping into my vision, the system window was so absurd I almost laughed.</p>

<p>"Hey, Park Seonggyeon."</p>

<p>He flinched and looked at me, still locked in his standoff with Mo Haein.</p>

<p>I grinned crookedly and said, "Last chance. Let me go."</p>

<p>"Have you lost your goddamn mind...?"</p>

<p>But his voice was trembling — faintly.</p>

<p>Like he was scared.</p>

<p>I selected 'Dooo it!'</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Executing 'Park Seonggyeon (Sample)'!</p></div>

<p>Fanfare rang out. A cheerful sound, like confetti should have been flying.</p>

<p>Then came the sound of fireworks.</p>

<p>No — not fireworks.</p>

<p>It was the sound of Park Seonggyeon's head bursting.</p>

<p>His exploded head turned to golden light. It scattered everywhere, glittering brilliantly like stars strewn across the sky.</p>

<p>His body, now headless, crumbled into golden dust after it.</p>

<p>Through the shimmering light, I saw Mo Haein.</p>

<p>She stood frozen, her gun still aimed.</p>

<p>She stared at me like that, without moving, until every last fleck of light was gone.</p>

<p>Her gun never lowered.</p>

<p>This is bad.</p>

<p>I'd thought Park Seonggyeon might die, but I hadn't expected it to be so... spectacular. And outside the Trial, no less. Such a bizarre phenomenon.</p>

<p>I look way too suspicious.</p>

<p>Cold sweat beaded on my back, but the system window only displayed a placid square.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Registered characters: '0.'</p></div>

<div class="systembox"><p>You wish to register 'Mo Haein' as a new character.</p></div>

<p>I ignored the system window fluttering before my eyes and opened my mouth.</p>

<p>"Um... Captain?"</p>

<p>I was just about to call out to her carefully when —</p>

<p>"Captain Mo Haein! You got your ass kicked in the Trial or what?"</p>

<p>I turned around.</p>

<p>The air split open, and a man in a TRA uniform popped out.</p>

<p>Tall, with a lean, muscular build. His dyed red hair tousled in the wind.</p>

<p>Planting his boots on the ground, he stood with a crooked stance and grinned playfully.</p>

<p>"So you finally crawled out, huh, Captain Mo."</p>

<p>The grin faded from his face.</p>

<p>His eyes hardened as he swept his gaze across the factory grounds. Hundreds of people lay unconscious, scattered everywhere. He understood immediately what it meant.</p>

<p>His voice dropped low as he asked, "Haein. Is this a True Ending?"</p>

<p>I couldn't see her, but Mo Haein seemed to nod in answer.</p>

<p>For a moment, the man stood there, speechless.</p>

<p>I knew who he was.</p>

<p>Captain Kwak Hanmuk. Team leader of TRA Field Team 3. One of the TRA's strongest operatives, alongside Mo Haein.</p>

<p>As expected, Captain Kwak is tall.</p>

<p>I wasn't short myself, but his eye level was way above mine.</p>

<p>While I was observing him curiously, he finally noticed me too. The moment he saw my injuries, he seemed to realize I'd contributed to the clear.</p>

<p>His eyes sharpened — and then Mo Haein muttered quietly.</p>

<p>"Didn't think I'd ever see the day I'd be glad to see you, Hanmuk."</p>

<p>I started to turn at her words, then stopped.</p>

<p>Arms wrapped around my waist from behind.</p>

<p>It felt a bit much for a celebratory back hug commemorating our escape from HapFactory...</p>

<p>"Hanmuk."</p>

<p>Ah.</p>

<p>"Bind."</p>

<p>The moment her short command fell, a long bead rosary shot forward with a rattling sound.</p>

<p>In the blink of an eye, I was bound tight in Kwak Hanmuk's rosary. It wrapped around only me, neatly avoiding Mo Haein — and on top of that, new handcuffs clicked onto my wrists.</p>

<p>Looking like a heinous criminal, I stared at Mo Haein in bewilderment.</p>

<p>"Han Goyo-ssi, I am placing you under emergency arrest on suspicion of being a National Calamity."</p>

<p>She continued rapidly.</p>

<p>"You have no right to remain silent. You may not appoint legal counsel. Everything you say and do from this moment forward may be used against you in court."</p>

<p>A TRA-style Miranda Warning.</p>

<p>She delivered the lines I never thought I'd hear, then finished coldly.</p>

<p>"Knock him out."</p>

<p>Light flashed from the rosary.</p>

<p>I lost consciousness.</p>
