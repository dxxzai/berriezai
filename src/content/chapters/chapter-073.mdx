<h1>Chapter 73</h1>

<p>“Captain Mooooo!”</p>

<p>Mo Haein frowned as she swung her sword at the Lava Golem.</p>

<p>“Captain Kwak. If you've got time to wag your tongue, use your skills.”</p>

<p>Only then did Kwak Hanmuk activate the Paladin skill, Judgment.</p>

<p>Five swords of light materialized above the Lava Golem's head before plunging down in rapid succession.</p>

<p>As the brilliant white blades pierced its black-and-crimson body, the Lava Golem trembled in agony before stomping its foot, causing boulders to rain down.</p>

<p>Mo Haein slipped effortlessly through the falling rocks and repeatedly slashed at the golem's core.</p>

<p>It was the Swordsman's skill, Flurry.</p>

<p>Unable to withstand the assault, the core cracked with a sharp snap before crumbling apart.</p>

<p>The Lava Golem collapsed into a heap of stones. A system window appeared announcing victory, but they were so used to seeing it that neither of them even looked.</p>

<p>Without exaggeration, they'd killed the Flame Lizards and Lava Golems well over a hundred times today.</p>

<p>They'd reached the point where they could do it with their eyes closed.</p>

<p>Mo Haein was already thinking only about quickly picking up the Lava Salt when Kwak Hanmuk started bothering her again.</p>

<p>“Haaaein-ah.”</p>

<p>She sighed and picked up the salt container the Lava Golem had dropped.</p>

<p>“What.”</p>

<p>“Nothing.”</p>

<p>With both hands clasped behind his head, Kwak Hanmuk slowly wandered over.</p>

<p>But she knew full well that whenever he said "nothing," it was never actually nothing.</p>

<p>She tossed him the salt container.</p>

<p>“Hanmuk. Do you believe what the trainee says?”</p>

<p>He snorted.</p>

<p>“Not a chance.”</p>

<p>The candy Han Goyo had shown them was a bound item he'd received after clearing &lt;Happy Smile Factory&gt;.</p>

<p>Maybe it had some incredible hidden function...</p>

<p>But at a glance, it certainly didn't look like something capable of displaying system windows to other Adaptees.</p>

<p>Neither of them had ever even heard of an item like that. More importantly, the golden veil that had appeared over them...</p>

<p>It was exactly the same golden color as Han Goyo's eyes when they'd changed.</p>

<p>If they couldn't connect that radiant golden veil with those dazzling golden eyes then they might as well hand back their Trial Response Agency captain insignias.</p>

<p>Even so, neither of them had questioned him.</p>

<p>Because they believed he had his reasons for staying silent.</p>

<p>'It doesn't seem like he has bad intentions.'</p>

<p>The feelings she'd experienced while enveloped by that golden veil were still vivid.</p>

<p>That wasn't something an item could produce.</p>

<p>Thinking back on that incomprehensible moment, Mo Haein finally spoke.</p>

<p>“Once we clear Sla2Plate... the trainee will probably make his decision.”</p>

<p>She didn't want to put any more pressure on Han Goyo than necessary.</p>

<p>If he chose to remain with the Trial Response Agency of his own accord, she planned to ask him everything then.</p>

<p>But if he didn't...</p><p>As thoughts of Director Jang Seokyun crossed her mind, Kwak Hanmuk interrupted them.</p><p></p>

<p>“No, Captain Mo. Seriously though... you really can't see that slime? I'm losing my mind because I'm the only one who can. It's always stuck to Goyo too.”</p>

<p>“You're serious?”</p>

<p>“Dead serious.”</p>

<p>“If it really isn't an infection... then maybe it's connected to the trainee's ability too?”</p>

<p>“Think so? Since when did Little Chestnut get such an adorable power?”</p>

<p>Just then, a system window appeared.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>The daytime dungeon has ended.
The nighttime dungeon has begun.</p></div>

<p>Mo Haein gestured toward the exit.</p>

<p>“Let's head down.”</p>

<p>Once the daytime dungeon ended, they'd agreed to finish gathering materials and meet in the first-floor lobby.</p>

<p>But even after waiting for quite some time, neither Je Hyun-oh nor Han Goyo showed up.</p>

<p>Just as they were about to go look for them, the teleportation circle finally lit up.</p>

<p>And the two captains were greeted by a thoroughly baffling sight.</p>

<p>Han Goyo was being carried under Je Hyun-oh's arm like a sack of luggage.</p>
<hr class="calibre3"/>
<p>At long last, we returned to the first-floor lobby.</p>

<p>I wanted to celebrate escaping Floor 45, but my current position was rather uncomfortable.</p>

<p>Because Je Hyun-oh had me tucked under one arm.</p>

<p>Maybe I'd overused my abilities, as I'd collapsed halfway to the teleportation circle in a thoroughly undignified heap.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh had kindly carried the trainee who'd exhausted himself helping him around like a sack of flour.</p>

<p>He'd even transformed one of his metal hands into his Crow Glove before picking me up. I was honestly touched enough to cry.</p>

<p>“Trainee!”</p>

<p>The moment Mo Haein saw me, she rushed over in alarm.</p>

<p>It was embarrassing that I was the only one who looked completely battered.</p>

<p>'Je Hyun-oh had literally ripped off his own wings yet somehow looked healthier than I did.'</p>

<p>His clothes were black as a crow's feathers, so you couldn't even see the bloodstains.</p>

<p>Mo Haein immediately turned toward him.</p>

<p>“Lieutenant Colonel Je Hyun-oh.”</p>

<p>She called him in a firm voice.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh answered without hesitation.</p>

<p>“Goyo started messing around first.”</p>

<p>That's not true, Captain Mo! Lieutenant Colonel Je was the one who went crazy first!</p>

<p>Unable to voice my grievance aloud, I quietly said,</p>

<p>“I merely did my best...”</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk wandered over lazily before staring at me in surprise.</p>

<p>“Goyo, what happened to you?”</p>

<p>Then, loud enough for everyone to hear, he whispered,</p>

<p>“Did you go Crazy Chestnut on Lieutenant Colonel Je again?”</p>

<p>...Maybe I had. I probably should've just run away.</p>

<p>After overcoming countless complications, we finally exited the dungeon.</p>

<p>The evening sky was glowing with sunset.</p>

<p>Ella, who had spent the entire time diligently grilling lizard tails while surrounded by mountains of items, spoke in a weary voice.</p>

<p>“It's evening now...
Go home already...”</p>

<p>Then she looked at me, and h er eyes flew wide open in surprise.</p>

<p>“Y-you... Little Lamb! What happened to you?!”</p>

<p>Still tucked under Je Hyun-oh's arm, I didn't forget to look pitiful.</p>

<p>“My abilities weren't sufficient. Even after you kindly opened the way for us... I'm sorry for showing such an embarrassing sight.”</p>

<p>Lowering my eyes as though dejected, I quietly continued,</p>

<p>“But at least the food...
I'll definitely serve you something delicious...”</p>

<p>Both Ella and Ellun frowned sympathetically.</p>

<p>Seeing the compassion in their eyes, I gave them a lonely smile.</p>

<p>The twins exchanged glances, then Ellun flapped over and held something out to me.</p>

<p>It was a High-Grade Recovery Potion.</p>

<p>It was exactly what I needed most right now, so I gratefully accepted it without refusing.</p>

<p>The moment I drank it, my nearly empty HP was completely restored.</p>

<p>Finally freed from Je Hyun-oh's grip, I bowed sincerely to the twins.</p>

<p>“Thank you so much!”</p>

<p>Ella simply huffed and looked away, while Ellun gathered up the grilled lizard tails and handed them to me.</p>

<p>Looking curiously at them, he asked,</p>

<p>“So tomorrow... these become food?”</p>

<p>“Of course, Ellun-nim. Please look forward to it. You too, Ella-nim.”</p>

<p>After saying goodbye, I took out a large cloth and wrapped up the mountain of gathered items.</p>

<p>Then I slung the makeshift sack over my shoulder.</p>

<p>It hadn't been possible in the game, but reality allowed tricks like this.</p>

<p>Each of us shouldered one large sack before leaving the dungeon together.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>6:00 PM</p></div>

<p>The shops in town all had different business hours, but most opened around 9:00 AM and closed at either 5:00 or 6:00 PM.</p>

<p>Originally, I'd planned to sell part of today's loot and buy beds from the furniture shop, but that plan had completely fallen apart.</p>

<p>Tomorrow morning we'd need to buy furniture and everything necessary to open the restaurant.</p>

<p>I glanced around at the group.</p>

<p>Aside from the soft, squishy Pudding Slime, everyone looked exhausted.</p>

<p>'Dinner should be something really good.'</p>

<p>One of the truly evil things about Sla2Plate's village was that, aside from simple snacks, nobody sold actual meals.</p>

<p>If players wanted to satisfy their hunger without entering the dungeon, their only option was wandering around collecting edible plants.</p>

<p>But once winter arrived, every forageable ingredient disappeared. Eventually, everyone was forced into the dungeon.</p>

<p>From what I'd heard, the captains had always finished clearing Sla2Plate before winter arrived, and survived entirely on gathered food.</p>

<p>For a normal clear, simply conquering the dungeon was enough, so it wasn't exactly a bad strategy.</p>

<p>Just not an efficient one.</p>

<p>Foraged food barely restored hunger, and unlike proper meals, provided no buffs.</p>

<p>The moment we arrived at the restaurant, I immediately lit the stove.</p>

<p>As I placed two Flame Lizard tails into the sink, Mo Haein looked uneasy.</p>

<p>“...This is edible, right?”</p>

<p>“When cooked, it's crispy, juicy, chewy, and packed with rich flavor.”</p>

<p>At least, it was in the game.</p>

<p>I kept the last part to myself as I tied on the apron I'd bought yesterday from the general store.</p>

<p>This time Kwak Hanmuk stared at me.</p>

<p>“Goyo...
You're seriously amazing.”</p>

<p>While tying the apron strings, I looked at him in confusion. Since the apron slightly increased cooking success rates thanks to its Luck bonus, I planned to wear it while cooking.</p>

<p>He gazed at me with distant eyes.</p>

<p>“If hyung-nim Roasted Chestnut ever rolled a maid outfit in Clean3... he'd probably just clean without thinking twice...”</p>

<p>I had no idea where that came from. Besides, I'd never roll a maid outfit anyway, so it was a meaningless hypothetical.</p>

<p>Dinner came first.</p>

<p>Ignoring him, I took out today's cooking equipment.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Obsidian Frying Pan: A frying pan crafted from the hardened obsidian shell of a Lava Golem. Prevents burning and cooks every dish to absolute perfection.
(Cooking Success Rate +20%)</p></div></div><br/>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Flame Slime Core: Once ignited, it never goes out. Produces intense heat capable of thoroughly cooking monster meat to its center.</p></div></div>

<p>The jelly-like cores obtained from Flame Slimes were one of the essential ingredients for monster cuisine.</p>

<p>Without them, monster meat simply wouldn't cook properly.</p>

<p>The problem was that they made the stove absurdly hot that cooking with them almost guaranteed burns.</p>

<p>Normally I'd use my Hellfire Oven Mitts...</p>

<p>I looked toward the person responsible for destroying my precious gloves.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh stood by himself against the far wall.</p>

<p>'Wait.'</p>

<p>'They don't have to be oven mitts.'</p>

<p>I confidently walked over and held out my hand.</p>

<p>“Lieutenant Colonel Je. Please lend me the Crow Glove.”</p>

<p>I declared it as though it were already mine.</p>

<p>He stared silently at me.</p>

<p>Even through his goggles, I could feel his gaze.</p>

<p>It didn't seem especially friendly.</p>

<p>After everything I'd done to save him in the dungeon, you'd think he'd at least like me a little more. Instead, it somehow felt like he'd started liking me even less.</p>

<p>'Does he want to kill me again?'</p>

<p>Then again, thinking back to DeZonDeal, maybe not.</p>

<p>He was impossible to understand.</p>

<p>Regardless, I repeated myself.</p>

<p>“My oven mitts were ruined because of your metal wings today. I have nothing to cook with now. At this rate, the captains and I will be eating grass.”</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh took out the Crow Glove.</p>

<p>But instead of handing it over, he dangled it casually from the tip of his metal fingers.</p>

<p>The instant I snatched it away, he pushed himself off the wall.</p>

<p>The captains immediately asked,</p>

<p>“Where are you going?”</p>

<p>“Going to clear the dungeon.
As far as I can.”</p>

<p>From Floor 45 to Floor 65, solo entry was allowed, so he meant he'd clear as much as possible.</p>

<p>Everyone present knew the real reason. He just wanted something to slice apart with his metal hands.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk walked over to open the door for him.</p>

<p>The captains knew nothing about what had happened earlier today.</p>

<p>But I'd seen everything, so I couldn't help worrying.</p>

<p>“Lieutenant Colonel Je...”</p>

<p>I quietly called after him.</p>

<p>He stopped walking.</p>

<p>Slowly, he turned to look at me.</p>

<p>“Goyo.”</p>

<p>“Yes.”</p>

<p>His deep, rough voice murmured lazily.</p>

<p>“You're getting cocky again.”</p>

<p>“......”</p>

<p>If I said even one wrong thing, I had a feeling I'd be sliced apart by his metal hand.</p>

<p>So I obediently backed down.</p>

<p>Come to think of it, today's situation had been an exception. Normally, Je Hyun-oh was highly resistant to mental attacks.</p>

<p>And within Sla2Plate, there wasn't a single monster with stronger mental abilities than Gorgos.</p>

<p>Maybe I didn't need to worry quite so much.</p>

<p>After Je Hyun-oh left for the dungeon, I checked the item's description.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Crow Glove:
What lies hidden beneath the black feathers?</p></div></div>

<p>I had no idea what that meant, but I made a point of remembering it.</p>

<p>It wouldn't be easy, but I wanted to understand Je Hyun-oh better.</p>

<p>The information available in the game simply wasn't enough.</p>

<p>Because while I knew about &lt;Zero&gt;, I didn't know what Je Hyun-oh had experienced inside &lt;Zero&gt;.</p>

<p>I could only assume he'd escaped through abnormal means.</p>

<p>Carefully, I put on the Crow Glove.</p>

<p>For a moment, I worried teeth might suddenly grow inside it and bite my hand off. Fortunately, nothing happened.</p>

<p>It just felt like an ordinary glove. It probably only reacted to Infected beings or provided stat bonuses.</p>

<p>Either way, it's durability was exceptional, so it should withstand the extreme heat with no problem.</p>

<p>Wearing the Crow Glove, I finally began cooking.</p>

<p>The moment I tossed the Flame Slime Core into the stove, the flames roared to life.</p>

<p>I quickly placed the Obsidian Frying Pan over the fire and while it heated up, I hurriedly washed the lizard tails and laid them across the cutting board.</p>

<p>Pulling out my Chinese cleaver, I chopped them into cubes. As my skills activated one after another, system windows flooded my vision.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Skill 'Tenderize' activated.
All meat becomes astonishingly tender.</p></div>

<div class="systembox"><p>Passive Skill 'Golden Hands' activated.
The completed dish gains a random buff.</p></div>

<div class="systembox"><p>Passive Skill 'Absolute Taste' activated.
Great Success rate increased.</p></div>

<div class="systembox"><p>Skill...</p></div>

<p>There were so many notifications I couldn't even read them all.</p>

<p>My level had skyrocketed today.</p>

<p>Tonight's menu: Flame Lizard Tail Chop Steak.</p>

<p>The captains might find the original tail shape unsettling, so I'd dice the meat into familiar chunks and mix it with gathered vegetables.</p>

<p>I poured the meat into the pan.</p>

<p>It sizzled as oil shimmered across the surface. After shaking the pan a couple of times, I grabbed another cutting board and chopped vegetables.</p>

<p>Thanks to my skills, my hands moved faster and more precisely than ever.</p>

<p>Once the vegetables were ready, I added them to the sizzling meat and began stir-frying everything together.</p>

<p>An incredible aroma filled the restaurant.</p>

<p>Then I sprinkled in Lava Salt and Volcanic Ash Spice. The smell became so enticing that even people walking outside would've turned around.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk, who'd fled to the far side of the room the moment I'd started cutting lizard tails...</p>

<p>And Mo Haein, who'd been standing nearby looking rather skeptical.
</p><p>Both found themselves drawn back by the smell.</p>

<p>I brought out the restaurant's nicest plate and served the finished dish.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Great Success!
You created Flame Lizard Tail Chop Steak (★★★).</p></div>

<div class="systembox"><p>Great Success grants additional proficiency and bonus buffs.</p></div>

<div class="systembox"><p>Proficiency greatly increased.
Current Proficiency: 8%</p></div>

<p>In addition to levels, The Chef class also had Proficiency.</p>

<p>The more different dishes you created, the higher it became. The higher the Proficiency, the higher a  dish's star rating.</p>

<p>Mine was still fairly low.</p>

<p>I'd only managed three stars thanks to my high level, strong skills, and premium ingredients.</p>

<p>Since dishes ranged from zero to six stars, three stars was roughly average.</p>

<p>I called over the captains.</p>

<p>“Dinner's ready!”</p>

<p>As I set the enormous plate on the table, Kwak Hanmuk's eyes widened.</p>

<p>“This... is the lizard tail?”</p>

<p>“Yes. Give it a try.”</p>

<p>As I served everyone onto individual plates, something soft pressed against my cheek.</p>

<p>“I'm fine. Please eat, Roasted Chestnut.”</p>

<p>I already knew Dominic didn't eat. Still, I nodded slightly as though I were taking his feelings into consideration.</p>

<p>The captains thanked me and began eating, then fell silent.</p>

<p>It was because they were too busy eating.</p>

<p>I took a bite myself, thankfully, itt was good. I'd been a little nervous since it had been a while since I'd cooked.</p>

<p>The outside was crispy, the inside remained delightfully chewy, and every bite released rich juices bursting with umami.</p>

<p>The vegetables retained a satisfying crunch andThe Lava Salt and Volcanic Ash Spice were perfectly balanced.</p>

<p>It was amazing that the flavor descriptions I'd only ever read as text in the game could actually be experienced exactly the same way in reality.</p>

<p>“It looks delicious. I'm looking forward to the dish you'll prepare for me as well, Roasted Chestnut.”</p>

<p>...</p>

<p>Dominic's praise nearly made me choke.</p>

<p>By the time we'd finished every last bite, a system window had appeared.</p>

<p>Mo Haein, who'd polished off even the final morsel, quietly read the notification.</p>

<p>“What is this?
A buff...?”</p>

<p>“It's only a three-star dish. Tomorrow I should be able to make four-star meals. Probably four buffs... around +30% Critical Rate and +150% Critical Damage.”</p>

<p>'I'll just make five hundred lizard skewers tonight before going to bed.'</p>

<p>Once my Proficiency increased, producing consistent four-star dishes tomorrow would be easy.</p>

<p>But, neither captain said anything.</p>

<p>I'd been secretly feeling rather proud. Suddenly feeling awkward, I quickly added,</p>

<p>“Um... if I work a little harder, I can probably get five buffs—”</p>

<p>Grab.</p>

<p>Without warning, Kwak Hanmuk seized my hand. Beside him, Mo Haein stared at me with burning intensity.</p>

<p>With complete sincerity, he declared,</p>

<p>“Crazy Chestnut... hyung-nim Goyo. Starting tomorrow, I'll bring back a thousand lizards every day.”</p>

<p>Just as Kwak Hanmuk was making his Lizard Oath—</p>

<p>BANG! BANG! BANG!</p>

<p>Someone pounded frantically on the restaurant door.</p>

<p>“Little Lamb! Open the door!
Immediately!”</p>

<p>It was Ella, one of the Fire Dragon twins.</p>
