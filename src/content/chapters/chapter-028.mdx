<h1>Chapter 28</h1>

<p>For a moment, I thought I'd misheard. Yi Gaon's tearful voice continued.</p>
<br/>
<em><p>"Sniff... Roasted Chestnut... huuuh... I'll wait for you in the lab..."</p></em>
<br/>
<p>The broadcast ended.</p>

<p>She'd been pretty calm for someone who'd just killed someone, but still — it was surprising.</p>

<p>What the hell happened up there?</p>

<p>I couldn't know until I checked, but I was dying of curiosity.</p>

<p>Tak Jucheol or Paeng Sangdo?</p>

<p>Paeng Sangdo was the talkative type — maybe Yi Gaon snapped and killed him in a fit of rage.</p>

<p>But Tak Jucheol had a way of running his mouth too, and he was no less irritating. Plenty of reason to kill him.</p>

<p>Wondering which one it was, I quietly shifted my eyes to check on Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein.</p>

<p>Mo Haein showed no reaction to the broadcast. Kwak Hanmuk, still pinning the Chef Zombie under his boot, let out an impressed whistle.</p>

<p>"Didn't think the researcher had it in her. Not bad."</p>

<p>The Chef Zombie, pinned under his boot, was still squirming even though the boss fight was over.</p>

<p>It was trying to pick up the scattered Goblin Stone pieces on the floor.</p>

<p>Blood-slicked fingers scraped against the steel floor.</p>

<p>Its body had reverted to normal after the boss fight ended, but if it kept using its hands like that, it'd really mess them up.</p>

<p>I quickly unhooked my carabiner and ran over to the Chef Zombie.</p>

<p>With Kwak Hanmuk's help, we tied the Chef Zombie up tight with climbing rope and secured it to a steel shelf in the Generator Room.</p>

<p>"You sure it won't break the rope?"</p>

<p>"I'm setting up a simple additional measure."</p>

<p>I climbed onto the steel shelf and wound a thread around a pipe on the ceiling.</p>

<p>At the dangling end of the thread, I tied a Goblin Stone.</p>

<p>The Goblin Stone trembled from the vibration of the generator engine.</p>

<p>The Chef Zombie couldn't take its eyes off the swaying stone.</p>

<p>"Even if it breaks the rope and climbs onto the shelf, its leg is injured, so it won't be able to reach the Goblin Stone."</p>

<p>It'd be too fixated on the stone just out of reach to cause any trouble.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk looked dissatisfied with my measure, but maybe because of the True Ending connection, he didn't say anything more.</p>

<p>He peeled off his Work Gloves, soaked in generator oil and blood, and stretched with a long, groaning noise.</p>

<p>"Man, I'm dead tired. Soon as we get back to the lab, I'm fucking demolishing some chocolate bars."</p>

<p>Then he cackled — "Fucking demolish some chocolate bars!" — and threw punches at the air.</p>

<p>Mo Haein made no effort to hide her scowl.</p>

<p>Watching them both, I asked carefully.</p>

<p>"Um... aren't you going to arrest me? Or is that after we clear this?"</p>

<p>I asked in advance to mentally prepare, then added my own excuse.</p>

<p>"I don't know why my eyes turned like that either. And the zombie virus infection somehow healed, but I don't know the reason for that eith—"</p>

<p>"You forgot?"</p>

<p>Mo Haein cut me off.</p>

<p>"That's why you're a Trainee."</p>

<p>"...Ah."</p>

<p>I've already been arrested!</p>

<p>All the worry I'd been carrying evaporated clean away.</p>

<p>Either way, it was already confirmed that I was some kind of weird existence. Right now, TRA was testing whether I could close three Trials within the month I'd promised.</p>

<p>Whether my eyes started glowing or not, what mattered to TRA was whether I could actually close the Trials.</p>

<p>Relieved, I gave a small smile and thanked her.</p>

<p>"Thank you."</p>

<p>"Nothing to thank me for."</p>

<p>Mo Haein drew a firm line, then nodded toward the emergency generator humming loudly nearby.</p>

<p>"Why did the generator suddenly do that?"</p>

<p>"I don't know."</p>

<p>Mo Haein stared at me.</p>

<p>Her look seemed to say, So there ARE things you don't know.</p>

<p>I don't know a lot of things, actually...</p>

<p>I knew every part of the game I'd played through, but nothing outside of it.</p>

<p>No — I couldn't know.</p>

<p>The sudden breakdown of the emergency generator was something I found just as puzzling.</p>

<p>The golden flash in my eyes — if I said it was related to me becoming the System, that would roughly make sense.</p>

<p>The System window had been rambling about something like that too.</p>

<p>If I'm the System, it'd be weirder if nothing happened at all.</p>

<p>But the emergency generator — no matter how I thought about it, I couldn't even find a clue.</p>

<p>The Chef Zombie wasn't that strong before it went berserk.</p>

<p>Without outside interference, there was no way the emergency generator could have suddenly broken down like that.</p>

<p>I'd even wondered at first if Je Hyun-oh had come to the lab and rammed the generator instead of delivering the items he was supposed to.</p>

<p>My only guess for now was that maybe my interference with the True Ending had caused it.</p>

<p>Not sure about that either.</p>

<p>Right now, my usefulness to TRA was determined by my knowledge of the Trials.</p>

<p>If things kept going off-script like this, I'd be in trouble.</p>

<p>If possible, I wanted to find the cause.</p>

<p>"I'm soooooo hungry... Let's just go already..."</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk's gloomy muttering snapped me out of my thoughts, and I got moving.</p>

<p>The way back to the lab was the complete opposite of before.</p>

<p>After defeating the Chef Zombie, part of the underground area gets sealed off, but the stairs become usable.</p>

<p>The emergency stairs — blocked when we first entered the underground area — were now open, but no one was surprised.</p>

<p>It was a Trial that meticulously mimicked reality, but in the end, it was still a virtual space.</p>

<p>A place that followed the rules of the game. Even DeZonDeal, for all its emphasis on realism, couldn't avoid having unrealistic elements.</p>

<p>I picked up a couple of food items on the way and handed them to Kwak Hanmuk.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk, grimy with grease stains, tore open the plastic wrapper of a chocolate pie with his teeth — no hands — and downed it in one bite.</p>

<p>He chewed as he beat back the zombies rushing at us.</p>

<p>This was my fault for turning back human.</p>

<p>Feeling apologetic, I promised myself I'd find him even better snacks.</p>

<p>When we reached the 6th floor, freshly spawned zombies greeted us.</p>

<p>While Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein dealt with them, I ran to the lab door and knocked.</p>

<p>"Yi Gaon! Yi—"</p>

<p>The door opened before I could call her name twice.</p>

<p>"Roasted Chestnut!"</p>

<p>Yi Gaon pulled me inside.</p>

<p>Exhausted from fighting the Chef Zombie, I let myself be dragged in — and then she threw her arms around me.</p>

<p>"Roasted Chestnut, Roasted Chestnut...! I knew you were alive!"</p>

<p>I was startled by the sudden hug, but after a moment, I awkwardly patted her on the back.</p>

<p>The spot where she pressed her face against my chest quickly grew damp.</p>

<p>Yi Gaon, who'd been sobbing for a long while, finally calmed down and lifted her head.</p>

<p>I carefully pulled away from her and asked.</p>

<p>"Are you hurt anywhere?"</p>

<p>"No. Just a little bruised."</p>

<p>Yi Gaon showed me her arm — a bruise so dark it was practically black.</p>

<p>In the normal world, it would've been shocking. But in the world of DeZonDeal, surviving at the cost of a bruise like that was practically a steal.</p>

<p>I let out a relieved breath and scanned the inside of the lab.</p>

<p>I was looking for Tak Jucheol and Paeng Sangdo, but they weren't in sight.</p>

<p>"I'm glad. Can you tell me what happened?"</p>

<p>"Well... it's like this."</p>

<p>Yi Gaon told me about what happened while I was in the underground area.</p>

<p>Not long after we left, an announcement came over the speakers saying the lab was being locked down.</p>

<p>Tak Jucheol and Paeng Sangdo, who'd been cooperating with the vaccine production, completely lost it the moment the broadcast came on.</p>

<p>They started screaming that we had to escape immediately and causing a ruckus — but it was pointless.</p>

<p>Because the noisy broadcast had woken up all the sleeping zombies, and they were tearing the place apart.</p>

<p>The 6th floor, which we'd cleared out, was now swarming with zombies again. There was no way researchers with pathetic physical abilities could escape.</p>

<p>Tak Jucheol and Paeng Sangdo soon realized that too.</p>
<br/>
<p><span class="flashback">"We're all dead! WE'RE ALL DEAD!!"</span></p>
<br/>
<p>As Tak Jucheol wailed in despair, Paeng Sangdo spoke very calmly.</p>
<br/>
<p><span class="flashback">"Not... quite."</span></p>
<br/>
<p>Tak Jucheol, who'd survived this long thanks to Paeng Sangdo's scheming, looked at him with hopeful eyes.</p>

<p>Paeng Sangdo extended his index finger and pointed at Yi Gaon.</p>

<p>Both Tak Jucheol and Yi Gaon understood the meaning of that pointed finger instantly.</p>

<p>Use Yi Gaon as bait to escape.</p>

<p>Just like they did with Yi Seon.</p>
<br/>
<p><span class="flashback">"...Ah, that's right. We've got our Gaon."</span></p>
<br/>
<p>Tak Jucheol grinned.</p>
<br/>
<p><span class="flashback">"Gaon. I'm counting on you. You're the only one left now. We'll make sure the vaccine gets made, so..."</span></p>
<br />
<p>"My brother wasn't enough? You want to turn me into a zombie too?"</p>

<p>"Now now, that's not what I meant."</p>

<p>"Then what DID you mean?! You're telling me to go out there and die!"</p>

<p>"Where do you get off talking back like that?!"</p><br/>

<p>Tak Jucheol shouted and jabbed a finger at her — and at the same time, Paeng Sangdo swung his fist.</p>

<p>"I got this bruise blocking that punch."</p>

<p>"That's..."</p>

<p>I had no idea what they thought they'd accomplish by swinging at Yi Gaon of all people.</p>

<p>"So I got so pissed off that I shot him."</p>

<p>"...Huh?"</p>

<p>"Ah, the Zombie Captain gave me a gun before she left. The female Captain who was the first to turn into a zombie."</p>

<p>That wasn't the part I was surprised by...</p>

<p>Misunderstanding my reaction, Yi Gaon blinked.</p>

<p>Her expression seemed to ask what else she needed to explain.</p>

<p>I made a mental note to be nicer to Yi Gaon from now on, then sat up straight and listened carefully to what came next.</p>

<p>Long story short: Paeng Sangdo, shot, ran out of the lab blubbering, and Tak Jucheol followed after him.</p>

<p>"I never knew a person could bleed that much. He's definitely dead..."</p>

<p>She'd pulled the trigger, but witnessing the aftermath with her own eyes had clearly shaken her.</p>

<p>Left alone, Yi Gaon trembled — then, waiting for the zombies to chase after Paeng Sangdo and Tak Jucheol, she went down to the broadcast room on the lower floor and called for me.</p>

<p>"Will you be okay without an assistant researcher?"</p>

<p>If necessary, I could track one down and drag them back.</p>

<p>But Yi Gaon shouted a refusal immediately.</p>

<p>"I don't need them!"</p>

<p>She grabbed my collar and spoke urgently.</p>

<p>"Just stay here where it's safe. I can handle everything myself."</p>

<p>Her eyes, trembling with anxiety, were desperate.</p>

<p>I lied for Yi Gaon's sake.</p>

<p>"Yeah. There won't be any more danger."</p>

<p>There was still one more thing left, but knowing in advance would only make her anxious — so I told a white lie.</p>

<p>Visibly relieved, Yi Gaon finally managed a small smile.</p>

<p>"But..."</p>

<p>She looked me over, then murmured as if something had just dawned on her.</p>

<p>"Roasted Chestnut... you got bitten by a zombie, didn't you...?"</p>

<p>Yi Gaon kept examining me.</p>

<p>She kept checking, confirming that there were no visible signs of zombification, so I decided to be honest.</p>

<p>"You might not believe this, but the infection healed."</p>

<p>The moment she heard me, she froze.</p>

<p>Her eyes and mouth fell open as she stared at me, dazed.</p>

<p>Hard to believe, I know.</p>

<p>She probably thought I'd lost my mind and was talking nonsense.</p>

<p>Whatever reaction she had, I figured I'd understand — but suddenly, Yi Gaon grabbed my arm.</p>

<p>Then, as if struck by lightning, she called out my name.</p>

<p>"Roasted Chestnut!"</p>

<p>The grip on my forearm was surprisingly strong.</p>

<p>I was startled by her sudden movement — but her next words caught me even more off guard.</p>

<p>"I'm going to take some blood."</p>

<p>Her eyes gleamed.</p>

<p>"Right now."</p>
