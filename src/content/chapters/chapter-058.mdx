<h1>Chapter 58</h1>

<p>A chilling silence descended.</p>

<p>It was the kind of silence where it felt as though I could hear the sound of the candles burning.</p>

<p>It took a moment to realize that every person in the underground chamber had stopped even breathing at the same time.</p>

<p>I swallowed dryly.</p>

<p>The decisive reason I became convinced that I had summoned him was the butlers of Clean3.</p>

<p>‘Dominic, just like the butlers, was connected to my past gameplay.’</p>

<p>However, I had failed to clear CallOVo.</p>

<p>The god I had summoned had been left behind inside an unfinished game, and after losing his memories, he became trapped within Doctor Dominic.</p>

<p>‘Although it’s possible the game broke because I summoned an existence like Dominic.’</p>

<p>Regardless, one thing was certain.</p>

<p>I had fulfilled the conditions for the true ending.</p>

<p>Because the player, as the cult leader, had summoned the God of the Void.</p>

<p>The fact that the choice window had been unable to force my actions and instead caused an error proved it.</p>

<p>But…</p>

<p>The system window announcing the ending had still not appeared.</p>

<p>Dominic also continued to exist.</p>

<p>He silently <em>stared at me</em>.</p>

<p>His gaze felt as if it were digging through my very essence.</p>

<p>My body naturally shrank back at the piercing sensation.</p>

<p>An instinctive fear dominated me.</p>

<p>‘I’ll die.’</p>

<p>I had the intuition that my existence would be torn apart completely by Dominic.</p>

<p>Strange thoughts kept appearing in my mind.</p>
<br/>
<em><p>But dying before His eyes is infinite happiness! Happiness happiness happiness happiness happiness happiness happiness happiness happiness happiness ahhh glory to Him!</p></em>
<br/>
<p>They were not thoughts I wanted to have.</p>

<p>The longer I remained locked in Dominic’s gaze,</p>

<p>the more I continued to think.</p>

<p>Something.</p>

<p>“…!”</p>

<p>The moment his gaze fell away, I immediately bent forward.</p>

<p>Unable to hold it back, I vomited, but only saliva and stomach acid came out.</p>

<p>Several red drops fell onto the floor.</p>

<p>I wiped beneath my nose with the back of my hand and raised my head.</p>

<p>Dominic stood with his arms crossed, looking down.</p>

<p>Those blue eyes, positioned impossibly high above me, were cold.</p>

<p>“It’s a boring joke.”</p>

<p>He was giving me an opportunity.</p>

<p>A chance to retract my statement and apologize.</p>

<p>Like teaching a child, Dominic spoke slowly once more.</p>

<p>“It was a joke, wasn’t it?”</p>

<p>But I could not cling to the mercy he offered.</p>

<p>I sincerely apologized to him.</p>

<p>“I’m sorry, Dominic-ssi.”</p>

<p>I truly felt sorry toward Dominic.</p>

<p>I did not know how I had brought an existence like Dominic into the game, but I felt even more guilty because I had no intention of helping him recover the memories he had lost.</p>

<p>Even from now on, Dominic would not easily regain his memories.</p>

<p>Before revealing the truth, I had become certain after hearing what Dominic said.</p>
<br/>
<em><p><span class="flashback">“Would I really regain my existence because of a few words spoken by those insignificant things?”</span></p></em>
<br/>
<p>No matter what I said, he would not recover all of his memories.</p>

<p>Dominic had to find his own existence.</p>

<p>Of course, hearing my words might restore fragments of his memories.</p>

<p>But if I wanted to clear the true ending and leave, this was a risk I had to accept.</p>

<p>After finishing my own calculations, I had told Dominic the truth.</p>

<p>That was why I…</p>

<p>Was prepared to pay the price.</p>

<p>“……”</p>

<p>When I refused to give him the answer he wanted until the very end, Dominic’s expression became blank.</p>

<p>A system window appeared before me.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Your companion relationship with Doctor Dominic has been dissolved!</p></div>

<p>He did not ask me anything else or say another word.</p>

<p>He simply expressed his emotions through his actions.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>You have died.</p></div>

<div class="systembox"><p>You revive before Doctor Dominic.</p></div>

<p>“Gasp…!”</p>

<p>The instant death and revival caused my vision to spin.</p>

<p>My stomach churned, and I felt like I would vomit at any moment.</p>

<p>There was nothing in my empty stomach, but the unbearable urge remained.</p>

<p>However, I could not even steady my breathing.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>You have died.</p></div>

<div class="systembox">
<p>
You revive before Doctor Dominic.
</p>
</div>
<p>“Haah… ugh….”</p>

<p>A groan escaped my mouth.</p>

<p>This was my first time experiencing death and revival repeatedly, and it was considerably painful.</p>

<p>Was it not simply something done through the system?</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>You have died.</p></div>

<div class="systembox"><p>You revive before Doctor Dominic.</p></div>

<p>“……”</p>

<p>A sensation I had never felt before crushed my entire body.</p>

<p>I felt that this action could not continue being repeated.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>You have died.</p></div>

<div class="systembox"><p>You revive before Doctor Dominic.</p></div>

<p>“……”</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>You have died.</p></div>

<div class="systembox"><p>You revive before Doctor Dominic.</p></div>

<p>“……”</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>You have died.</p></div>

<div class="systembox">
<p>
You revive before Doctor Dominic.
</p>
</div>
<p>“……”</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>You have died.</p></div>

<div class="systembox"><p>You revive before Doctor Dominic.</p></div>

<p>“……”</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>You have died.</p></div>

<div class="systembox"><p>You revive before Doctor Dominic.</p></div>

<p>“…….”</p>

<p>“Stop!”</p>

<p>A cry close to a scream rang out.</p>

<p>It was a desperate voice, clear enough to pierce through my muffled hearing.</p>

<p>Dominic stopped the action he had been repeating indifferently.</p>

<p>He lowered his gaze toward where the voice came from.</p>

<p>I was lying face-down on the ground, unable to move.</p>

<p>‘Am I… alive…?’</p>

<p>My body trembled with relief at surviving.</p>

<p>The aftermath of repeatedly dying and reviving in such a short period of time was horrific.</p>

<p>It was a sensation that could not be described simply as pain.</p>

<p>My outer skin was completely unharmed.</p>

<p>But deep inside, it felt as though something had been damaged.</p>

<p>It might be an exaggerated comparison, but it felt as if my soul itself had been torn apart.</p>

<p>It was a pain I never wanted to experience again.</p>

<p>If Dominic asked me once more whether I had been joking, I might have answered yes.</p>

<p>I forced myself to raise my gaze.</p>

<p>‘That was definitely Edel’s voice….’</p>

<p>Dominic was already in a very bad mood.</p>

<p>There was nothing good about getting caught up in this.</p>

<p>My vision was blurry, and I had to blink several times before I could see properly.</p>

<p>Edel was hanging below Dominic.</p>

<p>She had stabbed him with an obsidian dagger, but no blood flowed from Dominic.</p>

<p>The dagger almost looked as if it had simply been absorbed into Dominic’s body.</p>

<p>Edel’s eyes widened as she gasped for breath.</p>

<p>She seemed unable to control herself because of fear over what she had done.</p>

<p>Yet Edel did not close her mouth.</p>

<p>Even while cold sweat poured from her face under Dominic’s blue gaze, she spoke.</p>

<p>“Please forgive me, Doctor.”</p>

<p>Almost completely out of her mind, she begged for my life.</p>

<p>“If you would show mercy to the cult leader just once….”</p>

<p>It was an unexpected situation.</p>

<p>Edel was an apostle who worshiped Dominic.</p>

<p>Even if I was the cult leader, there was no reason for her to protect the target of her god’s anger.</p>

<p>The shallow companion relationship created by the system was not enough of a reason either.</p>

<p>And yet she attacked Dominic for my sake.</p>

<p>Edel lowered her head.</p>

<p>She had gathered her courage, but she could no longer look at Dominic because of her fear.</p>

<p>The woman who had been trembling so badly her teeth had been chattering suddenly turned her head.</p>

<p>Her eyes met mine.</p>

<p>I wanted to tell Edel thank you, but no voice came out.</p>

<p>So instead, I blinked several times.</p>

<p>Edel’s face suddenly crumpled.</p>

<p>The area around her eyes turned as red as her irises, and tears began falling.</p>

<p>The emotions in her gaze toward me were mixed together without order.</p>

<p>Resentment, worry, and fear.</p>

<p>It was as if she believed something terrible would happen to me at any moment.</p>

<p>Watching her cry silently, I could only blink helplessly.</p>

<p>‘At this point, I’m still doing fairly well.’</p>

<p>From the moment I told Dominic I had something last to say, I had prepared myself for something bad to happen.</p>

<p>I had prepared myself for something far more cruel, so compared to that, this was actually rather mild.</p>

<p>My heart had not been consumed, so I still had one extra life remaining.</p>

<p>Since I could not explain this and comfort Edel, all I could do was watch her cry.</p>

<p>Edel, struggling desperately to hold back her sobs, moved her lips.</p>

<p>“Roasted Chestnut-nim….”</p>

<p>I stared blankly at Edel.</p>

<p>But she seemed unaware of what she had just said.</p>

<p>She only continued looking at me and crying.</p>

<p>Then a calm voice filled with weight called my name.</p>

<p>“Roasted Chestnut.”</p>

<p>It felt as if ice water had been poured over my head.</p>

<p>I snapped back to awareness.</p>

<p>I spat out the blood pooled inside my mouth onto the floor.</p>

<p>Then I tried to stand, but my arms and legs had no strength left.</p>

<p>I wondered if this was what it felt like to have your limbs severed after failing an epic quest.</p>

<p>Perhaps impressed by my struggling attempt to move on the dirty floor, Dominic grabbed the back of my neck and lifted me up.</p>

<p>He placed me back onto the altar.</p>

<p>My body tried to fall forward, but Dominic lightly supported me with one hand.</p>

<p>“Are you all right?”</p>

<p>It was a question that almost made me laugh, but I did not even have enough strength to move the corners of my mouth.</p>

<p>I hung limply, gathered whatever strength I had left, and barely moved my tongue.</p>

<p>“Dominic-ssi….”</p>

<p>My voice sounded unfamiliar, as if it belonged to someone else.</p>

<p>“Has your… anger calmed… a little….”</p>

<p>I stopped speaking and clenched my teeth.</p>

<p>The pain made my hair stand on end.</p>

<p>Dominic looked down at me suffering and answered.</p>

<p>“I have cooled down enough to think clearly.”</p>

<p>“……”</p>

<p>“So, Roasted Chestnut. What will you do now?”</p>

<p>“……”</p>

<p>“I no longer see meaning in holding you accountable. What has happened has already happened. What I want is to find myself again.”</p>

<p>“……”</p>

<p>“Will Roasted Chestnut help me recover my memories?”</p>

<p>“……”</p>

<p>“If you cannot, then you should never have told me the truth from the beginning. I can make Roasted Chestnut, or those who matter to Roasted Chestnut, suffer.”</p>

<p>“……”</p>

<p>“I could simply keep Roasted Chestnut here forever. At least I would not be bored.”</p>

<p>“…Let’s.”</p>

<p>I forced myself to gather my strength.</p>

<p>And I spoke the final condition for the true ending.</p>

<p>“Let’s make a contract, Dominic-ssi.”</p>

<p>For the first time, Dominic, who had calmly continued speaking by himself until now, fell silent.</p>

<p>I could feel his gaze observing me.</p>

<p>It was so clear it felt as if it had physical form, making my skin ache.</p>

<p>I leaned my head back, my face damp with sweat and tears.</p>

<p>Looking into Dominic’s eyes, I said,</p>

<p>“And let’s go outside together.”</p>

<p>After some time passed, Dominic asked again.</p>

<p>“…Wasn’t your intention to keep me trapped here?”</p>

<p>So he knew.</p>

<p>When had he realized?</p>

<p>The feeling of having everything exposed made me let out a small laugh.</p>

<p>I immediately groaned in pain after laughing, though.</p>

<p>“That was the original plan, but now…. I want to leave with you, Dominic-ssi.”</p>

<p>At first, I truly intended to keep Dominic trapped inside CallOVo with everything I had.</p>

<p>But after realizing that I was the one who summoned him, my thoughts changed slightly.</p>

<p>‘Because I summoned him.’</p>

<p>It was an unintended accident, but I felt responsible.</p>

<p>With Dominic’s power, sooner or later, he would eventually leave CallOVo.</p>

<p>Since it would happen someday anyway, it would be better to keep him beside me from now on, observe him, and think of a solution.</p>

<p>Toward a being who might become the greatest disaster imaginable, I offered the best promise I could.</p>

<p>“As your companion, I will stay with you until you recover your memories, Dominic-ssi.”</p>
