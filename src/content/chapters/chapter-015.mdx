<h1>Chapter 15</h1>

<p>The system window, which had been glitching out like it was broken, finally displayed a normal message.</p>

<p>It felt like smacking a busted TV and having it suddenly work again.</p>

<p>But this wasn't the time to sit back and relax just because the system window was functioning properly.</p>

<p>'Why is Je Hyun-oh here?'</p>

<p>The Je Hyun-oh I knew should have been slaughtering zombies somewhere between the Yellow Zone at minimum and the Dead Zone at maximum.</p>

<p>He had absolutely no business setting foot in a peaceful Green Zone like this.</p>

<p>I stared at Je Hyun-oh, every nerve on edge.</p>

<p>He was dressed head to toe in pitch black.</p>

<p>Form-fitting combat gear and a hoodie pulled low.</p>

<p>His face was covered by goggles and a gas mask.</p>

<p>Standing beneath the star-filled night sky, Je Hyun-oh was looking up at the heavens.</p>

<p>His getup covered him so completely I couldn't read his eyes, let alone his expression, but looking at his profile, a strange thought crossed my mind.</p>

<p>He seemed like he was waiting for something.</p>

<p>For a very long time….</p>

<p>'Waiting for a meteor shower or something?'</p>

<p>The thought that popped into my head was so absurd I almost laughed at myself.</p>

<p>Here I was, right in front of Je Hyun-oh, and I was having these pointless thoughts. Clearly, I still wasn't tense enough.</p>

<p>A system window abruptly appeared, blocking my vision.</p>

<div class="systembox">

</p>
</div>

<p>'If it's going to be like that, there doesn't seem to be much point in sending the message.'</p>

<p>And no matter how much of a fuss it made, I had absolutely no intention of obediently registering him.</p>

<p>If anything, it just made me more resistant to the idea.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Would you like to register 'Je Hyun-oh'?</p></div>

<p>As I dismissed the system windows that kept popping up and blocking my view, a sudden chill ran down my spine.</p>

<p>Our eyes met.</p>

<p>Actually, I couldn't say for sure if 'met' was the right word.</p>

<p>His goggles covered his eyes, so I couldn't even be certain he was looking at me.</p>

<p>To put it more precisely, it was more like 'I had the distinct feeling our eyes had met.'</p>

<p>An LED lit up on the sleek black goggles.</p>

<div class="hyunoh"><p>^ ^</p></div>

<p>A smiling expression was drawn where his eyes would be.</p>

<p>Screech.</p>

<p>An ominous sound came from the radio tower beneath his boots. A creepy sensation crawled down my spine.</p>

<p>And I….</p>

<p>Ran.</p>

<p>Like a madman, with every ounce of strength I had.</p>

<p>I practically stomped my way down the emergency stairs. I nearly fell several times, but I didn't slow down.</p>

<p>If I tumbled down the stairs, I might at least have a chance of surviving, but if Je Hyun-oh caught me, I was dead on the spot.</p>

<p>But before long, there was no more need to run down the emergency stairs.</p>

<p>A black line was drawn from the wall to the floor.</p>

<p>Seeing the line that split the floor number sign exactly in half, I barely managed to swallow the curse that rose to my throat.</p>

<p>The black line slowly widened.</p>

<p>The building was being torn in two.</p>

<p>The internal steel frame ripped apart, letting out a horrific, screaming-like rupture.</p>

<p>Along with shards of glass windows pouring down like a waterfall, I was mixed in with all sorts of debris and fell.</p>

<p>DeZonDeal didn't have extra lives like HapFactory. Death by anything other than zombification was final.</p>

<p>But I absolutely did not want to die like this.</p>

<p>Dozens of thoughts flashed through my head and then faded away.</p>

<p>Just as I thought my eyes felt hot, something wrapped around my waist. A thunderous shout fell upon me.</p>

<p>"HAN GOYO!!"</p>

<p>The Zelkova wood Bead Rosary yanked me toward it. I barely managed to escape from the debris.</p>

<p>My view cleared, and I took in the surrounding situation at a glance.</p>

<p>The broadcasting station building, split vertically and collapsing to either side.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh, with a sharp crescent moon slung across his back.</p>

<p>When I lowered my gaze, I saw Kwak Hanmuk riding a spherical motorcycle.</p>

<p>I could tell all too well from his expression just how fucked this situation was.</p>

<p>Of course, I could have known that even without seeing Kwak Hanmuk's face.</p>

<p>CRUMBLE!</p>

<p>The building completely collapsed. The ground rumbled as thick dust and concrete particles shot up, obscuring everything.</p>

<p>In the meantime, the Bead Rosary was recalled to its owner, and I was able to land safely in front of Kwak Hanmuk.</p>

<p>"Shit! Goyo hyung-nim, I thought you were a goner."</p>

<p>He welcomed me with a fierce curse.</p>

<p>I was exactly that happy to see him too, so I didn't bother to correct him for calling me 'hyung-nim'.</p>

<p>The mood was almost one of a tearful embrace, but Kwak Hanmuk's face suddenly hardened.</p>

<p>"Goyo, your eyes are…."</p>

<p>"Huh?"</p>

<p>"…Never mind. Let's get the hell out of here first."</p>

<p>I quickly hopped onto the back seat of his motorcycle.</p>

<p>But Kwak Hanmuk didn't take off right away. He narrowed his eyes and glared into the gradually settling dust.</p>

<p>A unique idling exhaust note, similar to the sound of horse hooves, rumbled from the motorcycle.</p>

<p>Step. Step. Step.</p>

<p>Slow footsteps echoed from within the dust and the exhaust noise.</p>

<p>"Lieutenant Colonel Je Hyun-oh."</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk called out to him, his voice full of wariness, like he was dealing with a wild beast.</p>

<p>There was no reply. Instead, a series of sounds like clashing metal rang out.</p>

<p>Then, Je Hyun-oh emerged from the dust cloud.</p>

<p>His black arm was fully exposed in the moonlight.</p>

<p>It was an arm mutated by the BT-Z Virus infection, composed of a metal that didn't exist on Earth.</p>

<p>Where his fingers should have been, there were metal claws like those of a beast.</p>

<p>With both arms hanging loosely at his sides, Je Hyun-oh stopped walking and silently stared our way.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk spoke, ready to bolt at any moment.</p>

<p>"Lieutenant Colonel, what the hell is all this about? You've wrecked the building, so I'd say it's about time you came back to your senses."</p>

<p>When he politely cussed him out, a light flickered on Je Hyun-oh's goggles.</p>

<div class="hyunoh"><p>ㅠ ㅠ</p></div>

<p>Displaying a crying emoticon, Je Hyun-oh raised his hand. His fingertip pointed unwaveringly at something.</p>

<p>It was me.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk let out a hollow laugh.</p>

<p>"For a newbie initiation, Goyo is…."</p>

<p>The exhaust roared as the motorcycle shot forward.</p>

<p>"Still a Sweet Chestnut!"</p>

<p>What did Sweet Chestnut have to do with a newbie initiation?</p>

<p>I couldn't see the connection, but either way, Kwak Hanmuk and I were instantly far away from the building's wreckage.</p>

<p>But no one felt relieved.</p>

<p>"Goyo, did you mess with the Lieutenant Colonel or something?"</p>

<p>"There's no way…."</p>

<p>I told him everything I had done since encountering Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>Actually, there wasn't much to tell.</p>

<p>'Because I ran the moment I saw him.'</p>

<p>Knowing full well how dangerous he was, I had run faster than anyone. And yet, Je Hyun-oh had designated me as his target.</p>

<p>"Could the infection have spread to his brain?"</p>

<p>"He's in his right mind."</p>

<p>"What?"</p>

<p>"The Lieutenant Colonel's goggles lit up, didn't they? He's not crazy. He's just doing this because he feels like wrecking shit right now."</p>

<p>He's a goddamn lunatic who's perfectly sane, Kwak Hanmuk cursed.</p>

<p>'So the lights on the goggles mean he's still rational.'</p>

<p>I filed away that useful bit of info in my head.</p>

<p>"It's not like it's the first time he's attacked Adaptees, but this is the first time he's caused this much of a scene."</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk muttered that Je Hyun-oh had been acting strange ever since he was stuck alone in DeZonDeal for ten days.</p>

<p>"Shit."</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk suddenly spat out a curse.</p>

<p>Every zombie in the Green Zone was gathering and chasing after the roaring motorcycle.</p>

<p>The tremendous sound of the building's collapse had blanketed the entire Green Zone.</p>

<p>The zombies of DeZonDeal, whose sight and smell had atrophied, reacting only to sound, were all swarming toward it.</p>

<p>It was night, so the zombies' physical abilities were enhanced, but they weren't Dead Zombies. It was enough to outrun them on a motorcycle.</p>

<p>The problem was the bridge.</p>

<p>If we crossed the river via the main bridge, we'd be in the Yellow Zone, but the area up to the bridge was considered a neutral zone, so Yellow Zone zombies could wander in and out.</p>

<p>The Yellow Zone zombies that had swarmed in after hearing the collapse were now teeming on the bridge we had to cross.</p>

<p>Zombies ahead of us. Zombies and Je Hyun-oh behind us.</p>

<p>Both were shitty options, but if I had to choose, it was the former, no question.</p>

<p>Even so, the sight right in front of me was so overwhelming that cold sweat soaked my back.</p>

<p>The moonlight, unusually bright tonight, illuminated the bridge. Hundreds of rotting bodies scraped against the asphalt, panting and gasping.</p>

<p>The zombies covering the bridge rushed toward the only source of sound, their bodies grotesquely twisted.</p>

<p>Toward Kwak Hanmuk and me.</p>

<p>Clink. The sound of small beads rang out. Dozens of strands of the Bead Rosary extending from Kwak Hanmuk lashed out in all directions.</p>

<p>The rosary whipped out like a lash, crushing the zombies on impact.</p>

<p>Even the ones that seemed a bit tougher were sent flying by the force of the rosary and splashed down into the river.</p>

<p>It seemed like we could handle the zombies without much trouble.</p>

<p>…The zombies, at least.</p>

<p>My vision suddenly darkened, and I looked up at the sky. It wasn't because a cloud had covered the moon.</p>

<p>Six wings made of black metal feathers were blotting out the moonlight.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh flew leisurely, his wings spread wide. As if he were simply spectating the chaos on the bridge.</p>

<p>Our eyes met again.</p>

<p>I couldn't see him clearly, but I was sure of it. My skin stung from the intensity of his gaze.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh drew one arm back, then swung it wide.</p>

<p>Several pitch-black crescent moons shot out, tearing through the asphalt. Black lines were scrawled across the bridge like graffiti.</p>

<p>"Lieutenant Colonel, you son of a—!"</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk twisted the throttle to its limit. The speedometer needle edged into the red zone.</p>

<p>I held onto Kwak Hanmuk with all my strength. A vibration rose from below, accompanied by a crunching sound.</p>

<p>Cracks starting from the black lines spread rapidly, and the asphalt split apart with loud snaps.</p>

<p>The fractured sections began to collapse in layers.</p>

<p>"Kreeeeeh—!"</p>

<p>The zombies let out incomprehensible shrieks as they fell. Right after, a massive chunk of the bridge plummeted into the river.</p>

<p>KABOOM!</p>

<p>A gigantic pillar of water shot up, drenching everything around us.</p>

<p>Soaking wet, Kwak Hanmuk and I raced across the bridge.</p>

<p>The spreading cracks chased after us.</p>

<p>The motorcycle sped along, narrowly outpacing the rapidly approaching collapse.</p>

<p>But there was no way Je Hyun-oh would just sit back and watch us escape. He folded his metal wings in unison and dove sharply.</p>

<p>BOOM!</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh landed at the end of the bridge.</p>

<p>Slowly straightening up, he let his arms hang loose and waited for us.</p>
