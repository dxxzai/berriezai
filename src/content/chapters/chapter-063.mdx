<h1>Chapter 63</h1>

<p>…No way.</p>

<p>But it was far too obvious.</p>

<p>It was because Kwak Hanmuk’s gaze, after meeting mine, shifted toward Dominic and then returned to me.</p>

<p>‘Captain Kwak….’</p>

<p>He was the last person I expected.</p>

<p>‘Captain Kwak!?’</p>

<p>I barely managed to stop myself from jumping up in shock.</p>

<p>When I clenched my fist tightly, Dominic’s laughter rang out once again.</p>

<p>“Were you surprised? There is no need to be overly shocked. He is someone of a lower level than you, Roasted Chestnut.”</p>

<p>‘No, then how can he see Dominic, sir!?’</p>

<p>I waited anxiously, hoping he would continue.</p>

<p>But Dominic did not say anything more and simply wandered around on my forearm as if teasing me.</p>

<p>After settling onto the back of my hand, Dominic looked at me while his body gently wobbled.</p>

<p>It was as if he was telling me that if I wanted to know his story, I should move somewhere quiet on my own.</p>

<p>But being alone with Dominic would only be possible after entering Sla2Plate.</p>

<p>I barely managed to tear my gaze away from Dominic.</p>

<p>Since we were in the middle of a briefing, I couldn’t act too strangely.</p>

<p>‘Dominic-ssi. Please tell me the rest quickly.’</p>

<p>I suppressed the urge to squeeze the slime tightly and shake him around while focusing on the briefing, but countless thoughts continued racing through my head.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk’s gaze, which kept alternating between me and Dominic, also contributed to my distraction, so I had to put in a tremendous amount of effort just to properly listen.</p>

<p>“…The compatible individual who will enter alongside you is Lieutenant Won Taebeom from Field Team 2 of the Field Response Headquarters. He has entered trials multiple times while teamed with Captain Mo Haein, and he has also previously formed a team with Captain Kwak Hanmuk.”</p>

<p>Lieutenant Won Taebeom was someone I knew.</p>

<p>I had even played together with him in the Archive game. He was highly skilled, quiet, and the type of person who could keep secrets well.</p>

<p>In many ways, he was the most suitable personnel assignment for entering this trial together with the Special Mission Team.</p>

<p>“He is also an infected individual of the BT-DFH virus. However, his infection level is low, and he is able to maintain a clear state of mind consistently, so there are no concerns regarding that.”</p>

<p>Won Taebeom’s appearance had changed slightly due to the infection, but compared to Je Hyun-oh and other infected individuals, the difference was minimal.</p>

<p>I listened carefully to Samra’s explanation while recalling the Won Taebeom I had seen in the pixel graphics.</p>

<p>“In Lieutenant Won Taebeom’s case, he will stop his current mission and join the entry into &lt;Slay to Plate&gt;, so he is expected to arrive at the incubation site the latest.”</p>

<p>I was in no position to complain. I should be grateful that he was coming at all.</p>

<p>‘Especially since it’s a mission where he has to enter together with Lieutenant Colonel Je.’</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh had proudly held the number one spot for several years among the superiors that compatible individuals from the Trial Response Agency least wanted to enter trials with.</p>

<p>I felt endless gratitude toward Won Taebeom.</p>

<p>And finally, when the briefing ended.</p>

<p>I tried to find even a little time to speak with Dominic, but there simply wasn’t any opportunity.</p>

<p>Even if I said I was going to the bathroom, it seemed like soldiers would follow me.</p>

<p>With no other choice, I carried my anxious feelings with me and moved to the incubation site of Sla2Plate.</p>

<p>The incubation site of Sla2Plate was Dosan Park in Gangnam.</p>

<p>I had heard that a meteorite had caused chaos after falling into one of the most valuable areas of land, and after seeing the location, I understood why.</p>

<p>‘It really is right in the middle of Gangnam.’</p>

<p>When we arrived, the entire surrounding area, including Dosan Park, had been placed under a complete access restriction.</p>

<p>Thanks to that, we were able to get out of the vehicle in an empty area and move into the park.</p>

<p>The Sla2Plate meteorite had fallen between the trees.</p>

<p>All the nearby trees had fallen outward in a circle around the small meteorite at the center, creating a strange sight.</p>

<p>It looked almost as if the meteorite’s location was being emphasized.</p>

<p>Surrounded by soldiers, I crouched down alone beside the meteorite.</p>

<p>I stared blankly at the meteorite, which was about the size of my palm.</p>

<p>The Sla2Plate meteorite, with its red base mixed with white streaks, almost looked like fresh beef with marbling at first glance.</p>

<p>‘Someone with bad eyesight might have picked it up and grilled it in a frying pan.’</p>

<p>While I was thinking that it looked delicious, Dominic, who had moved onto the back of my hand, examined the meteorite with interest.</p>

<p>“Is this the entrance to the trial you mentioned, Roasted Chestnut?”</p>

<p>I glanced around.</p>

<p>The soldiers surrounding me stood at a distance, as if forming a perimeter.</p>

<p>A short whisper probably wouldn’t be heard.</p>

<p>“Yes, Dominic-ssi. If you touch this, you will be transported. Including the people around you.”</p>

<p>I quickly explained because I was worried Dominic might touch the meteorite.</p>

<p>Sla2Plate required a set number of people before entry was possible, so simply touching it wouldn’t immediately transport us. Still, since the person in question was Dominic, I couldn’t help but worry.</p>

<p>Dominic calmly answered while standing on my hand.</p>

<p>“I see. I can definitely feel an unfamiliar quality that does not belong to your planet, Roasted Chestnut.”</p>

<p>We spent some time looking over the meteorite together when a loud voice suddenly called out.</p>

<p>“Goyo!”</p>

<p>I jumped up and turned around.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein had arrived.</p>

<p>“Captains!”</p>

<p>I quickly placed Dominic on my shoulder and ran toward them.</p>

<p>I approached them happily, but my eyes widened.</p>

<p>Both of them looked like they had been rolling around somewhere. Their uniforms were a mess.</p>

<p>It seemed capturing the criminal had not been an easy task.</p>

<p>As I stared at them in surprise, Kwak Hanmuk suddenly rushed toward me and stopped right in front of my face.</p>

<p>He looked down at me.</p>

<p>His expression was that of someone who had too many things to say and didn’t know where to begin.</p>

<p>“Goyo, you….”</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk started to speak, but then flinched and shifted his gaze to the side.</p>

<p>He was obviously looking at Dominic, who was enjoying the middle of the day at the park while sitting on my shoulder.</p>

<p>In the meantime, Mo Haein approached.</p>

<p>Her face was flushed with emotional excitement as she looked up at me.</p>

<p>She grabbed my forearm tightly.</p>

<p>It hurt, but I couldn’t say anything and simply let her hold on.</p>

<p>Mo Haein soon let out a long breath. After regaining her composure with one breath, she called out to me.</p>

<p>“Trainee.”</p>

<p>“Yes…?”</p>

<p>“Let’s talk after we enter Sla2Plate.”</p>

<p>“Yes….”</p>

<p>Both Mo Haein and Kwak Hanmuk probably wanted to question me immediately.</p>

<p>But there were too many Trial Response Agency soldiers around us.</p>

<p>I bowed my head first.</p>

<p>“Thank you for coming, Captains. Because of me, you entered a trial right after finishing your mission….”</p>

<p>“Why would that be because of you, Goyo?”</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk cut off my words.</p>

<p>“That’s right. It’s not your fault, Trainee.”</p>

<p>Mo Haein quietly added after Kwak Hanmuk’s words.</p>

<p>“From the beginning, it was the Trial Response Agency that forced a Trainee into a one-month deadline.”</p>

<p>Mo Haein’s words made me feel strange. I awkwardly thanked them again.</p>

<p>“Even so… thank you so much. Are you two okay, Captains?”</p>

<p>Mo Haein frowned.</p>

<p>“I’m fine. My clothes are just a little dirty.”</p>

<p>Judging from her expression, it seemed like they had failed to capture the wanted criminal they were chasing.</p>

<p>‘Was it a smuggler?’</p>

<p>People who targeted trial items were always troublesome.</p>

<p>I was thinking about the most infamous criminal organization among them when—</p>

<p>A trailer carrying a container and several vehicles stopped at the entrance of Dosan Park.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh had arrived.</p>

<p>Thanks to his fast trial clear, I had been able to separate from Ham Jiwol, so I went to greet him with a happy heart.</p>

<p>The quiet park entrance, kept empty due to strict security control, quickly became packed with container trucks and vehicles surrounding them.</p>

<p>Dozens of soldiers wearing dark blue uniforms poured out of small SUV-style tactical vehicles.</p>

<p>They all immediately drew their weapons and aimed them at the container.</p>

<p>One soldier cautiously approached the container and opened the door.</p>

<p>From the darkness inside, the sound of someone walking out could be heard.</p>

<p>Step.</p>

<p>Step.</p>

<p>Step.</p>

<p>Unlike the surroundings, which were filled with extreme tension, the footsteps coming from inside the container were relaxed.</p>

<p>A man wearing sleek goggles and a gas mask appeared under the bright sunlight.</p>

<p>Dressed entirely in black, he idly clicked his metal hand.</p>

<p>It was nothing more than a habitual movement, but the soldiers surrounding him became even more tense.</p>

<p>‘Is it really necessary to go this far?’</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh had no interest in ordinary humans.</p>

<p>The only things that moved him were trials and compatible individuals.</p>

<p>Even if the soldiers fired bullets at him right here, Je Hyun-oh would not show any intention of attacking.</p>

<p>At most, he would find it slightly annoying, like insects flying around him.</p>

<p>While I thought the level of caution against Je Hyun-oh, who had never harmed civilians, was excessive, I also understood the Trial Response Agency’s position of needing to prepare for the worst.</p>

<p>If he, an infected individual, lost control of himself, immediate execution was the rule.</p>

<p>‘Still, they could at least let him travel normally.’</p>

<p>Seeing him transported in a container like cargo bothered me.</p>

<p>At this rate, he only had the rank of lieutenant colonel, but he was being treated no differently from a serious criminal.</p>

<p>I approached Je Hyun-oh with a great deal of dissatisfaction.</p>

<p>The soldiers slightly loosened their formation and made a path for me.</p>

<p>“Lieutenant Colonel Je Hyun-oh, you’re here.”</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh slowly turned his head.</p>

<p>The moment he noticed me, a light flashed on his goggles.</p>

<div class="hyunoh"><p>! !</p></div>

<p>The exclamation marks appeared, and he gestured toward me.</p>

<p>When I cautiously approached, he placed me in front of himself and silently examined me.</p>

<p>While he carefully checked me from head to toe, I simply stood there awkwardly.</p>

<p>Then Je Hyun-oh replaced one of his metal hands with the crow glove and grabbed the top of my head.</p>

<p>“Lieutenant C-Colonel…?”</p>

<p>He forcibly spun me around.</p>

<p>I stood in front of Je Hyun-oh and spun around about three times before he finally let go.</p>

<p>After finishing his surprisingly thorough inspection, Je Hyun-oh released my head and walked into the park as if satisfied with himself.</p>

<p>‘What was that?’</p>

<p>Left standing there alone, I stared after him in confusion before noticing a man getting out of a vehicle behind him.</p>

<p>He was enormous, wearing a Trial Response Agency uniform.</p>

<p>His body, made of thick and solid muscles, looked like a shield.</p>

<p>His grayish-brown skin, a color difficult for ordinary humans to have, was proof that he was an infected individual.</p>

<p>I quickly greeted him.</p>

<p>“I’m Special Mission Team Trainee Han Goyo.”</p>

<p>The man standing in front of me spoke in a blunt voice.</p>

<p>“I’m Lieutenant Won Taebeom from Field Team 2.”</p>

<p>I wanted to say something more, but Won Taebeom immediately headed toward the park.</p>

<p>I hurriedly followed.</p>

<p>Mo Haein and Kwak Hanmuk, who had been waiting near the meteorite, gave Je Hyun-oh a formal salute.</p>

<p>Seeing that, I realized my mistake.</p>

<p>A Trainee who had only enlisted one month ago had failed to salute a distant superior officer of the lieutenant colonel rank.</p>

<p>The captains didn’t normally act this formally either, but today, with so many soldiers watching, it felt like they were intentionally maintaining proper etiquette.</p>

<p>I hurried over beside the captains and saluted as well.</p>

<p>I had never actually saluted before, so it was probably awkward, but my intention was simply to match the atmosphere.</p>

<p>Seeing my salute, Je Hyun-oh displayed an emoticon on his goggles.</p>

<div class="hyunoh"><p>? ?</p></div>

<p>“Pfft….”</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk couldn’t hold it in and laughed out loud.</p>

<p>Even Mo Haein was quietly laughing.</p>

<p>The only one who didn’t laugh and remained expressionless was Won Taebeom.</p>

<p>‘Was it really that strange?’</p>

<p>I awkwardly lowered the hand I had saluted with.</p>

<p>Mo Haein smiled and spoke to me.</p>

<p>“I’ll teach you properly next time.”</p>

<p>Then, after briefly exchanging a look with Lieutenant Won Taebeom, she pressed her earpiece and reported to Samra.</p>

<p>“Lieutenant Colonel Je Hyun-oh, Lieutenant Won Taebeom has joined. We’ll proceed with entry.”</p>

<p>She placed her hand on the meteorite.</p>

<p>The world became stained with fluorescent colors and began to collapse, and just as the five of us were about to enter—</p>

<p>“…!”</p>

<p>Everyone on the Special Mission Team widened their eyes.</p>

<p>Won Taebeom had been pushed out of the entry area.</p>

<p>As if the required number of people had already been exceeded.</p>

<p>He looked at us in confusion, but the entry process had already begun.</p>

<p>I entered the trial.</p>

<p>The slime sparkled brightly with delight.</p>

<p>The one who pushed out Won Taebeom and entered Sla2Plate was none other than….</p>

<p>Dominic.</p>
