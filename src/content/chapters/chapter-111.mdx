<h1>Chapter 111</h1>


<p>I stared at the system window in disbelief.</p>

<p>‘It hit an affinity score of 100 just like that?’</p>

<p>Normally, raising affinity was incredibly difficult.</p>

<p>It was especially tricky in the early stages, as the creatures were highly wary.</p>

<p>One wrong move, and their suspicion levels would skyrocket.</p>

<p>Even in the game, I had brought them vine plants like this before, but I had never managed to reach an affinity of 100 instantly.</p>

<p>Much like the fluctuation I’d seen earlier in front of Cell No. 13, the magnitude of the change was massive.</p>

<p>High affinity offered numerous advantages.</p>

<p>It made communicating with the creatures much easier.</p>

<p>Consequently, it made it easier to reach the True Ending—the path to &quot;True Freedom.&quot;</p>

<p>However, I couldn't simply rejoice at such an abnormal spike in the score.</p>

<p>I worried whether the True Ending would still trigger if I followed the walkthrough I knew.</p>

<p>I examined Creature No. 1, which was wrapped around my body.</p>

<p>Its vines were densely covered in scars from blade cuts—marks left from harvesting Neo.</p>

<p>Some scars were particularly large and distinct; they were the result of body parts being severed and then reattached.</p>

<p>I tried to locate the small vine I had been carrying around, but it was nowhere to be seen.</p>

<p>It seemed to have already fully merged with Creature No. 1.</p>

<p>Through touching the vines, I could directly sense the affinity Creature No. 1 felt toward me.</p>

<p>‘Honestly, I didn’t treat it well enough to max out the affinity score.’</p>

<p>Sure, I had carried the vine plant around, but that was about it.</p>

<p>I had even struck it a few times with my baton, after all. I found the numbers hard to believe, so I checked the stats for Creature No. 1.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Creature No. 1<br />
&#45; Suspicion: 0<br />
&#45; Affection: 100</p></div></div>

<p>The figures were staggering.</p>

<p>In Cell-X, these were the kind of dream stats one could only hope for with a creature, yet I felt no joy.</p>

<p>I hadn't done anything to warrant such blind affection.</p>

<p>The thought that these numbers might be the result of a glitch actually left me feeling a bit uneasy.</p>

<p>I let out a slow breath.</p>

<p>This wasn't the time for that. I had only found Creature No. 1 so far; I needed to hurry and locate the remaining creatures.</p>

<p>But Creature No. 1 showed no signs of letting me go.</p>

<p>It simply writhed its vines, pulling me deeper into its embrace.</p>

<p>Before I knew it, I was buried within the creature's body.</p>

<p>Unlike the tough, rigid outer vines, the inner ones were made of incredibly delicate tissue.</p>

<p>Covered in soft fuzz—much like a newly sprouted shoot—they felt wonderful to the touch.</p>

<p>Part of me wanted to stay buried there, but I had work to do.</p>

<p>With only my upper body exposed, I called out to Creature No. 1.</p>

<p>“Um... Creature Inmate No. 1?”</p>

<p>As I tapped gently to get its attention, the vines writhed excitedly.</p>

<p>That wasn't exactly the message I’d intended to convey.</p>

<p>Watching Creature No. 1—a fugitive acting so incredibly laid-back—left me feeling both exasperated and amused.</p>

<p>I smiled faintly, stroked the vines for a moment, and then checked on Dominic.</p>

<p>Dominic had climbed up onto my head.</p>

<p>It seemed he didn't want to touch the vines.</p>

<p>Other than that, he remained silent, simply jiggling like a soft pudding slime. Reassured, I called out to Creature No. 1 again.</p>

<p>“Creature Inmate No. 1. Could you tell me where the other creatures are?”</p>

<p>Knowing it wouldn't understand my words, I added some gestures.</p>

<p>I held two vines in my hands and brought them together.</p>

<p>When I mimicked a handshake, the vine wriggled as if it understood.</p>

<p>Then, carrying me, it began to move sluggishly along.</p>

<p>It certainly made things convenient to have such a high affinity level.</p>

<p>As Creature No. 1 slithered through the shadows, I glanced up at the sky.</p>

<p>Every searchlight in the prison was tracking a dark figure.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh was really getting under the guards' skin.</p>

<p>Countless bullets streaked across the sky, but Je Hyun-oh nimbly dodged them by freely manipulating his metallic wings.</p>

<p>He would act as if he were about to get hit, only to narrowly evade the shots at the last moment; the infuriated guards were becoming increasingly fixated on him.</p>

<p>It seemed the guards wouldn't be able to take their eyes off the No. 13 escapee until he took at least one hit.</p>

<p>Watching that dark figure glide effortlessly through the night sky, a thought suddenly crossed my mind.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh had snatched me up, partly to drop me onto the creature.</p>

<p>But it seemed there was another reason, too.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk had warned me about Won Taebeom.</p>

<p>He had said that anything I did in Lieutenant Won's presence would be reported back to Jang Seokyun.</p>

<p>There was no way Je Hyun-oh was unaware of that fact. Perhaps he had deliberately kept me away from Won Taebeom, judging it best that I not show him how close I was with the NPCs or reveal the way my eye color changed.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh was the head of the Field Response Agency.</p>

<p>True to his nickname—&quot;The Madman&quot;—he often seemed to act on impulse, yet the vast majority of his decisions and actions were precise.</p>

<p>‘Except for the DeZonDeal incident.’</p>

<p>I still wondered why he had ridden me so hard back then.</p>

<p>In fact, there were countless other questions I had about Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>Like why he kept looking up at the sky...</p>

<p>I was pondering Je Hyun-oh while being carried by Creature No. 1.</p>

<p>Suddenly, a system window popped up with a chime, signaling a change in stats.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Affinity +10</p></div>

<div class="systembox"><p>Suspicion +12</p></div>

<p>It meant a creature or a guard was nearby.</p>

<p>Since no guard would gain affinity just by seeing me riding Creature No. 1, it had to be a creature.</p>

<p>I sharpened my senses and scanned the surroundings.</p>

<p>It was dark and shrouded in fog, making it difficult to see clearly.</p>

<p>For one thing, it didn't seem to be a large entity.</p>

<p>Even with the poor visibility, a large creature wouldn't have been completely obscured.</p>

<p>It had to be a medium or small-sized creature.</p>

<p>One capable of concealing itself and possessing enough intelligence to observe the situation.</p>

<p>There was a creature that fit those criteria perfectly.</p>

<p>No. 6.</p>

<p>I felt a mix of tension and a racing heart.</p>

<p>I had been prepared to meet him ever since I entered Cell-X. Yet, it was still a daunting prospect.</p>

<p>I slowly parted my lips.</p>

<p>“Creature No. 6...” </p>
<p>No sooner had my voice trailed off than a gasp of dismay erupted.</p>

<p>“Unbelievable!”</p>

<p>The shout came from the shadow of Creature No. 1.</p>

<p>The shadow began to writhe and bulge, and a tall figure sprang forth.</p>

<p>Patches of white marked his black hair, appearing only on either side of his head.</p>

<p>The man, standing over two meters tall, wore an eye patch.</p>

<p>Resembling an orca, the one-eyed man spread his arms toward me—I was currently half-buried in the body of Creature No. 1.</p>

<p>He bared his jagged teeth and spoke.</p>

<p>“How did you figure it out? You don't seem to possess the ability to detect my stealth.”</p>

<p>He was the only one among the twelve creatures who possessed a name and the ability to communicate freely with humans.</p>

<p>Creature No. 6, Orca.</p>

<p>And he was… the very reason I had come to loathe Cell-X.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_pixel"><p>“Roasted Chestnut. You can’t have everything you want. Sometimes, you have to accept the bitter and painful things, too.”</p></div>

<div class="bigbox2_pixel"><p>“Oh dear… Roasted Chestnut, are you crying? I’m sorry. That was my fault. It’s a hard thing for someone as young as you to accept…”</p></div>

<p>I struggled to suppress the memories flooding back and focus on the Orca standing before me in reality.</p>

<p>Fortunately, a system window popped up just then, making it a bit easier to pull myself together.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Creature No. 6 located.<br />
(Creatures found so far: 2/12)</p></div>

<p>I immediately checked Orca’s stats.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Creature No. 6 (Orca)<br />
&#45; Suspicion: 30<br />
&#45; Affinity: 72</p></div></div>

<p>‘Wait, an affinity of 72?’</p>

<p>Yet another unbelievable figure appeared. Flustered, I mentally retraced the notifications regarding stat changes that had flashed rapidly before my eyes back in Zone X.</p>

<p>Although multiple notification windows had popped up simultaneously, I remembered them all clearly; none had slipped past me.</p>

<p>Among them, I recalled system windows showing &quot;Affinity +62&quot; and &quot;Suspicion +18&quot;—stats that seemed to belong to Orca.</p>

<p>'Those are impossible numbers.'</p>

<p>The Chief of Security’s stat spike had been strange enough, but the figures for the creatures were just as erratic.</p>

<p>I wanted to know the cause behind these fluctuations.</p>

<p>After all, even an anomaly must have a cause to produce an effect.</p>

<p>To see affinity jump by such large margins meant there was definitely something going on that I didn't know about.</p>

<p>Without thinking, I stared intently at Orca.</p>

<p>Then, ding—another notification window popped up.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Affinity +2</p></div>

<p>All I did was look at him...</p>

<p>Just as I was feeling dumbfounded, I realized this situation was similar to the one with the Chief of Security.</p>

<p>Back then, too, the stats had changed simply because our eyes met, without any other specific action on my part.</p>

<p>'What am I missing?'</p>

<p>As I reached for a clue that felt just out of reach, Orca spoke to me first.</p>

<p>&quot;Medic. What is your name?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Little Lamb.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Ah, Little Lamb...&quot;</p>

<p>Upon hearing my name, Orca looked visibly disappointed for some reason.</p>

<p>Then, abruptly, another notification appeared.</p>
<div class="systembox">
<p>
Affinity -20
</p>
</div>

<p>He slashed the score by 20 points in one go, just because I stated my name.</p>

<p>At this point, I began to wonder if the stats were being determined by a random roulette spin.</p>

<p>Orca spoke again, his tone somewhat brusque. </p>
<p>“Little Lamb. I don’t know why you’re wearing a guard’s uniform or how you managed to lure No. 1 out, but… Argh!”</p>

<p>He screamed mid-sentence.</p>

<p>It was because Creature No. 1 had extended a vine and struck Orca hard.</p>

<p>Possessing the nature of a plant, Creature No. 1 could sense an opponent's emotions through the vibrations of their voice.</p>

<p>It seemed to have retaliated immediately because it sensed Orca was being hostile toward me.</p>

<p>I patted Creature No. 1 and then spoke to Orca.</p>

<p>“As Creature No. 1 realized, I don’t possess any special abilities, but I do have medical knowledge. I came here to help the creature patients, and also…”</p>

<p>Although my affinity score had dropped by 20, it was still above 50.</p>

<p>Given the anomalies occurring here, I decided it would be best to be direct—even if it meant taking a risk—to clear the scenario quickly.</p>

<p>I was about to say something he would never believe.</p>

<p>But I didn't get the chance.</p>

<p>“Did you come to set us free?”</p>

<p>Orca had voiced the very words I was about to speak.</p>

<p>How did he know?</p>

<p>As I froze in the face of this unexpected situation, a bright smile spread across Orca’s face as he looked at me.</p>

<p>He laughed heartily and exclaimed:</p>

<p>“Haha! It seems it wasn’t just a foolish delusion after all!”</p>

<p>Even though he was on the run as an escaped prisoner, he laughed with genuine delight, unable to hold back his mirth in that moment.</p>

<p>A notification popped up with a ding.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Affinity +30</p></div>

<p>This time, my affinity score had risen by a full 30 points.</p>

<p>As I repeatedly checked the system window, Orca reached out his hand toward me.</p>

<p>Creature No. 1 wriggled its vines and lowered me down to him.</p>

<p>“You might find this hard to believe.”</p>

<p>Orca said as he caught me in his arms.</p>

<p>“But I saw you in my dreams.”</p>

<p>His dark, glossy eyes were filled with affection for me.</p>

<p>“In them, I didn’t call you ‘Little Lamb’—I called you ‘Roasted Chestnut.’”</p>
