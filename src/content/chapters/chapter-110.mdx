<h1>Chapter 110</h1>


<p>Listening to the ear-splitting siren sound, I took a moment to assess the current situation.</p>

<p>And I realized that I had made a very wrong judgment.</p>

<p>As I was being dragged away by the Chief of Security, I urgently sent a message to Kwak Hanmuk.</p>

<p>… I sent the message ‘only’ to Kwak Hanmuk.</p>

<p>Given the chaos, Kwak Hanmuk likely didn't think to share the situation with Je Hyun-oh either.</p>

<p>It would have been even more difficult for a prison guard to contact Creature Inmate No. 13 separately, and it was even more so since I had urged him via message to come as quickly as possible.</p>

<p>Thus, Je Hyun-oh was left alone in Zone X, knowing nothing more than the fact that the trainee had been dragged away by the Chief of Security under ominous circumstances.</p>

<p>For him, there was no reason to wait patiently.</p>

<p>Naturally, he had escaped, and while he was at it, it seemed he had taken the opportunity to take out other creatures from the prison as well.</p>

<p>Since the main quest was to 'find true freedom for the creatures,' it was an action well worth attempting.</p>

<p>It was also the best decision Je Hyun-oh could make given his situation.</p>

<p>However, a complete escape of all the creatures would result in a bad ending.</p>

<p>This was because they would wander aimlessly on a deserted island in the middle of the ocean, unable to find a place to escape, only to be caught by guards and thrown back into prison.</p>

<p>Some of the escaped creatures would even be shot dead and thrown into the sea.</p>

<p>However, the escape that just occurred was clearly different from the game.</p>

<p>'Because the one who orchestrated the escape is Creature Inmate No. 13.'</p>

<p>It was a role that never existed in the game to begin with.</p>

<p>Since an anomaly had already occurred, he concluded that the situation might unfold in a different direction than a bad ending.</p>

<p>'A true ending might still be possible.'</p>

<p>Of course, a true ending cannot be established in the current situation.</p>

<p>This was because the 'true freedom' the creatures desired was not a prison break.</p>

<p>I absolutely did not want to see Cell-X explode on the rooftop of the Gyeonggi Regional Office.</p>

<p>Just as I was feeling anxious that I had to clear the game with the True Ending no matter what, the system window popped up.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<div class="systembox_wrap">
<p>
<span class="glitch_t2">◆Ma?in? Qu??es!!t!!</span>: Find the Escaped Creatures.<br />

Your subordinate is running wild.<br />

You resolve to resolve the situation caused by your subordinate in order to achieve the True Ending.<br />

The escaped creatures are scattered throughout the island.<br />

Finding the creatures while avoiding the prison guards will by no means be easy.<br />

This is because the eyes of the guards, hidden beneath their metal masks, reach very far.<br />

You must find the creatures before the guards <span class="glitch_t2">do?Really?findthem?Canyou?</span><br />

(Creatures found: 0/12)
</p>
</div>
</div>

<p>As soon as I saw the quest, I narrowed my gaze slightly.</p>

<p>The number of creatures I had to find was not thirteen, but twelve.</p>

<p>“Is Lieutenant Colonel Je not included?”</p>

<p>I was staring intently at the quest window when this happened.</p>

<p>A crackling noise came from the speakers.</p>

<p>A voice flowed from the speakers, which had terrible sound quality.</p>
<br />
<em><p>“Code Black. All guards to position.”</p></em>
<br />
<p>“It doesn’t sound like the Chief of Security.”</p>

<p>Seeing him issue the Code Black order, I wondered if it was the old-fashioned warden Kwak Hanmuk had grumbled about.</p>

<p>I was listening intently, hoping for a clue, when I flinched at the broadcast that followed.</p>
<br />
<em><p>“Report immediately if a Medic is found during the creature search.”</p></em>
<br />
<p>Why are they looking for me?</p>

<p>But I didn’t even have the luxury of wondering.</p>

<p>As soon as the broadcast ended, heavy thumping sounds followed one after another.</p>

<p>It was the sound of prison sections being locked down.</p>

<p>Won Taebeom gripped my forearm tightly and said.</p>

<p>“Trainee Han. You need to move.”</p>

<p>If I stayed here any longer, I might end up trapped inside a closed prison and framed for aiding an escape.</p>

<p>I left Zone X with Won Taebeom.</p>

<p>Running side by side, he asked:</p>

<p>“Do you have a plan in mind?”</p>

<p>“We need to find the escaped creatures. At this rate, even achieving the normal ending might be difficult.”</p>

<p>As I answered, I sent a message to Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Lieutenant Colonel Je, I am fine.</p></div>

<div class="systembox"><p>To clear the True Ending, we must find the escaped creatures. Please do not kill the creatures or the guards; I would appreciate it if you could bear with me just a little longer.</p></div>

<div class="systembox"><p>Please continue playing the role of the good creature.</p></div>

<p>I spun the messages while simultaneously racking my brain.</p>

<p>Saying it was just finding the creatures was a bit of a stretch; in reality, it meant I had to search every corner of the island.</p>

<p>It was no easy task to find the twelve creatures while avoiding the guards in the pitch-black darkness.</p>

<p>“I think we need to find Lieutenant Colonel Je first.” </p>
<p>Won Taebeom immediately understood what I was saying.</p>

<p>More than anything else, finding Je Hyun-oh was the most urgent task.</p>

<p>I had sent a message, but I didn't know if he would heed my words.</p>

<p>Even if we found the creatures, if Je Hyun-oh massacred all the prison guards, it would end in a normal ending.</p>

<p>I had to check on his condition first.</p>

<p>After running with all my might alongside Won Taebeom, we were able to get out of the prison not long after.</p>

<p>A cold sea breeze blew as if slapping my cheeks.</p>

<p>My hair was flying wildly, and the searchlights sweeping across the area and the sound of sirens mingled chaotically.</p>

<p>I gasped for breath and looked up at the night sky.</p>

<p>And in the black sky, I discovered a being with a color even darker than that.</p>

<p>He… was standing atop the prison building.</p>

<p>The figure, gazing up at the sky with his metal hands hanging down, was submerged in darkness.</p>

<p>Yet, for some reason, he caught my eye instantly.</p>

<p>I followed Je Hyun-oh's gaze.</p>

<p>In the night sky, thick with sea fog rising from the ocean, not even the moon was visible, let alone stars.</p>

<p>Yet, Je Hyun-oh stared at the sky persistently.</p>

<p>His gaze, which had refused to leave the night sky, slowly turned toward me.</p>

<p>As if he had known I was here from the very beginning.</p>

<p>Time was of the essence.</p>

<p>Yet, the moment our eyes met, the first thought that crossed my mind was a strange one.</p>

<p>It was the thought that he, after all, was well-suited for open spaces.</p>

<p>Even though this madness began with Je Hyun-oh's escape, I was actually...</p>

<p><em>Satisfied</em> that he had escaped from Cell No. 13.</p>

<p>&quot;...!&quot;</p>

<p>I flinched back to my senses.</p>

<p>I had just felt as if my eye color was about to change.</p>

<p>I quickly felt around my eyes with my hand, but fortunately, I didn't feel any heat.</p>

<p>Just then, the searchlight sweeping the ground turned toward the sky.</p>

<p>Because the sea fog was so thick, the light from the searchlights illuminating the ground was reflecting off the fog.</p>

<p>The reflection of the light was actually obstructing visibility.</p>

<p>Therefore, it seemed the intention was to shoot the searchlights toward the fog layer rather than the ground to diffuse the light.</p>

<p>As the searchlights hit the fog layer, an artificial light was created, and the area around the prison became softly illuminated.</p>

<p>It looked as if bright moonlight was shining, making it even easier to catch the escapees hiding in the darkness.</p>

<p>The searchlights aimed at the sky also located Je Hyun-oh on the rooftop.</p>

<p>Trapped in the light, he did not react at all, even though he knew he had been discovered.</p>

<p>He simply stared at me.</p>

<p>It might be an exaggeration, but it seemed as though he was waiting to see what I would do.</p>

<p>A Lieutenant Colonel waiting for a trainee.</p>

<p>Thinking it was nonsense from beginning to end, I sent him a message.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Please distract the guards while we search for the creatures.</p></div>

<p>Honestly, I thought he would do at least that much.</p>

<p>A vague sense of confidence that the escapee Je Hyun-oh didn't seem to be in too bad a mood also played a part.</p>

<p>I sent a message to Kwak Hanmuk as well, asking him to guide the prison guards toward Lieutenant Colonel Je as much as possible if he could.</p>

<p>Then, turning to Won Taebeom, I said:</p>

<p>“I intend to look for the Creature while Lieutenant Colonel Je distracts the guards. I will start by finding Creature Inmate No. 1…”</p>

<p>I stopped mid-sentence and wore a puzzled expression.</p>

<p>It was because Won Taebeom wasn't looking at me, but at my back.</p>

<p>Just as I was wondering why he was acting this way with a surprised look in his eyes...</p>

<p>“…Gasp!”</p>

<p>I was snatched up and dragged into the air.</p>

<p>With Won Taebeom’s bewildered expression as the final image, I was instantly lifted away from the ground.</p>

<p>The back of my prison guard uniform stretched taut, as if it were about to tear.</p>

<p>Still, perhaps because they weren't metal hands but crow gloves, the clothes held on precariously.</p>

<p>The uniform was made of a special material, so it didn't look like it would tear.</p>

<p>The vines on my forearm, startled by the sudden change, wrapped around me almost completely.</p>

<p>Dominic, his ears flattened by the strong wind, politely voiced his grievances to me.</p>

<p>“Roasted Chestnut. As I said before, the subordinate made of black metal needs education.”</p>

<p>If I could have educated Je Hyun-oh, I would have to become the Director of the TRA, not a trainee.</p>

<p>‘No, I don’t think he would even listen to the President.’</p>

<p>While I was lost in a moment of idle thought, Je Hyun-oh, who had snatched me up, stopped in mid-air only after rising high enough.</p>

<p>I unconsciously looked down.</p>

<p>And I witnessed an incredible sight.</p>

<p>It was the sight of all the prison guards running to catch Je Hyun-oh…</p>

<p>It was a pity that I hadn't been the only one left out here; it would have been perfect for going to find the Creature.</p>

<p>“Goyo.” </p>
<p>Je Hyun-oh called out to me in a remarkably calm tone.</p>

<p>His voice was so composed that anyone watching would have thought he had called me while I was eating cake at a cafe.</p>

<p>“Yeah…”</p>

<p>When I answered listlessly, he asked a question.</p>

<p>“Are you out of your mind?”</p>

<p>At those words, I immediately realized why Je Hyun-oh had snatched me up.</p>

<p>I was currently wearing the prison guard uniform I had worn during the new recruit training course.</p>

<p>Since a former Medic had suddenly appeared in a prison guard uniform, Je Hyun-oh seemed to have concluded that this was Clean3's Butler Part 2.</p>

<p>For him, it was necessary to verify my sanity.</p>

<p>I urgently informed Je Hyun-oh of my condition.</p>

<p>“I’m perfectly fine! I’m a Medic, but I ended up like this because of that bastard Chief of Security…!”</p>

<p>Dangling precariously in mid-air, I cursed at the Chief of Security with all my might.</p>

<p>At that, Je Hyun-oh moved one of his metal wings and lightly tapped the vine plant.</p>

<p>The trembling vine plant flinched and squeezed my forearm tightly.</p>

<p>“And this?”</p>

<p>“Ah, this is part of Creature Inmate No. 1.”</p>

<p>I quickly and swiftly summarized the situation to him.</p>

<p>However, it seemed that wasn't the answer Je Hyun-oh wanted.</p>

<p>He stared at me intently through his sleek goggles and repeatedly tapped the plant with his metal wings.</p>

<p>It appeared he was asking why I bothered carrying this around.</p>

<p>I offered a lame excuse.</p>

<p>“Um… this friend is a coward… so it’s a bit difficult to get rid of him.”</p>

<p>Then, I pleaded as pitifully as possible.</p>

<p>“Lieutenant Colonel Je. I really need to go find the creatures.”</p>

<p>I struggled to get my operation approved, claiming that the True Ending would be impossible if I didn’t find them now.</p>

<p>“If you find them all?”</p>

<p>I could guess the meaning behind that short question.</p>

<p>It must have meant whether I was going to send the creatures back to Zone X.</p>

<p>“For now… since you went to the trouble of helping them escape, it feels a bit unfair to put them back in prison, so I will consider a different approach.”</p>

<p>A rattling sound of metal wings moving was heard.</p>

<p>I watched the guards draw their guns and aim as I listened to Je Hyun-oh’s voice.</p>

<p>“Find them quickly, Goyo.”</p>

<p>He spoke like a very generous Lieutenant Colonel.</p>

<p>“Before I start wanting to kill all the guards.”</p>

<p>And then he flew off somewhere with a whoosh.</p>

<p>“…!”</p>

<p>He tossed me down with a thud.</p>

<p>I fell without even being able to scream.</p>

<p>“Lieutenant Colonel Je!!”</p>

<p>I was screaming inwardly, pouring out my resentment toward Je Hyun-oh, who had completely forgotten our camaraderie.</p>

<p>The place where Je Hyun-oh dropped me was behind the prison wall, a spot where the light barely reached.</p>

<p>Just as I thought I was helplessly crashing into the ground and was trying to wrap the vines on my forearm around them.</p>

<p>Something wriggling in the dim shadows reached out toward me.</p>

<p>I opened my eyes wide.</p>

<p>The moment I realized it was a plant stem, I saw a form hidden in the darkness.</p>

<p>Dark green and brown vines were tangled together, wriggling in a circle.</p>

<p>There were no flowers to be seen on the thick, rough stem, but leaves were sprouting sparsely.</p>

<p>A gigantic creature, appearing to be about 3 meters tall, stretched its stem toward me.</p>

<p>Wrapped tightly around my forearm the tangled vines also stretched their stems toward the creature.</p>

<p>The separate beings touched and intertwined, gradually transforming into one.</p>

<p>My falling body was caught by stems extending from all directions, causing me to lose speed.</p>

<p>As I stood completely entangled in the stems, like a spider caught in a web, a notification popped up.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<p>
Creature No. 1 found.<br />

(Creatures found: 1/12)
</p>
</div>

<p>At the same time, the sound of small firecrackers was heard, and a system window appeared.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Affection reached 100!</p></div>
