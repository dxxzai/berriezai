 <h1>Chapter 1</h1>

    <div class="messagewrap">
    <div class="messagebox"><p>Director: Goyo, there's a coaching position open. Come whenever you want.</p></div>
   
    <div class="messagebox"><p>SGY: Player-nim, this is Day News Sa Gongyeon. Would it be possible to schedule an interview...</p></div>
    </div>
  
    <p>I turned on my phone to check the time and saw unread message notifications staring back at me.</p>

    <p>It's been ages since I retired as a pro gamer, and they're still calling me "Player-nim."</p>

    <p>The director should've given up by now, but the messages kept coming, persistent as ever.</p>

    <p>I looked down at my hand for a moment.</p>

    <p>Four years after the accident, it looked perfectly fine on the outside &mdash; no issues with daily life either.</p>

    <p>It just couldn't do pro gaming anymore.</p>

    <p>With practiced ease, I flicked past the notifications and left only the live stream push alerts.</p>

    <p>It was almost time for my favorite M-Tuber's broadcast. I didn't have time to waste on message notifications I wasn't going to read anyway.</p>

    <p>I booted up my computer, connected to the livestream, and dropped a donation right away.</p>

    <p>My nickname and donation message appeared in large letters across the chat window.</p>

    <p>The M-Tuber beamed and called out to me.</p>
    <br/>

    <em><p>"Roasted Chestnut-nim! Welcome!"</p></em>

    <p>[Roasted Chestnut: Hello]</p>
    <br/>
    <p>My greeting was immediately buried under a flood of chat from the fervent viewers.</p>

    <p>[PLEASESAVEMEPLEASEGETMEOUTICANTBEATTHIS]</p>

    <p>[I CANT DO THIS ANYMORE HEHEHEHHEHEHEHEHEHE]</p>

    <p>[IMSORRYIMSORRYIMSORRYIMSORRY]</p>

    <p>[IMDYINGITSHURTSHURTSHURTS]</p>

    <p>[THEENDINGHOWTHEENDINGPLEASETHEENDING]</p>

    <p>The M-Tuber I loved didn't have many viewers.</p>

    <p>Barely a few dozen people, but they were all loyal regulars &mdash; the chat was way more active than other streams of this size.</p>

    <p>Another strange thing: in most small streams, viewers tended to socialize with each other, but here, nobody cared about anyone else.</p>

    <p>They were all too busy demanding what they wanted from the M-Tuber.</p>

    <p>Which I actually liked. No unwanted attention meant I could relax.</p>
    <br/>
    <em><p>"I was just waiting for you, Roasted Chestnut-nim."</p></em>

    <p>[Roasted Chestnut: I was waiting for the stream too.]</p>
    <br/>
    <p>And more than anything, I genuinely liked this M-Tuber.</p>

    <p>I smiled, a little embarrassed, and sent the message.</p>

    <p>I'd debuted as a pro gamer at nineteen, built a decent career, and retired.</p>

    <p>It was a forced retirement because of the accident, and becoming unemployed overnight was incredibly boring.</p>

    <p>So I looked for something to kill the boredom and ended up gaming.</p>

    <p>I dove into all kinds of online games, then moved on to packaged games &mdash; AAA titles, indie games, I devoured everything without discrimination.</p>

    <p>That's how I discovered an indie game that fit my tastes perfectly.</p>

    <p>I stayed up two nights straight to see every ending, and naturally searched for the creator's other games.</p>

    <p>Which led me to stumble onto the creator's livestream.</p>

    <p>That was two years ago.</p>

    <p>Becoming a regular viewer of the creator, I got to enjoy a huge privilege: testing games in development.</p>

    <p>Since they were a solo developer, everything was pixel-art indie games.</p>

    <p>But the creative settings and stories made them more immersive than any other game I'd played.</p>

    <p>The genres were diverse &mdash; never any overlap &mdash; and all the games were even connected within a single, massive world.</p>

    <p>The so-called Archive world.</p>

    <p>For two years, I'd test-play the games the creator handed me and spent a wonderful time inside their world.</p>

    <p>I typed into the chat, wondering what kind of game they'd made this time.</p>
    <br/>
    <p>[Roasted Chestnut: The last game you gave me was really fun.]</p>

    <em><p>"I knew you'd get the true ending, Roasted Chestnut-nim."</p></em>
    <br/>
    <p>How did they know I'd cleared the true ending?</p>

    <p>I blinked for a moment.</p>

    <p>But they knew I'd stayed up all night to see every ending for every game they'd given me, so it wasn't that strange.</p>

    <p>I shook off the confusion and asked.</p>
    <br/>
    <p>[Roasted Chestnut: What kind of game is it today?]</p>
    <br/>
    <p>The M-Tuber plucked my message out of the frantic flood of viewer chat and grinned.</p>
    <br/>
    <em><p>"Hmm, no game today."</p></em>
    <br/>
    <p>Then they suddenly leaned their face close to the camera.</p>

    <p>Their face filled the screen, and I instinctively leaned back.</p>

    <p>The M-Tuber's eyes glistened under the lighting as they whispered.</p>
    <br/>
    <em><p>"And there won't be any from nooow on."</p></em>
    <br/>
    <p>My heart plummeted.</p>

    <p>"No game?"</p>

    <p>For someone like me, who'd enjoyed all kinds of games thanks to this M-Tuber, it was like a bolt from the blue.</p>

    <p>Unlike me, frozen in shock, the M-Tuber seemed strangely excited today.</p>

    <p>Like they'd just won the lottery or something.</p>

    <p>[NOGAME? NOGAME? ICANLEAVE? LETMEOUT?]</p>

    <p>[IWANTTOGOOUTIMSORRYPLEASESAVEME]</p>

    <p>[Already over? Boring.]</p>

    <p>[FINALLYFINALLYDIEDIEDIEDIE]</p>

    <p>The viewers' chat went into overdrive at the M-Tuber's bombshell.</p>

    <p>I barely managed to move my fingers through the torrent of messages.</p>
    <br/>
    <p>[Roasted Chestnut: That's a shame... Can I ask why?]</p>

    <em><p>"Because it's over now."</p></em>
    <br/>
    <p>The M-Tuber's voice sounded completely carefree.</p>
    <br/>
    <em><p>"Thanks for everything. It's all thanks to you, Roasted Chestnut-nim."</p></em>
    <br/>
    <p>Were they saying they were quitting streaming entirely?</p>

    <p>Would they keep making games? Or stop that too...?</p>

    <p>It was common for solo developers to quit for various reasons.</p>

    <p>But it was such a waste. They were a creator with genius talent.</p>

    <p>Just look at how much the viewers of this stream loved their games.</p>

    <p>Can't they just release a few as early access or something?</p>

    <p>Knowing they already had several highly polished games, I bit my lip.</p>

    <p>I started typing a message to ask for details, then stopped.</p>

    <p>It couldn't be, but I felt like the M-Tuber on the other side of the screen was looking right at me.</p>

    <p>The M-Tuber stared straight into the camera and stretched their lips into a wide, sideways smile.</p>

    <p>At that moment, a hot sensation burst from my nose &mdash; a nosebleed.</p>

    <p>"Ghk..."</p>

    <p>My eyeballs burned and a throbbing headache split my skull.</p>

    <p>Was it from staying up all night and then watching the stream right after?</p>

    <p>I got up to find a tissue, and a voice came through.</p>
    <br/>
    <em><p>"Roasted Chestnut-nim is the best viewer I've ever met. If it's you, you can do it. Because I found you, and I confirmed it."</p></em>
    <br/>
    <p>I finally found a tissue, plugged my nose, and looked back at the screen.</p>

    <p>The M-Tuber was still staring straight into the camera.</p>
    <br/>
    <em><p>"From now on, I'll be your viewer, Roasted Chestnut-nim."</p></em>
    <br/>
    <p>Nothing they'd said since earlier made any sense. I pressed my nose and typed with one hand.</p>
    <br/>
    <p>[Roasted Chestnut: I don't understand what you mean.]</p>

    <em><p>"I can't wait. I've been waiting for so long. So, so, so, so long."</p></em>
    <br/>
    <p>By this point, I couldn't ignore that something was seriously wrong with the M-Tuber.</p>

    <p>They didn't seem drunk. Please don't tell me it was drugs...?</p>

    <p>As a long-time viewer, I was about to type that they should end the stream when &mdash;</p>
    <br/>
    <em><p>"Well then, shall we begin?"</p></em>
    <br/>
    <p>He said my name.</p>
    <br/>
    <em><p>"Han Goyo."</p></em>
    <br/>
    <p>A name I'd never once told him.</p>

    <p>I opened my mouth to say "huh," and the entire world twisted.</p>

    <p>The perfectly normal studio apartment that had been there just a moment ago warped, melting and crumpling as it stained into splotchy fluorescent colors.</p>

    <p>A cheerful chime rang out, and a system window appeared before my eyes.</p>
   
    <div class="systembox"><p>Player 'Han Goyo' announces their name to Akasha!</p></div>

    <div class="systembox"><p>Current recognition: 0.01%</p></div>

    <p>System windows with incomprehensible content surged up, blocking my view.</p>

    <p>I read the very last system window and lost consciousness.</p>

    <div class="systembox"><p>You are the System of planet 'Earth'.</p></div>
    
    <hr class="calibre3"/>

    <p>A string sound echoed through an old speaker.</p>

    <p>I frowned at the grating noise that kept mixing in between the long, drawn-out notes.</p>

    <p>Groaning, I opened my eyes.</p>

    <p>The sight before me was unlike anything I'd ever seen.</p>

    <p>A kaleidoscope of colorful interior design. A wall mural with a huge, cute character. Toy package boxes stacked as high as a Christmas tree.</p>

    <p>No matter how I looked at it, this place was a world away from my studio apartment.</p>

    <p>It looked like some kind of toy factory.</p>

    <p>I pressed my temples &mdash; the headache was still faintly there &mdash; and scanned my surroundings again.</p>

    <p>A signboard with round, friendly typography hung overhead.</p>

    <p>The letters were very subordinate. I couldn't believe my eyes, so I read them out loud.</p>

    <p>"&lt;Happy Smile Factory&gt;...?"</p>

    <p>I quickly looked back at the character wall mural I'd glimpsed earlier.</p>
    <div class="fontcwrap">
    <div class="fontc">
    <p>Welcome to the Happy Smile Factory!</p>

    <p>We are your toy friends who will make you happy!</p>

    <p>Shout it together! Happy Smile!</p>
    </div>
    </div>
    <p>Children's toys with cute slogans beamed with happy smiles.</p>

    <p>Cold sweat soaked the back of my neck.</p>

    <p>Because &lt;Happy Smile Factory&gt; was...</p>

    <p>...a game made by my favorite creator.</p>

    <p>Had the creator kidnapped me and locked me in a place that looked like their game?</p>

    <p>It was the most plausible theory I could come up with, but it made no sense.</p>

    <p>Who would kidnap a retired pro gamer &mdash; an unemployed nobody? The most pointless crime in the world.</p>

    <p>I stood frozen in the surreal situation, then slowly got up from the floor.</p>

    <p>"...Wow."</p>

    <p>I stopped mid-motion and let out a hollow laugh at what I was wearing.</p>

    <p>I was in a &lt;Happy Smile Factory&gt; uniform.</p>

    <p>The bright green work clothes had a name tag with the Happy Smile logo on the chest.</p>
    
    <div class="fieldset">
    <p>Employee: Roasted Chestnut</p>
    </div>
    <p />
    <p>I scraped at the embroidered name tag with my finger, but it was sewn on tight &mdash; wouldn't come off.</p>

    <p>I checked my pockets for anything useful, but they were empty except for dust.</p>

    <p>There was a flashlight pouch hanging from my belt, though.</p>

    <p>Exactly the same start as the game's intro.</p>

    <p>"This is insane."</p>

    <p>The curse slipped out on its own. I dragged my hand down my face.</p>

    <p>&lt;Happy Smile Factory&gt; was the first game of the creator's that I'd ever played.</p>

    <p>The player wakes up in a toy factory, searches for lost memories and the factory's hidden secrets, and escapes.</p>

    <p>The problem was the toys.</p>

    <p>The toys here hate adults. They try to eliminate any adult who trespasses into the factory &mdash; i.e., the player.</p>

    <p>So if this situation plays out like &lt;Happy Smile Factory&gt;...</p>

    <p>I had to avoid murderous toys and escape the factory.</p>

    <p>If there was a lunatic crazy enough to set up a factory this size, they'd probably also prepared murderers wearing toy masks.</p>

    <p>What should I do?</p>

    <p>I tried to abandon common sense and think like a lunatic.</p>

    <p>First, I figured I might as well treat it like playing the game.</p>

    <p>Since according to the game, the goal was to escape the factory.</p>

    <p>I moved cautiously, crossing the lobby. Just as I was about to enter the interior &mdash;</p>

    <p>CRASH!</p>

    <p>A massive explosion erupted from deep inside the factory.</p>

    <p>Are the toys already moving?</p>

    <p>If so, staying out here wasn't going to help.</p>

    <p>I vaulted over the turnstile gate and ran inside.</p>

    <p>Dust billowed up, obscuring my vision. I was covering my nose with my sleeve, looking for somewhere to hide, when &mdash;</p>

    <p>Suddenly, my throat was squeezed tight and my body lifted off the ground.</p>

    <p>"Gack!"</p>

    <p>The hard plastic against my windpipe choked me &mdash; I struggled for a moment, then they dropped me to the floor.</p>

    <p>A flustered voice spoke.</p>

    <p>"What the &mdash; a civilian?"</p>

    <p>The dust cleared, and I could see who'd been choking me.</p>

    <p>A short-haired woman with a small build and sharp features. She was beautiful.</p>

    <p>Beside her stood a man in the same work uniform.</p>

    <p>Our eyes met, and a rectangular system window appeared.</p>
    
    <div class="systembox"><p>You have discovered 'Mo Haein'!</p></div>
    
    <div class="systembox"><p>'Mo Haein' is a registrable character. Would you like to register?</p></div>
