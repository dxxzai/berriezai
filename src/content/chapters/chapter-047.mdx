<p>My vision cleared a little.</p>

<p>After reading through all the system windows that had appeared, nothing new surfaced.</p>

<p>Probably because nothing could surface.</p>

<p>The opening sequence of a new Trial was playing.</p>

<p>A choir of boys and girls swelled in the background.</p>

<p>Clear voices singing a sacred hymn to the accompaniment of an organ and strings — each note steeped in sorrow.</p>

<p>A dreamy funeral march of a song played as 'I' stepped off a train billowing smoke.</p>

<p>I smoothed my rumpled suit with my hands — wrinkled from sitting too long — and looked out at the city before me.</p>

<p>'I' was a reporter working for a small newspaper in London, sent to a tiny country town called Morneveil for an assignment.</p>

<p>The city was wrapped in a pale fog like a shroud, filled with a distinctive gloom.</p>

<p>Officially, 'I' had come to investigate a mysterious string of disappearances happening here.</p>

<p>But I didn't need to come all the way to Morneveil for a reason like that.</p>

<p>London had far more sensational and interesting incidents happening all the time.</p>

<p>If I wanted material for an article, London would have been the better choice.</p>

<p>The truth was, 'I' had heard a story.</p>

<p>That the disappearances here were serial murders committed by a cult hiding in the city — and that they were trying to summon a god from beyond the Earth through human sacrifice.</p>

<p>'I', who had always been interested in the occult, reached out to the people I suspected were part of the cult.</p>

<p>When I said I wanted to cover their group, surprisingly, they agreed readily.</p>

<p>And so, invited by the cult, 'I' came to Morneveil.</p>

<p>"Are you the Little Lamb?"</p>

<p>The woman who came to pick me up was kind and gentle.</p>

<p>She drove me around the city, introducing Morneveil in a friendly voice.</p>

<p>Then she stopped the car in front of an old hotel.</p>

<p>"This is a hotel run by our order. Everyone will make sure you're comfortable during your stay."</p>

<p>"Does this mean I'm a member now?"</p>

<p>I put on a serious face and joked — she burst into cheerful laughter.</p>

<p>"Of course not. We only accept those who sincerely wish to join. But feel free to look around."</p>

<p>Her eyes sparkled.</p>

<p>"I'm looking forward to your coverage, Little Lamb. I hope we get along."</p>

<p>She left. After checking into the hotel, 'I' was finally alone.</p>

<p>As soon as I set my luggage down in the room, a system window appeared.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>'Han Goyo (Little Lamb)' has entered &lt;Call of the Void&gt;!</p></div></div>

<p>Finally regaining my free will, I staggered to the window and wrenched it open.</p>

<p>Cool outside air flooded the dim room, but the nausea churning in my stomach didn't settle easily.</p>

<p>I took a deep breath.</p>

<p>My hands, which had been trembling uncontrollably, steadied.</p>

<p>I dropped onto the sofa in the room.</p>

<p>Watching dust motes drift through the air, I began sorting through the chaos in my head, one piece at a time.</p>

<p>'Clean3... is it closed?'</p>

<p>I'd definitely cleared it with a True Ending.</p>

<p>But I couldn't be sure Clean3 was actually closed.</p>

<p>Because in the final moment, the butlers hadn't disappeared.</p>

<p>They'd forced me into a new Trial.</p>

<p>And what they'd said then pointed toward a reunion.</p>
<br/>
<p><span class="flashback">"We'll be waiting for the day we return home with the Master."</span></p>

<p><span class="flashback">"We'll wait, Little Lamb."</span></p>
<br/>
<p>I covered my face with my hands.</p>

<p>I'd promised them I'd find their Master — I never expected that promise to boomerang back like this.</p>

<p>That Trial had been strange from the start.</p>

<p>It was connected to the records of a game I'd played in the past, so I'd fully accounted for the possibility of anomalies.</p>

<p>But NPCs not disappearing even after reaching the ending?</p>

<p>That was impossible under the structural rules of a Trial that followed game logic.</p>

<p>It felt like everything I knew about Trials was crumbling.</p>

<p>Even the name I used in the Trial had changed.</p>

<p>'It said the tutorial was complete. Is that related?'</p>

<p>I tried to speculate, but the system window was unresponsive, so nothing worked.</p>

<p>And of all the Trials to enter, it had to be &lt;Call of the Void&gt;...</p>

<p>"...Hah."</p>

<p>Letting out a short sigh, I pushed myself off the sofa.</p>

<p>'First, clear it and get out.'</p>

<p>I checked the system window floating before me.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>◆ &lt;Call of the Void&gt; Main Quest: Investigate the secret of Morneveil and make a choice.</p></div></div>

<p>The quest window looked very different from usual.</p>

<p>Normally, a lengthy quest description would scroll below, but now it was just a brief statement.</p>

<p>The objective was vague and unintuitive.</p>

<p>If I hadn't played this game before, I'd have no idea what to do.</p>

<p>'That's weird. Come on, show me the proper window.'</p>

<p>I tried talking to it in my head, but no response.</p>

<p>Seemed like it was broken again.</p>

<p>As always, it never showed up when I needed it.</p>

<p>At least the system window was appearing at all — I supposed I had to be satisfied with that.</p>

<p>I tried to check Je Hyun-oh and the Captains' status windows, but again, no response.</p>

<p>Cursing the system window's uselessness, I recalled what I knew.</p>

<p>This game — called CallOVo for short — was an interactive game where the story branched based on choices.</p>

<p>When you start playing, you have to choose between two routes.</p>

<p>Stop the cult's plot to summon an Outer God from beyond Earth — or become their leader and summon the Outer God yourself.</p>

<p>In the former, you expose the cult's brutal human sacrifices as a reporter. In the latter, you join the cult, rise to the position of leader, and summon the head of the Outer Gods.</p>

<p>I'd played both routes to see every ending.</p>

<p>I knew the game's content and how to play it well, but there was one critical problem.</p>

<p>I didn't know the True Ending of CallOVo.</p>

<p>It wasn't because the game was boring or too hard that I gave up halfway.</p>

<p>The Creator's games were always interesting, and I especially liked CallOVo for its unique setting.</p>

<p>But the reason I never saw the True Ending was frustratingly anticlimactic.</p>

<p>While progressing through the Cult Leader route, after summoning what I assumed was the head Outer God, the game just... stopped.</p>

<p>The screen went black, unresponsive, and only a somber funeral march kept playing.</p>

<p>When I told the Creator about it at the time, they said it was an error in an unfinished game and suggested I play something else first.</p>

<p>I had no choice but to move on to another game, and afterward, I kept waiting for CallOVo to be completed.</p>

<p>'And now I'm suddenly face-to-face with it.'</p>

<p>I'd never played the True Ending, but I guessed it involved becoming the cult leader and summoning the Outer God.</p>

<p>I was sure of it because I'd played every other ending.</p>

<p>But this time, I was giving up on the True Ending.</p>

<p>'Who knows what might happen.'</p>

<p>The butlers had sent me to wherever their Master was.</p>

<p>There was no way that Master was just an ordinary NPC.</p>

<p>Given how the powerful butlers served them with such loyalty, they had to be an incredible being — and I had no idea what kind of anomaly I'd face if I ran into them.</p>

<p>'If I become the cult leader and summon the Outer God, that Master NPC might get summoned too...'</p>

<p>I was going to choose the ending where I escape Morneveil as a reporter.</p>

<p>As for my promise to the butlers — someday, somewhere down the line, I'd make it happen.</p>

<p>Maybe I could re-enter CallOVo later, after I'd gotten stronger.</p>

<p>But not now.</p>

<p>There was also the Epic Quest to close three Trials within a month, but I'd think about that after getting out.</p>

<p>To proceed with the Reporter Route, there was one thing I needed to do first.</p>

<p>Win over the key NPC — 'Dr. Dominic' — to my side.</p>

<p>He was a psychiatrist running a small clinic. Originally, in peaceful Morneveil, he had little to do and lived quietly.</p>

<p>But as the cult's influence grew, people thrown into chaos started visiting his clinic, dragging him into the incident.</p>

<p>I absolutely had to recruit him as an ally from the early stages to make the Reporter Route's Exposure Ending possible.</p>

<p>Because he had the ability to break the cult's brainwashing.</p>

<p>I checked outside the window.</p>

<p>The sun hadn't set yet, so the doctor's clinic should still be open.</p>

<p>I was about to dash out of the hotel when I stopped and looked at the mirror.</p>

<p>I needed to tidy up my appearance a bit so I didn't look like a patient to Dr. Dominic.</p>

<p>Since the game was set in a fictional British city at the end of the 19th century, my clothes were a classic suit.</p>

<p>A frock coat, vest, and trousers — a ditto suit. My reflection looked unfamiliar.</p>

<p>I wiped the cold sweat from my face with a handkerchief and smoothed my disheveled hair.</p>

<p>Finally, I buttoned the shirt button that had been left undone, then left the room.</p>

<p>A system window appeared.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<div class="systembox_wrap">
<p>
First choice. Which path will you take?<br/>

&#45; As a reporter, investigate the cult of Morneveil and uncover their secrets.<br/>

&#45; Become a cultist, join the cult of Morneveil, and explore their secrets. (This choice cannot be changed midway.)
</p>
</div>
</div>

<p>It was the choice for which route to take.</p>

<p>You could switch from the Reporter Route to the Cultist Route, but not the other way around.</p>

<p>Without hesitation, I chose the Reporter Route.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Begin collecting evidence to expose the cult's secrets as a reporter.</p></div></div>

<p>Then, as soon as I stepped outside the hotel, another choice appeared.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<div class="systembox_wrap">
<p>
You have exited the hotel. Where would you like to go?<br/>

&#45; Shopping district<br/>
&#45; City Hall<br/>
&#45; Plaza
</p>
</div>
</div>

<p>Only the places I'd seen while riding with the cult woman were available as options.</p>

<p>I chose the Plaza, and my body moved on its own.</p>

<p>I bought a newspaper from a newsboy in the plaza.</p>

<p>Then I flipped through it until I found what I was looking for.</p>

<p>An advertisement for Dr. Dominic's clinic.</p>
<div class="news_wrapper">
<p style="font-size:1.60em;">&lt;A Sanctuary for the Weary Souls of Modern Times — Dr. Dominic's Clinic!&gt;</p>

Is your night troubled?<br/>

For the lost souls amid the city's noise, Dr. Dominic's Clinic has opened its doors.<br/>

Through hypnotherapy based on the latest medical theories and psychological relaxation techniques, we promise to thoroughly wash away the primal fears and anxieties hidden within you.<br/>

Dr. Dominic listens to your voice.<br/>

Visit us now and reclaim your inner peace!<br/>

&#45; Specialties: Insomnia, auditory hallucinations, visual hallucinations, delusions, emotional instability, etc.<br/>

&#45; Location: End of Willow Forest Path, western outskirts of Morneveil.<br/>
</div>

<p>After reading the ad, I left the plaza, and a new choice appeared.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<div class="systembox_wrap">
<p>
You have exited the plaza. Where would you like to go?<br/>

&#45; Hotel<br/>
&#45; Shopping district<br/>
&#45; City Hall<br/>
&#45; Dr. Dominic's Clinic
</p>
</div>
</div>
<p>I chose the clinic, and my body moved on its own again.</p>

<p>The clinic was located on the outskirts — quite a distance away.</p>

<p>A car would have been convenient, but apparently because of the 'poor reporter with shallow pockets' setting, 'I' walked.</p>

<p>After a brisk walk, a modest house came into view.</p>

<p>A Victorian-style house built from faded wood, using the first floor as a clinic and the second as a residence.</p>

<p>A well-tended garden with a gravel path led up to the house.</p>

<p>A car was parked in front — maybe a patient had come for treatment.</p>

<p>I walked carefully along the gravel path toward the doctor's clinic.</p>

<p>The crunch of stones under my shoes grated against the quiet.</p>

<p>I reached for the brass door knocker, about to knock — when I heard a voice from inside.</p>

<p>"Oh, it's all thanks to your treatment, Doctor!"</p>

<p>The lively voice was probably a patient who'd visited the clinic.</p>

<p>She seemed to be talking to the doctor at the door before leaving.</p>

<p>The patient spoke in a bright voice.</p>

<p>"Before, so many people were talking to me inside my head every single day. It was truly agonizing. I couldn't even function normally. But after your treatment, the auditory hallucinations and delusions all disappeared."</p>

<p>I held my breath, listening.</p>

<p>Had Dominic treated a cult member brainwashed by the cult?</p>

<p>But something felt off.</p>

<p>"Now I only hear your voice in my head, Doctor! I only think about you!"</p>

<p>...What?</p>

<p>'I don't think she's fully cured yet.'</p>

<p>Maybe she needed more treatment.</p>

<p>But contrary to my thoughts, the patient continued happily.</p>

<p>"All that unnecessary suffering I trapped myself in before... I'm so happy now, Doctor."</p>

<p>The emotion in her voice was genuine.</p>

<p>I sensed something was wrong.</p>

<p>Quietly, I let go of the door knocker and stepped back.</p>

<p>Just as I turned to flee —</p>

<p>The front door opened.</p>

<p>"Watch your step, Miss Mary."</p>

<p>A deep, refined voice.</p>

<p>A woman in an hourglass-style dress with a cinched waist hurried out.</p>

<p>Then from behind her.</p>

<p>He appeared...</p>

<p>With trembling eyes, I slowly looked up.</p>

<p>Bending his head low to pass through the doorway — far too low for his height — the man spotted me.</p>

<p>"Hello! Haven't seen you before."</p>

<p>He smiled gently and offered his hand.</p>

<p>"I'm Dominic."</p>

<p>The man introducing himself as Dr. Dominic.</p>

<p>An NPC I'd never seen in CallOVo.</p>

<p>And he was —</p>

<p>Three meters tall.</p>
