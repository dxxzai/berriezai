<h1>Chapter 25</h1>

<p>Ding. The System window appeared. The sound of a quest updating.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p><span class="glitch_t2">◆M?ain!! Q?uest?!</span>: Repair the emergency generator within 15 minutes….</p></div>

<p>I closed it without even reading the first line.</p>

<p>I didn't have time to read a useless System window.</p>

<p>'The emergency power couldn't have run out already.'</p>

<p>Something felt wrong. I was worried about Yi Gaon, but I couldn't go up and check.</p>

<p>If I didn't repair the emergency generator in the next 15 minutes, it was game over.</p>

<p>I had to trust that Yi Gaon was safe and focus on the repair.</p>

<p>"We're totally fucked, aren't we?"</p>

<p>"I think we can manage somehow."</p>

<p>And then I broke into a run, shouting. "Let's move!"</p>

<p>We sprinted through the cafeteria, weaving between the overturned tables and chairs.</p>

<p>Our footsteps echoed loudly, but the zombies only glanced at us before turning back.</p>

<p>They were all reaching for the speakers on the ceiling, wailing as the broadcast played.</p>

<p>It was a relief we were all zombies. If we hadn't been, finishing the repair in 15 minutes would have been nearly impossible.</p>

<p>I ran at full speed, calculating the time in my head.</p>

<p>'At least 5 minutes for the repair.'</p>

<p>6 minutes to get to the emergency generator facility in the basement, 1 minute for the boss fight event if there was one.</p>

<p>That left about 2 minutes of leeway, but that was only if everything fit together like clockwork.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk's role in handling the boss was the most important.</p>

<p>I glanced back at him following behind me.</p>

<p>He leaped over a fallen chair with his long legs, met my eyes, and raised an eyebrow.</p>

<p>I opened the cafeteria door and shouted into the hallway.</p>

<p>"Captain Kwak! How are you feeling right now?"</p>

<p>"Feeling better than usual! Maybe it's the zombification?"</p>

<p>"Good to know."</p>

<p>We ran down the long corridor, checking the signs. It was the same layout as the game, so I wouldn't need to waste time searching.</p>

<p>Seeing the sign for the sleeping quarters made my stomach twist slightly.</p>

<p>The sleeping quarters were for researchers working 24 hours who couldn't go home. There's an old diary hidden there that mentions the cure.</p>

<p>I remembered its contents well.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_pixel">
<p>
Paranoid Researcher's Journal Entry #1<br/>

The cure for the virus requires a painfully long time to find candidate substances. <br/>Still, humanity has always fought against viruses that threaten survival and found cures. <br/>But a virus from outside Earth… what happens then?
</p>
</div>


<div class="bigbox2_pixel">
<p>
Paranoid Researcher's Journal Entry #2<br/>

The standard antiviral development process is merely a record within the narrow confines of 'Earth's ecosystem.' <br/>If a virus from outside invades, humanity will not find a cure. <br/>Adapt, or be annihilated. <br/>No one understands my thoughts, but I am always afraid.
</p>
</div>


<div class="bigbox2_pixel">
<p>
Paranoid Researcher's Journal Entry #3<br/>

I finally met people who shared my fear.
</p>
</div>


<div class="bigbox2_pixel">
<p>
Paranoid Researcher's Journal Entry #4<br/>

Countless voices whisper in my head. <br/>Why was I afraid of a mere virus? <br/>It means nothing in the face of this overwhelming terror.<br/>Yes!<br/> <p style="font-size:1.75em;">I am awake!</p>
</p>
</div>


<div class="bigbox2_pixel">
<p>
Paranoid Researcher's Journal Entry #5<br/>

<p style="font-size:1.75em;">The writhing pulse of the dimension! <br/>The awe-inspiring void of chaos! <br/>Formless chaos!<br/> Everything is but a small amusement for Them!</p>
</p>
</div>

<p>It was a paranoid researcher's journal, so it was full of nonsense, but it did mention the cure.</p>

<p>I wanted to check if there were any items I hadn't found in the game near the diary, like a portable cassette player… but after repairing the generator, parts of the basement would be sealed off.</p>

<p>This was the last chance, but I had no choice but to turn away.</p>

<p>We ran down the long corridor in an instant, turned left, and arrived at the freight elevator.</p>

<p>The silver stainless steel doors gleamed ominously.</p>

<p>The elevator, running on emergency power, could only be used once. After that, the generator had to be repaired to move it.</p>

<p>Shortly after I pressed the down button, a ding sounded and the heavy doors slid open.</p>

<p>"Ugh…."</p>

<p>"Shit."</p>

<p>Mo Haein let out a low groan at the sight. Kwak Hanmuk immediately covered his nose with his hand and cursed.</p>

<p>The elevator was already at capacity. Researchers in tattered, blood-stained gowns and office workers stood frozen in grotesque poses, their unfocused eyes fixed on the open doors.</p>

<p>"Can't we lure them out? Make a loud noise or something?"</p>

<p>"They probably won't react."</p>

<p>They were set here to prevent players from using the freight elevator. No matter what, they couldn't be lured out; you had to ride with them.</p>

<p>'The freight elevator can't be used unless the player becomes a zombie.'</p>

<p>Sure enough, even when Kwak Hanmuk threw a large stone to make noise, the zombies just twitched and didn't come out.</p>

<p>Mo Haein asked, watching him struggle. "Any other routes?"</p>

<p>"Without the elevator, we'd have to use the ventilation ducts, but we don't have time, and Captain Kwak probably won't fit."</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk squeezed his eyes shut, accepting reality. "Let's go."</p>

<p>I stepped into the elevator first. Squelch. Instead of the sound of cold metal, a wet, sticky sound of squashed flesh and blood came under my feet.</p>

<p>I squeezed myself into the narrow gaps between the zombies. A zombie's soft, decaying flesh brushed against my shoulder. The stench was unbearable.</p>

<p>I pushed myself as far back as I could and nodded to Kwak Hanmuk and Mo Haein. They followed me in.</p>

<p>Just as Mo Haein was about to press B2, a siren went off.</p>

<p>The piercing siren made the zombies, who had been standing still, snap their eyes open.</p>
<br/>
<em><p>"Attention. All researchers, proceed to the shelter immediately." </p></em>
<br/>

<p>The automated announcement played, and the zombies, agitated by the sound, began to go berserk.</p>

<p>"RAAAH-!"</p>

<p>Short Mo Haein was swept away in an instant. The doors closed before she could press the button.</p>
<br/>
<em><p>"…Isolation shutters are activating in all sectors. The building is being sealed. Once again, all researchers…"</p></em>
<br/>
<p>The cramped space became a chaotic mess.</p>

<p>Inside the freight elevator, the lights were out, leaving it near pitch black. Only the faint glow of the floor buttons illuminated the space.</p>

<p>I was crushed against the wall by the frenzied, glowing bodies.</p>

<p>"Ugh…"</p>

<p>A groan escaped me from the pressure, as if I was being pressed by a blunt weapon.</p>

<p>The zombie pressing against me from the front grrk-ed and thrust its face forward. Its teeth, stained red from god knows what, were visible. I wanted to shove it away, but I couldn't.</p>

<p>Being a zombie didn't make me invincible. I just wouldn't be attacked first. The moment I was judged to have attacked a zombie, they would start attacking me too.</p>

<p>If a fight broke out in this tight space, it would be a disaster.</p>

<p>Cold arms stretched out from all directions, pressing hard against my chest. For a moment, my vision spun.</p>

<p>"Grrk!"</p>

<p>Luckily, the zombies pushed each other, and the ones pressing on me were knocked away.</p>

<p>I barely came to my senses after nearly passing out, and in the chaos, I felt a warm body heat.</p>

<p>"Captain Mo…!"</p>

<p>I pulled Mo Haein, who was almost crushed by the zombies.</p>

<p>As I pulled her close, Kwak Hanmuk gritted his teeth and reached an arm through the zombies. His fingertips barely grazed the buttons. A couple of misses. Then, on the last try, his finger brushed the button.</p>

<p>The B2 button lit up. With a jolt, the freight elevator began to descend.</p>

<p>I tried to secure some space and suppressed my breathing as much as possible. Every inhale felt like the smell of corpses was crushing my lungs.</p>

<p>In the sealed box, we struggled not to touch a single zombie finger. The time it took to go down one floor felt infinitely long. The freight elevator was slower than a normal one.</p>

<p>Ding!</p>

<p>Finally, the arrival signal sounded. The elevator doors opened slowly. The screaming zombies poured out in a rush.</p>

<p>"Haah!" Mo Haein and I gasped for air we'd been holding.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk, who had managed not to be swept away by the collapsing zombies, urgently looked for us. After confirming we were safe, he pushed Mo Haein out first.</p>

<p>Then, the three of us collapsed in front of the elevator, panting.</p>

<p>I thought I was really going to die… The crushing was bad, but the rotting flesh and the stench clinging to us was especially awful. I figured people with germophobia would have a really hard time as zombies.</p>

<p>Mo Haein was holding her head, looking a bit dizzy.</p>

<p>I wanted to lie down a little longer, but every second counted, so I couldn't rest any further.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Zombification….</p></div>

<p>I dismissed the rising System window and got up. "Let's move out."</p>

<p>The basement was filled with mechanical equipment, housing the distribution room and the generator room. Since the hum of engines was a constant background noise, the zombies here were always active, not asleep.</p>

<p>I had to find the distribution room first to get to the generator room.</p>

<p>The narrow corridor was marked with the desperate traces of those who had tried to escape. I had to run like an obstacle course, dodging zombies hidden in the scattered wreckage.</p>

<p>A zombie with its lower half missing crawled on the floor, reaching out a hand. It wasn't trying to attack, just reacting to sound, but it was annoying because I had to be careful not to trip.</p>

<p>Zombies that suddenly appeared from blind spots or burst out of doors also blocked the way.</p>

<p>Here, I just had to do my part. I didn't need to worry about Mo Haein or Kwak Hanmuk's physical abilities, so I focused tightly on avoiding the zombies.</p>

<p>Luckily, knowing exactly when and where things would appear, I could dodge them without issue.</p>

<p>The System window kept popping up to announce the progress of my zombification, which was an unexpected visual obstruction, but I just dismissed them.</p>

<p>"Stop for a second!"</p>

<p>Mo Haein and Kwak Hanmuk immediately stopped.</p>

<p>A TV, perched on a pile of failed barricades, fell right in front of us with a thud.</p>

<p>The screen flickered on.</p>
<br/>
<em><p>"Waaaaaaah!"</p></em><br/>
<p>Cheers and applause erupted from the TV. We ran again, avoiding the zombies drawn by the noise.</p>

<p>After running for a while, I finally saw the sign. </p>
<div class="fieldset"><p>Distribution Room</p></div>
<p>I closed the door as soon as we entered, to keep the zombies outside from getting in.</p>

<p>Not even a few seconds after closing the door, a thud sounded. Then, a series of thump-thump-thump noises.</p>

<p>The zombies that had followed the noise were ramming into the door. There was probably a mountain of zombies forming on the other side.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk gave a short review. "Damn, getting out is gonna be rough."</p>

<p>"Tell me about it…"</p>

<p>I kicked the removable panel under the large control panel covering the wall, a cable pathway. The gap opened with a click. A small key was resting on a bundle of dusty thick cables.</p>

<p>I picked up the key and stood in front of the soundproof door to the right. </p>
<div class="fieldset"><p>Generator Room</p></div>

<p>The engine noise that had been tormenting my ears since entering the basement was strong beyond the door. Mixed in were irregular thump, thump sounds of something being struck.</p>

<p>"7 minutes left. As soon as we open the door, the boss fight starts. Captain Kwak handles the boss, Captain Mo and I will do the repairs."</p>

<p>I had briefed them on the boss fight and repair method before entering the basement.</p>

<p>"Okay. Take these." Kwak Hanmuk pulled three pairs of work gloves and a can of rust remover spray from his sling bag.</p>

<p>These were some of the items I'd asked him to bring from the Green Zone.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Work Gloves: Watch out for the rubber smell! Disposable.</p></div></div>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>Rust Remover Spray: Wipe off 1 minute after spraying.</p></div></div>

<p>Mo Haein took the spray, and we each took a pair of gloves and put them on.</p>

<p>I inserted the key into the locked door. "Opening it."</p>

<p>As I opened the thick soundproof door of the distribution room, the overwhelming noise pounded against my entire body.</p>

<p>In the deepest part of the basement, a massive diesel generator stood tall. Beside the engine, countless hydraulic hoses and fuel lines were tangled together, feeling a bit out of place for a cutting-edge lab.</p>

<p>I felt that strange sensation again, the one from the old jeep and the portable player.</p>

<p>I looked at the figure staggering in front of the engine. The basement boss, the Chef Zombie.</p>

<p>The zombie, wearing a chef's uniform, apron, and hairnet, swayed its body and slammed the generator with its hand. Bang.</p>

<p>It convulsed, rummaged through the generator room, and then slammed the generator again. Bang. Each time the zombie's fist connected, the generator made an uneasy noise.</p>

<p>Ding. The System window appeared.</p>

<p>It seemed to be about the Chef Zombie, so I tried to read it this time, but something was strange.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Zombification is fading. (Current Progress: 1%)</p></div>

<p>The next window followed.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Zombification is fading. (Current Progress: 0%)</p></div>

<div class="systembox"><p>Completely recovered! You have returned to human.</p></div>

<p>"Grrrk…"</p>

<p>I locked eyes with the Chef Zombie.</p>
