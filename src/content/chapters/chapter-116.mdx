<h1>Chapter 116</h1>


<p>After waking up, I sat blankly on the bed for a while.</p>

<p>Even though I had a mountain of work to do, I just sat there doing nothing.</p>

<p>I had a very restless night's sleep. It was because I had a dream for the first time in a long while.</p>

<p>I wished I could have forgotten what the dream was about, but I remembered everything vividly.</p>

<p>However, my memory of the dawn I had briefly woken up from because of the dream was unclear.</p>

<p>Only a vague memory remained that having Dominic with me was a comfort.</p>

<p>In the past, I often saw memories of the past in my dreams.</p>

<p>But since I started playing the creator's game, I haven't had any dreams.</p>

<p>I was so busy playing games that I had forgotten the past for a while.</p>

<p>It was useless to think about it again now.</p>

<p>It was too late to regret it, and I had already regretted it enough...</p>

<p>I struggled to shake off the emotions weighing down my body.</p>

<p>Only after spending some time did my heart feel a little lighter.</p>

<p>I took a slow, deep breath and calmly retold last night.</p>

<p>It was a situation where dregs were banging on the door like crazy.</p>

<p>However, the moment I answered the incoming call, all traces vanished.</p>

<p>When I checked the hallway after the call ended, there was no one there.</p>

<p>At first, I thought it might just be a glitch, but it didn't seem like that was the case.</p>
<br/>
<span class="flashback"><p>—I hope you have sweet dreams!</p></span>
<br/>
<p>Having heard that meaningful greeting and even had a dream, I suspected that Black Block-ssi had intervened in this entire situation.</p>

<p>However, if I confirmed that Black Block-ssi had intervened, a few questions arose.</p>

<ol>

  <li><p>How did he intervene in the trial?</p></li>

</ol>

<p>Black Block-ssi had previously mentioned that he had repeatedly attempted to communicate with me but failed, and only succeeded in establishing a connection in Sla2Plate.</p>

<p>He barely managed to make a single phone call, yet this time he instantly cleared the debris from the hallway and even affected my dream.</p>

<p>‘Are communication and intervention different things?’</p>

<p>It was a difficult part to understand.</p>

<p>Anyway, assuming he did intervene, there was a question that followed.</p>

<ol start="2">

  <li><p>Why did he help me, and why did he make me see the past as a dream?</p></li>

</ol>

<p>Even the act of clearing away the dregs could be interpreted as him helping me as a fan.</p>

<p>Judging by Dominic's way of thinking, one could say that Black Block-ssi simply disposed of it because he disliked the noise during the phone call.</p>

<p>But… making me dream of the past was truly incomprehensible.</p>

<p>I don't know if Black Block-ssi really intended it, but of all things, why that memory?</p>

<em><p>
I felt uneasy.<br/>

I wanted to ensure he could never touch me again.
</p></em>

<p>“…?”</p>

<p>I blinked for a moment.</p>

<p>It felt as if I had experienced a momentary blackout.</p>

<p>However, as if it were just an illusion, my body felt perfectly fine.</p>

<p>I wondered if it was just because I was tired.</p>

<p>I continued my thoughts and formulated various hypotheses.</p>

<p>However, since there was absolutely nothing noteworthy, I eventually gave up on speculating.</p>

<p>I concluded that I needed to be wary of Black Block-ssi and moved on to the third and final point.</p>

<ol start="3">

  <li><p>Why didn't Dominic prevent the intervention of another god?</p></li>

</ol>

<p>This was the part I found most puzzling.</p>

<p>It felt even stranger because, both during our previous communication and because of the method of getting blood on him, I assumed Dominic would dislike Black Block-ssi.</p>

<p>Still, this was a problem where I had a chance of finding an answer.</p>

<p>I just had to ask.</p>

<p>‘I don’t know if he’ll give me the answer I want, though.’</p>

<p>I took a moment to be cautious before calling Dominic.</p>

<p>It was to avoid making the mistake of calling him Mr. Pudding Slime.</p>

<p>“Dominic-ssi.”</p>

<p>Dominic, who had been waiting quietly on my thigh, looked up at me.</p>

<p>He always waits like this without interrupting me whenever I was lost in thought.</p>

<p>Thinking of him as a truly dignified gentleman slime, I asked him. </p>
<p>“When I took the call yesterday, it looked like Black Block-ssi chased away the beings outside the door, is that correct?”</p>

<p>“That is correct.”</p>

<p>“Um… is it okay for me to contact Black Block-ssi?”</p>

<p>“There is still a price to pay him that hasn’t been paid yet.”</p>

<p>Slap.</p>

<p>Dominic jumped up and stuck to my cheek.</p>

<p>He chuckled softly and said.</p>

<p>“Roasted Chestnut. You don’t need to ask me about such trivial things one by one.”</p>

<p>It was a generous remark, but for some reason, it felt like he was telling me not to ask any more questions.</p>

<p>After hesitating, I asked just one more question.</p>

<p>“Regarding the trial of running the restaurant... I don’t remember having a meal with you at the end… Could you tell me what I ate back then?”</p>

<p>“Children’s nutritional supplements.”</p>

<p>“…….”</p>

<p>Did I eat lemon-flavored candy like Je Hyun-oh?</p>

<p>It was absurd.</p>

<p>However, Dominic wasn't a slime that made things up, so it seemed like he had roughly fed me some food that might be helpful.</p>

<p>‘Did that not suit me?’</p>

<p>Having only received more confusing answers, I gave up on asking any further questions.</p>

<p>After offering Dominic a hollow thank you, I got out of bed.</p>

<p>As I prepared to head to the infirmary, I checked the status windows of Kwak Hanmuk and Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk's status window was the same as yesterday.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>&#45; Current Status: Annoyed.</p></div></div>

<p>It was annoying when I checked yesterday morning, and it was annoying this morning as well.</p>

<p>At this point, it was clear that one of the prison guards was making Kwak Hanmuk annoyed.</p>

<p>‘Or it could be CEO Cha.’</p>

<p>He was a very strong candidate.</p>

<p>I closed Kwak Hanmuk's status window and opened Je Hyun-oh's.</p>

<p>He was someone whose whereabouts had been unknown since yesterday.</p>

<p>I scrutinized the status window thoroughly, hoping to find even the slightest clue as to where on earth he was and what he was doing.</p>

<p>Aside from the addition of children's nutritional supplements to his acquired items, there was nothing unusual.</p>

<p>It was when I reached the bottom of the status window, having scanned it all the way through.</p>

<p>In the section indicating his current status, I saw words I could not believe.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>&#45; Current Status: Frustrated.</p></div></div>

<p>Frustrated…?</p>

<p>I looked at it again and again, but the text did not change.</p>

<p>I tried to think calmly.</p>

<p>What could possibly frustrate Je Hyun-oh?</p>

<p>But no matter how much I thought about it, it was not easy to guess.</p>

<p>To begin with, I had never seen him show enough interest in anything to be frustrated.</p>

<p>At most, maybe trials or the Adaptees?</p>

<p>Naturally, he was absolutely not the type to feel emotions like frustration over such things.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>Lieutenant Colonel Je. Where are you?</p></div>

<p>Suddenly worried, I sent the message again, the same one I had been sending since yesterday.</p>

<p>It was inconvenient that messages are one-way communication in times like this.</p>

<p>Just because it was a two-way connection, I don't think Je Hyun-oh would have replied well, though.</p>

<p>‘I need to finish my medical duty quickly and find Lieutenant Colonel Je first.’</p>

<p>I sent a message to Kwak Hanmuk asking him to let me know if he found Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>Once I found Je Hyun-oh, I planned to contact Samra to hear about the situation outside as well.</p>

<p>‘I wonder if he's worried.’</p>

<p>Thinking of Samra, I checked the Tuesday assignments on the weekly schedule.</p>

<div class="fieldset"><div class="systembox_wrap"><p>▸ Tuesday<br/>
☐ Computerize medical records.<br/>
☐ Disinfect medical equipment and inspect hygiene conditions in the infirmary.<br/>
☐ Examine at least six patients during in-person consultations.<br/>
☐ <span class="highlight2">Make sure</span> to conduct the Zone X rounds that were not done on Monday.</p></div></div>

<p>The words ‘make sure’ were attached to the Zone X rounds.</p>

<p>It meant that if I couldn't make the rounds today, it would be a bad ending.</p>

<p>It was exactly what I had expected, as this is what happens when the same mission is failed twice in a row. I figured I should go to the infirmary to finish organizing the computer system and disinfecting and inspecting the equipment first, and then handle the medical examinations of human inmates and the Zone X rounds in order.</p>

<p>I went to work at the infirmary with prison guard Slime.</p>

<p>Then, to organize the computer system, I first located the computer monitor stuck in the large cabinet in the infirmary.</p>

<p>“…Wow.”</p>

<p>I let out a short exclamation of awe.</p>

<p>I had only ever seen thick CRT monitors with a protruding back in archive footage; this was the first time I was seeing one in real life.</p>

<p>When I lifted the heavy monitor and placed it on the desk, it took up more than half the space.</p>

<p>‘The monitor in the new guard training room was a touchscreen.’</p>

<p>Surely, the infirmary isn't the only place like this.</p>

<p>However, the computer did fit in with the overall atmosphere of the prison, so I dismissed my suspicion.</p>

<p>I connected the monitor to the main unit and turned on the power.</p>

<p>The slightly convex screen had large pixels when viewed up close.</p>

<p>The 'computer cleanup' task didn't actually involve organizing data; I just had to play a mini-game.</p>

<p>It was a simple structure where I had to hit the blue letters falling from the sky and dodge the red ones.</p>

<p>It was a bit fast, but not particularly difficult.</p>

<p>I diligently played the mini-game to complete the computer cleanup mission, and I quickly finished the equipment disinfection and hygiene inspection missions as well.</p>

<p>After taking out and eating cereal from my inventory, it was time for the human inmates' in-person consultations.</p>

<p>Fortunately, the guard who came today didn't cause me much trouble, so the consultations went smoothly.</p>

<p>Now, the only thing left was the Zone X rounds.</p>

<p>Normally, there are no rounds on Tuesdays.</p>

<p>However, as I completed the tasks on the weekly schedule, the words 'conduct rounds' appeared.</p>

<p>Today, I had to act on the assumption that Article 4 of the operational manual was false.</p>

<div class="oldpaper"><p>Article 4. Rounds in Zone X take place every <span class="highlight">Monday and Thursday.</span><br/>
<span class="highlight">Rounds are never conducted on any other day.</span> The prison guards accompanying you during rounds must always stand behind you.<br/>
If an accompanying guard walks ahead of you and blocks the way to a specific solitary confinement cell, do not look inside; simply walk past it.<br/>
There is a high probability that the cell contains a "creature" inmate—one left battered and broken by the guards' brutal abuse.<br/>
The moment you see it, you, too, will become trapped in that room.<br/></p></div>

<p>It seemed I just needed to make sure I walked ‘in front’ of the guard.</p>

<p>Normally, two guards accompany the rounds, but today there was only one, perhaps because they were trying to clean up the mess from yesterday.</p>

<p>I headed to Zone X under the guard's supervision.</p>

<p>The iron gate of Zone X, which Je Hyun-oh had vandalized last night, had been hastily repaired by adding a metal plate.</p>

<p>As if to check if it was working properly, the guard held up his pass, and the gate opened normally.</p>

<p>“…….”</p>

<p>Just before stepping inside, I held my breath for a moment.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_pixel"><p>“Listen, Roasted Chestnut! I used to love swimming. Long ago, I used to swim 100km a day. I can’t do that anymore, though.”</p></div>

<div class="bigbox2_pixel"><p>“Haha, aren’t I old and broken? Even if I return to the pod, no one would want to see me swimming.”</p></div>

<p>I told Orca to go back to the prison.</p>

<p>Even though it’s for the sake of the true ending, locking him up in a cramped space again was by no means a pleasant experience.</p>

<p>Clutching my medical bag, I walked ahead of the guard.</p>

<p>Then I checked Cell No. 1 first.</p>

<p>Solitary Cell No. 1 was empty. It seemed the vines had moved safely according to my plan.</p>

<p>I heard the guard grumbling from behind.</p>

<p>“I wonder where on earth No. 1 went… It was so satisfying to beat that bastard.”</p>

<p>Ignoring the guard’s words, I was heading toward Cell No. 2 when...</p>

<p>“Hey, Medic!”</p>

<p>“…Yes?”</p>

<p>The guard raised his fist at me and yelled at me.</p>

<p>“I’m going to collect Neo as soon as the rounds are over today, so hurry up and move if you don’t want to get beaten up.”</p>

<p>I answered, pulling up the corners of my mouth faintly.</p>

<p>“Understood.”</p>

<p>It was time to kill the guard.</p>
