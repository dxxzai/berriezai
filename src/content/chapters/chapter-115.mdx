<h1>Chapter 115</h1>


<p>As soon as he heard my answer, a boisterous burst of laughter erupted.</p>

<p>He was laughing wildly, as if he’d just heard the funniest joke imaginable.</p>

<p>Having been half-dazed from receiving such an unexpected call, I realized only then what I had done.</p>

<p>‘I called him Black Block-ssi.’</p>

<p>I had addressed him by the nickname I’d secretly given him.</p>

<p>He was a foreign deity—a being I assumed held considerable status.</p>

<p>My first thought was whether it was really okay to call such a figure by a bizarre nickname I’d made up on a whim.</p>

<p>I waited for his response, feeling a bit intimidated.</p>

<p>Fortunately, Black Block-ssi didn't seem offended.</p>

<p>In fact, he appeared thoroughly delighted.</p>

<p>—A nickname given personally by the Little Lamb—I am deeply moved and touched as a fan! I like it very much.</p>

<p>It seemed that way because the nickname didn't carry any negative connotations.</p>

<p>The phrase 'Black Block' did have a certain cute ring to it, after all.</p>

<p>I offered him a sincere apology.</p>

<p>“I’m sorry. I didn’t know your name... Could you tell me your name, even if it’s a bit late?”</p>

<p>I asked as politely as possible, but he started laughing heartily again—apparently finding something else amusing.</p>

<p>He stopped laughing fairly quickly, though—perhaps because it wasn't quite as funny as &quot;Black Block.&quot;</p>

<p>—I am ■■ ■■■!</p>

<p>For a split second, his voice became bizarrely distorted and warped.</p>

<p>No, it wasn't a voice.</p>

<p>A chaotic mix of frenzied cheers and applause, blood-curdling screams and weeping, maniacal laughter, and furious shouts shook my brain violently.</p>

<p>I muttered blankly.</p>

<p>“I didn’t catch that...”</p>

<p>At that, he began making sobbing sounds with his mouth again—heuk-heuk-heuk-heuk.</p>
<p>—It seems the Little Lamb cannot hear it yet. That is a great pity.</p>

<p>I felt an urge to hang up the phone.</p>

<p>However, since he had helped me out before, I patiently waited for his whimpering to subside.</p>

<p>Was there some issue arising from a disparity in our status?</p>

<p>Even on the system interface, his name appeared as a blacked-out block.</p>

<p>I had no idea what his name actually was, but it seemed like something I wasn't supposed to know yet.</p>

<p>—Please call me Black Block-ssi!</p>

<p>Amidst all that, he actually wanted to be called 'Black Block.'</p>

<p>Feeling a bit awkward, I addressed him just as he wished.</p>

<p>“Yes, Black Block-ssi.”</p>

<p>Listening to Black Block-ssi’s amused laughter, I glanced toward the door.</p>

<p>It was quiet outside.</p>

<p>That was strange, considering the dregs that roamed at midnight wouldn't simply quiet down on their own.</p>

<p>It was eerie how the silence had fallen the exact moment I answered the call.</p>

<p>For now, I needed to focus on the conversation.</p>

<p>Reminding myself that this was a call demanding debt repayment, I spoke up.</p>

<p>“I’m afraid the meal isn't ready yet. I’ve suddenly found myself in a new location... and there aren't any ingredients here worthy of serving you, Black Block-ssi.”</p>

<p>That was the honest truth.</p>

<p>Since Cell-X was set on an island in the middle of the ocean, the prison cafeteria was poorly stocked.</p>

<p>I was currently eating expired cereal myself, after all.</p>

<p>If I were to cook something here, I’d basically be serving cereal soaked in seawater.</p>

<p>—I understand perfectly, Little Lamb! Joy is found in the waiting.</p>

<p>Black Block-ssi readily accepted my excuse.</p>

<p>However, it seemed I would genuinely have to cook for him once I got out of Cell-X.</p>

<p>Making him wait too long likely wouldn't end well. </p>
<p>“Thank you for understanding, Black Block-ssi.”</p>

<p>As I spoke, I suddenly realized something.</p>

<p>The voice on the other end sounded much clearer than when we had spoken via Sla2Plate before.</p>

<p>Although there was still some static, the call quality was actually quite decent.</p>

<p>It was a far cry from before, when the audio was so garbled it sounded like a transmission from hundreds of light-years away in deep space.</p>

<p>It was as if the distance between us had closed, making communication smoother...</p>

<p>‘Ugh.’</p>

<p>It was a chilling thought, even though it had crossed my own mind.</p>

<p>I decided to just ask about his location directly.</p>

<p>“Black Block-ssi, are you still there... at PSR? Is that where you are?”</p>

<p>— You mean PSR B1919+21! Not anymore. That isn't a place where one can stay for long.</p>

<p>I had assumed that aliens could just live anywhere in space, but apparently, that wasn't the case.</p>

<p>Or perhaps Black Block-ssi was picky—like Dominic—and had strict standards for where he lived?</p>

<p>If he was a high-status alien with a class-conscious mindset similar to Dominic’s, that seemed like the most plausible explanation.</p>

<p>“Then where are you now? I was wondering if your location would allow me to send you some food...”</p>

<p>—Ah! The connection is about to cut off!</p>

<p>Black Block-ssi exclaimed, sounding truly regretful. Before I could even get a word in, he quickly said his goodbyes.</p>

<p>—Let’s try to communicate a bit longer next time! As a fan of yours, I’ll be keeping a close watch on you, Little Lamb.</p>

<p>Just before the connection ended, Black Block-ssi offered a different kind of farewell.</p>

<p>—I hope you have sweet dreams! Until next time...</p>

<p>Beep... beep... beep... The call, which had begun so abruptly, ended just as suddenly.</p>

<p>It felt like I had been swept up in a whirlwind. I held the receiver for a moment before putting it down.</p>

<p>It was still quiet outside the door. After a moment of hesitation, I opened it.</p>

<p>There was no one in the hallway.</p>

<p>All the dregs that should have been swarming there had vanished.</p>

<p>‘Is this a glitch, too?’</p>

<p>I surveyed the empty hallway for a while before closing the door again.</p>

<p>Then, I called out to the Pudding Slime, who had likely been patiently waiting for my call to end.</p>

<p>“Dominic-ssi.”</p>

<p>“Yes, Roasted Chestnut.”</p>

<p>I had expected a scolding, but his response was so calm that I didn't quite know what to say.</p>

<p>I had assumed he would dislike me talking to Black Block-ssi.</p>

<p>After all, Dominic had absolutely loathed the blood-based technique Black Block-ssi had taught me.</p>

<p>And there was something else, too.</p>

<p>Something Dominic had said about Black Block-ssi...</p>

<p>Was it back at the restaurant in Sla2Plate...?</p>

<p>A hazy memory clouded my mind.</p>

<p>Feeling a touch of dizziness, I sat down on the bed.</p>

<p>Dominic spoke to me with concern.</p>

<p>“It is very late.”</p>

<p>He suggested I go to sleep, noting that staying up any later wouldn't be good for my health.</p>

<p>I lay down, harboring a lingering sense of unease about why Dominic hadn't said anything.</p>

<p>Come to think of it, Dominic had been unusually quiet during the new prison guard training, too...</p>

<p>My mind was a jumble, yet sleep was washing over me.</p>

<p>I wanted to talk to Dominic, but I couldn't quite get my thoughts in order.</p>

<p>Dominic climbed up onto my chest. The soft, swaying motion against my chest felt like the gentle lapping of waves.</p>

<p>It felt as though I were being soothed into sleep, making me feel even drowsier.</p>

<p>Blinking my heavy eyelids, I quickly drifted off.</p>

<p>Just before falling asleep, a thought crossed my mind.</p>

<p>‘Why did he tell me to have sweet dreams?’</p>

<p>Was it because it was past midnight?</p>

<p>Black Block-ssi didn't seem like the type to be so considerate.</p>

<p>But given that he was barely managing to communicate through the trial, he couldn't possibly do anything to me right now...</p>

<p>Or could he?</p>

<hr class="calibre3"></hr>

<p>I know this is a dream.</p>

<p>Yet, the moment where a memory replays feels incredibly vivid.</p>

<p>It feels not like a dream, but reality—as if I’ve been transported back to that very moment.</p>

<p>‘My’ left arm is the first thing that catches my eye.</p>

<p>Bandages, starting from the palm of the left hand, wrap tightly up past the wrist to the middle of the forearm.</p>

<p>Wrapped in those white compression bandages, ‘I’ know the truth.</p>

<p>That it will never function properly again.</p>

<p>I see a man standing opposite me.</p>

<p>He, too, has been unable to tear his gaze away from ‘my’ left hand; now, he speaks.</p>
<br />
<em><p>“Hyung.”</p></em>
<br />
<p>‘I’ look at him, and he looks at me.</p>

<p>He asks me:</p>
<br />
<em><p>“Why did you do it?”</p></em>
<br />
<p>‘I’ cannot say a word.</p>

<p>He lunges at me.</p>

<p>He grabs me by the collar and shouts.</p>
<br />
<p>
<em>“I asked why you did it, you fucker!</em>”
</p>
<br />
<p>At that blood-curdling shout, ‘I’ part my lips.</p>

<p>I know the truth.</p>

<p>That this is just a dream, and the past cannot be changed.</p>

<p>Even so, I want to clamp my hand over ‘my’ mouth.</p>

<p>Don’t say it.</p>
<p>But ‘I’ speak.</p>
<br />
<em><p>“You wanted to win, too.”</p></em>
<br />
<p>The words finally spill out.</p>

<p>Upon hearing this, the man’s face hardens.</p>
<br />
<em><p>“…Me?”</p></em>
<br />
<p>He asks back slowly, as if unable to believe it.</p>
<br />
<em><p>“I wanted to win?”</p></em>
<br />
<p>The hand gripping my collar twitches, then slowly loses its strength.</p>

<p>He lets go.</p>

<p>Then, he looks down at me for a long time.</p>

<p>I hang my head.</p>

<p>Perhaps it is because this part is missing from my memory.</p>

<p>Even in the dream, his face blurs at this very moment.</p>

<p>Just as I did back then, the ‘me’ in the dream cannot lift my head for a long time.</p>
<br />
<em><p>“Han Goyo.”</p></em>
<br />
<p>Finally, he speaks.</p>

<p>The name he uses to address me feels unfamiliar; he rarely calls me that.</p>

<p>I slowly lift my head.</p>

<p>And upon seeing his face...</p>
<br />
<em><p>“…….”</p></em>
<br />
<p>Only then do I realize my mistake.</p>

<p>'My' eyes waver.</p>

<p>But the realization comes too late.</p>

<p>He is smiling a hollow, defeated smile.</p>

<p>He says to 'me'.</p>
<br />
<em><p>“Disgusting.”</p></em>
<hr class="calibre3"></hr>
<p>I woke up from the dream.</p>

<p>“…….”</p>

<p>It was night. It was still dark outside, and I was alone.</p>

<p>I quietly curled up.</p>

<p>Shivering, I pulled the blanket over myself, but the cold did not go away.</p>

<p>I tried to recall where I was and what I had been doing.</p>

<p>This was not the small studio apartment where I had been hiding.</p>

<p>I had entered Cell-X, been assigned the role of medic, and had fallen asleep—and now woken up—in the prison quarters. I kept reassuring myself that this was reality, not a dream.</p>

<p>But... I found it a bit hard to breathe.</p>

<p>I didn't recall the medics' quarters being this cold, yet the trembling of my body simply wouldn't stop.</p>

<p>A soft, yielding sensation touched my cheek.</p>

<p>The one pressed against my damp cheek asked in a low voice,</p>

<p>“Was it a nightmare?”</p>

<p>I tried to speak, but no sound came out. I simply shook my head slightly from side to side.</p>

<p>Dominic asked nothing more. Instead, he simply stayed by my side.</p>

<p>He remained quiet even when I impulsively grabbed the soft slime and hugged it tightly.</p>

<p>Only when my trembling had subsided and my damp cheek had dried did he gently whisper to me,</p>

<p>“It’s alright, Roasted Chestnut.”</p>

<p>He offered me a comfort I couldn't quite fathom.</p>

<p>“It is simply because you were born so recently. You may be swayed by these lingering traces now while you are young, but soon enough, they will amount to nothing.”</p>

<p>His gentle whispers reached me softly.</p>

<p>“You will forget it all. There will be no more pain.”</p>

<em><p>But I did not want to forget.</p></em>

<p>“Look at this. Haven’t you become quite adept at speaking as a god?”</p>

<p>He spoke softly, as if soothing a fretful child.</p>

<p>“You will be fine soon. Truly.”</p>

<p>I did not understand his words.</p>

<p>Yet, listening to that low whisper made me feel as though I really would be all right...</p>

<p>Slowly, I closed my eyes and drifted back to sleep.</p>
