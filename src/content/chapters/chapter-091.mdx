<h1>Chapter 91</h1>

<p>I remained tucked under CEO Cha's arm, taking in this unbelievable situation.</p>

<p>There was only one way to describe it.</p>

<p>'This is insane.'</p>

<p>After hearing Je Hyun-oh's words, CEO Cha let out a short laugh.</p>

<p>"Lieutenant Colonel Hyun-oh... you're pretty confident, aren't you?"</p>

<p>The two of them fell silent, simply staring at one another.</p>

<p>I knew this wasn't the time, but I still found myself looking back and forth between Je Hyun-oh and CEO Cha.</p>

<p>I just couldn't believe I was seeing the two of them standing together.</p>

<p>They were similar in some ways, yet completely different.</p>

<p>In the Archive world, they'd almost never been in the same place.</p>

<p>Putting two people with absurdly overwhelming individual combat abilities into the same Trial would have been a waste.</p>

<p>Even when the government-run Trial Response Agency and the private company Paseong cooperated, Je Hyun-oh and CEO Cha never cleared Trials together.</p>

<p>Because of that, they rarely ever met.</p>

<p>Yet they were both keenly aware of the other's existence.</p>

<p>They also understood that, in order to stop the ever-increasing number of Trials, they would ultimately need each other.</p>

<p>'Though... Lieutenant Colonel Je doesn't seem to realize that.'</p>

<p>Right now, he looked as though the only thing on his mind was chopping CEO Cha into pieces with his metal hand.</p>

<p>CEO Cha had certainly done something outrageous.</p>

<p>But under no circumstances could he be seriously injured or killed.</p>

<p>Not because he was my favorite.</p>

<p>If something happened to a Adaptee like CEO Cha, humanity's ability to defend against Trials would take a catastrophic blow.</p>

<p>There wasn't another Adaptee anywhere in the world who could compare to him.</p>

<p>He'd literally torn open the side of the Trial Response Agency building...</p>

<p>Even so, Director Jang Seokyun would never consider punishing him so severely that he could no longer enter Trials.</p>

<p>Maybe I'd been stealing too many glances.</p>

<p>The eyes of both Je Hyun-oh and CEO Cha, who had been watching each other, turned toward me.</p>

<p>I flinched and lowered my gaze.</p>

<p>Unfortunately...</p>

<p>My timing couldn't have been worse.</p>

<p>"Cough...! Cough!"</p>

<p>A violent coughing fit suddenly overtook me.</p>

<p>The air was still thick with dust.</p>

<p>It felt a little unfair.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh had a gas mask.</p>

<p>CEO Cha had his mask.</p>

<p>Even Seomun Chan didn't seem bothered.</p>

<p>It really felt like I was the only one who'd inhaled all the dust.</p>

<p>After noisily coughing by myself, an awkward silence settled over everyone.</p>

<p>My voice came out hoarse as I apologized.</p>

<p>"Sorry for interrupting your conversation. I inhaled a lot of dust..."</p>

<p>That was when loud laughter rang out.</p>

<p>Though distorted by the voice modulator, it was unmistakably laughter.</p>

<p>I blankly looked up at CEO Cha.</p>

<p>After laughing aloud for a while, he looked down at me.</p>

<p>"We really need to get you to Paseong."</p>

<p>For just that one moment, I wanted to sign the contract.</p>

<p>I wanted to know what expression he was making behind that mask.</p>

<p>Click.</p>

<p>The soft sound of metal shifting echoed through the air.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh had moved his metal hand.</p>

<p>CEO Cha immediately stopped laughing.</p>

<p>He sighed openly.</p>

<p>"You're so impatient."</p>

<p>Then he drew his sword.</p>

<p>Daeil Yeorae emerged from its sheath, scattering brilliant white light.</p>

<p>It seemed the two of them were really going to fight.</p>

<p>CEO Cha had already used an incredibly expensive consumable item just to avoid Samra's detection.</p>

<p>If it was the item I thought it was, it wasn't even something money could buy.</p>

<p>CEO Cha himself would have preferred not to clash with Je Hyun-oh or any of the Agency's elite Adaptees.</p>

<p>That's why he'd been willing to use something so valuable.</p>

<p>But if a confrontation became unavoidable...</p>

<p>'He never intended to back down...'</p>

<p>My head started aching.</p>

<p>Wasn't there any way to avoid this?</p>

<p>Probably not.</p>

<p>There was no need to even mention Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>And after coming this far, there was no way CEO Cha would simply accept a loss and walk away empty-handed.</p>

<p>At this rate...</p>

<p>One of them might actually have to die before today was over.</p>

<p>"CEO Cha. That's enough."</p>

<p>A familiar voice interrupted.</p>

<p>Director Jang Seokyun.</p>

<p>He stood beside Samra, accompanied by soldiers from the Trial Response Agency.</p>

<p>A step behind them stood Won Taebeom.</p>

<p>I immediately checked on Samra and Won Taebeom.</p>

<p>Fortunately, neither seemed seriously injured.</p>

<p>Samra's arms had already returned to normal.</p>

<p>The soldiers accompanying Director Jang Seokyun were fully armed, but none of them aimed their weapons at CEO Cha.</p>

<p>Looking utterly exhausted, Director Jang Seokyun spoke.</p>

<p>"This has become nothing more than a meaningless waste of effort."</p>

<p>But CEO Cha showed no intention whatsoever of putting Daeil Yeorae away.</p>

<p>Neither did Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>Director Jang Seokyun gestured toward me.</p>

<p>"Trainee Han doesn't appear to be in very good condition."</p>

<p>Samra looked directly at CEO Cha.</p>

<p>"Han Goyo regained consciousness only fourteen days after collapsing. He is currently suffering from paralysis in his lower body, along with significantly impaired physical functions."</p>

<p>His gaze almost looked like a glare.</p>

<p>"CEO Cha. Is this truly the outcome you intended?"</p>

<p>"As if."</p>

<p>CEO Cha pointed his sword at Samra.</p>

<p>"Samra. You know exactly why I went this far, don't you? Director Jang Seokyun knows too."</p>

<p>I desperately wished someone would explain it to me, too.</p>

<p>But that was all CEO Cha said.</p>

<p>Director Jang Seokyun's arrival had just begun to calm the situation...</p>

<p>When everything started falling apart again.</p>

<p>Still hanging beneath CEO Cha's arm, a thought suddenly occurred to me.</p>

<p>'...Isn't this actually a pretty good opportunity?'</p>

<p>At the moment, I basically had three choices.</p>
<br/>
<ol><li><p>Freelancer</p>
</li><li><p>Paseong</p>
</li><li><p>Trial Response Agency</p></li></ol><br/>
<p>Option one was objectively unrealistic.</p>

<p>Neither Paseong nor the TRA would simply leave me alone.</p>

<p>And I also needed the protection of an organization.</p>

<p>According to what Samra had told me before, information about me had already spread overseas.</p>

<p>Maybe I was worrying too much.</p>

<p>But kidnapping wasn't completely impossible anymore.</p>

<p>That left options two and three.</p>

<p>And right now...</p>

<p>Paseong had just handed me an enormous bargaining chip.</p>

<p>By tearing open the side of the TRA headquarters, they'd publicly demonstrated exactly how important "Han Goyo" was to them.</p>

<p>On top of that, I'd fulfilled my agreement with the Agency by closing three Trials within a month.</p>

<p>Officially speaking, my trainee contract had already ended.</p>

<p>'Which means... I can legally play both sides.'</p>

<p>Neither Paseong nor the TRA had wanted me to end up with a real choice.</p>

<p>But circumstances had gradually spiraled until we'd somehow arrived here.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh had appeared at exactly the right moment.</p>

<p>That made it impossible for CEO Cha to force me into signing.</p>

<p>Which meant, for the first time, I had a chance to squeeze the maximum possible benefit out of both organizations.</p>

<p>The problem was how to pull it off.</p>

<p>Anything ordinary would simply be ignored.</p>

<p>After thinking for a moment, I timidly raised one hand.</p>

<p>"Um..."</p>

<p>Everyone's eyes turned toward me.</p>

<p>With everyone's attention focused on me, I put on the most pitiful expression I could manage.</p>

<p>"Well... I have to stay alive before I can sign with either Paseong or the Agency, right? I assume we all agree on that?"</p>

<p>Nobody answered.</p>

<p>I decided to treat the silence as agreement and pretended to feel a little worse.</p>

<p>"I think... I'm dying."</p>

<p>I had just started explaining that if Lieutenant Colonel Je and CEO Cha fought, I'd probably die in the crossfire.</p>

<p>Then, unbelievably...</p>

<p>The moment I said it, I actually started feeling worse.</p>

<p>"Roasted Chestnut."</p>

<p>I heard Dominic call my name.</p>

<p>There was genuine surprise in his voice.</p>

<p>Come to think of it, I'd only regained consciousness after fourteen days.</p>

<p>Maybe I really had overexerted myself—</p>

<p>"...?"</p>

<p>Drip.</p>

<p>Something suddenly surged upward from deep inside me.</p>

<p>Before I could stop it, it spilled from my mouth.</p>

<p>I hurriedly covered my lips with my hand.</p>

<p>It felt wet.</p>

<p>Slowly, I lowered my hand.</p>

<p>My palm was stained bright red.</p>

<p>That was the last thing I saw before I lost consciousness.</p>

<p>I slowly opened my eyes.</p>

<p>It wasn't the familiar hospital room at TRA; it was an unfamiliar one.</p>

<p>‘Ah, TRA’s side got torn open.’</p>

<p>Since a massive hole had been punched in the side of the building, it would be unusable for the time being.</p>

<p>“…….”</p>

<p>After briefly thinking about the hospital, a chill slowly crept over me.</p>

<p>It was because of a dream that had surfaced vividly in my mind.</p>

<p>In that dream, I saw a memory I had forgotten.</p>

<p>In the past, I hadn't realized the Creator was acting like a madman right before my eyes.</p>

<p>I had a hunch that it hadn't been just that one instance.</p>

<p>Although the memories didn't come back to me immediately, there must have been other moments that should have seemed suspicious.</p>

<p>Yet, back then, I had simply accepted the bizarre phenomena I experienced while watching the Creator’s broadcasts and playing his games without a second thought.</p>

<p>Without a shred of suspicion.</p>

<p>‘Was it that I failed to be suspicious, or was I being manipulated?’</p>

<p>If the Creator was a god-like being, it was entirely plausible.</p>

<p>After all, it wouldn't have been difficult for him to sway the human mind.</p>

<p>After all, I had completely forgotten about it until now.</p>

<p>I didn't know why it had suddenly appeared in my dream, but...</p>

<p>As I retraced my memories, a sudden, chilling shiver ran down my spine.</p>

<p>The Trial had begun four years ago when a meteorite struck Earth.</p>

<p>And yet, in the past... I hadn't even been aware of the fact that a meteorite had fallen.</p>

<p>It was strange, even considering I had been cutting myself off from outside news at the time.</p>

<p>Even if I had been manipulated by the Creator, I hadn't started playing the Archive games until two years later.</p>

<p>It was even the case when I met Samra after HapFactory shut down. Even at the moment I realized—through Samra—that the events I had experienced in the game were actually unfolding in reality, I hadn't questioned it at all.</p>

<p>Only now did I recognize that my way of thinking was abnormal.</p>

<p>Upon realizing that my memories and perceptions were distorted, a fundamental doubt shook me to the core.</p>

<p>‘Am I even in my right mind?’</p>

<p>I couldn't even tell what had gone wrong or where it had all started to unravel.</p>

<p>“…….”</p>

<p>I slowly exhaled and wiped my face, which was slick with cold sweat.</p>

<em><p>If I could fully assimilate ■■■■, I might regain even more of what I had lost.</p></em>

<p>“Roasted Chestnut, how are you feeling?”</p>

<p>Dominic’s voice helped me barely regain my composure.</p>

<p>“I had no idea you were this frail, Roasted Chestnut.”</p>

<p>Listening to the Pudding Slime’s lament, I recalled the chaotic mess of a situation just before I’d blacked out.</p>

<p>‘Come to think of it... I wonder if things got sorted out?’</p>

<p>I had passed out immediately without managing to persuade anyone properly, so I had no clue how things had turned out.</p>

<p>It wouldn't even surprise me to learn that the hospital room I was in belonged to Paseong.</p>

<p>‘But why is there no one around again?’</p>

<p>I was groaning as I struggled to lift my upper body and check if my legs would move...</p>

<p>When the hospital room door opened.</p>

<p>Thinking it might be Samra, I turned my head.</p>

<p>“Trainee Han. You’re finally awake.”</p>

<p>Director Jang Seokyun was walking into the room.</p>
