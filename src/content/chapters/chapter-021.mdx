<h1>Chapter 21</h1>

<p>I'd been spacing out, but I flinched and snapped back to reality.</p>

<p>It was because of the sharp sound coming from Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>The metal hand moving at a leisurely pace was waiting for my answer.</p>

<p>I said what I had to say.</p>

<p>"...I can clear it."</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh's head tilted to the side. He seemed puzzled that I was still guaranteeing a clear.</p>

<p>It was a response that, as his suspicion suggested, had a high chance of being a lie.</p>

<p>But what did it matter? By the time my answer turned out to be a lie, we'd all be zombies anyway.</p>

<p>Well, Je Hyun-oh wouldn't get infected with DeZonDeal, so he'd be the only one left fine.</p>

<p>I pictured him slicing my zombie body to pieces.</p>

<p>'Well, zombies can't feel pain — so that's fine.'</p>

<p>Feeling a little calmer, I went back to basics.</p>

<p>It had crossed my mind that the researcher developing the vaccine might be the injured one.</p>

<p>But unlike Mo Haein's worry, it hadn't occurred to me that the vaccine researcher might already be dead.</p>

<p>Because the Trial fundamentally followed the framework of a game.</p>

<p>Here's how I saw the Trial:</p>

<ol>
<br/>
  <li><p>The Trial is a virtual space built based on the Archive games I'd played.</p></li>

  <li><p>Games have a minimum framework to allow progress.</p></li>
  <br />
</ol>

<p>Thinking with those premises in mind, the conclusion was one thing:</p>
<br />
<ol start='3'>
  <li><p>The Trial also has a minimum framework to allow progress.</p></li>
</ol>
<br/>


<p>Of course, there are cases where the researcher dies while the Player is meeting them and progressing.</p>

<p>'That's a Bad Ending.'</p>

<p>But a situation where the researcher meant to develop the vaccine dies before even meeting the Player — that goes against the game's framework.</p>

<p>It's no different from Smiley the NPC dying alone in HapFactory.</p>

<p>In short, it was an impossible situation.</p>

<p>There was no chance Je Hyun-oh, who'd entered first, had already met the researcher either.</p>

<p>Because Je Hyun-oh also knew the clear conditions — there was no reason for him to touch the researcher.</p>

<p>Unless he'd been killing an extra zombie in that time instead.</p>

<p>Then another question struck me.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh hated the Trial.</p>

<p>He hated staying in the Trial for long, so he always aimed for the shortest clear time.</p>

<p>Yet even though he was in a situation where he couldn't even clear it now...</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh was acting too normal.</p>

<p>Given his tendencies, he should be doing something crazy — but instead, he was acting like he was having fun.</p>

<p>'There's no way Je Hyun-oh knows something I don't.'</p>

<p>I couldn't follow the thought process of a madman at all.</p>

<p>Putting my questions about Je Hyun-oh aside for now, I looked at the researcher zombie again.</p>

<p>Something I hadn't noticed before caught my eye.</p>

<p>The zombie was still banging its head against the glass door.</p>

<p>Even though me and Je Hyun-oh were talking right next to it, it didn't care.</p>

<p>That meant it had already locked onto a target.</p>

<p>I looked beyond the glass door. At first, hidden in the darkness, I saw a person.</p>

<p>A woman, covering her mouth and crying silently, looked at me with trembling eyes.</p>

<p>Her face, soaked with tears, seemed around my age.</p>

<p>An ID card hung around her neck. She was wearing a researcher's coat.</p>

<div class="fieldset"><p>Yi Gaon</p></div>

<p>She was the essential condition for the True Ending I'd been trying to find — "the researcher's younger sibling."</p>

<p>Now I understood the situation.</p>

<p>The True Ending of DeZonDeal is the vaccine production ending. The other normal endings only give clues for vaccine production and end there.</p>

<p>The lone surviving researcher worries about their sibling's life throughout the vaccine development process, helped by the Player.</p>

<p>And they tell the Player stories about their sibling.</p>

<p>Trivial stories — how the sibling joined the lab as a research intern and worked alongside them, how their parents died early so they raised the sibling alone, that kind of thing.</p>

<p>But they never give a quest to find the sibling — and that's the trap.</p>

<p>The Player has to use the researcher's stories as hints and find the sibling on their own.</p>

<p>A so-called Hidden Quest.</p>

<p>If you find the surviving sibling, you can proceed on the True Ending route.</p>

<p>If the sibling is dead or turned into a zombie, you're forced into a normal ending.</p>

<p>Since some time had passed since Je Hyun-oh entered DeZonDeal, I'd been anxious that the sibling might have died in the meantime.</p>

<p>But instead, the researcher had become a zombie.</p>

<p>Probably when Je Hyun-oh swept through the lab's zombies, the researcher went out looking for their sibling.</p>

<p>And in the process, they must have met other surviving researchers.</p>

<p>They found the sibling — but then got bitten by a zombie and was abandoned by the other survivors.</p>

<p>'The useless-looking sibling was probably abandoned too.'</p>

<p>The researcher, bitten and left alone with the sibling, must have made a choice. And this was the result of that choice.</p>

<p>Shoving the sibling into the Laboratory Director's Office — the safest place in the lab, but not too remote, good for dealing with outside situations.</p>

<p>Tying their own limbs. Putting a gag in their own mouth.</p>

<p>They couldn't bring themselves to tell the sibling to kill them. That would be too horrible a request to make.</p>

<p>"Grrr..."</p>

<p>The gagged zombie slammed its crushed-skull head against the glass door again.</p>

<p>I remembered what the researcher had told me in the game.</p>

<div class="bigbox2_pixel"><p>"Roasted Chestnut-nim, it's my younger sibling. They're so spoiled. They won't even call me oppa — just my name. And when they're in a bad mood, they just yell 'Hey!' first."</p></div>

<div class="bigbox2_pixel"><p>"They joined the lab as an intern because of me... If it weren't for me, they could've been in a safer zone."</p></div>
<div class="bigbox2_pixel"><p>"They're smarter than me, you know. I already got their consent in advance."</p></div>

<div class="bigbox2_pixel"><p>"I just want them to live. If they can hold on until I make the vaccine..."</p></div>

<p>I'd thought I could finish with a happy ending. Like in the game.</p>

<p>I wanted to start over — but I couldn't.</p>

<p>Not only was there no time to re-enter, but the moment I cleared it through another method and left, Mo Haein — an infected — would be executed.</p>

<p>'If only this were a game.'</p>

<p>Then I'd just quit and start over from the beginning.</p>

<p>As I was indulging in impossible thoughts, a cold chill ran through me.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh, standing beside me, was raising his metal hand to deal with the zombie.</p>

<p>"Lieutenant Colonel Je, please wait."</p>

<p>I moved in front of Je Hyun-oh. Blocking the view of the woman looking this way with frightened eyes, I said:</p>

<p>"That female researcher is an important NPC for clearing. She's the vaccine developer's younger sister."</p>

<p>I continued, looking at my reflection in his black goggles.</p>

<p>"Even if she's a zombie now, killing family right in front of her might create a hostile relationship."</p>

<p>Fortunately, Je Hyun-oh listened to me.</p>

<p>Though it seemed less because he trusted my judgment and more because he wanted to see how I'd navigate this obstacle.</p>

<p>With Je Hyun-oh's cooperation, I tied up the researcher zombie more securely.</p>

<p>Then I knocked on the reinforced glass door.</p>

<p>Knock knock.</p>

<p>The gaunt body jumped.</p>

<p>"I'm a soldier. I'm here to help — please open the door."</p>

<p>But she was still wary. After a moment's hesitation, I said a name.</p>

<p>"...I know Yi Seon-ssi."</p>

<p>The name of the researcher who'd become a zombie.</p>

<p>The moment she heard her oppa's name, Yi Gaon's eyes changed.</p>

<p>She stood up abruptly and immediately unlocked the reinforced glass door.</p>

<p>I pulled the door open. Through the widening gap, a cracked, exhausted voice spilled out.</p>

<p>"Y-you know my... oppa? How... my oppa doesn't have friends..."</p>

<p>"We met briefly for work once."</p>

<p>"Ah..."</p>

<p>I pulled Yi Gaon out and took out a drink and a chocolate bar from my backpack.</p>

<p>"Wait over there for a moment."</p>

<p>After sending her to a corner, I isolated the zombified Yi Seon in the Laboratory Director's Office.</p>

<p>Then I piled up barricades in front of the glass door.</p>

<p>The Laboratory Director's Office was quite sturdy — he wouldn't be able to get out until someone removed the barricades from outside.</p>

<p>"Hnng, huff..."</p>

<p>In the meantime, Yi Gaon was sniffling with tears and snot running, munching on the chocolate bar and drink.</p>

<p>She must have been trapped alone for quite a long time. It was amazing she'd held out this long.</p>

<p>"Thank you..."</p>

<p>Yi Gaon thanked me.</p>

<p>Then she tried to thank Je Hyun-oh too — but the moment she saw his metal hand, she froze in fear, unable to say a word.</p>

<p>I turned to look at Je Hyun-oh, and I deeply understood how Yi Gaon felt.</p>

<div class="hyunoh"><p>^ ^</p></div>

<p>Even if he hid the metal hand, it wouldn't solve the problem.</p>

<p>Not to mention the goggles with emoticons, the brutal combat uniform, and the gas mask.</p>

<p>If I saw someone walking around like that on the street, I'd avoid them too.</p>

<p>...Actually, I still wanted to avoid him. I just wasn't in a position to.</p>

<p>"Can you explain what happened?"</p>

<p>The moment she heard my question, Yi Gaon burst into tears again. Fresh tears added to the dried tear tracks on her face.</p>

<p>I handed her another pack of jelly. She sobbed loudly as she ate it.</p>

<p>"My oppa, hic, my oppa..."</p>

<p>The explanation that followed was pretty much what I'd expected.</p>

<p>The surviving researchers had used Yi Seon thoroughly.</p>

<p>Using the excuse of turning a blind eye to his younger sibling — just an intern — taking shelter with them in the confidential lab space, they drove Yi Seon to the most dangerous places.</p>

<p>Yi Seon was half-forced to scrounge for food and supplies.</p>

<p>And the moment he was bitten by a zombie, he was immediately abandoned with his sibling.</p>

<p>Yi Gaon did everything she could to save Yi Seon as he turned into a zombie.</p>

<p>The researcher Mo Haein had met — that was Yi Gaon.</p>

<p>Yi Gaon went all the way to the Yellow Zone to get medical supplies, but there was no way to reverse Yi Seon's zombification once it was in progress.</p>

<p>All methods failed, and in the end, she had to watch her oppa turn into a zombie right before her eyes.</p>

<p>I waited for Yi Gaon's sobbing to subside a little before asking.</p>

<p>"Did Yi Seon-ssi leave any information about the vaccine?"</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>"I'm sorry. I know this is a difficult situation. But..."</p>

<p>"I don't know anything like that!"</p>

<p>Yi Gaon suddenly raised her voice. Then, startled by her own action, she immediately apologized.</p>

<p>"I'm sorry... But I really don't know."</p>

<p>"I see."</p>

<p>"Even if I did, what would be the point?"</p>

<p>The tears that had just stopped began to well up again.</p>

<p>"My oppa is already a zombie."</p>

<p>I looked at her silently. Thoughts raced through my mind.</p>

<p>I remembered the quest window I'd seen at the broadcasting station.</p>

<p>'Media literacy...'</p>

<p>The ability to filter the reliability of information.</p>

<p>It didn't take long to make my judgment.</p>

<p>"Yi Gaon-ssi."</p>

<p>"Sob, hic, yes?"</p>

<p>"You know how to make the vaccine, don't you?"</p>

<p>At my question, silence fell for a moment. Je Hyun-oh, who'd been watching like it was none of his business, leaned in with interest.</p>

<p>Yi Gaon answered, flustered.</p>

<p>"I really don't know... And I'm not a full researcher — I'm just an intern..."</p>

<p>This time, I cut her off.</p>

<p>"You do know."</p>

<p>What had felt off to me was the part where Yi Gaon said she'd 'gone to the Yellow Zone to get medical supplies.'</p>

<p>The lab, being a place that handled viruses, was well-stocked with high-level medical supplies.</p>

<p>There was no need to go all the way to the Yellow Zone — yet Yi Gaon had risked her life to go there.</p>

<p>There could only be one reason.</p>

<p>"Because you went to the Yellow Zone to get vaccine ingredients."</p>

<p>Yi Gaon's face turned pale.</p>

<p>I pulled up the quest again.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap_j"><p>◆Main Quest: Search the Laboratory and find the surviving vaccine developer.</p></div></div>

<p>The quest was already pointing clearly.</p>

<p>Find the 'vaccine developer.'</p>

<p>Whether they were a lab researcher or an intern didn't matter. As long as they were 'someone who could develop the vaccine,' the condition was met.</p>

<p>At that moment, a ding sound rang out and the System Window updated.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>You have found the surviving vaccine developer!</p></div>

<p>It was a System Window confirming that my judgment was correct.</p>

<p>'Could've told me sooner.'</p>

<p>It had waited until I was certain, then announced the quest success like a congratulations for getting the right answer.</p>

<p>At this rate, the System Window wasn't much — might as well call it an alarm.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>You—</p></div>

<p>I closed the System Window before it could display any additional messages. Then I looked down at Yi Gaon.</p>

<p>She couldn't say anything. Her face was pale, her fists clenched tight.</p>

<p>Her expression was resolute — as if she'd never give up information about the vaccine even if a knife were held to her throat.</p>

<p>But I didn't plan to threaten her. Instead, I asked a simple question.</p>

<p>"Doesn't it feel unfair? Your oppa turned into a zombie."</p>

<p>"..."</p>

<p>Yi Gaon, who'd been staring at the floor, slowly raised her head.</p>

<p>"I'll help you get revenge."</p>

<p>Her pupils trembled.</p>

<p>"...How?"</p>

<p>Fear laced her voice as she asked back. The researchers must have bullied her quite a bit.</p>

<p>Yi Gaon seemed to think the surviving researchers were incredibly powerful and dangerous, so I spread my hand and pointed to the side.</p>

<p>Following my hand, Yi Gaon's lips parted.</p>

<p>Not two researchers — even two platoons of heavily armed soldiers couldn't handle this being.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh was there.</p>

<p>"I'll deal with them however you want. Kill them, let them live, turn them into zombies too — anything."</p>

<p>Worried my expression was too hard, I softened my mouth as much as possible.</p>

<p>Yi Gaon looked dazed. She stared at me for a long moment, then spoke.</p>

<p>"So all I have to do is make the vaccine?"</p>

<p>The True Ending of DeZonDeal is vaccine production.</p>

<p>But I didn't want to close the Trial like this.</p>

<p>I glanced at Je Hyun-oh. His black goggles were looking at me.</p>

<p>If it were Kwak Hanmuk or Mo Haein, I'd hesitate to say this — but with Je Hyun-oh, it seemed fine.</p>

<p>I said the ending I wanted.</p>

<p>"Please make a cure too."</p>
