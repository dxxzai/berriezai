<h1>Chapter 155</h1>

<p>His eyes were smiling, but it wasn't difficult to tell his mood.</p>

<p>Because his gaze wasn't smiling at all.</p>

<p>I held my breath and looked up at Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh just stared at me silently without blinking once.</p>

<p>It seemed he would keep staring like that until I said something.</p>

<p>“Um... Lieutenant Colonel Je. Please calm down first...”</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh’s smile deepened.</p>

<p>He looked particularly unwell, which made me even more worried and scared.</p>

<p>‘Should I put a veil over him?’</p>

<p>I genuinely broke out in a cold sweat.</p>

<p>It felt as if even the alcohol that had been circulating in my body just moments ago was vanishing completely.</p>

<p>I had intended to have a proper conversation with Sa Gongyeon.</p>

<p>I was curious about exactly when she had started watching me, and I wanted to ask for information regarding Geumcheon.</p>

<p>However, killing Sa Gongyeon wasn't an option.</p>

<p>I never imagined that a low-level god like me could handle Black Block's subordinates.</p>

<p>My only plan was to coax Black Block and use Sa Gongyeon to my advantage.</p>

<p>'To think Lieutenant Colonel Je read the Day News...'</p>

<p>My mind went blank at this situation, which I hadn't anticipated at all.</p>

<p>Moreover, he knew too much.</p>

<p>I couldn't even guess how much he knew, so it was hard to hide anything.</p>

<p>As the silence dragged on, Je Hyun-oh placed his metal hand neatly on the rooftop door.</p>

<p>Screeeech...</p>

<p>The sound of a slow scraping downward echoed eerily right beside me.</p>

<p>For a brief moment, I wanted to use Prohibition and run away.</p>

<p>But no matter how hard I tried to escape, I would be caught within a couple of flaps of his wings.</p>

<p>I gave up and answered.</p>

<p>&quot;You can't kill them...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Why?&quot; </p>

<p>I felt like that metal hand would scratch me if I said something that displeased him.</p>

<p>I managed to utter words that tried to downplay the truth somehow.</p>

<p>“I gave permission.”</p>

<p>It was the best answer I could come up with right now, but there was not a particularly positive reaction.</p>

<p>The metal hand scratched at the rooftop door once again.</p>

<p>Faced with that silent demand for an explanation, I stammered out the rest.</p>

<p>“Well... it’s necessary for the operation of Earth...”</p>

<p>I glanced sideways at the metal hand placed right next to my face for a moment, then added.</p>

<p>“I made a deal to provide the photos in exchange for a small reward.”</p>

<p>When I explained that the contract had already been finalized, Je Hyun-oh tilted his head slightly to the side.</p>

<p>I apologized earnestly inwardly.</p>

<p>‘I’m sorry, Lieutenant Colonel Je. I need to earn some Star Fragments.’</p>

<p>Then, I heard him call out in a low voice.</p>

<p>“Goyo.”</p>

<p>“Yes...”</p>

<p>“Should I do the same?”</p>

<p>“Yes?”</p>

<p>Startled, my voice involuntarily rose.</p>

<p>Leaving me behind, he strode across the rooftop..</p>

<p>The moment I saw him about to unfold his metal wings, I had a hunch.</p>

<p>He was on the verge of losing his mind.</p>

<p>I hurriedly chased after him, grabbed Je Hyun-oh, and shouted.</p>

<p>“Actually, I didn’t do this because I wanted to!!”</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh stopped in his tracks. The truth immediately flowed from my lips.</p>

<p>“Well, it really was unavoidable given the circumstances. There was a certain god who had been watching over me for a long time...”</p>

<p>In the end, I spilled the beans about Black Block. After a long explanation, I added a remark that sounded as pitiful as possible.</p>

<p>“I’m still very weak. My abilities are lacking, too... Since it was a situation where I couldn’t do anything, I just thought it would be good to earn some money to spend in space.”</p>

<p>“.......”</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh did not answer. He turned his back to me for a moment before finally turning to look at me.</p>

<p>The moment our eyes met, my heart grew heavy.</p>

<p>It was because I had realized that I had made <em>my subordinate</em> anxious.</p>

<p>My mouth opened involuntarily, without me even realizing it.</p>

<p>“<em>I’m sorry</em>...”</p>

<p>And I placed a golden veil over him.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh said nothing. He simply closed and opened his eyes slowly and silently.</p>

<p>However, while he usually looked at the veil whenever I placed a protective veil over him, this time he looked at me.</p>

<p>I met his gaze and thought.</p>

<p>What is Je Hyun-oh?</p>

<p>Did he learn everything he knows from &lt;Zero&gt;?</p>

<p>But Je Hyun-oh knew too many <em>truths</em> and possessed abilities I was unaware of.</p>

<p>Everything was ambiguous, yet one thing was certain.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh was <em>my subordinate</em>, and I had to take responsibility for him.</p>

<p>I cautiously made a promise to him.</p>

<p>“I will grow strong quickly so that this will never happen again.”</p>

<p>The protective veil scattered. Even amidst the golden dust, Je Hyun-oh was looking at me unchangingly.</p>

<p>I felt a strange sense of satisfaction at the fact that he was no longer looking at the sky.</p>

<p>Just as our gaze lingered on one another, a screen appeared in mid-air.</p>

<p>—Lieutenant Colonel Je Hyun-oh. You must return now.</p>

<p>I had thought the TRA was quieter than expected, but it was thanks to Samra's cooperation.</p>

<p>Je Hyun-oh put his goggles back on.</p>

<p>Just as I breathed a sigh of relief at having barely escaped the crisis, a rough whisper reached me.</p>

<p>“Where is the cake?”</p>

<p>I looked at Je Hyun-oh in surprise.</p>

<p>I had sent a message saying I would buy him a cake upon his return.</p>

<p>However, I hadn't expected him to come back this quickly, so I was empty-handed.</p>

<p>I certainly hadn't expected him to remember my message and ask about it.</p>

<p>“I don't have any right now. Shall I go buy some? If you could wait just a moment...”</p>

<p>I was about to hurry and go to a nearby bakery when he reached out and blocked my path. I bumped into a sturdy forearm and looked up at Je Hyun-oh.</p>

<p>“...Ah.”</p>

<p>I belatedly remembered what he had said a while ago about hating waiting.</p>

<p>I hesitated for a moment before speaking.</p>

<p>“I’ll prepare in advance next time.”</p>

<div class="hyunoh"><p>^ ^</p></div>

<p>Je Hyun-oh, with an emoticon flashing on his goggles, took a step back.</p>

<p>Then, turning around, he walked toward the railing and spread his wings.</p>

<p>I pictured him flying through the city’s nightscape.</p>

<p>However, instead of flying freely, Je Hyun-oh vanished as if plummeting down from the building.</p>

<p>I ran to the railing and looked around, but I couldn't see anything.</p>

<p>I was left all alone on the rooftop.</p>

<hr />

<p>I stayed on the rooftop for quite a while to clear my thoughts, and when I returned home, Kwak Hanmuk was awake.</p>

<p>He said he had just dropped Mo Haein off at home.</p>

<p>“Where did you go?” </p>

<p>I wasn't sure if I should mention Je Hyun-oh's unauthorized absence, so I simply replied that I had gone out for some fresh air.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk didn't ask any further questions and just praised me for cleaning the house thoroughly.</p>

<p>I drank a hangover remedy alongside him and went to bed early.</p>

<p>When I woke up in the morning, I felt perfectly fine.</p>

<p>I wasn't entirely sure, but it seemed I had a high tolerance for alcohol.</p>

<p>Seeing me perfectly fine without a hangover, Kwak Hanmuk added a new pun to his list.</p>

<p>&quot;It wasn't a chestnut, it was an alcohol chestnut...&quot;</p>

<p>He shook his head and took out a hangover dessert to eat.</p>

<p>I thought he was even more impressive for eating dessert in the morning, but I didn't say it out loud.</p>

<p>Since training was starting in earnest today, I had to report to work at the TRA.</p>

<p>I checked my phone as I put on my uniform.</p>

<p>I had thought the messages would stop after last night, but there was another one waiting.</p>

<p>Anyway, thinking the sender had a bad personality, I replied and headed to work with Kwak Hanmuk, who had finished getting ready.</p>

<p>As we walked side by side toward the TRA, people glanced at us.</p>

<p>It seemed our uniforms stood out a bit.</p>

<p>Everyone knew that there was a military organization in this area. They just didn't know exactly what it did.</p>

<p>I heard that since they couldn't reveal the existence of the Trial, the TRA was pretending to be a new military organization to the outside world.</p>

<p>‘The TRA uniform is cool, though.’</p>

<p>It wasn't because I liked the Archive game; objectively, it was a cool uniform.</p>

<p>Kwak Hanmuk, walking beside me, muttered in a sad voice.</p>

<p>“I have to work like hell starting today...”</p>

<p>He meant that work had piled up just because we partied and played yesterday.</p>

<p>He had said he was constantly busy lately, but I wondered when he would finally get a break.</p>

<p>After arriving at the TRA, Kwak Hanmuk patted my shoulder and said.</p>

<p>“Good luck at your training. If anyone bothers you, tell me.”</p>

<p>“Yes. I’ll be back.”</p>

<p>I didn’t miss the opportunity and demonstrated the military salute I had learned in a crash course yesterday.</p>

<p>As soon as he saw my salute, Kwak Hanmuk burst into laughter like a snack bag bursting.</p>

<p>I blinked, wondering if it was still clumsy, but he chuckled and saluted back.</p>

<p>Then, patting my back hard, he said I could be a captain now, not just a trainee.</p>

<p>‘That’s a compliment, right?’</p>

<p>I felt my whole body shaking from Kwak Hanmuk’s huge hand.</p>

<p>After a brief commotion, I parted ways with Kwak Hanmuk and headed to the training area.</p>

<p>The pudding slime on my head asked as if it were genuinely curious.</p>

<p>“Roasted Chestnut. How do humans train a god?”</p>

<p>To him, this seemed like a bizarre spectacle. I explained it to him from an alien perspective.</p>

<p>“I am pretending to be human to collect necessary information. It is required for planetary operations. As you know, Dominic-ssi, I was born not long ago, so there are many things I do not know.”</p>

<p>I launched into a long explanation, implying that one must understand humans to govern them.</p>

<p>Pudding Slime still seemed unable to understand, but he did not add any further comments.</p>

<div class="systembox"><p>You constantly keep in mind the fact that you must close seven Trials within two months.</p></div>

<p>In the midst of this, a system window suddenly appeared.</p>

<p>This was something I had been thinking about as well, and I was devising a way to enter the trials while completing my training.</p>

<p>‘But why hasn’t it mentioned registering lately?’</p>

<p>Since it had appeared anyway, I asked what I was curious about, but it remained silent once again.</p>

<p>Anyway, it appeared and disappeared only when it suited it. I was used to it by now, so I didn't say much either.</p>

<p>I did have a hunch about what was going on.</p>

<p>I had consumed ■■■ and Infernum.</p>

<p>As the two things mixed, the rate of digestion definitely increased compared to before, and just handling this was overwhelming for me right now.</p>

<p>It seemed it judged that there was no immediate need for registration, given that the divine power would surge significantly once the digestion was complete.</p>

<p>I felt that if handling a <em>young god</em> was already a struggle, things might become truly unbearable once the digestion process finished.</p>

<p>I had to find a solution before that happened.</p>

<p>I arrived at the training area, pondering what the anchor might be.</p>

<p>“.......”</p>

<p>As soon as I opened the door, I was met with a barrage of stares directed at me.</p>

<p>In the space decorated like a classroom, about fifteen trainees were sitting in rigid postures.
I arrived ten minutes early for class, but everyone else had already arrived.</p>

<p>The gazes directed at me were a mixture of various emotions, ranging from curiosity to disapproval.</p>

<p>It seemed that not many viewed me favorably.</p>

<p>Yesterday, Mo Haein briefly told me about the rumors circulating about me within the TRA.</p>

<p>The superiors, who knew exactly what the Trial was, regarded me as monster.</p>

<p>It was understandable, given the many strange things I had done.</p>

<p>However, the lower-ranking personnel, lacking that knowledge and experience, were reportedly resentful that a mere trainee was receiving special treatment.</p>

<p>Their consensus was that I was able to shut down the Trials thanks to the Special Mission Team.</p>

<p>They argued that such a result was achieved because they assembled a team consisting of the Captains, Je Hyun-oh, and even Samra; they questioned whether it could have been purely due to a trainee.</p>

<p>The fact that I entered the TRA as a trainee when I signed the contract seemed to lend credibility to their reasoning.</p>

<p>I didn't give it much thought, knowing that if I had joined as a Captain, they would have bitten me for that too.</p>

<p>‘I would have stood out no matter what I did.’</p>

<p>What mattered to me was the Special Mission Team.</p>

<p>My goal was to be a moderately rough stone— a bit of a troublemaker, but one that wouldn't be too big of a liability to the name of the Special Mission Team.</p>

<p>Just as I was about to sit in the empty seat at the very back...</p>

<p>Won Taebeom entered the classroom holding a tablet.</p>

<p>Our eyes met immediately.</p>

<p>‘Is it now?’</p>

<p>I saluted him, and he looked at me silently before returning the greeting with a nod.</p>

<p>I still hadn't been able to apologize to Won Taebeom since the closure of Cell-X.</p>

<p>Samra offered to erase Won Taebeom's memories if I wanted, but I declined because that didn't feel quite right.</p>

<p>Since then, I'd been looking for an opportunity to talk to Won Taebeom, but it hadn't been easy.</p>

<p>To begin with, a trainee couldn't just go up to a Lieutenant—a superior far above him—and ask to talk. Still, it would have been ridiculous to ask the captains to arrange a meeting for me.</p>

<p>I was mulling it over when, fortunately, Won Taebeom was assigned to train the new recruits.</p>

<p>It seemed like the perfect time to strike up a conversation after this training was over, if I waited for an opportunity.</p>

<p>'I’ll be coming to kneel soon.'</p>

<p>Thinking this to myself, I looked at Won Taebeom with a somewhat gentle gaze.</p>

<p>For some reason, Won Taebeom couldn't meet my eyes for long.</p>

<p>He slightly lowered his gaze.</p>

<p>Then, he checked his watch and began the class as soon as it struck the hour.</p>

<p>“We will proceed with practical training today.”</p>

<p>Won Taebeom opened the metal bag placed on the podium.</p>

<p>Inside was a Trial Meteorite. It was a low-grade meteorite intended for training.</p>

<p>A tense atmosphere flowed through the trainees at the sight of the colorful meteorite.</p>

<p>Just as I was thinking how cute it looked, like a child's toy, Won Taebeom mentioned the name of the trial.</p>

<p>“The trial we are entering is &lt;Hop Drop Pop&gt;, rated two stars.”</p>

<p>&lt;Hop Drop Pop&gt;. I was even more delighted to hear it because it was a game I had enjoyed playing.</p>

<p>HoDroPop was a large-scale multiplayer game with a casual atmosphere.</p>

<p>Players start simultaneously and proceed through various mini-games; it was a survival style game where players navigated obstacles or engaged in light duels.</p>

<p>It was fun because players could both cooperate and hinder each other, and it had a significant advantage.</p>

<p>‘There is no death penalty.’</p>

<p>In HoDroPop, if a player falls off the stage, a heart is consumed.</p>

<p>Once all six given hearts are used up, the player switches to spectator mode.</p>

<p>If even a single participant completes the course, the spectators also clear the trial, allowing everyone to exit safely.</p>

<p>It was considered one of the safer trials.</p>

<p>Even if all the trainees became spectators, clearing the course was guaranteed as long as the instructor completed it, making it a trial perfectly suitable for educational purposes.</p>

<p>However, there was a separate, decisive reason why I liked HoDroPop.</p>

<p>It was the ability to customize the characters in various ways.</p>

<p>I liked that I could customize my character by selecting different options when starting the game.</p>

<p>I wondered if customization would apply to Pudding Slime as well.</p>

<p>“I will assign evaluation scores based on the ranking of those who complete the course.”</p>

<p>Won Taebeom added in a stiff tone after finishing the explanation of the trial.</p>

<p>“Trainees must cooperate with each other to complete the course.”</p>

<p>Cooperation, huh...</p>

<p>Judging by the atmosphere, it didn't seem like there were many people willing to cooperate with me.</p>

<p>Well, it didn't matter.</p>

<p>I was confident I could cross the finish line even if every single trainee here swarmed in to interfere.</p>

<p>However, since it was for the TRA's educational purposes, I should be careful not to clear it with the True Ending and have it shut down.</p>

<p>'Let's just make it into the top 5.'</p>

<p>I set a reasonable rank in my mind and resolved to myself.</p>

<p>Won Taebeom, having just explained the final precautions, announced the entry into the trial.</p>

<p>&quot;Then I will enter.&quot;</p>

<p>As he placed his hand on the meteorite, the world turned fluorescent.</p>

<div class="systembox"><div class="systembox_wrap_j"><p>&quot;Han Goyo (Little Lamb)&quot; has entered &lt;Hop Drop Pop&gt;!</p></div></div>

<p>However, having entered HoDroPop, I couldn't even read the main quest.</p>

<p>Someone had pushed me off the stage the moment I entered.</p>

<p>I plummeted rapidly against a backdrop that looked cute.</p>

<div class="systembox">
<p>
Down I go!
</p>
</div>
<p>With my vision turning gray, one heart was consumed.</p>

<p>And then I...</p>

<p>&quot;...Ha.&quot;</p>

<p>I was resurrected, slightly annoyed.</p>
